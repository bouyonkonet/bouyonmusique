DJ Snow: Bouyon Arena and the independence mixes

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

DJ Snow, whose YouTube channel is named Dj Snow767, is a DJ of mixes built around Dominica's calendar: “Bouyon Arena 2025”, a 69-minute bouyon mixtape published as Mas Domnik approached, and the independence mixes, “Independence Soiree Groovy” and “Power” in 2020, then “Dominica Independence Mix 2026” in October 2026. On October 26, 2026, the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster at The Realm, Roseau, names him among the night's DJs.

Bouyon Arena 2025: the Mas Domnik mixtape

On February 25, 2025, Dj Snow767 uploaded “DJ Snow Bouyon Arena 2025 (Vol 1)”, a 69-minute mixtape presented as a brand new bouyon mix “as we gear up for Mas Domnik 2025”, Dominica's carnival. The page names its voices: Trilla G, Signal Band, Triple Kay, Reo, Carlyn XP and Asa Bantan.

It is the channel's most explicitly bouyon page, and the “Vol 1” mention announces a series. For the context of these artists and of carnival, see our page on bouyon in Dominica.

The independence mixes: Groovy, Power, 2026

On October 27, 2020, “Dominica Independence Soiree - Groovy by DJ Snow” was published with a definition: a “slow tempo” mix, a “true taste of Dominica's culture through music”, with a download link; it was past 54,000 views when this profile was written. Its counterpart, “Independence Soiree - Power”, was close to 20,000. On October 14, 2026, “DJ Snow Dominica Independence Mix 2026” carried the tradition into the current season.

These mixes mark independence month, celebrated on November 3 in Dominica, and form, with Bouyon Arena, the backbone of the channel. The rest of the catalogue widens the repertoire: 2021 and 2022 soca mixes with Nadia Batson, Machel Montano, Lyrikal and Patrice Roberts, a 2021 soca “De Cure Riddim Mix”, a 2022 afrobeat mix, an R&B mix, a lovers rock reggae mix and an “XMAS Power Party”.

On the WCK 40 bill

On October 26, 2026, Jazz In The City staged the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser at The Realm, 80 King George V Street, Roseau, from 7 to 11 p.m., entry 40 dollars. The poster published on Instagram by @jazzinthecity767 names WCK, Delly, Skinny Banton, Carlyn XP, Shelly, Benji, Mr MV on the keys, DJ Snow and DJ Sleem. The post's caption announces “a whole year of epic celebrations” for the band's 40 years.

See the profiles of WCK and DJ Sleem. This profile is written from the YouTube and Instagram pages opened on October 27, 2026. None spells out where DJ Snow is from; his channel carries the 767 code and his mixes follow the island's calendar, and this site places him in the Dominican scene without stating a birthplace.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Snow?

A DJ behind the Dj Snow767 channel, maker of the Bouyon Arena 2025 mixtape for Mas Domnik and of Dominica's independence mixes, Groovy and Power (2020) and Dominica Independence Mix 2026.

What is Bouyon Arena 2025?

A 69-minute bouyon mixtape uploaded on February 25, 2025 as Mas Domnik 2025 approached, with Trilla G, Signal Band, Triple Kay, Reo, Carlyn XP and Asa Bantan.

What is the Independence Soiree Groovy?

A slow-tempo mix published on October 27, 2020, presented as a true taste of Dominica's culture through music, past 54,000 views; Independence Soiree Power is its counterpart.

Does DJ Snow mix anything other than bouyon?

Yes: his channel holds 2021 and 2022 soca mixes, a De Cure Riddim Mix, an afrobeat mix, an R&B mix, a lovers rock reggae mix and an XMAS Power Party.

Is he linked to WCK's 40th anniversary?

Yes: the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster, a Jazz In The City night at The Realm, Roseau, on October 26, 2026, names him among the DJs.
