Dominica bouyon music: Capleton, back after 20 years

By Smokiller · Published November 1, 2026

Twenty years. That is how long Dominica waited to see Capleton on one of its stages again. On the Friday of the World Creole Music Festival 2026, at Windsor Park, the Fireman played to a capacity crowd that knew his songs by heart, per DancehallMag.

A crowd singing every line

DancehallMag describes a crowd already warmed up before he walked out, singing along line for line as he worked through his best known songs.

Capleton called the night energetic and very interactive, and said the happiness of the crowd is what stayed with him, in comments reported by DancehallMag.

Catalogue and new material

The set did not lean only on the classics. Capleton used it to introduce new material, including Heights of Fire, a taste of his direction after more than 30 years in the business, per DancehallMag.

He also has a children s empowerment song with Luciano waiting to drop, executive produced by Contractor Music. No release date has been announced, per the same source. His next stop announced is the Welcome to Jamrock cruise.

Why the wait lasted 20 years

Per Capleton, there was never a falling out: his calendar simply never lined up with the dates Dominican organisers wanted, though they came calling several times.

He calls Dominica fans some of his biggest and most loyal fans, and speaks of nothing but love, respect and support, in comments reported by DancehallMag.

His read on the island

Capleton says he loves Dominica because it reminds him so much of Jamaica, naming ackee, June plum and bumpy banana. He also said he was impressed with the development he saw there.

His most quoted line: the island is literally a fiery ground with several volcanoes, so as a Fireman, he loves that. The WCMF, spanning bouyon, cadence-lypso, zouk, reggae, dancehall, soca and Afrobeats, was the natural setting for the return.

Frequently asked questions

When had Capleton last played Dominica before WCMF 2026?

Twenty years earlier, per DancehallMag: WCMF 2026 marks his return to a Dominican stage after two decades.

What new song did Capleton introduce in Roseau?

Heights of Fire, introduced during his WCMF 2026 set, per DancehallMag.

Why was Capleton away for 20 years?

Per his own comments reported by DancehallMag, his schedule never lined up with the dates organisers wanted. There was never a conflict.
