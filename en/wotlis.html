Wotlis: Unstick and Knock you with Fifin, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Wotlis is one of the voices of the Go Fast crew, a regular partner of Fifin and Eks, and a frequent name in the releases of Riddim Beast Studios, the studio of DJ Taffy. Five primary pages opened during this research tie him to bouyon in their titles, from “Just Pass Dat” in 2024 to “Give me Dat” in 2026, by way of “Unstick”, the video shared with Fifin. One page spells the name “wotless” in its title while crediting Wotlis: this profile keeps the spelling of the credits. No origin is stated in the opened sources.

With Fifin: Knock you and Unstick

The Wotlis and Fifin pairing signs two bouyon titles with DJ Taffy. “Knock you”, released on September 6, 2024 with the label bouyon 2025 and the name Go Fast Music, is written and performed by both voices, produced by DJ Taffy with additional production by Jackie Beats and live drums by Drummer Fridge. Riddim Beast Studios and Go Fast Music are its executive producers.

“Unstick” followed on April 7, 2025, this time as a 4 minute official video, still titled bouyon 2025. DJ Taffy produced it on a Jackie Beatz riddim, Untamed Production played live drums, and the whole thing was recorded, mixed and mastered at Riddim Beast Studios. The performers are credited as Fifin and Wotlis, with the word Go Fast.

Wet Already, Just Pass Dat and Give me Dat

Wotlis also carries titles without Fifin. “Wet Already”, released on June 26, 2024 and titled bouyon 2024, is written by Wotlis and Candy and produced by DJ Taffy, with Drummer Fridge on drums and recording at Riddim Beast Studios. That is the page where the title reads “wotless” while the credits read Wotlis. The day before, on June 25, 2024, the Jackie Beatz channel posted “Just Pass Dat”, a Go Fast title written by Eks, Fifin and Wotlis, produced, mixed and mastered by Don Carli.

The most recent verified title is “Give me Dat”, by Wotlis with Shyanne, posted on July 4, 2026 under the label bouyon 2026, with no credit description. Shyanne’s identity is not specified on the page, so this site ties her to no other profile. The same circuit also carries “Tie Up”, with Eks and Wotlis, produced by DJ Taffy with live bass by NattyBill: its page states no genre, so it is named here without entering the structured data.

What the credits say, and their limits

The opened credits sketch the same entourage as for Fifin and Eks: DJ Taffy and Jackie Beatz producing, Drummer Fridge and Untamed Production on drums, Riddim Beast Studios as the recording room. The “dominica” and “bouyon” tags under every DJ Taffy video belong to the whole channel and say nothing about Wotlis’s own origin.

No biography, no interview and no official artist page were opened for Wotlis in this research. The verifiable file fits into five bouyon titles dated 2024 to 2026 and one spelling variant. For other recent releases in the genre, see our new bouyon in 2026 coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Wotlis?

Wotlis is a voice of the Go Fast crew, credited as writer and performer on several bouyon titles produced by DJ Taffy and Jackie Beatz. No public biography was found.

Is it Wotlis or Wotless?

The credits read Wotlis on every opened page. Only one page, the one for “Wet Already”, spells “wotless” in its title. This site keeps Wotlis.

Which Wotlis bouyon titles are verified?

Five: “Just Pass Dat” (bouyon 2024), “Wet Already” with Candy (bouyon 2024), “Knock you” with Fifin (bouyon 2025), “Unstick” with Fifin (bouyon 2025) and “Give me Dat” with Shyanne (bouyon 2026).

Who produces Wotlis?

Mostly DJ Taffy and Riddim Beast Studios, with Jackie Beatz on riddims and additional production, and Don Carli on “Just Pass Dat”.

Where is Wotlis from?

No opened source says. The origin stays undocumented on this site.
