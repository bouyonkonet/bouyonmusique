Denmath: the bouyon producer from Saint-Martin

Denmath, @prod.denmath, is a producer, sound engineer and artistic director originally from Saint-Martin, of Haitian heritage, based between Nantes and Paris. His specialties: bouyon, kompa, shatta, dancehall, soca and zouk.

Bouyon riddims signed Denmath

His catalog includes his own bouyon riddims, Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim, released in 2022, and collaborations like Tarif by Koko Bwa, with Denmath and Boutcha Bwa, released in April 2025. He also has spotted credits on tracks like In She Garden by Lyte.

A profile Bouyon Shattaz follows

In October 2026, Denmath’s management contacted Bouyon Shattaz to present his bouyon work and open the door to collaborations with artists. The typical profile of the producer the Bouyon Shattaz network wants to connect to Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the key bouyon artists?

WCK created the genre, Asa Bantan is widely called the King of Bouyon, and the new generation includes Reo, Ridge, Signal Band and Shelly, while producers like Smokiller export the sound.

Who publishes these artist pages?

BouyonMusique.com is published by Bouyon Shattaz, the platform of Dominican-born producer Smokiller, with facts verified artist by artist.
