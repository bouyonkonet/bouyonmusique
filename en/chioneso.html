Chioneso: the voice of Here for You, bouyon, Brooklyn

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Chioneso is a singer who exists, in the bouyon archives consulted, through a single record only: “Here for You”, the Rudy Ru$h single whose official video puts her as a feature in its very heading, “Here for You - RudyRu$h ft. Chioneso”. The description of the video, opened in full for this profile, also credits her on the writing, alongside RudyRu$h and Yasmine, and presents the record as a meeting of bouyon energy. The music press that covered the release, 13th Street Promotions, presents her as a Brooklyn based recording artist: this profile carries that billing as a press billing, attributed, and not as a verified biography. No bouyon solo record, no artist page and no official account confirmed by opening was found. This profile publishes the feature, the writing credit, the making of the record as its sources tell it, and it states openly everything it does not know.

Who Chioneso is: a feature at the top of the video and a writing credit

Let us start with the exact inventory, because the whole file fits inside it. Chioneso is credited on a single verified record, a record by another artist. “Here for You” is the single of Rudy Ru$h, an artist born in Dominica and based in the United States, documented on this site. In the heading of the official video, Chioneso appears as a feature, shortened to ft., the formula of the guests. In the description of the same video, she appears a second time, in the writing credit, in the sentence that names the three writers of the record: RudyRu$h, Yasmine and Chioneso. No source consulted documents a bouyon record where Chioneso is the lead artist, nor a second feature under this name.

The description of the video also names her by a handle, @chionesobakr, printed on the second line, right after the name of the lead artist. This profile reproduces that handle as what it is in the source, a platform handle attached to her artist name, and it draws from it neither a stated family name nor a biography. No profile behind that handle was opened or verified during this research, so the structured data of this page links to no external account. The public form of the name, in both the primary and the secondary sources opened, is the mononym Chioneso: that is the form this site keeps, in the heading of the video, in the writing credit and in the heading of the press article.

The only location in the file comes from the press, and this profile treats it as such from the first paragraph to the last. 13th Street Promotions, a music press site that follows the New York Caribbean scene and covered the release on October 20, 2026, writes that “Here for You” features Chioneso, a Brooklyn based recording artist. That is a secondary press billing, not a line of the official description, which does not place the singer. It is carried here attributed to its author, because it is the only one in the file and because it places the collaboration inside the geography of the record: an artist of Dominican birth based in the United States, a guest the press says is from Brooklyn, and a producer from the island at the controls, as the next section shows.

The double credit, voice and writing, draws the exact role of Chioneso in this record. She is not a voice called in over a finished track: she co-writes the record she sings, alongside the lead artist and a third writer, Yasmine, who does not sing in the credit roll of the video and appears there only in the writing post. That triangle split, a lead artist who sings and writes, a guest who sings and writes, a writer who writes without appearing as a feature, is the complete credit roll on the song side of “Here for You”. For a feature artist, that is precise and checkable career information: she enters bouyon through a record of which she is also one of the three signatories.

Here for You: the official description, line by line

The record that carries Chioneso is documented by the page of its official video, opened in full for this profile. The heading of the video is “Here for You - RudyRu$h ft. Chioneso”, and its description opens with the words “HERE FOR YOU, OFFICIAL MUSIC VIDEO”, followed by the billing line: RudyRu$h feat. @chionesobakr. The presentation sentence of the record says, in the team own words, that the song brings together bouyon energy, rhythm and vibes, and that two artists come together there to create something special. The description then gives the credit roll: directed by @ReemGrafixStudios, written by RudyRu$h, Yasmine and Chioneso, casting and co direction by @multiartistryent, and four dancers named by their handles.

The rest of the description places the record inside its release economy. It states that “Here for You” can be heard on all streaming platforms, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music and more, and it points toward the account of the lead artist, @iamrudyrush, to follow what comes next. It closes on a slogan, “BOUYON TO THE WORLD”, and on a line of keywords that places the record inside its search territory: Bouyon, Bouyon Music, Dominica, Soca, Caribbean Music, among others. No song page of the platforms named was opened during this research, for Chioneso or for the lead artist: the official video is therefore, to date, the primary source of the record, and it alone carries the recording in the structured data of this page.

What the description does not say must be stated as clearly as what it says. It gives neither a release date for the single, nor a label, nor a duration, nor a production credit: the name of the producer comes from the release announcement, treated in the next section, not from the video page. It does not describe the sung part of Chioneso either, chorus, verse or bridge, and no documented listening describes it. This profile therefore does not describe her performance: it establishes her presence at the top of the bill of the video, her shared writing credit, and the making frame that the opened sources publish. The video itself, placed in a club by the press that covered it, shows the two artists among the dancers, according to 13th Street Promotions: that image is reported here as the account of that site, not as a description this profile would have taken shot by shot.

The making as the release tells it: Ridge, the writing, the casting

The complete making of the record comes from a release announcement dated September 9, 2026, from New York, distributed by the EIN Presswire service and opened in full for this profile. A point of method first, because it holds for everything that follows: a release of this kind is a text supplied by the artist team, here his agency Playbook MG, and carried as is by dozens of sites. When twenty sites publish the same paragraph, that is not twenty confirmations, it is one source repeated twenty times. This profile therefore attributes each fact of this section to the release, and it cross checks it with the description of the video whenever the two documents meet, which is the case for the writing and for the casting.

On the writing, the two documents agree, and the release adds the context. The record was written by Rudy Ru$h, Yasmine and Chioneso, the same line as the description of the video, and produced by Ridge, the Dominican producer whose role in the current wave this site documents. The release states that the record is inspired by real life relationships, that it tells of two people letting go, dancing together and reassuring each other that they are present for one another, and that the team, during the writing, took the playful dancefloor energy of the “Thong Song” by Sisqó as a starting point, to rework it through a modern bouyon and crossover lens. In the words of the artist carried by the release, “Here for You” is the meeting point between the high energy bouyon sound of his island roots and the crossover influences of his American experience. For Chioneso, the checkable consequence is plain: she is a co-writer of a record whose crossover intention is stated in black and white by its own writers, and her voice is the guest voice of that positioning.

On the video, the release completes the official description without contradicting it. The video was created with MultiArtistry Entertainment, which the description credits with casting and co direction, and the release insists on that casting: Caribbean women of different body types, heights and complexions, brought together to celebrate confidence, individuality and natural beauty, and to let a wider audience feel represented. The life of the record outside the video is also a fact of the release, attributed as such: over the Labor Day weekend, in early September in the United States, Rudy Ru$h is said to have performed at several venues where the record is said to have had patrons wining and jumping. None of those stage elements directly concerns Chioneso, and no opened document places her on those stages: this profile therefore files them in the life of the record, not in the biography of the guest.

The press independent of the release adds two touches, and they close the table of sources. 13th Street Promotions, in its article of October 20, 2026, places the action of the video in a club, with the two artists among the dancers, credits the direction to ReemGrafix, and reports the aim of the lead artist: to take things to another level for Carnival 2027, while the bouyon wave keeps growing. The same site recalls that Rudy Ru$h caused a scene with “Anywhere We Go” in 2025. None of those elements gives Chioneso a second record, a stage or a date of her own: they confirm the frame of her only appearance, a late summer single of 2026, a club video, a diaspora press that follows, and a carnival target carried by the lead artist.

Rudy Ru$h: the lead artist, and the circuit the feature plugs into

To place Chioneso, the artist who invites her must be placed, since his circuit gives the feature its context. Rudy Ru$h, also written RudyRu$h in the headings of his official videos, is an artist born in Dominica and based in the United States: his releases of 2024 place him in the Bronx, in New York, where he runs his OffDaBlock Records banner. His first documented milestone is reggae, not bouyon: “Exodus”, with Junior Reid, released in October 2024 with a video shot in Jamaica, verified on this site by opening the official video. The bouyon pivot comes next, with “Anywhere We Go” in 2025 according to the press, then “Here for You” in 2026, the record that carries Chioneso. His full path, including the claims of his releases that the lineups consulted do not confirm, is read on his page of this site.

The junction between the two artists rests on a third name, that of the producer. “Here for You” is produced by Ridge, a producer from the island, and not by an American club maker: that credit, given by the release, plugs the single into the Dominican workshop, while its two voices come from the New York diaspora, the lead artist based in the United States and his guest whom the press says is from Brooklyn. The record is thus, in its very making, an object of the bouyon diaspora: voices from outside, a producer from inside, a genre from Roseau reframed for American clubs. Chioneso is, to date, the documented female voice of that precise object, as a feature and as a writer.

The context of the genre recalls the depth of the language this single speaks. Bouyon was born in Dominica at the end of the 1980s, and bouyon soca was started in Dominica in 2003 by Dada, according to the first hand testimony of Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer and a participant in that era. A generation later, the genre travels through artists based outside who take it as an export language, while the wave of the island pushes in the other direction. “Here for You”, with its “BOUYON TO THE WORLD” slogan printed in the description of the video, claims exactly that export function, and the place of Chioneso in it is that of the invited co-writer: neither an extra in the video, nor an anonymous chorus voice, but a signatory of the record and the second name on its bill. What the file does not say: the limits, stated

This profile ends with its limits, because they form half of its information. No solo record by Chioneso was verified during this research in the bouyon field: no single under her name, no artist page of her own on the platforms consulted, no second feature, no other writing credit. No biography tied to this record, no interview, no official photograph and no social media account confirmed by direct opening was found: the handle @chionesobakr is a mention in the description of the video, not an opened profile, so the structured data of this page links to no external account. No family name is stated, no personal origin is published by the primary source, and the Brooklyn billing comes from a single secondary press text, attributed to 13th Street Promotions every time it appears here.

The verified scope fits into one record and two credits, cross checked by three opened documents. The record: “Here for You”, by Rudy Ru$h featuring Chioneso, whose official video presents the track as a meeting of bouyon energy and states it is available on all streaming platforms. The two credits: Chioneso as a feature in the heading and on the bill of the video, and Chioneso as a co-writer, with Rudy Ru$h and Yasmine, in the description of the video as in the release of September 9, 2026, which adds the production by Ridge. Anything circulating elsewhere about Chioneso without a source, a full biography imported from a neighbouring full name, a solo discography or stages of her own, should be read with the caution that scope imposes: this profile merges no file from outside the record that makes her appear here.

The listening, while waiting for what comes next, fits into one record, and through it into the path of its lead artist. “Here for You” is heard as the crossover bouyon single of Rudy Ru$h in 2026, and his path continues on his Rudy Ru$h page, from “Exodus” with Junior Reid to the pivot of 2025. For Chioneso, what comes next will tell whether this feature stays a single appearance or becomes a starting point: a solo record, an artist page or a second credit will bring her into the catalog on her own account. If one of those documents appears, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. In the meantime, it publishes what the sources give, no more and no less: a guest voice based in Brooklyn according to the press that covered her release, one co-writer among three, and a single record, “Here for You”.

On which record can Chioneso be heard?

On “Here for You”, by Rudy Ru$h, whose official video, headed “Here for You - RudyRu$h ft. Chioneso”, presents the record as a meeting of bouyon energy. The description of the video names her by the handle @chionesobakr, credits the direction to ReemGrafixStudios and the casting to MultiArtistry Entertainment, and states the record is available on all streaming platforms.

Did Chioneso write Here for You?

Yes, according to the two opened documents that give the credit roll: the description of the official video and the release announcement of September 9, 2026 both write that the record was written by RudyRu$h, Yasmine and Chioneso. The release adds that the production is signed by Ridge and that the writing drew on the dancefloor energy of the “Thong Song” by Sisqó, reworked through a bouyon and crossover lens.

Where does Chioneso come from?

The official description of the video does not place the singer, and no other opened source publishes her personal origin. This profile therefore carries the Brooklyn billing attributed to that press site, without turning it into a biography.

Has Chioneso released solo records?

No solo record was verified during this research, carried out on October 20, 2026, and no bouyon record under her name appears on the platforms consulted. No artist page of her own, no second feature and no other writing credit tied to bouyon were confirmed. This profile states that openly rather than filling the gap, and it will be completed if a release under her name is published.

Who is Rudy Ru$h, the lead artist of the record?

An artist born in Dominica and based in the United States, placed in the Bronx by his releases of 2024, where he runs his OffDaBlock Records banner. His record “Exodus”, with Junior Reid, released in 2024 in a reggae and dancehall register, is verified on this site by its official video. “Here for You”, in 2026, is his documented crossover bouyon single, produced by Ridge and co-written with Yasmine and Chioneso, who sings on it as a feature.
