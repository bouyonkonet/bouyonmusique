Judi Silva: three records in three years, from feature to lead name

Published October 20, 2026

Judi Silva is a voice of French Antillean bouyon, documented by three verified records in three years, and by nothing else in public. In July 2023, she sang as a feature on “EN LEY” by DJ Leguste, alongside Le Daah. In February 2024, she released “Monte Descend”, her only lead name record found, on 5106881 Records DK. In December 2025, she returned inside the circle of LGC on “Woody Wood”, by D.O.C FIX DEM, where she is credited on vocals and on writing. No biography, no origin and no official account of Judi Silva were verified during this research. This profile follows the three records one by one, reads their credits line by line, separates the namesakes, including jazz singer Judi Silvano, and says exactly where the file stops.

Three records, three years: the complete chronology of the verified file

The Judi Silva file fits on three listings, and their order already tells a path. First record, on July 15, 2023: “EN LEY”, a single by DJ Leguste featuring Judi Silva and Le Daah, released on the DJ LEGUSTE label, in French, at 160 BPM, with a World genre tag on its Shazam page, opened for this research, and about 930 identifications counted on that page. DJ Leguste is a French Antillean bouyon producer whose catalog this site documents from 2023: “EN LEY” is the second single of his first documented year, right after “LE GARS CHAUD”, already with Le Daah. Judi Silva therefore enters the archive of the genre through the door of a producer, as a guest voice, at the very start of that producer catalog.

Second record, on February 4, 2024: “Monte Descend”, released this time under the sole name of Judi Silva, on 5106881 Records DK, at 160 BPM, with an Afro-Beat tag on its page, and about 1,786 identifications, the highest counter of the three listings in the file. To date, it is her only lead name record found on the platforms consulted. Between the 2023 feature and this 2024 single, the movement is readable: the guest voice of a producer becomes, for the space of one record, an artist under her own name, with her own distribution banner, the numbered Records DK name being that of a generic digital distribution, not of an identified scene label.

Third record, on December 19, 2025: “Woody Wood”, released under the name of D.O.C FIX DEM, with Judi Silva and LGC as associated performers, on D.O.C FIX DEM, at 160 BPM, with a Caribbean tag. Nearly two years separate this record from “Monte Descend”, and the return is collective: three performer names on the cover, and, a new fact in the file, a writing credit for Judi Silva herself. The complete chronology therefore fits on three lines, 2023, 2024, 2025, one feature, one solo, one collective record, and no other verified listing joins them to date. No album, no EP: like almost the whole current bouyon wave, Judi Silva is a singles artist.

“EN LEY”: the DJ Leguste feature, and the writer question

“EN LEY” deserves a stop, because its listing holds the most delicate point of the file, and this profile treats it without smoothing it over. The record, released on July 15, 2023, credits DJ Leguste as lead artist and Judi Silva and Le Daah as features. But the writing section of the same listing gives three other names: judikael christine, Daryl Biabiany and Terry Baptiste, as songwriters. Daryl Biabiany is a known name in this catalog: he also co writes “LE GARS CHAUD”, the other 2023 single by DJ Leguste, and he belongs to the workshop of the producer, whose documented legal name is Ryan Auguste. Terry Baptiste completes the writing trio of this record.

There remains the first of those three names, judikael christine, and the obvious temptation to draw it close to Judi Silva, whose initials and part of whose sound it shares. This profile resists that temptation, and says why: no source consulted says that judikael christine is Judi Silva, or that Judi Silva writes under that name, or even that the two names designate one single person. Platform listings mix enough credit errors to forbid that kind of merging on a simple resemblance. The published fact is double and it is enough: Judi Silva is the credited guest voice of the record, and judikael christine is a credited writer of the same record. If a document one day links the two names, this profile will say so, dated. In the meantime, the two lines stay two lines.

Musically, the listing places “EN LEY” in the common regime of this scene: French, 160 BPM, single format, production by DJ Leguste, who signs the record on his own label, as he does for his whole documented catalog. On his artist page, “EN LEY” appears among the highlighted records of DJ Leguste, alongside “PIANOO PIANOO” and “SIWO”, which makes it one of the durably visible pieces of his catalog, and therefore the most exposed doorway of the voice of Judi Silva. Le Daah, the other guest voice of the record, is the recurring vocal collaborator of Leguste in 2023: Judi Silva therefore sings, from her first credit, inside the first circle of a producer, not on a peripheral record.

“Monte Descend”: the only lead name record, and what it establishes

“Monte Descend” is the central piece of the file, for a simple reason: it is the only record where Judi Silva is the lead artist. Released on February 4, 2024 on 5106881 Records DK, as a single, at 160 BPM, it counted about 1,786 Shazam identifications when its page was opened, nearly double the counter of “EN LEY”. The title itself, monte descend, up and down, go up go down, is a dance formula, a movement instruction of the kind bouyon and carnival musics produce by the hundred, and it announces a record made for the body before the text.

The listing of this record is also the poorest in credits of the three: no writer, no producer and no guest are named on the opened page. The genre tag, Afro-Beat, is the one the platform attaches to the record, while the tempo, 160 BPM, is exactly that of the two other records in the file, all of them bouyon. That gap between tag and tempo is frequent on aggregators, where small French Antillean releases are filed in approximate boxes, and this profile flags it without drawing any conclusion on the real style of the track, which no written source describes. What the listing firmly establishes is the existence of a lead name single, dated, labelled and counted: Judi Silva is not only a voice others invite, she has released under her own name.

The banner of the record also says something about its circuit. 5106881 Records DK is a numbered digital distribution name, of the kind automatic distributors generate for independent releases, the same kind of banner found on hundreds of bouyon singles of the same period. No scene label, no named producer, no displayed team: “Monte Descend” looks like a self carried release, where “EN LEY” and “Woody Wood” are workshop releases. The Judi Silva file therefore draws the two parallel lives of the voices of this scene: a guest in the studios of others, autonomous on her own record, without the two circuits joining into a followed catalog.

“Woody Wood”: the return inside the LGC circle, with a writing credit

The third record closes the loop opened in 2023, by moving Judi Silva from the circle of DJ Leguste to the neighbouring circle of LGC. “Woody Wood”, released on December 19, 2025 under the name of D.O.C FIX DEM, brings together three performers: D.O.C FIX DEM, Judi Silva and LGC. Its listing, opened for this research, gives the label, D.O.C FIX DEM, the tempo, 160 BPM, the Caribbean tag, and a counter of a single identification counted, that of a very recent record at the time of research. D.O.C FIX DEM is not unknown to readers of this site: the name appears among the composers of “BIG BELLY” by Lejuh, in the same Guadeloupe network.

The full credits of the record, as the listing publishes them, are the richest of the file. On performers: D.O.C FIX DEM, LGC, and on vocals, Gardiole Jean and Judi Silva. Gardiole Jean is the inverted form of Jean Gardiole, the pillar writer of the LGC catalog, already met on “Copain Copine” with Dézod: the same writing workshop therefore connects the two profiles of this publication wave. On writing: Gardiole Jean, Landry Mette and Judi Silva herself, credited as a songwriter for the first time in the file. On production: Landry Mette, and on mixing: Clarisse Daryl. For an artist whose first record credited her only as a guest voice, and whose second carried no workshop credit at all, this third record marks the fullest status: associated performer on the title, named singer and co writer.

The meeting of the two circles is the structural fact of the file. In 2023, Judi Silva sings with DJ Leguste, a producer whose catalog shares with that of LGC the same regime, French singles at 160 BPM, rotating guest voices, recurring writers. In 2025, she sings with LGC himself, under the hand of a third name, D.O.C FIX DEM, and with the central writer of the LGC catalog. The two studio scenes of bouyon gwada documented by this site therefore meet on this record, and Judi Silva is one of the voices bridging them. To date, it is the last verified trace of the file, and it leaves the path open: three years, three configurations, no published sign of stopping.

The namesakes: Judi Silva is neither Judi Silvano nor Judy Silva

The name Judi Silva touches two namesakes that search engines gladly mix, and this profile separates them cleanly. The first is Judi Silvano, an American jazz singer documented since the 1990s, with albums on Blue Note and on her own label, an international stage career and a complete biography in the jazz press. No source links Judi Silvano to bouyon, to Guadeloupe or to the records of this profile, and the resemblance of the names, Silva against Silvano, creates no link. The records “EN LEY”, “Monte Descend” and “Woody Wood” belong to Judi Silva, and to no one else.

The second bundle of namesakes is even more scattered: Judy Silva and Judi Silva names appear in American press archives, directories and publications with no relation to French Antillean music. None of those documents mentions a record, a label or a producer of the bouyon file. The rule applied here is the rule of this whole site: two people are not merged on a shared name, every fact is attached to the listing that publishes it. The three listings of this profile answer each other through their networks, Leguste in 2023, the lead name in 2024, the LGC circle in 2025, and it is that network coherence, not the name alone, which draws one and the same file artist from record to record.

The namesake inside the file itself must be added, already treated in the “EN LEY” section: the credited writer judikael christine. Same caution, same conclusion: as long as no document links that writer name to the artist name Judi Silva, the two stay distinct in this profile, one as a writer on a DJ Leguste record, the other as the guest voice of that same record. The bouyon gwada scene is small, its credits are young and sometimes approximate, and that is precisely why every merging must wait for its proof.

The limits of the file, and the place of these three records

The limits are frank and this profile states them. Nobody knows where Judi Silva comes from: no source consulted gives her island, her town, her legal name or her path, and this profile deduces her belonging to French Antillean bouyon only from the published facts, the French language of “EN LEY”, the labels and the Guadeloupe networks of her three records. Her age, her beginnings and her stages are all unknown. No verified official account, no interview and no press article about her were confirmed during this research. Even her counters stay modest and dated: about 930 identifications for “EN LEY”, about 1,786 for “Monte Descend”, a single one counted for “Woody Wood”, opening figures that will keep moving.

Those limits do not stop the file from counting. Three verified records, in three different configurations, with a readable rise in status, guest voice, lead name, co writer, compose the exact portrait of a voice of the studio generation of bouyon gwada, the generation that exists through credits more than through biographies. Judi Silva also connects two of the best documented workshops of this scene, that of DJ Leguste and that of LGC, and her name appears alongside Le Daah, Jean Gardiole, Landry Mette and D.O.C FIX DEM. In a genre where credit is the central currency, that network is worth documentation.

Listening follows the chronology: “EN LEY” with DJ Leguste for the entry point, “Monte Descend” for the lead name record, “Woody Wood” for the collective return, then the flow of our bouyon playlist and the context of our bouyon Guadeloupe page. If a fourth record, an official page or a biography of Judi Silva appears, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. In the meantime, it publishes three records, three dates and their credits, and nothing more, because nothing more is verified.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Judi Silva?

A voice of French Antillean bouyon, documented by three verified records: “EN LEY” with DJ Leguste and Le Daah in 2023, “Monte Descend” under her own lead name in 2024, and “Woody Wood” with D.O.C FIX DEM and LGC in 2025. No source consulted publishes her biography or her origin, and this profile holds to those three records and their credits.

What are the verified records of Judi Silva?

Three records. “EN LEY”, by DJ Leguste featuring Judi Silva and Le Daah, released on July 15, 2023, in French, at 160 BPM. “Monte Descend”, under her lead name, released on February 4, 2024 on 5106881 Records DK, at 160 BPM. “Woody Wood”, by D.O.C FIX DEM with Judi Silva and LGC, released on December 19, 2025, at 160 BPM. No album or EP is documented.

Does Judi Silva write her records?

On one record, yes, according to the published credits. The “Woody Wood” listing credits her as a songwriter, alongside Gardiole Jean and Landry Mette, and as a singer. The “Monte Descend” listing names no writer, and the “EN LEY” listing credits judikael christine, Daryl Biabiany and Terry Baptiste, with no source linking the first of those names to Judi Silva.

Are Judi Silva and Judi Silvano the same person?

No source says so, and the files are separate. Judi Silvano is an American jazz singer documented since the 1990s, with albums on Blue Note and a long international career. The three bouyon records of this profile are credited to Judi Silva, inside the networks of DJ Leguste and LGC, and this profile does not merge the two names.

What is the most identified Judi Silva record?

“Monte Descend”, her lead name single released on February 4, 2024, with about 1,786 identifications counted on its Shazam page during this research, against about 930 for “EN LEY” and a single one for “Woody Wood”, a very recent record at the time of counting. Those counters date from the day of research and keep moving.

Where can I hear Judi Silva?

Through her three records, “EN LEY”, “Monte Descend” and “Woody Wood”, best heard inside the catalog of DJ Leguste, where her voice entered the genre, then that of LGC. For the current Guadeloupe scene around her, the Bouyon Shattaz Bouyon Road Mix playlist takes over.

Who produced “Woody Wood” by D.O.C FIX DEM, Judi Silva and LGC?

Landry Mette, credited on production, with Clarisse Daryl on mixing. The listing of the record, released on December 19, 2025 on the D.O.C FIX DEM label, also credits the writing to Gardiole Jean, Landry Mette and Judi Silva. It is the richest record in credits of her file. See D.O.C FIX DEM.

Who produced “EN LEY” with Judi Silva?

DJ Leguste, a French Antillean bouyon producer whose documented legal name is Ryan Auguste, who released the record on his own label on July 15, 2023. “EN LEY” is the second single of his first documented year, right after “LE GARS CHAUD”, and it is among the highlighted records on his artist page.

Are Judi Silva and judikael christine the same person?

No source says so. On the “EN LEY” listing, Judi Silva is credited as a guest voice, and judikael christine as a writer, with Daryl Biabiany and Terry Baptiste. The two names look alike, but no document links them, and this site never merges two people on a mere resemblance: the two lines stay two lines.

What does “Monte Descend” mean?

Monte descend means up and down, go up go down: a dance formula, a movement instruction of the kind bouyon and carnival musics produce by the hundred. The title announces a record made for the body before the text. Its listing, at 160 BPM like the two other records in the file, names no writer, no producer and no guest.

On which label did Judi Silva release “Monte Descend”?

On 5106881 Records DK, a numbered digital distribution name, of the kind automatic distributors generate for independent releases. No scene label, no producer and no team are displayed: “Monte Descend” looks like a self carried release, where “EN LEY” and “Woody Wood” are workshop releases.

Which artists has Judi Silva worked with?

Her three records connect her to DJ Leguste and Le Daah in 2023, then to D.O.C FIX DEM, LGC, Jean Gardiole (credited as Gardiole Jean) and Landry Mette in 2025. She thus bridges two of the best documented workshops of bouyon gwada, that of DJ Leguste and that of LGC, brought together on “Woody Wood”.
