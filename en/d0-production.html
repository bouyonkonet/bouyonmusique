D0 Production: the production name behind the Gwada wave

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

D0 Production is a bouyon production name from Guadeloupe, placed in Les Abymes by the Viberate analytics page, whose catalog, between 2022 and 2025, feeds a large part of the French speaking wave of the genre. Under this name appear riddims as lead artist, “Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022” with 1T1 and “New Bouyon Wave #1” with five guests, and behind this name is signed the production of records carried by others, “Chien” by Totally Spice's and “Bouè'y Bay” with Tiitii NBA. In the credits, one writer name returns systematically next to the banner: Daryl Clarisse, composer, programmer, songwriter and at times mixing engineer. This profile follows the catalog record by record, shows that bundle of credits without turning it into a stated identity, and says what stays undocumented: no biography, no interview, and no verified official account.

What D0 Production is: a production name from Les Abymes

D0 Production presents itself first as a name, not as a face. The platforms treat it as a full artist: it has an artist page on analytics services, lead name records, features and producer credits on the records of others. But no source opened during this research publishes its face, its biography or an interview. The only published geographic anchor comes from the Viberate analytics page, which places D0 Production in Les Abymes, Guadeloupe, and describes its contributions to the reggae and dancehall scenes. This profile quotes that origin with its attribution, because Viberate is an automatically compiled analytics page, not an official biography.

The shape of the name already states the trade. “D0 Production”, with a zero in place of the O, follows the spelling of the studio banners and producer names of the Gwada bouyon wave, comparable to the DJ and beatmaker signatures that fill the credits of this scene. It works in three ways at once: as a lead artist name on its own singles and riddims, as a “D0 production Presents” mention on the records it presents, for example “Bouè'y Bay” by Totally Spice's, and as a discreet producer credit on the pages of others, for example “Chien”. One name, three positions in the making chain: that is the very definition of a production banner.

Its musical territory, though, is unambiguous. Every verified record in the catalog is a bouyon record, in French and Guadeloupean Creole, released between 2022 and 2025, at dance tempos sitting mostly at 160 BPM. The circle of artists is that of the wave of Les Abymes and mainland Guadeloupe: Tiitii NBA as the central partner, Totally Spice's, 1T1, Shanika, DJ Softee, Aknose, Nils, Theomaa, and guest voices such as D-Stylii and Billy BYBF. D0 Production is therefore no Dominican name lost inside Gwada production: it is a cog of the Guadeloupean bouyon scene, documented as such by its own releases.

One confusion the platforms maintain should be cleared at the start. Shazam also references an artist called D0d., with a small d and a dot, author of lofi records such as “True Love” and “Consumerism Simplified”. That D0d. has no relation with D0 Production: neither the records, nor the genres, nor the credits overlap. This profile speaks only of the bouyon banner from Guadeloupe, recognised by its riddims, its Gwada guests and the writer name that signs its credits, the subject of the next section.

The name in the credits: Daryl Clarisse, the repeated signature

Read the catalog pages one after another, and one name returns with a regularity that owes nothing to chance: Daryl Clarisse, also written Daryl Noah Clarisse or, in the reversed order of the databases, Clarisse Daryl. On “Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022”, he is a songwriter alongside Terry Baptiste. On “Chien”, he is composer and in charge of programming, while D0 Production is credited as producer. On “Sa Ka Pété”, he is a songwriter alongside Guenaël Diado and Terry Capitolin. On “ALL NIGHT”, he is a songwriter alongside Julian Axel White and Terry Capitolin. On “KEEP INSIDE”, he is a songwriter alongside Terry Capitolin. On “Mantè”, Deezer credits him alone as composer of the D0 Production record.

The bundle tightens further on the records where the two names hold neighbouring functions. On “Asi Mwen”, in 2025, the Shazam credits list Clarisse Daryl as songwriter, mixing engineer and producer, and, in the same production section, D0 Production as producer. On “TOMBER LOVE” by Tiitii NBA, Deezer credits Terry Capitolin with the lyrics and Daryl Clarisse with the composition. On “AN MÉLÉ”, again by Tiitii NBA with Kevni, the composers are Daryl Clarisse and Terry Capitolin. In other words, every time a record carries the D0 Production banner, or circulates in its immediate orbit, the composition carries the name of Daryl Clarisse.

This profile draws from that bundle exactly the conclusion the sources allow, and not one more. Daryl Clarisse is the visible writing core of the D0 Production catalog: that is a fact of credits, repeated across at least eight opened pages. On the other hand, no opened source states in one sentence that D0 Production is Daryl Clarisse, nor that the banner reduces to that single person. A production banner can cover a studio, a team or a pseudonym, and this profile leaves the question open, by publishing the bundle. On the day a source names the exact relation, this page will say it with that source.

Around that core gravitates a second circle of recurring writers, which draws the workshop. Terry Capitolin, the writer name of Tiitii NBA, signs the lyrics of most records of the partnership. Terry Baptiste, the writer name of 1T1, co-signs the founding riddim of 2022. Carcheline Cereme writes and sings “Chien” and “Bouè'y Bay” for Totally Spice's, over compositions by Daryl Clarisse. The D0 Production workshop is therefore readable as a small stable team: one banner, one central composer, and the singer writers of the Gwada wave who come to lay their voices over its riddims.

2022: the founding riddims

The first verified trace of the name is a riddim. On January 1, 2022, “Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022”, by D0 Production featuring 1T1, is released on 2873529 Records DK, the digital distribution label that will carry most of the banner lead releases. The Shazam page gives a tempo of 114 BPM, clearly lower than the 160 BPM that will become the dance signature of the workshop, and credits two songwriters: Terry Baptiste and Daryl Noah Clarisse. From that very first page, the whole device is in place: the banner as lead artist, a guest voice of the Gwada wave, and the composer who will sign the whole catalog.

The format chosen to enter the scene is significant. A riddim, in bouyon culture as in the dancehall it inherits from, is an instrumental matrix built to carry several voices and to circulate among DJs. By releasing a dated riddim, “Bouyon Riddim 2022”, on the first day of the year, the banner presents itself as a supplier of material for the scene, before presenting itself as a poster name. Viberate also holds from the same year a second record of this type, “1MPLIKE'W (Bouyon Riddim 2022)”, ranked among the durable popular records of the banner.

The label 2873529 Records DK deserves a line of explanation, because it returns on almost every page of this profile. It is a DistroKid type distribution label, identified by a number, of the kind hundreds of independent artists in the French Antilles use to appear on every platform without a record company. Its constant presence on the lead releases of D0 Production, in 2022, 2023 and 2024, indicates an independent and continuous distribution, held by the same hand or the same team across three years.

From that founding year, the file therefore holds only two named records, and this profile does not inflate the count. But those two records are enough to date the banner, to name its composer and to fix its original format: the bouyon riddim signed in Les Abymes, ready for the voices of the wave that would break the following year. The context of that wave is read on our bouyon riddim page and on our bouyon Guadeloupe page.

2023: the platform year, from “New Bouyon Wave” to “Bouè'y Bay”

The year 2023 turns the banner into a platform. On September 29, 2023, “New Bouyon Wave #1” is released under the sole name of D0 Production, with five featured guests: Tiitii NBA, Nils, DJ Softee, Aknose and Theomaa. The record, in French, at 160 BPM, on 2873529 Records DK, works as a family photograph of the new French speaking bouyon wave: five voices of the same generation gathered on a single banner record. Its numbered title, “#1”, announces a series, even if no “#2” was found in the catalogs consulted, and this profile assumes nothing on that point.

The same summer, on July 28, 2023, Deezer documents “Mantè”, a D0 Production record featuring Shanika, running 2 minutes 30, on the same label, and composed by Daryl Noah Clarisse. The end of the year adds two guest collaborations: on December 21, 2023, “Décapotable”, by Coucoune Rangers featuring D0 Production, at 157 BPM, on 6298563 Records DK, written by Kélyan Pierre-Louis, Leeroy Lison, Léo Emery and Mathias Montabord; and on December 22, 2023, “KEEP INSIDE (BOUYON 2025)”, by Tiitii NBA, on 5003099 Records DK2, at 160 BPM, written by Terry Capitolin and Daryl Clarisse, whose page address carries the D0 Production feature mention.

The record of the year, though, is the one where the banner steps back behind a presentation. “Bouè'y Bay”, by Totally Spice's featuring Tiitii NBA, is released under the mention “D0 production Presents Totally Spice's”. On the Tiitii NBA artist page on Shazam, it is his second most identified record of all time, with about 34,700 identifications counted when the page was opened, behind “Kitty Kitty”. Deezer gives the record a running time of 2 minutes 05, lyrics by Carcheline Cereme and a composition by Daryl Clarisse: the same team as “Chien”, widened to the central male voice of the wave.

By the end of 2023, the banner has therefore occupied every position in the chain: lead artist of a riddim and of a collective record, presenter of the biggest record of a rising women group, guest with Coucoune Rangers, and shadow composer on the records of Tiitii NBA. That versatility is the mark of the production banners of the Gwada wave, which survive by serving the whole scene rather than a single artist. The profiles of the voices of that platform year are read on our Tiitii NBA, Totally Spice's and Shanika pages.

2024 and 2025: from “Chien” to “Asi Mwen”

The year 2024 opens with the most identified record of the direct orbit of the banner. On February 6, 2024, “Chien”, by Totally Spice's, is released on iMD-DRECORDS, at 162 BPM, with more than 25,000 Shazam identifications counted in the index. Its credits are a model of the device: Totally Spice's performing, Carcheline Cereme on vocals and lyrics, Daryl Clarisse on composition and programming, and D0 Production on production. The official video of the record also circulates under the title “D0Production Feat. Totally Spice's - Chien”, where the banner passes in front of the group, like a star producer name.

The rest of 2024 extends the central partnership. On August 13, 2024, DJ Softee releases “ALL NIGHT [BOUYON 2024]”, featuring Tiitii NBA and D0 Production, on 5183189 Records DK2, in French, at 160 BPM, written by Julian Axel White, Terry Capitolin and Daryl Clarisse. On September 6, 2024, D0 Production releases as lead artist “Sa Ka Pété”, with D-Stylii and Tiitii NBA, on its usual label, in French, at 160 BPM, written by Daryl Clarisse, Guenaël Diado and Terry Capitolin. Viberate ties to the same period the Tiitii NBA partnership records “TOMBER LOVE”, “Pli Ô” and “KPT - BOUYON WARM UP 2024”, as well as “Plap Plap !”, “Lang An Lang”, “Stand By”, “Restart ? - Bouyon Remix” and “Krédib” among the popular records of the banner.

For “TOMBER LOVE”, the writing credit is verifiable independently of Viberate: the Tiitii NBA discography on Deezer credits the record, running 2 minutes 27, with lyrics by Terry Capitolin and a composition by Daryl Clarisse. The composer of the banner therefore also signs, under his own name in the databases, the records Tiitii NBA releases under his sole name. That continuity explains why the analytics pages describe the partnership between D0 Production and Tiitii NBA as the most important of both catalogs: it is not a series of meetings, it is a followed production relation, from “Bouè'y Bay” to “Sa Ka Pété”.

The most recent trace opens the circle toward the English speaking credits of Dominica. On September 19, 2025, “Asi Mwen”, by D.O.C FIX DEM, is released on FIX DEM, in English, at 160 BPM, featuring Billy BYBF, D0 Production, LGC and Le_Daah. Its credits list Clarisse Daryl as songwriter, mixing engineer and producer, and D0 Production as producer. The same year, Viberate signals recent singles of the banner under the titles “DACHINE”, “BADMOOD”, “Konté #Oups 3” and “Take a Lick”, which this profile quotes as signalled by that single analytics page, with no opened release page for each one.

The method of the banner, and the limits of the file

Taken together, the credits of D0 Production draw a stable method across four years. A constant dance format, 160 BPM on almost every record from 2023 to 2025, with three documented exceptions: 114 BPM for the founding riddim of 2022, 157 BPM for “Décapotable” and 162 BPM for “Chien”. An independent distribution under numbered digital labels, dominated by 2873529 Records DK for the lead releases. A tight writing team, Daryl Clarisse on composition, Terry Capitolin and Terry Baptiste on the lyrics of the two central voices. And a circle of loyal performers, Tiitii NBA in front, Totally Spice's, Shanika, DJ Softee and the guests of the collective records.

That method is the method of the current economy of Guadeloupean bouyon: the production banner supplies the riddims and the sound signature, the singers supply the voices and the hooks, and the records circulate through platforms and mixes at uniform tempos that let DJs chain them together. In that system, the most durable name is not always the one that sings: it is the one that composes and produces for everyone. D0 Production, with a verified catalog running from January 2022 to September 2025 without interruption, occupies exactly that place in the Gwada wave.

The limits of the file are clear, and this profile states them. No official biography, no interview and no verified official account of the banner were confirmed during this research: the only artist page opened is a third party analytics page, carried as a source in the header of this file, and no social profile is linked in the structured data of this page. The Les Abymes origin rests on that single analytics page. The exact relation between D0 Production and Daryl Clarisse, its omnipresent composer, is stated by no opened source, and this profile publishes the bundle of credits without converting it into an identity. The figures quoted, Shazam identifications and Viberate weekly streams, are those counted at the time of research.

If the banner one day publishes an official presentation, a name or a verified account, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on this site. In the meantime, listening starts with “Bouè'y Bay” and “Chien” with Totally Spice's, continues with “New Bouyon Wave #1”, “Sa Ka Pété” and “ALL NIGHT” with Tiitii NBA, then with the riddims of 2022, and the context is read on our bouyon Guadeloupe and bouyon playlist pages.

Frequently asked questions

What is D0 Production?

D0 Production is a bouyon production name from Guadeloupe, placed in Les Abymes by the Viberate analytics page. It appears as lead artist on riddims and collective records, as presenter on “Bouè'y Bay” by Totally Spice's, and as credited producer on records such as “Chien”. Its verified catalog runs from January 2022 to September 2025.

Who is Daryl Clarisse in the D0 Production catalog?

Daryl Clarisse, also written Daryl Noah Clarisse or Clarisse Daryl, is the writer name that returns on almost every page of the catalog: composer of “Chien”, “Mantè” and “TOMBER LOVE”, co-writer of “Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022”, “Sa Ka Pété”, “ALL NIGHT” and “KEEP INSIDE”, and songwriter, mixing engineer and producer on “Asi Mwen”, where D0 Production is also credited as producer. No opened source explicitly states that D0 Production is Daryl Clarisse, and this profile publishes the bundle without asserting the identity.

What is the biggest record linked to D0 Production?

“Bouè'y Bay”, by Totally Spice's featuring Tiitii NBA, released under the mention “D0 production Presents Totally Spice's”. On the Tiitii NBA artist page on Shazam, it is his second most identified record of all time, with about 34,700 identifications counted when the page was opened. The record runs 2 minutes 05, with lyrics by Carcheline Cereme and a composition by Daryl Clarisse.

What are the lead name records of D0 Production?

The opened pages document “Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022”, with 1T1, on January 1, 2022, “Mantè”, with Shanika, on July 28, 2023, “New Bouyon Wave #1”, with Tiitii NBA, Nils, DJ Softee, Aknose and Theomaa, on September 29, 2023, and “Sa Ka Pété”, with D-Stylii and Tiitii NBA, on September 6, 2024. Most appear on 2873529 Records DK, at 160 BPM for the dance records.

Which artists does D0 Production work with most?

The central partnership is Tiitii NBA, from “Bouè'y Bay” to “Sa Ka Pété”, through “ALL NIGHT” by DJ Softee and the compositions signed Daryl Clarisse in the singer discography. Then come Totally Spice's, with “Chien” and “Bouè'y Bay”, then 1T1, Shanika, Nils, Aknose, Theomaa, Coucoune Rangers, and in 2025 D.O.C FIX DEM with Billy BYBF and LGC on “Asi Mwen”.

Where can I hear D0 Production?

Through “Bouè'y Bay” and “Chien” with Totally Spice's, “New Bouyon Wave #1” and “Sa Ka Pété” with Tiitii NBA, “ALL NIGHT” by DJ Softee, “Mantè” with Shanika, “Décapotable” by Coucoune Rangers and “Asi Mwen” by D.O.C FIX DEM.
