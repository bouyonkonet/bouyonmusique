Cut Eye Riddim: Drummixx Productions' 2026 bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Cut Eye Riddim is a Drummixx Productions project released on October 10, 2025 and promoted in January 2026 under the tag “Bouyon 2026”. Five voices take turns on it: Khallion with “What You Cheating For”, Ebony Empress with “Horn”, Jah Fyah with “Shots”, Motto with “J'ouvert” and Wassy K with “Vex”.

An October release for the 2026 season

Drummixx Productions distributed the riddim through DistroKid. On Deezer the album “CUT EYE RIDDIM” is dated October 10, 2025, with Drummixx productions as both main artist and label and the singers credited as features. It holds seven tracks: the five vocal songs, a radio edit of “What You Cheating For” and the instrumental.

The songs are short. The vocal versions run about two minutes, from 2:01 for “Horn” and “Shots” to 2:28 for “J'ouvert”, while the instrumental lasts 3:18. On the auto-generated YouTube page for the instrumental, the name Razor On The Track sits next to Drummixx; the role is not specified. The DjeasyPromos mix, for its part, plainly credits the production to Drummixx Productions.

January 2026: the riddim gets its push

The riddim stayed quiet for three months. Then on January 26, 2026, JulianspromosTV uploaded four songs in one day, each billed “(Cut Eye Riddim) | Bouyon 2026”: Motto, Jah Fyah, Khallion and Ebony Empress. Wassy K's “Vex” followed on January 28. On January 29, DjeasyPromos posted a riddim mix, “Cut Eye Riddim Mix (Bouyon 2026)”, with all five names in the title.

The uploads landed in late January, with carnival season approaching, when a song called “J'ouvert” makes the most sense. The numbers stayed small, between 1,300 and 1,800 views per song when read, with “Horn” and “Vex” in front. For how one instrumental gets shared across several voices, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Five voices, several islands

The cast spans several islands. Khallion, from Dominica, opens the album with “What You Cheating For”, the only song with a radio edit. Ebony Empress, born in Lagoon near Portsmouth in Dominica and based in Saint Martin, sings “Horn”. Motto, the Saint Lucian Dennery Segment producer based in New York, voices “J'ouvert”, a nod to the opening of carnival.

Jah Fyah (“Shots”) and Wassy K (“Vex”) complete the list; the sources consulted do not say where they are from. “Shots” and “What You Cheating For” carry the explicit tag on YouTube. No official music video turned up among the pages consulted: the Cut Eye Riddim travels through audio uploads and DJ mixes.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Cut Eye Riddim?

Drummixx Productions, the main artist and label on Deezer, credited as producer in the DjeasyPromos mix; the name Razor On The Track also appears on the instrumental's page, with no role given.

When was the Cut Eye Riddim released?

On October 10, 2025 according to Deezer and the DistroKid-generated YouTube pages; the “Bouyon 2026” promotion came in late January 2026.

Which songs are on the Cut Eye Riddim?

What You Cheating For (Khallion), Horn (Ebony Empress), Shots (Jah Fyah), J'ouvert (Motto) and Vex (Wassy K), plus a radio edit of What You Cheating For and the instrumental.

Is the Cut Eye Riddim bouyon?

Yes: the JulianspromosTV pages and the DjeasyPromos mix all present it as “Bouyon 2026”.

Why are the songs so short?

The vocal versions last about two minutes on Deezer, against 3:18 for the instrumental; the sources consulted do not explain the choice.
