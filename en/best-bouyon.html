Frequently asked questions

Who are the best bouyon artists?

For Bouyon Shattaz, three names make up the essentials: WCK, the Roseau band that created bouyon in 1988, Asa Bantan from Grand Bay, identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals and widely nicknamed the King of Bouyon, and Reo, presented as the Prince of Bouyon of the new Dominican generation. See all bouyon artists.

Which bouyon styles should I know?

Four styles are enough to start: classic bouyon, born from WCK's fusion of cadence-lypso and jing ping, bouyon soca, the fusion with Trinidad soca, bouyon gwada, the hardcore branch born between Dominica and Guadeloupe, and shatta bouyon, its modern form with short, hard riddims built for clubs and social media.

What are the key places of bouyon?

Roseau, the capital of Dominica, for the origin: that is where WCK created bouyon in 1988 and where the Mas Domnik carnival takes place. Pointe-à-Pitre and Fort-de-France for the spread, in Guadeloupe and Martinique, where audiences call it jump up. And Paris for the market, where certifications, stages and distribution happen.

What is the best bouyon song of 2026?

According to the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, it is "Bouwéy" by 1T1 and Theomaa, crowned bouyon song of the year at the fourth edition, on September 19 in Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago. The same results give bouyon artist of the year to Mr. Ridge. "Bouwéy" comes from 1T1's EP CHORD, released on December 13, 2024.

Who is called the king of bouyon?

Asa Bantan, real name Asa Edwards, is widely nicknamed the King of Bouyon: it is an attributed, subjective nickname, and Dominica Festivals also identified him as the Bouyon Boss. Other titles circulate on the scene: Reo is presented as the Prince of Bouyon by 13th Street Promotions, and Ebony Empress presents herself as The Goddess of Bouyon.

Who are the best bouyon producers?

The Dominica Music Awards 2025 named six finalists in the Producer of the Year category: Arade "Maskman" Moses, Tha Wizzard, Andel "Fre3ze" Horsford, DJ Taffy (Riddim Beast), Litle Boy and Cross D Bridge Studio. On the remix side, this site's reference is Smokiller, who turns afro, pop and rap hits into bouyon. See the bouyon producers.

Who is the best bouyon DJ?

At the Dominica Music Awards, DJ MJ was named DJ of the Year at the second edition, on Saturday January 3, 2026, among finalists DJ MJ, DJ Sleem and Unstoppable Shax. A bouyon DJ from Dominica, often paired with keyboardist MV on the Keys, he plays as a duo with him on Night 1 of WCMF 2026. See DJ MJ.

What are the best bouyon bands?

The founding bands first: WCK, creators of the genre in Roseau in 1988, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade. WCK and First Serenade were still on the World Creole Music Festival bill in 2025. Today, Signal Band carries live bouyon with Shelly, and the band is booked for Night 3 of WCMF 2026, on Sunday October 25.
