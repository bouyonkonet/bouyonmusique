The best of bouyon, according to Bouyon Shattaz

If you were to keep only one gateway into bouyon, here it is: the essential artists, the styles that matter and the playlist that sums up the genre, from 1988 to 2026.

The essentials

The artists: WCK the creators, Asa Bantan the Bouyon Boss, Reo the Prince of the new generation. The styles: classic bouyon, bouyon soca, bouyon gwada and shatta bouyon. The places: Roseau for the origin, Pointe-à-Pitre and Fort-de-France for the spread, Paris for the market.
