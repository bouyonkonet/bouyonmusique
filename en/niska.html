Niska: when French rap meets a bouyon gold record

By Smokiller · Published October 1, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Niska is a French language rap artist, credited on 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' alongside Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS. The track passed 15,000,000 equivalent streams in France and was certified Gold Single by SNEP in 2026, according to SNEP's certification post. For Bouyon Shattaz, this record matters for one precise reason: it brings together a major French rap name, a Guadeloupean singer in full breakthrough and a beatmaker whose name carries 767, Dominica's area code, on a bouyon track that crosses a French gold threshold. This page stays focused on that track and what it proves for bouyon.

The credit that places Niska in this site's bouyon story

Niska does not appear on bouyonmusique.com as the subject of a general French rap biography. He appears for one precise, verified fact: his credit on 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou', a bouyon track certified Gold Single in France in 2026. That framing is deliberate. A site specialised in bouyon must say why a name from outside the genre enters its pages, and the answer should fit in one sourced sentence, not in vague admiration.

His presence on the track changes the record's reach. When a leading French language rap artist puts his name and voice on a bouyon track, he opens the song to a public that does not follow Dominican carnivals or Guadeloupean scenes day to day. That public discovers the rhythm through a voice it already knows. It is a classic genre crossing mechanism, and here it works at large scale, with official figures to support it.

In return, the track gives Niska a place in a story that is not his at the start: the story of bouyon, born in Dominica, carried by the Antilles and now certified in France. This page attributes to him no origin and no backstory that our dossier does not contain. It records the meeting at the exact point where it is documented, in the credits of a gold record, between his name, Miimii KDS's name and Litleboy LSBeats767's name.

'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou': 15 million and the Gold Single

The facts, per SNEP's certification post: 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' passed 15,000,000 equivalent streams in France and received Gold Single certification in 2026. SNEP is the reference body for French music certifications. Its post is therefore worth more, for establishing this threshold, than any counter passed from screenshot to screenshot. When Bouyon Shattaz cites this figure, it cites the certification, with its body and its year.

The song's double title, 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou', already signals its double public: a hook energy made for the sound systems of bouyon parties, and a catchphrase that can live on mainstream French platforms. Tracks that cross two worlds like this are rare, because they must stay credible on both sides. This one did better than cross: it was counted, certified and archived by the French market as a gold success.

Bouyon Shattaz covered the certification in October 2026 with a dedicated gold scheme reel. For a bouyon media outlet, celebrating a SNEP Gold Single is not celebrating three artists alone: it marks the moment the genre gains, in the largest Francophone market, an official line that promoters, brands and media can no longer treat as a party rumour. Niska is one of the names written on that line.

What the record proves for bouyon

'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' is one of the clearest proofs that bouyon sells inside the French market. Not only that it is danced to there, not only that it circulates there on social networks, but that it reaches the commercial thresholds the French industry uses for all its genres. That distinction sits at the centre of the Bouyon Shattaz strategy: documenting bouyon with the market's own measuring tools, so Dominica and the Antilles can negotiate with proof, not impressions.

The credits' composition sums up the genre's road. Miimii KDS brings the Guadeloupean breakthrough, a voice from the French Antilles where bouyon and shatta live daily. Litleboy LSBeats767 carries the Dominican 767 in his name, the area code of the island that created bouyon. Niska brings the scale of French rap. Remove any one of the three and the record no longer tells the same story: it is precisely a junction track, between the origin, the Antilles and the mainland French market.

For rising bouyon artists, this track serves as a concrete benchmark. It shows that a well built collaboration can carry the Dominican rhythm to a French certification without disowning it. It also shows that credit names matter over time: in ten years, when the 2026 Gold Singles list is read again, the three names in the credits are what history will keep. Writing them correctly today, with the SNEP source, is archive work as much as promotion.

This profile claims neither to summarise Niska's career nor to rank this track inside his full discography, which our dossier does not cover. It does something else, which is this site's job: recording, with its sources, the moment a major French language rap artist crosses bouyon on a certified success. If further verified facts connect Niska to bouyon in the future, they will find their place here. Until then, the Gold Single for 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' amply justifies his page.

And for readers who discovered bouyon through this track, the lesson is simple: follow the credits. Behind the voice you know, there is a Guadeloupean singer breaking through, a beatmaker signing with Dominica's area code and a whole genre that was waiting for its French gold proof. Niska is this record's best known doorway, but the door opens onto Dominica and the Antilles. That is exactly why Bouyon Shattaz covers it.
