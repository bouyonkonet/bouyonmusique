Ezee WMN: Love Me Or Not and Make She Sing, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Ezee WMN, also written EZEE WMN in the listings, enters this site through two 2025 titles whose YouTube pages carry the word bouyon in their titles: “Love Me Or Not”, a duet with CbgYvngsta, and “Make She Sing”, a three way track with CbgYvngsta and Gwada G. Both are produced by Shako Production and released under CbgYvngsta’s reference. Around those two titles sit a riddim posted on the artist’s own channel in October 2026 and a few older collaborations with no bouyon label. No opened page names an origin, so this profile assigns none.

Two bouyon titles from 2025 with CbgYvngsta

The first verified title is “Love Me Or Not”. The official audio, posted on July 11, 2025 on the channel of beatmaker Jackie Beatz, is titled “Love Me Or Not (Official Audio) (Bouyon 2025)” and runs 2 minutes 27. The description credits the artists CbgYvngsta and Ezee WMN and the producer Shako Production. The distributed listing from Amuseio AB, released the same day, fills in the rest: both voices are credited for rap and as composers, and mixing and mastering go to Waco Moyenga.

The second title is “Make She Sing”, subtitled “LaLaLaLaLa” in the visualizer version posted on September 14, 2025, also on the Jackie Beatz channel, with “(Bouyon 2025)” in its title. The track brings together CbgYvngsta, Ezee WMN and Gwada G. Shako Production is credited for production and keys, recording for Yeah Boss Studios and XavvoKnockin, and mixing and mastering for Waco Moyenga and Xavvoknockin. The official listing, released on September 12, 2025, names three composers: Kurt Gwada G Douglas, Stevens Macathley Jeanfort and Eleni Constandinou. It does not say which name belongs to which artist, and this profile does not guess.

Ezee WMN’s channel and the other titles

Ezee WMN also releases under that name alone. On October 19, 2026 the artist’s YouTube channel posted “JUMBIE RIDDIM”, with Multiii and C4, running 2 minutes 44. The description thanks listeners for their support and lists the team: riddim by Multii_production and WMN, keys by C4everhumble, mixing, mastering and recording by Waco_moyenga and Dj boops, visualizer by HookMe971. That page states no genre: this site cites it as a recent release, not as a bouyon title.

Two older collaborations round out the picture, again with no bouyon label. “STEP REFIX”, with Ricoblvck, was posted on September 23, 2023 by Poppalox Entertainment, a promotion channel that files it under soca and presents it as a GNS X WMN collab. “Super Hot”, by Sk2official featuring Ezee WMN, dates from December 10, 2022. The thread running through these pages is the same name, WMN, found in the artist name, in a riddim credit and in the collaboration note.

What the file does not say

None of the opened pages gives an island, a town or a country for Ezee WMN, and none publishes a biography. The only public markers are an Instagram account, ezee_wmn, cited in the descriptions, and a YouTube channel. The titles released on the Jackie Beatz channel, with producers and engineers who recur across current bouyon, clearly place the artist in that circuit, but a circuit is not an origin.

To hear these titles in context, the site’s bouyon playlist and the tracking of new bouyon in 2026 are good starting points. This profile will be expanded if a primary source states the artist’s origin or career.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ezee WMN?

Ezee WMN is a vocal artist credited for rap and as a composer on “Love Me Or Not” with CbgYvngsta, and featured on “Make She Sing” with CbgYvngsta and Gwada G, two titles billed as bouyon 2025. No public biography was found.

Which Ezee WMN bouyon titles are verified?

Two: “Love Me Or Not (Bouyon 2025)”, released on July 11, 2025, and “Make She Sing” (Bouyon 2025), released on September 12, 2025, both produced by Shako Production.

Who produced those titles?

Shako Production, with Waco Moyenga on mixing and mastering, and Xavvoknockin credited on recording and mastering for “Make She Sing”.

Is JUMBIE RIDDIM a bouyon title?

Its page does not say so. This 2026 title, posted on Ezee WMN’s channel with Multiii and C4, carries no genre label, so this site does not file it as bouyon.

Where is Ezee WMN from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns the artist no origin.
