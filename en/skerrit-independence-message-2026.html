Bouyon music: Skerrit independence message 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026

On the eve of the big day, the Prime Minister addressed the country. In his independence message, published this Monday 2 November 2026, Roosevelt Skerrit told Dominicans they must reflect honestly on the work of nation building, per the CANA agency (Caribbean Media Corporation).

Reflect honestly, on the eve of 48 years

Dominica celebrates 48 years of political independence from Britain on Tuesday 3 November 2026. Roosevelt Skerrit, head of government since 8 January 2004, said independence calls on every generation to build on the achievements of those who came before, respond to the challenges of its time and prepare the way for those who will follow, per CANA.

His central line, quoted by CANA: it requires us to ask what we can each contribute, how we can strengthen our communities, and what kind of country we want to leave our children.

The everyday acts that build a country

The message goes down to the everyday level. We strengthen Dominica, Skerrit said per CANA, when we support a local farmer or small business, guide a young person, care for an elderly neighbour, protect our environment or carry out our duties with honesty and pride.

He also thanked citizens living overseas for the love, support and commitment they continue to show their homeland: wherever they reside, they remain part of Dominica s story and its future, per CANA.

The celebration lives in the music and the traditional dress

For a music site, one passage counts double. Independence, Skerrit said per CANA, lives in the pride we feel when our flag is raised, in the sound of our music and the beauty of our traditional dress, in our Kweyol language, our food and the customs passed from one generation to the next.

He encouraged Dominicans to cherish these traditions and give children every opportunity to know and love them, saluting the essential role of cultural practitioners, elders, teachers and families who keep this inheritance alive. The 2026 season runs under the theme Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future.

The course: towards fifty years

The Prime Minister recalled the government s responsibility: that national development brings meaningful improvements to people s lives, better infrastructure, investment in education, stronger healthcare and economic opportunities, per CANA.

His closing line looks ahead: as we look towards fifty years of independence, move forward with confidence, a clear sense of responsibility and respect for one another. The national ceremony on Tuesday 3 November, at 5:30 pm at Windsor Park, with national honours for this year s seventeen citizens, gives the season its official close, before the National Day of Community Service on 4 November.

What did Roosevelt Skerrit say in his 2026 independence message?

He told Dominicans they must reflect honestly on the work of nation building, in a message published on Monday 2 November 2026 and reported by the CANA agency, on the eve of 48 years of independence.

What does the message say about music and culture?

That independence lives in the pride of the flag being raised, in the sound of Dominica s music, the beauty of its traditional dress, the Kweyol language, the food and the customs passed down, per CANA.

When is the 2026 national ceremony?

On Tuesday 3 November 2026 at 5:30 pm, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, with national honours presented to this year s seventeen honourees.
