Bouyon in France: the genre’s big market

Published September 9, 2026

France has become the big economic market for bouyon: it is there that tracks get certified, that distribution is being structured and that a large part of Antillean fans live. This reality gives France enormous power over a genre born in Dominica.

Certifications and industry

Bouyon tracks have won gold single certifications in France, proof that the genre has grown beyond its historic Antillean audience. French distribution and publishing structures play a central role, from Believe and TuneCore to the independent labels. This is exactly the ground where Bouyon Shattaz is building its distribution network, keeping the Dominican origin at the center.

The diaspora as audience

Paris and France’s big cities concentrate a Guadeloupean, Martinican, Guyanese and Dominican diaspora that keeps bouyon nights and the networks alive. For this audience, bouyon is a direct link to home.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
