Backside Riddim: Khaos Entertainment's bouyon riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Backside Riddim is a riddim produced and released by Khaos Entertainment on December 12, 2025. Its four vocal tracks came out that day on Deezer and YouTube, each title marked “Bouyon 2026”. The voices are King Tipsy, SiahOTB, Nefta Kojo and Raw Nitro.

Four voices on one day

On Deezer, the “Backside Riddim” album is dated December 12, 2025, on the Khaos Entertainment label, which is also listed as the main artist. It has four tracks and no instrumental. Three of them run exactly 2:18; Raw Nitro's “Cut & Clear” is much shorter at 1:41.

The same day, the Khaos Entertainment YouTube channel posted all four videos, each titled “Backside Riddim | Bouyon 2026”. The descriptions are bare: the performer, the song and a 2025 copyright line for Khaos Entertainment. No individual producer is named.

King Tipsy, SiahOTB, Nefta Kojo and Raw Nitro

The fullest credits come from King Tipsy, who put a lyric video of “Bumpa Back” on his own channel. Its description says the song is performed on the Backside Riddim by Khaos Entertainment, files it as “2026 Soca/Bouyon” and credits Alvin Bonas as writer. It has nothing to do with “Tipsy”, the song by MadameZelle.

The other three are SiahOTB with “Feeling It”, Nefta Kojo with “Live Yuh Life” and Raw Nitro with “Cut & Clear”. On YouTube that last one is shared with Don Gana, a name Deezer leaves out. The audience is still small: on October 28, 2026, “Live Yuh Life” led with 176 views on the label channel, and King Tipsy's lyric video had 276.

Bouyon, soca or both?

Every official title calls the riddim bouyon, but several of its singers work elsewhere too. Raw Nitro posts “Cut & Clear” on his own channel as “SOCA 2026”, Nefta Kojo has released calypso, and King Tipsy writes “Soca/Bouyon” side by side. Deezer files the album under reggae and dancehall, an automatic tag that says little about the sound.

It is a common pattern: a riddim built for bouyon, voiced by artists who also move in soca. Our bouyon vs soca page explains what sets the two apart, and the bouyon riddim page covers how one instrumental gets shared between several singers.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Backside Riddim?

Khaos Entertainment, credited for production on King Tipsy's lyric video and holding the rights on all four videos. No individual producer is named in the pages we checked.

When did the Backside Riddim come out?

On December 12, 2025, on both Deezer and the Khaos Entertainment YouTube channel.

Which artists are on the Backside Riddim?

King Tipsy with “Bumpa Back”, SiahOTB with “Feeling It”, Nefta Kojo with “Live Yuh Life” and Raw Nitro, joined by Don Gana on YouTube, with “Cut & Clear”.

Where can I listen to the Backside Riddim?

On the Khaos Entertainment “Backside Riddim” album on Deezer and on the label's YouTube channel, which carries all four songs. King Tipsy also has a lyric video of “Bumpa Back” on his own channel.

Is the Backside Riddim bouyon?

Yes by its official titles, all marked “Bouyon 2026”. King Tipsy describes it as “Soca/Bouyon”, and Raw Nitro presents his version as soca.
