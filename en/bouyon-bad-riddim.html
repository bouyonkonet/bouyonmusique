Bouyon Bad Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

The Bouyon Bad Riddim is a bouyon riddim composed by Punz, the DJ and producer behind Punz Entertainment, also credited as DJ Pun or DeeJay Punz. The first video, “Feel It” with Nessa Preppy, came out on January 18, 2025, and the project ran across the whole year up to “Push and Go” by Safo and Shanika, posted on December 19. Every song carries Punz's name next to the singer's.

A Punz project, spread over all of 2025

The video descriptions all point to the same “project”: “Off the 'Bouyon Bad Riddim' project” sits under “Feel It”, “Pokey” and “Malas”. The “Push and Go” credits are blunter still and file the composition under “BOUYON BAD RIDDIM | Punz”. The producer's name shifts from page to page, Dj Pun, Dj Punz, DeeJay Punz, but it is the same man, and the “Pokey” page puts the rights with Punz Entertainment.

The release pattern is unusual for a riddim. There was no batch drop: “Feel It” opened on January 18, 2025, “Pokey” followed on January 26 and “Helicopter” on February 16, then the riddim went quiet until “Malas” on October 5 and “Push and Go” on December 19. No album collecting the songs turned up on Deezer, and no public instrumental appears on the Punz channel.

Who sings on the Bouyon Bad Riddim

Nessa Preppy leads off with “Feel It”, the riddim's flagship, past 342,000 views when read on October 29, 2026. Mafi follows with “Pokey”, whose page lists Trinidad, Guyana and Jamaica in its tags and is past 83,000 views. Klassik Frescobar delivers “Helicopter”, billed “Bouyon Soca 2025”, with a download link for DJs.

Maaya Real arrives in the autumn with “Malas”, the only song from the riddim posted on the Punz Entertainment channel itself. The last one in comes from the French-speaking scene: “Push and Go” by Safo and Shanika was recorded, mixed and mastered at HYT Studio, edited by Voodoo Studio, and is past 226,000 views. That is the second biggest figure on the project, behind Nessa Preppy.

Bouyon first, from a trinibad producer

On his own channel, Punz mostly puts out trinibad and dancehall: Steelpan Badness Riddim, Bad Santa Riddim, TriniBad Riddim. The Bouyon Bad Riddim breaks from that catalogue, and its name says so. The video titles read “Bouyon Bad Riddim”, the “Push and Go” tags open with #BOUYON, and “Helicopter” is filed under “Bouyon Soca 2025”.

The riddim has also travelled as a mix. A compilation titled “Bouyon Bad Riddim Mix! 2.0” was posted on YouTube by the Echo Chamber channel, and Punz has his own “Bouyon Mix Vol. 01” with Selectah Renzo, covered on our Punz page. For how the format works, see the page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Bouyon Bad Riddim?

Punz, also credited as Dj Pun or DeeJay Punz, of Punz Entertainment. The “Push and Go” credits read “Composition : BOUYON BAD RIDDIM | Punz”.

When did the Bouyon Bad Riddim come out?

The first video, “Feel It” by Nessa Preppy, has been up since January 18, 2025. The other songs came out one at a time, up to December 19, 2025.

Which artists are on the Bouyon Bad Riddim?

Nessa Preppy, Mafi, Klassik Frescobar, Maaya Real, then Safo and Shanika, each time alongside Punz.

What is the most watched song on the riddim?

“Feel It” by Nessa Preppy, past 342,000 views in October 2026, ahead of “Push and Go” by Safo and Shanika at over 226,000.

Is the Bouyon Bad Riddim bouyon?

Yes. The word is in the riddim's own name, and the video pages show it, with “Bouyon Soca 2025” for Klassik Frescobar and #BOUYON for Safo and Shanika.
