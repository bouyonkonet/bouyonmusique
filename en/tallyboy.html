Tallyboy: Saint Lucia on the sound map

Published October 12, 2026

Tallyboy is a recording artist and songwriter from Saint Lucia, in the eastern Caribbean. The 758 in his Instagram handle, @tallyboypondmic_758, is Saint Lucia's area code, and for him it works as a signature: the island is inside the very name the public follows him under. At the October 17, 2026 check, he had 17,846 followers on Instagram. His bio points to a second account, @tallyboymkg_758, and to a YouTube channel, with MKG 4L in the bio. This page presents him with only the verified facts from our dossiers, and it says so clearly when information is missing: the title of the track in his October 2026 reel, its credits and the exact nature of the 50k milestone he celebrates are not verified in our records. Rather than inventing those elements, the page tells the moment as the artist himself presented it, and explains why Bouyon Shattaz follows him as one of the lead names on the Saint Lucia side of the sound, a direct neighbor of bouyon.

Saint Lucia, the 758 and MKG

The first verified fact about Tallyboy is geographic, and it is written into his public identity: Saint Lucia, area code 758. Across the Caribbean, those area codes have become belonging codes that artists display like flags, and his points the public toward @tallyboymkg_758 and toward his YouTube channel. The line MKG 4L appears in his bio, reproduced here as written, without development our dossiers do not confirm.

Saint Lucia has its own scene, the Dennery Segment. Our Real Reel profile describes it as the Saint Lucia soca sound born in the town of Dennery, in the Mabouya Valley, built on rhythms drawn from Angolan kuduro, zouk and Lucian percussion, with lyrics in Creole. It is a Saint Lucian cousin of bouyon, not a branch of the Dominican genre. Our records classify none of Tallyboy's tracks in a specific genre, so the page places the artist in that island landscape without labelling his music.

Five tracks on file, 2024 to 2025

Our sources log lists five recordings by Tallyboy, verified on their Shazam listings. Two are singles under his name: D Knock, released in 2024, and Blah Blah Blah, released in 2025. They are the two tracks where he is sole headliner, and the most direct ways into his voice.

The other three go through riddims. Our bouyon riddim page describes the riddim as the instrumental rhythm several artists lay their voices on, and those projects often carry a name. Hit & Run appears on Frass Man Riddim, a 2024 EP. Pop & Drop, where Tallyboy is credited as a featured artist with Dej, appears on Cout Tchui Riddim, in 2025. More Long, credited as featuring “Tallyboy (MKG)”, is on Caralax Riddim, a 2025 single.

That last credit matters: the MKG tag next to his name on a platform listing matches the MKG 4L in his bio and his second account, @tallyboymkg_758. It is a cross-check through the credits that ties that track to the same artist as the Instagram account. Our records, however, give neither the lead artist of More Long and Pop & Drop, nor the producers of those riddims, nor their genre, so the page does not attribute them.

The October 15, 2026 reel and the 50k milestone

The moment behind this page is dated: a reel posted on October 15, 2026, which showed 3,591 likes and 77 comments at the check. Its caption was a few signs long: Feh ahh, followed by a Saint Lucia flag and the line 50k and counting, tagging @mikadoo972, 972 being Martinique's code.

The title of the track carried by that reel is not verified in our dossiers, nor are its credits, nor what exactly the 50k counts. The page therefore presents that moment as the artist framed it: a public celebration in Saint Lucia colours, with a nod toward Martinique, which draws in one caption the real circulation of these musics between neighbouring islands.

Tally Boy on the World Creole Music Festival 2026 bill

A World Creole Music Festival guide published by writeups24 lists a Tally Boy, spelled in two words as printed, among Saint Lucia's DYP Band features for the festival. According to the writeups24 and Sweet TNT guides cited on our Edday page, Night 2, on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, brings together Ne-Yo, Alkaline, Asa Bantan, Reo, Trilla G, Edday, DYP and Tally Boy, among others.

Sweet TNT presents that Night 2 as the night with the broadest crossover of the festival, combining international R and B, dancehall, and Haitian, Grenadian, Trinidadian, Saint Lucian, Guadeloupean and Dominican sounds. The World Creole Music Festival is Dominica's great Creole stage, where bouyon is at home. The name and the island match Tallyboy, but the guides' spelling differs and no other source confirms the match, so the page reports the announced bill with its spelling, and does not describe a performance that has not yet taken place.

Tallyboy, not to be confused with Tolly Boys

A neighbouring name circulates in bouyon and invites confusion. Our Bernard Gumbs (Wizito) profile documents YMW Tolly Boys, with an o, the label published on the Wizito records and the circle of Tolly Boy’s YMW, around Youry Fleming. Tallyboy, with an a, is another artist, from another island, with another catalogue. The five tracks above are those of Tallyboy from Saint Lucia, and no record from the Tolly Boys circle is attached to this page.

Between Dennery, bouyon soca and the Creole stage

Saint Lucia is the Creole-speaking island closest to Dominica. Our bouyon in Saint Lucia page recalls that bouyon soca was launched in Dominica by Dada in 2003, according to the first-hand account of Sean “Smokiller” Francis, and that Saint Lucia became one of the islands where the fusion grew the most. The audiences of Roseau and Castries share language and a taste for partying, and tracks travel from one carnival to the other.

DJs play those branches together: our Kenny G profile cites DJ Sargent Unstoppable's Carnival 2025 mix ROAD MONSTER P.1, which lines up soca, Dennery Segment and bouyon. That circulation is why Tallyboy matters to a site devoted to bouyon: a Saint Lucia voice, tracks on named riddims, and an announced place on the Roseau stage.

For listening, start with D Knock and Blah Blah Blah for his voice as headliner, then Hit & Run, Pop & Drop and More Long for his riddim work.
