Tallyboy: Whoopty Doo with Gwada G (Bouyon 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Tallyboy is a singer of Saint Lucia's Dennery Segment scene, published by the MKG collective and mixed by Ransum Records. In 2026 he crossed into bouyon with two titles billed as such: “Whoopty Doo”, with Dominica's Gwada G on a riddim by Joshii G, and “No Kiss & Tell”, with Dhirv2Funny. The same year, his Dennery titles “Uh T Tak” and the “Bum Bum Whistle” remix were past 300,000 and 160,000 views respectively.

Whoopty Doo with Gwada G: the Saint Lucia-Dominica bridge

On January 7, 2026, the MKG official channel uploaded “Tallyboy x Gwada G - Whoopty Doo (Bouyon 2026)”. The page credits writing to Tallyboy and Gwada G, the riddim to Joshii G, mixing and mastering to Ransum Records, and gives the singer's Instagram handle, Tallyboypondmic_758. On Deezer, “Whoopty Doo (feat. Tallyboy (MKG) & Gwada G)” was released on January 1, 2026 under Joshii G's name, label Run Tha Beat Records.

Gwada G, whom the owner of this site places in Dominica, gives the title its bouyon anchor. On April 30, 2026 came “Dhirv2Funny ft Tallyboy - No Kiss & Tell (Bouyon) 2026”, whose page states “Genre: Bouyon”, riddim and mix by Ransum Records; the single is dated May 5, 2026 on Deezer, label Ransum Recordz / Riddimstream. For the traffic between the two genres, see our page on bouyon in Saint Lucia.

Uh T Tak and Bum Bum Whistle: the 2026 Dennery year

On February 20, 2026, MKG official published “Tallyboy x Samo - Uh T Tak (Pawa Pee Riddim) Prod. By Mikado Dennery Segment 2026”, more than 307,000 views when read, mixed by Ransum Records. On July 10, “Bum Bum Whistle Remix (Dennery Segment 2026)”, billed Tallyboy x Sedale x Prod Bozo x SweetBread and tagged #StLucia, was past 163,000 views; the page credits production to Prod Bozo (Bad Sound), recording to Ransum Records and the mix to Yannick Plante of Minor Productionz.

On Deezer, “Bum Bum Whistle” is dated May 18, 2026, label Bad Sound, with Tallyboy, Sedale and Prod Bozo as contributors. “Vivre La Vie”, with Jasse on Teamfoxx's By A Bar Riddim, followed on August 7, 2026. These titles place Tallyboy at the centre of the 2026 Dennery season, in the same circuit as Motto.

From Drop It Low to the Piton Riddim: the trail since 2022

Deezer keeps two pages under his name: Tallyboy, twelve releases from “Drop It Low” (May 31, 2022) to “By Patat” (September 19, 2026), with “Dog Life” (2023), “No Boss” and “Blah Blah Blah” (2025), the Benzel Riddim (EP, 2025); and Tallyboy (MKG), five releases from 2024 to 2026, including “Bap” (March 15, 2024, label MKG, producer Laurent Yohan Music, lyrics credited to Ryal Prospere) and “Mother In-Law” (September 9, 2026).

On June 16, 2023, DJ Kalli's channel had already published “Tallyboy, Leonus & DJ Kalli - Give Her (Piton Riddim) [Dennery Segment 2023]”: the producer of “Eye Spy” and the singer of “Whoopty Doo” crossed paths three years before their bouyon titles. This profile is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026; none states his birthplace, and this site places him in the Saint Lucian scene his billings name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Tallyboy?

A singer of Saint Lucia's Dennery Segment scene, published by the MKG collective and mixed by Ransum Records, maker of Whoopty Doo with Gwada G and No Kiss & Tell with Dhirv2Funny, billed Bouyon 2026.

What are his bouyon titles?

Whoopty Doo with Gwada G (January 7, 2026, riddim by Joshii G) and No Kiss & Tell with Dhirv2Funny (April 30, 2026, Genre: Bouyon, Ransum Records).

What is his most viewed title?

Uh T Tak (Pawa Pee Riddim), with Samo, billed Dennery Segment 2026, more than 307,000 views on October 27, 2026; the Bum Bum Whistle remix had more than 163,000.

What is MKG?

The collective and label publishing his titles: the MKG official YouTube channel and the Deezer page Tallyboy (MKG), including Bap (2024).

Where is Tallyboy from?

No opened page spells it out. His billings are Dennery Segment and #StLucia, and his handle carries 758; this profile places him in the Saint Lucian scene without stating a birthplace.
