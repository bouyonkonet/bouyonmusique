Here for You: Rudy Ru$h puts romance on bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 31, 2026

On 9 September 2026, from New York, Dominica-born artist Rudy Ru$h released the official music video for his single Here for You, per his release distributed by EIN Presswire. The record presents itself as a vibrant track bringing romance and bouyon energy to the dance floor, inspired by real life relationships.

The credits of the song

The release gives the credits plainly: Here for You was written by Rudy Ru$h, Yasmine and Chioneso, during a collaborative studio session, with production by Ridge.

13th Street Promotions, in its coverage of Dominican music, adds that the song features Brooklyn based recording artist Chioneso, and that the music video was directed by ReemGrafix.

A claimed inspiration

During the writing, Rudy and his collaborators say they drew on the playful dancefloor energy of Sisqo's classic Thong Song, reimagining it through a modern bouyon and crossover lens. That lineage is the release's own, and it is carried here as theirs.

Rudy describes the song, again per the release, as the meeting point between the high energy bouyon sound of his island roots and the crossover influences of his American experience. The single streams on Spotify and Apple Music.

The video and the stage

The video was created in collaboration with MultiArtistry Entertainment, which cast the featured dancers. The release underlines the aim of showcasing Caribbean women of different body types, heights and complexions, celebrating their confidence and natural beauty.

The same release says that over Labor Day weekend Rudy performed the song at several venues, where patrons were wining and jumping in celebration. That is a release account, carried as such, but it matches what the song is built for: a love song proved on the dance floor.

Where the song sits in his catalogue

The release places Here for You in a catalogue that already includes Bouyon Fete, Anywhere We Go and Exodus, featuring reggae legend Junior Reid in the release's own presentation.

On this site, the Rudy Ru$h profile traces his path as a Dominica-born, US based artist. Here for You takes its place there as the 2026 song where his crossover bouyon becomes a couple's song: two people dancing together and reassuring one another that they are there for each other.

When did the Here for You video come out?

Per the release, a romance inspired by real life relationships: two people dancing together and reassuring one another, over a bouyon and Caribbean groove.
