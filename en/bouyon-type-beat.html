Bouyon type beat: the formula of the bouyon beat

Published October 10, 2026

A bouyon type beat is an instrumental built on the codes of bouyon: fast tempo, a recognizable keyboard loop, percussion inspired by Dominica and a bass that pushes. Beatmakers look for it to place bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals.

The structure that works

The bouyon type beat starts fast: little intro, the main loop arrives early, the drums follow the carnival pattern with regular build-ups. The vocal has to be able to shout, sing and get the crowd to respond. The energy almost never comes back down: it is a party beat, not a lounge beat.

Stay faithful to the origin

A good type beat cites its sources without copying them: a percussion that recalls the lapo kabwit, an accordion or keyboard melody in the jing ping way, and Creole in the background vocals. For the full method, see how to make a bouyon beat.

Frequently asked questions

Who produces bouyon today?

Producers like Smokiller (Bouyon Shattaz), Denmath, HYPNO! and Savion work the bouyon sound across riddims, remixes and original productions. This site's guide to bouyon producers also covers MB On The Track and the new school of riddims.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the shared instrumental that several artists record their vocals over. Inherited from Jamaican and Dominican riddim culture, riddims sit at the heart of bouyon and dancehall production. A bouyon type beat is built precisely to carry bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals.

What is a bouyon type beat?

A bouyon type beat is an instrumental built on the codes of bouyon: fast tempo, a recognizable keyboard loop, percussion inspired by Dominica and a bass that pushes. Beatmakers look for it to place bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals on a party beat.

How do you structure a bouyon type beat?

A bouyon type beat starts fast: little intro, the main loop arrives early and the drums follow the carnival pattern with regular build-ups. The vocal has to be able to shout, sing and get the crowd to respond, and the energy almost never comes back down: it is a party beat, not a lounge beat.

How do you keep a type beat faithful to Dominica's sound?
