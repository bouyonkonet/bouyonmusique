Bouyon type beat: the formula of the bouyon beat

By Smokiller · Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

A bouyon type beat is an instrumental built on the codes of bouyon: fast tempo, a recognizable keyboard loop, percussion inspired by Dominica and a bass that pushes. Beatmakers look for it to place bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals.

The structure that works

The bouyon type beat starts fast: little intro, the main loop arrives early, the drums follow the carnival pattern with regular build-ups. The vocal has to be able to shout, sing and get the crowd to respond. The energy almost never comes back down: it is a party beat, not a lounge beat.

Stay faithful to the origin

A good type beat cites its sources without copying them: a percussion that recalls the lapo kabwit, an accordion or keyboard melody in the jing ping way, and Creole in the background vocals. For the full method, see how to make a bouyon beat.

Frequently asked questions

Who produces bouyon today?

Producers like Smokiller (Bouyon Shattaz), Denmath, HYPNO! and Savion work the bouyon sound across riddims, remixes and original productions. On the ready-to-voice side, MB On The Track posts his bouyon beats on video and offers custom work by private message. This site's guide to bouyon producers covers the whole new school of riddims.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the shared instrumental that several artists record their vocals over, inherited from Jamaican and Dominican riddim culture. The nuance with a type beat: a riddim often carries a name and travels between several singers, while a bouyon type beat is an instrumental built to carry one bouyon, shatta or dancehall vocal on a party beat, ready to be voiced.

What is a bouyon type beat?

A bouyon type beat is an instrumental built on the codes of bouyon: fast tempo, a recognizable keyboard loop, percussion inspired by Dominica and a bass that pushes. Beatmakers look for it to place bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals on a party beat.

How do you structure a bouyon type beat?

A bouyon type beat starts fast: little intro, the main loop arrives early and the drums follow the carnival pattern with regular build-ups. The vocal has to be able to shout, sing and get the crowd to respond, and the energy almost never comes back down: it is a party beat, not a lounge beat.

How do you keep a type beat faithful to Dominica's sound?

By citing bouyon's sources without copying them: a percussion that recalls the lapo kabwit, an accordion or keyboard melody in the jing ping way, and Creole in the background vocals. For the full method, see how to make a bouyon beat.

What BPM should a bouyon type beat be?

Around 160 BPM, the most frequent tempo of the bouyon records documented on this site. HYPNO shows 160 BPM on Mauvais Plan, and DJ Skaytah's “Raggaboday” instrumental, composed and programmed by Bradley Paulin, is listed at 160 BPM. Some records drop toward 157 BPM, but tempo alone does not make a bouyon. See the bouyon BPM page.

Which percussion goes into a bouyon type beat?

First the percussion of Dominica: the lapo kabwit, the bele drum, the chakchak and the hits of chanté mas. Then the modern elements: fast kick and snare, tight hats, electronic percussion and keyboard sounds for the main loop. Many producers build their kit by sampling recordings of jing ping and carnival bands. See the bouyon drum kit.

Where can I find bouyon instrumentals?

From the producers themselves. MB On The Track posts instrumentals on his YouTube channel titled “Cigarettes”, “Boyz N Girlz” and “Xo” (Bouyon Instrumental 2026), plus “Waka”, in funk x bouyon, and offers custom productions. Smokiller's site smokiller.com also offers free downloads of instrumentals and remixes. See MB On The Track.

Can a bouyon type beat be used for shatta?

Yes, it is one of its uses: beatmakers look for it to place bouyon, shatta and dancehall vocals. But shatta, a dancehall genre from Martinique, is not a branch of bouyon, and some producers label their beats separately: MB On The Track posts “Bouyon Instrumental 2026” beats and, apart, “Rebel”, a Shatta Instrumental 2026. See bouyon vs shatta.

How do you launch a bouyon beat on social media?

In short video: a riddim now launches as a clip of about fifteen seconds, the producer dances on it and the audience demands the long version. On October 16, 2026, MB On The Track danced at night in a bus shelter to his own instrumental, under the line “Rock Bouyon effect”, and the comments asked for the track. See the bouyon producers.
