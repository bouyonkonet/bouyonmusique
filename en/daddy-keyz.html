Daddy Keyz: keyboardist and bouyon riddim producer

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Daddy Keyz is a keyboardist and riddim producer. His Daddy Keyz Productions channel publishes instrumentals billed bouyon, “Vix Riddim (Bouyon)” in October 2026, live arrangements where he holds the keys with a drummer and a bassist, and yearly Riddim Mixes gathering his productions. On the credits side, he is the producer of Reo's “Under Pressure”, billed Bouyon 2026, and he takes the stage with Reo, Edday, Youry and Strosey for the 2026 New Years Jam.

Bouyon riddims for sale: Vix, Indian, Tip

On October 7, 2026, Daddy Keyz Productions uploaded “Vix Riddim (Bouyon)”. The page is explicit: VIX riddim, produced by Daddy Keyz Production, “Genre: Bouyon”, a lease link for artists who want to record on it, and the tags #Bouyon #Dominica #Carnival. It is the riddim producer's model as bouyon practises it: the instrumental is published, heard, then offered to singers.

“Indian Riddim”, published on October 10, 2025, followed the same logic, with a download link. On July 16, 2025, “Tip Riddim Remix” was presented as a live arrangement: riddim produced by Daddy Keyz, keyboard Daddy Keyz, bass Jaylik, drums Drummer Boii Elvz. To understand what “riddim” means in this genre, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Live arrangements and Riddim Mixes: the keyboard at the centre

On July 8, 2026, the channel published “Sé Miimii Live Arrangement - Daddy Keyz ft. Legend The Drummer (Bouyon)”, a live reading of “Sé Miimii” by Miimii KDS and DJ Skycee, keys Daddy Keyz, drums Legend The Drummer. “Riddim Mix Vol. VIII”, uploaded on August 28, 2026, runs 49 minutes and, according to its page, holds only riddims produced or remixed by Daddy Keyz Productions, with Itz Jaylik on bass and Drummerboii Elvz on drums.

The earlier volumes, “Riddim Mix Vol. VII” (2025) and “Riddim Mix 2021”, show the regularity of the work. The oldest pages go back to 2020 through the Poppalox Entertainment channel: “Lovely Riddim (2020)”, close to 50,000 views, and “Gooba (Bouyon Remix) (2020)”, a remix billed bouyon.

Reo's Under Pressure and the 2026 New Years Jam

On February 3, 2026, Reo published “Under Pressure (Bouyon 2026)”. The page credits writing to Reo, production to Daddy Keyz, recording to Xavvo and ThaWizZarD, mixing to Thawizzard and cover art to DJ Realboss. A month earlier, on January 2, 2026, the same channel uploaded “2026 New Years Jam Live - Reo x Edday x Youry x Strosey x Daddy Keyz”, more than 55,000 views when read.

Those two pages place Daddy Keyz in the circle of Reo, with Edday, Youry, Strosey and Tha Wizzard. No opened page spells out where he is from; this profile, written from the YouTube pages opened on October 27, 2026, places him in the Dominican circuit his tags and credits name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Daddy Keyz?

A keyboardist and riddim producer behind the Daddy Keyz Productions channel, producer of Reo's Under Pressure (Bouyon 2026) and a musician of the 2026 New Years Jam with Reo, Edday, Youry and Strosey.

Which bouyon riddims has he published?

Vix Riddim (Bouyon) on October 7, 2026, Indian Riddim (2025), Tip Riddim Remix as a live arrangement (2025), and earlier Lovely Riddim and Gooba (Bouyon Remix) in 2020 through Poppalox Entertainment.

What is a Daddy Keyz live arrangement?

A live reading of a riddim or a title, keys by Daddy Keyz, with a drummer and a bassist: Sé Miimii with Legend The Drummer (2026), Tip Riddim Remix with Jaylik and Drummer Boii Elvz (2025).

What is Riddim Mix Vol. VIII?

A 49-minute mix uploaded on August 28, 2026, made only of riddims produced or remixed by Daddy Keyz Productions, with Itz Jaylik on bass and Drummerboii Elvz on drums.

Where is Daddy Keyz from?

No opened page says. His Vix Riddim page carries the #Dominica tag and his credits sit in Reo's circle; this profile places him in the Dominican circuit without stating an origin.
