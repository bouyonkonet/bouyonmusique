About Ncore

Ncore is a bouyon band from Dominica, documented under two names: Ncore, and its earlier name MFR, or MFR Band. The reference works that cite it credit it with an idea almost no other band in the genre has claimed: rythmcore, a fusion of rock, heavy metal and bouyon, carried by a single named “RIDDIM NATION”. The same sources place it, with Triple Kay, among the Dominican bands whose tours of the French Antilles gave birth to bouyon gwada. And the old name never left the stage: MFR played the carnival openings in 2008 and 2009, and MFR stands on the bill of Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl 2026, with a dated slot on Tuesday October 20, 2026. This profile gathers everything that can be verified, and says clearly what cannot: no source names the members, and no music platform page for the band was found.

The kinship of the two names is written in black and white in the bouyon band lists. The list published by Alchetron, which carries the older reference article on bouyon, lines up the old school bands, WCK, First Serenade, RSB and Triple K, then the new school bands, Triple K International, Kross Vybez, Xs Groove, Signal and the others, and it enters “Ncore (formerly known as MFR)”. That is the clearest wording in the file: one band, two name eras.
