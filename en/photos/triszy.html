About TriSzy

TriSzy is the artist name of Tristan Shillingford, a singer and rapper born in Brooklyn, schooled in Dominica, then back in the United States, where he started in music as a hip hop rapper with the collective The Mixed Breed. For five years his catalog, released on the ThaMixedBreedz banner, stays on the rap side and in what he himself calls Island Pop. Then, in April 2025, he releases “Bouyon Mood”, a bouyon single produced by Fr3EzE and mixed by Tha WizZarD, and the record becomes the centre of his story: the Caribbean press picks it up, and Shazam ranks it among his most streamed songs. This profile follows the catalog year by year, from “Farenheit” in 2020 to “Eat Dat Up” in 2026, reads “Bouyon Mood” in the text, and separates what the platforms document from what only the press reports.

The full name is given by the press coverage of May 2025 and confirmed by the platform credits: Tristan Shillingford, known as TriSzy. The Shazam pages of “Bend & Bounce” and “Gade Sa” credit Tristan Shillingford as songwriter, and the page of “Eat Dat Up” credits him on vocals, alongside Corel Domnique. The identity behind the stage name is therefore no mystery: this is one of the rare files of this wave where the legal name, the artist name and the writing credits line up cleanly on opened sources.
