About Yu Meï

Yu Meï, whose name the platforms also print as Yumei, is a bouyon singer from the Guadeloupe scene. Her public record is a run of short, direct singles released between 2020 and 2024, a duo with an unapologetic name, Ultimes Pétasses, a collaboration with Team Bwè Tou Sa, a notable feature on Yellow Gaza “PLAN A 3” in 2024, and a second, quieter life in the writing credits of other artists. In October 2026 she also spoke publicly, in France-Antilles, about intimate videos of her shared without her consent, refusing shame and stating that she had taken the matter to the authorities. This profile gathers those verified facts, record by record and credit by credit, and states clearly what the sources do not yet allow anyone to say.

Yu Meï is a bouyon singer. That is how the two podcasts that hosted her present her, and that is how France-Antilles presents her, describing her in October 2026 as a 29 year old bouyon singer. Her documented territory is Guadeloupe: the Guadeloupe episode of the Faya podcast, recorded around Pointe-à-Pitre, counts her among its guests from the local scene, and her catalogue circulates inside the crews of Guadeloupe bouyon, from Team Bwè Tou Sa to the Yellow Gaza universe. No source consulted publishes her birthplace, so this profile invents none: what is established is her scene, not her full civil record.
