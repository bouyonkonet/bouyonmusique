About King Dice

King Dice, Dennison Joseph by name, is first of all a calypsonian from Dominica: Writeups24 credits him with ten Calypso Monarch crowns, the latest in February 2024. He keeps stepping into bouyon, though. You hear him with WCK on “You!” in 2017, with Shelly and Shadowflow on the Champion Riddim in 2019, and on Dada's Kryptic Riddim in 2025.

In June 2011, the Vincentian paper Searchlight announced his visit to Vincy Mas and traced his early run: three crowns in a row from 2004 to 2006, with “Peace Is My Business” and “Animal Farm”, then “Help My Brothers For Me” and “Puppet Master”, then “These Are The Days” and “I Eh Fraid Of Them”. The same article credits him with five straight road marches, from “Tarzan” in 2003 to “Lights” in 2007.
