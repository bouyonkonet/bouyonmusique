About Kkrytical

Kkrytical, born Ronald Longmore, is a Jamaican dancehall artist from Waterford, Portmore, Jamaica, raised between Portmore and Clarendon. He competed in Magnum King and Queen and migrated to the United States at 21, and he is now based in New York, United States. On Instagram, at @kkryticalofficial, he had 34,310 followers at the October 17, 2026 check, the widest audience of the five artists in this series of pages. His bio presents him as an international recording artist, songwriter and composer, with bookings via @showtime_services. Why does a Jamaican dancehall artist appear on a site devoted to bouyon? Because dancehall is one of bouyon's parent influences: the vocal delivery inherited from ragga lives inside bouyon and bouyon-muffin. The New York Caribbean circuit Kkrytical works is also the same diaspora map Bouyon Shattaz documents page after page. This page gathers his verified facts, drawn notably from his official EPK of July 2024, and clearly flags what is not verified.

Kkrytical was born Ronald Longmore in Waterford, a district of Portmore, Jamaica, and he grew up between Portmore and Clarendon. That double Jamaican geography is the base of his path: Portmore, the large city on Kingston's edge, is a known dancehall breeding ground, and Clarendon is one of the parishes feeding the island's sound system culture. Our dossiers keep those places as his press pack presents them, without adding unconfirmed biographical details.
