About Les Ptits du RZT

Les Ptits du RZT are a group whose name travels through the credits of recent bouyon and shatta, but for whom no verified public biography was found during the research for this profile. No confirmed official page, no interview located, no published list of members. What does exist, and can be checked record by record, comes down to three recordings and one party: a feature on LESTEF KJF BOYZ “Malpalé” in February 2024, a single of their own, “Tata Yéyé”, in June 2024, a co-credit on “Look My Style” in 2025, and a party under their name at L’Empire Club in Chilly-Mazarin in October 2026. This profile tells exactly that, no more and no less, and it states clearly where the public record stops.
