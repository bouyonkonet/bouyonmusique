About DJ Taffy

DJ Taffy is a bouyon DJ and producer from Dominica, known as the Riddim Beast. His label is called Riddim Beast Studios, his songwriting credits published by Shazam carry the name Tafari Maffei, and his name appears where Dominican bouyon is being made in 2025 and 2026: Producer of the Year finalist at the Dominica Music Awards 2025, co-credited name on “Make Her Bawl” with Shelly, Trixx and Signal Band, headline name of the Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl Super Mix with Taffy and Co, and a name on Night 3 of the World Creole Music Festival 2026, on Sunday, October 25, in Roseau, according to the published festival guides. This profile documents that path with its sources, no more and no less.

Apple Music lists two albums by DJ Taffy on Riddim Beast Studios. Can't Stop Won't Stop came out on 19 September 2024 and includes Baddie, Jiggle, Tie up, Bachanal and Lobster. Pick your speed, Know your limit came out on 17 September 2025 and has seven tracks, among them Do 4 Do, Up is Up and Pay day.
