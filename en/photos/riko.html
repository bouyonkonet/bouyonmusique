About Riko

Riko is the most regular voice of Team Bleudeu, a collective whose YouTube channel has published bouyon since 2019, always under the same signature: the crew name, the year, and the word bouyon in the title. His first big hit was a group effort: “Bokit & T-Max”, with BaxX on a beat by Dj WeezZ, has passed 1.7 million views. Then came “Mytho” in 2021, “Pasé lang an mwen” in 2022, “Métrizé Tchad La” in 2023, “Gestu” produced by Dj Dirtee, “Zing Chap” with Team Bwè Tou Sa and Billy BYBF in 2025, and “Bodycount” in 2026. No biography was found: this profile follows the published discography.

Apple Music lists a single by Riko, Vendredi, released on 11 December 2025. Its video was published on the Team Bleudeu YouTube channel on 14 December 2025. Apple Music also credits Riko as a featured artist on two releases: BAISE-MOI by Laka (22 November 2023), with Billy Bybf and Team Bwè Tou Sa among the credited artists, and Sit down #Oups 2 by Lünik (18 December 2024), with Billy BYBF and MA6MO, issued under Team Bwè Tou Sa. The Apple Music record for Bokit & T-max by Ba2x shows an online release on 18 April 2021 and a 2019 Team Bleudeu copyright line, which matches the 2019 date on this page. On 3 November 2026, the video for Métrizé Tchad La on the Team Bleudeu channel had 174,727 views.
