About Ransom Beatz

Ransom Beatz is a producer who releases instrumentals at an unusual pace, with more than two hundred releases under his name. Long focused on afrobeat and dancehall, in 2026 he gave a full album and an EP to bouyon, and he licenses his riddims to singers.
