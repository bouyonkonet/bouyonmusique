About Kubu

Kubu is a singer who has been posting bouyon songs on his own YouTube channel since summer 2026. His most played track, “Hennessy”, rides Wetty Beatz's Link Up Riddim. His pages are written in French and Creole and name Guadeloupe, Grenada and Dominica, but never say where he is from.
