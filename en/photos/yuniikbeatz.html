About YuniikBeatz

YuniikBeatz is a producer name, and almost nothing else in public sources. No biography, no place, no official account verified during this research: the name exists only through its credits, and this profile publishes only those credits. There are three of them, each verified by opening the platform pages directly. In 2024, YuniikBeatz is credited as composer of the “B.A.B Remix” by gapman and Enima, in French, on WAVY BOUNCE RECORDS. In 2025, the name becomes the billed producer of TIITII NBA on two bouyon singles from the HEAVEN STUDIO house, “Nah Nah” on March 28, 2025 and “BOSS” at the end of the year. It is a small record, it is precise, and it is exactly what a beatmaker file is worth in current bouyon: the name travels on other people records, at 160 BPM, while the person stays out of frame.
