About Pumpa

Pumpa is a soca singer from the US Virgin Islands, known well beyond his island since “The A List” in 2022. In July 2025 he cut “Savage” on the Kryptic Riddim, a bouyon riddim produced in Dominica by Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence, who lists him among its regional headliners. In April 2026 a bouyon take on “The A List” also surfaced on the WYFL Warm Up Riddim.
