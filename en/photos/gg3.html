About GG3

GG3 is a composer, singer and performer from Guadeloupe. After about ten years in music and a rap album in 2024, he found his first real hit with “World Cup Bouyon”, released on June 11, 2026 as the World Cup began.

On streaming services GG3 shows up as early as October 2021, as a guest on “Tristesse originale” with 99 and Har2Nok. In March 2024 he released the album “Fè mannèv” on his own label, GG DIAMANT 3, filed as rap: seven tracks, including “Mélange” with Chanceko and “Followers / Lagaba” with Aknose.
