About Halibut

Halibut, born Cletus Abraham, was the lead singer of Belles Combo and one of the big voices of Dominican cadence-lypso. In 2014 he recorded “False Hair”, a bouyon song produced by Dada for carnival. He sang at the World Creole Music Festival one last time in October 2025.

For Mas Domnik 2014, Dada produced, mixed and mastered “False Hair” on the Madras Riddim, recorded at Cotton Groove Studio in Dominica and released on the Machine Dominik label. The producer's channel calls it a fusion of bouyon and soca and credits it to “Legendary Halibut of Belles Combo”, who also wrote the lyrics.
