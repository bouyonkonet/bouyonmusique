About Shanna Raymond

Shanna Raymond describes herself as a singer, songwriter, actress and model, between Jamaica and Saint Kitts. Known first for her soca, from “Bend Yuh Back” to “Gypsy”, she has two bouyon titles: “Hustle Hard”, written for Dada's Kryptic Riddim, mixed in Dominica, in 2025, and “Bam Bam”, released in October 2026 on the SHANNA Entertainment label.
