About Hxnter

Hxnter, credited as Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt on a Big Red Productions page, is a producer whose channel posts bouyon and nothing else. His YouTube channel lines up 67 instrumentals, all billed bouyon, split between original riddims and remixes of pop hits. At least three acts have recorded on his productions: Sully Jacob, ShaSha with Big Red HD, and very probably Vibwoy.
