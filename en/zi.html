Zi: a Guyanese artist singing bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Zi describes himself as a Guyanese artist making original bouyon. The distribution credits on “Woman” name him Azizi Gray. Since May 2025 he has released singles billed as bouyon, nearly all mixed by dernz.wav, and one of them went out on DJ Taffy's channel.

From Guyana to bouyon

Bouyon comes from Dominica, but Zi sings it from another country. Under the video for “Woman”, posted in December 2025, he writes plainly: “Original Bouyon music from a Guyanese artist”, next to the flag of Guyana. The same flag opens the description of “Ramming & Beating” in 2026.

On Deezer, the name Zi gathers dozens of unrelated releases, from rap to Brazilian pop. His own songs stand out by their distribution labels and credits. The YouTube listing for “Woman” pairs ZI with Azizi Gray, the name also shown on his Facebook page.

More Rum and IS PIPE, the 2025 run

“More Rum” came out on May 26, 2025, billed “Bouyon 2025”. On June 6, DJ Taffy posted it on his channel, where it is close to 5,700 views, by far his best number. The description credits production to Dboss, engineering to dernz.wav and the video to mindurframe.

August brought “IS PIPE”, again produced by Dboss and released under the Grayvybz label. The visualizer, by Blk Lens, tells listeners to “ride the Bouyon wave” and promises a bassline that hits hard. The song runs just over three minutes, longer than most of his singles.

Woman, Ramming & Beating and Wa Yah Drinkin?

“Woman” opened his “Bouyon 2026” run as early as December 7, 2025. In April 2026, “Ramming & Beating” showed an artist doing almost everything himself: the description credits him with recording, cover art and visuals, leaving the riddim to skako.767 and the mix to dernz.wav.

In late August 2026 he shared “Wa Yah Drinkin?” with Wackaa, on a riddim by Hamzxl868. The hashtags mention Jouvert, carnival and Guyana, which places the song in the festival season. His numbers stay small, a few hundred views at most on his own channel.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Zi?

An artist who describes himself as Guyanese and sings bouyon. The distribution credits on Woman name him Azizi Gray.

What are his bouyon songs?

More Rum and IS PIPE (2025), then Woman, Ramming & Beating and Wa Yah Drinkin? with Wackaa, billed as bouyon 2026.

What is his most played song?

More Rum, posted by DJ Taffy in June 2025, with close to 5,700 views. His other videos stay at a few hundred views or fewer.

Who produces his songs?

Dboss on More Rum and IS PIPE, skako.767 on Ramming & Beating and Hamzxl868 on Wa Yah Drinkin?. dernz.wav mixes most of them.

Why is he hard to find on Deezer?

The Deezer profile “Zi” mixes several artists with the same name. His bouyon singles are easy to spot by their titles, which carry Bouyon 2025 or Bouyon 2026.
