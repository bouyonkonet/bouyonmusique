By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Eye Spy and Back Hurt: two bouyon releases on the same day

On January 16, 2026, the Klassik Frescobar Official channel uploaded “DJ Kalli, Klassik Frescobar, V'ghn - Eye Spy (Bouyon 2026)”. The page credits the writing to Klassik Frescobar and V'ghn, and the production to DJ Kalli and Boogy Rankss. The same day came “Back Hurt (Bouyon 2026)”, with Klassik Frescobar, PNDRN and Nessa Preppy. On Deezer both singles are dated January 16, 2026, label Codeway: “Eye Spy” runs 2 minutes 54, “Back Hurt” 3 minutes 13, and DJ Kalli is credited first on both.

The cast shows how bouyon travels in 2026: Klassik Frescobar, V'ghn, whom this site places in Grenada per WIPO Magazine, and Nessa Preppy, from Trinidad and Tobago. The keywords on the “Eye Spy” YouTube page list bouyon, dominica, martinique, grenada and guadalupe: a title built for the whole Caribbean, in the wake of what our page on bouyon soca describes.

Voila! and I Testify: four singles in three months

DJ Kalli's Deezer artist page lists four releases, all from 2026. After the two January titles, “Voila!” came out on February 20, 2026 on Codeway Digital, with Klassik Frescobar and Krys, the Guadeloupean singer; the YouTube page generated for that title was past 34,000 views when read. On March 24, 2026, “I Testify” was released on DJ Kalli Productions, LLC, with Dash, Boogy Rankss and Jay-Blu.

Boogy Rankss, co-producer of “Eye Spy” and guest on “I Testify”, links DJ Kalli to another name on this site: he also produces “Whine Down” for Th3rd. That network of producers, from Codeway to Th3rdHouse, makes part of the bouyon exported in 2026.

Before bouyon: the Piton Riddim and Dennery Segment

DJ Kalli's YouTube channel keeps the trace of earlier work in another Saint Lucian genre, Dennery Segment. On June 16, 2023 he published “Tallyboy, Leonus & DJ Kalli - Give Her (Piton Riddim) [Dennery Segment 2023]”, followed by “Maddrass & DJ Kalli - Bak In It (Piton Riddim)”. The Piton Riddim is therefore his production billed Dennery Segment, two and a half years before his bouyon titles.

No page opened for this profile says where DJ Kalli is from: neither Codeway, nor his YouTube pages, nor Deezer. This profile therefore assigns him no island. It is written from the pages opened on October 27, 2026 and will be extended if an official page or an interview documents his path.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Kalli?

A producer and DJ credited first on Eye Spy (with Klassik Frescobar and V'ghn) and Back Hurt (with Klassik Frescobar, PNDRN and Nessa Preppy), two singles billed Bouyon 2026 released on January 16, 2026 on Codeway.

What are his bouyon titles?

Eye Spy and Back Hurt (January 16, 2026, Codeway), Voila! with Klassik Frescobar and Krys (February 20, 2026, Codeway Digital) and I Testify with Dash, Boogy Rankss and Jay-Blu (March 24, 2026, DJ Kalli Productions).

Who wrote and produced Eye Spy?

According to the Klassik Frescobar Official YouTube page, the title was written by Klassik Frescobar and V'ghn and produced by DJ Kalli and Boogy Rankss.

What is the Piton Riddim?

A DJ Kalli production billed Dennery Segment 2023, published on his YouTube channel in June 2023 with Give Her (Tallyboy and Leonus) and Bak In It (Maddrass).
