R.B.M: Champion Riddim, the Nature Island Records bouyon EP

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

R.B.M is the name carried by “Champion Riddim”, a six-track EP released on February 14, 2019 on Nature Island Records. The label's name states the Nature Isle, Dominica's nickname, and the credits confirm the anchor: all six tracks are composed by Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green, and voiced by a line of Dominican singers, Carlyn XP, Colton T, Raii, Shelly, King Dice, Shadowflow and Daddy Chess. The opening track is called “Bouyon Army”.

Six tracks, one composing team

The EP's Deezer listing gives the credits track by track. “Bouyon Army” features Carlyn XP, written by Michelle Henderson and Carlyn Xavier-Phillip. “Champion” is written and voiced by Colton T (Colton Thomas in the credits). “Cya Hold Me Back” is written by Raheem Henderson and voiced by Raii. “Cya Stop de Sewo” lines up Shelly, King Dice and Shadowflow, written by Sheldon Alfred, Dennison Joseph and Wayne Robinson. “Voltage Up” is written by Chester Letang and voiced by Daddy Chess. An instrumental of the riddim closes the EP.

The constant is the composing team: Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green sign the music of all six tracks. In the opened sources, R.B.M therefore reads as the signature of a production project rather than a documented stage name: no biography, no named member, no expansion of the initials was found. See the profiles of Colton T, Shelly, Carlyn XP and Daddy Chess for the EP's voices.

Bouyon Army, a title that keeps circulating

The EP's first track gave its name to a banner: “Bouyon Army”. In 2026, Carlyn XP still files the hashtag #BouyonArmy among the tags of her “Culture Me Love” video on her official channel. The 2019 title therefore lives beyond the EP, in its own singer's communication.

As of October 27, 2026, the EP remains the only release tied to R.B.M in the opened sources, apart from two Spanish-language singles from 2020 displayed on the same Deezer artist page (“Quisiera”; “solo te quiero a ti”). Those two tracks share no credit with the Champion Riddim team and visibly belong to a namesake; they are excluded from this profile, as this site does with merged artist pages.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file rests on a complete EP listing and its credits: a label, a date, six tracks, three constant composers and seven voices. Nothing states who stands behind the initials R.B.M, or whether the project continued under another name.

This profile will be updated if a primary source names the people behind R.B.M or if a new release appears under that signature.

Frequently asked questions

Who is R.B.M?

The name on the “Champion Riddim” EP (Nature Island Records, February 14, 2019). No opened source expands the initials or names the project's members.

What are the Champion Riddim tracks?

“Bouyon Army” (feat. Carlyn XP), “Champion” (feat. Colton T), “Cya Hold Me Back” (feat. Raii), “Cya Stop de Sewo” (feat. Shelly, King Dice & Shadowflow), “Voltage Up” (feat. Daddy Chess) and the riddim instrumental.

Who composed the Champion Riddim?

Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green compose all six tracks, according to the credits displayed by Deezer.

Did R.B.M release anything else?

Not in the opened sources tied to this team. Two Spanish-language singles from 2020 sit on the same Deezer page with no credit in common; this profile treats them as a namesake.

What does Bouyon Army mean?

It is the EP's opening track, voiced by Carlyn XP, who still uses #BouyonArmy in her video tags in 2026.
