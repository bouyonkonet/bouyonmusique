Natoxie: Shatta Bouyon, O Top with Lünik, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Natoxie is a shatta singer and producer who often works with bouyon material. His “Shatta Bouyon” mix, posted for the start of the 2022 carnival, is close to five million views. In 2023 he made the instrumental and the mix for “O Top” by Lünik, released as bouyon 2023. His accounts carry the suffix 972, the Martinique dialling code.

Shatta Bouyon, the carnival mix

On January 22, 2022 his channel posted “Shatta Bouyon”, billed as a new mix to kick off carnival. The video is close to five million views. A follow-up, “Shatta Bouyon 2”, is past 216,000 views, and he came back in 2026 with “Shatta Carnaval 2026”.

The title sums up where he stands: a shatta artist who owns the crossover with bouyon. Our shatta bouyon page covers that meeting point between the two genres.

O Top and the producer's work

On January 22, 2023 his channel posted “O Top” by Lünik, of Team Bwè Tou Sa, featuring Natoxie, tagged bouyon 2023. The credits say “Instru & Mix by Natoxie”: he made the riddim and the mix. The video is past 450,000 views.

On Deezer his catalogue starts on March 2, 2021 with “Ka'k It Up”, then the EP “Gros Bonda Riddim” on June 4, 2021. Riddims keep coming back in his titles: “EDF Riddim 3”, an album dated June 13, 2026, and the Code Lyokox and Koké Sal tags on his songs with TKD.

Big numbers and features

His most watched videos run into the millions: “Applaudissement” with TKD, past eight million, “Pineco” with X-Man, on the Bereta Riddim, past seven million, and “BMW” with Bamby, past ten million, according to the platform listings.

He also guests for others: “Pa fè rol” by Kevni, tagged Shattastyle, “Full Tube” by FLW, “Bubble” by Ti Couby with 1T1, and Meryl's “Yozo”. With Kenjox he put out “Pikéy” and “Position”.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Natoxie?

A shatta singer and producer who often crosses into bouyon, known for the 2022 Shatta Bouyon mix and songs such as Applaudissement and Pineco.

What is Shatta Bouyon?

A mix Natoxie posted on January 22, 2022 for the start of carnival, now close to five million views. A follow-up, Shatta Bouyon 2, came later.

Is Natoxie also a producer?

Yes. On Lünik's O Top, released as bouyon 2023, the credits give him the instrumental and the mix. He also releases riddim projects, such as EDF Riddim 3 in 2026.

Who has he sung with?

TKD, X-Man, Bamby, Lünik, Kevni, FLW, Ti Couby, 1T1, Meryl and Kenjox, among others.

Where is Natoxie from?

The pages consulted do not say it outright. His accounts carry the suffix 972, the Martinique dialling code.
