DJ TKrys: the DJ behind the Active ton Mode Bouyon series

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DJ TKrys is a DJ and composer anchored in Guadeloupe whose YouTube channel has published, every autumn since 2022, a bouyon mix under the same title: Active ton Mode Bouyon. The volume dated 2023 has passed 880,000 views. He does more than mix: he holds the composer credit on “Work It”, an Edday track tagged Bouyon 2020, he releases freestyles with Aknose and Lestef KJF, and he is one of the eight guests credited on 1T1's “Mas La Rivé”. This profile sticks to what his own pages publish: no biography, no legal name and no birthplace were found.

A mix series with the genre in its title

The core of the DJ TKrys file is a series. On September 9, 2022, his channel published “Active ton Mode Bouyon 2023 | The Best of Bouyon 2023”, a 28 minute mix made with Mizericord Prod and KB Agency. The description gives a 32 line tracklist, and several lines carry his name: “TKRYS X TBTS - BADABOOM” and “TKRYS x TBTS Ass Fess Sex” with Team Bwè Tou Sa, “ALPHA X TKRYS - PA BOUJE TWOP”, “BMY - PLACE AW X TKRYS”, and an “Intro Bouyon TKrys Palamam”. Around those house versions, the list runs through LGC, Sukie, Litleboy, Lunik, Tenslay, Le Bluff, Asa Bantan and Ridge: Guadeloupe and Dominica in the same set.

The series carries on year after year. “Active ton Mode Bouyon 2024 (Mix Bouyon)” came out on September 29, 2024, running 32 minutes 51, and “Active ton Mode Bouyon 2025 (Mix Bouyon)” on September 25, 2025, running 29 minutes 59, filmed by KB Agency and Ruddy GST. The rhythm is that of a fixture: one bouyon mix at the end of every summer, like an annual compilation. Alongside it, the channel runs the La Bringue and La Boulangerie series, whose titles list dancehall, shatta, afrobeat, amapiano and soca bouyon, and a long Active Ton Mode Shatta series. Bouyon is not his only ground, but it is the only genre with its own yearly volume.

The composer: Work It, Gwo Fess Gwo TT and Pumpy

DJ TKrys also shows up in creative credits. The page for “Work It”, posted on his channel on August 7, 2020, presents the track as part of Edday's Wake Me Up Mixtape, recorded at Just Bring It Records and directed by Joseph Grx, and it names one composer: Dj TKrys. The hashtag Bouyon2020 sits at the bottom of the page. It is the clearest credit in his file: a bouyon track by a major Guadeloupe voice, with the music signed by him.

On June 21, 2023, the channel posted two short formats. “Gwo Fess Gwo TT”, with Aknose, and “Pumpy (Freestyle)”, with Lestef KJF, two minutes each, filed by their tags under Guadeloupe and Bouyon, the second even reusing the series name, Active ton mode Bouyon. The link with Aknose goes back: in 2021 the channel uploaded the audio of “Ding Dong”, by Dj TKrys and Aknose, with a 2018 copyright line in the name of Le Bon Tuyau Music. That page states no genre, and Deezer dates the single to September 28, 2018: this profile cites it as a milestone without classifying it.

The 1T1 circle and recent releases

On January 12, 2024, 1T1 released “Mas La Rivé”, distributed by DistroKid, with eight guest names: Theomaa, Aknose, DJ SOFTEE, Dj TKrys, Bismok, Jicypie, Dj Kylled and Lejuh. The track listing states no genre, so this profile does not count it among his verified bouyon titles, but it places DJ TKrys in the same circle as the producers and DJs who make today's bouyon gwada.

His Deezer page lists six singles: “Ding Dong” (2018), “Ti Mo Dou” and “Cho deh” (2021), “We Coming” (September 19, 2025, with Les P’tits du RZT), “Guadeloupe Carnival 2026” (January 15, 2026) and “Samoussa” (February 6, 2026), whose lyrics and composition he signs alone according to the video page. None of those listings uses the word bouyon: they stay in the text and out of the structured data. The channel also shows a working DJ, from the Gwada Color Fun Run to the Montpel Carnival 2025, with the LBTM label and the KB Agency team as recurring signatures.

What the file does not say

No opened page gives a legal name, a birth date or a birthplace for DJ TKrys. His Guadeloupe anchoring reads from his own tags (Guadeloupe, 971), his carnival dates and his list of partners, and this profile presents it as anchoring, not as a civil record. The Active ton Mode Bouyon volumes are mixes: they document a DJ, they do not turn every mixed track into a DJ TKrys production.

To place this work in context, see the bouyon gwada page, the list of Guadeloupe artists and the bouyon mix page. If a DJ TKrys title later states its genre on its own listing, it will join this page with its date.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ TKrys?

DJ TKrys is a DJ and composer anchored in Guadeloupe. His YouTube channel publishes the Active ton Mode Bouyon mix series (2023, 2024 and 2025 volumes), and he holds the composer credit on Edday's Work It, tagged Bouyon 2020. No public biography was found.

What is the Active ton Mode Bouyon series?

A series of bouyon mixes posted on his channel at the end of each summer: the 2023 volume on September 9, 2022 (28 minutes 20), the 2024 volume on September 29, 2024 (32 minutes 51) and the 2025 volume on September 25, 2025 (29 minutes 59). The first lists 32 tracks, including several versions signed TKrys.

Which bouyon titles carry his name?

Work It with Edday (2020, where he is the credited composer), Gwo Fess Gwo TT with Aknose and Pumpy (Freestyle) with Lestef KJF (both from June 21, 2023, filed as Bouyon by their pages). These are the three titles entered in the structured data of this profile.

Has he worked with 1T1?

Yes. Dj TKrys is one of the eight guests credited on 1T1's Mas La Rivé, released on January 12, 2024, with Theomaa, Aknose, DJ SOFTEE, Bismok, Jicypie, Dj Kylled and Lejuh. The track listing states no genre.

Where is DJ TKrys from?

No opened source gives his birthplace. His own pages anchor him in Guadeloupe (Guadeloupe and 971 tags, Gwada Color Fun Run, Guadeloupe collaborators): this profile presents him as anchored in Guadeloupe, without treating that as a civil record.
