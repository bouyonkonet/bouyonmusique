Fiddy ii

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Fiddy ii turns up on the Okra Slime Riddim with “All Kinda Gal”, uploaded on April 18, 2026 to the channel “Jamar fiddy belgrave” under a title that carries both “Barbados Bouyon” and “Soca 2026”. The description credits the writing to Jamar “Fiddy” Belgrave and Stephan “C4” Hope, and production, mix and master to 10 Star Records. The nickname Fiddy links the stage name to the credited name, as the page itself writes it; no biography or birthplace appears there.

All Kinda Gal, a third voice on the riddim

The published title reads “Fiddy ii - All Kinda Gal (Okra Slime Riddim) Official Audio [Barbados Bouyon] Soca 2026”, running 2 minutes 40. It puts the track on the same instrumental as Sharkie’s “Okra Slime” and Stella Billy’s “Energy”, both produced by 10Star Records; the BimTraveler channel also lists Ian Stylez’s “Crazy Jump” on this bouyon riddim. The All Kinda Gal credits follow the same pattern: 10 Star Records on production, mix and master.

The writing is shared between Jamar “Fiddy” Belgrave and Stephan “C4” Hope. That second name carries the same nickname as an artist already on this site, but no opened source links the two: this profile quotes the credit as published, with no link and no assumption.

A double label, and a list of carnivals

The title joins two labels, Barbados Bouyon and Soca 2026, which places the track on the border our page on bouyon soca describes. The description’s tags go further and aim at a whole calendar: Crop Over, the Trinidad, Grenada and Saint Vincent carnivals, Miami, New York Labour Day and Notting Hill, plus the dennery segment. That is a distribution map, not a biography.

As with the other titles in the batch, the Barbadian label describes the music and the Crop Over season. It does not name Fiddy ii’s birthplace, which this site leaves open.

The limits of the file

The artist’s channel showed eleven videos when opened, and only one carries the word bouyon in its title. This profile therefore rests on “All Kinda Gal” alone, with its published credits.

The page will be updated if new bouyon releases or a biography appear, for example in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Fiddy ii?

Fiddy ii is the artist on “All Kinda Gal”, on the Okra Slime Riddim, billed as Barbados Bouyon and Soca 2026. The channel and credits connect the name to Jamar “Fiddy” Belgrave.

When did All Kinda Gal come out?

On April 18, 2026 on the “Jamar fiddy belgrave” channel, running 2 minutes 40.

Who wrote and produced All Kinda Gal?

Jamar “Fiddy” Belgrave and Stephan “C4” Hope wrote it; 10 Star Records produced, mixed and mastered it.

Is Stephan C4 Hope the C4 already profiled on this site?

No opened source says so. The credit is quoted as it stands, without a link to another profile.

No opened page names a birthplace. The title bills the music as Barbados bouyon tied to Crop Over.
