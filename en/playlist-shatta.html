What is shatta bouyon?

Shatta bouyon is the meeting between the modern riddim aesthetic of shatta and bouyon, the historic genre born in Dominica. The term is used when today's French Caribbean and French producers work the bouyon material on riddims made for dance and social media.

Why does a shatta playlist include bouyon?

Because shatta and bouyon are the same family. Shatta is the family of modern Antillean sounds fed, among other things, by bouyon. People looking for a shatta playlist are often looking, without knowing it, for the bouyon half of that sound: a good shatta playlist therefore includes modern bouyon tracks.
