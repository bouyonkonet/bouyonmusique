DEE ZEN: from the single BOUYON to SWAGLOKAL

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DEE ZEN is an artist whose public pages mix Creole rap, dancehall and bouyon, and who gave the genre a whole single in its name: “BOUYON”, released on June 11, 2025 under his DEE MONDE DEE VISION banner and produced with Novysto. His anchoring signals are published by himself: an Instagram account signed 971, singles titled “NEUFSEPT” and “+590”, two Guadeloupe codes, and labels named 97TODIWORLD. In 2026, he appears on “Tik Tak” by D.O.C FIX DEM with Billy BYBF, then invites Magbeatz on two tracks of his album SWAGLOKAL.

Who DEE ZEN is: a name, a banner, some codes

DEE ZEN presents himself through a banner as much as a name. His first singles listed on Deezer, from “KOB AN MWEN” in October 2024 onward, come out under DEE MONDE DEE VISION, and his 2026 albums under two versions of the same slogan, 97TODEEWORLD then 97TODIWORLD, which his video descriptions repeat as a rallying cry: the 97 to the world. The play on his name and the English word is obvious, and it comes back everywhere.

The anchoring signals are many and all published by the artist. The Instagram account linked under the “BOUYON” video is deezen971, and the one linked under “GAZA” is deezen97. Two singles carry Guadeloupe codes as titles: “NEUFSEPT”, in January 2025, and “+590”, the island’s phone prefix, in August 2025. This profile reads those signals as a claimed anchoring in Guadeloupe, not as a birth record: no opened page gives a place or date of birth, or a civil name.

The release pace tells the rest. Between January and December 2025, Deezer lists a dozen singles, from “LADY GAGA” to “KILL DEM” by way of “AN NOU AY”, “KARIBBEAN” and “AN TAN LONTAN”. It is a streaming generation method, a release every few weeks, and it is in the middle of that run that the track bringing him to this site lands.

BOUYON, June 2025: a single with the genre in its title

“BOUYON” came out on June 11, 2025, according to its distribution page on YouTube and to Deezer, with DEE ZEN and Novysto credited. The official video followed on June 16 on his channel: production by Novysto and DEE ZEN himself, mix by Novysto, images by HUGOPNC, edit by 0BIDULE, styling by OKANDEUXFOIS. The track runs 1 minute 51, a short format cut for the platforms and dance videos.

The title is the primary tie to the genre, and this profile holds to it. It should also say what the Deezer listing shows: the platform files this single under Rap/Hip Hop, like much of his output. There is no contradiction, rather a portrait: a catalogue the platform files as rap and a single that names bouyon in its title, a meeting that our shatta and bouyon page helps to place.

Two weeks later, “BOOMIN”, produced by stayawakesanto and again mixed by Novysto, keeps the same image team. Novysto returns later as a guest on “BBB”, on the album FROM: he is the most constant partner in the record, even though no opened page says who he is beyond his credits.

Tik Tak: DEE ZEN in the D.O.C FIX DEM workshop

On February 6, 2026, “Tik Tak” came out under the name D.O.C FIX DEM, with three credited voices: Billy BYBF, DEE ZEN and DDK. The track runs 2 minutes. It is the clearest tie between DEE ZEN and the Guadeloupe bouyon circuit as this site documents it: D.O.C FIX DEM is a workshop whose catalogue is already profiled here, and Billy BYBF a regular voice of that circuit.

Two weeks earlier, on January 23, 2026, he released his first listed album, FROM, eight tracks under the 97TODEEWORLD label. It holds “MASS”, with Atox, first released as a single in December 2025, “BBB” with Novysto, “ARAWAK FREESTYLE”, “WATA”, “ONE DROP (Unknown Riddim)”, “ORCHIDÉE”, “PA MÉLÉ” and a track titled “ASA BANTAN”. That last title borrows the name of a bouyon artist already profiled on this site, but no opened page explains its meaning or links the two artists, and this profile draws nothing from it.

Atox’s name deserves a line: he is credited as co producer of Malcolm’s “SAFE PLACE (TAKE MY MONEY)” with Billy BYBF in 2026, which places DEE ZEN in the same producer neighbourhood as the year’s Gwada releases.

SWAGLOKAL and the limits of the record

Spring 2026 brings DEE ZEN closer to Magbeatz, a producer already profiled on this site: “BANGA” came out on May 29 and “MÉLANINE” on June 26, both featuring Magbeatz. The two tracks open the album SWAGLOKAL, released on August 19, 2026 under 97TODIWORLD, eight tracks including “ROSE GOYAVE”, “GAZA”, “SWAG LOKAL” with Implaccable, “SOUND SYSTEM”, “MADRAS & FENTY” and “SPLEET”. The “GAZA” video, produced by Tito, came out on September 25, 2026 as an extract of the project.

What the record does not say, this profile says too. No biography, no interview and no official account were opened. Apart from “BOUYON”, no opened track page names the genre, and the labels on other releases are not taken here as facts. DEE ZEN’s bouyon tie therefore rests on one titled single, a part on a D.O.C FIX DEM track and a producer neighbourhood.

For listening, the shortest path runs through “BOUYON”, then “Tik Tak” and “MÉLANINE” with Magbeatz. Our bouyon playlist and our bouyon gwada page place these tracks in the island’s scene.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DEE ZEN?

DEE ZEN is an artist who releases under the DEE MONDE DEE VISION banner, then under the 97TODEEWORLD and 97TODIWORLD labels. He released the single “BOUYON” on June 11, 2025, the albums FROM in January 2026 and SWAGLOKAL in August 2026, and he appears on “Tik Tak” by D.O.C FIX DEM with Billy BYBF and DDK.

Where is DEE ZEN from?

No opened page gives a birthplace. His own publications claim a Guadeloupe anchoring: an Instagram account signed 971, singles titled “NEUFSEPT” and “+590”, the island’s prefix, and labels named around the 97. This profile presents those signals as anchoring, not as an attested origin.

What is DEE ZEN’s bouyon track?

“BOUYON”, released on June 11, 2025 on DEE MONDE DEE VISION, produced by Novysto and DEE ZEN, running 1 minute 51. The official video was posted on June 16, 2025 on his channel. Deezer files the single under Rap/Hip Hop, which shows the artist’s double belonging.

Who has DEE ZEN worked with?

With Novysto, producer of “BOUYON” and guest on “BBB”, with Atox on “MASS”, with Magbeatz on “BANGA” and “MÉLANINE”, with Implaccable on “SWAG LOKAL”, and with D.O.C FIX DEM, Billy BYBF and DDK on “Tik Tak”, released on February 6, 2026.

Where can I hear DEE ZEN?

Start with “BOUYON”, then “Tik Tak” by D.O.C FIX DEM and “MÉLANINE” with Magbeatz. Our bouyon playlist continues through the Gwada circuit.
