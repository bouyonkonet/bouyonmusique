Riko: seven years of bouyon with Team Bleudeu

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Riko is the most regular voice of Team Bleudeu, a collective whose YouTube channel has published bouyon since 2019, always under the same signature: the crew name, the year, and the word bouyon in the title. His first big hit was a group effort: “Bokit & T-Max”, with BaxX on a beat by Dj WeezZ, has passed 1.7 million views. Then came “Mytho” in 2021, “Pasé lang an mwen” in 2022, “Métrizé Tchad La” in 2023, “Gestu” produced by Dj Dirtee, “Zing Chap” with Team Bwè Tou Sa and Billy BYBF in 2025, and “Bodycount” in 2026. No biography was found: this profile follows the published discography.

Team Bleudeu and Bokit & T-Max

Team Bleudeu is first of all a channel and a crew. Its tracks pair several voices, with Riko, BaxX and La Mokerie at the front, and its pages repeat the same keywords: Team Bleudeu, Bouyon, Guadeloupe, Gwada. La Mokerie released “Bleudeu (Bouyon 2020)” and “Tap Tap (Bouyon 2K20)” there, BaxX titles such as “An wout” with Riko, and a group track, “Madanm an mwen”, brings together La Mokerie, Le Fred and Riko.

The track that made the crew known came out on June 15, 2019: “Bokit & T-Max”, by BaxX, Riko and Dj WeezZ, labelled Team Bleudeu, Bouyon 2019. Two minutes six, a beat credited to Dj WeezZ, direction by Skarry Cam, and two everyday Guadeloupe references in the title, the bokit sandwich and the T-Max scooter. The video has passed 1.7 million views, by far the highest figure in the catalogue.

From Mytho to Gestu: a yearly signature

Riko then released a dated title almost every year. “Mytho”, on March 25, 2021, is filed as Bouyon 2021, on a beat by LeMax, recorded at Darklion Production and directed by Thery Armani. “Pasé lang an mwen”, on September 1, 2022, with La Mokerie, is a bouyon 2022 warmup on a beat credited to Litleboy, recorded and mixed at VGM Product, and has passed 320,000 views.

2023 is the busiest year. “Oui vs Non”, with BMY, came out on April 7 on a production credited to Dj Guyguy. “Nou an chyen”, on May 19, brings together Riko, Lünik of Team Bwè Tou Sa and Billy BYBF, on a beat by Dj Leguste. “Métrizé Tchad La”, on August 12, carries the HeureDeVol1 hashtag, later reused on “Gestu”, and is close to 175,000 views. Finally “Gestu”, posted on December 21, 2023 and dated Bouyon 2024, is produced by Dj Dirtee. From “Nou an chyen” on, recording and mixing move to Gamma Lab, the studio also found behind Team Bwè Tou Sa.

Zing Chap and Bodycount: 2025 and 2026

On June 27, 2025, Team Bleudeu opened a new series, Banger, with “Zing Chap”: Riko, Team Bwè Tou Sa, Billy BYBF and Team Bodé, produced by Gamma and directed by Dylan Farah. The description announces more titles in the series and closes with the hashtags bouyonriddim, bouyonmusic, Guadeloupe and Bouyon2025. The Team Bwè Tou Sa profile on this site cites the same track in the same period.

On June 30, 2026 came “Bodycount”, by Riko with Kenro and Lebling, dated Bouyon 2026, on a production by Joshii G, with mixing and mastering at Gamma Lab and the hashtags Bouyon, 971 and FWI. Seven years after “Bokit & T-Max”, the method is the same: a crew, a loyal studio, guests from the same circle and the genre written in the title.

Limits of the file

No opened page gives a legal name, a birth date or a birthplace for Riko. The Guadeloupe anchoring comes from the constant Team Bleudeu keywords (Guadeloupe, Gwada, 971, FWI), and this profile presents it as anchoring. The credits Dj WeezZ, Litleboy and Dj Guyguy are quoted as published, without links to profiles on this site, since no page establishes identity.

For other crews on the same circuit, see Team Bwè Tou Sa, the bouyon gwada page and the list of Guadeloupe artists.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Riko?

Riko is a singer with Team Bleudeu, a Guadeloupe collective whose channel has published bouyon since 2019. He sings on Bokit & T-Max (2019), Mytho (2021), Métrizé Tchad La (2023), Gestu (2023), Zing Chap (2025) and Bodycount (2026). No public biography was found.

What is Riko's most viewed track?

Bokit & T-Max, by BaxX, Riko and Dj WeezZ, posted on June 15, 2019 under the label Team Bleudeu, Bouyon 2019, with more than 1.7 million views.

Who has Riko worked with?

With La Mokerie and BaxX in Team Bleudeu, with BMY, with Lünik of Team Bwè Tou Sa and Billy BYBF on Nou an chyen, with Team Bwè Tou Sa, Billy BYBF and Team Bodé on Zing Chap, and with Kenro and Lebling on Bodycount.

Which producers have worked with Riko?

The published credits name Dj WeezZ, LeMax, Litleboy, Dj Guyguy, Dj Leguste, Dj Dirtee, Gamma and Joshii G; the Gamma Lab studio has recorded and mixed most titles since 2023.

Where is Riko from?

No opened source gives his birthplace. The Team Bleudeu pages are all tagged Guadeloupe, Gwada, 971 or FWI: this profile presents Riko as anchored in Guadeloupe, without treating that as a civil record.
