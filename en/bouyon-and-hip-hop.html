Bouyon and hip-hop: rap, trap and drill in Dominica's sound

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Bouyon never listened only to the music of Dominica. From the start, it has taken the flows, the samples and the bass of hip-hop. In 2025, critics describe bouyon as a mix of trap, Southern hip-hop and electro, and Dominican artists name the greats of American rap as their teachers. What follows traces that bridge, from trap to bouyon drill.

Bouyon listens to hip-hop

The Road Fever compilation, reviewed by Resident Advisor in May 2025, is described as a breakneck compilation of high-voltage trap, drill, dancehall, konpa and kuduro instrumentals. Bouyon hovers at the 150 BPM mark there, and the review says bouyon in 2025 melds its long tradition of French Caribbean carnival music with trap, Southern hip-hop and even tinges of techno and electro.

The link is concrete in the tracks. On Road Fever, Ransum's Soca Panda Riddim interpolates the woozy synth riff from Desiigner's 2016 trap megahit Panda. Another track on the compilation could be mistaken for a 2000s Atlanta hip-hop instrumental, the kind Lil Jon or T-Pain would use. Like hip-hop beats, these riddims exist mostly for artists to lay vocals on top of.

Trilla-G, student of the rap greats

Trilla-G, born Gael Jno Baptiste in Goodwill, just outside Roseau, built his writing on American rap. His first local hit was a remix of Soulja Boy's Pretty Boy Swag. He flipped Magic System's Premiere Gaou into Sweet Talk in 2017, and he freestyled over TLC's No Scrubs, changing scrubs to pirat. He says it himself in his Billboard interview: he practised freestyling a lot because greats like Jay-Z and Lil Wayne say they do not write, and he wants to be like the greats.

His stated influences are Kanye West, his favourite artist of all time, and Drake, down to the way he puts an album together. His track Someone Else, released in summer 2024 with Quan and Litleboy, won soca collaboration of the year at the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, and his debut album Take Me as I Am was named Billboard's No. 4 Best Caribbean Album of 2025. Trilla-G was also Dominica's Calypso King in 2023, and he says calypso taught him the stage, diction and writing.

Rapatwa and bouyon drill

Tazzo Thaboss built the bridge on the production side. On his own YouTube channel, he presents Rapatwa, Rap Patois, as a rap movement he started in 2005, which uses the French Patois language of Dominica over hip-hop instrumentals. His track Glizzy Spray, released on 26 March 2021, is presented by himself as the first ever song labelled bouyon drill.

Bouyon drill is therefore a cross between Dominica's bouyon and UK and Chicago drill. The first-song label stays Tazzo's own claim, and this article reports it as such. Bouyon drill shows how the Dominican sound absorbs a style from elsewhere without losing its tempo or its Creole.

The bridge to the world

The bridge between bouyon and hip-hop also runs through the big stages. Major Lazer released G.O.A.T., with Bunji Garlin and America Foster, and a bouyon remix EP, G.O.A.T. Bouyon Mixes, on Mad Decent, with remixes from Precision Productions of Trinidad and Dadamanufakture of Dominica. Soca News notes that Billboard spotlighted the genre's growing global reach.

Bouyon has therefore gone from an island sound to a sound that talks with rap, trap and drill from all over the world. Dominican artists do not copy hip-hop: they take its tools, its flows and its bass, and lay them over the tempo and Creole of Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Has hip-hop influenced bouyon?

Yes, through sampling and flows. Resident Advisor describes bouyon in 2025 as a mix of trap, Southern hip-hop and electro, and Trilla-G names Jay-Z, Lil Wayne, Kanye West and Drake as direct influences.

What is bouyon drill?

A cross between Dominica's bouyon and UK and Chicago drill. According to Tazzo Thaboss himself, his track Glizzy Spray, released on 26 March 2021, is the first song labelled bouyon drill.

Which bouyon artists name American rap as an influence?

Trilla-G first of all, with Jay-Z, Lil Wayne, Kanye West and Drake, and beginnings that ran through remixes of Soulja Boy and TLC. Tazzo Thaboss built Rapatwa on hip-hop instrumentals from 2005.
