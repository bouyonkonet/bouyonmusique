Road Fever: when the bouyon riddim becomes the track

By Smokiller · Published October 26, 2026

In 2025, London label Soundway Records released Road Fever, a compilation of instrumental carnival riddims from Saint Lucia, Dominica and Guadeloupe. For once, the tracks built to carry voices are presented on their own, as pieces of music in their own right.

A compilation compiled by Rozaly

The official Soundway page presents the compilation as the work of Rozaly, an Amsterdam based, Curacao raised compiler. It came out on vinyl, digital and an exclusive cassette mixed by DJ Intl. Prostyle, with an IG reel formatted short film by Selwyn De Wind and Rozaly.

Bandcamp Daily made it Album of the Day on 12 May 2025, noting that the tracks are produced with laptops, sample packs and Fruity Loops.

14 riddims, three islands

The track listing published by retailers counts 14 riddims: Soca Panda by Ransum Records, Smash Or Pass by Viral Riddims, Bouyon Beam by Shazdown Mmw, On Di Road by DJ Demafidem, 2 Stroke by G6 Productions, Both Twanche Riddim Refix by Bad Sound and DJ Marfox, Dirty Lows by Veaygel Productions, Tic Tac Toe by Krome Productions, Daf by LMT Mafia, Heat Wave by Viral Riddims, Fire Ball by Veaygel Productions, Bad Weather Riddim Part 2 by Slaughter Arts Media, Walk It by Ransum Records and Midnight Fever by ScarnX.

Soundway places Dominica bouyon sound as increasingly influential, carried here by ScarnX, while Shazdown Mmw and DJ Demafidem represent the Guadeloupe based side.

150 BPM and over, carnival material

The Soundway presentation describes minimal productions at 150 plus BPM, built from FL percussion packs, DJ and vox samples and synth riffs, bridging trap, drill, dancehall, sped up Haitian kompa and Angolan kuduro.

These riddims are made for walls of speakers on carnival trucks and for bluetooth car speakers. Producer G6, the late trailblazer saluted in the compilation notes, is quoted there on the Dennery Segment: a sound that sparks your inside, one you cannot hear and not move.

What Road Fever changes for bouyon

Releasing the instrumentals on their own shifts the focus: the producer becomes the artist, and the riddim becomes the work. That is exactly the movement this site producer pages document from Dominica.

The compilation also gives Dominican bouyon a showcase on a reference London label, alongside Saint Lucia Dennery Segment. The profiles of ScarnX, Shazdown Mmw and DJ Demafidem on this site tell the verifiable part each plays in that story.

Frequently asked questions

What is Road Fever?

A compilation of instrumental carnival riddims released by Soundway Records in 2025, compiled by Rozaly, bringing together Dennery Segment and bouyon from Saint Lucia, Dominica and Guadeloupe.

Which bouyon producers are on Road Fever?

The published listing counts ScarnX with Midnight Fever, Shazdown Mmw with Bouyon Beam and DJ Demafidem with On Di Road, among 14 riddims.

How fast are the Road Fever riddims?

Soundway describes minimal productions at 150 plus BPM, made for carnival trucks and big speaker systems.
