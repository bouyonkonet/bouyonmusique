DPENG: Secret Bay with Soca Inferno, Do For Do, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DPENG publishes his music on his own YouTube channel, DPENG Official, and every title there carries the word bouyon with its year. The newest, “Secret Bay”, released on Christmas Day 2025 and billed Bouyon 2026, is sung with Soca Inferno, who also produced it. Before it, the channel lists “Lots Of Cash” and “I Would Have” in 2023, a run of duets with the artist Heidy from 2024, then “Move”, “Do For Do” and “Where Were U”. The credits of one of these tracks give his full name: David “DPeng” Pendenque. His pages present his music as music from Dominica, with the hashtags Dominica and Dominicamusic, but none gives a birthplace, so this profile keeps to that framing.

A catalogue published one track at a time

The DPENG Official channel works like a catalogue: one track, one video, one bouyon year in the title. “Lots Of Cash”, posted on September 10, 2023, is presented as an introduction to DPeng, with the genre line Bouyon Music and a writing and composing credit to David “DPeng” Pendenque, produced by Black World Entertainment. The same year “I Would Have” came out with a guest billed as “littleboy” in the title. This site has a profile of Litleboy LSBeats767, but DPENG’s page gives only a billing name, with no link to that artist, so the match stays a hypothesis, not a fact.

In 2025 DPENG released two solo tracks. “Move”, posted on February 15, 2025 and billed Bouyon 2025, was produced by Jackie beatz, recorded, according to the description, on the artist’s phone, then mixed and mastered by Soca Inferno. “Do For Do”, from May 30, 2025, was recorded at Blackworld Ent Studio, produced by B Money, with beats by FR3EZE. “Where Were U”, posted on October 21, 2025 and billed Bouyon 2026, tells, in its own description, a love story wrecked by a mother, which mother and daughter both end up regretting.

Secret Bay, with Soca Inferno

“Secret Bay” is the track that brought DPENG into this site’s research. Posted on December 25, 2025, running 2 minutes 29, it is billed Bouyon 2026. The published credits split the roles precisely: written by DPENG, sung by DPENG and Soca Inferno, chorus and backing vocals by Soca Inferno, recorded at Inferedsoundz Studio, produced by Soca Inferno, beats by Joshii. One hashtag names the riddim, Storybook riddim, and another files the track under music from Dominica.

The pairing is not a one off: Soca Inferno had already mixed and mastered “Move” in 2025. The Soca Inferno profile on this site covers his side of the record. The page does not comment on the choice of title, and this profile reads nothing into it.

The duets with Heidy, between bouyon and merengue

From 2024 to 2025 the channel posted several DPENG tracks with Heidy. “Why You Lying”, posted on June 24, 2024 and billed Bouyon 2024 for Antigua’s Carnival 2024, is presented as bringing a new culture into bouyon to unite two nations, with the flags of Dominica and the Dominican Republic. Another channel carries the same song under the bilingual title “Why You Lying (Porquè Mientes)”, billed bouyon x merengue 2024. “Make Me Cry”, from September 2024, is a tribute to a friend who died, and “Falls In Love”, from February 2025, credits DPENG and Heidy for writing and performance, with production by Littleboy, spelled as published.

The description of “Falls In Love” sums up the series: bouyon, it says, has brought two distant islands together by mixing two cultures. This site reports that line as the artist’s own. It says something real about how the genre travels far beyond Dominica, as our page on bouyon in Antigua also shows.

What the record does not say

None of the opened pages gives a date or place of birth, a biography, or a record label in the sense of a company: Black World Entertainment and Blackworld Ent Studio appear as recording and production credits. The name David Pendenque comes from a single credit, on “Lots Of Cash”. A 2023 track with littleboy, “Dca Fuck Up”, was only seen in a results listing because its page is age gated: it is named here and kept out of the structured data.

The verifiable record is therefore an artist channel followed since 2023, detailed credits and a clear thread: bouyon presented as Dominican, open to duets and neighbouring languages. New releases can be followed in our new bouyon 2026 page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DPENG?

DPENG is a bouyon artist who has published his tracks on his YouTube channel DPENG Official since 2023. A credit on “Lots Of Cash” gives his full name, David “DPeng” Pendenque. His newest tracks are “Secret Bay” with Soca Inferno and “Where Were U”.

Where is DPENG from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. His own pages present his music as music from Dominica, with the hashtags Dominica and Dominicamusic, and his channel name contains 767, the island’s area code. This site reports that framing without turning it into a biography.

Who produced Secret Bay?

According to the published credits, “Secret Bay” was produced by Soca Inferno, with beats by Joshii, recorded at Inferedsoundz Studio. DPENG wrote it and sings it with Soca Inferno, who also handles the chorus and backing vocals.

Who is Heidy on DPENG’s tracks?

Heidy is the featured artist on “Why You Lying”, “Make Me Cry” and “Falls In Love”. The pages present these duets as a meeting of Dominica and the Dominican Republic, and one version is billed bouyon x merengue. Heidy also has a profile on this site.

Which DPENG tracks are billed bouyon?

Every track named here: “Lots Of Cash” and “I Would Have” (2023), “Why You Lying” and “Make Me Cry” (2024), “Falls In Love”, “Move” and “Do For Do” (2025), “Where Were U” and “Secret Bay” (billed 2026).
