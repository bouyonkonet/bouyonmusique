Bouyon music: Dominica Independence Day 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 3, 2026

Today is the day. Dominica celebrates the 48th anniversary of its independence this Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the anniversary of independence on November 3, 1978, under the theme Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future, per the Office of the Prime Minister.

The morning: the grand parade at Windsor Park

The day opens with the grand Independence Parade at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, per Dominica News. The parade brings together the Commonwealth of Dominica Police Force, the Dominica Fire and Ambulance Services, the Sea Cadets, Scouts and Girl Guides, regional civil defence and community nursing association cadets.

After the parade, the Prime Minister delivers his national address to the country, per Dominica News. His message, published on November 2, called on Dominicans to reflect honestly on the work of nation building, 48 years after independence.

5:30 pm: the national ceremony and its 17 honours

The National Observance Ceremony begins at 5:30 pm at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, per the Office of the Prime Minister. Seventeen Dominicans receive national honours there for their contribution to national development, across four categories.

The Sisserou Award of Honour, the second highest national award, goes to Vivian Trotter for engineering, Dr Rhonda McIntyre for paediatric medicine, Janice Jean-Jacques Thomas for social security and Francis Stewart Paris for construction, per the official release of October 29.

The evening other categories

The Meritorious Service Award honours Johanna John-Bertrand for education and social security, Romualda B. Hyacinth for culture, Cedric J. Harris for sports, education and youth development, Colette Pauletta Prosper for disability rights, Rawle Leslie for agriculture and education, Colbert Pinard for youth development and Rosalind Adora Scotland Rabess for nursing, per the Office of the Prime Minister.

The Long Service Award honours McPierreson Eric Frampton, Florestine Anthea Lewis, Alice Laronde and Veda George. The Services Medal of Honour goes to Clayton Lawrence Jno Baptiste for public service and Ronald Jno Baptiste for community development.

A season that closes tomorrow

The 48th anniversary celebrations began on September 19 and close tomorrow, Wednesday November 4, with the National Day of Community Service, per the official programme. The World Creole Music Festival, held in late October in Roseau, sits inside this independence season: three nights of bouyon, cadence-lypso, zouk and soca at the heart of the national celebrations.

Frequently asked questions

What happens on November 3, 2026 in Dominica?

The country marks 48 years of independence: a grand parade in the morning at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, the Prime Minister national address, then the national ceremony at 5:30 pm with honours for 17 citizens, per the Office of the Prime Minister and Dominica News.

Who receives the Sisserou Award of Honour in 2026?

Vivian Trotter, Dr Rhonda McIntyre, Janice Jean-Jacques Thomas and Francis Stewart Paris, for engineering, paediatric medicine, social security and construction, per the official release of October 29, 2026.

When does the 2026 independence season end?

On Wednesday November 4, 2026, with the National Day of Community Service, the last day of the official programme that began on September 19, per the Office of the Prime Minister.
