V'ghn: Grenada's soca star on Take Me As I Am REMIX, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

V'ghn is a front rank soca voice, and he enters this site through two bouyon collaborations. WIPO Magazine presents him under his civil name, Jevaughn John, as Grenada's prince of soca, and recalls that he was the first Grenadian to place in the top three of the International Soca Monarch in Trinidad. His own Deezer discography, seventy one releases, never carries the word bouyon. His Dominican partners' pages do: the “Take Me As I Am” remix with Trilla-G, labelled Bouyon 2026, and “Eye Spy (BOUYON 2026)” with Klassik Frescobar and DJ Kalli.

A documented soca career

The WIPO Magazine article, published when V'ghn became the organization's intellectual property youth ambassador for Grenada in April 2025, gives the core facts: six years of training at the Spices Dance Company in Gouyave, a first solo recognition at Grenada's National Soca Monarch in 2013, at 16, then a top three place at the International Soca Monarch in Trinidad with “Trouble in the Morning”, in the Groovy category, a first for a Grenadian artist according to the same source. The article does not state his birthplace: this profile keeps to the Grenadian anchoring WIPO gives him.

His Deezer page confirms a large soca catalogue, from “Jab Decisions” and “Jabaholic” to “Soca Nice” and “Calypso”. The title “Explore”, on the Alien Head Riddim produced by Travis World in 2024, is presented by the JulianspromosTV channel as soca, with no bouyon mention: it is named here to place the artist, not as a bouyon title.

Take Me As I Am REMIX, with Trilla-G

On October 12, 2025, the Trilla-G channel posted the visualizer for “Take Me As I Am REMIX”, titled [Bouyon 2026], with the Bouyon hashtag in the description. The credits are complete: performed by Trilla-G and V'ghn, written by Gael Jno Baptiste and Je’vaughn John, produced by Andel “Fr3EzE” Horsford, with additional production, recording and engineering by Dernel “Dernz” Green. The Je’vaughn John writing credit matches the civil name given by WIPO. On Deezer, the title came out on October 11, 2025 as “Take Me As I Am (feat. V'ghn) (Remix)”.

This remix is a bridge between two scenes: a Grenadian soca artist on a production by the bouyon team of Fr3EzE and Dernz. The description also announces Trilla-G's “Take Me As I Am: The Album” for an October 22. For context on the genre, see our page bouyon soca, a term coined by Dada in Dominica in 2003.

Eye Spy, with DJ Kalli and Klassik Frescobar

On January 16, 2026, the official Klassik Frescobar channel posted “EYE SPY (BOUYON 2026)”, billed to DJ Kalli, Klassik Frescobar and V'GHN. The description credits the writing to Klassik Frescobar and V'GHN, the production to Dj Kalli and Boogy Rankss, and the video tags name bouyon, Dominica, Martinique, Guadeloupe and Grenada. Deezer lists the title “Eye Spy” on the same date, on Codeway, with all three names as contributors.

The lyrics published on the page travel through Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique: a circuit song, written to move from one island to the next. With the Trilla-G remix, it is the second title where V'ghn's name sits under a bouyon label, and the only two entered in the structured data of this page. Frequently asked questions

Who is V'ghn?

Jevaughn John, known as V'ghn, a soca artist, songwriter and producer presented by WIPO Magazine as Grenada's prince of soca. He was the first Grenadian to place in the top three of the International Soca Monarch, with “Trouble in the Morning”.

Does V'ghn make bouyon?

His own discography is soca. He does sing on two titles labelled bouyon by their primary pages: “Take Me As I Am REMIX” with Trilla-G (Bouyon 2026) and “Eye Spy (BOUYON 2026)” with DJ Kalli and Klassik Frescobar.

Where is V'ghn from?

WIPO Magazine presents him as an artist from Grenada, trained in Gouyave and named WIPO youth ambassador for Grenada in 2025. The article does not give his birthplace.

Who produced the Take Me As I Am remix?

Andel “Fr3EzE” Horsford, with additional production, recording and engineering by Dernel “Dernz” Green. The writing is credited to Gael Jno Baptiste and Je’vaughn John.

Is Explore a bouyon title?

No, not according to the opened page: “Explore”, with Travis World on the Alien Head Riddim, is presented as soca. It does not enter the structured data of this page.
