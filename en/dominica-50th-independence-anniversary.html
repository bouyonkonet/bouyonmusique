Bouyon music: Dominica prepares for 50 years of independence

By Smokiller · Published November 4, 2026

The 48th anniversary season has barely closed and Dominica is already looking two years ahead. In his national address on the evening of Tuesday November 3, Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit announced a bipartisan national committee, to be established in January, to prepare the country 50th anniversary of independence, per the Caribbean Media Corporation wire carried by CANA News.

A bipartisan committee from January

The committee will draw participation from across the country political and civic life, building on the experience of its elders, the ideas of its young people and the contributions of Dominicans at home and abroad, per the Prime Minister. Its work will shape a national programme to honour the journey, reflect honestly on progress and identify shared priorities for the years ahead.

Skerrit compared the milestone to a cricket match in which the batting team needs two more runs to reach the half century. Completing both runs requires one condition above all, he said: unwavering unity, extending beyond the island shores to the Dominican diaspora, whose distance has never cancelled their citizenship or diminished their belonging.

The question put to every Dominican

Before reaching fifty, the Prime Minister asked every Dominican how they will contribute. If you serve the public, remove one delay. If you own a business, open a door to a young person. If you farm, fish or practise a trade, pass on what expertise you possess. If you are a parent or teacher, help a child discover a gift, he said in the address.

The same message closed the 2026 season: Skerrit urged Dominicans to use Wednesday November 4, the National Day of Community Service, for a project that benefits the island. Choose a river, a roadside, a playing field or a neighbour in need, and leave one corner of the Nature Isle better than you found it, he said, adding that nation building is not a spectator sport.

Between now and 2028: the airport and the big projects

Between now and fifty, the government aims to strengthen the electricity grid towards fully renewable energy by 2030, keep building homes, deliver titles to the families who live in them and prepare communities for the opening of the international airport, per the address.

The international airport is the largest project the country has ever undertaken: it employs more than 400 Dominicans, is on schedule for completion in the first quarter of 2028 and is being built without a loan that the country children will have to repay, per the Prime Minister. Investors have also shown interest in the cruise facilities, cargo port and ferry terminal: consultations were directed by November 18, and a delegation from the United States International Development Finance Corporation is expected this month after talks in Washington.

A music season on the road to the big date

The 50th anniversary will be celebrated on November 3, 2028, fifty years to the day after independence on November 3, 1978. The road there runs through the independence seasons of 2027 and 2028, with the World Creole Music Festival in Roseau as the central music appointment: three nights of bouyon, cadence-lypso, zouk and soca at Windsor Park.
