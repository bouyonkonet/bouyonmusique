Trilla-G and Shelly: the portraits by Reisha Felix-Harry

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Dominican artist Reisha Felix-Harry has unveiled a new portrait series celebrating Dominican culture today. The named subjects are Trilla-G and Shelly, two faces of bouyon in Dominica. The series follows a major public art showcase of her work on Durham Region transit in Canada, described as a first major public art presentation of her work in that country.

Two portraits, two faces of Dominican bouyon

Trilla-G is one of the voices of bouyon in Dominica, a former Calypso Monarch, crowned in 2023, and now qualified for the quarter-finals of Dominica calypso 2027. Shelly is the voice of Signal Band, one of the bouyon bands of Dominica. The two portraits place bouyon at the centre of a celebration of contemporary Dominican culture, through portraiture, not through the stage.

The series was unveiled on 10 November 2026, according to Dominica 1. The paper says the portraits celebrate contemporary Dominican culture. No other subject is named in the reporting opened for this page, so none is invented here.

After transit in Canada

Before these portraits, the work of Reisha Felix-Harry was the subject of a major public art showcase on Durham Region transit in Canada. The Dominica 1 report says it plainly: the full details of that Canadian display, dates, sizes and sponsors, are not available in the reporting. This page does not invent them.

The basis of this artist profile is a profile published by Dominica News Online, presenting the artist work in Canada. The portrait series of Trilla-G and Shelly is the new chapter of that work, back in Dominica through its subjects.
