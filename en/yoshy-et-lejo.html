Yoshy and Lejo: Chut and Holiday, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Yoshy and Lejo are two voices that the published credits never separate: on every opened page their names arrive together, and the release listing for “CHUT” even carries the pair as a name in its own right, YOSHY X LEJO. This site therefore gives them one page instead of two identical profiles. Their file holds two titles stated as bouyon, both released by Jixels: “HOLIDAY”, with Le Cus, in February 2026, and “CHUT”, in June 2026, whose release version was past 646,000 views when checked.

HOLIDAY, the entry with Le Cus

The duo’s first trace is a riddim. “INDECISION RIDDIM”, posted on January 2, 2026 on the Jixels channel, brings two titles together: “HOLIDAY”, by Le Cus, Yoshy and Lejo on a DJ Skylight production mixed by Yas Beats, and “BRING ME”, by Jixels himself. That page does not use the word bouyon. The “HOLIDAY” video, posted on February 12, 2026, does: it carries #BOUYON in its title and credits Skylight on production, Yas Beats on mix and recording, Middle Vision on direction and Jixels as executive producer.

It is the usual bouyon riddim pattern: one instrumental, here filed under the name Indecision, carries several voices, and each voice can then live its own life as a video.

CHUT, the title that carries the duo

“CHUT” is their own title. The release version, distributed by DistroKid on the JX PRODZ label, is dated June 26, 2026 and runs 2 minutes 16; it credits Jixels as main artist, Yoshy and Lejo as guests, then the pair YOSHY X LEJO and the team DJ Skylight and Naoyah. The official video followed the next day, June 27, 2026, with #bouyon in the title, production by DJ Skylight and Naoyah, mix, studio and executive production by Jixels, and direction by JX Vision.

The counts recorded during this research, 646,228 views for the release version and 167,361 for the video, make “CHUT” one of the most played titles in the recent Jixels catalogue. Those numbers are snapshots on the day, not chart positions.

What this profile does not do

It does not mix up names. The same searches return Yo Joshy, YoJoshii and MC YOSHI: no opened page links any of them to the Yoshy of this duo, so this profile does not merge them. It does not give an origin either: no page names an island, a town or a legal name for Yoshy or for Lejo. The scene context is covered on our page about bouyon in Guadeloupe, without any source tying the duo to it.

If either of them releases solo one day, with a page that states the genre, this site can give that artist a page of their own. Until then: one page, two voices, two verified bouyon titles.

Frequently asked questions

Who are Yoshy and Lejo?

Two bouyon voices always credited together, as YOSHY X LEJO, on releases from the Jixels channel. Their titles stated as bouyon are HOLIDAY (with Le Cus) and CHUT.

Why one page for two artists?

Because no opened page shows them apart: every credit pairs them, and the CHUT listing carries the pair as a name. Two profiles would have said exactly the same thing.

Who produced CHUT?

DJ Skylight and Naoyah on production, Jixels on mix, studio and executive production, JX Vision directing the video. The release is on the JX PRODZ label, June 26, 2026.

Is Yoshy the same as Yo Joshy or YoJoshii?

Nothing says so. They are different names, and no opened page links them to this duo.

Where are they from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns them no island.
