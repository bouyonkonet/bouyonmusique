Bouyon gwada: D0 Production, the riddim machine

By Smokiller · Published October 25, 2026

D0 Production is a name, not a face: a bouyon production banner placed in Les Abymes, Guadeloupe, by the Viberate analytics page. Between 2022 and 2025, its catalog feeds a large part of the French speaking wave of the genre. This page explains how the machine works, from credits opened one by one.

A name that works three ways

D0 Production, with a zero in place of the O, follows the spelling of the studio banners of the Gwada bouyon wave. The name works three ways at once: lead artist on its own singles and riddims, production mention on the records of others, and signature behind tracks carried by known voices of the scene.

Every verified record in the catalog is a bouyon record, in French and Guadeloupean Creole, released between 2022 and 2025, at dance tempos sitting mostly at 160 BPM. The circle is that of Les Abymes and mainland Guadeloupe: Tiitii NBA as the central partner, Totally Spices, 1T1, Shanika, DJ Softee and others.

2022, entering through the riddim

The first verified trace of the name is a riddim: on 1 January 2022, Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022, by D0 Production featuring 1T1, was released on 2873529 Records DK, the digital distribution label that would carry most of the banner lead releases.

The format is significant: a riddim is an instrumental matrix built to carry several voices and to circulate among DJs. By entering through a dated riddim, the banner presents itself as a supplier of material for the scene before presenting itself as a poster name.

Daryl Clarisse, the visible core of the credits

Read the catalog pages one after another, and one name returns: Daryl Clarisse, also written Daryl Noah Clarisse. On Crescendo Bouyon Riddim 2022, he is a songwriter alongside Terry Baptiste. On Chien, he is composer and in charge of programming, while D0 Production is credited as producer.

On Asi Mwen, in 2025, the Shazam credits list Clarisse Daryl as songwriter, mixing engineer and producer, with D0 Production as producer in the same section. The conclusion the sources allow is measured: Daryl Clarisse is the visible writing core of the catalog, repeated across at least eight opened pages. No opened source states in one sentence that D0 Production is Daryl Clarisse, and this page does not say it either.

The partnership with Tiitii NBA

Around the core gravitates a workshop: Terry Capitolin, the writer name of Tiitii NBA, signs the lyrics of most records of the partnership, and Terry Baptiste, the writer name of 1T1, co-signs the founding riddim of 2022. Carcheline Cereme writes and sings Chien and Bouey Bay for Totally Spices.

The full profile of the banner, with its catalog verified track by track, is on the D0 Production page of this site. This page keeps the mechanics: one banner, one writing core, one network of voices, and riddims built to circulate.
