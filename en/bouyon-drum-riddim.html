Bouyon Drum Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Bouyon Drum Riddim is a riddim recorded, produced, mixed and mastered by DJ Jeanie at Digital Empire Studio. Four vocal tracks went up on the DJ JEANIE YouTube channel on July 6, 2021, followed by a Deezer album on July 9. The videos bill it as 2021 soca, with bouyon right at the front of the riddim's name.

A DJ Jeanie riddim, made start to finish at Digital Empire

The four YouTube descriptions say it in one line: the Bouyon Drum Riddim was recorded, produced, mixed and mastered by DJ Jeanie at Digital Empire Studio. No other producer or player is credited. The same channel carries other DJ Jeanie riddims under the Digital Empire banner, such as the Money Counter Riddim, the Simma Riddim and the Screw Tip Riddim, the last one tagged "2023 Soca".

Deezer files the "Bouyon Drum Riddim" album under "Varios Artistas", on the DJ Jeanie label, dated July 9, 2021. It has just four tracks, each 2 minutes 57, and no instrumental. The sources we read say nothing about which island the producer or the singers come from.

Four voices, each with its own writer

Per the credits, every singer wrote their own song. Kilogram, real name Keon Romanno Samuel, sings "Mischief". Scarbz, credited as Tracy Cozier, sings "Wikkid". Jace, Lee Jace Bullock, wrote "Ease Up (Outside)", and Tandre, Joshwayne Jason Gaymes, sings "Start It".

Don't confuse it with a bouyon drum kit, the set of drum sounds producers gather to build their tracks, which our bouyon drum kit page covers. Here, Bouyon Drum Riddim is the name of one specific project with four songs on it.

Mischief out front, and a soca label

When we checked on October 28, 2026, Kilogram's "Mischief" had passed 12,700 views on DJ Jeanie's channel. The other three sit close together, between 4,700 and 5,400: "Wikkid", then "Ease Up (Outside)" and "Start It".

The riddim carries bouyon in its name, yet every video is filed under "2021 Soca". That is the overlap our bouyon vs soca page tries to untangle. For how a shared riddim works, see also our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Bouyon Drum Riddim?

DJ Jeanie, who also recorded, mixed and mastered it at Digital Empire Studio, according to the YouTube descriptions.

When did it come out?

The four videos have been up since July 6, 2021; the Deezer album is dated July 9, 2021.

Who sings on the Bouyon Drum Riddim?

Kilogram (Mischief), Scarbz (Wikkid), Jace (Ease Up (Outside)) and Tandre (Start It).

Is it bouyon or soca?

The riddim's name says bouyon, the video titles say "2021 Soca". Both labels come from the same source.

Is it a drum kit?

No. It is a riddim voiced by four artists, not a pack of drum sounds.
