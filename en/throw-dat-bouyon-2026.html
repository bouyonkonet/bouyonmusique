Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026): Klass Money, produced by Smokiller

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Throw Dat is a Klass Money track released in 2026, carried in the site record as Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026). The official credit block for the track, as YouTube publishes it, gives production to Smokiller and composition to Sean Smokiller Francis. This page sticks to the verified credits, with no invented biography added.

The verified credits for the track

The block provided to YouTube by Route Change Music Group reads: Throw Dat, Klass Money, copyright 2026 Route Change Music Group, released February 13, 2026, producer Smokiller, composer lyricist Jefferson Scotland, composer Sean Francis, publishers ASCAP and BMI.

Those lines are the whole factual base of this page: one track, one artist, one producer, two writing names and one label, with a date. The rest is a reading of the track inside the Smokiller bouyon catalogue.

Klass Money in the Smokiller bouyon circle

Klass Money is not an isolated name in the Smokiller work: he is among the collaborators on the Wrecketeng revival album Smokiller released in December 2025, alongside DJ Cut and Charlie Black, according to the site verified record.

Throw Dat extends that circle in 2026, this time with Smokiller producing a solo Klass Money track, rather than featuring on a collective project.

Why this track matters for bouyon 2026

A bouyon track produced by a Dominican-born producer, released by a label in February 2026, with ASCAP and BMI publishing credits shown, shows bouyon in current production mode, not only in remix mode or festival mode.

For listening, the track sits alongside the other recent productions documented on this site, like the Coi Leray and Shenseea flips, in the Bouyon Road Mix playlist and the bouyon songs pages for 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced Throw Dat by Klass Money?

Smokiller, according to the credit block provided to YouTube by Route Change Music Group. The same block credits Sean Francis as composer and Jefferson Scotland as composer lyricist.

When was Throw Dat released?

On February 13, 2026, on Route Change Music Group, according to the official credit block for the track on YouTube.

What is the link between Klass Money and Smokiller?

Klass Money is a collaborator in the Smokiller circle: he appears on the December 2025 Wrecketeng revival album, and Smokiller produces his Throw Dat track in 2026.
