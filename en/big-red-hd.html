Big Red HD: Grenada jab, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Big Red HD, Jason Phillip according to the credits of his titles, is a singer and producer whose pages bill Grenada, soca and jab: “Jab Army (Soca 2019) Grenada”, “Spirit of Busta (Grenada Soca 2026)”, Spicemas keywords. Since autumn 2024 he has also signed bouyon titles: “Bend Down” and “Bouyon Freestyle”, billed Bouyon 2025, then “Put Something On Me” with ShaSha, billed Bouyon 2026. His Deezer page lists 40 releases, from 2016 to “Dem”, released on October 8, 2026 on his Big Red Productions label.

Bouyon Freestyle and Bend Down: the 2025 season

On October 20, 2024, the Big Red Productions Official channel published “Bend Down” with the tag #Bouyon2025: written and performed by Big Red HD, produced by Big Red Productions, recorded at Rebel 1 Studios, executive producer Ace Management & Entertainment. On November 17 came “Bouyon Freestyle (Visualizer) #Bouyon2025”, produced by DJ Flip Tha Boss and Maskman, mixed and mastered by Big Red Productions; its keywords line up bouyon, Grenada, Jab jab and experience spicemas.

On Deezer, “Bouyon Freestyle” is a single dated November 17, 2024, label ACE Management & Entertainment, 3 minutes 03. On December 31, 2024, “Waist” completed the run, billed “grenada soca 2025 | #bouyon”, with Kerick Roop on background vocals and artwork by Deano Moss. In three titles, the jab artist wrote the name of the Dominican genre into his own billings.

Put Something On Me with ShaSha, billed Bouyon 2026

On May 21, 2026, “ShaSha x Big Red HD - Put Something On Me (Bouyon 2026)” was uploaded, more than 10,000 views when read. The page gives the full credits: performed by Sharisse “ShaSha” Fabien and Jason “Big Red HD” Phillip, written by Donavan “Donsi” Fabien and Jason Phillip, produced by Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt, mixed and mastered by Judah “Diyoute” Jno. Baptiste, executive producer DFinite Music.

Diyoute's name on the mix ties this title to the bouyon circuit this site documents elsewhere, from Mr Gwada to AC Tafari. ShaSha, the other voice on the title, has no profile on this site yet. For the context of the exchanges between Grenadian jab and bouyon, see our page on bouyon soca.

Ten years of releases, from Feeling Ah Vybz to So Sick Riddim

Big Red HD's Deezer artist page lists 40 releases. The oldest, “Feeling Ah Vybz (No Chaser Riddim)”, is dated October 16, 2016; the most recent, “Dem”, October 8, 2026, on Big Red Productions. In between: “Rising” (2018), written with Fabian Worrell, produced by Minim for Maveriq Records, recorded at King Heart Beat Studio in Brooklyn, mixed at Casa 22 Studio in Barbados and mastered by Will Quinnell at Sterling Sound in New York; “Jab Army (Soca 2019) Grenada”, whose page credits “bouyon instruments” to Fr3EzE Production; the EP “Free Up” (2020); and for 2026, “Give Her Good” on the Bang Head Riddim, “Spirit of Busta”, produced with Xpert Productions, and “Back Work”, all billed Grenada Soca 2026.

Big Red HD is also a producer: the EP “HD Link-Up” (January 7, 2026) and the album “So Sick Riddim” (September 4, 2026), with Cubanis, Dellz and Skenguh, came out on Big Red Productions. This profile is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026. None of them spells out where he was born: this site places him in the Grenadian scene his billings name, without stating a birthplace.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Big Red HD?

Jason Phillip, a singer and producer whose pages bill Grenada soca and jab, maker of Bouyon Freestyle and Bend Down (Bouyon 2025) and of Put Something On Me with ShaSha (Bouyon 2026).

What are his bouyon titles?

Bend Down (October 20, 2024) and Bouyon Freestyle (November 17, 2024), billed Bouyon 2025, Waist (December 31, 2024, grenada soca 2025, #bouyon) and Put Something On Me with ShaSha (May 21, 2026, Bouyon 2026).

Who produced Put Something On Me?

Reyshaun Hxnter Hunt, with mixing and mastering by Judah Diyoute Jno. Baptiste and executive production by DFinite Music, according to the title's YouTube page.

How many releases does Big Red HD have?

40 on his Deezer page, from Feeling Ah Vybz (2016) to Dem (October 8, 2026), including the EP HD Link-Up and the album So Sick Riddim (2026) on his Big Red Productions label.

Is Big Red HD from Grenada?

His pages bill Grenada soca, Jab Army and Spicemas; none states his birthplace. This profile places him in the Grenadian scene without stating an origin.
