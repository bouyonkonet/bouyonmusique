Heidy

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Heidy comes into bouyon through the Spanish language. On DPENG’s channel the name first appears as a feature on three tracks from 2024 and 2025, “Why You Lying”, “Make Me Cry” and “Falls In Love”, presented as a meeting of two countries. Then, in July 2025, the same channel posted a track under Heidy’s name alone: a cover of “Moriré Bebiendó”, composed by Teodoro Reyes, billed BOUYON 2025, with credits written in Spanish. No opened page gives a biography or an origin for Heidy.

A Teodoro Reyes cover as bouyon

“Moriré Bebiendó”, posted on July 30, 2025 on DPENG’s channel, running 2 minutes 55, is the only track in the record credited to Heidy as lead. The credits, written in Spanish, are clear: composition by Teodoro Reyes, performance and chorus by HEIDY, production, recording, mixing and mastering at WLPM Music Studio, beats by LaBprod. The title carries the label BOUYON 2025, and the hashtags tie together Spanish music, Dominica and bouyon.

The page presents the track as a version of Teodoro Reyes’ song. This site does not comment on the original beyond that credit: what matters here is the move itself, a song from the Spanish language repertoire carried over a rhythm billed as bouyon.

The duets with DPENG

The first track is “Why You Lying”, posted on June 24, 2024 on DPENG’s channel, billed Bouyon 2024 and tied to Antigua’s Carnival 2024. The page speaks of bringing a new culture into bouyon to unite two nations, with the flags of Dominica and the Dominican Republic. Another channel carries the same song as “Why You Lying (Porquè Mientes)”, billed bouyon x merengue 2024, with a Spanish caption about uniting two countries.

Then come “Make Me Cry”, in September 2024, a tribute to a friend who died, with the flags of the Dominican Republic, Dominica and Antigua and Barbuda, and “Falls In Love”, in February 2025. The latter credits DPENG and Heidy for writing and performance, Heidy on the chorus, production by Littleboy, spelled as published, and mixing by B Money.

What the record does not say

Every opened page sits on DPENG’s channel or on a third party channel: no channel, official account or biography under Heidy’s name was found. The flags and the use of Spanish describe how the tracks are framed, not an origin, so this site assigns Heidy no country.

The record comes down to four tracks billed bouyon between 2024 and 2025, one of them as lead. It shows a real side of the genre: bouyon open to other languages and other rhythms, here merengue, as other pages on this site show for bouyon in Antigua.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Heidy?

Heidy is the voice on DPENG’s duets “Why You Lying”, “Make Me Cry” and “Falls In Love”, and the performer of a bouyon cover of Teodoro Reyes’ “Moriré Bebiendó”, posted in 2025 on DPENG’s channel. No public biography was found.

Which bouyon track has Heidy as lead?

“Moriré Bebiendó”, billed BOUYON 2025, composed by Teodoro Reyes, performance and chorus by HEIDY, produced at WLPM Music Studio with beats by LaBprod.

Where is Heidy from?

No opened page says. The tracks show the flags of Dominica and the Dominican Republic and credits in Spanish, but this site does not turn those signals into an origin.

Why bouyon and merengue?

Because one version of “Why You Lying”, posted as “Why You Lying (Porquè Mientes)”, is billed bouyon x merengue 2024. That is what the page says, not an analysis by this site.
