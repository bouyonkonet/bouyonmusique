Maurane Voyer: Martinique sings bouyon

Maurane Voyer is a Martinican singer. In 2026, she lends her voice to Joli BB, the track by the Dominican Reo, one of the most noticed bouyon collaborations between Martinique and Dominica.

A collaboration that makes sense

Joli BB brings together two shores of the same Creole world: Dominica, which created bouyon, and Martinique, which dances to it under the name jump up. The Reo and Maurane Voyer collaboration shows that modern bouyon is built as a network, island by island.

Bouyon in the feminine

Female voices count more and more in modern bouyon, as singers, songwriters and headliners. Maurane Voyer is the example of that on the Martinique side, on a track written notably with Dario Yankey and Nicolas Lanclume, released on the Mauve label.
