Supalyne: Dominica dancehall artist and his bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Supalyne, real name Shain Alexander, was born in Fond Cole, Dominica, and grew up in Marigot according to RichesKarayib. He presents himself first as a dancehall artist, but Caribbean Life also counts bouyon in his blend. Between February 2022 and February 2023 he put out a run of songs billed as bouyon, including “Burna Boy” on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim.

Fond Cole, Marigot and dancehall

In October 2024, Caribbean Life wrote that he was “born Shain Alexander in Fond Cole” and blends dancehall, reggae, hip hop and bouyon. The spark, the article says, goes back to childhood, watching Chris Brown's “Run It” on BET. His aim is plain: to become Dominica's first international dancehall star.

RichesKarayib added in April 2025 that he grew up in Marigot and named his producers: Cecil “thaWizzard” Joseph, Dylan “Producer Dlo” Lowe and the Jamaican Countree Hype. His first stage was Color Me More in 2019. Promoter Jael Joseph, quoted by Caribbean Life, says he has shared bills with Skeng, Jahmiel and 450 at the World Creole Music Festival and Carnival Gopwell.

The bouyon run, from Backshot to IDK Why

On February 24, 2022, DjeasyPromos posted “Bandé” by Trixz Migos and Supalyne, billed “Bouyon 2022”. Two days later Deezer dates “Backshot”, with Trixz Migos and Kenny Gee, which his channel also bills “Bouyon 2022”. Early 2023 sped things up: “2 Face” with Jem Jem on January 27, then “Wet” with Dr. Eevil on the 30th, produced by ThaWizZarD and mixed by Diyoute.

On February 3, 2023, DJ Taffy uploaded “Burna Boy” on the Funny Riddim, written and sung by Supalyne with Trixx and Gwada G. It is his most watched bouyon song, past 102,000 plays. Ten days later came “IDK Why” with Gabrielle, which he wrote alone, produced by Hilaire Beatz and Bryce Beatz at Yw3 Studios.

Bounce, Wicked Tight and what came next

After that run, his releases no longer carry the bouyon tag and lean toward dancehall. In 2023 he released “Pain” and “Up Is Up” with Young Dinero, then “Nokia 3310” with ProducerDlo in June 2024. Back in 2021 he had already sung “One More Dance” with Nikki Dee on the Mimosa Loca Riddim, posted by ProducerDlo's channel.

In late 2024, “Bounce”, with $hyanne, became his biggest video, close to 400,000 views. “Wicked Tight”, whose video came out on Valentine's Day 2025, has passed 140,000. On January 1, 2026 he opened the year with “Resolute”, subtitled “Level Up Freestyle”.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Supalyne?

Shain Alexander, a dancehall artist from Dominica, born in Fond Cole and raised in Marigot, who also mixes in reggae, hip hop and bouyon according to Caribbean Life.

What are his bouyon songs?

Bandé with Trixz Migos and Backshot (2022), then 2 Face with Jem Jem, Wet with Dr. Eevil, Burna Boy with Trixx and Gwada G, and IDK Why with Gabrielle (2023).

Which riddim is Burna Boy on?

DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim, posted on February 3, 2023 on the producer's channel, with over 102,000 views.

Which producers does he work with?

RichesKarayib names Cecil “thaWizzard” Joseph, Dylan “Producer Dlo” Lowe and Countree Hype; the credits add DJ Taffy, Hilaire Beatz, Bryce Beatz and Diyoute on the mix.

What is his most watched song?

Bounce, with $hyanne, close to 400,000 views on YouTube.
