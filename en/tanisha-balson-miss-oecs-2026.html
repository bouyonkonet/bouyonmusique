Bouyon music: Tanisha Balson, Miss OECS 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026

The regional crown stays in Dominica. Tanisha Balson, Miss Dominica 2025, has been crowned Miss OECS 2026, receiving the title from outgoing queen Kiana Dyer, also Dominican, according to the Saint Lucia Ministry of Home Affairs release reporting the pageant.

A crown that stays in Dominica

The Miss OECS Pageant is hosted by the OECS Pageant Committee in partnership with the OECS Commission, and it celebrates the leadership and unity of young women across the Eastern Caribbean, according to the release. This year the theme was Crowning Radiance, Embracing Unity.

At the end of the evening, Tanisha Balson of Dominica emerged as the winner and kept the crown on Dominican soil. She received it from Kiana Dyer, the outgoing Miss OECS, also of Dominica, who took her final walk before officially passing on the title. OECS Communications Specialist Jacques Hinkson-Compton noted that the pageant highlighted the collective brilliance and leadership of young women across the region.

Five territories, five judged segments

The delegates came from Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, the British Virgin Islands, Dominica, and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. They competed in five judged segments: creative national wear, performing talent, swimwear, evening gown, and question and answer.

The competition aims to promote leadership, cultural pride and regional unity among the young women of the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States. In that frame, Balson win extends a 2026 season already rich for Dominica, between the Miss Dominica pageant staged under a bouyon theme and the wob dwiyet national dress pageants.

From Miss Dominica 2025 to Miss OECS 2026

Tanisha Balson had won the Miss Dominica 2025 crown on Thursday, February 27, 2025, at Carnival City, on the Windsor Park Sports Stadium Forecourt in Roseau, beating four other contestants, according to Writeups 24. She took six titles in the same evening: Miss Photogenic, Best National Spectacular Wear, Best in National Spectacular Wear, Best Talent, Best Evening Wear and Best in Evening Wear.

Aliyah Jean Jacques finished first runner-up and Meeya Francis second runner-up. After her win, Balson said she wanted to inspire the young girls of her community and her country to show their talent and elegance, and she spoke of her art as a way to tell stories of joy, resilience and freedom. Music, she said, is a universal language that connects her to her roots, especially at carnival.

A pageant season that honoured Dominica

Balson win adds to the other Dominican crowns of 2026, already covered on this site. Dainisha Eusebe of Grand Bay was crowned Miss Dominica 2026 on February 13, in a pageant staged under the theme Bouyon Bloom, a tribute to bouyon music and the artists who shaped its cultural influence.

The same month of October saw Samantha Bazil of Wesley win Miss Wob Dwiyet 2026, then Hazel-Ann Anthony of Mahaut crowned Madam Wob Dwiyet 2026. The Miss OECS 2026 crown therefore completes a year in which Dominica shone in the region pageants, with Creole culture and bouyon music as the backdrop.
