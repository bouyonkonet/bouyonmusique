LMS: from F*ck it with Lucky Lukee to Siwo

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

LMS is a singer anchored in Guadeloupe who writes his own lyrics and often makes his own instrumentals. His most visible track is a duet: “F*ck it”, with Lucky Lukee, composed by DJ Skycee and Qwann, recorded by Magbeatz and released in March 2026 under the bouyonmusic tag. On his own channel, “PRESSURE” and “Siwo” carry the same tag, with Guadeloupe as a keyword, and “Ochanterie”, on Lawcy Music, is filed as bouyon. This profile sticks to those pages: no biography was found.

F*ck it and Friday Night: the Lucky Lukee circle

On March 6, 2026, the Lucky 11 channel posted the video for “F*ck it”, 2 minutes 02, with two credited artists, Lucky and Lms. The listing details the team: composition by DJ Skycee and Qwann, direction by sck music prod, editing by Mighty Production, recording at Magbeatz, mixing by jordanomix. It also gives LMS's accounts, under the names lms_97s and lms97s, and closes with the bouyonmusic hashtag. That is the page that brings LMS onto this site.

The link with Lucky Lukee goes on. On August 21, 2026, DistroKid released “Friday Night”, a Lucky Lukee track with LMS, Le Yo, Le Cus and D.O.C FIX DEM, on beats by D.O.C and Skycee. That listing states no genre and stays in the text here, but it shows LMS in the same studio team as the producers of today's bouyon gwada.

PRESSURE, Siwo and BONBON: an artist who also makes the beats

On his own channel, LMS posts short visualizers where he often holds several roles. “PRESSURE”, posted on March 20, 2026, credits LMS for the lyrics and the instrumental, with mixing and arrangement at kuumbastudio, and is filed by its hashtags under bouyonmusic, guadeloupe, 2026 and summer. “Siwo”, on June 5, 2026, uses the same hashtags, with an instrumental by Joshii G and mixing by kuumbastudio.

“BONBON”, on July 29, 2026, is again written and produced by LMS alone, and Deezer lists it as a single on the same date, next to “Sunday Morning” and “Ochanterie”. Its listing does not carry the word bouyon: this profile cites it without classifying it. The file sketches a self sufficient artist who writes, produces and releases without a big structure, and whose bouyon register is stated title by title.

Ochanterie on Lawcy Music, and the Yugah Records question

“Ochanterie” is LMS's most structured release. The visualizer, posted on November 8, 2025 by the Lawcy Music channel, credits LMS as writer, DJ Matix as composer, recording at Kuumba Studio and mixing by DJ Syxtee, under a 2026 Lawcy Music copyright, and it closes with the BOUYON hashtag. Deezer dates the single to July 26, 2026.

One last lead calls for caution. A track called “MAN A GYALIS”, by an artist named LMS with Boutcha Bwa, came out on April 4, 2025 on Yugah Records, with a credit in the name of Mathias Naranin, and has passed 467,000 views. The name and the lms.yg.97 account make the match plausible, but no opened page establishes it, and the listing states no genre: the track is mentioned here, not attributed with certainty.

Limits of the file

No opened source gives a legal name, a birth date or a birthplace for LMS. The Guadeloupe anchoring rests on his own hashtags and on the 97 in his account names, and this profile presents it as anchoring, not as a civil record. LMS is a short name shared on the platforms by unrelated artists: only the pages cited above are attached to this profile.

To place this circle, see the bouyon gwada page and the list of Guadeloupe artists.

Frequently asked questions

Who is LMS?

A singer anchored in Guadeloupe, known for F*ck it with Lucky Lukee (March 2026) and for his own titles PRESSURE, Siwo and BONBON, for which he writes the lyrics and often the instrumentals. No public biography was found.

Who composed F*ck it?

DJ Skycee and Qwann, according to the listing of the video posted on March 6, 2026 on the Lucky 11 channel. Recording is credited to Magbeatz, mixing to jordanomix and direction to sck music prod.

Which LMS titles state bouyon?

F*ck it with Lucky Lukee, PRESSURE (March 20, 2026), Siwo (June 5, 2026) and Ochanterie (Lawcy Music). These are the four titles entered in the structured data of this profile.

Does LMS produce his own tracks?

Yes, on several titles: PRESSURE and BONBON credit LMS for both lyrics and instrumental. Siwo is on an instrumental by Joshii G, and Ochanterie on a composition by DJ Matix.

Where is LMS from?

No opened source gives his birthplace. His hashtags (guadeloupe) and accounts (lms_97s, lms97s) anchor him in Guadeloupe; this profile presents him as anchored in Guadeloupe, without treating that as a civil record.
