Caribbean Music Awards 2026: bouyon leaves with three awards

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

The fourth Caribbean Music Awards were held on 19 September 2026 at the National Academy for the Performing Arts in Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago, staged in the Caribbean for the first time after three editions in Brooklyn. The winners lists published by World Music Views and Caribbean E Magazine give bouyon three lines: artist, bouyon song and French Caribbean song.

Mr Ridge, Bouyon Artist of the Year

The Bouyon Artist of the Year award goes to Mr Ridge, presented by World Music Views as Ridge of Dominica in its account of the regional winners. Soca News reports the same result for Dominica.

This 2026 award extends the run already documented on the Ridge profile page on this site: the same title at the 2024 and 2025 editions, the second one as a second consecutive win per Dominica News Online. Bouyon therefore keeps its artist of the year at the Caribbean Music Awards for a third edition in a row.

Bouwey, Bouyon Song of the Year

The Bouyon Song of the Year award goes to Bouwey by 1T1 and Theomaa, per both winners lists opened for this page. The track gives its name to part of the current Gwada repertoire, and the award places it at the top of the bouyon songs of the year for the Caribbean Music Awards jury and vote.

This page repeats no certification claim for Bouwey: the award reports opened here publish none, and the gold mention circulating elsewhere has not been verified at the source.

Ouba, French Caribbean Song of the Year

The same duo, 1T1 and Theomaa, won a second award the same night: Ouba was named French Caribbean Song of the Year, per World Music Views and Caribbean E Magazine.

That double matters for the map of bouyon: one pairing wins both in the bouyon category and in the French Caribbean category, the two banks of the sound, Dominica where it was born and Guadeloupe where it now spreads.

A ceremony that came home to the Caribbean

Caribbean E Magazine presents this edition as the fourth, the first held in the Caribbean, hosted by Majah Hype and Nailah Blackman, with more than 40 categories. Neighbouring results set the context: Umpa took Dennery Artist of the Year, Dansa by Klassik Frescobar took Dennery Song of the Year, and Joe Dwet File took French Caribbean Artist of the Year.

For bouyon the tally is clear: three awards, one Dominican artist confirmed at the top of his category, and one Gwada song winning twice. The next check comes on the Creole season stages, where Ridge, 1T1 and Theomaa carry these titles through 2026.

Mr Ridge of Dominica, per the Caribbean Music Awards 2026 winners lists published by World Music Views and Caribbean E Magazine. He extends the 2024 and 2025 titles documented on his profile page.

Which song won Bouyon Song of the Year 2026?

Bouwey by 1T1 and Theomaa. The same duo also won French Caribbean Song of the Year with Ouba.
