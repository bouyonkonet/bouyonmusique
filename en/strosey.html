Strosey

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Strosey has released bouyon under his own name since at least 2025, and says so in his titles: “1 View” is labelled Bouyon 2025, while “We Outside”, “Doe Stress Me” and “Doe Call My Name” all carry the bouyon 2026 tag. That last title, with Reo and Youry Ymw, produced by Fr3EzE, is the most visible record in his catalogue. Around him sits a clearly identified studio circle: Knockin Records and XavvoKnockin on the mix, Yung Pierre on instrumentals, and two titles shared with Redpunchdrip and Devon. No opened page gives his birthplace, so this profile keeps to the credits.

Doe Call My Name, the title with Reo and Youry

“Doe Call My Name” went up on Strosey’s channel on March 25, 2026, as “Strosey ft Youry X Reo- Doe Call My Name (Bouyon 2026)”, running 3 minutes 30. The description names the three voices, Strosey, Youry Ymw and Reo, credits production to Fr3EzE and recording, mixing and mastering to Knockin Records. The distributed version, released through DistroKid under the line Yam Master Records / Knockin Records, is dated March 29, 2026.

The description links to each performer’s accounts. For Youry it points to a TikTok account named youryfleming, which ties the credit to the singer already profiled on this site as Youry Fleming, whose page records the Youry Ymw billing. Reo is the Dominican artist presented by his supporters as the Prince of Bouyon.

The solo singles of 2025 and 2026

Strosey’s first verified bouyon title is not on his own channel: “1 View”, published on June 17, 2025 on the channel of DJ Taffy, labelled Bouyon 2025, runs 3 minutes 09. Production is credited to Tha WizZard, mixing and mastering to Xavvo Knockin Records.

On his own channel, two 2026 singles follow. “We Outside” (January 9, 2026, 3 minutes 04) is produced and mixed by Yung Pierre. “Doe Stress Me” (May 26, 2026, 3 minutes 03) rides a Yung Pierre instrumental, mixed and mastered by Xavvo Knockin. Both titles carry the bouyon 2026 tag. The descriptions stay bare: a producer, an engineer, and nothing about the artist himself.

Why Should I? and Why Did I?, the trio with Redpunchdrip and Devon

“Why Should I?” brings together Redpunchdrip, Strosey and Devon on an XavvoKnockin production. The video was published on January 14, 2026 on the Knockin Records channel, labelled Bouyon 2026, running 3 minutes 10; the distributed version, under the Knockin Records line, is dated January 21, 2026 and credits Devon as Devon George.

The same trio returns, in a different order, on “Why Did I?”, published on July 22, 2026 on the Gama Production channel: Devon, Redpunchdrip and Strosey, production, mixing and mastering by Mushroom, guitar by Jo, 3 minutes 45, tagged bouyon 2026. These two titles place Strosey inside a small circle of artists who record together around Knockin Records.

Stage, origin and the limits of the file

Strosey’s channel also shows the stage: a 31 minute live set with Youry, Daddy Keyz and Joseph the drummer, published on September 14, 2026, and his appearance in the “2026 New Years Jam Live” published by Reo on January 2, 2026 with Edday, Youry and Daddy Keyz. These videos state no genre and are used here only to document live work.

Two origin signals appear without being proof. The artist’s Instagram account is named strosey767, and 767 is the Dominica dialling code; several of his pages also carry the hashtag sxmbouyon2026, which points to the bouyon scene of Saint Martin. No opened page says where Strosey was born or where he lives: this site does not decide, and leaves the origin open.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Strosey?

Strosey is an artist whose titles have carried the bouyon label since “1 View” in 2025. His 2026 releases include “We Outside”, “Doe Stress Me” and “Doe Call My Name” with Reo and Youry Ymw. No public biography was found.

Who produced Doe Call My Name?

Fr3EzE, according to the description of the video published on March 25, 2026. Recording, mixing and mastering are credited to Knockin Records, and the distributed version is dated March 29, 2026.

Who did Strosey record Why Should I? with?

With Redpunchdrip and Devon, credited as Devon George on the distributed version, on an XavvoKnockin production released in January 2026. The trio returned in July 2026 on “Why Did I?”, produced by Mushroom.

Where is Strosey from?

No opened source says. His Instagram name, strosey767, and the hashtag sxmbouyon2026 are signals, not proof: this site assigns Strosey no island.

What is Strosey’s first verified bouyon title?

“1 View”, published on June 17, 2025 on DJ Taffy’s channel with the Bouyon 2025 tag, produced by Tha WizZard and mixed by Xavvo Knockin Records.
