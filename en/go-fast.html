Free Up, billed Bouyon 2026

On January 26, 2026, DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast uploaded “Go Fast Free Up (Bouyon 2026)”. The page credits writing and performance to Go Fast, production to Jackie Beats, recording to Yw3 Studios, mixing and mastering to Di Youte, and executive production to Riddimbeast Studios. The title was past 43,600 views on October 27, 2026.

Jackie Beats is also the maker of the riddim of “Nouveau Vitesse” by DJ MJ and MV On De Keys; Di Youte, on the mix, returns on a large share of the circuit's titles documented by this site. “Free Up” thus sits in the most active production chain of 2026 Dominican bouyon, that of DJ Taffy.

The Dive In Partymix: three titles produced by Shako

Three days earlier, on January 23, 2026, the same channel published “Go Fast - Dive in Partymix (Bouyon 2026)”, a 5 minute 48 run produced by Shako, with a tracklist in three parts: “Sandy (Bright like Sunday morning)”, “I know what you want (bouyon Remix)” with Juicy, and “Married to the money”. Recorded at Yw3 Studios, mixed and mastered by Di Youte, the mix was past 30,600 views when read.

Shako is the producer credited on Coipy's “Let You Go” and on Motto's “Say My Name”: a name that moves between Dominica's new singers and artists from Saint Lucia. The partymix format, several short titles glued together for the fete, belongs to DJs and sound systems; Go Fast takes it on himself.

Straight to de Top, 40 Days and the RiddimBeast & crew lives

Before 2026, DJ Taffy's channel kept “7. Straight to de top - Go fast”, published on September 17, 2025, more than 18,800 views, and the live “RiddimBeast & crew (Go fast, Freddy, Quan) Sammy's Hide Out Live ft Unstoppable Shax, Nice, Gwada G part 2”, uploaded on July 30, 2024, more than 53,000 views: Go Fast shares the stage with Freddy, Quan, Unstoppable Shax, Nice and Gwada G.

On October 9, 2026, “Go Fast - 40 Days (Bouyon 2027)” opened the next season, 7,500 views when read. This profile is written from the YouTube pages opened on October 27, 2026; no Deezer or Apple Music page under Go Fast's name was found, no page states his origin, and this site places him in the Dominican Riddim Beast circuit without stating a birthplace.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Go Fast?

A bouyon singer published by DJ Taffy's channel, DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast, maker of Free Up (Bouyon 2026), the Dive In Partymix and 40 Days (Bouyon 2027).

Who produced Free Up?

Jackie Beats, with recording at Yw3 Studios, a mix by Di Youte and executive production by Riddimbeast Studios, according to the YouTube page of January 26, 2026.

What is the Dive In Partymix?

A run of three titles produced by Shako, published on January 23, 2026: Sandy (Bright like Sunday morning), I know what you want (bouyon Remix) with Juicy, and Married to the money.

Does Go Fast perform live?

Yes: he appears in RiddimBeast & crew Sammy's Hide Out Live (July 2024) with Freddy, Quan, Unstoppable Shax, Nice and Gwada G.

Where is Go Fast from?

No opened page says. His titles come out in DJ Taffy's Riddim Beast circuit; this profile places him in the Dominican scene without stating an origin.
