Msina: Bouyon (2022) and Bouyon puissanci (2023)

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Msina comes from rap, judging by the titles and keywords of his channel, and yet he released two tracks named after bouyon: “Bouyon”, a short title of 1 minute 25 released on June 15, 2022, then “Bouyon puissanci”, an official video posted in June 2023. Both releases are verified on his Deezer page and on YouTube. No opened page gives his origin or his biography, and this profile does not invent one.

Two titles, two years

The first, “Bouyon”, came out on June 15, 2022 on 2151952 Records DK, a digital distribution reference. It runs 1 minute 25, which makes it more of a sketch than a full single. The YouTube version, distributed by DistroKid, carries the same date and the same label, and Deezer files the title under Música Africana, a platform tag that does not specify the style.

The second, “Bouyon puissanci”, arrived as an official video on June 14, 2023 on the Msina officiel channel, then on Deezer on June 23, 2023. The description presents the track as his new single and points to the streaming platforms. The video runs 2 minutes 06 and showed 9,131 views at the time of the check, against 2,367 for “Bouyon”.

A rapper who passes through bouyon

The rest of the channel speaks another musical language: “FLOW”, “HOT”, “Dans les poches”, tagged drill, and a freestyle “YAKUZA FLOW PART 3” produced by NEVUS. The keywords of the bouyon video itself mix bouyon, French rap and urban music. Bouyon therefore appears here as a declared detour within a rap path, a case also found among other artists of the French speaking scene described on our page about bouyon in France.

This site does not present Msina as a full time bouyon artist: it presents him as the author of two titles that claim the genre by name, which is exactly what the opened pages show.

What the file does not say

The two videos sit on two separate channels both named Msina officiel. Neither publishes a biography, a birthplace or a home town, and the handle used on social media, quoted in the video description, is not an address. The origin therefore stays undocumented.

For other titles in the genre, see the bouyon playlist. If Msina releases new bouyon titles or an official introduction, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Msina?

Msina is an artist whose YouTube channel and keywords mostly belong to rap, and who released two bouyon titles: “Bouyon” in 2022 and “Bouyon puissanci” in 2023. His Deezer page counts eight releases.

What are Msina’s bouyon titles?

“Bouyon”, released on June 15, 2022 on 2151952 Records DK (1 minute 25), and “Bouyon puissanci”, an official video posted on June 14, 2023 and released on Deezer on June 23, 2023.

Which one has the most views?

At the time of the check, the “Bouyon puissanci” video showed 9,131 views on YouTube, and the “Bouyon” audio 2,367 views.

Where is Msina from?

No opened source says. This site assigns Msina no origin and draws no conclusion from his social media handle.

Does Msina mostly make bouyon?

No. The other titles on his channel, such as “FLOW”, “HOT” or “Dans les poches”, belong to rap and drill. Bouyon takes up two titles there.
