Shatta bouyon: Maureen, the Martinique voice

By Smokiller · Published October 24, 2026

Shatta is the party sound of Martinique, neighbour to the bouyon of Dominica and Guadeloupe. Its best known voice is Maureen, a singer born in Fort-de-France, often called the queen of shatta by the press and media that follow her. This page gathers her verified path, and explains why the bouyon scene watches her as a direct neighbour.

From Dillon to the first songs

Maureen, full name Maureen Lucea, was born on January 27, 1999 and grew up in the Dillon district of Fort-de-France, Martinique, in a music loving family. A dancer first, passionate about Jamaican dancehall, she then sang for shatta artists at parties before recording her own titles.

Her first productions in the opened sources are Flex in 2019 and then Tic in 2020. In 2021, Tic won a new audience when the track was used in Thierry Mugler fashion shows, to illustrate the steps of model Bella Hadid.

Laptop, the hit that changed the scale

With Kalash, Maureen records Laptop, presented by regional press as the first shatta hit certified platinum single by the SNEP. The track installs shatta in the French charts and makes Maureen the figure the media call the queen of shatta, a title this page attributes to her the way the sources do, without posing it as an official fact.

Her first album, Queen, was released on Friday October 24, 2025. Interviews published around the release show her singing in French and in Creole, between dance tracks and calmer songs.

Why bouyon watches her

Shatta and bouyon share the same publics between Martinique, Guadeloupe, Dominica and the diaspora. Scene playlists mix the two sounds, and artists travel between the islands for the same parties, the same carnivals and the same festivals.

On this site, the shatta bouyon page and the bouyon or shatta page already set out the comparison of the two sounds. Maureen is the name that comes back when the French public discovers shatta, the way bouyon names come back when it discovers Dominica.

What this page does not claim

Maureen is a shatta artist, not a bouyon artist, and this page presents her as such: a Martinique neighbour whose sound talks with bouyon. No ranking, no sales figure and no certification is advanced here without the source that carries it.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Maureen?

Maureen, full name Maureen Lucea, is a singer and rapper from Martinique born on January 27, 1999, raised in Dillon, Fort-de-France. She is the best known voice of shatta and is often called the queen of shatta by the media.

What is Maureen biggest hit?

Laptop, with Kalash, presented by regional press as the first shatta track certified platinum single by the SNEP. Her titles Flex (2019) and Tic (2020) launched her career.

When was Maureen first album released?

Her first album, Queen, was released on Friday October 24, 2025, according to the interviews published around the release.
