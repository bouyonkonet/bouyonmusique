Timii Mignonne: Petite Mignonne, bouyon 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Timii Mignonne is a new name in bouyon, heard on two 2026 titles released on the ICM Records label: “Love On Repeat”, a duet with Colton T (credited as ColtonT), out in August, then “Petite Mignonne”, a first solo single, released on October 9, 2026 on a riddim by Foster “Litle Boy” Xavier. Both official pages file these tracks under bouyon through their hashtags and tags. Neither names an origin for the artist.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

Petite Mignonne, the solo single

“Petite Mignonne” is the title that carries the artist’s name. The official listing, distributed by DistroKid under the ICM Records reference, gives a release date of October 9, 2026 and a running time of 2 minutes 52. The video, posted on October 12, 2026 on Timii Mignonne’s channel, runs 2 minutes 57 and ends with three hashtags: the artist’s name, the title and bouyon.

The video credits are complete. Writing goes to Timii Mignonne, production to ICM Records, the riddim to Foster “Litle Boy” Xavier, mixing and mastering to Kirky Beats, named Kirk Blackmoore on the official listing. Direction and editing are by Colton E Thomas, who shares the color grading with Jorg Wong and appears as executive producer on the listing as Colton Emmanuel Thomas. The beatmaker is the same Foster Xavier as on the Litleboy LSBeats767 profile, where his full legal name is already given.

Love On Repeat with ColtonT

Before the solo, Timii Mignonne appears on “Love On Repeat”, by ColtonT and Timii Mignonne, posted on August 7, 2026 on ColtonT’s channel. The video runs 3 minutes 09. Writing is shared between Colton Thomas and Timii Mignonne, production goes to ICM Records, the riddim to Ashille “Dj Shill” Eli, mixing to Ajmal Peters and mastering to Kirk “Kirky Beats” Blackmoore. Kimani Casimir shot the footage, Colton Thomas directed and edited, and the location is credited to Big House Of Content.

The video tags list bouyon, bouyon 2027, soca, dancehall, as well as dominica and guadeloupe, and the description carries the bouyon hashtag. Those tags serve the video’s search visibility: this site does not treat them as a statement of origin. ColtonT’s links in the description, coltont.com and @coltontmusic, are those of the Colton T profile already published here.

What is known, and what is missing

The file holds two titles, one label, ICM Records, and a studio team that repeats from one track to the next: Colton Thomas directing, Kirk Blackmoore mastering. The public accounts cited are petitemignonnee on Instagram and Facebook, Timii.mignonne on TikTok and a YouTube channel in the artist’s name, plus an Apple Music artist page linked in the Love On Repeat description.

No biography, no birth date and no island are given on these pages. This profile will be expanded if the artist publishes more. In the meantime, the two titles sit naturally beside new bouyon in 2026 and the bouyon playlist.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Timii Mignonne?

Timii Mignonne is a bouyon artist name released on ICM Records, writer of Petite Mignonne and co writer of Love On Repeat with ColtonT, two 2026 titles. No public biography was found.

When was Petite Mignonne released?

On October 9, 2026 according to the official listing distributed by DistroKid, and the video was posted on October 12, 2026 on the artist’s YouTube channel.

Who produced the Petite Mignonne riddim?

Foster “Litle Boy” Xavier, credited for the riddim in the video and as beat maker under the name Foster Xavier on the official listing.

What is the link with Colton T?

Colton Thomas co writes and sings Love On Repeat as ColtonT, directs the Petite Mignonne video and appears as executive producer on its official listing.

Where is Timii Mignonne from?

No opened source says. The dominica and guadeloupe tags on one video are not enough: this site assigns no origin.
