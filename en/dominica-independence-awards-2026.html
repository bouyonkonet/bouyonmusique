Bouyon music: Dominica independence awards 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 30, 2026

There are seventeen of them. On 29 October 2026, the Office of the Prime Minister published the list of Dominicans to be honoured for their contribution to national development at the ceremony for the 48th anniversary of independence, on Tuesday 3 November at Windsor Park. The full list, category by category, with no name added and none invented.

On this page
  1. The Sisserou Award of Honour, the top honour on the list
  2. The Meritorious Service Award: culture, sport, education, care
  3. Long Service and the Services Medal of Honour

  5. Frequently asked questions

The Sisserou Award of Honour, the top honour on the list

Four people receive the Sisserou Award of Honour: Vivian Trotter for engineering, Dr Rhonda McIntyre for paediatric medicine, Janice Jean-Jacques Thomas for social security and Francis Stewart Paris for construction.

The official release ties each name to its field, in that order: engineering, paediatric medicine, social security and construction. The Dominica Gazette, carrying DBC Radio, published the same four names the same day.

The Meritorious Service Award: culture, sport, education, care

Seven Dominicans receive the Meritorious Service Award: Johanna John-Bertrand for Education and Social Security, Romualda B. Hyacinth for Culture, Cedric J. Harris for Sports, Education and Youth Development, Colette Pauletta Prosper for Disability Rights, Rawle Leslie for Agriculture and Education, Colbert Pinard for Youth Development and Rosalind Adora Scotland Rabess for Nursing.

For readers of this site, one name speaks directly to the scene: Romualda B. Hyacinth is honoured for Culture, in a season when Dominica made its heritage, its Kweyol language and its music the heart of its celebrations.

Long Service and the Services Medal of Honour

Four people are recognised for Long Service: Mcpierreson Eric Frampton for Public Service, Florestine Anthea Lewis for Nursing, Alice Laronde for Education and Veda George for Early Childhood Education.

The Services Medal of Honour goes to two Dominicans: Clayton Lawrence Jno Baptiste for Public Service and Ronald Jno Baptiste for Community Development. In total: four plus seven plus four plus two, seventeen honourees, as the release announces.

The presentation, on 3 November at 5:30 p.m.

The awards will be presented during the National Day Observance Ceremony, which commences at 5:30 p.m. at the Windsor Park Sports Stadium, per the same Office of the Prime Minister release.

The Government of Dominica congratulates the honourees and thanks them for their dedication to the country and its people. This page prints the names and fields exactly as the official release publishes them; press spellings vary in places, and the Office of the Prime Minister spellings govern here.

Frequently asked questions

How many Dominicans are honoured in 2026?

Seventeen, per the Office of the Prime Minister release published on 29 October 2026: four Sisserou Awards of Honour, seven Meritorious Service Awards, four Long Service awards and two Services Medals of Honour.

Who receives the Sisserou Award of Honour in 2026?

Vivian Trotter (engineering), Dr Rhonda McIntyre (paediatric medicine), Janice Jean-Jacques Thomas (social security) and Francis Stewart Paris (construction), per the official release.

When are the awards presented?

During the National Day ceremony, on Tuesday 3 November 2026 from 5:30 p.m. at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, for the 48th anniversary of independence.
