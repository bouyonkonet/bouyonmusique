TinyToon: You Are My Girl, a Guadeloupe bouyon

By Smokiller · Published November 11, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

You Are My Girl is a bouyon by TinyToon Christian Jean-Philippe, dated 8 October 2026 by the description of its YouTube video. The track was written and sung by TinyToon at the NCS97 prod studio, in Raizet, Guadeloupe, with Mister Babass on recording, mixing and mastering. It is a romantic bouyon, presented by its own description as a hit onedaground bouyon music.

A track dated 8 October 2026

The YouTube video description dates the track to 8 October 2026. That is the date given by the uploader, not platform metadata, and no second source confirms it. We publish it as the date shown by the description, and it is the only verified date for this track.

The video carries the full title TinyToon Christian Jean-Philippe, You Are My Girl, with the hashtag bouyonmusic and the word Lyricks in its title. It is presented as a YouTube exclusive. No Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer or Shazam page was found for this track: for now, YouTube is its only home.

NCS97 prod, Mister Babass and No Face Beatz

The track was written and sung by TinyToon Christian Jean-Philippe at the NCS97 prod studio, in Raizet, Guadeloupe, in the French West Indies Caribbean. Recording, mixing and mastering are credited to Mister Babass, at the same NCS97 prod studio.

The description also credits a remix of the WYFL riddim, for Wah You Feel Like, by No Face Beatz. NCS97 is a studio and production name here, not a person's name. No further information on the studio or on Mister Babass was found in the opened sources.

A romantic bouyon, lyrics online

The video description prints the full lyrics of the track, the chorus repeated twice plus two verses. We do not reproduce them here. The theme is a romantic ode praising a girl as a queen and a favourite, with dance and party imagery.

The tone is that of a declaration: the track speaks of love, dance and celebration, in the soft register of Guadeloupe bouyon. The presentation of the track as a hit onedaground comes from its own description, and we carry it as such.

What is not known yet

No artist biography, Instagram account, label or other TinyToon track was found in the opened sources. Guadeloupe is the only verified origin: Raizet is named as the location of the NCS97 prod studio, not as the artist's hometown, and we publish no town.

The file is thin, as is often the case for new Guadeloupe bouyon that comes out first on YouTube. This page says what the track's description gives, no more and no less, and it will grow if other sources appear: a streaming page, an interview, or a second track.
