JCro: Dominica gospel singer and his bouyon song

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

JCro is a gospel singer from Dominica who writes under the name Karlvin Deravariere. Since 2019 he has moved from one style to the next, gospel soca, reggae, dancehall, afro gospel, while keeping one subject: faith. In July 2025 he released “All Unto You”, his first song billed as gospel bouyon.

A worship singer in Roseau

In 2022, GL365 radio had him on its On The Beat show and introduced him simply as “Gospel Artist - JCro (Dominica)”. His early songs were recorded at Elevate Studios in Dominica, with Jamieson Julien producing and mixing. That goes for “Let Dem Kno”, a video from December 2019, and for “You Know Me”, billed gospel soca 2020, still his most watched song at over 9,300 views.

In January 2026 the Caymanian Times announced Dominica's national gospel festival, Glory in Paradise, set for February 28 at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. J-Cro was on the bill with DJ Alfie and Champion in God. The paper spells his name Calvin Deravariere, while his own credits read Karlvin.

All Unto You, the step into bouyon

On July 29, 2025 his channel JCro Music posted “All Unto You”, with “Gospel Bouyon 2025” in the title. JCro wrote the lyrics; Olan Prince composed, produced and engineered the riddim. The video, a single still artwork by K|Reality, is close to 3,800 views. Among his songs online, it is the only one billed as bouyon.

The song keeps what defines gospel bouyon: a party tempo carrying a worship lyric. Six months later, in January 2026, the page for “Steadfast”, produced by Trinidadian Adrian “Always Kreative” Emily on the Bubble Up Riddim, points first to “All Unto You (Gospel Bouyon)”.

A catalogue that keeps changing rhythm

Before bouyon, JCro mostly sang over soca. “Anchored”, gospel soca 2022, is the official theme song of the Caribbean Baptist Fellowship youth festival, and the DiscipleDJ channel reposted it the next day. The same year, “Flame” was dedicated to anyone who had lost a loved one, built around Psalm 73.

He has also tried reggae with “Thanks and Praise” in 2020, dancehall with “Vision” and “Eyes Above” in 2021, then amapiano with “Praise Zone”, a duet with Emrand Henry produced by KU Beats in January 2024. Nearly every video description quotes a Bible verse, from Jeremiah 1:5 to Hebrews 6:19.

Frequently asked questions

Who is JCro?

A gospel singer from Dominica, credited as Karlvin Deravariere, who has mixed soca, reggae, dancehall, afro gospel and bouyon since 2019.

What is his gospel bouyon song?

All Unto You, posted on July 29, 2025 on his JCro Music channel as Gospel Bouyon 2025, produced by Olan Prince.

What is his most watched song?

You Know Me, billed gospel soca 2020 and produced by Jamieson Julien at Elevate Studios, with over 9,300 views on YouTube.

Did he play Dominica's gospel festival?

The Caymanian Times lists him on the bill of Glory in Paradise, the national gospel festival set for February 28, 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau.

What other styles does he sing?

Gospel soca (You Know Me, Anchored), reggae (Thanks and Praise), dancehall (Vision, Eyes Above) and afro gospel (Praise Zone with Emrand Henry).
