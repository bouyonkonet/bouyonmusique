Heaven Baby: the Ayra Starr bouyon remix by Smokiller

By Smokiller · Published October 22, 2026

Heaven Baby is the Ayra Starr track that Sean "Smokiller" Francis, the Dominican-born producer, rebuilt in bouyon under the Bouyon Shattaz banner. The remix, at 128 BPM for 3 minutes 24, became the centre of a rare public story for a Dominican sound: a Meta advertising test running since October 19, 2026, radio servicing and a free download. This page collects the verified facts of that path.

The remix: 128 BPM, 3 minutes 24

The track sheet of Rotation Pack 01 by Bouyon Shattaz, the pack sent to radio on October 19, 2026, describes the record this way: Ayra Starr and Smokiller, Heaven Baby, Bouyon Shattaz Remix, genre bouyon and Afrobeats crossover, 128 BPM, duration 3 minutes 24. The pack's DJ version adds a 15 second intro loop taken from the track's peak, for a total duration of 3 minutes 39. The remix cover art exists and is embedded in the pack's MP3 files, unlike the pack's other two tracks, delivered without cover art.

The remix is also one of the public free downloads on smokiller.com, where it is presented as a Bouyon Shattaz remix. Ayra Starr performed Heaven Baby in her NFL halftime set in London on October 11, 2026, a first for an African artist headlining such a show according to Bouyon Shattaz coverage, which gave the track worldwide exposure just before the campaign.

The campaign: a split test published on October 19, 2026

The remix is the record behind the @bouyonshattaz growth campaign on Meta, published by Sean himself on October 19, 2026 and still active: Traffic objective, optimisation on Instagram profile visits, destination the @bouyonshattaz profile only, Instagram only and no Stories. The campaign compares two ad sets at 75 dollars lifetime budget each: ad set A carries the Tiwa Savage interest, ad set B is broad, with no interest. Advantage+ audience is on for both ad sets, so the Tiwa Savage interest works as a soft inclusion that Meta may expand beyond.

The first full delivery day, read on October 20, showed 1.73 dollars spent and 172 profile visits, with the broad ad set ahead of the Tiwa Savage ad set, 105 visits to 67. The campaign runs until November 16, 2026. To this site's knowledge, it is the first time a Dominican bouyon remix has carried a measured acquisition campaign of this kind, visit by visit.

Radio servicing: the lead track of Rotation Pack 01

Heaven Baby is the lead track of the first Bouyon Shattaz servicing pack, built on October 19, 2026: three tracks normalised for broadcast, with radio versions and DJ intro versions, offered to stations that contact the platform in Instagram direct messages in exchange for spin reports. The pack promises weekly servicing: radio edits in MP3 and WAV, DJ edits, exclusives and interview access during WCMF week.

One precision this site keeps visible, as the pack itself does: the clean status of the pack's tracks has not been verified track by track, and the pack does not claim it. Stations receive the files and the track sheet, with the durations and BPM above, and Sean confirms each track's status before any promise is made.

Why Heaven Baby matters in the catalogue

In Smokiller's flip series, Heaven Baby holds a special place: it is the only track to receive both structured radio servicing and a measured paid campaign. Nothing Into Something, the Coi Leray flip, showed the organic reach of the format on TikTok; Heaven Baby tests the other lever, paid amplification toward the platform's profile.

Both experiments serve the same goal, stated by Bouyon Shattaz from the start: put the sound born in Dominica in front of the audiences of global artists, with the island's name attached to the sound.
