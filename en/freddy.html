Freddy: the bouyon name the tracklists and the festival bills share

Published October 20, 2026

Freddy is a bouyon artist of the Dominica scene whose name appears in very precise places: credited with Trixx and Beevo on “Fall In De Trap”, with Quan and Litle Boy on “Your Friend”, in the “Nasty Freestyle” medley, and on two major bills of 2025, the official Bouyon Day of the Discover Dominica Authority in Roseau and the first all bouyon festival in St. Maarten. Freddy is also an extremely common name, carried by artists with no connection to him. This profile therefore does two things: it documents, source by source, the Freddy of Dominican bouyon, and it states openly everything the public record does not allow us to know, without ever borrowing the biography of a namesake.

Who Freddy is: a common name, a record to disambiguate

Freddy, in this profile, designates one artist only: the one the tracklists and festival bills of Dominica bouyon credit under that single name. He has, in the sources opened for this research, no published full name, no biography, no artist page on the streaming platforms and no verified official account. What the record holds is appearances: three track credits in mixes by DJs of the scene, and two bill lines on documented bouyon events of 2025. That is little, and it is already a coherent trace, because all those appearances point to the same world, the Dominican bouyon of Roseau and its regional circuit.

Disambiguation is a duty here, not a stylistic precaution. Freddy is one of the most carried artist names in popular music: an American rapper signs Trapboy Freddy, a legendary singer was called Freddie Mercury, and dozens of other Freddys fill the platform catalogues. None of those artists is the Freddy of this profile, and none of their information, origin, dates or discography, is used here. The reverse rule also holds: this profile lends the bouyon Freddy only what his own published credits establish, line by line.

What those lines establish, however, is not minor. The names around Freddy in his credits are the heart of the current Dominican scene: Trixx, Beevo, Quan, Litle Boy, Gwada G. And the bills that carry him in 2025 are the two moments when Dominican bouyon showed itself in large format: an official street celebration in the capital of Dominica, and the first festival dedicated to the genre on another island. An artist can therefore be almost invisible on the platforms and perfectly visible where his scene actually lives: in the mixes of the DJs and on the stages of the circuit.

Fall In De Trap: the starting credit, with Trixx and Beevo

“Fall In De Trap” is the track through which Freddy enters the record of this site. The Dj Close Connections podcast, in its Bouyon Passport 2026 episode published on 4 February 2026, lines up a long soca and bouyon selection where the track is credited to Trixx, Freddy and Beevo. The three names are given as co-performers, with no published hierarchy, and this profile reports them in the order of the tracklist, as the other profiles on this site report the credits of that same mix.

The context of the mix gives the track its exact weight. The selection places “Fall In De Trap” among the Dominican lines of the moment: “Make Her Bawl” by Shelly, Trixx and DJ Taffy, “Take Me As I Am” by Trilla G, “La Vie Doux” by Reo and Tehilah, “Bouwey” by 1T1 and Theomaa, “Se Miimii” by Miimii KDS, tracks by Asa Bantan, Signal Band and TK International. Freddy therefore appears, from this credit onward, in the new generation the DJs of the scene circulate ahead of the platforms, alongside Trilla G, Reo and the established names of Roseau.

For “Fall In De Trap”, no commercial release date, no label and no published production credits were found: the track is documented as a tracklist line, and it is presented here as such. It is kept in the structured data of this page, with its published co-performers, Trixx and Beevo. It is the first of the three verified tracks of Freddy, and the only one where his name holds the central position of a trio published in that order.

Your Friend and Nasty Freestyle: the other mix lines

The second appearance of Freddy reads in the pre-carnival selection of the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY, a mix built around the Dominica scene. Its tracklist credits “Your Friend” to Quan and Litle Boy, with Freddy as featured artist. The same mix lines up Ridge, Wawa, Trilla G, Reo, Asa Bantan, Signal Band, Triple K, Kenny G and Trixx and Beevo on “Doe do me Dat”: it is, line by line, the repertoire of the Dominican scene before carnival, and Freddy is again placed among its central names, this time as the guest of Quan and Litle Boy.

The third appearance returns in the same Bouyon Passport 2026 episode of the Dj Close Connections podcast as “Fall In De Trap”: a medley, “Nasty Freestyle”, is credited there to Litleboy, Gwada G, Trixx and Freddy, the podcast tracklist truncating the line. A second tracklist, that of the Blaqrose Supreme podcast, gives the full credit: Litleboy, featuring Gwada G, Trixx, Freddy and Niice, on “Nasty Freestyle Medley”. The two sources agree on the presence of Freddy in this collective medley, a typical format of the scene, where several voices share one rhythm in freestyle.

These three tracks form the whole verified discography of Freddy as of 20 October 2026: “Fall In De Trap”, with Trixx and Beevo; “Your Friend”, with Quan and Litle Boy; and “Nasty Freestyle Medley”, with Litleboy, Gwada G, Trixx and Niice. No solo track, no commercial single and no platform release under his name alone were found. This profile says it plainly: the public discography of Freddy is, to date, a discography of collaborations documented by DJs, and it is published here in exactly that form.

The stages of 2025: Bouyon Day in Roseau and the first bouyon festival in St. Maarten

It is on the bills that the Freddy record becomes clearest, and it is the point that profiles built only on platforms miss. On 24 February 2025, the Discover Dominica Authority, the official tourism authority of Dominica, published the programme of Bouyon Day 2025: a free event, Bouyon in de Streets, on 25 February 2025 from 6 PM to midnight on Hanover Street, in Roseau, near the Banana Tree Bar and Grill. The official artist list lines up Asa Bantan, Litle Boy, Quan, Reo, Freddy, Clint H, Najee, Likkle Man, Skinny Bantan, Rah, Delly, Kenny G and TK International, with Unstoppable Shax and DJ Snow. Freddy is named there by the official authority of the country, in the headline list of bouyon, for the national celebration of the genre in the capital.

Three months later, his name crossed the sea. Black Island Girl Multimedia reported, in an article of 12 May 2025, on the Strictly the Best Festival, Bouyon Edition, organised by Two Brothers Entertainment at Lotterie Farm, in St. Maarten: more than 5,000 spectators for the first festival entirely dedicated to bouyon on that island. The artist list includes Pudaz, Litle Boy, Quandan, Freddy, Reo, Nice, Kenny G, Trilla G, Trixx, Unstoppable Shax, Khallion and Edday, with Mr Ridge closing, bringing all the bouyon artists back on stage for a closing act of solidarity. Freddy is therefore, according to that published report, among the Dominican artists that first St. Maarten bouyon festival presented to its public.

Read together, the two bills draw the real position of Freddy in the bouyon of 2025: a stage artist of the Dominican circuit, booked at home by the official tourism authority, then exported with the delegation of the scene to the first large bouyon stage of St. Maarten. That is the historic circuit of the genre, born in Dominica and carried from island to island by its artists, the one our history of bouyon page retraces. Neither of these two sources publishes a biographical detail on Freddy, and this profile adds none: the bills establish his presence and his company, not his life.

What this profile does not know: the limits of the Freddy record

This profile ends with its limits, written out in full. Not present in the sources opened for this research, and therefore not written here: the full name of Freddy, his date and place of birth, his personal origin beyond his documented presence on the Dominica bouyon circuit, his path before 2025, his verified official accounts, his listening figures and any solo release. None of these elements is guessed or borrowed from a namesake, and the structured data of this page contains no official link, for lack of a link verified by opening it.

The recording list in the schema reflects exactly the state of the record: three tracks, all collaborations, “Fall In De Trap”, “Your Friend” and “Nasty Freestyle Medley”. Where the established artist profiles on this site list eight to fifteen tracks, this one lists three, because three is the true number as of 20 October 2026. In return, this profile holds two stage facts that platform discographies do not show: the official Bouyon Day 2025 line of the Discover Dominica Authority and the bill of the first St. Maarten bouyon festival, which place Freddy among the names the Dominican scene presents in public.

In the meantime, listening runs through the mixes that document him: the Bouyon Passport 2026 episode of the Dj Close Connections podcast, for “Fall In De Trap” and the “Nasty Freestyle” medley, and the pre-carnival selection of the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY, for “Your Friend”, then through this site bouyon playlist for the rest of the scene. If Freddy publishes a track under his name, a biography or full credits, this profile will be completed and dated. Until then, it remains the exact trace of a name that DJ tracklists and the official bills of 2025 share, and that the platforms have not yet caught up with.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Freddy, the bouyon artist?

Freddy is a bouyon artist of the Dominica scene, credited under that single name. His verified appearances are three collaboration tracks in DJ mixes, “Fall In De Trap” with Trixx and Beevo, “Your Friend” with Quan and Litle Boy and the “Nasty Freestyle” medley with Litleboy, Gwada G, Trixx and Niice, and two bills of 2025: the official Bouyon Day in Roseau and the first bouyon festival in St. Maarten. No biography and no full name are published in the sources opened for this profile.

What are the verified tracks of Freddy?

Three tracks, all documented by mix tracklists: “Fall In De Trap”, credited to Trixx, Freddy and Beevo in the Bouyon Passport 2026 episode of the Dj Close Connections podcast, on 4 February 2026; “Your Friend”, credited to Quan and Litle Boy with Freddy featured in the pre-carnival selection of the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY; and “Nasty Freestyle Medley”, where a Blaqrose Supreme podcast tracklist credits Litleboy featuring Gwada G, Trixx, Freddy and Niice. No solo track was found.

Did Freddy play Dominica Bouyon Day 2025?

Yes, according to the official source: the press release of the Discover Dominica Authority of 24 February 2025, announcing Bouyon in de Streets on 25 February 2025 on Hanover Street in Roseau, lists Freddy among the booked artists, alongside Asa Bantan, Litle Boy, Quan, Reo, Kenny G and TK International. It is a stage fact published by the tourism authority of Dominica, and it is the kind of evidence that platform only profiles do not see.

Did Freddy play in St. Maarten?

Yes, according to the report published by Black Island Girl Multimedia on 12 May 2025: the Strictly the Best Festival, Bouyon Edition, organised by Two Brothers Entertainment at Lotterie Farm, the first festival entirely dedicated to bouyon in St. Maarten with more than 5,000 spectators, counted Freddy among its artists, with Pudaz, Litle Boy, Reo, Trilla G, Trixx, Khallion and Edday, and Mr Ridge closing.

Is this Freddy Trapboy Freddy or another known artist of the same name?

No, and this profile is keen to say it clearly, because confusion is the main risk of this record. Trapboy Freddy is an American rapper, Freddie Mercury was a British singer, and neither has any connection with the bouyon of Dominica. The Freddy of this page is only the artist credited under that name in the Dominican bouyon tracklists and bills cited here; no information from a namesake is used in this profile.

Where can I hear Freddy?

In the mixes that document his tracks: the Bouyon Passport 2026 episode of the Dj Close Connections podcast, for “Fall In De Trap” and “Nasty Freestyle”, and the pre-carnival selection of the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY, for “Your Friend”. No commercial artist page of Freddy was found on the platforms consulted for this profile; our bouyon playlist follows the rest of the Dominica scene to which his credits attach him.
