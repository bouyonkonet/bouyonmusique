Fifin: Unstick with Wotlis, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Fifin is one of the voices of the Go Fast crew, a name that keeps coming back in the credits of Riddim Beast Studios, the studio of DJ Taffy, and on the channel of beatmaker Jackie Beatz. The most visible title is “Unstick”, an official video shared with Wotlis and billed as bouyon 2025, produced by DJ Taffy on a Jackie Beatz riddim. Four primary pages opened during this research tie Fifin to bouyon in their own titles. None of them gives an origin, a real name or a biography: this profile sticks to the published credits.

Unstick, the lead title with Wotlis

“Unstick” went up on DJ Taffy’s channel on April 7, 2025 as a 4 minute official video, titled as bouyon 2025. The credits are detailed: production by DJ Taffy, riddim by Jackie Beatz, live drumming by Untamed Production, recording, mixing and mastering at Riddim Beast Studios, camera and edit by Director Vision. The performance belongs to Fifin and Wotlis, with the word Go Fast in brackets, which places both voices in the same crew.

The description also links to the single on Apple Music, whose address names the lead artist as FiFiNDaSteppa. That is the only name variant found for Fifin in the opened sources, and it is quoted here as it appears, with no further conclusion. To place the sound within the genre, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Knock you, G Spot and Just Pass Dat

Before “Unstick”, Fifin appears on three other titles whose page states bouyon. “G Spot”, released on January 16, 2024 under the bouyon 2024 banner, is billed to Fifin with Eks as guest: production by Jackie Beatz, live drums by Nikita Christmas, recording at Riddim Beast Studio and mixing by DJ Taffy. “Just Pass Dat”, posted on June 25, 2024 on the Jackie Beatz channel, is presented as a Go Fast title written by Eks, Fifin and Wotlis, produced, mixed and mastered by Don Carli.

“Knock you”, released on September 6, 2024 with the label bouyon 2025 and the name Go Fast Music in its title, brings Wotlis and Fifin together again as co writers and performers. DJ Taffy produces, Jackie Beats adds production, and live drums are credited to Drummer Fridge. The executive producers named are Riddim Beast Studios and Go Fast Music, which confirms that Go Fast also works as a release banner.

Go Fast, the crew around Fifin

The opened credits sketch a small and steady circle: Fifin, Wotlis and Eks on vocals, Jackie Beatz and DJ Taffy producing, Drummer Fridge and Untamed Production on live drums. The same channel also carries “Go Fast Free Up”, a bouyon 2026 title credited to the Go Fast crew as a whole with no member list, and one title with no genre mention, “Up is Up (Back door)”, by Smiler G with Eks and Fifin. Both pages are named here for context: the first does not name Fifin, the second does not state bouyon, and neither enters the structured data of this profile.

The “dominica” and “bouyon” tags visible under DJ Taffy’s videos are applied to every upload on the channel. They say something about the studio catalogue, not about where Fifin comes from. No opened page names an island, a town or a birth date for this artist, so this site assigns none.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file on Fifin fits into four bouyon titles dated 2024 and 2025, one name variant in an Apple Music address, and production credits that agree from page to page. There is no biography, no interview and no official artist page opened in this research. This profile will grow if new primary sources appear, like the other pages tracked in our new bouyon in 2026 coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Fifin?

Fifin is a voice of the Go Fast crew, credited as writer and performer on several bouyon titles produced at Riddim Beast Studios and by Jackie Beatz. No public biography was found in the opened sources.

What is Fifin’s best known title?

“Unstick”, with Wotlis, an official video released on April 7, 2025 on DJ Taffy’s channel, titled bouyon 2025, produced by DJ Taffy on a Jackie Beatz riddim, with live drums by Untamed Production.

Which Fifin bouyon titles are verified?

Four: “G Spot” with Eks (bouyon 2024), “Just Pass Dat” with Eks and Wotlis (bouyon 2024), “Knock you” with Wotlis (bouyon 2025) and “Unstick” with Wotlis (bouyon 2025).

Are Fifin and FiFiNDaSteppa the same name?

The “Unstick” description links to an Apple Music page whose address names the single with FiFiNDaSteppa and Wotlis. This site quotes that variant as it stands, with no other source explaining it.

Where is Fifin from?

No opened source says. The “dominica” tags on DJ Taffy’s channel cover the whole channel, not the artist: the origin stays undocumented.
