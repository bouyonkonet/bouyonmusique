CK Gee: Back It Up (Bouyon 2K25) with Pinpin OSP and Ted La

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

CK Gee comes onto this site through a February 2025 title that spells out its genre: “Back It Up (Bouyon 2K25)”, shared with Pinpin OSP and Ted La, produced by ThaWizZarD and recorded, mixed and mastered at VGM Product’. Four years earlier, the same channel released a bouyon remix of “Lighter” on which an act billed CK G shares the bill with Trouble Sto, under DJ Micky’s name. This profile covers both titles, explains why it attaches them to the same artist, and says plainly that the link is a deduction: no biography, no origin and no legal name appear in the opened pages.

Back It Up, the 2025 title

The “Back It Up” video came out on February 7, 2025 on the VGM PRODUCT’ channel, under a title that leaves no doubt about the label: “CK GEE x PINPIN OSP x TED LA, BACK IT UP (BOUYON 2K25)”. The listing names the three voices, CK Gee, Pinpin OSP and Ted La, and credits production to ThaWizZarD, the name under which this site covers the beatmaker Tha Wizzard. Recording, mixing and mastering are credited to VGM Product’, and the description closes on the tags bouyon, tendance and VGM. The video version runs 2 minutes 24.

The same day, the audio version reached YouTube Music through Pinpin OSP’s automatic channel, distributed by DistroKid under the label line 8199102 Records DK. That listing flips the billing: Pinpin OSP is the main artist, and CK Gee appears as a guest next to Ted La, spelled “CK gee”. The release date, February 7, 2025, matches the video. It is the same song seen from two angles: the video puts CK Gee first, the distribution lists CK Gee among the guests.

Lighter, the 2021 remix, and the question of the name

On February 7, 2021, VGM PRODUCT’ released “Lighter (Bouyon Remix)”, a DJ Micky title with CK G and Trouble Sto. The credits are detailed: production by DJ Taffy and Skyo Beatz, recording, mixing and mastering at VGM Product’, direction by Nvthvn.J and DJ Micky, dancers from the Wash Queen crew, and an ISRC code, FR-9W1-21-00919, printed in the description. The word bouyon sits in both the title and the hashtags. The description points to an Instagram account with the handle ck.gz.

CK G in 2021, CK Gee in 2025: this site attaches both billings to the same artist because they come from the same house, VGM Product’, and because the spellings are very close. No opened page says so in so many words. It is therefore a deduction, flagged as one, and this profile will be corrected if a primary source separates two artists.

What the file does not contain

The opened pages give no biography, no legal name, no birthplace and no home island for CK Gee. The VGM Product’ studio and its partners, Pinpin OSP and Ted La, work in the French Caribbean bouyon circuit that this site covers on its page about bouyon in Guadeloupe, but a studio affiliation is not a birth certificate: no origin is assigned to CK Gee.

The verified listening therefore comes down to two titles, four years apart, from one studio. If CK Gee releases more titles that state their genre, this page will be completed and dated, like the other releases tracked in our new bouyon of 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is CK Gee?

CK Gee is an artist credited on “Back It Up (Bouyon 2K25)”, with Pinpin OSP and Ted La, released by VGM Product’ on February 7, 2025. No public biography was found in the pages opened for this profile.

Who produced Back It Up?

The video description credits ThaWizZarD as producer and VGM Product’ for recording, mixing and mastering. The DistroKid release carries the label line 8199102 Records DK.

Are CK G and CK Gee the same artist?

This site treats them as one artist because both billings come from the same channel, VGM Product’, and the names are very close. No page confirms it explicitly: it is a deduction, flagged as such.

What is Lighter (Bouyon Remix)?

A DJ Micky title with CK G and Trouble Sto, released on February 7, 2021 by VGM Product’, produced by DJ Taffy and Skyo Beatz, with the ISRC code FR-9W1-21-00919.

Where is CK Gee from?

None of the opened pages names an origin for CK Gee. This site therefore assigns no island.
