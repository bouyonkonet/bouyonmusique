DeLuxe F: bouyon medleys and riddims from Dominica

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

DeLuxe F is a bouyon artist from Dominica. His credits name him Dean “DeLuxe F” Desiree, and he does nearly everything himself: writing, mixing, mastering, sometimes shooting the videos. His catalogue runs from 2015 to 2025, with an unusual habit of putting out each song both as a vocal and as an instrumental riddim.

From DjeasyPromos to DeLuxeFMuzik

His first songs went around in 2016 on the DjeasyPromos channel. “GeTTinG On”, on the Wahjang Riddim, passed 25,000 views. In December, “I Got A Feeling”, with Kevni, came out as a bouyon remix for the 2017 carnival, followed by “Mad Or Wa” and “Lollipop” on a riddim by DjBryce.

In 2020 he launched his label, DeLuxe F Entertainment, with the album “Dis One Gonna Hurt Dem”. Edday sings on “Plenty Money” and “Sweet”, Gucci C on “Shake Dat”, and Keks Mafia on “Anything For A Like”. In January 2022 he followed with “When I Was Young”, featuring Reo and Trilla G on “Undercover”, then “Bouyon To The World”, seven of his songs redone as bouyon remixes.

Cuz She Like It and Gwada Bouyon

In October 2022 he released the “Cuz She Like It” EP in four versions: the original, a Gwada Bouyon take and both instrumental riddims. J2Mo and Maskman made the riddim, and DeLuxe F wrote, sang and mixed. The video, shot by Ambo Visuals, thanks the staff of Jungle Bay in Dominica.

He used the same approach on “My Woman Better”, sung with Gucci C. First released in January 2022, it came back in February 2023 as a Gwada Bouyon version produced by Dj Dirtee. The video carries the 971 and 767 codes, Guadeloupe and Dominica. The same year he sang “Up is Up” with Quan, and later released its riddim on its own.

The medleys: Free Up, The Good Time, Y U Mad

In May 2023, the “Bouyon Free Up Medley” brought together Jerquan Mark, Alyjah Baron and Nahim Bedminster on a riddim by DjCM. DeLuxe F mixed, shot and edited the video, which has passed 26,000 views. In August, “The Good Time”, billed as soca and bouyon, added Tidy, Keanu and Gucci C over a JyzBeatmaker production: close to 38,000 views.

His biggest number came in February 2025 with “Y U Mad”, a bouyon medley he wrote for Alyjah, Keyshia, Lexie and Talickson, keeping one verse for himself. It has passed 51,000 views. In between he released “Link Up” with Trixx and “Zoom Ta La La” with Ground God, then “Let's Do It” with Edday and Alyjah in September 2025.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DeLuxe F?

A bouyon artist from Dominica, credited as Dean Desiree. He writes, mixes and masters most of his songs on his DeLuxeFMuzik label.

What are his bouyon songs?

I Got A Feeling (2016), Bouyon To The World (2022), Cuz She Like It (2022), the Gwada Bouyon version of My Woman Better (2023), Bouyon Free Up Medley (2023), The Good Time (2023) and Y U Mad (2025).

What is his most watched song?

The Y U Mad medley, with Alyjah, Keyshia, Lexie and Talickson, past 51,000 views, ahead of The Good Time at close to 38,000.

Why does he release his riddims separately?

He often puts out an instrumental version of his songs, such as Roll On That Bumpa, How I Like It, Catching Feelings, Up is Up and Feel My Riddim, which other artists can pick up.

Who produces his songs?

J2Mo and Maskman on Cuz She Like It, Dj Dirtee on My Woman Better, DjCM on the Bouyon Free Up Medley, JyzBeatmaker on The Good Time and MegaBeats on How I Like It.
