Kai Du M: the La Grande Borne rapper whose records carry the Blax signature

Published October 20, 2026

Kai Du M is a French drill rapper, documented between 2021 and 2023 by one album, “SHOOTER CLAN”, a numbered freestyle series and a handful of collaborations. This profile does not file him among bouyon singers, and it should say why he appears on this site: three tracks on his album, his founding freestyle and his single “SC” all carry, in the official platform credits, the signature of one composer, credited as Blax or Dj Blax, the same name this site documents at the heart of the bouyon and shatta writing network on our Dj Blax page. The Kai Du M file is therefore a credits file as much as an artist file: dated records, named labels, verified features, and, facing them, everything the sources do not say, starting with his real name.

The frame: a drill rapper, and a credits thread that leads to this site

Start with classification honesty. The platforms file Kai Du M under hip hop: Viberate presents him as a French contemporary hip hop artist, Shazam classes his records as Hip-Hop/Rap, and AllMusic files his single “Rain-T” under rap. His own 2021 video carries the keywords Drill and DrillFr, and his first label is called 100 Fidjo INC, written 100FIDJO in his own descriptions. Nothing, in any source consulted, presents him as a bouyon singer, a shatta artist or a French Caribbean musician, and this profile will not pretend otherwise.

What ties him to the world of this site fits in one credit name. On “Freestyle GB 2”, his 2021 record, Shazam credits a composer named Blax. On “SC”, Deezer credits the same Blax. On the album “SHOOTER CLAN”, Deezer credits a composer named Dj Blax on three tracks, “Acteur”, “Maudit” and “Violence urbaine”, and the YouTube page of that last record spells the signature out in full: Composer: DJ Blax. Dj Blax is one of the composers this site follows from catalog to catalog, from bouyon gwada to shatta and French R&B, because his name keeps surfacing in the credits of a whole generation of Caribbean and diaspora records. Kai Du M is one of the places that name lands inside French rap, and it is on that ground, documented credit by credit, that his profile exists here.

The rapper territory, for its part, is given by his own metadata. The description of his “FREESTYLE GB #2” video, published on his channel, ties the record to the keywords Grigny and La Grande Borne, the Grigny housing estate in Essonne whose initials match the GB of his freestyle series. This profile reads GB as Grande Borne because the artist himself places those words in the page of his own record, and it states that as a document backed reading, not as a biography. The same description gives his social handles of the time: Instagram Kai_dum350 and Snapchat Kaizerdeepboyz, two identifiers published by the artist in 2021 and reproduced here as period traces, with no guarantee that they are still active.

The rest of the public identity fits in three banners. 100 Fidjo INC, his first label, executive producer of his 2021 video and publisher of his first singles. Hazi.Corp, the label of “Freestyle GB#4” in 2023, whose name echoes Le Hazi films, the production company behind his 2021 video. And a distribution step up to national level for the album: the YouTube page of “Violence urbaine” states that “SHOOTER CLAN” is a 100 Fidjo INC record distributed by Epic Records France, a division of Sony Music Entertainment France. An estate rapper, an independent label, a major distribution: the frame is that of early 2020s French drill, and the following sections tell it record by record.

The Freestyle GB series: the estate as a signature

The first dated trace of the name is a freestyle. “Freestyle GB 2” was released on May 30, 2021 on 100 Fidjo INC, in French, at 146 BPM according to Shazam, which counts it at about 1,500 identifications. The credits page is already complete: lyrics by Kai Du M, composition by Blax, mixing and mastering by Hoby Studio. The single has a video, produced by Le Hazi films and directed by Kuss, whose description credits musical realization and arrangement to Z.E and mixing to Mathis, with 100FIDJO Inc as executive producer. Two pages, two angles: the platform keeps the composer Blax, the video keeps the arranger Z.E. This profile keeps both credits as they stand, because that is exactly the kind of gap this scene credits produce, and it is not for this site to settle between them.

The number in the title raises its own question. A “Freestyle GB 2” implies a first instalment, and the series runs at least to number 4: “Freestyle GB#4” was released on June 11, 2023 on Hazi.Corp, in French, at 71 BPM, with a songwriting credit to Steff Becker and mixing by Shino, for a little over 200 Shazam identifications. Between 2 and 4, the sources consulted document neither a GB 1 nor a GB 3 as platform singles, and this profile does not invent them: the series is attested at numbers 2 and 4, full stop. The contrast between the two numbers is instructive in itself: GB 2 is a fast, drill cut record, while GB#4 drops to 71 BPM, the slow tempo of atmosphere freestyles. The series is a banner, not a fixed format.

Around the series orbits a short record that counts double for this site: “SC”, three minutes and four seconds according to Deezer, written by Kai du M and composed by Blax. The record appears in the artist most popular songs list on Viberate, alongside “Freestyle GB 2” and the album tracks. “SC” shows that the work with Blax is no single freestyle accident: before the album even arrives, the composer signs at least two of the rapper records, the May 2021 freestyle and this single, and he will return on three tracks of “SHOOTER CLAN”. It is that repetition, and that alone, which justifies the full section this profile gives to the Blax thread.

The sound of this first period, as the credits describe it, is a small workshop sound: one recurring composer, Blax, an engineer named Mathis who will reappear as recording engineer on “Violence urbaine”, a regular video director, Kuss, also credited as co-writer of “Batman” on the album, and a home video production, Le Hazi films, whose name will serve again for the Hazi.Corp label in 2023. Estate drill often works this way, in a short and stable team, and the Kai Du M catalog is a readable specimen of it: almost every credits page leads back to the same four or five names.

“SHOOTER CLAN” (2022): thirteen tracks, and SDM as a guest

The album “SHOOTER CLAN” came out in 2022 on 100 Fidjo INC, distributed by Epic Records France according to the YouTube page of its closing track. Deezer gives the full matter: thirteen tracks, thirty nine minutes in total, a dense drill album format with no filler. The list deserves a full reading, because it is the centre of the file: “Intro”, “Ça débite”, “Froid”, “Acteur”, “Shooter Clan”, “BS”, “Batman”, “Maudit”, “Bedo”, “Chez toi”, “Bénéfice”, “Toc Toc / Mapess” and “Violence urbaine”.

The guests state the record ambition. “Ça débite”, the second track, hosts SDM, one of the most established French rappers of his generation, a feature that by itself lifts the album beyond the perimeter of the estate. “BS” hosts MALTY 2BZ, “Bedo” hosts Theodorepourdevrai, and the closing track, “Violence urbaine”, brings together Kossdar, Rosdagang and Murs OG alongside Kai Du M. Rosdagang is no passing guest: Viberate also places “C’est Nous”, their joint record, at the top of the rapper most popular songs. The clan of the album title is therefore not just an image: it is a circle of recurring voices, documented track by track.

The composers, for their part, draw two pillars. The first is AC Beats, signed on the title track “Shooter Clan”, on “Batman”, “Chez toi”, “Bénéfice” and “Toc Toc / Mapess”: he is the album house producer, and his name will return in 2023 on “Rain-T”. The second pillar is Dj Blax, signed on three tracks: “Acteur”, “Maudit” and “Violence urbaine”. Around them the list hands out the remaining signatures: Cage Got That Cold on the introduction, Flem on “Froid”, LS Beats and SA Beats on “BS”, Exi on “Bedo”, So Eazy alongside AC Beats on the long closing “Toc Toc / Mapess”, five minutes and forty one seconds, the most extended track on the record.

“Violence urbaine”, the closing track, is the best documented record on the album. Its YouTube page, generated by 100 Fidjo INC, dates the release to June 10, 2022, credits the four voices as performers and lyricists, names DJ Blax as composer, Sur Neptune on mixing and mastering, and Mathis Potter as recording engineer, the same Mathis already credited on the 2021 video mix. An album track whose date, composer, recording engineer and distributor are all known: that is the level of trace this site asks for in order to write, and “SHOOTER CLAN” reaches it on its closing track as on its full list.

The Blax thread: five credits, two spellings, one network

Gather the pieces. Credit one: “Freestyle GB 2”, May 2021, composer Blax according to Shazam. Credit two: “SC”, composer Blax according to Deezer. Credits three, four and five: “Acteur”, “Maudit” and “Violence urbaine”, composer Dj Blax according to Deezer, confirmed as DJ Blax in full on the YouTube page of the last one. Five credits, two spellings, Blax and Dj Blax, spread across the rapper three formats: the freestyle, the single and the album. No source consulted says whether Blax and Dj Blax are two signatures of one person or two distinct people, and this profile does not decide it: it notes that both spellings work on the same catalog, in the same years, which, in the credits of this industry, most often points to one workshop under two name forms.

Why this thread matters to a bouyon site: because Dj Blax is also a composer of the bouyon and shatta world, whose trace this site follows on its Dj Blax page, alongside singers from the French West Indies. The same credit name therefore links Grigny drill to the studios that build the records our bouyon playlist follows week after week. It is not a kinship of style, and this profile claims neither that Kai Du M sounds bouyon, nor that Dj Blax imported anything into his drill: it is a kinship of workforce, the circulation of one composer between French speaking scenes, which is exactly the kind of fact credits can establish and biographies never tell.

The comparison has a clear limit, and it must be written: Kai Du M raps in the French of France, on drill BPMs, 146 for GB 2, 71 or 72 for the slow 2023 records, very far from bouyon tempos, and his themes, reading his titles, are those of his genre: the clan, the street, Batman and Tupac as rap culture figures. Viberate lists among his popular titles “GTA #23”, “Fumer”, “Glock”, “Tupac” and “Charge”, a vocabulary that owes nothing to the West Indies. The link is professional, not musical, and this profile keeps it in that exact place: one shared composer, nothing more, nothing less.

One question remains, the one every credits reader asks: is this Blax of Grigny the Dj Blax of the bouyon credits? The sources opened for this profile give names, dates and titles, but no biographical page on the composer that would let anyone sign the identity of the two signatures. This site therefore chooses the wording the documents support: one credit name, under two spellings, present on both sides, and a Dj Blax page gathering what the credits allow us to know. On the day a source names the man behind the signature, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site.

2023: the year of collaborations

After the album, the Kai Du M catalog becomes a catalog of invitations, and 2023 lines up four dated ones. On February 10, 2023, he guests for Blasko on “Addition”, a track on the album “Carré Noir” on Mal Luné Music, in French, with about 780 Shazam identifications; the Qobuz page of the album, which dates it to 2022, places it second of seven tracks and credits Kai Du M as featured artist. On February 24, 2023, he appears on “Ça Bosse”, a track on the “Jeune OG” album on Play Two, alongside Mr Dillinger, DJ MS and Jeune OG, at 72 BPM. Two features in fifteen days, on two other rappers albums: the name is circulating.

The same year, Viberate lists two further records among his recent releases: “402” and “Rain-T”. For “Rain-T”, AllMusic gives the complete page: a two minute and forty five second single, in 2023 on Universal, where Kai Du M and Le Rain-T are primary artists, and whose composers and producers are ALF4 and AC Beats, the house producer of “SHOOTER CLAN”. The workshop loyalty therefore runs beyond the album: the beatmaker of “Batman” and “Bénéfice” still signs the following year single. For “402”, the sources opened give the title and the year, with no complete credits page, and this profile cites it at that level of certainty only.

Viberate finally documents two collaborations that state the level of the scene where the name appears: “Charge”, with Alkpote, and “Valar Morghulis”, with Ol’ Kainry, Alkpote and A2H. Alkpote is a veteran of French rap, Ol’ Kainry a rapper of the previous generation: sharing records with them places Kai Du M, for the length of those features, inside the circuit of established rappers in France, far from a simple estate phenomenon. This profile cites those two records at the level of the source that gives them, the Viberate artist page, with no dates or labels that the opened pages do not provide.

And then the thread stops, at least in the sources consulted. After “Freestyle GB#4” in June 2023, no more recent release was found during this research: no announced album, no documented 2024 or 2025 single, no interview. The public Kai Du M catalog therefore fits in three years, 2021, 2022 and 2023, one album, two freestyle numbers, one single with Blax and a run of features. That is little for a career, and it is exactly the matter of the last section: what this file, honestly, does not allow anyone to say.

The limits of the file: no name, no biography, no documented sequel

Draw up the list of absences, because it is part of the profile. No source consulted gives Kai Du M real name, nor an age, nor a birth date, nor an interview, nor a press portrait. The only identity information is what he published himself in 2021 in his video description: an Instagram account, Kai_dum350, a Snapchat account, Kaizerdeepboyz, and the keywords Grigny and La Grande Borne. The sources say nothing verifiable about his life, and this site does not invent: this profile is a catalog and credits profile, and it owns that in its title as in its sections.

The figures, too, must be read at their scale. The Shazam identifications counted run from about 120 for “Ça Bosse” to about 1,500 for “Freestyle GB 2”, with about 780 for “Addition”: those are the counters of a local scene and features artist, not those of a national hit, and no chart placing, no certification and no award appears in the sources consulted. The presence of SDM on “Ça débite” and the Epic Records France distribution of the album state a real ambition and a professional circle, but the catalog remains that of a La Grande Borne rapper whose public trace is thin and stops, for now, in 2023.

What remains is the proper value of this file for the readers of this site: five credits of a composer named Blax or Dj Blax, checkable in a few clicks on Shazam, Deezer and YouTube, linking Essonne drill to the writing network that the bouyon and shatta scene shares with French rap and R&B. It is to document those crossings, invisible in biographies and obvious in credits, that this site keeps profiles like this one. If Kai Du M publishes a new album, a biography or a verified official account, this profile will be completed and dated. In the meantime, listening starts with “Violence urbaine” and “Freestyle GB 2”, continues with “SC”, “Acteur” and “Maudit”, and the context is read on our Dj Blax page and in our bouyon playlist.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kai Du M?

A French drill rapper, filed as contemporary hip hop by Viberate and Hip-Hop/Rap by Shazam. His own video metadata ties him to Grigny and La Grande Borne, in Essonne. He is documented from 2021 to 2023 by the Freestyle GB series, one album, “SHOOTER CLAN”, and a run of features. No source consulted gives his real name, his age or a biography.

What is the Kai Du M album?

“SHOOTER CLAN”, released in 2022 on 100 Fidjo INC and distributed by Epic Records France according to the YouTube page of “Violence urbaine”. Deezer counts thirteen tracks for thirty nine minutes, including “Ça débite” with SDM, “BS” with MALTY 2BZ, “Bedo” with Theodorepourdevrai and “Violence urbaine” with Kossdar, Rosdagang and Murs OG.

What is the link between Kai Du M and Dj Blax?

A credits link, verified on the platforms. Blax is credited as composer of “Freestyle GB 2” on Shazam and of “SC” on Deezer. Dj Blax is credited as composer of three “SHOOTER CLAN” tracks on Deezer: “Acteur”, “Maudit” and “Violence urbaine”, the last one confirmed by the track YouTube page. No source consulted says whether Blax and Dj Blax are one and the same person. See Dj Blax.

What does GB mean in Freestyle GB?

The sources do not define it in so many words. The reading kept by this profile rests on the description of the “FREESTYLE GB #2” video, published by the artist, whose keywords tie the record to Grigny and La Grande Borne, the Grigny estate: GB is read there as the initials of La Grande Borne. The series is attested at number 2, in May 2021 on 100 Fidjo INC, and number 4, in June 2023 on Hazi.Corp.

Which rappers has Kai Du M worked with?

On his album: SDM, MALTY 2BZ, Theodorepourdevrai, Kossdar, Rosdagang and Murs OG. As a guest: Blasko on “Addition” in 2023, Jeune OG, DJ MS and Mr Dillinger on “Ça Bosse” in 2023, Le Rain-T on “Rain-T” in 2023. Viberate also cites “Charge” with Alkpote and “Valar Morghulis” with Ol’ Kainry, Alkpote and A2H, plus “C’est Nous” with Rosdagang among his most popular songs.

Is Kai Du M a bouyon artist?

No, and this profile says so plainly. Kai Du M is a drill rapper from Essonne, with no documented bouyon or shatta record. He appears on this site because his credits cross those of Dj Blax, a composer this site follows across bouyon and shatta catalogs: it is a credits link between French speaking scenes, not a kinship of style.

Where is Kai Du M from?

Grigny, in Essonne, according to his own metadata: the description of his “FREESTYLE GB #2” video, published in 2021, carries the keywords Grigny and La Grande Borne, the estate whose initials name the Freestyle GB series. It is a reading backed by a document the artist published, not a biography: no source gives his birthplace.

Who produced Kai Du M's album “SHOOTER CLAN”?

Two pillars according to Deezer. AC Beats, the house producer, signs “Shooter Clan”, “Batman”, “Chez toi”, “Bénéfice” and “Toc Toc / Mapess”, the last with So Eazy. Dj Blax signs “Acteur”, “Maudit” and “Violence urbaine”. The rest goes to Cage Got That Cold, Flem, LS Beats and SA Beats, and Exi. The album came out on 100 Fidjo INC, distributed by Epic Records France.

Who produced “Freestyle GB 2” by Kai Du M?

Shazam credits Blax on composition, Kai Du M on lyrics and Hoby Studio on mixing and mastering. The video, directed by Kuss for Le Hazi films, credits musical realization and arrangement to Z.E and mixing to Mathis, with 100FIDJO Inc as executive producer. Released on May 30, 2021, at 146 BPM, it is his most identified record on Shazam.

What is “Violence urbaine” by Kai Du M?

The closing track of “SHOOTER CLAN”, released on June 10, 2022 according to its YouTube page. Kai Du M brings together Kossdar, Rosdagang and Murs OG on it, credited as performers and lyricists. DJ Blax signs the composition, Sur Neptune the mixing and mastering, and Mathis Potter the recording. It is the best documented record on the album.

What is “Rain-T” by Kai Du M and Le Rain-T?

A 2023 single released digitally on Universal according to AllMusic, running 2 minutes 45, where Kai Du M and Le Rain-T are primary artists. Its composers and producers are ALF4 and AC Beats, the house producer of “SHOOTER CLAN”: the workshop loyalty therefore runs beyond the album.

What are Kai Du M's most popular songs?

Viberate puts “C’est Nous” with Rosdagang first, then “GTA #23”, “Charge”, “Freestyle GB 2”, “Intro”, “Batman”, “SC”, “Fumer”, “Glock” and “Tupac”. On Shazam, “Freestyle GB 2” leads with about 1,500 identifications, ahead of “Addition” with Blasko, about 780. Those are local scene counters, with no documented chart placing or certification.

Which label does Kai Du M release on?

His first label is 100 Fidjo INC, written 100FIDJO in his descriptions, executive producer of his 2021 video and publisher of “SHOOTER CLAN”, distributed by Epic Records France, a division of Sony Music Entertainment France. In 2023, “Freestyle GB#4” came out on Hazi.Corp, whose name echoes Le Hazi films, the company that produced his 2021 video.
