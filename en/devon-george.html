Devon George: Overload, Gwada Man, bouyon 2022 to 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Devon George, who also signs as Devon G or simply Devon, has been posting bouyon on his own channel since December 2021. His early pages present themselves explicitly as bouyon music, with writing credits in his name. Since 2026 he also appears in a trio with Redpunchdrip and Strosey, on two titles produced by Xavvoknockin and by Mushroom. Six primary pages opened during this research tie him to bouyon. None says where he was born or where he lives.

The beginnings: Overload and Gwada Man

“Overload”, posted on December 8, 2021 under the title bouyon 2022, is the most explicit page in the file: Devon George is credited as artist and songwriter, the riddim is produced by Bryelle Joseph, known as Bryce Beatz, recording takes place at Bobos Studios, and one line of the description names the genre outright, bouyon music. The lyric is a darker confession than the genre average, centred on pain and loyalty.

“Gwada Man” followed on January 27, 2022. The title itself does not carry the word bouyon, but the tags the artist placed on his own video file it as bouyon 2022. Devon George wrote it; Bryce Beatz, Dj Beaw and Dj Shingo produced it, and Bjorn Davis, known as BjornFSE, mixed it. The word Gwada, which comes back in this title and in his 2023 lyrics, is a circuit marker, not a statement of origin.

Clown Show and FQ to DQ

“Clown Show”, of April 9, 2023, titled bouyon 2023, rides a Jackie Beatz riddim, with writing and vocals credited to Devon George, recording at SMTG Records and mixing credited to C4. That last name is carried by several artists, and this site ties it to no existing profile without a source that says which one.

After a visible pause on his channel, he returned on July 25, 2026 with “FQ to DQ”, an official audio titled bouyon 2026, on a Dbossriddimz riddim, recorded, mixed and mastered by Mushroom. The page credits him as Devon “Devon G” George, which confirms the link between his two signatures.

The trio with Redpunchdrip and Strosey

In January 2026, Knockin Records released “Why Should I?”, by Redpunchdrip, Strosey and Devon, produced by Xavvoknockin and titled bouyon 2026. The distribution listing, released on January 21, 2026, credits him this time under his full name, Devon George. On July 22, 2026, the Gama Production channel posted the answer, “Why Did I?”, by Devon, Redpunchdrip and Strosey, produced, mixed and mastered by Mushroom, with guitar by Jo.

Both pages carry the hashtag sxmbouyon2026, which ties these releases to the bouyon circuit of Saint Martin, see our page on bouyon in Saint Martin. Devon George, for his part, puts the Dominica Bouyon tag on his own 2021 and 2022 videos. These are two scene signals, not an origin: no opened page names his island, so this site assigns none.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Devon George?

A bouyon writer and performer who has posted on his own channel since December 2021, under the names Devon George, Devon G or Devon. No public biography was found.

Which Devon George bouyon titles are verified?

“Overload” (bouyon 2022), “Gwada Man” (tagged bouyon 2022), “Clown Show” (bouyon 2023), “Why Should I?” with Redpunchdrip and Strosey (bouyon 2026), “Why Did I?” with the same pair (bouyon 2026) and “FQ to DQ” (bouyon 2026).

Are Devon, Devon G and Devon George the same person?

Yes according to the opened pages: the “FQ to DQ” page credits him as Devon “Devon G” George, and the “Why Should I?” distribution listing gives Devon George where the video title reads Devon.

Who produces Devon George?

Bryce Beatz on his first titles, Jackie Beatz on “Clown Show”, Dbossriddimz and Mushroom in 2026, and Xavvoknockin on “Why Should I?”.

Where is Devon George from?

No opened source says. His Dominica Bouyon tags and the sxmbouyon2026 hashtag on his trio titles are circuit signals, not a stated origin.
