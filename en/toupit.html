Toupit: String Volé and Un problème ! with Leyou, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Toupit is a voice who mostly works in pairs. His name comes forward in 2026 next to Leyou, on “String Volé”, posted by Leyou with Bouyon2k26 in the title, and then on “Un problème !”, tagged bouyonmusic in its description. The distributed version of “String Volé” also credits him as co producer. Before that, he appears as a guest of DJ Leguste and in a trio with Locksey and Le Rebelle. This profile sticks to what those pages say, and leaves his origin open because no source names it.

String Volé, the title that put him forward

On February 22, 2026, Leyou’s channel posted “Leyou ft TOUPIT, STRING VOLE”, with Bouyon2k26 attached to the title and the tags bouyon and 2k26 in the description. It is short, 2 minutes 19, and the description points to two TikTok accounts: Leyou’s and one named toupit130. It is the clearest page in the file: it names Toupit, it names the genre, and it does so on his partner’s channel.

The distributed version came out on June 24, 2026 as “Leyou ft TOUPIT (STRING VOLÉ)”, on a DistroKid release page under the name KELESH, with the line LEYOU play two. The credits there are few but specific: Leyou on sampler, Toupit as co producer. So Toupit is not only a guest voice on this record; going by that credit line, he also helped build it.

Un problème !, the duo continues

On August 16, 2026, Leyou posted a second shared title: “Leyou feat Toupit, Un problème !”, 2 minutes 04, with bouyonmusic among the description tags. The page is bare, with no detailed credits, but it is enough to show the partnership did not stop at one song. For more releases from the same calendar, see new bouyon in 2026.

Earlier titles, with no genre label

Two more pages carry Toupit’s name, and neither one names bouyon. “Pa Fè Konsi”, posted on June 21, 2024 on Locksey’s channel, brings together Toupit, Locksey and Le Rebelle, produced by ThaaWizzard and directed by Skydeluxe. Readers can find Locksey on the Ti Locksée page and the producer on the Tha Wizzard page, both already on this site.

“An sé an chien (feat. Toupit)” came out on December 12, 2025 under the DJ LEGUSTE label, according to its release page, with an extra credit to allan sainte luce. It belongs in Toupit’s story, but the opened page states no genre: it is named here and kept out of the structured data.

What this file does not say

No opened page gives a biography, a legal name or an origin for Toupit, so this site assigns him no island. Two similar names also turn up on YouTube, Toupite and Toupit MC, on other channels and with other partners. Nothing in the pages consulted proves they are the same artist, and this profile does not merge them. If there is a link, it will take a source that shows it.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Toupit?

Toupit is an artist credited on several recent bouyon titles, mainly next to Leyou: “String Volé”, posted with the Bouyon2k26 label in February 2026, where he is also credited as co producer, and “Un problème !”, in August 2026. No public biography was found.

Which Toupit titles are tied to bouyon?

Two titles whose pages state bouyon: “String Volé” with Leyou (Bouyon2k26, tag bouyon) and “Un problème !” with Leyou (tag bouyonmusic). “Pa Fè Konsi” and “An sé an chien” also exist, but their pages name no genre.

What is his role on String Volé?

He is Leyou’s guest voice, and the DistroKid release page credits him as co producer, with Leyou credited on sampler.

Has he worked with DJ Leguste?

Yes. “An sé an chien (feat. Toupit)” came out on December 12, 2025 under the DJ LEGUSTE label, according to the release page. That page does not state a genre.

Are Toupit, Toupite and Toupit MC the same person?

Nothing confirms it. Those names appear on different channels, with no page linking them. This profile covers only titles credited to Toupit under this spelling.

Where is Toupit from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns him no origin.
