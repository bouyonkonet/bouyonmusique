Bouyon music: Contour Airlines links Dominica and Trinidad

By Smokiller · Published November 4, 2026

One month after launch, the Contour Airlines nonstop route between Dominica and Trinidad and Tobago is filling its aircraft. Discover Dominica Authority CEO Marva Williams said load factors during the introductory period have remained positive, speaking on the sidelines of the Wato Festival on October 30, per Caribbean Pulse.

The load factor has been great

The load factor has been great, Williams told Caribbean Pulse. We want to continue that interest. Dominica is not only about festivals, we are not only about nature, adventure, you can get married and so many other things here, she said, inviting Trinidadians to make the island their next destination.

The airline offered an introductory price of US$160 one way, per Caribbean Pulse. Trinidad and Tobago remains a top source market for visitors to Dominica and also serves as a stopover point for travellers from Canada and Latin America.

The schedule and the aircraft on the route

The Dominica to Trinidad flight operates on Monday and Thursday, departing at 6:05 pm and arriving at 7:25 pm, while the return leg runs on Tuesday and Friday, departing at 8:30 am and arriving at 9:50 am, per Caribbean Pulse. The service continues onward to St Thomas in the US Virgin Islands on Friday and to San Juan, Puerto Rico, on Thursday.

Contour Airlines operates the route with a 30 seat Embraer ERJ-145 regional jet, offering extra legroom and complimentary snack and drink service, per Caribbean Pulse. The first rotation ran in early October: the inaugural Dominica to Port of Spain flight on October 5, and the first arrival at Douglas-Charles Airport on October 6.

A route that fills a real gap

The need was real: Caribbean Airlines ended its service to Dominica on June 1, in a move that cut several Eastern Caribbean routes, following combined losses of US$18.84 million, with the Dominica route alone accounting for US$730,000 in losses, per Caribbean Pulse. Williams thanked both the government and Contour Airlines for working to re-establish the connection.

The DDA wants to maintain the momentum generated by the recently concluded World Creole Music Festival and the Independence festivities. Williams cited more than 527,728 visitors welcomed by Dominica so far in 2026, a figure reported by Caribbean Pulse, and said her team keeps engaging stakeholders and source markets.

The headwinds, stated plainly

The DDA chief also named the obstacles: strikes in Guadeloupe, Martinique and France that could affect arrivals, part of the North American diaspora less comfortable with travelling, and fuel prices that have made travel more expensive, per her remarks reported by Caribbean Pulse.

For music fans the route matters: Port of Spain is a natural bridge between the soca stages of Trinidad and the bouyon stage of Roseau, under ninety minutes away by air. The next big meeting of the two islands is the World Creole Music Festival, already announced for October 29 to 31, 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Contour Airlines flight times between Dominica and Trinidad?

Dominica to Port of Spain on Monday and Thursday, 6:05 pm to 7:25 pm. Port of Spain to Dominica on Tuesday and Friday, 8:30 am to 9:50 am, per Caribbean Pulse.

Which aircraft operates the Dominica to Trinidad route?

A 30 seat Embraer ERJ-145 of Contour Airlines, with complimentary snack and drink service, per Caribbean Pulse.

Is the new Dominica to Trinidad route working?

Yes, per Discover Dominica Authority CEO Marva Williams, who said load factors in the introductory period remained positive and the load factor has been great, with an introductory fare of US$160 one way.
