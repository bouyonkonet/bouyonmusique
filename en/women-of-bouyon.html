Women in bouyon: voices that count

Bouyon has long been told in the masculine, bands and producers. But women’s voices are everywhere in the genre in 2026: headlining, in collaboration, and at the heart of the tracks that travel the furthest.

Maurane Voyer and the collaboration generation

Maurane Voyer from Martinique, the voice of Joli BB with Reo, represents this new reality: collaborations where the singer is not a decorative guest but half of the track, with her audience and her island.

A female audience driving it

On social media as at carnival, the female audience does a large part of the work of spreading bouyon: dances, covers, shares. Bouyon dance, in particular, lives through its women creators as much as through its producers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it called bouyon?

Bouyon means soup in Kweyol: the genre stirs Dominica's traditional musics together with electronic production.

Is bouyon part of Creole culture?

Yes. Bouyon was born in Dominica's villages and carnival and soundtracks Creole celebrations across the French Caribbean.
