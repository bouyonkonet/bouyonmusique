ShaSha: Shadows, Dead To Me and other bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

ShaSha, full name Sharisse Fabien according to the credits, has been singing bouyon since 2025. The songs first came out through Maskman Muzik, then, in 2026, on the DFinite Music label with producer Hxnter. ShaSha is not Shashabim, a different artist.

2025, the Maskman year

On May 16, 2025, the Maskman Muzik channel posted “Shadows”, billed Bouyon 2025, with Floyd “Mr. Challenger” Challenger. Arade “Maskman” Moses produced and arranged it, ThaWizZarD mixed it and Cornel Phillip mastered it. At over 63,000 views, it is still ShaSha's most played song.

The pair returned on July 10 with “Dead To Me”, also billed Bouyon 2025, its vocals recorded by Joe Remo at Grand Bay Studio in Florida. August 5 brought “Bouyon Summer”, with Fetty Mark, carried by live bass from Giovanni Green.

Hxnter and DFinite Music

In 2026 the work moved to a new team. On May 21, Big Red Productions posted “Put Something On Me”, billed Bouyon 2026, with Big Red HD. Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt produced it and Judah “Diyoute” Jno. Baptiste mixed it. The two singers had already released “Superhero”, dated May 6 on Deezer.

On July 2, “What Do You Want From Me” came out on the DFinite Music channel, with the same team and the same Bouyon 2026 tag. It has passed 16,000 views. September 17 brought “Jealousy”, also produced by Hxnter, which has passed 34,000 views without naming a genre on its page.

A writer behind the voice

One name runs through almost all of ShaSha's credits: Donavan Fabien, sometimes listed as Donsi or Dons Eye. He wrote Shadows, Dead To Me, What Do You Want From Me and Jealousy, co-wrote Put Something On Me with Big Red HD, and designs the covers for DFinite Music, where he is executive producer.

ShaSha sings and is often credited on backing vocals as well. On Jealousy the ad-libs are shared with a voice credited as Golda. None of the pages opened names an island of origin, and this profile assumes none.

Frequently asked questions

Who is ShaSha?

Sharisse Fabien, an artist whose songs have been billed as bouyon since 2025, first through Maskman Muzik, then on DFinite Music.

Is ShaSha the same artist as Shashabim?

No. Shashabim is a different artist with a separate page on this site. ShaSha's credits give the name Sharisse Fabien.

What are ShaSha's bouyon songs?

Shadows and Dead To Me with Floyd Challenger (Bouyon 2025), Put Something On Me with Big Red HD and What Do You Want From Me (Bouyon 2026).

Who produces ShaSha's music?

Arade “Maskman” Moses in 2025, then Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt in 2026, with Diyoute on the mix. Donavan Fabien writes most of the lyrics.

What is ShaSha's most watched song?

Shadows, with Mr. Challenger, at over 63,000 views on the Maskman Muzik channel.
