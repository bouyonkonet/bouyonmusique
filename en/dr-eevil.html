Dr Eevil: We Trippy with T.C and Sticky, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Dr Eevil is a bouyon artist known, at the time of this research, through a single verified release: “We Trippy (Bouyon)”, published on YouTube under the title “DR Eevil, T.C AND Sticky - We Trippy (Bouyon)”, where his name comes first, ahead of T.C and Sticky, his two co-artists. The release listing, for its part, is generous with credits: it names him among the three writers of the record, credits Riddim Box Studio as executive producer and recording studio, Jackie Beats and DJ Blaze on production, and Yw3 Ent on mixing and mastering. It also gives his public contacts: Instagram, TikTok and a professional email address. What it does not say is where he comes from, or who he is beyond this record, and no independent coverage was found. This profile therefore documents one record, one team and verified contacts, and it states openly everything it does not know, starting with his origin, which no opened source states in words.

Who Dr Eevil is: a headline name, an account, and no biography

The artist public name is written Dr Eevil. It is the spelling in the title of his release, in the writing line of the listing, “written by: Dr Eevil, sticky and T.C”, and on his Instagram account opened during this research, which shows the name “Dr.EeviL” under the handle dreevi1. Variants circulate inside the same listing: the contacts header writes “Connect with dreevil”, all in lower case, and the published professional email address is dreevilofficial@gmail.com. These forms all point to the same artist, the one on the record “We Trippy”, and this site keeps Dr Eevil as the main form, the one on the record billing.

The Instagram account verified by direct opening is a personal account which, at the time of opening, counted 118 followers and 140 following, and whose website field links out to a YouTube video. That linked video could not be opened during this research, its page returned a request limit error, and it is therefore not counted as a verified record: it appears neither in the text as a confirmed release, nor in the structured data of this page. The account confirms the public existence of the artist under this name and handle, nothing more, and this profile leaves it there.

For the rest, the silence is complete, and it is reported as such. No opened source gives a full name, an age, a place of birth or residence, or a path before “We Trippy”. No interview, no music press article and no platform page with a biography were found: searches on his name return his own release and unrelated namesakes, led by the film character Dr. Evil, who obviously has nothing to do with this bouyon artist. Dr Eevil therefore exists publicly, for now, as the first signature on a collective record and as an account of 118 followers: that is little, it is exact, and it is the material of this profile.

We Trippy: one record, three signatures, and full credits

The verified record is titled “We Trippy”, and its page files it explicitly under bouyon: the full release title is “DR Eevil, T.C AND Sticky - We Trippy (Bouyon)”. Dr Eevil appears there as the lead artist, named first, with T.C and Sticky as co-artists. The listing states that the record was written by the three artists themselves: “written by: Dr Eevil, sticky and T.C”. It is the only release opened and confirmed for Dr Eevil during this research, and it alone feeds the structured data of this profile, under its exact title.

The listing gives a complete production chain, which sets it apart from many releases on the scene. Riddim Box Studio is credited twice there: as executive producer, “Executively produced by: Riddim box studio”, and as the studio where the record was recorded, “Record by: Riddim box studio”. Production of the record is by Jackie Beats and DJ Blaze, “Produced by: Jackie beats and dj blaze”. Mixing and mastering are by Yw3 Ent, “Mix and master by: Yw3 Ent.”. The spellings of the listing are kept here exactly as it prints them, because credits are documents: they say who did what, and they allow the record to be placed inside a circuit of named studios and producers, rather than in a cloud of approximate attributions.

What the listing does not give matters too: no release date in words, no label distinct from the credited studios, no duration, no link to a streaming page for the record in the opened description. This profile fills none of those gaps. It records that “We Trippy” is a collective record of three voices, built by a team of five names around them, and that Dr Eevil is both its first billed signature and one of its three declared writers.

The origin question: what the studio circuit suggests, and what it does not prove

The “We Trippy” listing nowhere states, in words, where Dr Eevil comes from. No country, no island, no town: the word origin appears in no form in the opened description. This profile therefore assigns him no country. It is a method rule of this site, and it applies here in its strictest form: an artist whose origin is stated by no opened source stays an artist of undocumented origin, even when everything around him invites a guess.

Honesty requires saying what invites the guess, and why this site refuses it. The mixing and mastering credit, Yw3 Ent, ties the record to a studio circuit that other recent bouyon release listings also draw on: during this same research, another bouyon record listing consulted credited Yw3 Ent, in the form “Yw3 Ent (DiYoute)”, with the recording and mixing of a record by Skrazy, Dboss and Ras B presented as a bouyon of the 2027 generation. That neighbourhood places “We Trippy” inside an active, named bouyon making circuit. But a studio circuit is not a birth certificate: artists record, mix and master their records far from home, and a technical credit does not state the nationality or the residence of a singer. Until an opened source states Dr Eevil origin in words, this page will leave it blank, and say why.

The practical consequence for the reader is simple. Any filing of Dr Eevil under a precise flag, read elsewhere without a source, should be treated as unverified as of October 20, 2026. If the artist, his team or press coverage one day states his origin, this profile will be completed and dated. In the meantime, the record files where its own listing files it: under bouyon, with no flag added, and our bouyon soca page gives the context of the genre, from the bouyon soca started by Dada in 2003 in Dominica, per the first hand testimony of Smokiller, to the studios making the sound of today.

His public contacts: what the listing publishes, verified one by one

The “We Trippy” listing ends with a contacts block under the header “Connect with dreevil”, and that block is part of the verifiable facts of the file. It gives an Instagram account, / dreevi1, a TikTok account, / d.octor_e_evi, a Facebook entry printed in the truncated form “/ 17yi5ovgpb”, and an email address, dreevilofficial@gmail.com. This profile reports those details because the artist publishes them himself in his release listing, for the public and for professionals. It does not comment on them beyond their verification.

The verification gives mixed results, and they are reported as they stand. The Instagram account was opened and confirmed: it shows the name Dr.EeviL, the handle dreevi1, 118 followers and 140 following at the time of opening, and it is the only social account of the artist confirmed by direct opening, hence the only one linked as such in the structured data of this page, alongside the release page itself. The TikTok account page returned no readable content on opening: the handle d.octor_e_evi is therefore reported as an identifier given by the release listing, not as a verified account. The Facebook entry, printed in a truncated form that does not allow it to be opened reliably, is neither linked nor completed: guessing the full address of a page from a fragment would be exactly the kind of invention this site forbids itself.

The professional email address, dreevilofficial@gmail.com, is published by the listing and repeated here as a fact of the listing. In its own way, it says something about the artist stage: a direct contact point, with no agency or label interposed in the opened credits, for a record made inside an independent studio circuit. Our bouyon playlist and our bouyon songs page follow that circuit record by record, as verifications land.

What the file does not say: the limits, stated

This profile ends with its limits, because they are the most useful information it can give about Dr Eevil today. One verified record, “We Trippy”, shared with T.C and Sticky, whose profiles this site publishes in the same wave. No confirmed solo record, no second opened collective record, no stage work, no concert date and no counted reception are documented in the sources consulted. The YouTube video linked from his Instagram account could not be opened during this research and is therefore claimed as nothing at all: not a second record, not a video, not proof of anything.

Two confusions are to be avoided, and this profile sets them aside by name. The first is namesake confusion: Dr Eevil, with two e letters and one l, is a bouyon artist; the Dr. Evil of the search screens is a comedy character, and no content about him was used here. The second is team extrapolation: the credits of “We Trippy” name Riddim Box Studio, Jackie Beats, DJ Blaze and Yw3 Ent as the team of this record, and nothing in the opened sources says that this team is Dr Eevil permanent team, or that he is signed to anyone. A credit covers the record it covers, not a whole career.

While waiting for what comes next, the listening fits into one record: “We Trippy”, in full, because that is where, and only where, the voice of Dr Eevil is documented, alongside those of T.C and Sticky, on a production by Jackie Beats and DJ Blaze, a recording by Riddim Box Studio and a mix and master by Yw3 Ent. If the artist publishes a solo record, a biography or a stated origin, this profile will grow with him, and its modification date will say so, like every page on this site.

Who is Dr Eevil?

Which Dr Eevil record can be heard?
