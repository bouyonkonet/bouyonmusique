Ian Stylez: Crazy Jump and Gi Mi Gi Mi, Crop Over bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Ian Stylez is a singer on the Crop Over scene, the big Barbados festival. He has posted soca on his own channel since 2019, and in spring 2026 he put out two songs billed bouyon: “Gi Mi Gi Mi” with JK The Boss on the Cassava Riddim, then “Crazy Jump” on 10Star Records' Okra Slime Riddim. His videos are still small, under 1,000 views each.

From Bajan soca to the bouyon hashtag

His YouTube channel, under the name ian stylez, starts in July 2019 with “Sweet d.”, billed “Bajan soca/calypso”, followed by a teaser, “Shake It Up”, in 2020. In April 2024 “Badmind Friends”, on the One Chicken Riddim, is billed soca, yet its description already stacks the hashtags bouyon, bouyonriddim and bouyonsoca next to cropover2024, barbados and bajansoca.

None of the pages we read names his home island or his real name. Everything he posts does place him in the Crop Over season, and that is how bouyon reaches him: through Barbadian riddims taking up the Dominican tempo.

This Time and 10Star (2025)

In June 2025 the BimTraveler channel posted “Ian Stylez | This Time | Jam Jam Riddim | Crop Over 2025”. It is the only one of his songs with full credits: written by Ian Stylez, produced by 10Star and 2Mono, mixed and mastered by 2Mono.

The 10Star name matters for what came next. The same producer launched the Okra Slime Riddim in 2026, billed “Barbados Bouyon 2026” song after song, with Sharkie opening it and Quan De Artist among the voices.

Gi Mi Gi Mi and Crazy Jump (2026)

On April 28, 2026, BimTraveler posted “Ian Stylez x JK The Boss - Gi Mi Gi Mi” on the Cassava Riddim, with “Crop Over 2026” and the word bouyon in the title. The same riddim carries other songs posted on the channel under the same label, by Intrigue with Rag Crew, Mistah RB and King Glutt9n. The page gives no production credits.

On June 4, 2026 he followed with “Crazy Jump” on the Okra Slime Riddim, titled “Bouyon Crop Over 2026”, again with no credits. It is the latest song we found on that riddim. The two videos had 781 and 579 views when read, and no release under his name shows up on Deezer.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ian Stylez?

A singer on the Barbados Crop Over scene, active on YouTube since 2019, who posts soca and, since 2026, songs billed bouyon.

Where is Ian Stylez from?

The pages we read do not say. His channel talks about “Bajan soca” and uses the hashtags barbados and bajansoca, and all his recent songs target Crop Over.

What are his bouyon songs?

“Gi Mi Gi Mi” with JK The Boss on the Cassava Riddim (April 28, 2026) and “Crazy Jump” on the Okra Slime Riddim (June 4, 2026), both billed Crop Over 2026 bouyon.

Has he worked with 10Star?

Yes: “This Time” (Jam Jam Riddim, Crop Over 2025) is produced by 10Star and 2Mono, and “Crazy Jump” sits on 10Star Records' Okra Slime Riddim.

Where can I hear his songs?

On YouTube, mainly on the BimTraveler channel and his own; no release under his name was found on Deezer.
