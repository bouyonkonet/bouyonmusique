By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

OG Park, credited as Omar “OG Park” Phillips, enters this site through a duet: “Whale Fu See”, with Rum Lawd, on the Whale Oil Riddim released by Boss Raw Records on April 13, 2025. The rest of his visible path, from work with Skinny Fabulous to Vincy Soca titles, belongs to the Saint Vincent soca circuit.

Whale Fu See, a title with two labels

“Whale Fu See” was released on April 13, 2025 on the Boss Raw Records channel as “OG Park & Rum Lawd - Whale Fu See (Whale Oil Riddim) | Bouyon 2025 | Vincy Soca 2025”, running 3 minutes 27. The description lists five writers: Rasheid “Rawsheid” Sutherland, Enrique “RumLawd” Matthews, Giovanni “GEO” Collins, Rajiv “Suhrawh” Sutherland and Omar “OG Park” Phillips. Suhrawh handles production, mixing and mastering, and Rasheid Sutherland executive production for Boss Raw Records.

The ONErpm release, filed under the riddim name Whale Oil Riddim, carries the same date of April 13, 2025. OG Park reposted the title on his own channel on October 1, 2025, without a description. The double label, bouyon and Vincy Soca, says what this record is: a title from the Vincentian circuit that claims the colour of the sound born in Dominica, on a riddim shared by several voices.

The rest of the path, on the soca side

The other opened pages place OG Park in soca. “Wey Yuh Wha”, on the Road Runner Riddim, is presented as Vincy Soca 2023, performed by Omar “OG Park” Phillips and produced by Rhous “Rhome” Morgan. “My Head Very Bad”, with Skinny Fabulous, published on March 27, 2026 on Skinny Fabulous’s channel, is produced by Prince Pronto and Vibez Productionz and distributed by MarvMent.

None of those titles states a bouyon tie, and they stay in the text here, outside the structured data. They place the artist without stretching his bouyon file beyond “Whale Fu See”.

Origin and limits

The Vincy Soca labels on his titles tie OG Park to the Saint Vincent circuit, but no opened page gives his birthplace or home. This site keeps to those signals, attributed to the titles that carry them.

OG Park’s bouyon file therefore comes down to one title, shared with Rum Lawd, credited in detail and dated. That is enough for a short and exact profile, and this profile will grow if more titles labelled bouyon appear under his name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is OG Park?

OG Park is the stage name credited to Omar “OG Park” Phillips, co writer and performer of “Whale Fu See” with Rum Lawd, titled Bouyon 2025 and Vincy Soca 2025.

Which riddim is Whale Fu See on?

The Whale Oil Riddim from Boss Raw Records, produced, mixed and mastered by Rajiv “Suhrawh” Sutherland, released on April 13, 2025.

Who wrote Whale Fu See?

Five writers are credited: Rasheid Sutherland, Enrique Matthews (Rum Lawd), Giovanni Collins, Rajiv Sutherland (Suhrawh) and Omar Phillips (OG Park).

Where is OG Park from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. The Vincy Soca labels on his titles tie him to the Saint Vincent circuit, as a signal.

Has OG Park worked with Skinny Fabulous?

Yes: “My Head Very Bad”, Skinny Fabulous x OG Park, published on March 27, 2026. That title states no bouyon tie.
