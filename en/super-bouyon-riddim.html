Super Bouyon Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Super Bouyon Riddim is a riddim produced by Kross Beatz, with Shattah Ayow as executive producer. Its five vocal tracks came out together on April 17, 2025, on Shattah Ayow's YouTube channel and as an album on Deezer. The videos bill it as "Vincy Soca 2025", and Flanka's "Want Ah Cat" is by far the song that got the most plays.

Kross Beatz on production, Shattah Ayow behind the release

All five YouTube descriptions share one set of credits: riddim produced by Kross Beatz, executive produced by Shattah Ayow, mixed and mastered by Chemical X, artwork by Graflex. So the riddim you sometimes see listed under Kross Beatz and the one released under Shattah Ayow's name are the same thing.

On Deezer, the "Super Bouyon Riddim" album sits under Shattah Ayow and is dated April 17, 2025, like the videos. It holds only the five vocals, with no instrumental, and every track runs about two and a half minutes. No named record label appears on it.

Bouyon in the name, Vincy soca on the label

The riddim puts bouyon in its name, yet every video is also filed under "Vincy Soca 2025", Vincy being the common nickname for St Vincent and the Grenadines. It isn't a rare case: a riddim carrying the bouyon name, released inside another island's soca season. Our bouyon vs soca page looks at where that line falls.

The sources we read don't say where each singer is from. The mixes kept the same double label: M.A.D. One Music posted a "Super Bouyon Riddim Mix (Soca 2025)" and Echo Chamber put out its own, also filed as soca.

Want Ah Cat, the song that carried it

When we checked on October 28, 2026, Flanka's "Want Ah Cat" had passed 111,000 views on Shattah Ayow's channel. Ozarie's "For De Iron (Diane)" and DJ 20's "Pencil" followed at around 14,000 to 15,000. The Ozarie song is also up on the Ozarie channel.

The last two are quieter: about 4,900 views for D'termine's "Play Wid De Kitty" and 3,600 for "Super Wine", performed by Shattah Ayow, who also runs the project. For how one riddim gets shared between several voices, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Super Bouyon Riddim?

Kross Beatz, according to the YouTube descriptions, with Shattah Ayow as executive producer and Chemical X on mixing and mastering.

When did it come out?

On April 17, 2025, the date of the five videos on Shattah Ayow's channel and of the Deezer album.

Who sings on the Super Bouyon Riddim?

Flanka, Ozarie, DJ 20, D'termine and Shattah Ayow, one song each.

Is it bouyon or soca?

Both words are on the videos: Super Bouyon Riddim for the riddim, Vincy Soca 2025 for the season. The mixes posted since file it as soca.

Is the Kross Beatz Super Bouyon Riddim the same one?

Yes. Kross Beatz produced it and Shattah Ayow released it as executive producer; there is only one project.
