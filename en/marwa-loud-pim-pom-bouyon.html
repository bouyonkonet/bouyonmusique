Pim-pom: Marwa Loud brings bouyon into French pop

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

On 16 October 2026, French singer Marwa Loud unveiled Pim-pom, a single available on streaming platforms with an accompanying video. ActuaNews, which covered the release in France, presents the track as an exploration of the rhythms of bouyon, a musical genre originating in Dominica. This page follows that article, fact by fact.

A single released on 16 October 2026

Per ActuaNews, Pim-pom has been available since Friday 16 October 2026. The single follows Fendi gang, recorded with rapper TK, among other recent releases.

The article states that no new album directly linked to Pim-pom has been confirmed, so the track should be presented as a standalone single, and that is exactly what this page does.

Bouyon, explained to a French audience

ActuaNews describes bouyon as a genre that appeared in Dominica, in the Caribbean, marked by a strong rhythmic dimension, electronic percussion, prominent bass lines and structures built for dancing.

The article notes that bouyon is gaining visibility through digital platforms and through artists who carry its sounds to new audiences. Marwa Loud choice sits, per the article, inside that circulation between rap, urban pop, dancehall and Caribbean music.

Dance, energy and self-confidence

The track favours a festive, dance-led mood while carrying a theme of self-affirmation: following your own choices and keeping confidence in your personality, regardless of outside opinion, per ActuaNews.

The video supports that approach by giving the single a visual dimension, built for social networks and video platforms.

What this track says about the bouyon moment in France

When a French urban pop artist devotes a full single to bouyon rhythms, and a French music outlet explains the genre to its readers starting from Dominica, bouyon takes a visibility step in mainland France.

This page attributes to the track no stream counts and no chart placing, which no opened source documents at this stage. Its reception over time remains to be seen, as ActuaNews itself notes.
