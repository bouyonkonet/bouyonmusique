MB On The Track, the producer behind the Rock Bouyon effect

By Smokiller · Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

MB On The Track is a bouyon producer whose main ground is Instagram, under the account @mbonthetrack, with about 9.1K followers at the October 17, 2026 check. He bills himself as a Diamond Producer and sells custom beats by DM, with a management email address shown in his bio. His real name and his base are not established in our records, and this page does not invent them. What can be told instead is his format: bouyon beats shown on video, a largely French audience, and a reel of a few seconds, Rock Bouyon, that was enough to launch the Producer Session series at Bouyon Shattaz.

A producer of the Instagram era

For the new generation of bouyon producers, Instagram is not a side showcase, it is the studio opened to the public. MB On The Track publishes his work there, recruits his clients there and tests his ideas there. His Diamond Producer billing and his offer of custom productions by DM say the essentials of his model: an independent producer, reachable directly, who makes beats to order for artists who discover him online. That model, unthinkable fifteen years ago, has become the norm for bouyon, a genre whose audience lives between the Antilles, Dominica, France and the diaspora.

The audience of MB On The Track runs largely on the French side, which places him in the same circuit as the producers followed on our page about the producers and passers: makers of sound who work for the French-speaking bouyon and shatta market without always standing in the front line. On this site their place is central, because modern bouyon is decided in those exchanges of beats, messages and versions, long before the official videos.

It should also be said what this profile does not contain: no confirmed real name, no verified home base, no rebuilt official discography. That restraint is deliberate. An honest profile is worth more than a biography filled with guesses, and the visible work of MB On The Track, his videos, his reactions and his community, is enough to explain why Bouyon Shattaz follows him.

The October 16, 2026 reel: dancing to his own beat

On October 16, 2026, MB On The Track posted a simple and effective reel: himself, at night, in a bus shelter, dancing to his own bouyon instrumental. The on-screen text announced Rock Bouyon effect. At the Bouyon Shattaz check, the video had 242 likes and 11 comments. The numbers are modest, and that is exactly what makes the moment interesting: no big machine, no expensive video, just a producer physically showing what his beat does to a body, in a street setting everyone recognises.

Rock Bouyon is his own framing, shown on his video. At this stage it is not a verified movement or an established trend, and this page does not present it as one. It is the name a producer gives to the energy of his instrumental, with what the word rock suggests of direct, raw and immediate. In bouyon, those formulas matter: they give the audience a word to share the video with, and give artists an idea of the kind of beat they can order.

The comments under the reel tell the logical sequel: people asked where the song was and demanded a long version. That is the classic path of the 2026 bouyon snippet, an excerpt creates demand, and demand decides the release. The producer does not push a finished product, he measures a desire in public, within a few hours.

Producer Session 01: Bouyon Shattaz puts producers in front

From that reel, Bouyon Shattaz built Producer Session 01, the pilot of a series entirely dedicated to the producers behind the sound. The principle is simple: take the moment where the producer presents his beat, frame it in the platform’s vertical format, and support it with French and English captions written under the Bouyon Shattaz search standard, so these behind-the-scenes profiles become findable. The draft was filed for review, awaiting Smokiller’s decision, and nothing in this series is published without his go-ahead.

The choice of MB On The Track to open the series is no accident. His reel already contains the whole story: a producer alone, a beat that speaks before words, and an audience that answers. The Producer Session series wants to document that moment for other makers of the genre, from the same family as the profiles gathered in the site’s bouyon playlist, where singers’ tracks meet the work of the producers who carry them.

For artists looking for a beat, this kind of content is worth a catalogue: in fifteen seconds you hear the touch, the energy level and the kind of dance being aimed at. For producers, it is rare recognition. Bouyon has long celebrated its singers and its bands; putting producers in front of the camera, with their name in the title, is part of the groundwork Bouyon Shattaz is doing.

Ordering a custom beat: the new bouyon circuit

The offer shown by MB On The Track, custom productions ordered by direct message, describes the current economic circuit of independent bouyon. An artist hears an excerpt, writes to the producer, discusses tempo, key and intended use, then the beat is made or adapted for his voice. That short circuit favours speed and closeness, but it also needs clear rules on ownership and release conditions, the same subject Bouyon Shattaz handles with its partner producers in its pool agreements.

This site does not print the management email address shown in the producer’s bio: contact goes through his Instagram account, as he organised it himself. That is also a basic rule of respect for the profiles followed here, to publish only what serves the reader, the public account and the work, not the backstage contact details.

Until further documented releases arrive, the profile of MB On The Track remains that of a producer to follow through his videos: every reel is a business card, every comment a measure of demand. If Rock Bouyon becomes a full track, this page will be updated with its verified references, as it is for every artist on the site.
