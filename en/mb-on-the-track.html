MB On The Track: Rock Bouyon and custom productions

MB On The Track, @mbonthetrack, is a producer who presents himself as a Diamond Producer and offers custom bouyon productions by private message. He got noticed with a video where he dances to his own beat, Rock Bouyon.

The video that launched the Producer Session

In his video, MB On The Track dances at night, at a bus stop, to his own bouyon instrumental, with the text Rock Bouyon effect on screen. The comments demand the long version of the track. Bouyon Shattaz made it the first episode of its Producer Session series, dedicated to the producers behind the sound.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the key bouyon artists?

WCK created the genre, Asa Bantan is widely called the King of Bouyon, and the new generation includes Reo, Ridge, Signal Band and Shelly, while producers like Smokiller export the sound.

Who publishes these artist pages?

BouyonMusique.com is published by Bouyon Shattaz, the platform of Dominican-born producer Smokiller, with facts verified artist by artist.
