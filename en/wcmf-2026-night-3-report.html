Bouyon music: WCMF 2026 night 3, the closing report

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

Night three, the final night of the World Creole Music Festival 2026, was played on Sunday 25 October at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. The report published by Writeups 24 on 27 October leads with Maxi Priest, Uncle Waffles and Fridayy. This page follows that report name by name, and adds the Sint Maarten press account of DJ Outkast.

A night that starts earlier

Per the report, the gates opened at 4 pm and the performances started at 5 pm, earlier than the first two days, giving visitors the chance to enjoy even more genres.

The stadium offered multiple zones, from general admission on the ground pitch to premium elevated VIP decks, and the standalone Day 3 ticket was priced at 300 Eastern Caribbean dollars, per the same report.

Les Aiglons, Jean-Marc Ferdinand and Diblo Dibala

Guadeloupe band Les Aiglons entertained the audience with Creole music, brass instruments leading, in a show the report presents as a link between generations of the French Caribbean public.

Jean-Marc Ferdinand also performed. Dominica Festivals, quoted by the report, summed his set up this way: Jean Marc Ferdinand brought the music, and the crowd brought the energy. Congolese guitarist and singer Diblo Dibala delivered a full band soukous show.

Maxi Priest, Uncle Waffles, Fridayy and the bouyon names of the night

Maxi Priest, the British-Jamaican reggae singer, was one of the main highlights of the night, with his well known songs sung back by the Windsor Park crowd, per the report.

The line-up also included South African DJ and producer Uncle Waffles, American R and B artist Fridayy, and the Dominican and bouyon names of the evening: Shelly and Signal Band, Nice, Gwada G, Shaxx and DJ Taffy, plus Trinidadian soca artist Machel Montano. Melody God is also named in the opening of the report.

DJ Outkast after Maxi Priest, Machel Montano closes

The Sint Maarten press, The People s Tribune SXM, tells the same Sunday from another angle: DJ Outkast, known as The Cut Creator and marking 30 years in DJing, played the festival for the first time, following Maxi Priest, and raised the Sint Maarten flag before the crowd.

Per that second account, Machel Montano closed the night. DJ Outkast also hosts The Power Hour on Laser 101 FM, and his slot shows the room the festival leaves for neighbouring islands, well beyond its headline names.

The next edition is already dated

The festival is jointly hosted by the Government of Dominica, the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority, as part of the island work to promote its cultural heritage, the report notes.

Organizers have confirmed that the next edition of the World Creole Music Festival will take place from 29 to 31 October 2027. Night 3 of 25 October 2026 therefore closes the festival, but Creole season continues: Jounen Kweyol arrives on Friday 30 October.
