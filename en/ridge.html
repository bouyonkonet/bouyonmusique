A Dominican bouyon artist, keyboardist and producer

Ridge comes from Dominica, the island where bouyon was born. That origin is not a simple line on a biography: it places his work at the source of the genre, in the country whose bands, producers and audiences invented this music in the late 1980s. Our history of bouyon page traces that birth. Ridge belongs to the current generation of that source, the one that must keep the Dominican sound and carry it onto the big Creole stages at the same time.

His distinction is his double profile: a bouyon artist, but also a keyboardist and a producer. Many of the current faces of bouyon are first of all voices, DJs or studio producers. Ridge presents himself with an instrument at the centre, the keyboard, and with the culture of a musician who can build a track and play it live. That difference may sound technical, it is in fact about style: it changes the way a bouyon concert sounds, holds together and reaches the audience.

On Instagram he is present as @crossdbridge, a creator account that stood at 25,567 followers and 714 posts at our October 17, 2026 check. That volume of posts speaks of a regular presence, work shown over time, and not a single appearance around one song. His bio also lists him as a Bleu Magic rum ambassador, an element of his public image that we report as it appears in his own presentation.

Bouyon Artist of the Year at the 2026 Caribbean Music Awards

In 2026 Ridge was a Caribbean Music Awards nominee for Bouyon Artist of the Year, according to Dominica News, and then won the award: results published in September 2026 give him the title of bouyon artist of the year. He had already won the same title in 2024 and 2025, two years in a row according to Dominica News Online. That win is the verified marker of his year: it places a Dominican artist, keyboardist and producer at the top of the bouyon category of a Caribbean ceremony. For a genre looking for recognition equal to its influence, seeing a bouyon category, Dominican artists nominated and a Dominican winner counts for a lot.

This award should be read with precision. We report the 2026 nomination as published by Dominica News, and the win as given by the results published in September 2026. This win also confirms the three-times-awarded line in his bio: Dominica News Online reports that in 2025 he took Bouyon Artist of the Year for the second consecutive time, after 2024, the first year of the bouyon category. With 2026, Ridge holds three consecutive titles, all reported by news outlets.

For readers discovering Ridge through this page, the award is a simple entry point: if he is the 2026 Bouyon Artist of the Year at the Caribbean Music Awards, he deserves careful listening, in particular live. The rest of the file, with WCMF and the rehearsal footage from Roseau, shows an artist preparing the stage like a band musician, and not like a name placed over a backing track. That is where his award takes its most concrete meaning.

The Roseau rehearsals: Nikita Christmas and the live band

On October 17, 2026 his drummer and musical director Nikita Christmas, present as @drummerfridge, posted rehearsal footage from Roseau. From it we learn the make up of the live band: Nikita Christmas on drums, with Ableton and musical director duties, bass with @nattybilloriginal, guitar with @hilroyh, and keyboards with @earlsonmatthew and @mrbrockshot. That list of musicians and roles comes from the rehearsal posts themselves, and it gives the WCMF concert a precise reality, far from a simple bill announcement.

Nikita Christmas's role deserves a closer look: drummer and musical director, with Ableton among his duties. In a modern bouyon concert, that post holds the balance between live playing and programmed elements. The drummer gives the physical energy, the musical direction keeps the whole set coherent, and Ableton lets the studio sounds connect to the stage without crushing the musicians. Around him, a bass, a guitar and two keyboard players draw a complete band, built to play, and not only to accompany a track.

Bouyon Shattaz turned that rehearsal into a full coverage package, because it reveals Ridge's signature: a keyboardist producer leading a real live band, in an era strongly marked by producers and studio formats. Why a real live band changes bouyon on stage

Bouyon was born as band and street music, before it also became studio music, riddims and social media music. Keeping a real live band at the centre of a concert is therefore not a nostalgic return, it is a choice that reconnects the genre with part of its history, the part traced in the history of bouyon. When Ridge presents himself with a drummer as musical director, a bassist, a guitarist and keyboard players, he defends a way of playing where the audience sees the work as much as it hears it.

That format also has an effect on the sound. A live band can stretch a passage, answer the crowd, bring a chorus back or let a keyboard breathe at the right moment. The producer who is himself a keyboardist knows both sides: the precision of the recorded track and the freedom of the concert. That double skill is what makes Ridge's profile valuable in the 2026 landscape, among artists often defined by a single role, singer, DJ or beatmaker.

For young artists watching Ridge, the lesson is practical: learning an instrument, knowing how to rehearse with a band, naming your musicians and preparing musical direction are skills that last longer than a viral moment. His Bouyon Shattaz coverage insists on that point, because Dominican bouyon needs strong stages as much as strong songs. An artist able to hold Windsor Park with a band rehearsed in Roseau sends a message of professional level to the whole scene. It also gives a simple reading grid for the rest of his season: judge Ridge first on stage, where his keyboardist and producer profile makes full sense, then on the way his band carries Dominican bouyon in front of a festival audience. Nominations draw attention and bills confirm the place, but with Ridge, rehearsals and the concert are what state the artist's real level in 2026.

That is also why this profile names no unverified songs and repeats no self claimed award count. Ridge's strongest public evidence in October 2026 is not a list, it is a working band in Roseau, a dated festival confirmation and a reported awards win. Readers who want to judge him have something better than claims to go on: they can watch how the band was built, note every musician's role, and then hear what that preparation produces on the Windsor Park stage.

For Dominica itself, that working image matters beyond one concert. A home artist preparing the national Creole festival with a full local band, named player by player, shows younger musicians a route that still exists inside bouyon: learn an instrument, build a band, rehearse in public and carry the island's sound onto its biggest stage as a group, not only as a file sent to a DJ.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ridge?

Ridge, or Mr Ridge, is a Dominican bouyon artist, keyboardist and producer, known on Instagram as @crossdbridge. He won Bouyon Artist of the Year at the Caribbean Music Awards in 2024 and 2025, according to Dominica News Online, and again in 2026 according to results published in September 2026, and he is confirmed on the WCMF 2026 bill, where he leads a real live band.

Where is Ridge from?

He belongs to the current generation of that source, the one that must both keep the Dominican sound and carry it onto the big Creole stages, up to Night 1 of WCMF 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau, in front of his island's audience.

Ridge or Mr Ridge: what is his artist name?

Both forms refer to the same artist: Mr Ridge, artist name Ridge, according to his profile, and both circulate on posters and in the press. On Instagram he posts as @crossdbridge, a creator account with 25,567 followers and 714 posts at our October 17, 2026 check. His bio also presents him as an ambassador for Bleu Magic rum.

What does Ridge do in bouyon?

Ridge is a bouyon artist, keyboardist and producer. Where many faces of current bouyon are first voices, DJs or studio producers, he presents himself with an instrument at the centre, the keyboard, and a musician's culture able to build a track in the studio and play it live with a full band.

What are Ridge's songs?

Ridge's listed recordings include the album “Cross D Bridge (Live)” (2021), with “Sunset Warm up”, “Come & Cocay”, “China Tune”, “Caught in Your Trance”, “Doh Call My Phone”, “Long Time I Watching You” and “Don't Go”, plus “Fym” (2025). Scene mixes also cite “No Love” with J Lion and “WDMA” with King Kembe.

Who has Ridge collaborated with?

Ridge is featured on “Blue”, from Trilla-G's album “Take Me As I Am” (2025), and mix tracklists credit him on “No Love” with J Lion and “WDMA” with King Kembe. On the production side, 1T1, from the Guadeloupean wave, has produced for Mr Ridge, and TIITII NBA cites his freestyle approach as an influence. See Trilla-G.

What awards has Ridge received?

According to Black Island Girl's report, Mr Ridge received the producer of the year award at the second Dominica Music Awards, on January 3, 2026. In 2026, nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards in the Bouyon Artist of the Year category according to Dominica News, he also won that award, according to results published in September 2026, after already winning it in 2024 and 2025, two years in a row according to Dominica News Online: three consecutive titles.

When does Ridge play WCMF 2026?

According to published guides, including Sweet TNT and writeups24, he plays Night 1, Friday October 23, 2026, at Windsor Park in Roseau. His place is confirmed by the official DDA media release. Before the festival, his name is also among the first twenty artists confirmed for Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl 2026, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau.

Who is in Ridge's live band?

According to rehearsal footage posted on October 17, 2026 by Nikita Christmas, the band includes Nikita Christmas on drums, Ableton and musical direction, @nattybilloriginal on bass, @hilroyh on guitar, and @earlsonmatthew and @mrbrockshot on keyboards. A bass, a guitar and two keyboards around a drummer who is also musical director: a band built to play, not to back a tape.

Where did Ridge play before WCMF 2026?

In May 2025, according to Black Island Girl, Mr Ridge closed the Strictly the Best Festival, Bouyon Edition, at Lotterie Farm in Saint-Martin, presented as the island's first festival devoted entirely to bouyon, with more than 5,000 people. His closing set ended with every bouyon artist present coming on stage together, as a gesture of solidarity.
