The Bouyon Boyz play their last two Nations League matches

By Smokiller · Published November 4, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026

Dominica's senior men's national football team, nicknamed the Bouyon Boyz, returns to action in November for its last two group matches of the 2026-27 Concacaf Nations League. Two dates to close a difficult campaign, against Puerto Rico and then the Cayman Islands.

Two matches in November

The team is scheduled to face Puerto Rico on Friday, November 13, before taking on the Cayman Islands on Tuesday, November 17. Both matches kick off at 3:00 p.m. Dominica time at the Leonora National Track and Field Centre in Guyana. They are the last two group matches of the 2026-27 campaign.

The team's nickname says everything about its link to the country: Bouyon Boyz, the boys of bouyon, like the music born in Dominica. The name follows the team in every report, in Dominica and abroad.

A difficult campaign

The campaign has been hard. It began with a goalless draw against the Cayman Islands at Windsor Park in Roseau on September 24. Three days later, the Bouyon Boyz lost 2-0 against Puerto Rico, again at home.

The team then travelled to Puerto Rico for two encounters against Guyana, with two heavy defeats, 5-1 and then 8-1. The two November matches are therefore a chance to close the group with points, after a run without a win.

Reon Cuffy's goal

The heaviest match, the 8-1 defeat against Guyana, was played at Estadio Centroamericano de Mayaguez, in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, according to Concacaf's report. Dominica still scored: Reon Cuffy put the Dominican goal on the scoresheet in the 67th minute.

That goal remains the notable fact of the campaign away from home. It kept the team from leaving the match with nothing on the board, against a Guyana side that finished top of the group.

What the last two matches decide

The November matches close the group stage. Against Puerto Rico, then against the Cayman Islands, the team plays for its final position in the group, after a draw, a home defeat and two away defeats.

For supporters, the two dates are also a chance to see the team together again, carried by a nickname that links football and the music of the country. The Bouyon Boyz close their 2026-27 campaign in November, and what comes next depends on the results of these two matches.
