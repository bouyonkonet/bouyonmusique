D'yonté: Tingah Ling and Beat Ah Rum, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

D'yonté has two tracks billed as bouyon on the Next Gen Records YouTube channel: “Beat Ah Rum”, on the Nasty Chant Riddim, released on June 14, 2024, and “Tingah Ling”, released on March 24, 2025. Both listings credit D'yonté Telemaque as writer and performer, and production to Next Gen Records, meaning Dré Soundz and JJW Productions. The descriptions also present the tracks as Vincentian soca, which places the artist within the Saint Vincent circuit as the page displays it. No biography or birthplace appears in the opened sources.

Two tracks, one team

The first verified track is “Beat Ah Rum”, posted on June 14, 2024, running 2 minutes 13, billed “Bouyon 2024” in its title and tied to the Nasty Chant Riddim. It had passed 10,000 views when we opened it. The second, “Tingah Ling”, arrived on March 24, 2025, labelled “Bouyon 2025” and running 2 minutes 45. On both, the writing and performing credit goes to D'yonté Telemaque, production to Next Gen Records (Dré Soundz and JJW Productions) and mixing and mastering to Hunttaflow Productions.

The visual credits change from one track to the other: the “Tingah Ling” cover is by Dan Formula and its visuals by Dré Soundz, while the “Beat Ah Rum” visuals are credited to JJW Productions. It is the mark of a small team doing everything in house, from beat to image, around a single label.

Bouyon in the title, Vincy Soca in the description

Both listings carry a double label. The title says bouyon, the first line of the description says “Vincy Soca 2024” or “Vincy Soca 2025”. The “Beat Ah Rum” hashtags add #Stvincent and #vincy, and the booking contact on “Tingah Ling” gives a number with the 784 area code, the code of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. This site therefore presents these releases as part of the Vincentian circuit, which is what the pages show, without assigning the artist a birthplace.

The Next Gen Records channel shows this double billing is not isolated: a large share of its 2024 and 2025 uploads are labelled bouyon, on the Tequila Riddim, the Love Maps Riddim or the same Nasty Chant Riddim, which also carries “Heh” by Elle. The bouyon riddim practice, one instrumental for several voices, is applied there as is, and the line between bouyon and soca stays deliberately porous.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file on October 23, 2026 comes down to two YouTube listings posted by the label, with clear credits but no biography, no interview and no streaming artist page found during this research. The civil name quoted here is the one in the credits, nothing more.

If D'yonté releases new tracks or a biography, this profile will be updated, like the pages followed in new bouyon in 2026.
