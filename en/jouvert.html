J’ouvert: carnival starts before the sun

Published September 8, 2026

J’ouvert, from jour ouvert in French Creole, is the dawn fete that opens carnival’s big days: from 4 in the morning, the street belongs to the crowd, to mud, to paint and to music, with bouyon leading in Dominica.

The freest fete of carnival

At J’ouvert, no beautiful costumes: clothes you can get dirty, mud, paint, cocoa depending on the island, and one simple rule, dance until sunrise. In Dominica, the sound systems and bands play the bouyon of the year while Roseau wakes up in fete mode.

A shared Creole tradition

J’ouvert lives across the whole Creole and Caribbean world, from Trinidad to Dominica and as far as the diaspora carnivals in Brooklyn and London. It is one of the moments when the link between bouyon and the street is most visible.

Frequently asked questions

What is the big bouyon festival?

The World Creole Music Festival is held every year in Roseau, Dominica, in October, at the time of the island's independence celebrations. The 2026 edition runs October 23 to 25 at Windsor Park: three nights of Creole music where bouyon shares the stage with cadence, zouk and kompa.

When is Dominica carnival?

Dominica carnival, Mas Domnik, takes place before Lent, with its two big days. J'ouvert, at dawn, is a peak moment for bouyon and soca: from 4 in the morning, sound systems and bands play the bouyon of the year while Roseau wakes up in fete mode. See Mas Domnik.

What is J'ouvert?

J'ouvert, from "jour ouvert" in French Creole, is the dawn fete that opens carnival's big days. From 4 in the morning, the street belongs to the crowd, to mud, to paint and to music, with bouyon leading in Dominica. It is the freest moment of carnival, when people dance until sunrise.

What do you wear to J'ouvert?

At J'ouvert, no beautiful costumes: people wear clothes they can get dirty, because the fete is lived in mud, paint and, depending on the island, cocoa. The only rule is to dance until sunrise. It is one of the moments when the link between bouyon and the street is most visible.

Where is J'ouvert celebrated?

J'ouvert lives across the whole Creole and Caribbean world, from Trinidad to Dominica, and as far as the diaspora carnivals in Brooklyn and London. In Dominica, it plays out in the streets of Roseau, where sound systems and bands run the bouyon of the year until morning.
