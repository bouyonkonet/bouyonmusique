Avatar Omish: the alternative rapper who entered bouyon through the cypher

Published October 20, 2026

Avatar Omish is the stage name of Omari James, a rapper, singer and songwriter from Dominica, a member of the Waitukubuli Artist Association, whose public path does not begin in bouyon but in alternative rap: Christian rap at the start, then a claimed fusion of emo rap, indie, rock and grunge, an EP titled “See Through Cup”, competitions in 2022 and 2023, and even an art video, “Bloom”, screened in an exhibition in 2024. Bouyon arrives in October 2026, suddenly and centrally: he is one of the six guest artists on Trilla-G “Up Next Bouyon Cypher, Vol. 1”, released on October 2, 2026 on Still Trill Entertainment, ranked number one on the Apple Music Top Songs chart in Dominica and viewed 83,000 times on YouTube in one week according to BuzzDA, then confirmed with the cypher collective at Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl 2026. This profile follows that unusual path, states the spelling difference in his full name between the sources, and separates clearly what is verified from what is not yet.

Omari James, known as Avatar Omish: the association portrait

The central biographical source of this file is the member page that the Waitukubuli Artist Association, an artists association based in Canefield, Dominica, devotes to Omari James. It presents him under his stage name, Avatar Omish, with the roles of singer, rapper and songwriter, and it describes him as a Dominican alternative rapper, known for his fusion of emo rap, indie, rock and grunge. It is an association portrait, written to present a member inside a frame that mixes music and visual arts, and it is also, to date, the only complete public biographical portrait of the artist found during this research.

On the full name, the sources differ by one letter group, and this profile says so instead of choosing in silence. The association writes Omari Daryl James. BuzzDA, in its cypher launch article, writes Omari Dayl James, in brackets behind Avatar Omish, in the list of the six guests with their full names. Dayl and Daryl obviously point to the same person, in the same role, but the exact spelling of the middle name is not settled by the opened documents. This profile therefore uses Omari James in the running text, gives both forms when the full name is quoted, and will wait for an official document, a platform credit or an official artist page to fix the spelling.

The rest of the association portrait is precise and sober. Influenced at first by Christian rap, Omish found his passion for writing songs at the age of 14: he wrote numerous amateur rap songs and recorded them on his phone, until he later got a laptop and a mic. Gifted, the page says, with a natural affinity for rapping, he then pivoted into other styles, constantly trying out new genres from song to song to explore his creative potential. His artist statement, quoted by the association, holds one ambition: making something, a song, that could be described as undeniably beautiful, a goal he says he has dedicated his artistic journey to.

That starting point matters for reading what follows. Avatar Omish is not a product of the bouyon system: no riddim school, no carnival band, no house producer in the sources. He is a writer who started with a phone and a bedroom, passed through gospel and open stages, and built an alternative identity before touching the national genre. When bouyon reaches him in 2026, it is therefore not a catalog conversion, it is a meeting, organised by an elder of the genre, Trilla-G, in a format designed for new voices: the cypher.

First stages: competitions, open stages, an EP and an art video

The public timeline of Avatar Omish before bouyon fits in four lines, all given by his association page. In 2022, he entered a NIPP Gospel competition, in continuity with the Christian rap influence of his beginnings. In 2023, he took part in the Riches Karayib competition. Between and around those dates, he performed at numerous Lyrics Under the Star shows, open stages that serve, in Dominica, as a first testing ground for singer songwriters. And he released an EP titled “See Through Cup”. None of those steps comes, in the opened sources, with a prize list, a placing or a listening figure, and this profile adds none.

The EP deserves a clear stop, because it is the least documented point. The association page says he released an EP called “See Through Cup”, with no date, no track listing, no platform and no production credit. During this research, no platform page for that EP was opened and verified: no artwork, no contents, no release date were confirmed. “See Through Cup” is therefore quoted here as a path fact reported by his association, and it does not enter the structured data of this page, which keeps only recordings shown by an opened primary source. On the day its streaming page is found, its songs will be documented one by one.

The fourth line is the most unexpected: the catalogue of the Echoes of Resilience exhibition of the Waitukubuli Artist Association, in 2024, credits Avatar Omish and m0$h Blac with a five minute video titled “Bloom”, played in the screening room of the exhibition, alongside films and installations by other Dominican artists. That is not a music track and this profile does not count it as one. But it is a path fact: before the cypher, the artist already circulated inside the visual arts network of his island, as a member of an association where music, video and fine art sit side by side. His stage name, Avatar, and his claimed taste for mixed genres gain, in that context, a coherence: Omish presents himself from the start as a cross form creator, not as a specialist in one sound.

One limit remains to be stated: from that whole period, no individual song is documented track by track in the opened sources. The stages, the competitions and the EP are known, the songs are not. A listener who wants to hear Avatar Omish before the cypher therefore has, to date, no verified track in hand in this file, and that is exactly why the 2026 record that follows changed everything for him: for the first time, a major platform, dated, credited and measured, carries his voice.

“Up Next Bouyon Cypher, Vol. 1”: the record that places him in bouyon

On October 2, 2026, Still Trill Entertainment releases “Up Next Bouyon Cypher, Vol. 1”, the Trilla-G project designed as a platform for the new bouyon generation of Dominica. The principle is the classic cypher principle: one instrumental, a passing microphone, and artists taking turns to show, each of them, his personality and his writing. Around Trilla-G, six guest voices: G’z Up, Ras B, Avatar Omish, Don Lash, Pa Pel and Pryhmex. Production is by Kwib La Beats, the instrumental is by Shane Massicott according to the first BuzzDA article, and ThaWizZarD handles the lead keys, the mixing and the mastering. The production runs almost ten minutes.

The origin of the project, told by Trilla-G to BuzzDA, lights up the place of Omish in the list. The cypher was born from a gathering organised in late September 2026 with young artists who had, most of them, already approached him about collaborating or using his platform. Trilla-G says he always wanted to do a bouyon cypher, that he chose artists whose craft he respects, and that he kept, in the edit, the exact order in which the artists recorded, as an order that came about by itself. He also says everyone exceeded his expectations and treated the opportunity with the utmost respect. Inside that frame, the presence of an alternative rapper trained in gospel and emo rap is no anomaly: it is the bet of the project, mixing different identities on one bouyon riddim and letting each voice bring its personality, its swagger and its youthful energy, in Trilla-G words about this generation.

The official video, published on YouTube with Apple Music and Spotify links in its description, was shot at The Lot Bar and Grill, filmed and edited by Sois Genial Multimedia; the first BuzzDA article adds that it was shot by Shane St. Claire of Sois Genial Multimedia and directed by Trilla-G with his manager and creative collaborator Tridel “Wins” Edwin. The measured reception, reported by the BuzzDA interview published on October 12, 2026, is the first audience fact of the Omish file: number one on the Apple Music Top Songs chart in Dominica, and 83,000 YouTube views in one week. The same interview notes that the cypher launch coverage warns that some lyrics contain adult language and mature themes, a warning this profile repeats on its own account without quoting lyrics, which no opened source transcribes for the Omish part.

In the economy of the cypher, one name had already broken out by the time of the articles: G’z Up, whose hook was turned into memes and who released, as early as October 10, an extended solo version of his passage. This profile notes it because it honestly fixes the position of Avatar Omish in the known reception of the record: he is one of the six launch voices, he shares the collective number one and the views, but no opened article names him as the breakout of the record and no dated solo follow up is documented for him yet. It is a start of visibility, not an individual consecration, and writing it that way protects both the artist and the reader. Hear the record in our bouyon playlist, where it stands as the manifesto of the 2026 new wave.

Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl 2026: the collective confirmed on stage

The second dated fallout of the cypher arrives on October 18, 2026. The confirmed artist list of Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl 2026, the Creole village of the independence celebrations in Roseau, grows from 20 to 29 names, and among the nine additions stands a collective line: Gz Up, Don Lash, Avatar Omish, Papel, Prymex. Five of the six cypher guests are therefore carried over as a stage collective, with two spellings changing on the way, Pa Pel becoming Papel and Pryhmex becoming Prymex, while Ras B, the sixth cypher guest, does not appear in that collective line of the list consulted. This profile reports those three facts, the spellings and the absence, without smoothing them: on a small scene, those are exactly the details that make poster and credit mistakes.

The frame of the event gives the measure of the step taken. Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl holds its fifth edition from October 19 to 22, 2026, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, on the theme Tout Biten Domnik, everything Dominican, with more than 90 entrepreneurs announced and a programme of live music, bouyon, quadrille and cultural demonstrations, in the week before the World Creole Music Festival. The list mixes the institutions of the island, Shelly and Signal Band, Chubby and the Midnight Groovers, TK International, Ridge, Reo, and the rising voices, among them the cypher collective. For an alternative rapper whose first public fact was a gospel competition in 2022, appearing, four years later, on the official list of the national Creole village is the most concrete sign that the cypher worked as it was designed to work: as a door in.

A calendar limit must be written: as of October 18, 2026, according to the list consulted, no day by day schedule had been released, and no opened source therefore says on which day the collective, nor Avatar Omish in particular, performs, nor with which repertoire. This profile assumes no date, no solo set and no “See Through Cup” revival on that stage. It notes only the confirmation, which is a fact, and its context, which is documented. What comes next, the performance itself and its fallout, will belong to the next update of this page.

An alternative path inside a collective genre: reading and limits

Put end to end, the facts draw a rare profile in current bouyon: an artist whose first identity lies elsewhere. Bouyon is a collective genre, of bands, producers and parties, which you enter through a riddim, a carnival or a group. Avatar Omish enters it through another door: solitary writing begun at 14, gospel, open stages, an association of visual artists and musicians, an art video. His arrival in the genre through a cypher, a format of individual writing and performance by definition, fits that path: where a carnival song would have melted him into a crowd, the cypher gives him a verse, a name on screen and a direct comparison with five peers.

What this profile cannot yet say must close the page, as always on this site. No verified solo artist page of Avatar Omish was confirmed on the platforms during this research: the Apple Music and Spotify links opened in this file are those of the collective Trilla-G record, not of a solo catalog. No solo track is therefore carried in the structured data of this page, which holds a single recording, the cypher, verified through the official video description and the two BuzzDA reports. The “See Through Cup” EP remains to be documented, the exact spelling of the full name remains to be fixed between Dayl and Daryl, and no official social account is linked here, for lack of direct verification.

What comes next will depend on simple, dated moves: a streaming page in his name, the documented release of “See Through Cup” or of a first solo single, the Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl performance and its follow up, and the second edition of the cypher, which Trilla-G said he wants to continue, with no date announced. Until then, the verified listening of Avatar Omish fits in one collective record of almost ten minutes, “Up Next Bouyon Cypher, Vol. 1”, to be heard in full to place his voice among those of G’z Up, Ras B, Don Lash, Pa Pel and Pryhmex, with the context of the genre on our bouyon soca page and the full new wave in our bouyon playlist.
