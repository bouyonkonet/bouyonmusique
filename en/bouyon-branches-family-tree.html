The bouyon branches family tree: the complete family

By Smokiller · Published October 24, 2026

Bouyon is not one sound: it is a family. At the root, the cadence-lypso and jing ping of Dominica. At the trunk, the bouyon of WCK. As branches, Wrecketeng, bouyon soca, RnBouyon and New Bouyon. As close neighbours, the shatta of Martinique and the Dennery Segment of Saint Lucia. This page draws the whole tree, following the branches research published on October 20, 2026 and the chapters of this site.

The roots: cadence-lypso and jing ping

Before bouyon, Dominica plays the cadence-lypso of Exile One and the Grammacks, and the jing ping of traditional music groups, with accordion, drum and flute. WCK takes those roots and plugs them into carnival rhythms and calypso at the end of the 1980s.

The site chapters on cadence-lypso, jing ping and the Midnight Groovers document that base, band by band.

The branches of the bouyon trunk

Wrecketeng is the hard and fast branch, and its name is spelled exactly Wrecketeng: the official spelling comes from DJ Cut himself, a founding member from Bushtown Clan. The spelling reketeng, widespread on the internet, is an error and is cited on this site only as such.

Bouyon soca mixes bouyon with soca. RnBouyon, spelled exactly so, sets R and B voices over bouyon riddims. New Bouyon is a name to attribute with care: New Bouyon Age is the title and brand of a 2023 EP by TiiTii NBA, and the concept claimed by Ricky Bishop dates from 2024, presented as his own claim.

The neighbours: shatta and Dennery Segment

Shatta is the party sound of Martinique, carried by voices like Maureen. Dennery Segment is the carnival sound of Saint Lucia, born in the parish of Dennery. Both talk with bouyon, share its publics and its stages, without being branches of bouyon itself.

Two names do not appear in this tree: Bouyon Delight and Alternative bouyon. The branches research of October 20, 2026 found no living use of those two labels, and this site does not publish them as branches.

Reading the tree on this site

Each branch has its chapter: rnbouyon, new bouyon, wrecketeng, bouyon soca, shatta bouyon and dennery segment are documented one by one, with their sources. This tree works as a map: it shows where each sound comes from and how the scenes of Dominica, Guadeloupe, Martinique and Saint Lucia answer each other.

Frequently asked questions

What are the branches of bouyon?

The documented branches are Wrecketeng, bouyon soca, RnBouyon and New Bouyon, on a bouyon trunk born from cadence-lypso and jing ping. Shatta and Dennery Segment are neighbours, not branches.

How is Wrecketeng spelled?

Wrecketeng is the official spelling, given by DJ Cut himself, a founding member. Reketeng is a widespread internet error, cited only as an error.

Is Bouyon Delight a branch of bouyon?

No. The branches research of October 20, 2026 found no living use of Bouyon Delight or of Alternative bouyon, and this site does not publish them as branches.
