Pahjo: Mash Up Kingdom, award winning bouyon soca

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Pahjo is a Guyanese soca singer and songwriter based in the UK. His bouyon link is “Mash Up Kingdom”, a 2024 song with Zamoni and Mr Benji billed as bouyon soca, which won Soca Song of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards on January 3, 2026.

Mash Up Kingdom, between Antigua, Dominica and the UK

Pahjo posted the official audio of “Mash Up Kingdom” on April 24, 2024, titled “Antigua Soca 2024 | Bouyon”. The song brings together Antigua's Zamoni, Dominica's Mr Benji and Pahjo. The credits list Pahjo and Mr Benji as writers, ShakerHD and QT 2hype as producers, and two studios: SD Productions in the UK and Home Alone Studios in Dominica.

On January 3, 2026, at the Dominica Music Awards run by Creole Heartbeat in Portsmouth, the song took Soca Song of the Year. Antigua News called it a “Bouyon Soca track”, and DBS Radio listed the award under Mr Benji, Zamoni and Pah-Jo. For the style itself, see our bouyon soca page.

A writer before a singer

According to NY Carib News, Pahjo started his solo career in January 2020, after a first writing job in 2019 on the Tool Box Riddim project. On the Soca Chronicles podcast he explained that he spent years in soca as an event organiser, behind the scenes, before going full time around 2019, with Shaker and Daryl Gervais helping him learn song structure.

In February 2026 he was named among the writers of “Encore”, the Machel Montano song that won Road March at Trinidad Carnival. He also sings “Identity” with Problem Child, and NY Carib News describes him as the first Guyanese artist booked for the Ubersoca Cruise, in November 2025.

Riddims and recent releases

Most of his catalogue is soca. “Risk It All” on the Ayo Riddim, produced by DJ ShakerHD, came out in 2022 with a video. “Gyal Fete”, on Dj Desktop's Sweet Jam Riddim, was posted by JulianspromosTV in March 2024. There is also “Zombie” on the Super Saiyan Riddim and “Jam Of Your Life” on the Mosaic Riddim.

In March 2026 he released “On Sight” with Team Foxx, part of the Feels Collective project, produced, mixed and mastered by Team Foxx. For the season in which “Mash Up Kingdom” was honoured, see our Dominica Carnival 2026 page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Pahjo?

A Guyanese soca singer and songwriter based in the UK, working as a solo artist since January 2020.

What is his bouyon song?

Mash Up Kingdom, with Zamoni and Mr Benji, released in April 2024 and billed as Bouyon.

What award did Mash Up Kingdom win?

Soca Song of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards, presented on January 3, 2026 in Portsmouth.

Has he written for other artists?

Yes. He is credited among the writers of Encore, Machel Montano's 2026 Road March.

Is Pahjo from Dominica?

No. NY Carib News describes him as a Guyanese artist based in the UK; his partner on the song, Mr Benji, is Dominican.
