Bouyon and trap

By Smokiller · Published November 11, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Bouyon meets trap, and the crossover is young. Tap by Kelzk, with David Campana, blends bouyon and trap in a direct formula, on the DLB II album in 2026. Behind that track, the drill wave of Tazzo Thaboss shows the way: since 2021, bouyon has been absorbing the sounds of trap and drill without losing its drum. The crossover is still thin, but it is real.

Kelzk, Tap and the DLB II album

Kelzk is a rising artist on the Montreal rap and urban scene. On the DLB II album, in 2026, the track Tap, with David Campana, blends bouyon and trap. The PAN M 360 review says it plainly: the album blends Afro R&B, bouyon, trap and rap, with forays into Afrohouse sounds, and Tap blends bouyon and trap into a direct and effective formula.

The album also brings together Shah Frank, Raccoon, Ya Cetidon and MADRiiNA, with production by Majosty, Fistoon, K.Status and Agoldencoast. Shazam lists DLB II as a 2026 album. The exact release date of the album is unverified in the opened sources, and we do not publish it. The official video for Tap is online on YouTube.

The drill wave shows the way

Before Tap, drill opened the door. Tazzo Thaboss released Glizzy Spray on 26 March 2021, and the video description presents the track as the first track labelled Bouyon Drill. That first-ever claim comes from the artist-supplied description, not from independent reporting, and we carry it as such.

The wave followed: DJ Flip Thaboss released the Bouyon Drill Riddim in May 2021, then DJ Max released Bouyon Drill (Gazo Remix) on 28 July 2022, on an instrumental by Litleboy767. Drill is not trap, but it comes from the same world: heavy bass, tight flows, and the bouyon drum keeping the rhythm.

Producers are already selling the blend

Producers understood the blend before the charts did. Alexay Beats offers a 2026 Bouyon x Afro Trap type beat called VENDETTA. It is an instrumental for sale, not a released song, but it proves that the bouyon and trap blend is already a product beatmakers offer to artists.

That type beat market is a weak but telling signal: when producers label a sound Bouyon x Afro Trap, it means artists are looking for it. Bouyon trap is no longer only a one-track experiment, it is becoming a category in the beat catalogues.

A young crossover, to follow without exaggerating it

It is only honest to say so: the bouyon and trap crossover today rests on one strong track, Tap, and on a drill wave that came before it. It is not yet a full scene, with its classics and its codes. No other bouyon trap track of the same weight is verified in the opened sources.

What is verified is enough to open the page: a 2026 album that blends the genres, a track that fuses the two sounds, a drill wave documented since 2021, and producers selling the blend. What comes next depends on the next releases, and this page will follow them.

Frequently asked questions

Which track blends bouyon and trap?

Tap by Kelzk, with David Campana, on the DLB II album in 2026. The PAN M 360 review says the track blends bouyon and trap into a direct and effective formula.

Is bouyon drill the same as bouyon trap?

No. Drill comes from the same world as trap, with heavy bass and tight flows, but it remains a distinct style. Tazzo Thaboss and DJ Flip Thaboss opened the bouyon drill way in 2021, before Kelzk's Tap.

Is bouyon trap already a full scene?

Not yet. The crossover rests on one strong track, Tap, on the drill wave documented since 2021, and on Bouyon x Afro Trap type beats offered by producers. No other track of the same weight is verified for now.
