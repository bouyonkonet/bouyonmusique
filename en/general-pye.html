General Pye: Largent, Open Dat, Top Shelf (bouyon 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

General Pye is a singer whose titles come out in Xavvo's Knockin Records circuit and on the More Sewo and DJ Taffy channels. Since “Up” with King Kembe, billed Bouyon 2025, he has strung together releases billed bouyon: “Largent” with Ebony Empress, “Keep Bouncing” and “Raw” with Strosey, “Open Dat” produced by Daddy Keyz, and “Top Shelf Live 2” with Xavvo, tagged SxmBouyon2026. His Deezer page lists 17 releases since “Looking” in 2018.

Up, Largent, Keep Bouncing: the Knockin Records circuit

On June 12, 2025, General Pye's channel published “Up (Official Audio) (Bouyon 2025)” with King Kembe, produced, mixed and mastered by Xavvoknockin, with the Knockin Records and Arctic Willow Firm tags; the single is dated June 11, 2025 on Deezer. On October 13, 2025, More Sewo uploaded “Largent (Bouyon 2026)” with Ebony Empress, produced by Fr3EzE, mixed by Xavvoknockin, close to 5,900 views when read.

On January 19, 2026, “Keep Bouncing (feat. Strosey)” reached Deezer, label Arctic Willow Firm. Xavvoknockin, producer and engineer of most of these titles, is the pivot: it is his Knockin Records label that carries General Pye's name from one release to the next.

Open Dat and Top Shelf Live 2: the 2026 season

On July 4, 2026, DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast published “General Pye - Open Dat (Ft. Daddy Keyzz) (Bouyon 2026)”: produced by Daddy Keyz, guitar by Jovani, mixed and mastered at Ignite Studios; the single is dated September 7, 2026 on Deezer. On September 25, 2026, Knockin Records uploaded “Xavvo x General Pye - Top Shelf Live 2 (Bouyon 2027)”, tagged SxmBouyon2026, close to 4,800 views; on Deezer, “Top Shelf Live 2 (feat. General Pye)” was released the same day under Xavvoknockin's name, label Knockin Records & Knockin Nation.

On October 24, 2026, “Raw” with Strosey, billed bouyon 2027 and produced by Denmath, opened the next season on his own channel. In sixteen months General Pye thus lined up six titles billed bouyon with five different producers. For the setting of the SxmBouyon tag, see our page on bouyon in Saint Martin.

Seventeen releases since 2018

General Pye's Deezer artist page lists 17 releases: “Looking” (June 2, 2018), the album “Are You Alive ?” (August 18, 2019), “Money Walk” and “Brand New” (2023), the EPs “Hearts III” (February 28, 2024) and “Tornado” (November 11, 2024), “Royalties” and “The Shit Show” (2024), then the 2025 and 2026 run, “Boat Ride”, “Free Up”, “Spaceship”, “Up”, “She Askin Meh”, “Largent”, “Keep Bouncing”, “Open Dat” and “The Switch” (October 7, 2026).

An older page, “She Want Di Best”, published on November 11, 2020 by the Old School USVI and Caribbean Music channel, keeps the trace of a title from before the bouyon run. This profile is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026; none states his birthplace, and this site places him in the scene his SxmBouyon billing names, without stating an origin.

Frequently asked questions

Who is General Pye?

A singer of Xavvo's Knockin Records circuit, maker of Up with King Kembe (Bouyon 2025), Largent with Ebony Empress and Open Dat with Daddy Keyz (Bouyon 2026), Top Shelf Live 2 with Xavvo and Raw with Strosey (Bouyon 2027).

What are his bouyon titles?

Up (June 2025), Largent (October 2025), Keep Bouncing (January 2026), Open Dat (July 2026), Top Shelf Live 2 (September 2026) and Raw (October 2026), all billed bouyon on their pages.

Who produces his titles?

Xavvoknockin (Up, mix of Largent), Fr3EzE (Largent), Daddy Keyz (Open Dat) and Denmath (Raw), according to the opened YouTube pages.

How many releases does General Pye have?

17 on his Deezer page, from Looking (2018) to The Switch (October 7, 2026), including the album Are You Alive ? (2019) and the EPs Hearts III and Tornado (2024).

Where is General Pye from?

No opened page says. Top Shelf Live 2 is tagged SxmBouyon2026 and a USVI channel published one of his titles in 2020; this profile places him in the Saint Martin scene that billing names, without stating a birthplace.
