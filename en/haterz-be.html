Haterz BE: a profile waiting for evidence

Published October 20, 2026

Haterz BE is a name that circulates in bouyon artist listings, and almost nothing else circulates with it. For this profile, the research was run platform by platform, press included: no verifiable track, streaming catalog, article or official account was found under that exact name. This page therefore publishes the real state of the dossier, explains the possible confusions with other documented “Haterz” acts, and states what it would take to turn this name into an artist profile.

A name on the lists, and nothing behind it that can be verified

Let us start with the only solid fact in this dossier: the name Haterz BE exists in the landscape of artist listings devoted to bouyon and to French and English speaking Caribbean music. It is under that form, with that spelling, that it calls for a profile. Everything else, the origin, the members if it is a group, the tracks, the dates, remains to be established. This site rule is to publish sourced facts only; when the sources are missing, it says so instead of embroidering, and that is the case here, from the first line to the last.

It is worth spelling out what “nothing found” means in practice. An active bouyon act normally leaves fast traces: a referenced single on a platform, a video, a mention in a DJ mix, a line in the Dominican, Guadeloupean or Martinican local press, an account followed by the pages of the scene. For Haterz BE, none of those traces could be confirmed under that exact name during the research for this profile, run in French and in English. That is not proof that the act does not exist: it is proof that it does not publicly exist, under that spelling, in the open archives.

Several explanations remain possible, and this profile will not choose between them. The name may belong to a very recent or very local project, active on stage or on social media without referenced digital distribution. It may be a listing spelling that distorts a real name, close but different. It may also be an abandoned alias, or a collective whose releases appear under another name. Each of those hypotheses is checked differently, and none can be settled from a desk: it will take a track, a link or a direct testimony.

The other “Haterz” of the archives: four confusions to rule out

The word Haterz, however, is well documented in music, and it is the first source of confusion. First case: “Haterz Be Damned”, a track by the New York hardcore band 25 ta Life, released on March 12, 2005 on Back Ta Basics, on the album “Hellbound Misery Torment”. The resemblance with “Haterz BE” is purely graphic: an American hardcore band of the 2000s has no documented link with bouyon, and this profile lends it none.

Second case: “Haterz”, the single by rapper Glasses Malone with Lil Wayne and Birdman, from the album “Beach Cruiser”. Again a title, not an artist, and an American hip hop world with no established relation to the Caribbean scene. Third case, closer in spelling but not in genre: Moustik Haterz, a French hybrid jazz group formed in Grenoble in 2022, spotted by the French jazz networks. Fourth case: “Haterz”, an Afrobeats track by Ghanaian artist QUEENIE MUZIKK, published on Audiomack. None of these four dossiers matches a bouyon act named Haterz BE.

There is even a trace of the word in Antillean music, and it deserves to be quoted in order to be set aside properly: in 2013, NRJ Antilles carried “HATERZ”, a track by Mickige featuring Dasinga. To this profile knowledge, that is the earliest appearance of the word in a French Caribbean context, but it is the title of a song by two artists named otherwise, not an artist called Haterz. If the name Haterz BE comes from an artist listing, it does not come from there, or only through a misreading that remains to be demonstrated.

This disambiguation section has a practical use: it prevents this profile from being read as someone else. Search engines happily mix these dossiers, and a reader looking for Haterz BE can land on New York hardcore or Grenoble jazz. This site answer is simple: those acts exist, they are documented, and they are not this one.

“BE”: two letters this profile will not translate

The second element of the name raises the same problem as the first. In Caribbean artist names, a two letter suffix can be many things: the initials of a member or a collective, a place code, the mark of a structure, or a part of the name itself with no separable meaning. Without a source, choosing one reading would mean inventing an origin. This profile therefore writes BE as the problem in full: two unresolved letters.

That restraint has a precedent in this site method. Bouyon names often carry precise stories that only the people concerned can confirm: a village, a school nickname, a studio tag. When an artist dossier is empty, the suffix is exactly the kind of detail a hurried editor wrongly “explains”, and that the artist then has to correct everywhere. Better a page that admits two open letters than a page that closes them with an invention.

In practice, this page title therefore places Haterz BE in the Caribbean in the broad sense, for lack of a documented place: neither Dominica, nor Guadeloupe, nor Martinique, nor any other territory can be claimed for this name on the current record. If a place is confirmed, the title, the description and this section will be corrected together, with the correction date.

Why this page exists, and how it will be completed

One may ask why publish a profile with no material. The answer has two parts. The first is documentary parity: this site is building the most complete and most honest reference on bouyon artists, and a name that appears on the scene listings deserves an answer, even if the answer is “not yet documented”. The second is transparency: a page that states its limits is worth more than an absent name, which readers would take for an oversight, or an invented text, which readers would take for a fact.

Completing it is simple, and the procedure is public. A single verifiable element is enough to reopen the dossier: a link to an official track, a platform page under the name Haterz BE, a press article, or an official account confirmed by the artist or their circle. On receipt, this profile will be rewritten under the same rules as the other pages of this site: sourced facts, tracks checked one by one, official links only after opening and control, and a stated update date.

While waiting for evidence, bouyon listening has plenty of verified doors in. Our artist pages present the pioneers and the new generation with their documented catalogs, from WCK to the current wave, and our bouyon playlist gathers the tracks driving the scene today. Haterz BE will join those lists the day its dossier contains something more than a name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Haterz BE?

On the current public record, nobody can say with evidence. The name Haterz BE circulates in artist listings linked to bouyon, but the research run for this profile, in French and in English, across streaming platforms, the press and social media, found no verifiable track, catalog, article or official account under that exact name. This profile publishes that result honestly rather than inventing a biography.

Which Haterz BE tracks can be heard?

No verifiable track was found. No song page under the name Haterz BE appears in the catalogs consulted, so this profile lists no recording in its schema. As soon as an official track is confirmed, it will be added with its date and label, as on the other artist pages of this site.

Is Haterz BE connected to “Haterz Be Damned” or to Glasses Malone “Haterz”?

No, no connection is documented. “Haterz Be Damned” is a track by the New York hardcore band 25 ta Life, released in 2005 on the album “Hellbound Misery Torment”. “Haterz” is a single by rapper Glasses Malone with Lil Wayne and Birdman. Both share one word with Haterz BE, nothing more, and this profile quotes them only to rule out the confusion.

What does the “BE” in Haterz BE mean?

It is not established. A two letter suffix can point to initials, a place or a structure tag, but no source documents the case of Haterz BE. This site therefore does not translate those two letters: guessing them would mean inventing an origin for an artist who has not yet been identified.

Where is Haterz BE from?

That is not documented either. Neither Dominica, nor Guadeloupe, nor Martinique, nor any other territory can be assigned to this name on the current record, and this page title prudently places it in the Caribbean in the broad sense. Any more precise claim will wait for proof: a track, an article or an official account.

How can this profile be corrected or completed?

By bringing one verifiable element: the link of an official track, a platform page under the name Haterz BE, a press article, or the confirmation of an official account by the artist or their circle. The profile will then be rewritten with sourced facts, a track list checked one by one, and the update date, under the rule applied to every page of bouyonmusique.com.
