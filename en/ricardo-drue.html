Ricardo Drue

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Ricardo Drue enters this site through his own YouTube channel, ItsDrueMusic, where two 2023 videos carry the label “Bouyon/Soca 2023” in their titles: “Last One Standing”, which the page bills as the Antigua Road March 2023 on the Funny Riddim, and “Birthday Suit”, with Psycho. Both are produced by Dj Taffy. This profile stays with those two pages: they date from 2023, before the 2024 to 2026 window this wave covers, and no opened page gives a birthplace or nationality for the artist.

Two 2023 titles labelled bouyon

The first, “Last One Standing”, went up on May 31, 2023 and runs 3 minutes 39. Its title stacks the markers: Antigua Road March 2023, Bouyon/Soca 2023, and the Funny Riddim hashtag. The description credits Ricardo Drue as performer and writer, Dj Taffy as producer, N.M.G Music for recording, mixing and mastering, and Wildfire on background vocals. The video is directed by Jamzpari and Ricardo Drue, shot by Jamzpari and edited by G. Kingston. The “Road March” claim is the page’s own billing; this profile quotes it as such and adds no ranking from elsewhere.

The second, “Birthday Suit”, followed on June 21, 2023 (3 minutes 48), credited to Ricardo Drue x Psycho x Dj Taffy, with the same Bouyon/Soca 2023 tag. Writing is shared between Ricardo Drue and Psycho, production again goes to Dj Taffy, and mixing and mastering to Nikholai Greene of N.M.G. Of the 68 videos the channel listed during this research, these are the only two that carry the word bouyon.

What the Bouyon/Soca label says

The double “Bouyon/Soca” label says something about the moment: a soca artist putting the word bouyon ahead of soca, on his own channel, for titles built for carnival. The common thread of both pages is Dj Taffy, a Dominican producer already profiled on this site, which links these titles to the sound of Dominica, where bouyon was born. That link is read from the credits; this profile goes no further and does not describe the music beyond what the pages show.

For Antigua’s place in the genre, our page on bouyon in Antigua gathers the verified context. The Antigua mention in “Last One Standing” refers to a carnival contest, not a statement of origin: no opened page says where Ricardo Drue was born, so this site assigns him no island.

The limits of the file

The verified file is two 2023 videos and one channel listing. No page opened during this research shows a Ricardo Drue title labelled bouyon for 2024, 2025 or 2026, and no primary biography was read. This profile says so plainly rather than stretching the label to other titles in his catalogue that do not carry it.

If a new Ricardo Drue release carries the word bouyon, it will be added here and in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026. For now the verified listening is two titles: “Last One Standing” and “Birthday Suit”.

Frequently asked questions

Which Ricardo Drue titles are labelled bouyon?

Two videos on his ItsDrueMusic channel: “Last One Standing”, uploaded May 31, 2023, and “Birthday Suit” with Psycho, uploaded June 21, 2023. Both carry Bouyon/Soca 2023 in their titles.

Who produced them?

Dj Taffy, credited as producer in both descriptions. Mixing and mastering are credited to N.M.G, with Nikholai Greene named on “Birthday Suit”.

Is Last One Standing the Antigua Road March 2023?

That is how the video title bills it: “Antigua RoadMarch 2023”. This site reports that billing as it stands, with no other opened source on the result.

Where is Ricardo Drue from?

No page opened during this research gives his birthplace or nationality. The Antigua mention concerns a carnival contest, not an origin, so this site assigns him no island.

Has he released bouyon after 2023?

No opened page shows it. Of the 68 channel videos listed during this research, only the two from 2023 carry the word bouyon.

Why is this profile short?

Because it rests on two primary pages only. It will grow if other primary sources tie Ricardo Drue to bouyon.
