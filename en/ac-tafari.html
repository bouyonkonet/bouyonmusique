AC Tafari: from Antigua to No Ex Back (Bouyon 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

AC Tafari is a singer from Antigua. His own channel presented him in 2016 as a 16-year-old Antiguan native and a student at Jennings Secondary School, on a dancehall title. Antigua Carnival Jr Soca in 2017 and 2018, then “Soca Purge” in 2019, followed. Bouyon came next: the “Hey Cutie” remix with Charmpian, billed Bouyon 2022, and “No Ex Back” with Mr Gwada, billed Bouyon 2026 and tagged #Dominica #Antigua, more than 26,000 views when this profile was written.

An Antiguan teenager, from dancehall to Jr Soca

On March 18, 2016, the Ac TafariVeVo channel published “Man A Star” with a short biography: AC Tafari is 16, an Antiguan native and a student at Jennings Secondary School; management is credited to YoungKiingz Ent. On April 13 came “Party Shot (Everybody Like That)”, billed “Antiguan Dancehall 2016”, produced by Jordan and Teddy, shot by YKE Films.

Antigua Carnival took over. The official channel of Antigua's Carnival Festivals Commission uploaded “Roll Up Your Bamber” on July 4, 2017, billed Antigua Carnival 2017 Jr Soca, then “So We Jam” on July 16, 2018, billed Antigua 2018 Jr Soca. In 2019, DjeasyPromos published “Soca Purge (2019 Soca)” and “Ride”. The ILOVESOCA365 channel also credits him on “Shake Ya Batty” with K-Lee, Island Prince, Laurena Davis and Ovadoo. For Antigua's place in the genre, see our page on bouyon in Antigua.

Hey Cutie, the first bouyon billing

On July 1, 2022, the Dj CashMoney 767 channel published “Charmpian x AC Tafari - Hey Cutie [Remix] (Bouyon 2022)”. The page names the backing: a Bouyon Drill Riddim produced by Dj Flip Tha Boss, the same producer found on Big Red HD's “Bouyon Freestyle” two years later.

The remix is AC Tafari's first title whose page carries the word bouyon. It arrived through a DJ whose name carries the 767 code, Dominica's: the sign of an Antiguan singer already played in the Dominican circuit before his 2025 title.

No Ex Back with Mr Gwada, billed Bouyon 2026

On April 23, 2025, the MR GWADA BOUYON NATION767 channel uploaded “AC Tafari x Mr Gwada - No Ex Back (Bouyon 2026)”, with the tags #Dominica #Antigua #bouyon. The page credits the lyrics to Kernel Stevens, the performance to Kernel Stevens and Theodore Daisy, the riddim to J2mo and the mix and master to Di Youte. It does not say which of the two legal names is AC Tafari and which is Mr Gwada: this profile quotes them as given.

The duet links two islands: Mr Gwada, whom the owner of this site, who knows him, places in Dominica, and an Antiguan singer. J2mo, maker of the riddim, is a producer present elsewhere on this site. This profile is written from the YouTube pages opened on October 27, 2026; no Deezer or Apple Music page under AC Tafari's name was found, and the profile will be extended when his titles appear there.

Frequently asked questions

Who is AC Tafari?

A singer from Antigua, presented by his own channel in 2016 as a 16-year-old Antiguan native and Jennings Secondary School student, who came through Antigua Carnival Jr Soca in 2017 and 2018.

What are his bouyon titles?

Hey Cutie [Remix] with Charmpian, billed Bouyon 2022, on a Bouyon Drill Riddim by Dj Flip Tha Boss, and No Ex Back with Mr Gwada, billed Bouyon 2026, uploaded on April 23, 2025.

Who produced No Ex Back?

The riddim is by J2mo and the mix and master by Di Youte, according to the Mr Gwada Bouyon Nation767 YouTube page; the lyrics are credited to Kernel Stevens.

Did AC Tafari sing at Antigua Carnival?

Yes: Roll Up Your Bamber (Antigua Carnival 2017 Jr Soca) and So We Jam (Antigua 2018 Jr Soca) were published by the Antigua's Carnival Festivals Commission channel.

Where can I listen to AC Tafari?

On YouTube: his Ac TafariVeVo channel, the Antigua Carnival channel, DjeasyPromos, Dj CashMoney 767 and Mr Gwada Bouyon Nation767. No Deezer or Apple Music page under his name was found on October 27, 2026.
