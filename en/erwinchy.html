Erwinchy: the tracklist name scheduled at the Botanic Gardens

Published October 20, 2026

Erwinchy is an artist of the bouyon scene whose verified public record holds three traces, and this profile gives all three, without inventing a fourth. First trace: a duet track with Gwada G, “Make A Woman Cum”, placed seventh in Sewo Jam 2023-2024, Digga D homage mix to Dominica. Second trace: a track with Kross Beatz, listed as “Secret Riddim Up”, in a bouyon mix from the Blaqrose Supreme podcast. Third trace, the most recent: the official Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 schedule lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 2:32 PM, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, for the festival grand finale. No biography, no full name and no documented home town: this profile tells the scene those three traces draw, and states its limits in plain sight.

Who Erwinchy is: three verified traces, and nothing else solid

Start with the traces, in their exact form. The published tracklist of the “Sewo Jam” mix, presented by Digga D in the Soca Podcast for the 2023-2024 seasons, places seventh “Erwinchy x Gwada G: Make A Woman Cum”. The mix presents itself as a homage to Dominica, where bouyon reigns, and its list lines up the Dominican scene of those seasons: Trilla G and Shelly opening, Asa Bantan, Triple Kay International, Signal Band, First Serenade Band and Carlyn XP. Erwinchy therefore appears there, through his track, in the bouyon selection DJs were running for that carnival.

The second trace comes from another mix, the Blaqrose Supreme podcast, whose timed tracklist lists at 52 minutes 07 “Erwinchy x Kross Beatz: Secret Riddim Up”. The title is reproduced here exactly as published, because no song page allows it to be split more cleanly, for example between a title and a riddim name. The neighbourhood is the same world: Shelly and Signal Band live, Trilla-G, Ridge, Carlyn XP and a run of riddims and free ups from the scene producers. Two independent mixes, two collaborations, one circuit: the DJs who document Dominican bouyon season after season.

The third trace changes scale, because it is no longer a DJ tracklist but an official schedule. The Ministry of Tourism and Discover Dominica published the full Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 schedule, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, from 19 to 22 October 2026, for the fifth anniversary of the event, under the theme Tout Biten Domnik. On Thursday 22 October, grand finale day, the schedule lists at 2:32 PM “Ignition and Erwinchy”, between Explosive Band and Derick “Rah” Peters, on a day that also lines up Reo and Faithii, Trilla G, Asa Bantan and, closing at 7 PM, Shelly and Signal Band. An artist the mixes were running is therefore also an artist the Creole festival of independence week schedules in its finale.

Beyond those three traces, the research carried out for this profile in October 2026 found nothing verifiable that belongs to Erwinchy. No biography. No full name. No interview, no label, no confirmed official page. A Spotify statistics aggregator does list an artist under this name, but its page returned no usable discography or biography during the research, and its exact Spotify link was not opened and verified: it is therefore not printed here, and the sameAs field stays empty. This profile refuses to turn three traces into a novelised life.

Sewo Jam and the mixes: how Dominican bouyon documents itself

To understand the value of the first two traces, what these mixes are has to be understood. Dominican bouyon lives to the rhythm of carnival seasons and fetes: tracks are born for one season, run in parties and on radio, then DJs fix them in mixes that become, in effect, the sound archive of the season. “Sewo Jam”, the Digga D mix, presents itself exactly that way: a homage to Dominica where bouyon pride reigns, with a tracklist published title by title, kept by platforms such as Podbay and Listen Notes.

Appearing in such a tracklist is not a technical detail. It means being selected, from the production of a season, by a DJ building the sound story of Dominican carnival for an audience that also listens from the diaspora. The Sewo Jam list mixes the headliners, Asa Bantan and Triple Kay, the bands, Signal Band and First Serenade Band, and the names of the new generation. “Make A Woman Cum”, the Erwinchy duet with Gwada G, holds seventh place there, just before the Mr Gwada and DJ Taffy tracks: an early mix position, where a DJ sets the energy of the season.

The Blaqrose Supreme mix plays a neighbouring role, in another format, the bouyon mix podcast followed by the diaspora. Its timed tracklist even allows the Erwinchy passage to be located: at 52 minutes 07, after the DJ Taffy riddims and before a closing stretch dominated by Signal Band, Trilla-G and Ridge. Those two lists, published by two different DJs, are to date the only open documents that name Erwinchy tracks. This profile therefore cites them as primary discography sources, with their limits: a tracklist proves a title ran under this name, it gives neither its exact release date nor its full credits.

Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026: the trace that moves from mixes to the official stage

Ti Vilaj Kweyol is the Creole village of the Dominican independence season, set in the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, with its days of food, culture and music, from noon to 8 PM. The 2026 edition, the fifth, runs from Monday 19 to Thursday 22 October, a few days before the nights of the World Creole Music Festival. Its programme mixes cultural groups, schools, historic bands such as Midnight Groovers and Swinging Stars, and the current bouyon scene: it is the gathering where Dominica presents itself to itself, before receiving the world.

The official schedule, published on 19 October 2026 by the Ministry of Tourism and Discover Dominica and carried by Dominica Weekly, gives each day its times. On Thursday 22 October, the grand finale opens with the Kalinago Dancers, UMOJA and the schools, passes through Explosive Band at 1:36 PM, then lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” at 2:32 PM. Derick “Rah” Peters, the founder of WCK, follows at 3:29 PM, then Reo and Faithii, Trilla G and Prymex at 4:26 PM, Asa Bantan at 5:43 PM, and Shelly and Signal Band closing at 7 PM. The schedule wording pairs Erwinchy with Ignition, without stating whether it is a duo, a backing band or a shared slot: this profile reproduces the pairing as published, without deciding what the source does not decide.

That scheduling is, to date, the strongest fact in the record, and it is also the one a quick profile would not give: it connects the tracklist name to a verifiable stage, date, time and place. It places Erwinchy in the densest week of the Dominican music calendar, the one leading to the World Creole Music Festival of 23, 24 and 25 October 2026 at Windsor Park. For an artist with no published biography, being on the official programme of the Ti Vilaj Kweyol finale counts as a stage recognition, delivered by the cultural institutions of the country itself.

The bouyon of Erwinchy: collaborations, riddims and the season register

The two verified Erwinchy tracks are collaborations, and that trait draws his positioning better than any speech. “Make A Woman Cum” pairs him with Gwada G, one of the recurring names in the bouyon tracklists of these seasons, present throughout Sewo Jam, solo, in duets and on DJ Taffy riddims. “Secret Riddim Up” pairs him with Kross Beatz, a production signature of the scene. The mix artist is therefore a network artist: he moves through duets and riddims, the natural format of season bouyon, where a track counts through the riddim carrying it and the voices answering each other on it.

The register of the titles also speaks of the branch. Dominican carnival bouyon has carried, since its beginnings, direct, teasing and often lewd language, inherited from chante mas, the carnival song tradition of double meanings and collective response. A title such as “Make A Woman Cum”, reproduced here as the tracklist publishes it, belongs to that adult fete register, that of parties and mixes, more than that of daytime family programming. Yet Ti Vilaj Kweyol schedules Erwinchy in the afternoon, inside a cultural village: the same Dominican scene makes both registers coexist, and its artists move between them according to the hour and the audience.

That positioning also places Erwinchy inside the long mechanics of the genre. Bouyon was born in Dominica in the late 1980s with WCK, it became a carnival band music with Triple Kay, then a music of producers and branches: bouyon soca, started in Dominica in 2003 by Dada according to the first-hand testimony of Sean “Smokiller” Francis, a Dominican-born producer and a participant in that era, then popularised by Triple Kay, and Wrecketeng, whose official spelling comes from DJ Cut himself, the faulty form reketeng that circulates on the internet being only a misspelling flagged as such on this site. The Erwinchy generation inherits all those layers at once: bands, riddims, season duets and official Creole stages.

What this profile claims, and what it leaves open

Let us sum up the record, because on a thin profile, clarity is worth more than emphasis. Claimed and sourced: Erwinchy has a duet track with Gwada G, “Make A Woman Cum”, listed seventh in Sewo Jam 2023-2024, the Digga D homage mix to Dominica. Claimed and sourced: a track with Kross Beatz, listed as “Secret Riddim Up”, appears in the timed tracklist of a bouyon mix from the Blaqrose Supreme podcast. Claimed and sourced: the official Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 schedule lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 2:32 PM at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, on grand finale day.

Open means: full name unknown to this record, home town and birth date not documented, path before the mixes not documented, label unknown, complete discography unknown beyond the two tracklist titles, no official account confirmed. The two tracks in this page schema are therefore exactly the two tracklist titles, not one more and not one less, and the sameAs field stays empty for lack of an opened and verified link. If an official page, an interview or a documented discography appears, this profile will be completed, dated and corrected like every page on this site.

In the meantime, listening starts with the documents themselves: the Sewo Jam tracklist, to hear Erwinchy inside the selection of his season, then our bouyon soca page and our bouyon playlist for the lineage of the Dominican sound. And for anyone in Roseau in October 2026, the meeting is already written in the programme: Botanic Gardens, Thursday 22 October, 2:32 PM, Ignition and Erwinchy.
