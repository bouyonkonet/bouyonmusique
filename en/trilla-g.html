Trilla-G: a voice of Dominican bouyon

Trilla-G, whose real name is Gael Jno Baptiste, is a bouyon artist from Dominica followed closely by Bouyon Shattaz. His account notably served as a source for the track FIX UP by G’z Up, relayed by the platform.

The Dominican scene day to day

The strength of Dominican bouyon lies in its pool of talent: artists like Trilla-G who release, test their tracks on the networks and build their audience from the island. Bouyon Shattaz documents this scene day by day, from snippets to official releases.
