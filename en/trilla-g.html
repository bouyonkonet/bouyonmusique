Trilla-G: the voice linking calypso and bouyon

Published October 7, 2026

Trilla-G, real name Gael Jno Baptiste, is an artist from Dominica whose path links two worlds the island knows well: calypso, where he was crowned, and bouyon, the sound of today's carnival and fetes. On Instagram, where about 23,600 followers track him as @trillaonthemic, he embodies a generation that refuses to choose between heritage and the new wave.

Who is Trilla-G: Gael Jno Baptiste, an artist from Dominica

Behind the stage name Trilla-G stands Gael Jno Baptiste, a singer from Dominica. His stage name and his Instagram handle, @trillaonthemic, already tell his approach: Trilla-G is a microphone artist, shaped by the stage, the competitions and the island's fetes before the platforms.

His path only makes sense inside Dominica's musical context. The island holds a double tradition: on one side calypso, the art of competition and social commentary, with its monarchs crowned every carnival; on the other bouyon, born in the late 1980s with WCK and now the dominant fete sound. Trilla-G grew up and broke through where those two worlds cross, and that position is his artistic identity.

He tells his beginnings himself. In an interview with Billboard, picked up by the press, Trilla-G explains that while still at school he got close to two artists then on the rise, Reo and Keks Mafia: they performed together and even formed a group for a short time. That account places him from the start in the Dominican generation that carries bouyon today, the one he later meets again on the same collective tracks and the same bills.

2023 Calypso Monarch: the crown that established him

In 2023, Trilla-G won the title of Dominica Calypso Monarch, the crown of Dominica's carnival calypso competition. According to the Caribbean Times report, he won on his debut in the competition, aged 24, in a final at the Windsor Park Sports Stadium Forecourt where Dennison "Dice" Joseph, presented as a nine-time monarch, placed second and Chester "Daddy Chess" Letang third. A newcomer ahead of two former champions: this is not a nickname, it is a trophy won in competition, in front of the Mas Domnik crowd and judges.

That calypso foundation explains what came next. When Trilla-G steps onto bouyon and soca productions, he brings what the competition demands: built verses, presence, and the hook that stays.

Someone Else: the soca success that got awarded

In 2024 came "Someone Else", one of the most noticed songs of his path. The Qobuz credits of the compilation Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1, released by DSM Music and Monk Music, present it as a track by Litleboy, with Quan and Trilla G as featured artists, written by Foster Marcel Xavier, Gael Jno Baptiste and Quan Denis. Trilla-G is therefore both a featured voice and a co-writer.

The song was honoured twice. At the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, it received Soca Collaboration of the Year. At the 2025 Dominica Music Awards, presented on January 3, 2026, it won Collaboration of the Year according to the winners list published by Soca News, in a category that also included "RAGS", by Trilla-G with Skinny Fabulous and Shelly. DJs had already adopted it: it runs through the Miami Carnival 2024, Prelude 2025 and Bouyon Passport 2026 mixes.

Take Me as I Am: the album of maturity

On October 22, 2025, according to The Bajan Reporter, Trilla-G released his debut album, "Take Me as I Am: The Album", a 13-track project recorded at Th3rdHouse Studios in the US Virgin Islands. The paper lists production credits from across the Caribbean, including, for Dominica, Fr3EzE, Mask Man and DernzMusik. The lead song, "Take Me As I Am", released in September 2024, had already passed one million views on YouTube.

Ten of the thirteen tracks are in this site's recordings log, from the album's Shazam page: "Testimony", "Single Again", "Action", "Blue" with Ridge, "Kyoné Me", "Rock, Paper, Scissors", the "Take Me As I Am" remix with V'ghn, "Hennessy & Malibu", "Marry You" and "Toxic Love" with Th3rd.

The recognition kept building the following spring: in April 2026, Billboard named him Caribbean Artist of the Month.

Up Next Cypher and Fix Up: Trilla-G as executive producer

In 2026, Trilla-G opened another chapter, the platform. The "Up Next Cypher, Vol. 1", released by Still Trill Entertainment on October 2, 2026 according to BuzzDA, runs close to ten minutes: one instrumental, by Kwib La Beats, the project of Shane Massicott, with ThaWizZarD on keys, mixing and mastering, and around Trilla-G a line of emerging voices: G'z Up, Ras B, Avatar Omish, Don Lash, Pa Pel and Pryhmex. According to his comments to BuzzDA, a volume 2 is already announced.

Singles came out of the cypher. "Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya)" takes up the standout verse of G'z Up, its lead artist: the official description credits writing and performance to G'z Up, production to Kwib La Beats and ThaWizZarD, and executive production to Trilla-G. On October 16, 2026, Time Warner Entertainment published a review singling out G'z Up's rhythm-switching over ThaWizZarD's keys. "IDGAF (Bouyon 2027)" by Pa Pel follows the same credit pattern: the cypher becomes a singles factory, with Trilla-G as its source and executive producer.

Trilla-G's network: collective tracks and nominations

"Without You", a collective track presented as Bouyon 2025, brings together Unstoppable Shax, Nice, Reo, Trilla-G and Quan Dawg, on a production by A-Plus Muzik, mixed and mastered by Th3rd House Studios.

The official lists draw the same circle. At the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, Trilla G was among the nine nominees for Bouyon Artist of the Year, with Asa Bantan, Kenny G, litleboy, Mr. Ridge, Quan, Shelly Alfred, Triple K and Pudaz. In 2026 he is a finalist in the category again, and Black Island Girl groups him with BRG Hollywood and 1T1 in "a new wave of performers". At the 2025 Dominica Music Awards, Trilla G and Reo appear in the artist and video categories.

Trilla-G in 2026: the WCMF stage and what comes next

In the autumn of 2026, Trilla-G is among the names announced in the Night 2 programme of the World Creole Music Festival, on Saturday, October 24 in Roseau, according to the festival guides that published the night-by-night lineups, on the same night as Ne-Yo, Alkaline, Asa Bantan, V'ghn and Reo. Two days earlier, on Thursday, October 22, the official Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl programme lists him at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau at 4:26 pm, with Prymex, in the block of Reo and Faithii. In May 2025, he was already on the bill of the Strictly the Best Festival, Bouyon Edition, in Saint Martin.

In the mixes, his presence goes back a long way. Original DJ-Sly's "D Riddim Box", a best of the 2020 season, opens with Trilla G's "The Prayer"; DJ Close Connections' Bouyon Passport 2026 opens with Trilla G and Ridge and plays "Take Me As I Am". The Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1, made by Sean "Smokiller" Francis for Bouyon Shattaz, names him among the best new bouyon songs from Dominica.

Between the calypso crown, the awards, the album and the cypher, Trilla-G holds a singular place: the bridge, linking competition calypso to fete bouyon and handing the microphone to the next generation. Frequently asked questions

Who is Trilla-G and where is he from?

Trilla-G, real name Gael Jno Baptiste, is an artist from Dominica, followed by about 23,600 people on Instagram as @trillaonthemic. He was crowned Dominica Calypso Monarch in 2023, on his debut in the competition, and, while still at school, he performed with Reo and Keks Mafia, briefly forming a group with them, according to his Billboard interview.

What is Trilla-G's role in bouyon?

Trilla-G links competition calypso to fete bouyon. A singer and writer, he has also become an executive producer: his "Up Next Cypher, Vol. 1", released by Still Trill Entertainment in October 2026, puts emerging voices forward, and G'z Up's single "Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya)" came out of it.

What are Trilla-G's key songs and releases?

"Someone Else" (2024), with Litleboy and Quan, Soca Collaboration of the Year at the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards and Collaboration of the Year at the 2025 Dominica Music Awards. Then "Take Me as I Am: The Album", 13 tracks released on October 22, 2025, including "Blue" with Ridge and the "Take Me As I Am" remix with V'ghn.

Who has Trilla-G worked with?

His documented credits link him to Litleboy LSBeats767 and Quan on "Someone Else", to Skinny Fabulous and Shelly on "RAGS", to Nice, Reo, Unstoppable Shax and Quan Dawg on "Without You", to Ridge, V'ghn and Th3rd on his album, and to Kwib La Beats and ThaWizZarD on the cypher.

Where can I hear or see Trilla-G?

On stage, he is announced for Night 2 of WCMF 2026, Saturday, October 24 in Roseau, and for Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl on October 22. For listening, the Bouyon Shattaz Bouyon Road Mix, on Spotify and YouTube, opens onto the Dominican scene he belongs to, and his songs run through DJ mixes such as Bouyon Passport 2026.
