Sully Jacob: Où est mon argent?, Bandi (bouyon)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Sully Jacob is a bouyon singer who does almost everything himself: he writes, records, shoots and edits his videos, and often produces his own riddim. Three titles billed bouyon mark his pages: “She Lied to Me” in February 2025, carried by DJ Taffy, “Où est mon argent? #RUNDAT” in June 2025, picked up by More Sewo, and “Bandi” in September 2026, already billed Bouyon 2027. On each, mixing and mastering are by Dernel “Dernz” Green.

She Lied to Me and Où est mon argent?: the 2025 year

On February 16, 2025, DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast published “Sully Jacob - She lied to me (Official Video) (Bouyon 2025)”, subtitled “Kay Ka Fet” and “Gwopwel Bouyon 2025”, more than 9,300 views when read. The page credits the riddim and recording to Sully Jacob himself, mixing and mastering to Dernel Green. On June 21, 2025, his own channel uploaded “Où est mon argent? (Bouyon 2025) #RUNDAT”, a title marked with the Sint Maarten and France flags: riddim by Hxnter, mix by Dernz, recording and cover by Sully Jacob.

On July 19, 2025, More Sewo, the Dominican scene's channel, carried the official video, close to 7,800 views, with the same credits and the name of his video collective, Mind Ur Frame. Two titles, two relay channels, the same engineer: Dernel Green, whose name runs through the 2025 and 2026 bouyon releases documented by this site.

Bandi, billed Bouyon 2027

On September 22, 2026, Sully Jacob published “Bandi (Official Video) Bouyon 2027”, whose description presents it as the official video for “Bandi (Bouyon 2026)”, performed and produced by Sully Jacob, mixed and mastered by Dernz (Dernel Green), shot and edited by Sully Jacob. The page states that the weapons and props in the video are fictional props. The title is distributed through DistroKid; no Deezer page under the artist's name was found.

On March 4, 2026, his channel had also published an 8 minute 45 video, “Sukie on Bouyon vs Soca + How He Brought Bouyon Back (His Real Come-Up)”, a filmed conversation on bouyon and soca. For the setting of titles marked Sint Maarten, see our page on bouyon in Saint Martin.

An artist who shoots, records and produces himself

The credits of his three titles draw a method: riddim, recording, filming and editing by Sully Jacob, with one fixed partner, Dernel Green, on the mix, and one guest producer, Hxnter, on “Où est mon argent?”. His handles, sullyjacob971 on Instagram and sullyjacob97150 on Facebook, carry Guadeloupe's code and Saint-Martin's postal code, and his titles' flags point to Sint Maarten and France.

No opened page spells out where he is from: this profile places him in the Saint Martin scene his titles mark, without stating a birthplace. It is written from the YouTube pages opened on October 27, 2026 and will be extended when his titles appear on Deezer or Apple Music with their credits.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sully Jacob?

A bouyon singer who produces his riddims, records, shoots and edits his videos himself, maker of She Lied to Me and Où est mon argent? #RUNDAT (Bouyon 2025) and Bandi (Bouyon 2027).

Who mixes his titles?

Dernel “Dernz” Green, credited for mixing and mastering on She Lied to Me, Où est mon argent? and Bandi.

Who produced Où est mon argent?

The riddim is by Hxnter (hxnterz beat), according to the Sully Jacob and More Sewo YouTube pages; Sully Jacob recorded the title and made the cover.

Where can I listen to Sully Jacob?

On his YouTube channel and through his titles' DistroKid links; She Lied to Me is also on DJ Taffy's channel and Où est mon argent? on More Sewo.

Where is Sully Jacob from?

No opened page says. His titles carry the Sint Maarten and France flags and his handles the 971 and 97150 codes; this profile places him in the Saint Martin scene without stating an origin.
