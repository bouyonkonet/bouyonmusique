Fowko: Guadeloupe bouyon from L'Adultère to Kon Avan

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Fowko has been singing bouyon in Guadeloupe since the early 2010s. His first hit, “L'Adultère”, was already making the rounds in 2011, and he still puts out songs on his own channel, the latest in September 2026. In between, he cut two songs on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim and made a habit of writing about whatever the island is talking about.

L'Adultère, the summer hit of 2011

The song that put him on the map is “L'Adultère”. The official video, posted by Greg Mixey under the tag “Jump Up 2011”, is past 85,000 views. Réunion DJ Illan's made a dubplate version, uploaded in October 2011, and calls it “one of the big summer hits in the Antilles”, with a simple dance routine and keywords that call it a “hit bouyon”.

The dance travelled with the song. In January 2013, the group Les Choses Mass posted its take on “le bouyon dance de Fowko”, worked up for the Guadeloupe carnival. “Poul La”, another video from the same period on Greg Mixey, is past 57,000 views. Both songs are still in his set: in July 2025 he sang them live on La Désirade, to a crowd he called the best in Guadeloupe.

Songs that follow the news

Fowko often writes from what is going on around him. In May 2020, in the middle of lockdown, he presented “Soukoumiaw” live on Guadeloupe la 1ère. In October 2021, “Bloké Bloké”, produced by MJ Beats Vybz, dealt with the airport and tourists stuck there in the covid period. Then in 2022 came “Bouboune la”, tagged #bouyon.

He kept at it. “Kon Avan”, out in September 2025, describes itself as “a 100% vibes bouyon, no gunshot”, made for parties the old way. “Lé Manman Ti Moun”, from September 2026, is a short back-to-school bouyon about the hours between 9 and 3 when the kids are in class. On his channel he presents himself as a Guadeloupean artist, and he also posts videos about Deshaies, Sainte-Rose and Marie-Galante.

Two turns on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim

In April 2023, Fowko uploaded “I Need a Girl”, on the Funny Riddim by DJ Taffy, labelled “Bouyon 2023”. That put him in a long line of voices from several islands. In July he came back to the same riddim with a second song, simply called “Funny riddim #2” and also billed “Bouyon 2023”, which got its own video on his channel.

Not everything in his catalogue is bouyon. On Deezer his recent singles come out on GK Prod, among them “la route” on DJ Dirtee's No Limit Riddim and “Stopé la Vyolans” in October 2026, and he also dips into dancehall and shatta. For the wider scene, see our page on bouyon in Guadeloupe.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Fowko?

A bouyon singer from Guadeloupe, active since the early 2010s and known for “L'Adultère” and “Poul La”.

What is his best known song?

“L'Adultère”, billed in 2011 as one of the big summer hits in the Antilles; the official video is past 85,000 views.

Has he sung on the Funny Riddim?

Yes, twice: “I Need a Girl” in April 2023 and “Funny riddim #2” in July 2023, both on DJ Taffy's riddim.

What are his recent bouyon songs?

“Kon Avan” in September 2025, a bouyon with “no gunshot”, and “Lé Manman Ti Moun” in September 2026.

Has he been on the radio?

Yes, in May 2020 he presented “Soukoumiaw” live on Guadeloupe la 1ère.
