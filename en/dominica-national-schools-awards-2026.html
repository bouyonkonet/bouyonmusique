Bouyon music: Dominica National Schools Awards 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026

Dominica has created its own ceremony for its schools. The first edition of the National Schools Awards gathered 42 student winners on Monday 26 October 2026, at St. Alphonsus Parish Hall in Roseau, per DOM767.

A new ceremony, born this year

Until now, students who shone in Caribbean Examinations Council assessments were honoured during the National Youth Rally. This year the Ministry of Education created a separate ceremony, dedicated to the achievements of the 2025-2026 school year, per DOM767.

The ceremony was held under the theme Celebrating Excellence, Recognizing Achievement, Inspiring Greatness. Education officials said it will become an annual event, a dedicated national platform for student achievement and school development.

More than exams

Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit warned against measuring students by exam results alone: every child has different abilities, interests and strengths, and the system must help each learner find and develop them, per DOM767.

The school awards reflect that breadth: performing arts, modern languages, agriculture, community service, cultural preservation, 4-H, pastoral care, safety, innovation, creativity, social clubs and sports. Academic performance sits alongside practical skills, leadership, culture and community involvement.

CSEC results above the region

Education Minister Octavia Alfred presented the 2026 Caribbean Secondary Education Certificate figures: Dominica sits above the regional average in 30 of the 32 subjects assessed, with an 88.4 per cent pass rate in English and 94.9 per cent in Mathematics, per DOM767.

Writeups 24 adds that the country is 11.3 per cent above the regional CSEC average, that numeracy is improving, and that creative writing remains a task. The minister praised the work of students, teachers, parents and school leaders.

Thinking for yourself, in the age of artificial intelligence

Roosevelt Skerrit called for reforms to prepare students for the world of artificial intelligence, technology and changing work: less memorising, more independent thought, analysis and problem solving, per Writeups 24.

His line, reported by Writeups 24, sets the direction: we cannot prepare them for that world by simply teaching them to memorise information and repeat it during an examination. He also asked for better literacy, numeracy, technical and vocational training, well used classroom technology and better provision for students with special needs.

Frequently asked questions

What are Dominica s National Schools Awards?

A national ceremony created in 2026 by the Ministry of Education to honour students and schools. The first edition took place on Monday 26 October 2026 at St. Alphonsus Parish Hall in Roseau and honoured 42 students, per DOM767.

What did Dominica score at CSEC 2026?

Above the regional average in 30 of the 32 subjects assessed, with an 88.4 per cent pass rate in English and 94.9 per cent in Mathematics, per figures presented by Minister Octavia Alfred and reported by DOM767.

Will the ceremony happen every year?

Yes, per education officials cited by DOM767: it becomes an annual event dedicated to student achievement and school development in Dominica.
