College Boy Jesse: a Cunupia soca voice on bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

College Boy Jesse, real name Jesse Stewart, comes from Cunupia in Trinidad. He is a groovy soca singer who pulled off an upset at the 2020 International Soca Monarch, winning his category with “Happy Song”. His bouyon record so far comes down to one song, “Nocturnal Energy”, released in January 2026 on ProducerDlo's Bouyon Rave Riddim.

Nocturnal Energy, his bouyon page

On January 13, 2026, ProducerDlo uploaded “College Boy Jesse - Nocturnal Energy | Bouyon 2026”. The description leaves no doubt about the setting: “Bouyon Rave Riddim 2026”. The lyrics are not his but Wrenroy “Blaka Dan” Ogiste's, who also sings “Forewarned” on the same riddim. Production, mixing and mastering go to Dylan “ProducerDlo” Lowe and Dylan “Hossi” Augustine, with Akim “Haze” Hazel on drums, Jervon “Nattybill” Henderson on bass and Cornell Phillip tracking the drums.

On Deezer, the Bouyon Rave Riddim album, dated the same January 13, opens with “Nocturnal Energy (feat. College Boy Jesse)”, 3 minutes 18. He shares the record with Shelly, Blaka Dan, XS Groove, Benja and Sledge HammaH. The video had just over 1,200 views when read, far from his soca hits, but it is the only page opened that ties his name to bouyon.

Happy Song and the 2020 groovy crown

The “Happy Song” video came out in September 2019 on JulianspromosTV, tagged “2020 Soca”. The credits list Jesse Stewart among the writers, with Curleen Stewart and Stevie Sealy, who recorded it at EPT Entertainment. It is now close to 229,000 views.

On Friday, February 21, 2020, at the Queen's Park Savannah, he played his first International Soca Monarch final and won the groovy title. MyTriniLime called it an upset: he finished ahead of defending champion Swappi with “Jumbie Head” and Ding Dong with “Outside”. A Trinidad Guardian piece by Kristy Ramnarine later described him as “the Cunupia artiste”, performing since the age of seven.

A soca catalogue, with Blaka Dan close by

The rest of his discography sits under the soca label. In late 2018, OJO World TT's Sound of the Streets had him on for “Temptation” and “Wifey Material”, two songs on a single riddim. Then came “Live Your Life”, “Doing It” on the Brunch Fete Riddim, “Jook On De Junction” with Yung Bredda, and “BenOva” with Sackie in July 2025, billed “Trinidad Soca 2026”.

Blaka Dan keeps turning up in his credits. He co-wrote “BenOva” with Sackie, wrote “Nocturnal Energy”, then shared the mic with him on “My Time Now”, out October 19, 2026 and produced by Damion “Pappy Boi” La Pompe of Loyalty Family Production. On the bouyon side that makes one song to date, but a well-connected one.

Frequently asked questions

Who is College Boy Jesse?

Jesse Stewart, a soca singer from Cunupia, Trinidad, who won the groovy category of the 2020 International Soca Monarch with Happy Song.

What is his bouyon song?

Nocturnal Energy, posted on January 13, 2026 by ProducerDlo under the Bouyon 2026 tag, on the Bouyon Rave Riddim.

Who wrote Nocturnal Energy?

Wrenroy “Blaka Dan” Ogiste, per the video credits; production is by Dylan “ProducerDlo” Lowe and Dylan “Hossi” Augustine.

Who else sings on the Bouyon Rave Riddim?

Shelly with Burn Dem, Blaka Dan with Forewarned, XS Groove with Only You, and Benja with Sledge HammaH on Till Morning.

When did he win the Groovy Soca Monarch?

On February 21, 2020 at the Queen's Park Savannah, with Happy Song, ahead of Swappi and Ding Dong.
