Dexterman: Azoukinkin with Edday and Miaou, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Dexterman is a singer whose Deezer catalogue starts in 2020 and who works mostly with the Voodoo Studio team. His most watched bouyon songs are collaborations: “Azoukinkin” with Edday, whose upload by Edday is past 460,000 views, “Miaou” with Shanika, and DJ Luchshiy's Whatcha Say Riddim, all released under a bouyon or warm up tag.

A catalogue that opens in 2020

On Deezer, Dexterman's first releases date from 2020, with “Warning” and “Ma Nana”, then “Lyrical criminel” and “Night and Day” in 2021. The “Night and Day” video, posted by Mighty Production, is past 200,000 views. None of these carry a bouyon tag on the pages opened.

On March 25, 2023 came the album “2ème Quart Temps” on Upfull Music, sixteen tracks including “Ti Moun a Moun La” with Haterz B and DJ Skaytah. The same year he appears on the “Voodoo Team, Vol. 1” compilation, the studio behind the bouyon songs that followed.

Azoukinkin, with Edday

On October 2, 2024, Deezer listed “Azoukinkin”, credited to Dexterman and Edday, on BMD. Edday's upload of October 9, produced by Yg Gucci, is past 460,000 views. The official video's tags present it as a remix of “Azoukinking”, the Coupé Cloué song.

The video came out on April 9, 2025 on the DEXTERMAN TV channel. Voodoo Studio directed it, YG Gucci composed it and BMDMusic recorded it, with thanks to the Pondi market in Basse Terre. It is tagged “Warm up Bouyon” and is past 94,000 views.

Miaou, Whatcha Say and Voodoo Studio

In 2025 he teamed up twice with Shanika. “Sé vou”, with I Paf Hau, came out in January on Voodoo Studio. “Miaou” followed on August 28, composed by Yvng Gucci and recorded, mixed and mastered at the studio. The video, posted in October on Shanika's channel with the tags #WARMUP and #BOUYON, is past 26,000 views.

In September 2025, Voodoo Studio posted the visualizer for the Whatcha Say Riddim, composed and mixed by DJ Luchshiy. Dexterman and Lestef KJF voice “She Say” and “No Come Back” on it, presented as a bouyon remix, with over 46,000 views. In 2026 he released “An Nou Dance”, billed as carnival soca, then “Queen”, “Discret” and “Gwada Paradise”.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Dexterman?

A singer whose Deezer catalogue starts in 2020, close to the Voodoo Studio team. No opened source gives his real name or birthplace.

What is his most watched bouyon song?

Azoukinkin with Edday: Edday's upload is past 460,000 views, and the official video, tagged Warm up Bouyon, past 94,000.

What is Miaou?

A Shanika song featuring Dexterman, out on August 28, 2025 on Voodoo Studio and composed by Yvng Gucci. The video is tagged bouyon.

What is the Whatcha Say Riddim?

A Voodoo Studio session composed by DJ Luchshiy, posted in September 2025, with Dexterman's She Say and Lestef KJF's No Come Back.

Where is Dexterman from?

No opened source says. His videos point to Guadeloupe, with thanks to a market in Basse Terre and a Gosier tag, and his songs come out on Voodoo Studio. This profile still does not give him a birth island.
