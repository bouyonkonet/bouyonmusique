Stalk Ashley: two bouyon invitations, Nobody and Put That On That

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Stalk Ashley is a singer whom The FADER presents as a Jamaican dancehall artist, and her name entered Dominica bouyon through two precise doors. In June 2025, Khallion released the remix of “Put That On That” featuring her, with the bouyon label printed in the video title itself. In October 2025, she sang and co-wrote “Nobody” on the Trilla-G album “Take Me As I Am: The Album”, a track the Trilla-G channel files as Bouyon 2026. This profile sticks to those two verified records, their credits and what they say about current bouyon, which goes as far as Jamaica for its guest voices.

Who Stalk Ashley is

The only published portrait kept here comes from The FADER. In December 2020, the magazine premiered the video for “Incognito”, a track on which she hosts Alkaline, and described her as a Jamaican dancehall artist whose song blends R and B with a dancehall colour. This profile takes that frame and cites it as such: no source opened gives a place or date of birth, or a civil name, and nothing is added beyond what the magazine writes.

Her own catalogue keeps living on her YouTube channel. In October 2026 she published the official video for “NBM” there, distributed by Onerpm Music and produced by ChinnaMan Records and Street Blocks Records. That track claims no link to bouyon, and this profile lends it none: it only shows that bouyon remains, for her, a field of invitations beside a main career rooted in dancehall.

That is exactly what makes her passage through bouyon interesting. The genre born in Dominica, which our dancehall and bouyon page follows closely, has always borrowed voices and manners from Jamaican dancehall. With Stalk Ashley, the borrowing becomes a credited collaboration, on two official releases by two leading Dominican names.

Put That On That (Remix) with Khallion, June 2025

“Put That On That” first existed without her. Khallion released the original version on his channel on 20 September 2024, with Bouyon in the title, production by ShakoProduction and writing signed Bertist Khallion Jeffers and Derrel Peter. The track passed one and a half million views on that video alone, which explains why a remix arrived the following year.

The remix came out on 13 June 2025. The Khallion video carries the ft Stalk Ashley mention and the Bouyon label, and the Deezer page of the radio edit, released by KhallioNation Records, credits Khallion and Stalk Ashley as contributors, for a running time of 3 minutes 55. The credits change on one point: the remix writing line holds three names, Bertist Jeffers, Adiann Morrison and Derel Peter, spelled that way. No source opened ties the name Adiann Morrison to the singer, so this profile draws no conclusion from that line.

The team around the remix is the Khallion circle: ShakoProduction on production, DeeArr and Brittant Maner on background vocals, Kiomii on additional vocals. Stalk Ashley therefore came into bouyon through the very workshop that makes the KhallioNation releases, not through a verse added from a distance.

Nobody with Trilla-G, on Take Me As I Am

“Nobody” is the eleventh of the thirteen tracks on “Take Me As I Am: The Album”, the first Trilla-G album, released on 22 October 2025 under Still Trill Ent. according to Deezer, where the track runs 2 minutes 44. The same day, the Trilla-G channel published the official audio as Nobody, with the Bouyon 2026 label, and the credits name her both as performer and as writer, beside A Plus and Gael Jno Baptiste.

The technical sheet of the official audio is precise: production by A Plus and DernzMusik, recording at Th3rdHouse in the US Virgin Islands, vocals engineered by Th3rd, mixing and mastering by Dernel Green, under his DernzMusik banner. The lyric video published on 23 December 2025 repeats those credits with one difference that this profile flags rather than smooths: it credits A Plus alone with the production.

The lyric video sits on a channel shared between Trilla-G and Stalk Ashley, a sign that both artists carry the track. It passed 47,000 views when consulted, a platform snapshot dated to the day the page was opened.

What this record says, and its limits

Two official releases, two leading Dominican artists, two bouyon mentions written on the release pages themselves: that is the verified core of the Stalk Ashley link with the genre. In both cases she is more than an added voice, since the “Nobody” credits count her among the writers.

This profile does not say she is a full time bouyon artist. Her own recent catalogue, from “Incognito” to “NBM”, is still presented in the dancehall register, and no source opened places her in Dominica or describes a bouyon stage performance. View counts are snapshots, and any new verified element will complete this page with its date.

To listen, start with “Nobody” on the Trilla-G album, then the “Put That On That” remix with Khallion, and carry on with our bouyon playlist.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Stalk Ashley?

Stalk Ashley is a singer whom The FADER presents as a Jamaican dancehall artist. In bouyon, she is a guest on two official releases: the Khallion remix of “Put That On That”, in June 2025, and the Trilla-G track “Nobody”, in October 2025, which she also co-wrote.

Which album is “Nobody” on?

On “Take Me As I Am: The Album”, the first Trilla-G album, released on 22 October 2025 under Still Trill Ent. It is track eleven of thirteen. The official audio is filed as Bouyon 2026 by the Trilla-G channel, with production by A Plus and DernzMusik and mixing by Dernel Green.

What is the difference between “Put That On That” and its remix?

The original, released by Khallion on 20 September 2024, is a solo track written by Bertist Khallion Jeffers and Derrel Peter. The 13 June 2025 remix adds Stalk Ashley as featured artist, a third writer name and background vocals by DeeArr, and came out on KhallioNation Records as a radio edit.

Where is Stalk Ashley from?

The only indication kept here is that of The FADER, which describes her as a Jamaican dancehall artist. No source opened for this profile gives a place or date of birth, and this profile invents none.

Where can I hear Stalk Ashley in bouyon?

Listen to “Nobody” with Trilla-G, then the “Put That On That” remix with Khallion. Our bouyon playlist follows the rest of the Dominica scene.
