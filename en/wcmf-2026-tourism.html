Bouyon music: WCMF 2026, what the festival weighs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

The World Creole Music Festival was played from 23 to 25 October 2026 at Windsor Park, Roseau. What does this festival weigh for Dominica's tourism? This page lines up only the figures published by the Discover Dominica Authority and the official statistics reported in the travel press, and it also says what is not yet known for 2026.

On this page
  1. The organiser's 2025 record: 42,000 attendees, 10,540 visitors
  2. The 2025 national context: 488,091 arrivals, EC$405.5 million spent
  3. What these figures do not say
  4. Come for the music, stay for the culture
  5. Frequently asked questions

The organiser's 2025 record: 42,000 attendees, 10,540 visitors

The Discover Dominica Authority release of 19 August 2026 gives the previous edition's record: the 2025 festival recorded more than 42,000 attendees across its three nights.

During the nine-day period surrounding the 2025 festival, Dominica welcomed 10,540 visitors, a 48 percent increase over 2024 and 61 percent over 2023. The Authority presents WCMF as one of the country's signature tourism events and a driver of arrivals during Independence season.

The 2025 national context: 488,091 arrivals, EC$405.5 million spent

Travel and Tour World, in its festival report published on 26 October 2026, reports the country's official statistics for 2025: 488,091 total passenger arrivals, growing by about 13 percent, and 49 percent above 2019.

Stay-over arrivals including yachts reached 112,195, cruise passenger arrivals 375,646, and direct visitor spending is estimated at EC$405.5 million for the full year.

What these figures do not say

The same article says it twice, and this page repeats it: no verified figure establishes the festival's exact 2026 share of hotel occupancy, employment or visitor spending, and the 2025 annual totals cannot be attributed to WCMF alone.

The organisers have not yet published an official 2026 results statement as this page is written. The only numbered record available therefore remains the 2025 one, given by the organiser itself.

Come for the music, stay for the culture

The Chief Executive Officer of the Discover Dominica Authority, Marva Williams, sums up the strategy in the release: come for the music, but stay for the culture, the cuisine, the traditional wear, the village celebrations and the beauty of the Nature Island.

The 2026 season illustrates the line: around the festival's three nights, the official programme lined up Ti Vilaj Kweyol, the Wob Dwiyet pageants, Jounen Kweyol and the cultural gala. The festival sells tickets; the whole season sells the country, and bouyon is its soundtrack.

Frequently asked questions

How many attendees did WCMF draw in 2025?

More than 42,000 attendees over three nights, per the Discover Dominica Authority release of 19 August 2026, which also gives 10,540 visitors during the nine days around the festival, up 48 percent on 2024.

What is Dominica's 2025 visitor spending figure?

EC$405.5 million in estimated direct visitor spending for the whole of 2025, per the official statistics reported by Travel and Tour World. That total covers all of the country's tourism, not the festival alone.

Is the economic impact of WCMF 2026 known?

No. No verified figure yet gives the 2026 edition's exact contribution, and the organisers had published no numbered results statement by 29 October 2026.
