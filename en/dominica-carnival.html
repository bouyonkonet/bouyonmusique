Dominica Carnival: the kingdom of bouyon

By Smokiller · Published September 23, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

Dominica’s carnival, celebrated in Roseau before Lent, is considered one of the most traditional in the Caribbean. And its soundtrack has a name: bouyon, created on this same island in 1988.

On this page

  2. The season of bouyon tracks

Mas Domnik, a carnival that stayed Creole

Where other carnivals have become tourist products, Mas Domnik keeps its old forms: chanté mas, lapo kabwit bands, traditional characters and the sensays. Bouyon found its natural ground there, because it is itself made from these traditions.

The season of bouyon tracks

Every year, carnival season launches the competition of bouyon tracks: the bands and producers release their new material, the public picks its favorites, and the winners become the classics of the year. Following Dominica’s carnival means following the beating heart of bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

When does Dominica Carnival take place?

Dominica Carnival takes place every year in Roseau, just before Lent. Its highlights are Carnival Monday and Tuesday, preceded by J’ouvert at dawn. Carnival season also launches the yearly competition of bouyon tracks, whose winners become the classics of the year.

What music is played at Dominica Carnival?

Bouyon first: created on the island in 1988, it is the soundtrack of Roseau carnival. You also hear soca and calypso, along with the traditions of chanté mas and the lapo kabwit bands, the very traditions bouyon itself is made from.

What is Mas Domnik?

Mas Domnik is the Creole name of Dominica's carnival, considered one of the most traditional in the Caribbean. Where other carnivals have become tourist products, it keeps its old forms: chanté mas, lapo kabwit bands, traditional characters and the sensays. See the Mas Domnik page.

Why is bouyon linked to Dominica Carnival?

Because bouyon was born on the same island, in 1988, and is made from the carnival's traditions, such as chanté mas and lapo kabwit. Every year, carnival season launches the competition of bouyon tracks: bands and producers release new material, the public picks its favorites and the winners become the classics of the year.

Who won Dominica's first Bouyon Road March?

“RAGS”, by Trilla G, Shelly and Skinny Fabulous, according to the March 2025 reports, which present it as the first Bouyon Road March title of Dominica. Triple Kay's official site, for its part, presents “Heat”, in 2019, as the first ever People's Bouyon Road March. The two accounts come from different sources and this site quotes them as they stand.

Has Triple Kay won Road March in Dominica?

Yes, several times according to the band's official site: “Please” in 2008, the band's first People's Road March, “Lock De Gate” in 2015, “Roll dat Boom Boom” in 2017, then “Heat” in 2019, presented as the first ever People's Bouyon Road March. “Nous Konnet Vive” was also the official theme song of the 2014 carnival. See Benji, a voice of the band.

Who won Bouyon Monarch in Dominica?

Carlyn XP, a singer from Roseau, won it two years in a row according to eMage DM. Her own biographies disagree on the dates: 2016 and 2017 according to StageIt, 2017 and 2018 according to Bandcamp. Those wins are what the Caribbean press bases the Bouyon Queen nickname it gives her on. She had also won Groovy Soca Monarch in 2010. See Carlyn XP.

Who are Dominica's recent Calypso Monarchs?

Trilla-G was crowned Dominica Calypso Monarch in 2023, and Daddy Chess won the crown again on February 14, 2026, twenty-five years after his first win in 2001. The most decorated name on record is Dennison “Dice” Joseph: eight crowns according to A Virtual Dominica, nine according to a later Caribbean Times article. See Trilla-G.

What is the difference between calypso, soca and bouyon at Dominica Carnival?

Calypso is the art of competition and social commentary, with monarchs crowned every carnival. Bouyon, born in the late 1980s with WCK, is the dominant sound of the fetes, played by bands and sound systems. Soca is carried by the orchestras. All three live on the same carnival stages, in front of the same Roseau crowds. See bouyon vs soca.
