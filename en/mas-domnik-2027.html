Bouyon music: Mas Domnik 2027, 9 January to 10 February

By Smokiller · Published November 1, 2026

Dominica 2027 calendar is already partly known. Mas Domnik, billed as the Real Mas, is announced from 9 January to 10 February 2027, per Dominica News, reporting the 2026 2027 calendar published by the Ministry of Tourism.

On this page
  1. Three 2027 dates already announced
  2. Carnival, the season of street bouyon
  3. Jazz n Creole and Glory in Paradise

Three 2027 dates already announced

The Ministry of Tourism published a calendar of 34 events: 31 events from 21 May to 3 November 2026, and three events already announced for 2027, per Dominica News.

For 2027: Mas Domnik from 9 January to 10 February, Glory in Paradise on 14 March, and Jazz n Creole on 2 May. These three dates are the only 2027 entries in the calendar published so far.

Carnival, the season of street bouyon

Mas Domnik is Dominica national carnival, the season of j ouvert, parades and street bands, where bouyon carries the soundtrack from dawn.

The 9 January to 10 February 2027 window covers the build-up of the season, from the first January events to the February carnival days. The detailed programme, calypso competitions and routes will be announced later by the organisers.

Jazz n Creole and Glory in Paradise

Jazz n Creole, announced for 2 May 2027, is Dominica spring jazz and Creole appointment, in the DOMFESTA and Tourism Awareness Month season, per the calendar reported by Dominica News.

Glory in Paradise, on 14 March 2027, completes the trio of 2027 dates already public. No detailed programme accompanies these announcements yet.

Why publish a calendar this early

Dominica News explains the aim of the calendar: increasing community participation and supporting tourism by attracting international participation.

For the diaspora and visitors, these dates make it possible to plan flights and stays months ahead, while the 2026 Independence season wraps up in Roseau.
