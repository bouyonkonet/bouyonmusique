Bouyon riddim: one rhythm, a thousand voices

By Smokiller · Published October 15, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

In bouyon as in dancehall, the riddim is the instrumental rhythm over which several artists can lay their voice. The bouyon riddim is the backbone of the genre’s modern production. To find who sang on which riddim, see our bouyon riddims list, with the verified songs on each riddim.

Riddim culture

A producer releases a riddim, the artists seize it, the audience picks its favorite versions. Bouyon riddims often carry a name, like Denmath’s Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim, and become trademarks. It is a creative system that came from Jamaica and that Dominica made its own.

Why riddims make the hits

Because a good riddim concentrates the identity of the moment: a tempo, a loop, a dance energy. The riddim snippets that producers dance to on social media have become the first launch step of a bouyon track.

Frequently asked questions

Who produces bouyon today?

Producers like Smokiller (Bouyon Shattaz), Denmath, HYPNO!, Savion and MB On The Track work the bouyon sound across riddims, remixes and original productions. On the riddim side, Denmath, from Saint Martin and based between Nantes and Paris, releases his own riddims, DJ Taffy, known as the Riddim Beast, produces from Dominica, while Smokiller, a producer born in Dominica, is known for his bouyon remixes.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the instrumental several artists record over. In bouyon, it brings together the loop, the bass and the percussion. A producer releases it, the artists seize it and the audience picks its favorite versions. Riddims sit at the heart of bouyon and dancehall production.

What are some examples of bouyon riddims?

Tropical Riddim, released in summer 2022, and Harley Riddim, released the same year, are two bouyon riddims by producer Denmath. As in dancehall, bouyon riddims often carry a name and become trademarks, taken up by several singers who each lay down their own version.

What is the difference between a riddim and a bouyon instrumental?

The riddim is the rhythmic base several artists lay their voices over. A bouyon instrumental is that riddim without the voice: the loop, the bass and the percussion in pure form. Released that way, it becomes a tool that DJs mix, dancers use and producers show off.

How does a bouyon riddim become a hit?

A good riddim concentrates the identity of the moment: a tempo, a loop, a dance energy. The riddim snippets that producers dance to on social media have become the first launch step of a bouyon track, and each new riddim calls for its own dance, from Roseau carnival to videos shot in Gwada and Paris.

Which riddims has Dada produced?

Dominican producer Dada, who launched bouyon soca in 2003 according to Smokiller's testimony, is behind the Smoke Riddim, which he presents, according to Ebuzztt, as a hybrid riddim blending soca and Dominica's bouyon: Bunji Garlin sings “Forward” on it and Shurwayne Winchester “Wet Dem Down”. The Daydream Riddim, produced by Krishna “Dada” Lawrence, carries Mr Gwada's “Come Join Me”. See Dada.

Which riddims has DJ Taffy produced?

DJ Taffy, a Dominican producer nicknamed the Riddim Beast, produced the Yeki Riddim, on which Quan and Bobby voiced “Kay Ve Yo” in 2020, and the Funny Riddim, which carries the “Kay ve yo” video by Shanika, an artist of the Guadeloupe scene. Viberate also lists “Maltete Riddim” and “LLSR (Last Lap Solo Riddim)” among his tracks. See DJ Taffy.

What is a one riddim project?

It is a project built on a single riddim, where several artists each lay their own track over the same base. Opening such a project means laying the first voice on a rhythm that others will then inhabit. In June 2023, TIITII NBA opened the Keneth Riddim project that way, with the track “No Sex Over Money”. See TIITII NBA.

Can several artists sing on the same riddim?

Yes, that is the very principle of a riddim. The “Mega Mix High Lights Riddim” by producer J2MO, known as The Beat Cooker, brings together for example Mad General, MIKTY and Miky Ding La on the same rhythm, at 160 BPM. The High Lights Riddim was released on September 27, 2021 by Up Full Media Group. See J2MO.

Which recent bouyon riddims should I listen to?

Among the riddims documented on this site: “Skyfall”, presented as Bouyon Riddim 2024 and signed by Fr3EzE, a producer from Dominica, plus his “Nothing At All Riddim” and “Hold Me Closer Riddim”, programmed in the Blaqrose Supreme podcast. In 2025, J2MO followed up with “Riddim Up (By J2mo)”, “Street Fighter Riddim” and “Future Riddim”. See Fr3EzE.
