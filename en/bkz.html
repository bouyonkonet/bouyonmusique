BKZ: the rapper behind Joyeux Bouyon and Bouyon vs Shatta

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

BKZ is a rapper who releases his music under the Fill The Bag banner. After several rap EPs, he tried bouyon at the end of 2025 with two “Joyeux Bouyon” singles, then set the two styles against each other in 2026 on the EP “Bouyon vs Shatta, Vol. 1”.

Fill The Bag, rap first

The Fill The Bag name shows up as early as June 2022 with the EP “Fill The Bag (Saison 1)”, five tracks including “Birmingham II” and “Arc-en-ciel”. In February 2025, “Menace to Society” stayed with rap, with “Douleur”, “Gotham” and “Cicatrice”, where he brings in RVPIDO.

Fill The Bag then became the label on almost all his releases. Summer 2025 brought “Maximum”, a single that returned a year later on the EP “Amor Fati”, next to “Nasty”, “Rocks” and “Dans le bloc”.

Joyeux Bouyon 1 and 2

On October 10, 2025 he released “Joyeux Bouyon 1 #BBL”. “Joyeux Bouyon 2 #CHABINE” followed on November 28. Both carry the genre in their name and a hashtag as a subtitle, like a series.

The numbers are modest, around 1,000 views each on YouTube. Still, the pair marks a clear change of direction for an artist credited until then as a rapper.

Bouyon vs Shatta, Vol. 1

In March 2026, the single “Mechant” became his most watched track, with more than 24,000 views. In September it opened the EP “Bouyon vs Shatta, Vol. 1”, alongside “Shake It”, “Ballerina” and “T Fou”.

The EP title sets up a face-off between the two Caribbean styles. The credits list BKZ on rap, and also as producer on “Ballerina”. Fishtai recorded the three new tracks. The “Vol. 1” tag suggests more to come.
