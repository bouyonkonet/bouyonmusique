Sa Sa Sammy: Whisper, H.A.T.E and the 2017 Bouyon Monarch

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Sa Sa Sammy is a bouyon voice and a 2017 finalist in Dominica's Bouyon Monarch. From 2017 to 2019, Sa Sa Sammy put out songs billed as bouyon every season, from “Whisper” to “Roll Up”, then “H.A.T.E” on a DJ Taffy riddim, the most watched song in the catalogue.

Finalist in 2017

In February 2017, Soca News published the finalists for the second Bouyon Monarch, set for Carnival City in Roseau. Sa Sa Sammy is on the list, with reigning monarch Carlyn XP, Fetty Mark and eight other contestants. The final placings do not appear in the sources we checked.

The same season brought “Whisper”, posted at the end of June 2017 as “2017 Bouyon” and picked up by other channels. The sources we checked give no real name, island or age. For the setting of the contest, see our Dominica Carnival page.

One release a season

In 2018, “What Is Love” and “Looters Paradise” came out as “2018 Bouyon”. That December, “Roll Up”, on the Chaud Riddim, opened the 2019 season; another upload credits the production to Bryce Beats. “I Will Give It” was also posted as “Bouyon 2019”.

These songs sit between 1,300 and 2,500 views each on YouTube. They are not hits, but the pattern matters: every carnival brought something new, posted by the channels that follow bouyon closely.

H.A.T.E, with DJ Taffy

In September 2019, DJ Taffy's channel posted “H.A.T.E”, on the Maltete Riddim. Sa Sa Sammy wrote and sang it, DJ Taffy produced it and YW3Ent mixed it. At close to 4,900 views, it is the most played Sa Sa Sammy song on YouTube.

After that the trail thins: “Anything Goes” and “Dreaming” on promo channels, and in 2020 a preview of “What We Need”, posted on the SaSa Sammy channel as “Bouyon 2020”.
