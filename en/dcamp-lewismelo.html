Dcamp and Lewismelo: BOUYON, quite simply

Dcamp and Lewismelo form a Franco-Creole bouyon duo. Their track BOUYON, with the name that says it all, is one of the videos Bouyon Shattaz chose to represent the genre to its audience.

Bouyon on the French side

The duo works the bouyon material on the side of the French Antilles and France, with the flag of Guadeloupe put forward in Bouyon Shattaz’s content. Their path illustrates modern bouyon: a Dominican sound, Creole artists from several islands, an audience in network.
