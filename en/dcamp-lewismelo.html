Dcamp & Lewismelo, the duo that dared to call its track BOUYON

By Smokiller · Published October 8, 2026 · Updated October 28, 2026

Dcamp & Lewismelo, Dylan and Lewis Campbell, are a French-Creole bouyon duo in which Lewis also produces their riddims. Their reference track carries the name of the genre itself: BOUYON, with an official video on YouTube. Calling your song BOUYON is a statement of intent: no detour, no translation, the Dominican sound claimed as the title. In October 2026, Bouyon Shattaz built a 55-second vertical edit of the official video, on the active section from 1:20 to 2:15, in its producer-reel style. Their precise island origins are not confirmed in our records: this page therefore speaks of their work from the French and Creole side of bouyon, without turning it into an invented biography.

A duo, two roles: the voices and the riddims

The way Dcamp & Lewismelo works fits in one sentence: Dylan and Lewis Campbell sing as a duo, and Lewis also makes the riddims. When the producer is inside the duo, the beat is not bought outside and then endured by the voices: it is built with them, for their tones and their way of answering each other.

The published credits confirm that split, and our Dcamp profile details them track by track. The two full names are Dylan Davis Campbell, Dcamp, and Lewis Wayne Campbell, Lewismelo. They co-sign the writing on almost every duo track, and the musical side goes to Lewis: credited producer on BAY LA BÈT and On My Way, sole credited composer in the official video descriptions of YOU AND ME and LUSHENANA.

That duo-plus-in-house-production model brings the project close to the founding bands of bouyon, who made their sound themselves, and sets it apart from circuits where an artist records over an anonymous beat. It also joins the family of profiles followed on our page about the producers and passers, except that here the passer and the singer are the same person. No verified official profile of the duo appears in our records, so this page gives no account link.

BOUYON, the manifesto title

It takes a certain confidence to title your song BOUYON. The title does not only describe the song’s genre, it claims a place inside it. For a French-Creole duo, it is also a way of speaking to the audience of the French Antilles and France, for whom bouyon is the sound of parties and playlists, with no user manual needed.

The official video, published on YouTube, is the source for the Bouyon Shattaz coverage: a 55-second vertical edit of the section from 1:20 to 2:15, the song full frame, the title in a pill, and the local Gwada flag, green bands, a red middle band and a yellow star, placed by the title. That flag is Smokiller’s branding choice to signal the French and Antillean side of their work, without asserting an origin our records do not confirm.

The other tracks on file, one by one

Beyond BOUYON, our sources list seven recordings by the duo, verified on Shazam and YouTube.

On My Way, released in 2026, is the most recent. Its listing credits Dylan Campbell and Lewis Campbell on rap and writing, and Lewis Campbell on production. Its lyrics move from French to Creole to English inside the same hook: “Baby comen i lé, dis moi ce qui ne va pas, I am on my way”. Two voices, one in-house producer, three languages: the duo’s method in a single track.

BAY LA BÈT exists in two versions on the single of the same name, released in 2025: the standard version and an Extended version, released on January 24, 2025 through Lewismeloproduction, with Lewis Wayne Campbell on rap, production and writing shared with Dylan Davis Campbell. Its published tempo, 134 BPM, is more laid back than the 157 BPM of TOURNEVIS, the track Dcamp carries as lead artist: the tandem’s catalogue does not hold to a single speed.

YOU AND ME brings the duo together with Admiral T, on the ELITE BASE label. The official video description, directed by Merce De Bax Ville, credits Admiral T and Dcamp as authors and Lewis Campbell on composition, and the lyrics move between English, French and Guadeloupean Creole, around the hook “nou pépa zanmi”. Admiral T carries the civil name Christy Campbell in his credits, but no source consulted describes a family link with the two brothers, and this site asserts none.

LUSHENANA, with T-Stone, came out as a single in 2025 according to T-Stone’s artist page. Its official video, directed by Jima Kanor, credits T-Stone, Lewis Campbell and Dcamp as authors, and composition again goes to Lewis Campbell. Two tracks complete the list taken from the duo’s Shazam artist page: LA BOULANGERIE, and GOUTÉW, where Dcamp & Lewismelo are credited as featured artists. Our records give neither their date nor their credits.

Premières Notes and the network of Guadeloupe sessions

The richest thread in the record runs through Lewismelo’s album, Premières Notes, released on November 1, 2024. Its track list, detailed on the profiles of its guests, opens with an INTRO credited to Lewis Wayne Campbell, then lines up one guest per track: 5 KILOS with Admiral T and Shanika, VIN TCHEK NOU with Le Bluff, Leyou and Tedla, POMPÉ with Dcamp and KEKS MAFIA, ALOUETTE with Lejuh, KEY with Virtuose and HULLABALOO with Jixels.

Each guest leads to a page on this site. Shanika co-wrote 5 KILOS with Christy Campbell, and NRJ Antilles presented the track as a new hit signed Lewismelo, Admiral T and Shanika. Le Bluff and Leyou hold their place as guest voices on VIN TCHEK NOU. On POMPÉ, the published writers are Lewis Wayne Campbell, Dylan Davis Campbell and Kevin Lennard, the name the credits tie to the place of Keks Mafia, a rapper from Dominica.

Outside the album, Sòti Gwadloup, a 2024 single listed on Admiral T’s artist page, brings together Admiral T, Dcamp, Lejuh and Lewismelo, a meeting between generations according to our Lejuh profile. And the Viberate platform lists Dcamp and Lewismelo among the artists close to Nils, with Pinpin OSP and Aknose. That network is why our Guadeloupe artists chapter counts their manifesto track among the four routes of bouyon gwada, next to Miimii KDS, TIITII NBA and Ricky Bishop.

The French and Creole side of a Dominican sound

Bouyon was born in Dominica, and our page on the history of bouyon tells how the sound became regional and then international. Dcamp & Lewismelo work it from the other shore of its current history: the French Antilles and France, where Creole artists sing it in Creole and French for an audience fed on excerpts and playlists. Our page the Guadeloupe chapter from Dominica cites the duo precisely on the French-Creole side of the scene. The precise home islands of the two brothers are not confirmed in our records, so the page sticks to what is established: a duo, an in-house producer, a manifesto title, a credited catalogue and a published network.

Why the duo matters now, and how to hear it

The duo matters first as a production model: two writers credited under their full names, an in-house composer, two release structures, Dcampproduction and Lewismeloproduction. It matters next as a crossroads: Lewismelo’s album serves as a common roof for a generation of Guadeloupe voices, and Admiral T and T-Stone connect the tandem to established artists. It matters finally in the DJ circuit: the published tracklist of Digga D’s Sewo Jam 2026 show lists “Dcamp Lewismelo - Coco” in fifth position.

For listening: BOUYON for the manifesto, On My Way for the method, YOU AND ME for the Admiral T step, Premières Notes for the network. The Bouyon Shattaz ROAD MIX bouyon playlist, on Spotify and YouTube, then places those tracks within the bouyon of Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique.

Frequently asked questions

Who are Dcamp & Lewismelo, and where are they from?

Dcamp & Lewismelo are two brothers, Dylan Davis Campbell and Lewis Wayne Campbell, who form a French-Creole bouyon duo. Their precise home islands are not confirmed in our records. Their work sits inside the published network of the Guadeloupe scene, alongside Admiral T, Shanika and Lejuh.

What role does each member play in the duo?

Both brothers sing and write together: their full names return as writers on almost every track. Lewis produces and composes the riddims, credited on production for BAY LA BÈT and On My Way and on composition for YOU AND ME and LUSHENANA. Dcamp also carries one track as lead artist, detailed on our Dcamp profile.

What are the key tracks by Dcamp & Lewismelo?

BOUYON, their manifesto track with an official YouTube video, opens the catalogue. Then come On My Way (2026), BAY LA BÈT and its Extended version (2025), YOU AND ME with Admiral T and LUSHENANA with T-Stone (2025). Add Premières Notes, Lewismelo’s album released on November 1, 2024, where Dcamp appears on POMPÉ.

Who have Dcamp & Lewismelo worked with?

With Admiral T on YOU AND ME and Sòti Gwadloup, the latter with Lejuh, and with T-Stone on LUSHENANA. Lewismelo’s album Premières Notes adds Shanika, Le Bluff, Leyou, Tedla, KEKS MAFIA, Lejuh, Virtuose and Jixels, one guest per track. The credits are detailed on our Le Bluff page.

Where can I hear Dcamp & Lewismelo?

The documented entry point is the official BOUYON video on YouTube, with the 55-second vertical edit by Bouyon Shattaz. Their other tracks are on the streaming platforms, and Coco circulates in mixes such as Digga D’s Sewo Jam 2026. For context, the ROAD MIX bouyon playlist gathers the bouyon of Dominica and the French Antilles.

What are Dcamp & Lewismelo's real names?

Dcamp is Dylan Davis Campbell and Lewismelo is Lewis Wayne Campbell: those are the two full names the published credits print as writers on almost every duo track. The tandem also releases through two structures, Dcampproduction and Lewismeloproduction, and Lewis is the one credited on production and composition. See the Lewismelo profile.

Are Dcamp & Lewismelo related to Admiral T?

No family link is documented. Admiral T carries the civil name Christy Campbell in his credits and works with the brothers on YOU AND ME and Sòti Gwadloup, and with Lewismelo on 5 KILOS, but no source consulted describes a family link between him and the two Campbell brothers, and this site asserts none. The shared surname proves nothing on its own.

Who produced BAY LA BÈT?

Lewismelo. The Extended version, released on January 24, 2025 through Lewismeloproduction, credits Lewis Wayne Campbell on rap, production and writing, the writing shared with Dylan Davis Campbell. The single holds a standard and an Extended version, with a published tempo of 134 BPM, more laid back than the 157 BPM of TOURNEVIS, the track Dcamp carries as lead artist.

What is Lewismelo's album Premières Notes?

Premières Notes is Lewismelo's album, released on November 1, 2024. After an INTRO credited to Lewis Wayne Campbell, it lines up one guest per track, from Admiral T and Shanika on 5 KILOS to Jixels on HULLABALOO, with Dcamp and KEKS MAFIA on POMPÉ. NRJ Antilles presented 5 KILOS as a new hit signed Lewismelo, Admiral T and Shanika: the album serves as a common roof for a generation of Guadeloupe voices.

Calling your song BOUYON is a statement of intent: no detour, no translation, the Dominican sound claimed as the title. The title does not only describe the song's genre, it claims a place inside it, and for a French-Creole duo it is also a way of speaking to the audience of the French Antilles and France, for whom bouyon is the sound of parties and playlists.

What languages do Dcamp & Lewismelo sing in?

French, Creole and English, often in the same track. The hook of On My Way (2026) moves from French to Creole to English: “Baby comen i lé, dis moi ce qui ne va pas, I am on my way”. YOU AND ME, with Admiral T, moves between English, French and Guadeloupean Creole, around the hook “nou pépa zanmi”. Two voices, one in-house producer, three languages.

Do Dcamp & Lewismelo make bouyon gwada?

Their published network is that of the Guadeloupe scene, alongside Admiral T, Shanika and Lejuh, and our Guadeloupe artists chapter counts their manifesto track BOUYON among the four routes of bouyon gwada, next to Miimii KDS, TIITII NBA and Ricky Bishop.
