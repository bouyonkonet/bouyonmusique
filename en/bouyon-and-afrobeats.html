Bouyon and afrobeats, the meeting through remixes

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Afrobeats, the West African pop that tops charts around the world, and bouyon, the fast sound of Dominica, look alike: dance rhythms, choruses that stick in the head and party energy. So far, their meeting runs through the remixes of Smokiller, who flips afrobeats songs into bouyon. Two flips are verified: Heaven Baby by Ayra Starr and Shake It To The Max by Moliy.

Afrobeats, the sound of West Africa

Afrobeats, with an s, is an umbrella term for contemporary West African pop, developed in Nigeria, Ghana and the United Kingdom in the 2000s and 2010s. It blends highlife, hiplife, juju, hip-hop, dancehall, R&B, house, ndombolo and palm-wine music. It should not be confused with Afrobeat, without an s, the jazz-funk genre of the 1960s and 1970s created by Fela Kuti.

Bouyon, for its part, was born in Dominica in the late 1980s, from the mix of cadence-lypso and jing ping, with the lapo kabwit drum and chante mas. Both musics share a taste for dancing, repeated choruses and rhythms that make people move. That shared ground is what makes the remixes possible, even though the two scenes have not yet recorded a song together.

Heaven Baby, the flip that crossed the Atlantic

Ayra Starr is a Nigerian singer. Her third studio album, Starrgirl, came out on 14 August 2026 on Mavin Records and Republic Records. Heaven Baby, featuring ZAYN, is the fourteenth track of the album and runs 2 minutes 38. The song entered the UK Official Afrobeats Chart at No. 2, then reached No. 1 on the chart dated 17 to 23 October 2026. Ayra Starr performed it to open her NFL London halftime set on 11 October 2026, and the official video arrived on 5 October 2026.

Smokiller turned it into a bouyon remix at 128 BPM, running 3 minutes 24, with a DJ edit of 3 minutes 39 and a 15-second intro loop. The remix is the first track of Rotation Pack 01 and is a free download on smokiller.com. The full story of the song, the remix and the campaign is already told in depth on this site, on the Heaven Baby page. What matters here is the principle: a Nigerian afrobeats song, taken up by a Dominican producer, becomes a bouyon song.

Shake It To The Max, from Moliy to Dominica

The second verified flip is Shake It To The Max. The original is by Ghanaian singer Moliy and Jamaican producer Silent Addy, released on 3 December 2024, between Afropop, dancehall and afrofusion. The remix, released in February and March 2025, added Jamaican dancehall stars Shenseea and Skillibeng. The song spent 22 consecutive weeks at the top of the Billboard US Afrobeats Songs chart, passed one billion streams by early 2026, and won Dancehall Song of the Year at the Caribbean Music Awards on 19 September 2026 in Port of Spain.

Smokiller offers a bouyon remix of it, listed on smokiller.com. Watch the credit: Smokiller site calls its video Shenseea, Shake It To The Max (Bouyon Remix), but the song is Moliy, with Shenseea and Skillibeng on the remix. Moliy is the originator. Smokiller remix therefore turns a Ghanaian afrobeats hit, already passed through Jamaican dancehall, into Dominican bouyon.

A meeting that still runs one way

That should be said plainly: to date, no Nigerian or Ghanaian artist has been verified on a bouyon riddim, and no bouyon artist has been verified on an afrobeats track. The meeting exists, but it runs through Smokiller remixes and through producers, who sell bouyon type beats using the names of afrobeats stars such as Wizkid or Burna Boy. Those are producer listings, not collaborations.

Other leads were checked and set aside: a song called Afro Bouyon, Pt. 1, by Kosar and Flatt boy, could not be confirmed, and a mention of Congolese bouyon in an article about the Starrgirl album refers to a Congolese use of the word, not Dominican bouyon. The two verified flips are enough to show the direction: bouyon digests afrobeats, as it digested dancehall and R&B. The next step, a song recorded together by an afrobeats artist and a bouyon artist, does not exist yet.
