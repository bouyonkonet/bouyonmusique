By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Motto, Lashley Winter by name, is a Saint Lucian singer and producer based in New York, whom Caribbean Beat presented in 2019 as the leading producer of Dennery Segment, Saint Lucia's fast genre. The producer who took Dennery Segment out of Saint Lucia

In her “Dennery Style” backstory (Caribbean Beat, issue 155, January/February 2019), Laura Dowrich describes Lashley “Motto” Winter as a Saint Lucian resident in New York and the leading producer of Dennery Segment. His method, per the article: matching big soca names from other islands with Dennery riddims to carry the genre beyond Saint Lucia. The examples cited: Machel Montano's “Showtime” on the Pim Pim Riddim, “Party Lit” with Lyrikal, the Gwada Riddim with Bunji Garlin and Fay Ann Lyons, “Pick Yuh Position” with Skinny Fabulous in late October 2018.

The article also recalls his own titles, “Bend Dong”, remixed with Problem Child and Mr Legz, and “Force” with Lava Man, Loose Cannon and Hypa 4000, as well as the Force It Riddim album. The parallel with bouyon is direct: two fast genres of the Windward Islands, two neighbouring scenes, and a producer used to making a sound travel. See our page on bouyon in Saint Lucia and the profiles of Machel Montano, Lyrikal, Bunji Garlin and Skinny Fabulous.

Bouyon Rave and Jouvert: January 2026

On January 12, 2026, “Bouyon Rave” reached Deezer, credited to Motto, Blaka Dan, Teamfoxx and Boogy Rankss, label TeamFoxx / Boogy Rankss, 3 minutes 31. On January 14, the TeamFoxx channel uploaded the visualizer, billed “2026 Bouyon Power Soca”, produced by TeamFoxx, written by Lashley Motto Winter and Wrenroy “Blaka Dan” Ogiste; it was past 10,000 views when read.

Two weeks later, on January 26, 2026, JulianspromosTV published “Motto - Jouvert (Cut Eye Riddim) | Bouyon 2026”. The Cut Eye Riddim is a Drummixx Productions project dated October 10, 2025 on Deezer, which carries “J'ouvert (feat. Motto)”; the riddim mix published by DjeasyPromos on January 29, 2026 lines up Khallion, Ebony Empress, Motto, Wassy K and Jah Fyah.

Say My Name: a St Lucia-Dominica title

On March 20, 2026, TeamFoxx published “Say My Name - Motto (Official Music Video) 2026 St Lucia-Dominica Bouyon Music (Prod. by Shako)”. The page is explicit: genre bouyon, producer Shako, executive producer Khallion, mixing and mastering by Teamfoxx and Khallion, lyrics written by Lashley Motto Winter. The video was past 7,500 views when read and the title was also carried by Julianspromos and Kayus Entertainment.

The credit “Prod. by Teamfoxx - Lashley Motto Winter”, read on the page of “Slick” (Victony x Teamfoxx, September 2026), confirms that Teamfoxx is Motto's own production name. This profile is written from Caribbean Beat and the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026; the Deezer artist page under the name Motto mixes several namesakes and is not cited for its counts.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Motto?

Lashley Winter, a Saint Lucian singer and producer based in New York, presented by Caribbean Beat (2019) as the leading producer of Dennery Segment; he produces under the name Teamfoxx.

What are his bouyon titles?

Bouyon Rave with Blaka Dan (January 12, 2026, label TeamFoxx / Boogy Rankss), Jouvert on Drummixx's Cut Eye Riddim (Bouyon 2026) and Say My Name (March 20, 2026), billed St Lucia-Dominica bouyon.

Who produced Say My Name?

Shako, with Khallion as executive producer and a mix by Teamfoxx and Khallion, according to the TeamFoxx YouTube page; the lyrics are by Lashley Motto Winter.

What is Teamfoxx?

Motto's production name: the Slick page (September 2026) credits “Prod. by Teamfoxx - Lashley Motto Winter”.

What did he produce before bouyon?

Per Caribbean Beat: Machel Montano's Showtime (Pim Pim Riddim), Party Lit with Lyrikal, the Gwada Riddim with Bunji Garlin and Fay Ann Lyons, Pick Yuh Position with Skinny Fabulous, and his own titles Bend Dong and Force.
