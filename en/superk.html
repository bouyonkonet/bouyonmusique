SuperK: Heart Broken, Vincy Soca and Bouyon 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

SuperK is a producer and singer of St Vincent's soca scene, whom iWitness News named in 2019 as the producer of Magikal, that year's Soca Monarch. He bills his own titles: “Heart Broken”, with Jay-R, came out on April 12, 2026 under the double billing “Vincy Soca 2026 | Bouyon 2026”; “Home”, with the same Jay R, followed in May and was past 12,000 views when this profile was written. As early as 2023, “BankCard FreeStyle” on his ATM Riddim carried the word bouyon in its title.

Heart Broken: Vincy Soca and bouyon in the same billing

On April 12, 2026, the SuperK channel uploaded “SuperK x Jay-R - Heart Broken | Vincy Soca 2026 | Bouyon 2026 (Lyric Video)”. The page credits writing to SuperK and Jay R, production to SuperK, mixing and mastering to Adigun, and gives the Instagram handle superk784. The double billing states the intent: a title of the Vincentian soca season, also presented as bouyon.

On May 3, 2026 came “Jay R x SuperK - Home | Lyric Video | Vincy Soca 2026”, written by Javid “Jay R” Rouse, produced by SuperK with additional production and a mix by Parry Jack, video by Nex Media Entertainment; the title was past 12,300 views when read. For how St Vincent takes up the bouyon label, see our page on bouyon soca and the profile of Skinny Fabulous.

BankCard FreeStyle and the ATM Riddim: the 2023 bouyon billing

On June 9, 2023, SuperK published “BankCard FreeStyle (Official Audio) ATM Riddim (Vincy Soca 2023) Bouyon”, produced by SuperK, with additional production by DJ Jeanie and a mix by Dougal “Fresh” Allen. The page's tags line up St Vincent, Antigua and Dominica: the producer was aiming, as early as 2023, at the circuit of islands where bouyon travels.

Jay R, guest on “Wild (Official Audio) ATM Riddim (Vincy Soca 2023)” on the same channel, returns on the two 2026 titles. The SuperK and Jay R pair thus forms the thread of the pages opened for this profile, from the 2023 ATM Riddim to “Heart Broken” and “Home”.

Magikal's producer

When iWitness News told, on July 7, 2019, of Magikal's Vincy Mas Soca Monarch win with “Bloody Hell Crazy”, the article named his producer: Super K. Three years later, the YouTube page of “Wah Deh (vincy soca 2022)” credits production to Super-K production and Mr Vik. SuperK is therefore the producer behind the crowning title of the singer this site presents for “Whappa”, and behind his most viewed 2022 title.

This profile is written from the YouTube pages opened on October 27, 2026 and from iWitness News. Deezer shows an artist page under the name Superk, not verified as the same producer: no release count is cited. No page states his birthplace; his billings are Vincy Soca and his handle carries 784, and this site places him in the Vincentian scene without stating an origin. See also the profile of Magikal.

Frequently asked questions

Who is SuperK?

A producer and singer of the Vincy Soca scene, named by iWitness News (2019) as Magikal's producer, maker of Heart Broken with Jay-R (Vincy Soca 2026 | Bouyon 2026).

What are his titles billed bouyon?

Heart Broken with Jay-R (April 12, 2026, Vincy Soca 2026 | Bouyon 2026) and BankCard FreeStyle on the ATM Riddim (June 9, 2023, Vincy Soca 2023, Bouyon).

Who is Jay R?

Javid Rouse, credited for writing Home (May 2026), SuperK's partner on Heart Broken, Home and the ATM Riddim (Wild, 2023).

Which Magikal title did he produce?

Bloody Hell Crazy, the 2019 Soca Monarch title, according to iWitness News, and Wah Deh (2022), credited to Super-K production and Mr Vik.

Where is SuperK from?

No opened page spells it out. His billings are Vincy Soca and his Instagram handle carries 784; this profile places him in the St Vincent scene without stating a birthplace.
