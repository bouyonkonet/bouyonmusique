Lov'Nee: a shatta voice that crosses into bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Lov'Nee is a name whose catalogue, from 2020 to 2026, sits mostly on the shatta side, with detours through kompa, reggae and dancehall; the channel describes itself with the words Dancehall, Rap, Trap, Hip-Hop, Reggae and Carib’Urban. Lov'Nee enters this site through one specific title: “An Tchou Ay”, a freestyle posted on March 30, 2026 on the artist's channel under the label Remix Bouyon. The same year Lov'Nee sang on “Va Dire Ça” with Aknose and released “Wukkup” with DJ Killerz, two names from French Caribbean bouyon. No opened source gives an origin, and this profile assigns none.

An Tchou Ay: the bouyon remix

The page that qualifies Lov'Nee is short: a two minute video, posted on March 30, 2026 on the artist's own channel, with no description, under the title “LOV'NEE - AN TCHOU AY (FREESTYLE) 2026 REMIX BOUYON”. The title says three things: a freestyle, the year, and a voice laid over a bouyon rhythm. It is the only page in the file that states the genre, and the only title this profile enters in its structured data.

That title is a side step in a catalogue that labels itself differently. The channel titles name their genres: “Shatta Rédhibitoire” (close to 494,000 views, and performed on May 30, 2026 on Skyrock's La Nocturne with Fred Musa), “Kanibal” on the WYFL Riddim, “Oublié’w” as Kompa’Shatta, “One Love” as reggae. Lov'Nee is therefore presented here as a shatta voice that has recorded bouyon, not as an exclusively bouyon name.

Aknose, DJ Killerz and the bouyon circuit

On May 27, 2026, Lov'Nee's channel posted “Va Dire Ça”, an Aknose track featuring Lov'Nee, produced by Lijay; Deezer lists the single on May 29. On June 19, 2026 came “Wukkup (Don Njano)”, credited to DJ Killerz and Lov'Nee. Neither listing states a genre, and this profile does not classify them: they simply show Lov'Nee working with names this site already documents for their bouyon.

Lov'Nee's most regular producer is Jyz, credited as Jyzbeatmaker on “Ga Létaw” (August 14, 2026, about 286,000 views) and on “Kay Ka Fèt” (July 24, 2026), two videos directed by William Thomas. Deezer also keeps an album, “LUNAR”, released on July 25, 2024, and an older title that names a riddim, “Mission Bordel (Overload Riddim 2021)”.

Limits of the file

No opened page publishes a legal name, a birth date or an origin for Lov'Nee, and this site assigns the artist no island. Two artist pages coexist on Deezer under close spellings (Lov'Nee and Lov’nee): only the first, which carries “LUNAR” and the singles cited, is attached to this profile.

For the border between the two genres in this repertoire, see the shatta bouyon page, and for the Guadeloupe scene around Aknose, the bouyon gwada page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Lov'Nee?

A name whose catalogue from 2020 to 2026 is mostly shatta, with kompa, reggae and dancehall titles. In March 2026 Lov'Nee posted an An Tchou Ay freestyle as a bouyon remix, and sings with Aknose on Va Dire Ça.

What is Lov'Nee's bouyon title?

“An Tchou Ay (Freestyle) 2026 Remix Bouyon”, posted on March 30, 2026 on the artist's YouTube channel. It is the only opened page in the file that states the genre.

Has Lov'Nee worked with Aknose?

Yes. Va Dire Ça, an Aknose track featuring Lov'Nee, produced by Lijay, was posted on May 27, 2026 on the artist's channel and is listed on Deezer on May 29, 2026. The listing states no genre.

Where is Lov'Nee from?

No origin is named in any of the sources opened during this research. This site therefore assigns the artist no island.

Is Lov'Nee a shatta or a bouyon name?

The artist's own titles point first to shatta. This profile presents Lov'Nee as a shatta voice who has recorded at least one stated bouyon title, and who works with bouyon artists such as Aknose and DJ Killerz.
