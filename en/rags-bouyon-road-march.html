RAGS: the track that won the first Bouyon Road March in 2025

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

In 2025 Dominica hosted a Bouyon Road March competition for the first time, during Mas Domnik, its annual Carnival. The winning track is RAGS, a collaboration between Trilla G, Shelly and Skinny Fabulous, chosen by popular vote. This page gathers what opened sources document about the track, its credits and its context, with no lyrics reproduced.

A first title, decided by popular vote

The Bajan Reporter announced RAGS as the winner of the Bouyon Road March 2025 title, won by popular vote as Mas Domnik drew to a close. Z89, carrying the Billboard investigation into bouyon, confirms it was the very first Bouyon Road March competition held in Dominica, and that RAGS took just over a third of the vote.

The creation of a separate bouyon category, campaigned for in part by Emile Depooter, who previously managed WCK Band and Triple K per Z89, marks the place bouyon has taken inside Dominica Carnival season.

Three voices, three territories

RAGS brings together Gael Trilla G Jno. Baptiste and Sheldon Shelly Alfred for Dominica, and Gamal Skinny Fabulous Doyle for Saint Vincent, per The Bajan Reporter.

Shelly, the lead singer of Signal Band, also placed second and third in the same competition with his band, with Bye Bye Bye and My Band, per Z89. He describes there the band decision to change the narrative that Carnival was not for Signal Band.

The credits of the track

Per The Bajan Reporter, RAGS was penned by Trilla G, Shelly and Christon DJ Oberr Jno Baptiste, and produced by A Plus Musik of Th3rd House Studios in St. Thomas, US Virgin Islands, where Trilla G vocals were recorded.

The melody was inspired by Never Enough, from the movie musical The Greatest Showman, per both sources. Mixing and mastering are credited to Dernell Dernz Green, and backing vocals to M Kayla Eustache, per The Bajan Reporter.

A recording path across several studios

Per The Bajan Reporter, a first draft existed as early as November 2024. Skinny Fabulous recorded his part at Black Shadow Studios in Miami, Trilla G added vocals at 47 Studios in Trinidad, and Shelly recorded at Signal Band Studio in Dominica, just before the February 2025 release.

That path, four places for one track, shows how a bouyon road march is built today between Dominica, the Caribbean and the diaspora, with published credits to follow it by.

Frequently asked questions

What is RAGS?

A bouyon track by Trilla G, Shelly and Skinny Fabulous, released in February 2025, which won Dominica first Bouyon Road March title at Mas Domnik 2025.

How was the 2025 Bouyon Road March winner chosen?

By popular vote, per The Bajan Reporter. Z89 adds that RAGS took just over a third of the vote.

Who produced RAGS?

A Plus Musik of Th3rd House Studios in St. Thomas, per The Bajan Reporter. Mixing and mastering are credited to Dernell Dernz Green.
