Kubu: Hennessy and his 2026 bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Kubu is a singer who has been posting bouyon songs on his own YouTube channel since summer 2026. His most played track, “Hennessy”, rides Wetty Beatz's Link Up Riddim. His pages are written in French and Creole and name Guadeloupe, Grenada and Dominica, but never say where he is from.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

Hennessy, on Wetty Beatz's riddim

On August 22, 2026 Kubu uploaded “Hennessy”, billed “bouyon 2026 Link up riddim”. The same day, the DistroKid release came out as “Hennessy bouyon”, credited to Kubuli and Wetty Beatz. At over 3,300 views, it is his most followed song.

The Link Up Riddim came out on May 25, 2026 with Magikal, Problem Child and Nerdy. “Hennessy” is not on that six-track EP: it arrived three months later, outside the official release. On October 16 Kubu posted a video for it, made with artificial intelligence, as the description says.

I Love You and Red Flag

On September 8, 2026 came “I Love You”, announced in capitals as “BOUYON 2026”, which the DistroKid release titles “I love you (bouyon)”. It has stayed small, around 165 views when read.

“Red Flag” is a duet with Slaaky. The DistroKid release, out on May 11, 2025 on the Flybeu record label, is credited to Slaaky featuring Kubu, with Slow, Litle boy and Flybeu. Kubu posted it on his own channel on August 24, 2026, tagged “bouyon”.

One name, several spellings

On YouTube the artist signs as Kubu; on DistroKid his releases are credited to Kubuli. That name is also a Dominican beer and part of Waitukubuli, the Kalinago name of the island, but no source links the artist to either. Several titles carry the year 2027, with no explanation.

On October 13, 2026 he posted “Mwen ka palé créole”, a freestyle billed as soca, claiming his language right in the title. His descriptions are in French and his tags mix #guadeloupe, #971 and #grenada. No age, real name or home island appears on his pages.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kubu?

A singer posting bouyon songs on his YouTube channel since 2026, credited as Kubuli on streaming platforms; his pages do not say which island he is from.

Which riddim is Hennessy on?

Wetty Beatz's Link Up Riddim, according to the title of the August 22, 2026 video and the Wetty Beatz credit on the DistroKid release.

Is Hennessy on the Link Up Riddim EP?

No. The EP released on May 25, 2026 has six tracks without Hennessy, which Kubu posted three months later.

What are his other bouyon songs?

I Love You (September 2026) and Red Flag with Slaaky, released on Flybeu record in May 2025 and posted on his channel in August 2026.

Was the Hennessy video filmed?

No, its description says it was made with artificial intelligence; it came out on October 16, 2026.
