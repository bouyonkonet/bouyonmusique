Claudette Peters: Antigua's soca queen sings Play De Bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Claudette Peters, known as CP, was born on August 9, 1979 in St. John's, Antigua. A soca and soul singer billed as Antigua's Soca Diva and Queen of Soca, and the lead vocalist of the Taxik Band, she holds ten Soca Monarch titles according to the Antigua Observer. In May 2024 she released “Play De Bouyon” on Brukway Production: an Antigua soca institution on a track named after the genre born in Dominica.

From church to the Taxik Band

The documented path starts in church: Claudette Peters was singing by the age of nine, passed through the Anglican Cathedral Youth Choir, then sang in hotels before becoming the female lead voice of several Antiguan bands, Ken Phoenix and Company, Dread & the Baldhead, High Intensity and Da Bhann. In 1996 she won Antigua's Miss Teenage Pageant. It was with the Taxik Band, which she joined as lead vocalist, that her name took hold.

In 2005, according to the Antiguan biography consulted for this profile, she became the first woman to win Antigua's Soca Monarch competition, with “All I Know”. Jumpy and Groovy Party Monarch crowns followed in number, and the country honoured her with the Order of the Most Princely Heritage. The Antigua Observer, which counts her as a ten-time Soca Monarch champion, now shows her stepping back from competition to mentor younger artists and prepare a music foundation.

Play De Bouyon, a bouyon title for a soca voice

“Play De Bouyon” was released in May 2024 on Brukway Production. The single's Deezer listing gives it at 3 minutes 24, written by Eric Magloire, Thalia King and Wayne Benjamin, composed by Eric Magloire and Garfield Whaul. The title states the programme: play bouyon. For a singer whose whole career was built in soca, it is a documented step sideways, at a moment when Dominica's bouyon is circulating across the English-speaking and French-speaking Caribbean.

An Antiguan biography also notes Dominican heritage for the Antigua-born singer: if that family link holds, the single reads as a reach back toward the sound of the island across the water. This site presents the fact as that biography gives it, without turning it into a certainty. For the wider traffic between the two genres, see bouyon vs soca.

A discography that runs to 2026

Claudette Peters' Deezer artist page lists 27 releases, from “Nasty” (2015) to the 2026 singles “Trending”, “Pan Man Play” and “Star On D Road”. In between sit the carnival titles (“Its Carnival”, 2024), the groovy cuts (“Need It”, 2022) and an open soul and R&B side, such as “Claudette CP Peters - IDC (SELF LOVE)” (2024). “Play De Bouyon” remains, in the opened sources, her only title named after the genre.

This profile is written from the encyclopedia, the Antiguan press and the platform listings opened on October 27, 2026. It will be extended if the singer releases more bouyon titles or if an interview documents the Antigua-Dominica link her biography mentions.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Claudette Peters?

A soca and soul singer born on August 9, 1979 in St. John's, Antigua, known as CP, lead vocalist of the Taxik Band, and a ten-time Antigua Soca Monarch champion according to the Antigua Observer.

What is her bouyon track?

“Play De Bouyon”, a single released in May 2024 on Brukway Production, written by Eric Magloire, Thalia King and Wayne Benjamin, composed by Eric Magloire and Garfield Whaul.

Does Claudette Peters have a link to Dominica?

An Antiguan biography consulted for this profile notes Dominican heritage. The fact is presented here as that source gives it.

Is Claudette Peters still active?

Yes: her Deezer page lists singles up to 2026, and the Antigua Observer shows her preparing a music foundation and a mentoring role.

Why is she on a bouyon site?

Because a major Caribbean soca singer released a single titled “Play De Bouyon”, documented here with its full credits.
