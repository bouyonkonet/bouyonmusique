Dominica bouyon music: Flavours of the World 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 31, 2026

On Sunday 4 October 2026, the forecourt of Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau hosted the third annual Flavours of the World festival, from 2 p.m. to 9 p.m., per Writeups 24. The official Discover Dominica page presents the island as the melting pot of the Caribbean: Creole classics, Kalinago traditions, curry, roti and flavours from around the globe, with live music, dance and cultural performances.

Born in 2024, back in 2025 and 2026

Writeups 24 recalls the festival's short history: first held in 2024, returned in 2025, then this third edition in 2026. The first brought together the traditions and cuisines of China, Haiti, India, Lebanon, Venezuela, Cuba, Africa and Dominica.

The spirit stayed the same in 2026: cuisines and traditions from several communities sharing the table alongside Dominican cuisine, in a cultural exchange event at the start of the Creole season.

Heritage fashion and a chefs competition

Among the 2026 additions, Writeups 24 names Flavours of Fashion: Wear Your Heritage, a cultural fashion show where attendees were expected to wear clothes showcasing their culture, heritage or nationality.

The Archipelago Chefs Competition held the other headline slot: a live cooking competition with demonstrations, innovative plate presentation and international tastes. A dedicated Kids Zone rounded out the programme, with a bouncy castle, face painting, basketball, bean toss, sand art and games.

The music of the festival

Live entertainment was part of the event: the official lineup reported by Writeups 24 brought together Ophelia Marie, Midnight Groovers, Extasy Band, Swinging Stars, Daryl Bobb, Sye and Pan in Harmony, and other performers.

That bill ties the festival to the island's musical history: Ophelia Marie and Midnight Groovers carry cadence-lypso, while Swinging Stars and Extasy Band belong to the generation of bands that kept Dominica dancing. A food festival that ends, as Roseau events often do, in concert.

The 2026 ticket prices

Writeups 24 gave the edition's prices: a VIP Dining Experience for EC$250, with a separate entrance, a welcome drink, wine and local juices, buffet style international dining and dedicated table service.

For general admission, adults paid EC$60 in advance and EC$75 at the gate, children aged 5 to 12 paid EC$20, students aged 13 to 18 paid EC$35, with an early bird adult ticket at EC$50. Tickets were sold at Depex Colour Lab and Bullseye Pharmacy, and online at 767Events.com.
