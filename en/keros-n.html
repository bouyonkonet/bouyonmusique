Keros-N: Plis ki zanmi, Meritey, Ukulélé, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Keros-N, also written Keros'n, has sung since the early 2010s around the Jutsu label. His biggest hit, “Plis ki zanmi” with Larose, is past six million views. On the bouyon side he is on “Hot Pussy” in 2011, on “Meritey” with Nicy in 2023, and on “Ukulélé”, his 2025 video tagged bouyon.

With Nicy and Miky Ding La

In 2011 he sang “Hot Pussy” with Miky, Nicy and Babou, a song that went around as jump up 2011, one of the names bouyon goes by. One copy is past 92,000 views. The group has stuck together since: it comes back on the “Traka” remix, on “Pliss Money” and on “Quarantenaire”, out July 22, 2026.

On March 24, 2023, Nicy's channel posted “Meritey”, with Miky Ding La, Archy and Keros-N, produced by DjBeaw and tagged bouyon. The video is past 66,000 views. Our Miky Ding La page also lists “Kichta”, a song the two share.

Plis ki zanmi and the big numbers

In September 2013 came “Plis ki zanmi” with Larose. Deezer dates the single to September 23, 2013, and the video is past 6.9 million views according to the platform listings. The remix, “Byen pliss ki zanmi”, is also past a million. The album “Sky Is the Limit” followed on December 16, 2013.

His other big numbers are solo songs: “On son a Gyal” as a freestyle, past 2.3 million, and “Gyalis”, past 1.3 million. He also shares “Freestyle No. 3” with Kevni and “Ou Pé” with Goldn.B.

Ukulélé and Grand Cru

On August 13, 2025 he posted the video for “Ukulélé”, from the album “Grand Cru”. He wrote the lyrics, the music is by Yungspliif and Little micke, and production is credited to 97115 Records and Jutsu Media Group. The video is tagged bouyon and is past 114,000 views.

In 2026 he keeps a foot in each sound. “WYFL Riddim”, posted in February by a shatta channel, is past 218,000 views, and the album “Shatta Hard” came out on July 10. “A perpète” followed in October. Our shatta bouyon page explains how the two genres cross.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Keros-N?

A singer from the Jutsu label camp, also written Keros'n, active since the early 2010s across bouyon, dancehall and shatta.

What is his biggest hit?

Plis ki zanmi with Larose, released in September 2013, past 6.9 million views according to the platform listings.

What are his bouyon songs?

Hot Pussy with Miky, Nicy and Babou in 2011, Meritey with Nicy, Miky Ding La and Archy in 2023, and Ukulélé, tagged bouyon, in 2025.

Who does he often sing with?

Nicy and Miky Ding La, from Hot Pussy in 2011 to Quarantenaire in 2026, and Larose on Plis ki zanmi.

Where is Keros-N from?

The pages consulted do not say. His productions are credited to 97115 Records and Jutsu, and he sings with Guadeloupe artists such as Larose.
