MV On De Keys: the keyboard behind DJ MJ's bouyon lives

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

MV On De Keys, also written MV On The Keys and billed as Mr MV, is the keyboardist of DJ MJ's bouyon lives. His name sits on “Who Can Party Like St Joe?” and “The Kingshill Free Up”, two titles billed Bouyon 2026, on the 2025 “Trippy Solo” live and on the World Creole Music Festival 2026 stage. On October 26, 2026, the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster at The Realm, Roseau, names him “Mr MV on the keys”.

The DJ MJ and MV duo: the keyboard at the centre of the live

The YouTube page of “Who Can Party Like St Joe? (Bouyon 2026)”, uploaded on February 5, 2026 by DJ MJ OFFICIAL, states the roles: performed by DJMJ, “Keys by: Mv On De Keys”, mixed and mastered by Tha Wizzard, riddim by DJ Shill. The single reached Deezer on February 1, 2026 on the DJMJ ANYWAY label, 2 minutes 45. Two days later, on February 7, “The Kingshill Free Up (Bouyon 2026)” followed on the same channel.

On Deezer, “The Kingshill Freeup (feat. MV On De Keys)” is an EP dated February 8, 2026, credited to DjMj and Mv On De Keys, label DJMJ ANYWAY. The auto-generated YouTube page for that release specifies “Piano: MV ON DE KEYS” and names Anyway Records as the production company: the keyboardist is credited as an artist in his own right, not as a session musician. That is why DJ MJ and he are presented as a duo, “DJMJ × MV”, on every page opened for this profile.

Bouyon lives counted in hundreds of thousands of views

The duo's format is the filmed live: one DJ, one keyboard, one bouyon riddim. “DJMJ × MV Trippy Solo Live (Bouyon Riddim 2025)”, uploaded on July 24, 2025, was past 755,000 views when read. “Nouveau Vitesse (The Carnival 2026 Bounce)”, published on December 23, 2025 on a riddim by Jackie Beats with artwork by Mv Designs767, was near 377,000. “The Lot Live (Bouyon 2026)”, from January 17, 2026, was close to 149,000 views, and “The Kingshill Free Up” above 443,000.

Those figures, read on the YouTube pages on October 27, 2026, place the duo among the most watched bouyon lives of the 2026 season. The titles name places, St Joe and Kingshill, and DJ MJ's pages show a +1 767 booking number, Dominica's dialling code. This profile draws no origin from those clues: it records that the duo works in the Dominican bouyon circuit and points to our page on bouyon in Dominica.

WCMF 2026 and the WCK 40 bill

On October 24, 2026, the Miracle J - The Illusionist channel published “DJ MJ x MV On The Keys live performance at WCMF 2026”, a filmed excerpt of their set at the World Creole Music Festival in Roseau. Two days later, on October 26, 2026, the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser, a Jazz In The City night at The Realm, 80 King George V Street, Roseau, billed “Mr MV on the keys” alongside WCK, Delly, Skinny Banton, Carlyn XP, Shelly, Benji, DJ Snow and DJ Sleem.

The keyboardist of the 2025 and 2026 lives therefore stands on the stage that opens the 40th year of the band that created bouyon. See the profiles of WCK and Skinny Banton. This profile is written from the YouTube, Deezer and Instagram pages opened on October 27, 2026; it will be extended if MV On De Keys releases titles under his own name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is MV On De Keys?

The keyboardist of DJ MJ's bouyon lives, credited “Keys by: Mv On De Keys” on Who Can Party Like St Joe? and as an artist on the EP The Kingshill Freeup (DJMJ ANYWAY, February 8, 2026). He is also billed as Mr MV or MV On The Keys.

What are his bouyon titles?

Who Can Party Like St Joe? (Bouyon 2026), The Kingshill Free Up (Bouyon 2026), The Lot Live (Bouyon 2026), Trippy Solo Live (Bouyon Riddim 2025) and Nouveau Vitesse (The Carnival 2026 Bounce), all with DJ MJ.

Did he play WCMF 2026?

Yes: an excerpt titled “DJ MJ x MV On The Keys live performance at WCMF 2026” was published on October 24, 2026 by the Miracle J - The Illusionist channel.

Is he linked to WCK's 40th anniversary?

The WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster, a Jazz In The City night at The Realm, Roseau, on October 26, 2026, names him “Mr MV on the keys”.

Where is MV On De Keys from?

No opened page says. The titles name St Joe and Kingshill and DJ MJ's pages carry a +1 767 number; this profile places him in the Dominican circuit without stating an origin.
