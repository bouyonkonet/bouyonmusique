Reo: the Prince of Bouyon

Reo is one of the faces of Dominica’s new bouyon generation. Nicknamed the Prince of Bouyon and nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, he pushes the genre toward the French Antilles without ever taking his eyes off his island.

Joli BB, the bridge with Martinique

His track Joli BB, with the Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, illustrates the bouyon of 2026: a Dominican production, a Martinican collaboration, an audience shared between Roseau and Fort-de-France. Bouyon Shattaz published the track as soon as it came out and follows Reo as one of the artists at the crossroads of the two bouyon worlds.

The next generation of Dominican bouyon

Reo belongs to the generation that has to turn the legacy of WCK into an international career: its own distribution, a polished image, collaborations in the French Antilles. That is exactly what is at stake in the network Bouyon Shattaz is building.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the key bouyon artists?

WCK created the genre, Asa Bantan is widely called the King of Bouyon, and the new generation includes Reo, Ridge, Signal Band and Shelly, while producers like Smokiller export the sound.

Who publishes these artist pages?

BouyonMusique.com is published by Bouyon Shattaz, the platform of Dominican-born producer Smokiller, with facts verified artist by artist.
