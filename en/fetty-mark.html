Fetty Mark: Bacchanal, Chofé Time, bouyon from Dominica

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Fetty Mark is a bouyon singer, writer and producer whose release pages tie the music to Dominica themselves: the description of “Ale lave bouda w”, posted on his own channel in October 2026, presents the title as a bouyon song from the Commonwealth of Dominica performed by Fetty Mark, and a 2019 page already names him “Fetty Mark (Dominica)”. Nine primary pages opened during this research cover seven years of releases, from “Bacchanal” in 2019 to a freestyle on the Maypwee Riddim in October 2026, with a writer credit to Markly John on two of them. No birthplace or birth date is given.

From Bacchanal to Chofé Time

The oldest page opened is the lyric video for “Bacchanal”, posted on October 2, 2019 by the promotion channel JulianspromosTV under the label bouyon 2020. Fetty Mark wrote it, Tewa Beatz, credited as Marvin Jno.Baptiste, produced it, and Yw3Ent, also known as Diyoute, mixed and mastered it. The description presents the track as a song about the way people party in Dominica, and places it in the genre history summed up in our page on bouyon in Dominica.

On February 13, 2023, his own channel posted “Chofé Time”, the radio version of a title shared with Dondada and billed as bouyon 2023. Production goes to Tewa Beatz and Fetty Mark himself, writing to Markly John and Josiah Cadeau, and mixing to Yw3 Ent. The distribution link names this version “Kokay Time”, and the description points to a bouyon album by the artist without dating it.

2025: Rum, the Kassav tribute, and Dominique

In October 2025, two titles show an artist who also produces for himself. “Rum”, with Dondada, posted on October 1, 2025 on the channel of DJ Taffy, carries the label Kassav Bouyon: the description explains the aim of saluting the late Jacob Desvarieux of Kassav by blending his style with bouyon. Fetty Mark produces, writes and sings with Dondada, backed by live players, Mr. Keyz on keyboards, Natty Bill on bass and Untamed Productionz on drums.

On October 14, 2025, with the independence season flagged in his hashtags, he released “Dominique”, which he calls cultural bouyon. He produced it with Mr Keyz as co producer, live drums by Untamed Productions and overall production by Eye Opener Studios, the studio that comes back on most of his recent releases.

2026: You Baby, Another Night and the Bing Boong Riddim

2026 is the busiest year. “You Baby”, a lyric video of January 6, is presented as a bouyon love song for the ladies, written, sung and produced by Fetty Mark, with Mr. Keyz on keyboards. “Another Night” came out on January 28 under the name of DJ Flip Tha Boss with Fetty Mark as guest, on Flipside Recordz; the DjeasyPromos promotion page files it as bouyon 2026, with production by DJ Flip Tha Boss and writing by Markly John.

In October 2026, two titles from his own channel close the file: “Ale lave bouda w”, bouyon 2026 on the Bing Boong Riddim, produced by Riddim Beast, the studio name of DJ Taffy, and mixed by Eye Opener Studios, then a freestyle on the Maypwee Riddim, described as an easy freestyle on a bouyon beat. To follow releases like these, see our new bouyon in 2026 coverage.

What the file establishes, and what it leaves open

The file establishes three things: continuous activity from 2019 to 2026, a double role as performer and producer, and a stated tie to Dominica on his own pages. It leaves the rest open: no opened page gives a birthplace or a birth date, no biography or interview was found, and the name Markly John appears only as a writer credit, never presented as his legal name. This profile quotes that credit as it stands.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Fetty Mark?

A bouyon singer, writer and producer active since at least 2019, whose release pages connect the music to Dominica. He produces several of his own titles, often with Mr. Keyz and Eye Opener Studios.

Is Fetty Mark from Dominica?

His own page for “Ale lave bouda w” presents the title as a bouyon song from the Commonwealth of Dominica performed by him, and a 2019 page writes “Fetty Mark (Dominica)”. This site repeats that origin as stated, without a precise birthplace.

Which of his bouyon titles are verified?

“Bacchanal” (bouyon 2020), “Chofé Time” with Dondada (bouyon 2023), “Rum” with Dondada (Kassav Bouyon), “Dominique” (cultural bouyon), “You Baby”, “Another Night” with DJ Flip Tha Boss (bouyon 2026), “Ale lave bouda w” (bouyon 2026) and a freestyle on the Maypwee Riddim.

What is Rum, with Dondada?

A 2025 title conceived as a tribute to Jacob Desvarieux of Kassav, borrowing his style to blend it with bouyon, according to the published description.

Who is Markly John?

The name credited as writer on “Chofé Time” (with Josiah Cadeau) and on “Another Night”. No opened source states the exact link with the artist name Fetty Mark.
