Kentin FcN: a DJ producer between shatta, bouyon and club

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Kentin FcN is a DJ and producer whose tie to bouyon is written into one release: “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)”, with Oso J, released on 24 July 2025 on Stars Prod. The rest of his public work is made of club singles filed as dance and dozens of mixes, including several bouyon mixes and a shatta bouyon live set recorded at the Shatta Wine festival in 2026. Chupa Chups (Bouyon): the release that ties him to the genre

“CHUPA CHUPS”, signed Kentin FcN and Oso J, came out on 12 June 2025 on Stars Prod. Six weeks later, on 24 July, came “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)”, same duo, same label, one minute 43, with the genre in the title. The day before, Kentin FcN posted on his channel the video of what he calls his bouyon remix of the track.

Releasing a bouyon version of an existing track is a DJ producer reflex: the song is reworked for the dancefloor, on a bouyon tempo and rhythm, and published as a release of its own. It is the same move as the remixes and edits followed by our bouyon remix page.

The mixes: Shatta Wine 2026 and the Bouyon Mix series

His YouTube channel is above all a mix channel. On 4 February 2026 he posted a 48 minute live set recorded at the Shatta Wine festival 2026, titled shatta bouyon, where he slips in his own tracks, “Chupa Chups” with Oso J, “Yup” with Buckstar and “18” with Barlem B, among “Bouyon Faut Koker” by Taliixobeatz and tracks by Theodora and Meryl.

In June and July 2026 came two mixes of about twelve minutes titled Bouyon Mix 2026, built from bouyon edits and remixes by other DJs and tagged bouyon and bouyonriddim. These mixes are not his recordings and are not listed in this page’s data, but they show where he plays the genre: in clubs, in remix form, next to shatta. Our bouyon mix page follows that format.

Catalogue, origin and limits

On Deezer his catalogue counts ten releases since “Bouger Bouger” in September 2023: “Pum Pum” and “ALL AROUND” in 2024 with Buckstar, “Junpad”, “18” with Barlem B in 2025, “Maya (Remix)” for P.L.L, the two “CHUPA CHUPS”, “Yup”, then “CLAP CLAP” with Mattkilla in June 2026. Deezer files most of them as dance, and only “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” carries the genre in its title.

No source opened says where Kentin FcN is from or where he is based. The only marker is a hashtag he displays under the “CLAP CLAP” video, frenchdj, reported here as a self description and not as an origin. Our bouyon in France page gives the context of the Antillean party DJ scene in mainland France.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kentin FcN?

A DJ and producer who released “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” with Oso J on 24 July 2025 on Stars Prod, and who posts shatta and bouyon mixes, including a live set recorded at the Shatta Wine festival 2026.

Where is Kentin FcN from?

It is not documented in the sources opened. He displays the frenchdj hashtag under one of his videos, which this profile reports without drawing a town or region from it.

Is Kentin FcN a bouyon artist?

In part. One release carries bouyon in its title, “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)”, and several of his 2026 mixes are bouyon mixes. The rest of his catalogue is filed as dance by Deezer.

Where can I hear Kentin FcN?

Start with “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” with Oso J, then his Shatta Wine 2026 shatta bouyon live set on his channel. Our bouyon playlist takes the listening further.
