Imran Nerdy: Start D Party, bouyon, Saint Lucia

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Imran Nerdy posts three titles billed bouyon on his YouTube channel between 2024 and 2026. The most watched, “Start D Party”, is presented on its page as an EDM and bouyon fusion made for Saint Lucia Carnival 2024, and has passed 234,000 views. “Cool Down”, the same year, sits on the Jeremie Street Riddim from Vibez Productionz, and “Feelings”, in 2026, takes the genre into a song about tired love. The pages tie these releases to Saint Lucia through their descriptions and tags; none gives a birthplace, and this profile keeps to what they say.

2024: two titles for carnival

“Start D Party”, posted on March 15, 2024, runs 2 minutes 20 and carries “2024 Bouyon” in its title. The page describes it as an EDM and bouyon fusion aimed at Saint Lucia Carnival 2024, produced by Vibez Production, mixed and mastered by Yannik Plante, with vocal production by Rapid Entertainment. The tags add Saint Lucia Soca 2024 and 758 soca, the island’s phone code. With more than 234,000 views when opened, it is the title that carries the channel.

“Cool Down”, posted on May 21, 2024 (2 minutes 36), is billed Bouyon 2024 and belongs to the Jeremie Street Riddim presented by Vibez Productionz. The page lists the other voices on the riddim, DYP, Sedale with Mata and Bronxx, Jiggy and Orion, and credits Zack Popo on guitars, Dean Benn on bass and Yannick Plante on the mix. The riddim principle, one instrumental for several artists, is explained on our bouyon riddim page.

2026: Feelings

“Feelings”, posted on April 16, 2026 (3 minutes 50), is billed Bouyon 2026 and had passed 53,000 views when opened. Its page presents it as a song about a worn relationship where love survives the weariness, carried by a bouyon rhythm. The tags repeat Saint Lucia, Rapid Entertainment and several variants of bouyon 2026.

The move from a party title to a heart song shows bouyon serving several moods here. The same shift appears with other artists from the neighbouring islands, some of whom are covered on our page on bouyon in Saint Lucia.

What the pages say, and no more

The link between Imran Nerdy and Saint Lucia comes from his own pages: the description of “Start D Party”, aimed at Saint Lucia Carnival 2024, and tags such as Saint Lucia and 758 soca. This site reports that frame as it stands. No page gives a birthplace, a legal name or a biography, and this profile invents none.

The other uploads on the channel are not billed bouyon and are not treated as such here. If Imran Nerdy releases further bouyon titles, this page will be revised and dated, for example in our tracking of new bouyon in 2026.
