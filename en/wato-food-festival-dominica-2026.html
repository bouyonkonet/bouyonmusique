Dominica food festival: WATO 2026 Ancestors edition

By Smokiller · Published October 30, 2026

It is tomorrow. This Saturday 31 October 2026, the WATO Food & Culture Festival returns to Rosalie Bay Resort & Spa, in Rosalie, for its 12th edition, named ANCESTORS. From 12 PM to 8 PM, Chef Nyanda's festival turns outdoor cooking into a celebration of Dominica's food, music and traditions.

An outdoor cooking experience, by Chef Nyanda

WATO presents itself as an outdoor cooking experience created by Chef Nyanda, and the official ticket page on 767 Events describes a programme of authentic cuisine, live cooking, music, storytelling, cultural traditions and cooking competitions.

The setting changes this year: after the 2025 edition, the festival moves to Rosalie Bay Resort & Spa, on the island's east coast. Tickets are on sale at 767events.com/wato-2026, the official link shown by the festival's account.

ANCESTORS: from the low fires of yesterday to fires lit high

The theme of the 12th edition holds in one image the festival repeats across its posts: the ancestors kept their fires low so they would not be detected, and this generation lights its fires high so the world can know it is here.

The festival's ancestors film tells the chain: the Kalinago name of the island, Waitukubuli, the wato, the fire, bitter cassava turned into bread, smoked fish, the canari still in use today, then the Neg Mawon who escaped into the mountains and kept cooking. Cooking is presented there as a way of saying: we are still here.

Chefs from across the Caribbean around the fire

The festival introduced its guest chefs one by one on its account: Chef Vita, from Jamaica, for plant-based cooking; Chef Alaric A. Arache, from St. Kitts and Nevis, formerly of Park Hyatt St. Kitts; Chef Shaun Alexander, from Trinidad and Tobago, co-founder of Foodie Nation.

Dominica holds its own place at the table: Chef Brian, head chef of Saltwood Bistro at InterContinental Dominica Cabrits Resort & Spa, and Travis Daly, a young Dominican pastry talent, introduced on the very eve of the festival.

Where bouyon meets the cooking

The festival's own account says it in one of its posts: at WATO, bouyon plays while bouyon cooks, with a big up to Marceline Renault and WCK. The festival lands the day after Jounen Kweyol and the day before National Day, at the heart of the Independence season's final weekend.

Bouyon Shattaz carries the date as an Instagram video: the festival's soundtrack and the images of its fires announce this 12th edition, so Dominica's table also reads as a stage.

Frequently asked questions

When is the WATO Festival 2026 in Dominica?

On Saturday 31 October 2026, from 12 PM to 8 PM, at Rosalie Bay Resort & Spa, in Rosalie. It is the festival's 12th edition, named ANCESTORS.

Who organises the WATO Food & Culture Festival?

WATO is an outdoor cooking experience created by Chef Nyanda. The festival brings together chefs from Dominica and across the Caribbean around open-fire cooking, outdoors.

Where do I buy WATO 2026 tickets?

At 767events.com/wato-2026, the official link shown in the @wato_dominica bio.
