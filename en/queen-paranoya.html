Queen Paranoya

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Queen Paranoya enters this site through a single title, and that title already says what it is about: “Ba yo bouyon”, released on May 6, 2022 on What time is hit, running 3 minutes 15, shared with Guérisson, credited as the second main artist. The track is online on Deezer and on YouTube. Her artist page counts only one other release, “Owgriish”, in January 2023. No biography and no origin appear in the opened sources: this profile stays short, in line with the file.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

Ba yo bouyon, a title for two voices

“Ba yo bouyon” is the first of the two releases listed on the Queen Paranoya Deezer artist page. It came out on May 6, 2022 on What time is hit, as a single, and the Deezer listing credits two main artists on equal footing: Queen Paranoya and Guérisson. The YouTube version, posted on an automatic Topic style channel and distributed by iMusician Digital AG, carries the same two names and the same line “℗ What time is hit”. It showed 2,193 views at the time of the check.

The title is in Creole, and “ba yo bouyon” reads literally as an invitation to give them bouyon. The title itself places the track in the genre, which is enough to bring it onto this site. Deezer, for its part, files the release under the Reggae tag, a platform category that neither contradicts nor clarifies anything.

Guérisson, the second name

Guérisson appears on both opened pages as a co main artist, not as a mere guest. No other release under that name was found on Deezer during this research. A YouTube channel with a similar name came up in the results, but nothing on the opened pages establishes that it is the same person, so this profile does not connect it to Guérisson.

Queen Paranoya’s second release, “Owgriish”, dates from January 6, 2023. Its listing carries no bouyon mention, and it does not enter the structured data of this page.

The limits of the file

One title, two names, one label, one date: that is the whole verifiable file as of October 23, 2026. No opened page names an island, a country or a town for Queen Paranoya or for Guérisson, and this site deduces none from the language of the title. Creole is spoken across several territories, from Dominica to Guadeloupe, and does not point to an address.

To hear the genre beyond this title, our bouyon playlist gathers tracks by documented artists. If Queen Paranoya releases new titles or a biography, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Queen Paranoya?

Queen Paranoya is the artist of the single “Ba yo bouyon”, released on May 6, 2022 on What time is hit with Guérisson. Her Deezer page lists two releases. No public biography was found during this research.

What is Queen Paranoya’s bouyon title?

“Ba yo bouyon”, 3 minutes 15, released on May 6, 2022 on What time is hit, available on Deezer and on YouTube. It is the only title on this page entered in the structured data.

Who is Guérisson?

Guérisson is credited as the second main artist of “Ba yo bouyon” on Deezer and on YouTube. The opened sources give no other information about him, and this site links him to no other channel or page without proof.

Where is Queen Paranoya from?

Her origin is named in no opened source. The title is in Creole, but a language is not a stated origin: no island is assigned to her.

Has Queen Paranoya released other titles?

Only one other release appears on her Deezer page: “Owgriish”, on January 6, 2023. Its listing states no bouyon tie.
