Kaution Band: the Antigua band and its bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Kaution Band, also billed as Kaution Band Int., is a band from Antigua that leans first toward soca. Its most present voice is Kid Fresh, real name Keontae Willock, who writes and produces much of the material. Since 2024 the band has regularly crossed over into Dominica's bouyon.

From Antigua to Dominica

On April 12, 2024, the band posted “Freak!”, billed Bouyon 2024, with Nice. The description sums up the idea in one line: “We going straight from Antigua to Dominica”. Richie and Nice sing, Danzel Dangleben, known as Megabeats, produces, and Roghae Emmanuel recorded live drums at Cadence Studio.

On August 30, 2025, the band posted “Mind Your Business”, fronted by Kid Fresh, subtitled “Trap Riddim” and billed Bouyon 2025. He wrote and produced it, with Kendel Laurant on drums and Deron Samuel on bass. The band has also posted a bouyon take on “Feliz Navidad”, titled Kaution Christmas.

Frenemy, with Shelly and Signal Band

On May 8, 2026, the video for “Frenemy” went up on the band's channel, co-posted with Shelly and Signal Band, tagged Bouyon 2026. The lyrics are by Keontae Willock and Sheldon Alfred, Shelly's legal name. Kid Fresh produced it, Dernel “Dernz” Green added production and Deron Samuel played bass.

The video was shot by Cinematize268 and Kiron21 production, directed by Kiron O'garro. It has passed 11,000 views. Deezer dates the single May 7, 2026, a day before the video.

A soca base

Bouyon remains a side road. Most of the catalog is billed soca, and that is where the big numbers sit: “Top A Da Mawnin Jam”, with Empress, has passed 160,000 views, and “De Man”, from the Top Of Da Morning Album of April 2025, is close to 150,000.

In April 2026 the band released the EP “Proceed With Kaution”, led in views by “Bass In Yah Waist” at over 55,000. It also appears on “Wiggle Jiggle”, a 2026 Antigua carnival song with Dj Quest, Empress, Soca Villan and Island Prince.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kaution Band?

A soca band from Antigua, also called Kaution Band Int., whose lead voice is Kid Fresh, Keontae Willock, and which also releases bouyon songs.

What are its bouyon songs?

Freak with Nice (Bouyon 2024), Kid Fresh's Mind Your Business (Bouyon 2025), Frenemy with Shelly and Signal Band (Bouyon 2026) and Kaution Christmas.

Who produced Frenemy?

Kid Fresh, with additional production by Dernel “Dernz” Green; the lyrics are by Keontae Willock and Sheldon Alfred, and Dj Riddim Master mixed it.

Where is the band from?

Antigua: the Freak description presents the song as a trip from Antigua to Dominica.

What is its most watched song?

Top A Da Mawnin Jam, a 2024 soca song with Empress, at over 160,000 views.
