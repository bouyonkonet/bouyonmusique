Oso J: between shatta and bouyon, from ASF to La File Indienne

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Oso J is an artist who stands on the border between shatta and bouyon, and says so himself. His video for “ASF (Antillais Sous Fidélité)”, posted in February 2025 and viewed more than 300,000 times, carries both shatta and bouyon among its description tags, and “JE M” is presented on his channel as a shatta plus bouyon track for the summer. In July 2025, the DJ and producer Kentin FcN released “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” with him, the bouyon version of their joint track. This profile follows that catalogue of ten releases and separates, track by track, what belongs to bouyon and what is presented as shatta.

Who Oso J is: what his own pages say

Oso J publishes on his YouTube channel, Oso J OFF, and under the Zwing prod. and later Zwinghouse imprints for most of his singles. His descriptions detail the same workshop from one track to the next: recording with JKR at Élite Studio, visuals by Zwing House or Jean Laforest Prod, and changing producers, Ouby_, Albrey beats, Lkn 972, Youngogbeats.

No biography and no date or place of birth were found. There are only two markers: the spoken line that opens the ASF lyrics, as he prints them in his description, ties his name to Mada, the Creole nickname for Martinique, and the producer of “BADMAN X BADGYAL” signs as Lkn 972, the Martinique code. This profile gives them for what they are, published markers, and points to our bouyon in Martinique page for the scene context.

The rest of his world is Antillean in the broad sense: his titles play with clichés about Antillean men, fidelity and monogamy, in French and Creole, in an openly comic tone. The lyrics are not reproduced here.

2024 and 2025: from BADMAN X BADGYAL to ASF and JE M

The Deezer catalogue starts on 7 December 2024 with “BADMAN X BADGYAL”, produced by Lkn 972 and released as a visualizer under the shatta and dancehall tags. “CALMA TO” followed on 2 January 2025. Neither carries any bouyon mention.

“ASF (Antillais Sous Fidélité)”, released on 5 February 2025, changed the scale. The video posted on 26 February passed 318,000 views on the day it was opened. The description credits Oso J as writer and Ouby_ as composer, the recording to JKR, and its tag line puts shatta and bouyon side by side. It is the first page where Oso J himself files a track on the bouyon side.

“JE M”, released on 3 July 2025, goes further into the hybrid. Subtitled around a character who discovers shatta, it is presented on his channel as a new shatta plus bouyon track, in two parts produced by Albrey beats and by Ouby_. It is the clearest definition of his zone: one foot in each genre, as our shatta bouyon page describes.

Chupa Chups (Bouyon) with Kentin FcN

“CHUPA CHUPS”, signed Kentin FcN and Oso J, first came out on 12 June 2025 on Stars Prod. On 24 July came “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)”, same duo, same label, one minute 43, with the word bouyon in the very name of the release. The day before, Kentin FcN had posted on his channel the video of what he calls his bouyon remix of the track.

The track then circulates in his mixes: the tracklist of his “Shatta Bouyon Mix 2026”, recorded live at the Shatta Wine festival and posted in February 2026, includes Kentin FcN x Oso J, “Chupa Chups”. It is, to date, the most clearly documented Oso J bouyon track, because the genre is written in the name of the release itself.

2026: ASM, La File Indienne and the limits of the record

After “SAVEZ-VOUS” in September 2025 and “COUCOU LES HYPOCRITES” in December, Oso J released “ASM (Antillais Sous Monogamie)” on 19 July 2026, a direct answer to the 2025 title, presented this time as a shatta track, with an EP announced. On 8 October 2026, “La File Indienne”, with Mada4bang, Sagaya and the composer Foxii, came out on La Mescla, and its video reached his channel on 15 October under the shatta tag.

The record is therefore clear on one point: bouyon is part of what Oso J does, not all of it. Three releases claim it on their own pages, ASF, JE M and CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon), and most of the others are presented as shatta. View counts are snapshots from the day the pages were opened.

To listen, start with “ASF”, then “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” and “JE M”. Our bouyon playlist and our bouyon in France page place these tracks in the Antillean party circuit.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Oso J?

Oso J is an artist who blends shatta and bouyon. His video for “ASF (Antillais Sous Fidélité)”, posted in February 2025, passed 300,000 views and carries both the shatta and bouyon tags. He also released “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” with Kentin FcN in July 2025.

Where is Oso J from?

No published biography says. The ASF lyrics printed in his description tie his name to Mada, the Creole nickname for Martinique, and one of his producers signs as Lkn 972. These are markers, not a birth record.

Does Oso J make shatta or bouyon?

Both. ASF and JE M are presented on his channel with both genres, CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon) carries bouyon in its title, and his 2024 and 2026 releases, such as ASM and La File Indienne, are presented as shatta.

What is “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)”?

The bouyon version of the joint track by Kentin FcN and Oso J, released on 24 July 2025 on Stars Prod, six weeks after the 12 June original. Kentin FcN played it in his live mix from the Shatta Wine festival 2026.

Where can I hear Oso J?

Start with “ASF”, “CHUPA CHUPS (Bouyon)” and “JE M”. Our bouyon playlist takes the listening further.
