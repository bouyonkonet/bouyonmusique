Wawa: Coming Down, Stress Free, Signal's bouyon voice

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Wawa, credited as Anwar “Wawa” Cadette, is a bouyon voice tied to Signal Band. He sings and co-wrote “Coming Down”, released as bouyon 2020, then “Stress Free” from the album “Controversy”. He is also on “On My Way (Side Life)”, past 930,000 views. In 2025 he put out “Be Alright” with Shadow Flow on his own channel.

Coming Down, the first credit sheet

On January 16, 2020, Signal Band's channel posted “Coming Down”, billed as bouyon 2020. The credits are precise: performed by Anwar “Wawa” Cadette, written by Anwar Cadette and Sheldon Alfred, with Giovannie “Gio” Green on bass. Dernel Green arranged and mixed it.

On Apple Music his name comes back on a run of the band's songs: “Largé Douleh” with Shelly and Beetle J, “Spray Dem” and “Carnival Shagweh” with Shelly, released as bouyon 2022. Wawa is not a one-off guest: he returns song after song, with the same writing team.

Stress Free and the Controversy album

“Stress Free” came out as a video on November 29, 2021, from Signal Band's album “Controversy”, and is past 37,000 views. Wawa leads it alone, on lyrics written with Sheldon Alfred. The chorus repeats “we doh worry”, and the first verse takes aim at rival bands calling themselves number one: “But jouvert in our hands”.

The same album holds “Love to Whine”, with Starsha. The three voices meet on “On My Way (Side Life)”, with Shelly and Starsha, by far the most watched song of this run according to the platform listings.

Be Alright, under his own name

On February 18, 2025, Wawa posted “Be Alright” on his own channel, with Shadow Flow, credited as Wayne Robinson, under the bouyon 2025 tag. Both singers wrote the lyrics. Dernel Green composed and engineered it, and Giovannie Green is still on bass: the Signal team stays around him.

The video is by GK Media Production, with backing vocals by Shernita Charles, Shanna Bernard and Shanica Bogeart. None of the pages consulted gives his age or his town. The 2025 video only carries the tag Roseau, Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Wawa?

A bouyon singer credited as Anwar “Wawa” Cadette, tied to Signal Band, who has performed and co-written the band's songs since Coming Down in 2020.

What is his most watched song?

Signal Band's On My Way (Side Life), with Shelly and Starsha, past 930,000 views according to the platform listings.

Does he write his songs?

For several, yes: Coming Down and Stress Free are written by Anwar Cadette and Sheldon Alfred, Be Alright by Wawa and Shadow Flow.

Has Wawa released songs on his own?

Yes. Be Alright, with Shadow Flow, came out on February 18, 2025 on his own channel, released as bouyon 2025.

Where is Wawa from?

The pages consulted do not say. His 2025 video carries the tag Roseau, Dominica, and he sings with Signal Band, a Dominica band.
