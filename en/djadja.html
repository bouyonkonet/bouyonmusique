DjaDja

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DjaDja enters this site through a page on his own channel: “RAFALÉY”, a visualizer posted on July 4, 2025 under the name DjaDja (Djp Family), with Favé, and labelled Bouyon 2025. The name had already travelled further thanks to “Pam Pam (ke lo ke)”, released in April 2025 with Zepek and Les p’tits du Rzt. This profile explains why it ties those two traces to the same artist, lists his 2026 titles, and states what it does not mix up: according to the opened pages, DjaDja has nothing to do with the rapper Djadja of the duo Djadja and Dinaz, or with the Aya Nakamura song “Djadja”.

RAFALÉY, the page that says bouyon

On his channel, DjaDja posted “RAFALÉY” on July 4, 2025, a 2 minute 37 visualizer featuring Favé. The video title carries two useful facts: the Djp Family tag beside the name, and the Bouyon 2025 label. The description only announces a new title and asks viewers to share it. It is little, but it is the artist’s own page placing the title in the genre, and this profile rests on it.

The same channel lists three more videos: “DISCO TIME”, whose page could not be opened during this research, “Viniw”, a June 20, 2026 video presented as a collaboration with A.K.H and Tybi Production, and “In Your What?”, an October 2, 2026 video featuring Leseb, with A.K.H, directed by .Thoughfull. None of those pages names a genre, and they stay out of the structured data.

Pam Pam (ke lo ke), and why it is tied here

“Pam Pam (ke lo ke)” was released on April 18, 2025 on the LTMG label, distributed by DistroKid, with a listing that credits Djadja, Les p’tits Rzt and Zepek. The official video, posted the same day on the Les p’tits du Rzt channel, credits AKH on production, Mighty Production and Thoughtful on filming, Thoughtful on editing and TybiProduction on mix and master. Neither page uses the word bouyon.

The link to DjaDja rests on the team: the same producer, A.K.H, the same mix studio, Tybi Production, and the same director, Thoughtful or .Thoughfull depending on the spelling, appear on the DjaDja channel videos. No page states outright that the two names are one person: it is an inference from those shared credits, and this profile presents it as one.

What stays out of the profile

A December 2025 video, “Ou vlé vwey”, posted on the MBI Family and Fremar channel, is billed DJADJA with Kima, Money Shaun and DJ MK. Its page names no genre and no team shared with the DjaDja channel: nothing allows it to be attributed to this artist, and this profile names it only as a title carrying the same name.

No opened page gives an island, a town, a legal name or an official account for DjaDja, and the Djp Family tag is explained nowhere. This site therefore assigns no origin. New releases from the scene are tracked in new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DjaDja?

An artist who posts on his own channel as DjaDja, sometimes DjaDja (Djp Family). His title stated as bouyon is RAFALÉY, with Favé, labelled Bouyon 2025.

Is he the Djadja of Pam Pam (ke lo ke)?

This site thinks so, on a specific basis: the producer A.K.H, the Tybi Production mix and the director Thoughtful appear both on Pam Pam and on the DjaDja channel videos. No page says it directly.

Is he the rapper from Djadja and Dinaz?

No. Nothing in the opened pages links DjaDja to that French rap duo, or to the Aya Nakamura song Djadja.

What are his 2026 titles?

Viniw, a June 20, 2026 video with A.K.H and Tybi Production, and In Your What?, an October 2, 2026 video with Leseb and A.K.H. Neither states a genre on its page.

Where is DjaDja from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns him no island.
