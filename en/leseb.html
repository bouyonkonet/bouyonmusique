Leseb: Cho, She Like TNB and VODY 22, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Leseb has been working with Jixels since at least 2023, and he is one of the few artists to share a duo title with him under their two names alone. Three opened pages present his titles as bouyon: “CHO”, an October 2023 video with JB and Jixels under the Team MDC banner, “SHE LIKE TNB”, a duo with Jixels labelled Bouyon 2024, and “VODY 22”, a group video from December 2025. Three 2026 collaborations complete the run. No biography and no origin were found.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

From Team MDC to a duo with Jixels

The first trace is “CHO”, posted on October 11, 2023 by Mighty Production. The video title says everything the page states: JB, Leseb and Jixels, an official video, the word bouyon and the name Team MDC, repeated in the hashtag LATEAMMDC. The page names no producer or studio, and this profile adds none.

Three months later, on January 27, 2024, the Jixels channel posted “SHE LIKE TNB”, short for She Like True Nuh Boy according to the description, with Bouyon 2024 in the title. It is a two name duo, Jixels and Leseb, with Jixels on production, at Jixels Studio’Z, on the mix and on the cover, signed Jix’Art. For Leseb it is the clearest page in the file: a title of his own with his producer, stated as bouyon.

The group videos of 2025 and 2026

“VODY 22”, posted on December 17, 2025, puts Leseb beside Le Cus, Sheyta and Uglyboy, on a Qwann production mixed by Yas Beats and directed by Mighty Production, with #BOUYON in the title. In 2026 three further titles show him with no genre statement: “ARE U FEELING”, a May video with Le Cus, Managyalis and Sheyta, produced by Dinho with Jixels on piano, “BAY LI”, by DJ Skylight and DJ Screem with Le Cus and Leseb, released on June 19 on SKYLIGHT STUDIO, and “In Your What?”, an October video by DjaDja featuring Leseb, with A.K.H.

Those unlabelled titles are named to follow the run, not to widen the bouyon file: only the three pages that state the genre enter the structured data. For listening, our bouyon playlist covers the genre more broadly.

The limits of the file

No opened page gives an island, a town, a legal name or an official account for Leseb. The Guadeloupe number printed under “SHE LIKE TNB” is the Jixels contact, and this site does not turn it into an origin for Leseb. JB and Team MDC are not described by the opened pages either. This profile will be updated if Leseb publishes a biography or an official page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Leseb?

Leseb is a bouyon voice close to Jixels, present on his videos since 2023. Three of his titles are stated as bouyon on their page: CHO, SHE LIKE TNB and VODY 22.

Which is his most personal title?

“SHE LIKE TNB”, a duo with Jixels posted on January 27, 2024 with Bouyon 2024 in the title, produced, recorded and mixed by Jixels.

What is Team MDC?

It is the name written in the title of the “CHO” video (JB, Leseb and Jixels, October 2023) and in its hashtag. The page does not describe the group, and this profile says no more.

Where is Leseb from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns him no island.

Who did he record with in 2026?

With Le Cus, Managyalis and Sheyta on ARE U FEELING, with DJ Skylight, DJ Screem and Le Cus on BAY LI, and with DjaDja and A.K.H on In Your What?.
