Daddy2Sec: Check ur Scene with DJ Taffy, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Daddy2Sec publishes two titles carrying the word bouyon on his own YouTube channel: “Check ur Scene”, an official video with DJ Taffy, labelled St.Kitts Bouyon 2026, and “Jacket”, labelled 2025 Bouyon. The first is the better documented, with writing credited to Kyle Roberts, composition to Dj Taffy and the label Netfa Records. The St Kitts label is the page’s own; no opened page says where the artist was born.

Check ur Scene, the documented title

The “Check ur Scene” video went up on December 5, 2025 and runs 2 minutes 25. Its title reads Daddy2Sec x DJTaffy with the label St.Kitts Bouyon 2026. The description credits are full: performed by Daddy2Sec, written by Kyle Roberts, composed by Dj Taffy, engineering by Chabo Productions. On the video side, direction goes to Dunga and Glow Gang Productions, shooting and editing to MLP. The label given is Netfa Records. Kyle Roberts is quoted as a writing credit, with no conclusion about the artist’s legal identity.

Dj Taffy, a Dominican producer already profiled on this site, links the title to the sound of Dominica, where bouyon was born, through the composition credit.

Jacket, and the St Kitts label

“Jacket”, uploaded June 11, 2025 (4 minutes 08), carries the label 2025 Bouyon and has no description, so this profile says nothing more about it than its title and length. Of the 35 videos the channel listed during this research, these two are the only ones carrying the word bouyon.

“St.Kitts Bouyon 2026” is a circuit label, of the same kind as the Antigua, Barbados or Saint Vincent bouyon tags in titles of the same season. It places the music on the St Kitts scene without establishing a personal origin; this site assigns Daddy2Sec no island.

The limits of the file

Two videos and a channel listing, no primary biography, no streaming platform page opened. This profile will grow if Daddy2Sec publishes more titles labelled bouyon, tracked in new bouyon in 2026, or an official presentation that names his origin.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Daddy2Sec?

An artist whose YouTube channel publishes two titles labelled bouyon, including “Check ur Scene” with DJ Taffy. No public biography was found.

Which bouyon titles are verified?

“Check ur Scene” (St.Kitts Bouyon 2026), uploaded December 5, 2025, and “Jacket” (2025 Bouyon), uploaded June 11, 2025.

Who wrote and composed Check ur Scene?

Writing is credited to Kyle Roberts and composition to Dj Taffy; engineering to Chabo Productions, the label to Netfa Records.

Is Daddy2Sec from St Kitts?

The page labels the title St.Kitts Bouyon 2026 but gives no birthplace or home base. This site therefore assigns no origin.

Why is this profile short?

Because it rests on two primary pages, one of them without a description. It will grow with future sources.
