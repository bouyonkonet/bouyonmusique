Supaman Enskred: the Bouyon Time series and his riddims

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Supaman Enskred, also credited as Supa or Supa Man, makes bouyon riddims under the Enskred Fondation name and sings on some of them. His Bouyon Time series has reached six volumes since 2023, and his most loyal partner is the singer Mihom.

Enskred, a name that goes back to 2013

The Enskred Fondation channel still holds old videos. In November 2013 it posted “Pozer Dozer”, with Supaman, Karu, Darmon and Mihom. January 2014 brought Mihom's “Voyey Ba Yo”, on a “Bouyon Time Riddim” described as “100% local Enskred”. The name of the later series was already there.

Today the same channel works as a shop window for his production. The descriptions say his instrumentals must be leased for commercial use and point to his Instagram account, supaman_enskred_. He also posts mixes of his own riddims there, such as the one that went with Bouyon Time 4 in July 2024.

Bouyon Time, six volumes

The series began on Deezer in June 2023 with a first six track EP: “Rose”, “Shine”, “Back It”, “Balance”, “Faya” and “Coco La”. Two more volumes followed before the end of the year, then “Bouyon Time 4” in July 2024 and “Bouyon Time 5” in February 2025.

“Bouyon Time Part.6” came out on November 3, 2025 on the Enskred Fondation label, with nine tracks including “Yello”, “Double face” and “In da pocket”. Two weeks later, a video mix of the volume passed 800 views. Between volumes he releases standalone riddims, such as Go Riddim, Bad Time Riddim and Dowlis Riddim.

Bouyon Bling and the Mihom songs

On February 8, 2025 he released “Bouyon Bling”, a single over five minutes long, credited to Supaman Enskred and Supa Man. On YouTube it is one of his most played tracks, with more than 750 views.

Mihom keeps coming back in his credits: “Le Vaisseau” in 2022, “Ça tue” and “Deranger” in 2023, “Pa ni dlo” with Krooger Mc in 2024. February 2026 added a batch, including “Maco”, posted with a “Bouyon” label and featuring Karu, and “Ou paté sav” with Dma Once and Bee Mc.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Supaman Enskred?

A bouyon riddim maker who releases under the Enskred Fondation name and sings on some of his tracks. He is also credited as Supa or Supa Man.

What is the Bouyon Time series?

A six volume series of bouyon riddims and songs, released between June 2023 and November 2025. The name already appears in 2014 on a Bouyon Time Riddim.

What are his best known bouyon songs?

Back It and Balance on the first Bouyon Time, Bouyon Bling in 2025 and Maco with Karu and Mihom in 2026.

Who does he work with most?

Mihom, on Le Vaisseau, Ça tue, Deranger, Pa ni dlo, Maco and Ou paté sav. Karu, Krooger Mc, Bee Mc and Dma Once also come back.

Can I use his riddims?

His videos say his instrumentals are sold under lease and must be purchased for commercial use.
