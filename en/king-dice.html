King Dice: Dominica's calypso king on bouyon riddims

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

King Dice, Dennison Joseph by name, is first of all a calypsonian from Dominica: Writeups24 credits him with ten Calypso Monarch crowns, the latest in February 2024. He keeps stepping into bouyon, though. You hear him with WCK on “You!” in 2017, with Shelly and Shadowflow on the Champion Riddim in 2019, and on Dada's Kryptic Riddim in 2025.

A calypsonian who collects crowns

In June 2011, the Vincentian paper Searchlight announced his visit to Vincy Mas and traced his early run: three crowns in a row from 2004 to 2006, with “Peace Is My Business” and “Animal Farm”, then “Help My Brothers For Me” and “Puppet Master”, then “These Are The Days” and “I Eh Fraid Of Them”. The same article credits him with five straight road marches, from “Tarzan” in 2003 to “Lights” in 2007.

The run went on. A BrBpTV video shows his 2016 crowning at Pottersville with “My Dominica Trade House Dot Com”. In March 2019, Soca News reported a ninth title, won in Roseau ahead of Bobb and Tasha P. On February 10, 2024, at the Windsor Park Sports Stadium Forecourt, he won with “Wanted” and “Bring Money”, ahead of Trilla G; Writeups24 called it a tenth crown. The counts do not always match from one source to the next, but the record is out of the ordinary either way.

His bouyon titles, from WCK to the Champion Riddim

On April 24, 2017, DjeasyPromos posted “You!”, billed “2017 Bouyon”, where WCK, the band that founded the genre, brings in King Dice and Shadow Flow; it is his most watched bouyon page, past 12,000 plays. In September 2018, Love Bouyon Music uploaded “System La”, again with Shadowflow, presented as Dominica “2019 Bouyon”.

February 2019 brought “Cya Stop De Sewo” on the Champion Riddim by R.B.M. The credits name Dennison “King Dice” Joseph next to Shelly and Shadowflow, with vocals cut at Sonic Art Studios by Dernel Green. Shelly sums up the idea in the description: what others call a fete or a party, Dominica calls sewo.

From Blame to Lévé: the recent releases

In February 2023, DjeasyPromos posted “Blame”, on the 4 D Kulture Riddim produced by KubuliBad, with bouyon in the title. On August 28, 2025, Dada's channel added “Me” to the Kryptic Riddim, after the album was out; it had over 3,300 views when read.

Since late 2025 his singles have come out on the 2266120 Records DK label: “Debouye cor nou” in November 2025, the “Heritage” EP with “La-vie-sa-la”, then “Lévé” in October 2026. That last one, produced by Clayton Hazel, was recorded at Wizdom studio in the Bronx, with Keith Goddard on bass. The Deezer page under his name mixes several namesakes, so only these titles are used here.

Frequently asked questions

Who is King Dice?

Dennison Joseph, a calypsonian from Dominica, a many-time Calypso Monarch (ten titles per Writeups24 in 2024) and a guest voice on several bouyon riddims.

What are his bouyon titles?

You! with WCK (2017), System La with Shadowflow (2018), Cya Stop De Sewo with Shelly and Shadowflow on the Champion Riddim (2019), Blame on the 4 D Kulture Riddim (2023) and Me on the Kryptic Riddim (2025).

How many times has he been Calypso Monarch?

Writeups24 reported a tenth crown in February 2024 and lists 2004, 2005, 2006, 2008, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2017, 2019 and 2024; a BrBpTV video also shows his 2016 crowning.

Who sings with him on the Champion Riddim?

Shelly (Sheldon Alfred) and Shadowflow (Wayne Robinson), on Cya Stop De Sewo, released in February 2019.

What is his latest single?

Lévé, uploaded to his channel on October 7, 2026, produced by Clayton Hazel and out on Deezer the next day.
