DJ Sleem: the yearly bouyon mixes filmed in Dominica

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

DJ Sleem is a DJ whose yearly bouyon mixes, filmed at Dominican sites, count among the most watched of the genre. “2025 Bouyon Mix”, shot at the Freshwater Lake in Laudat as carnival approached, was past 541,000 views when this profile was written; “Bouyon Mix 2024 Plus”, at the Emerald Pool, past 234,000. The series goes back to “Bouyon Nation” in 2017 and 2018 and the summer “Bouyon Bacchanal” started in 2019. On October 26, 2026, the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster at The Realm, Roseau, names him among the night's DJs.

Mixes filmed at the island's sites

“2025 Bouyon Mix | The Best 2025 Bouyon by DJ Sleem”, uploaded on January 31, 2025, opens with a statement of intent: with Dominica's carnival on the horizon, DJ Sleem takes his audience to the Freshwater Lake, in the village of Laudat, for a compilation of the 2025 bouyon titles set to dominate the road on carnival Monday and Tuesday. The mix was past 541,000 views on October 27, 2026.

The previous edition, “Bouyon Mix 2024 Plus”, published on March 30, 2024, called on “all Dominicans and lovers of Bouyon music” and showcased the Emerald Pool, one of the island's tourist sites; it was past 234,000 views. “Sizzling Hot 2024 Bouyon Mix” rounded off the summer. The method is the same each time: a mix of the genre, a Dominican location on screen, and the Instagram handle djsleemofficial. For the setting of these titles, see our page on bouyon in Dominica.

Bouyon Nation and Bouyon Bacchanal: the series since 2017

The channel keeps the trace of the beginnings. “Dj Sleem Presents: Bouyon Nation (2017 Bouyon Mixtape)” opens the series; “Bouyon Nation 2018”, uploaded on May 4, 2018, announces 45 minutes of the bouyon titles of the moment and publishes its tracklist, which starts with “DJ Sleem - Everyday We Litt ft Keks Mafia, Reo & Trilla G” and lines up Keks Mafia, DiYoute and Reo.

On June 10, 2019 came “2019 Summer Bouyon Mix”, presented as the first of a yearly summer bouyon mix dubbed #BouyonBacchanal, with Triple Kay, Asa Bantan, Keks Mafia, ColtonT and Reo, more than 161,000 views. From 2017 to 2025, DJ Sleem's mixes thus form a yearly chronicle of the titles that count in the genre.

On the WCK 40 bill

On October 26, 2026, Jazz In The City staged the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser at The Realm, 80 King George V Street, Roseau, from 7 to 11 p.m. The poster published on Instagram by @jazzinthecity767 names WCK, Delly, Skinny Banton, Carlyn XP, Shelly, Benji, Mr MV on the keys, DJ Snow and DJ Sleem. The DJ of the yearly mixes was therefore invited to the night that opens the 40th year of the band that created bouyon.

See the profiles of WCK and DJ Snow. This profile is written from the YouTube and Instagram pages opened on October 27, 2026. None spells out where DJ Sleem is from; his pages address Dominicans and film the island's sites, and this site places him in the Dominican scene without stating a birthplace.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Sleem?

A DJ whose yearly bouyon mixes, filmed at Dominican sites, count among the most viewed of the genre: 2025 Bouyon Mix (past 541,000 views) and Bouyon Mix 2024 Plus (past 234,000).

Where was the 2025 Bouyon Mix filmed?

At the Freshwater Lake, in the village of Laudat, according to the mix's page uploaded on January 31, 2025; the 2024 edition was shot at the Emerald Pool.

Since when has DJ Sleem published bouyon mixes?

Since Bouyon Nation (2017 Bouyon Mixtape), followed by Bouyon Nation 2018, then the summer Bouyon Bacchanal started on June 10, 2019.

Does DJ Sleem have a title of his own?

The Bouyon Nation 2018 tracklist opens with DJ Sleem - Everyday We Litt ft Keks Mafia, Reo & Trilla G.

Is he linked to WCK's 40th anniversary?

Yes: the WCK 40th Anniversary Teaser poster, a Jazz In The City night at The Realm, Roseau, on October 26, 2026, names him among the DJs.
