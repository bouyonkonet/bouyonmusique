DiYoute: Get High, Losing It and Your Life, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

DiYoute is a singer, writer and sound engineer whose songs come out on Young Wild & Free Entertainment and go through Yw3Ent Studios. His bouyon trail starts with covers made with Xs Groove from 2014, then runs through “Losing It” in 2021, “Get High” in 2025 and “Your Life” in 2026, credited “Bouyon Dominica”.

Bouyon covers with Xs Groove

In October 2014, the DjeasyPromos channel posted “Live Life Remix”, a cover of Alkaline's song by Xs Groove with DiYoute, filed as bouyon soca. It is close to 9,300 views. Another remix, “Drunk In Love (Bouyon Remix)”, circulates under a bouyon 2015 label with Xs Groove in the credit.

In April 2018, DiYoute posted “Perfect [Bouyon Cover]” on his own channel, with music by Xs Groove, recorded and mixed at Wizard House Studio. Same formula each time: a well known song, rebuilt on a bouyon riddim.

Young Wild & Free and Yw3Ent Studios

From 2020, his releases carry the Young Wild & Free Entertainment name. “Nasty lil Boy”, with Trilla-G, came out in April 2020, produced by Wavy Gang, around his “Dream Big” project. April 2021 brought “Losing It [Bouyon Remix]”, produced by Bryce Beats, with a video directed by ByAccident Films and mixed by Dj Flip Tha Boss.

On July 1, 2025 came “Get High”, titled “Bouyon 2025” on his channel, with Hussey, Real Reel and Trixz Migos. Savion Beats produced it, it was recorded at Yw3Ent Studios, and DiYoute also designed the cover. On Real Reel's “Tropikini”, he is credited as engineer.

Your Life, Dominica bouyon

In October 2025, Xs Groove Band released “Your Life”, titled “Bouyon 2026”, with Milla, Trilla G and DiYoute. The credits read “Bouyon Dominica” and pair Untamed Productionz with Young Wild & Free Entertainment. The song samples T.I. and Rihanna's “Live Your Life” and was recorded at Yw3Ent Studios and Showtime Empire.

On Deezer his catalogue runs to some twenty releases, from “Dream Big” in 2020 to “GR3AT3R” in 2024, then a run of singles in 2026. No opened source gives his real name, age or birthplace. This profile places him in the Dominica scene, as his credits do, without giving him a birth island.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DiYoute?

A singer, writer and sound engineer whose songs come out on Young Wild & Free Entertainment and are recorded at Yw3Ent Studios.

What are his bouyon songs?

Live Life Remix with Xs Groove (2014), Perfect [Bouyon Cover] (2018), Losing It [Bouyon Remix] (2021), Get High (2025) and Your Life with Xs Groove (2026).

Who produced Get High?

Savion Beats. The song brings together DiYoute, Hussey, Real Reel and Trixz Migos, and it was recorded at Yw3Ent Studios.

How is he linked to Xs Groove?

Xs Groove made the music for his early bouyon covers, and DiYoute sings on Your Life, released by Xs Groove Band in 2025.

Where is DiYoute from?

No opened source says. His credits place him in the Dominica bouyon scene, including the “Bouyon Dominica” line on Your Life.
