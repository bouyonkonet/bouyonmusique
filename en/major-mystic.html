Major Mystic: Dominica bouyon mixes, remixes and songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Major Mystic plays two parts in Dominica bouyon. He sings, first next to Asa Bantan in 2016 and later on Dada's Kryptic Riddim in 2025. He also mixes: since 2022 his channel has put out a bouyon selection every carnival season, and in 2026 he made the official promo mix for a World Creole Music Festival night.

From Mr Dj to Taste: the vocal tracks

The oldest trace is “Mr Dj”, a duet with Asa Bantan, posted in June 2016 by Dj CashMoney 767 under the tag “Bouyon 2016”. The description credits the riddim to Shelly and Popcan, and the video is past 53,000 views. In February 2018, Love Bouyon Music uploaded “Lolipop”, billed as Dominica “2018 Bouyon”, on the Mal Kase Riddim by Earlson Matthew, made at Sonic Art Studios in Canefield.

Seven years on, he was back on a group riddim with “Taste”, on the Kryptic Riddim by Dada, uploaded on July 4, 2025. The press text puts him among the local talent, next to Pudaz and Zwady, while the guests from elsewhere get their island next to their name. The song was written by Kellis Mitchell and Major Mystic and produced, mixed and mastered by Krishna “Dada” Lawrence.

The remixes: soca turned bouyon

His own channel is mostly remixes. In February 2022 he reworked “On My Way” by Signal Band, with Shelly, Starsha and Anwar in the tags and a download link for DJs. The remix is past 22,000 views.

A year later, in March 2023, he put out a bouyon version of “Hard Fete”, the soca song by Bunji Garlin, noting that the song and its writing belong to Bunji Garlin. The idea is plain: keep the voice everyone knows and set it on a bouyon tempo, ready for a carnival set.

Carnival mixes and WCMF 2026

The “Bouyon Carnival Mix 2022”, a quarter of an hour posted in March 2022 with the tags #Dominica, #SignalBand and #TripleKay, is his most watched video, at over 76,000 plays. The series has run every year since: “Bouyon Carnival 2023” and its “Fan Luv Edition”, the “2023 Bouyon Essentials”, “The Best of International Bouyon 2024”, then “Bouyon Carnival 2025”.

In September 2026, the Discover Dominica channel posted the official promo mix for the first night of the World Creole Music Festival, by Major Mystic. It runs close to fifty minutes and walks through the October 23, 2026 bill at Windsor Park, from Capleton and Skillibeng to Pudaz, Ridge and DJ MJ. For context, see our page on bouyon in Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Major Mystic?

An artist from Dominica who sings and mixes bouyon, presented as local talent on Dada's Kryptic Riddim in 2025.

Which songs has he sung?

“Mr Dj” with Asa Bantan (2016), “Lolipop” on the Mal Kase Riddim (2018) and “Taste” on the Kryptic Riddim (2025).

Who wrote Taste?

Kellis Mitchell and Major Mystic, according to the official description; Krishna “Dada” Lawrence produced, mixed and mastered it.

Which bouyon remixes has he made?

Signal Band's “On My Way” in 2022 and Bunji Garlin's “Hard Fete” in 2023, among others.

What is his link to WCMF?

He made the official promo mix for the October 23, 2026 night of the World Creole Music Festival, posted by Discover Dominica.
