HollyG and Je l'ai vu: the gold of bouyon gwada

By Smokiller · Published October 25, 2026

In October 2021, HollyG released Je l'ai vu with Arendi, and the track became, per the opened sources, the first bouyon sound by an Antillean artist certified gold single in France. This page tells what the credits and the certifications verify, line by line, without inflating the file.

A family trio from Guadeloupe

The Qobuz published biography presents HollyG as a family trio: Holly and her nieces, known as 6 Tresses and Cheveux Rouge, from the communes of Trois-Rivieres and Vieux-Habitants, in Basse-Terre, Guadeloupe.

Their track Bandit was released on 16 September 2021. One month later, on 22 October 2021, Je l'ai vu came out on SL Prod, distributed by Sony Music Entertainment France, in the Bouyon Kings Mixtape.

The clip credits and the mixtape credits

The official clip description credits HollyG and Arendi as writers, Dj Flip tha boss as composer, Voodoo Studio for the recording, Mighty Production for the camera work and Vadore Concept among the productions. Filming is placed at Galets Beach, at the Domaine de la petite sucrerie.

The mixtape track metadata, though, credits Gildon Gorge as composer. Both credit lines coexist in the opened sources: this page publishes both, attributed, without merging them.

The certification that made history

The Bouyon article on Wikipedia, which cites the SNEP certifications, writes that in October 2021, Je l'ai vu, performed by HollyG and Arendi, is the first bouyon sound by an Antillean artist certified gold single in France, and the second bouyon track certified after Kongolese sous BBL by Theodora in the order of published certifications.

For the Gwada scene, the reach is clear: a bouyon track sung in Creole, produced between Guadeloupe and its studios, enters the French certification system. It is a milestone in the history of bouyon gwada, documented on the bouyon gwada page of this site.

What came after

Qobuz also reports that on 16 May 2024, HollyG became the first Guadeloupean bouyon group nominated at the BET Awards. In December 2025, HollyG and Theodora released a track together, Coller la petite, linking the two names tied to the first bouyon certifications in France.

The full HollyG profile, with her verified recordings, is on her artist page on this site. This page keeps only the track and the milestone: Je l'ai vu, October 2021, gold single.

Frequently asked questions

When was Je l'ai vu by HollyG released?

Je l'ai vu was released on 22 October 2021 on SL Prod, distributed by Sony Music Entertainment France, in the Bouyon Kings Mixtape, per platform metadata.

Who are the artists on Je l'ai vu?

The track is performed by HollyG, the family trio from Trois-Rivieres and Vieux-Habitants in Guadeloupe, with Arendi. The official clip also associates VJ Ben with the title.

What certification did Je l'ai vu receive?

Per the Bouyon article on Wikipedia, which cites the SNEP, Je l'ai vu is the first bouyon sound by an Antillean artist certified gold single in France.
