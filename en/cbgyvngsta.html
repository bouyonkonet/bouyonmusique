CbgYvngsta: Suck it, bouyon, Make She Sing and Love You 2

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

CbgYvngsta, also written CBGYVNGSTA, has four titles whose pages state bouyon, from 2024 to 2026: a solo, “Suck it”, then two titles shared with Ezee WMN, “Love Me Or Not” and “Make She Sing” (the latter with Gwada G), and finally a feature on “Love You 2” by PoyZin. The name keeps returning to the same studio circle: Jackie Beatz, Shako Production, Waco Moyenga and Knockin Records. No opened page names an origin.

Suck it, the 2024 solo

The oldest verified title is “Suck it”, posted on June 21, 2024 on the channel of beatmaker Jackie Beatz as “CbgYvngsta - Suck it (Bouyon 2024) EXPLICIT”, running 2 minutes 24. The description is short: writing by CbgYvngsta, production by Jackie Beatz, live drums by a drummer credited as Drummer Frigde, spelling as published. The explicit tag in the title marks an uncensored version.

The same description ends with three hashtags: the artist’s name, the producer’s name and SXM, the usual short form for Saint Martin. This site reads that as a circuit signal, a label placed on a video, not as a stated origin. The island’s scene is covered separately on the bouyon in Saint Martin page, without CbgYvngsta being tied to it by any source.

Two 2025 titles with Ezee WMN

In 2025 CbgYvngsta released two tracks under the artist’s own reference, distributed by Amuseio AB. “Love Me Or Not”, released on July 11, 2025, is a duet with Ezee WMN: the audio posted on the Jackie Beatz channel carries “(Bouyon 2025)”, production goes to Shako Production, and the official listing credits both voices for rap and composition, with Waco Moyenga on mixing and mastering.

“Make She Sing”, released on September 12, 2025, adds Gwada G. The visualizer, posted two days later and also titled “(Bouyon 2025)”, credits Shako Production for production and keys, recording to Yeah Boss Studios and XavvoKnockin, and mixing and mastering to Waco Moyenga and Xavvoknockin. The official listing names three composers, Kurt Gwada G Douglas, Stevens Macathley Jeanfort and Eleni Constandinou, without saying which stage name each belongs to.

Love You 2 with PoyZin

The most recent title is a feature: “Love You 2”, by PoyZin featuring CBGYVNGSTA, posted on October 20, 2025 on PoyZin’s channel and titled “[Bouyon 2026]”, ahead of the next season. The track runs 2 minutes 38. The description credits performance and writing to both artists, the riddim to Jackie Beatz, recording to Knockin Records and Waco Moyenga, and mixing and mastering to Waco Moyenga.

These four titles trace a consistent path: a solo produced by Jackie Beatz, two shared titles built around Shako Production, then a collaboration on a Jackie Beatz riddim. To hear them alongside other tracks of the period, see the bouyon playlist and new bouyon in 2026. No biography or origin was found: this profile sticks to the credits.

Frequently asked questions

Who is CbgYvngsta?

CbgYvngsta is an artist name credited on four titles billed as bouyon between 2024 and 2026: Suck it, Love Me Or Not, Make She Sing and Love You 2. No public biography was found.

What is CbgYvngsta’s first verified bouyon title?

“Suck it (Bouyon 2024)”, posted on June 21, 2024 on the Jackie Beatz channel, written by CbgYvngsta and produced by Jackie Beatz.

Who has CbgYvngsta worked with?

With Ezee WMN on Love Me Or Not and Make She Sing, with Gwada G on Make She Sing, and with PoyZin on Love You 2. On the studio side, the recurring names are Jackie Beatz, Shako Production, Waco Moyenga and Xavvoknockin.

Is CbgYvngsta from Saint Martin?

No opened page says so. The SXM hashtag on the Suck it page is a signal, not a stated origin, so this site assigns no island.

Where can I hear Love Me Or Not?

The official audio is on the Jackie Beatz YouTube channel, and the listing distributed by Amuseio AB has been on the platforms since July 11, 2025.
