Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1: 41 minutes of bouyon by Smokiller

By Smokiller · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

One mix, a whole season. Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1, mixed by Smokiller for Bouyon Shattaz, lines up 41 minutes of new bouyon songs beat matched back to back, per the description of the official video on YouTube. The year around it is told in our guide to bouyon music in 2026; one of the 2026 singles on this site, Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money, was produced by Smokiller, who mixed this one.

The tracklist, chapter by chapter

The mix opens with Unstick by Fifin and Wotlis at 0:00, then the Time Skip Riddim at 1:55 and the Bouyon Shattaz anthem at 2:45. Come Ova by KTB follows at 4:40, Take Another Man by J Lion at 6:33 and the IDFWU Riddim at 8:46, per the chapters published under the video.

The second stretch lines up MadameZelle by Tipsy at 10:58, Pull D Pin by Machel Montano, Skinny Fabulous, Lil Natty and Thunda at 12:10, then three Trilla-G songs: Action at 14:21, Hennessy and Malibu at 16:33 and Take Me As I Am at 20:58, with De Other Man by Shelly, Trilla-G and Unstoppable Shax at 18:45.

From RnBouyon to La Vie Doux: the closing stretch

CAPITAL by Mical Teja and Patrice Roberts arrives at 23:11, followed by an RnBouyon instrumental at 25:23, Bend Down by Khallion and Green Apple at 26:24 and Whale Fu See by OG Park and Rum Lawd, on the Whale Oil Riddim, at 28:36, per the official tracklist.

The mix closes with EARTHQUAKE by DOVA at 30:48, Back and Forth by Slyme Hoyss at 33:00, Secret Bay by DPeng and Soca Inferno at 35:12, and La Vie Doux by Reo and Tehilah at 37:02.

A photograph of the 2026 bouyon season

The selection mixes Dominica engines, Trilla-G, Reo, Khallion and DOVA, with regional soca heavyweights who stepped into bouyon, Machel Montano, Skinny Fabulous and Patrice Roberts. The riddims hold their place, from the Time Skip Riddim to the Whale Oil Riddim, with an RnBouyon breather in the middle.

The video description says it plainly: the songs belong to their artists and labels, and the mix celebrates and promotes Dominica bouyon culture. To go further, the Bouyon Music 2026 playlist gathers the songs one by one, and the bouyon mix page presents the other mixes of the season.

Frequently asked questions

Who mixed Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1?

Smokiller, for Bouyon Shattaz, per the official video description. The mix runs 41 minutes and chains the songs beat matched, without a break.

Which artists are in Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1?

Among others Trilla-G, J Lion, Tipsy, Machel Montano, Skinny Fabulous, Lil Natty and Thunda, Shelly, Unstoppable Shax, Mical Teja, Patrice Roberts, Khallion, DOVA, Reo and Tehilah, per the tracklist published under the video.

Where can I find the mix songs one by one?

In the Bouyon Music 2026 playlist, linked from the video description, which gathers the best new bouyon songs of the season.
