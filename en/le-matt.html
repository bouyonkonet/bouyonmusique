Le Matt (with Pepito)

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Le Matt comes onto this site through a single title shared with Pepito: “Pa di mwen JTM STP”, posted as a video on the ISLANDS ROLLS channel on December 26, 2025, tagged bouyonriddim and bouyon2026. The recording is credited to DJ Leguste and the mix to Saintamuzik. Since Pepito is documented only by this same song, this page covers both voices together instead of publishing two near identical profiles. It states what the video page establishes, and nothing more.

One title, two voices

The video runs 2 minutes 20. Its description bills the pair as Le Matt X Pepito and gives a short credit list: recording by Dj LeGuste, mix by Saintamuzik, and a note that the song is out on every streaming platform. Each artist is tied to a TikTok account, le_matt55 for Le Matt and pepito9799 for Pepito. The tags bouyonriddim and bouyon2026 place the song in the genre and the year.

The title, “Pa di mwen JTM STP”, mixes Creole with texting shorthand: don’t tell me I love you, please. It is a love refusal turned into a hook line, the kind of phrase bouyon often builds for social media. This site does not comment on the lyrics beyond what the title itself says.

The DJ Leguste connection

DJ Leguste’s presence at the recording matters. His profile on this site already lists this same title among his releases, and the video description links to his Instagram and to a management address in his name. Le Matt and Pepito therefore sit inside the circle of voices this producer rotates through his singles. For the scene around that circle, the bouyon in Guadeloupe page gives context, without this site assigning an island to either artist.

Why one page for two artists

In the opened sources, Pepito appears only on this title. A separate page would repeat the same lines under another name, so this site prefers one shared page that says so plainly. Other YouTube results carry the name Pepito, but no page consulted links them to this voice, and they are not used. If either artist releases more titles, a biography or an official page, this profile will be expanded, or split.
