Candy: Miss Grippy and Wet Already, bouyon with DJ Taffy

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Candy is one of the voices of DJ Taffy's Riddim Beast studio. Since “Miss Grippy” in 2023, a title past 410,000 YouTube views when this profile was written, Candy has kept releasing bouyon with the producer: “Kokay ce soir” and “Watch Dem” on the album Release De Beast in January 2024, “Wet Already” with Wotlis in June 2024, then “Something New”, billed Bouyon 2026, in February 2026. The opened pages give neither the artist's origin nor full name: this profile sticks to the credits.

Miss Grippy, the entry title

“DJ Taffy x Candy - Miss Grippy” was uploaded on April 28, 2023 on the DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast channel. The page credits writing and performance to DJ Taffy and Candy, production to DJ Taffy “Riddim Beast”, and recording, mixing and mastering to the Riddim Beast Studio. The single reached Deezer on May 27, 2023 as “Miss Grippy (feat. Candy)” on the Riddim Beast Studios label, 3 minutes 12.

The YouTube page's keywords set the frame: bouyon, dominica, djtaffy, riddim beast. It is inside the stable of DJ Taffy, one of the most present producers of 2020s bouyon, that Candy appears, and it is this first title, the most viewed of the pages opened for this profile, that established the name.

Release De Beast and Wet Already: the 2024 year

On January 4, 2024, DJ Taffy published two titles from the album Release De Beast with Candy. “Kokay ce soir (bouyon 2024)”, written and performed by DJ Taffy and Candy, with Nikita “Fridge” Christmas on drums, Natty Bill on bass, mixing and mastering by Dernel “Dernz” Green and cover art by Dwayne Skerrit. And “Watch Dem”, written and performed by Trixx, Nice and Candy, produced by DJ Taffy, past 281,000 views.

On June 26, 2024 came “Wet Already (Bouyon 2024)”, billed Wotless ft Candy, written by Wotlis and Candy, produced by DJ Taffy, live drums by Drummer Fridge, more than 230,000 views. On Deezer, “Wet already (feat. wotlis & Candy)” is dated July 26, 2024, label Riddim Beast Studios, with Dj Taffy, Wotlis and Candy as contributors. The 2024 titles confirm Candy's place among the studio's features, next to the best known names of the circuit.

Something New, billed Bouyon 2026

On February 9, 2026, DJ Taffy's channel uploaded “Taffy x Candy - Something New (Bouyon 2026) Raw”. The page credits the riddim to Savion Beatz, production to DJ Taffy / Riddimbeast, executive production to Riddimbeast Studios, writing to Taffy and Candy, mixing and mastering to DJ Taffy. The title is Candy's fourth duet with the producer published on that channel since 2023.

This profile is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026. None of them gives the artist's full name, origin or gender: we do not guess them. To place the sound of these titles, see our page on bouyon in Dominica; the profile will be extended when an official Candy page or an interview documents the artist's path.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Candy?

A voice of DJ Taffy's Riddim Beast studio, credited on Miss Grippy (2023), Kokay ce soir and Watch Dem (album Release De Beast, 2024), Wet Already with Wotlis (Bouyon 2024) and Something New (Bouyon 2026).

What is Candy's most viewed title?

Miss Grippy with DJ Taffy, uploaded on April 28, 2023, was past 410,000 YouTube views on October 27, 2026. Watch Dem with Trixx and Nice had more than 281,000.

Who produces Candy's titles?

DJ Taffy, known as Riddim Beast, on every title opened for this profile; Something New (2026) is built on a riddim by Savion Beatz.

Where can I stream Candy?

Miss Grippy (feat. Candy) and Wet already (feat. wotlis & Candy) are on Deezer under Dj Taffy's name, label Riddim Beast Studios; the other titles are on the DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast YouTube channel.

Where is Candy from?

The opened pages do not say. They place the artist in DJ Taffy's Riddim Beast circuit, tagged dominica on YouTube; this profile states no origin.
