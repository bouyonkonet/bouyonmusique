Mikty: Guadeloupe singer and Mikty Pwod, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Mikty is a singer from Guadeloupe, a guitarist who directs his own videos and releases under the Mikty Pwod banner. He moved into bouyon in 2021 with “Good Lights” on J2MO's High Lights Riddim, then followed with “Bang Bang” alongside DJ Taffy, “Kind Of Girl” and “No Mercy”. His channel also runs the Feel Di Riddim series of bouyon mixes.

From Guadeloupe to Sizzla

The oldest trace sits on the Mikty 971 channel. In 2012, Mikty travelled to Jamaica for the country's 50th independence anniversary and met Sizzla Kalonji there. Together they recorded an acoustic take of “Happy Birthday Majesty”, a tribute to Haile Selassie, uploaded on December 1, 2012. The description calls him “native of Guadeloupe”.

The credits on that video say a lot about how he works. Mikty plays guitar, shoots part of the footage with Kevin Madeleine and directs. Editing is by Selekta Gwadad, and production by Jugment Yard and Mikty Pwod, the name he later kept for his channel.

Good Lights and the bouyon songs

On September 27, 2021, Up Full Media Group released the High Lights Riddim by J2MO. Mikty sings “Good Lights”, written with Christophe DeMocrite, and appears on the megamix with Mad General and Miky Ding La. On October 5, Mikty Pwod posted its own upload, billed “Bouyon, Prod by J2MO”, with a French description: the portrait of a woman who knows what she wants, and above all what she no longer wants.

In February 2024, JulianspromosTV put out “Bang Bang” by Mikty and DJ Taffy, billed “Bouyon 2024”; Mikty Pwod also carries a freestyle version. On January 6, 2025 came “Kind Of Girl”, “Bouyon 2025”, a song for the woman worth choosing. Then, on January 23, 2026, “No Mercy”, on Shako's That Is It riddim, which he describes as “bouyon conscious”.

Feel Di Riddim, the channel's mixes

Next to his songs, Mikty Pwod posts bouyon mixes signed Selekta Gwadad. The first volume of Feel Di Riddim, uploaded on December 20, 2024, was past 115,900 views when we read it. Its description sums up the genre in one image: bouyon is a long fuse stick of dynamite, building from the warm up to the nasty business without ever hitting a limit.

Volume two, in 2026, brings together Asa Bantan, Pudaz, DJ Taffy, TK International and Trixx, and a special mix goes with the 2026 World Creole Music Festival. No source says whether Selekta Gwadad and Mikty are the same person; all we know is that Selekta Gwadad was already editing his videos in 2012.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Mikty?

A singer and guitarist from Guadeloupe who directs his own videos and releases under the Mikty Pwod name.

Are Mikty and Mikty Pwod the same?

Mikty Pwod is his production banner: it sits next to Mikty, the director, in the 2012 credits, and the Mikty Pwod channel posts his own songs such as Good Lights and No Mercy.

What are his bouyon songs?

Good Lights on J2MO's High Lights Riddim (2021), Bang Bang with DJ Taffy (2024), Kind Of Girl (2025) and No Mercy on Shako's That Is It riddim (2026).

What is Feel Di Riddim?

A series of bouyon mixes signed Selekta Gwadad on the Mikty Pwod channel; the first volume, from December 2024, is past 115,000 views.

Who did he record with in Jamaica?

Sizzla Kalonji, on an acoustic version of Happy Birthday Majesty filmed in 2012.
