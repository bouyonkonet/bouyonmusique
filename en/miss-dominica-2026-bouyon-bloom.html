Miss Dominica 2026: Bouyon Bloom, the national pageant salutes bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 31, 2026

Bouyon did not only fill stages in 2026: it gave its theme to the country's biggest pageant. The Miss Dominica Pageant 2026 was held in February at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, Carnival City, under the theme Bouyon Bloom, and Dainisha Eusebe of Grand Bay was crowned Miss Dominica 2026.

Bouyon Bloom, the theme of the year

Writeups 24 presents the theme this way: Bouyon Bloom celebrated and honoured the rising bouyon music and the talented artists promoting the genre. Caribbean Pulse, in turn, calls it a tribute to bouyon music and the artistes who have helped shape the genre's cultural influence in Dominica.

The organisers, quoted by Caribbean Pulse, said this year's pageant celebrated the vibrancy of bouyon music, highlighting its cultural importance while showcasing the talent, creativity and community involvement of the contestants.

Five contestants, five rounds

Five contestants competed across five rounds: Interview, National Spectacular Costume, Talent, Swimwear and Evening Wear, per Writeups 24. The platforms ranged from animal welfare to confidence through sustainable style, from silence to strength, and breaking the stigma around PCOS.

The contestants were Darna Eugene, 22, of Mahaut; Dainisha Eusebe, 24, of Grand Bay; Daina Graneau of the Atkinson and Kalinago Territory; Sheraine Mills, 24, of Jimmit and St Joseph; and Sanchia Tyson, 19, of Crayfish River in the Kalinago Territory, per Writeups 24's presentation.

Dainisha Eusebe crowned

Dainisha Eusebe won the title, ahead of Sheraine Mills as first runner up and Sanchia Tyson as second runner up: both opened reports, Writeups 24 and Caribbean Pulse, agree on the podium. They also agree on two of the winner's awards: Best Response to Question and Best in Swimwear.

Caribbean Pulse adds that Eusebe was sponsored by Kingdom Bank Corporation and championed an animal welfare platform, advocating humane treatment of animals. Writeups 24 describes her talent round: she sang while playing guitar. The remaining round awards differ between the two reports and are left to each of them.

A date question, stated plainly

The two opened sources do not give the same day: Writeups 24 dates the pageant to Thursday 12 February 2026, Caribbean Pulse to 13 February. Both place it at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, Carnival City, in the heart of Dominica's carnival season, Mas Domnik.

That one day disagreement does not change the central fact of the year: in 2026, the national pageant chose bouyon as its official theme. For a genre born in Dominica in the late 1980s, seeing its name headline Miss Dominica says something about its place in the country's culture.
