G’z Up and FIX UP, the Ya ya ya hook turned calling card

Published October 12, 2026

G’z Up, on Instagram as @gz_upp, is the lead artist of the bouyon track FIX UP, also written Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya). The song is carried by its hook, the Ya ya ya cry that opens the track, the only lyric line verified in our records and the only one this page prints. His location is not verified in our records and no other biography facts are there: the profile is therefore built around the track, its video, its cover and its reception. FIX UP: a track carried by its opening

Some bouyon tracks are told through their chorus, FIX UP is told through its first three syllables: Ya ya ya. That opening cry works as an immediate sound signature, recognisable in a video, in a mix or in a crowd, before the rest of the song even unfolds. In a genre where tracks must hook listeners within seconds, on social media as at a party, a hook of this kind is a major asset, and it is what justifies the full title in its Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya) form.

This page prints no other lyric from the track, because no other line is verified in our records. That is the rule applied to every artist on the site: better one certain hook than a text rebuilt from memory. A reader who wants the full song will find it in the genre’s playlists, starting with the ROAD MIX bouyon playlist, where FIX UP belongs among the tracks carrying the current sound.

G’z Up’s name stays attached to the track as its lead artist, a point Bouyon Shattaz is careful to establish clearly in its coverage: in online bouyon, the accounts that repost an excerpt are often confused with its authors. Here the credit is clean, FIX UP is G’z Up’s track, and it is under his name that the platform prepared and presented it.

The October 16, 2026 press review: from cypher to release

On October 16, 2026, a Time Warner Entertainment press review of Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya) traced the track’s path, from its cypher origin to its release as a G’z Up track. The cypher, the circle where artists take turns on the microphone, is a frequent birthplace for bouyon and Creole rap hooks: a formula is tested live, in front of peers, before it becomes a studio chorus. Reading FIX UP along that path explains its opening, the Ya ya ya has the shape of a formula born to be taken up by several voices.

The review also singled out G’z Up’s rhythm work, his rhythm-switching over producer ThaWizZarD’s keys. That production detail matters: on a bouyon riddim, the voice does not simply follow the loop, it can push against it, speed up and land again, and that is often where an artist’s personality is played out. The producer’s credit is therefore named here as it is in the review, ThaWizZarD on keys, G’z Up in front.

For an independent bouyon track, a review of this kind changes the song’s status: it no longer circulates only as excerpts and posts, it enters a written memory that search engines and future articles can cite. This page takes part in the same movement, with the verified facts it holds, no more, no less.

Frequently asked questions

Who is G'z Up?

G'z Up, @gz_upp on Instagram, is the lead artist of the bouyon track FIX UP, also written Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya). The song was born from his standout verse in Trilla-G's Up Next Cypher, where he is one of six emerging voices put forward. His location and other biographical details are not verified in our files.

How do you spell G'z Up?

The name is written G'z Up, with an apostrophe and as two words, as in the official credits of Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya). His Instagram account, however, is spelled @gz_upp, with two p's. In online bouyon, accounts that repost a clip are often mistaken for its authors: G'z Up is the lead artist of FIX UP.

What is the hook of FIX UP?

The track is carried by the Ya ya ya cry that opens it, a sound signature you recognise in a video, a mix or a crowd before the rest of the song even unfolds. It is the only verified lyric line in our files, and this page reproduces no other lyrics from the song.

Are FIX UP and Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya) the same track?

Yes. FIX UP is the short title, and Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya) the full form, which adds the opening hook to the song's name. Both refer to G'z Up's single, drawn from his verse in Trilla-G's Up Next Cypher. It is the same track in playlists, in the press and in the official credits.

Who produced G'z Up's FIX UP?

According to the track's official YouTube description, writing and performance go to G'z Up, production to Kwib La Beats and ThaWizZarD, mixing and mastering to ThaWizZarD, and executive production to Trilla-G. ThaWizZarD's keys sit at the heart of the track's melodic architecture. See ThaWizZarD's profile.

What is the link between FIX UP and Trilla-G?

FIX UP was born in Trilla-G's world: the track takes G'z Up's standout verse from Trilla-G's “Up Next Cypher, Vol. 1” and releases it as a full single, with Trilla-G as executive producer. Trilla-G is the source, G'z Up the lead artist. See Trilla-G's profile.

What is Trilla-G's Up Next Cypher?

It is a cypher of nearly ten minutes released by Still Trill Entertainment on October 2, 2026, according to BuzzDA: one instrumental, by Kwib La Beats, and a line of emerging artists taking turns on the mic, G'z Up, Ras B, Avatar Omish, Don Lash, Pa Pel and Pryhmex. ThaWizZarD handles keys, mixing and mastering.

When did G'z Up release Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya)?

According to BuzzDA, G'z Up's extended solo version, with an extra verse, was released on Saturday October 10, 2026, a few days after the cypher came out on October 2. In its October 12, 2026 article, BuzzDA already counted around 15,000 views on YouTube, a figure given with its source and date.

What did the press say about Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya)?

A Time Warner Entertainment review dated October 16, 2026 traced the track from its cypher origin, filmed in an open-air bar, to its release under G'z Up's name. It highlights the relaxed way he switches from one bouncy rhythm to another while ThaWizZarD's keyboard melodies grow progressively wilder.

Does G'z Up make rap or bouyon?

FIX UP is presented as a bouyon track, but its form comes from a cypher, the circle where artists take turns on the mic, which bouyon shares with Creole rap. The Time Warner Entertainment review reads him as a rapper, focused on his rhythm switches over ThaWizZarD's keys. His voice does not just follow the loop: it pushes against it.

Where can I listen to G'z Up's FIX UP?

FIX UP has its place in the Bouyon Shattaz Bouyon Road Mix, among the tracks carrying today's sound, on Spotify and YouTube. To hear it in context, then play Trilla-G's “Up Next Cypher, Vol. 1”, where his verse comes from. The track is also among the 2026 bouyon releases followed by this site: bouyon playlist.
