Blaka Dan: Grenada soca and bouyon, Bouyon Rave (2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Blaka Dan, real name Wrenroy Ogiste, has been singing soca since at least 2016 on pages that bill it as Grenada soca. His biggest title, “Blessing”, is past 7 million views on his channel. In 2026 he crossed into bouyon three times: “Bouyon Rave” with Motto, “Forewarned” on ProducerDlo's Bouyon Rave Riddim, then “Warn You” with the singer Hunter, filed as “Bouyon Soca”.

A career in Grenada soca

The oldest page opened for this profile is from June 2, 2016: “My Time Now” on the Scrap Paper Riddim, posted by Xpert Productions under the label “Grenada Soca 2016”. Wrenroy “Blaka Dan” Ogiste wrote it with Hollice “Mr. Killa” Mapp and Aaron Stafford, and Valene Nedd sings the additional vocals. Valene Nedd is still on backing vocals on “Blessing” and “Forewarned”, ten years later.

The real turning point is “Blessing”, uploaded on October 8, 2024 for the 2025 season. It was produced by Damion “Pappy Boi” La Pompe and Joseph “Tus” Thomas of Loyalty Family Production, with Jeavid Neckles on guitar, and its page was past 7.1 million views when read. “Police Report”, on the Interpol Riddim, followed in May 2025, carried by JulianspromosTV with the tag “Grenada”. On Deezer his catalogue holds 21 releases, from “Not in Dat” (March 2018) to “My Time Now” (October 2026).

Bouyon Rave and Forewarned: January 2026

On January 12, 2026, “Bouyon Rave” reached Deezer, credited to Motto, Blaka Dan, Teamfoxx and Boogy Rankss. The visualizer TeamFoxx posted two days later is billed “2026 Bouyon Power Soca” and names both writers, Lashley Motto Winter and Wrenroy Blaka Dan Ogiste; it had 10,800 views when read. A month earlier the TeamFoxx label had already released “Quarter Pass Whine”, a Blaka Dan duet with Salty.

That same month he cut “Forewarned” on the Bouyon Rave Riddim by Dylan “ProducerDlo” Lowe and Dylan “Hossi” Augustine, a riddim partly played by hand: Akim “Haze” Hazel on drums, Cornell Phillip tracking the drums, Jervon “Nattybill” Henderson on bass. ProducerDlo posted the song on January 13, then a second upload on January 22, for more than 10,000 views between them.

Warn You with Hunter, and autumn 2026

On September 11, 2026, the singer Hunter posted “Warn You” on his channel, written with Blaka Dan, produced by Litleboy and mixed by Damion “Pappy Boi” Stephen for Tempo Networks. The page's hashtags pair bouyon with Grenada and the Virgin Islands, and MyuzikBox reposted it a week later under the genre “Bouyon Soca”. Mind the shared name: this Hunter is a soca singer, not the bouyon riddim maker Hxnter.

Meanwhile Blaka Dan stays loyal to Loyalty Family Productions, which released “Family” in May, “The Loyal Sideman” in September and a new “My Time Now” with College Boy Jesse on October 19, 2026. Those are presented as soca; his three bouyon pages of the year are all collaborations. This profile rests on the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 29, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Blaka Dan?

Wrenroy Ogiste, a soca singer whose songs are billed as Grenada soca, best known for Blessing (2024), which is past 7 million views.

What are his bouyon titles?

Bouyon Rave with Motto (January 12, 2026), Forewarned on ProducerDlo's Bouyon Rave Riddim (January 2026) and Warn You with Hunter (September 2026, Bouyon Soca).

Who produced Forewarned?

Dylan “ProducerDlo” Lowe and Dylan “Hossi” Augustine, who also mixed and mastered it; Blaka Dan wrote it alone.

Who produces his soca?

Mostly Loyalty Family Production, around Damion “Pappy Boi” La Pompe: Blessing, Police Report, Family, The Loyal Sideman and My Time Now (2026).

Is the Hunter on Warn You the producer Hxnter?

No. Warn You is sung by Hunter, a soca singer, and produced by Litleboy; Hxnter is a different musician who makes bouyon riddims.
