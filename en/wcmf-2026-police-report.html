WCMF 2026: no major incidents at the festival, police say

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

The World Creole Music Festival 2026 ended without any major incidents, according to the Commonwealth of Dominica Police Force. That is what police said after the three-night festival, in a statement reported by BuzzDA on 30 October 2026. No attendance figure and no incident figure has been published: this article reports what is verified, and nothing more.

No major incidents

According to the Commonwealth of Dominica Police Force, as reported by BuzzDA on 30 October 2026, the 2026 festival ended without any major incidents. In its statement after the three-night event, police credited the professionalism and dedication of officers and other security personnel who kept patrons, performers and visitors safe.

Police also thanked the festival organisers, partner agencies and members of the public for their cooperation and support, and reaffirmed their commitment to public safety. It has to be said precisely: no major incidents does not mean no incidents. Minor incidents, arrests or medical cases are covered in no opened source, and this article does not invent them.

Three nights at Windsor Park

WCMF 2026 ran from 23 to 25 October 2026 at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. It was the 26th edition of the festival, over three nights. The headliners across the three nights included Capleton, Skillibeng, Klass, Alkaline, Tabou Combo, Machel Montano, Uncle Waffles and FRIDAYY, with Ne-Yo as the big moment of night 2.

The police report is the only opened source on the security side of the festival. The Dominica News closing piece confirms the festival ended successfully, but it says nothing about police or incidents. No second report confirms the police statement so far.

The security operation

The clean outcome is partly explained by the operation put in place before the festival. At the final police press conference before WCMF, the following measures were announced: search teams at the entrances, admitting only ticket holders, and bags searched at the Bath Road gate.

Banned items included firearms and ammunition, even licensed, illegal drugs, knives, cutlasses, scissors, ice picks, cigarette lighters and glass bottles, and cannabis was banned in public spaces. The venue was divided into three zones, each with a gazetted officer, inspectors, private security and volunteers. A police safety notice was also issued on opening day, 23 October 2026.
