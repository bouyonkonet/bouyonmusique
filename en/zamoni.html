Zamoni: Mash Up Kingdom, bouyon soca from Antigua

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Zamoni is an Antigua-based soca singer who has been part of Antigua Carnival releases since 2017. His bouyon story comes down to one song: “Mash Up Kingdom”, shared with two Dominican voices and named Song of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards 2025.

A singer from Antigua Carnival

His early videos all carry the same label: Antigua soca. “Defend” in 2017, then “Work It” and “Bamya” in 2018, which were also posted by Antigua's Carnival Festivals Commission, the body that runs the carnival. There, “Work It” sits on the Service Riddim and is past 10,000 views.

In 2019 came “Chip” and “Sanctify”, billed as uptempo soca, which is close to 60,000 views, one of his most watched videos. Up to then nothing tied him to bouyon: he was a party singer from the island, working the Antigua summer season.

Mash Up Kingdom, with Dominica

On April 21, 2024 he released “Mash Up Kingdom” with Mr Benji, the Triple Kay singer, and Pahjo, both from Dominica. His own channel bills it “Antigua soca / Bouyon 2024”. The lyrics are credited to Patrick “Pahjo” Joseph and Wayne “Mr Benji” Benjamin.

The riddim comes from ShakerHD and QT 2hype, with Javier Javis on drums and mastering by Andre “Dre Major” Stewart. It was recorded in two places: SD Productions Studios in the UK and Home Alone Studios in Dominica. On YouTube, Zamoni's upload is past 17,000 views, well ahead of the other two singers' uploads.

Song of the Year 2025

In early January 2026, Antigua News reported that “Mash Up Kingdom” had won Song of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards 2025. The site called the track bouyon soca and described it as a groundbreaking collaboration across the Caribbean.

Since then Zamoni has gone back to carnival soca. In 2026 he put out “Fete Starter” in March, then “Good Friends”, “What We Drinking” and “Olly Olly Olly” during the Antigua summer. In bouyon terms his name stays tied to that one song, a clear example of how the genre now travels as far as Antigua.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Zamoni?

An Antigua-based soca singer who has been part of Antigua Carnival releases since 2017.

What is his bouyon song?

Mash Up Kingdom, released in April 2024 with Mr Benji and Pahjo, billed as Antigua soca and bouyon 2024.

What did Mash Up Kingdom win?

Song of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards 2025, according to Antigua News in January 2026.

Who produced Mash Up Kingdom?

ShakerHD and QT 2hype, with Javier Javis on drums, mixing by ShakerHD Productions and mastering by Andre Dre Major Stewart.

Is Zamoni from Dominica?

No, Antigua News describes him as an Antigua-based singer. Mr Benji and Pahjo are the ones from Dominica.
