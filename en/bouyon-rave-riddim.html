Bouyon Rave Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Bouyon Rave Riddim is a bouyon riddim produced, mixed and mastered by Dylan "ProducerDlo" Lowe and Dylan "Hossi" Augustine. It came out on January 13, 2026, on the ProducerDlo & Soundproof Music ENT label, with five vocal tracks and the instrumental. The most played of them, by a wide margin, is Shelly's "Burn Dem".

Two Dylans at the desk, real players in the room

The video descriptions on ProducerDlo's YouTube channel all carry the same credits. Production, mixing and mastering go to Dylan "ProducerDlo" Lowe and Dylan "Hossi" Augustine of Soundproof Ent. This is not a purely programmed riddim: Akim "Haze" Hazel plays live drums, Jervon "Nattybill" Henderson plays bass, and the drum tracking was done by Cornell Phillip, the keyboard player who founded WCK.

On Deezer, the "Bouyon Rave Riddim" album is dated January 13, 2026, with six tracks including a 3 minute 15 instrumental. The vocal videos went up the same day, tagged "Bouyon 2026". Only Blaka Dan's "Forewarned" got a second upload, on January 22, with the riddim's name in the title. Don't mix it up with "Bouyon Rave", the Motto and Blaka Dan single on Teamfoxx: that is a different song.

Five voices, and Blaka Dan writes twice

Shelly leads with "Burn Dem", which he wrote alone. Backing vocals come from Darren Green, Dernel Green, Giovannie Green and Liam Sebastien, the Signal Band circle. XS Groove sings "Only You", written by Delon "Tete G" Daniel, DiYoute and Milla.

Blaka Dan shows up twice: he performs "Forewarned" and he also wrote "Nocturnal Energy" for College Boy Jesse. The fifth song, "Till Morning", pairs Sledge HammaH and Benja, credited as writers under their own names, Brenal Mills and Benjamin Stewart. The sources we read don't say which island those last three singers come from.

Burn Dem out front, then the mixes

When we checked on October 28, 2026, "Burn Dem" had passed 46,000 views on ProducerDlo's channel. The rest trail well behind: roughly 6,400 for "Till Morning", 4,200 for "Forewarned", 3,400 for "Only You" and 1,200 for "Nocturnal Energy".

DJs picked up the set quickly. DJ Stallion posted a "Bouyon Rave Riddim Mix", DJ Smoove 4EvaHype made his own, and M.A.D. One Music filed it under "Soca Mix 2026". For how several singers end up sharing one instrumental, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Bouyon Rave Riddim?

Dylan "ProducerDlo" Lowe and Dylan "Hossi" Augustine, who also mixed and mastered it, according to the YouTube descriptions.

When did the Bouyon Rave Riddim come out?

On January 13, 2026, the date of the Deezer album and of the vocal uploads. "Forewarned" was uploaded again on January 22.

Who sings on the riddim?

Shelly, XS Groove, Blaka Dan, College Boy Jesse, plus Sledge HammaH and Benja as a duo.

Is it the same as Motto's Bouyon Rave?

No. "Bouyon Rave" by Motto and Blaka Dan is a Teamfoxx single. The Bouyon Rave Riddim is a ProducerDlo project.

What is the most played song on it?

Shelly's "Burn Dem", past 46,000 YouTube views in October 2026.
