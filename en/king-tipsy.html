By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

King Tipsy enters this site through a single title, “Bumpa Back”, posted as a lyric video on December 12, 2025 on his YouTube channel with “Bouyon 2026” in the title. The track rides the Backside Riddim by Khaos Entertainment, billed as soca and bouyon 2026, and is credited to the writer Alvin Bonas. An older title, “De Dawgs Dem”, belongs to dancehall according to its own page. Not to be confused with “Tipsy”, a title by the artist MadameZelle.

Bumpa Back, soca and bouyon 2026

The “Bumpa Back” lyric video runs 2 minutes 19. Its description says King Tipsy performs the track on the “Backside Riddim” by Khaos Entertainment, then files it under a double label, “2026 Soca/Bouyon”. Copyright and the recording right are dated 2025 in the name of Khaos Entertainment, which also handles production, and the writing is credited to Alvin Bonas. The hashtags name the riddim, soca and bouyon.

A double label is common in riddim season, when one instrumental travels between the soca and bouyon scenes. This site files the title as bouyon because its page says so, in the title and in the description, without drawing conclusions about the rest of the artist’s catalogue. For the line between the two genres, see bouyon vs soca.

A wider catalogue

King Tipsy’s channel also holds “De Dawgs Dem”, an official video posted on May 22, 2020, running 2 minutes 52, produced by Siemma prod and recorded and mixed by Money Circle Records. Its description links to Apple Music and Audiomack and carries the hashtags Khaos, KingTipsy, Dancehall, Trinidad and Jamaica. There is no bouyon mention: this title is cited here as a career marker, not as a bouyon title.

The name Khaos appears on both pages, in 2020 and in 2025, which places King Tipsy in the orbit of Khaos Entertainment over at least five years. The Instagram account cited is kinqtipsy.

Origin: signals, not a statement

The Trinidad and Jamaica hashtags on the “De Dawgs Dem” page are distribution tags, common on Caribbean videos, not a statement of origin. No opened page names an island, a town or a country for King Tipsy, or publishes a biography. This profile therefore assigns no origin.

The verifiable file holds one bouyon title, one label, one riddim and one credited writer. It will be expanded if the artist releases more titles stated as bouyon. For other releases of the same season, see new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is King Tipsy?

King Tipsy is an artist linked to Khaos Entertainment, performer of Bumpa Back on the Backside Riddim, a title billed as soca and bouyon 2026. No public biography was found.

What is King Tipsy’s bouyon title?

“Bumpa Back”, a lyric video posted on December 12, 2025 under the Bouyon 2026 title, written by Alvin Bonas and produced by Khaos Entertainment.

Are King Tipsy and Tipsy the same thing?

No. King Tipsy is an artist; “Tipsy” is a title by MadameZelle produced by Smokiller. No page links the two.

Is De Dawgs Dem a bouyon title?

Not according to its page, which files it under dancehall through its hashtags and does not mention bouyon.

Where is King Tipsy from?

No opened page says. The Trinidad and Jamaica hashtags on one video are signals, not an origin.
