Bouyon sound engineers: the names the credits tell

By Smokiller · Published October 26, 2026

In bouyon, the singer takes the light, but published credits tell another chain: the engineers who compose, record, mix and master. This page follows two names that opened, verified credits place behind recent tracks: Dernel Green and ThaWizZarD.

On this page

  2. ThaWizZarD, the cypher mix

Dernel Green, three hats on one track

The production notes published for Where Did You Come From, performed by Shelly and Signal Band, credit the song as written by Sheldon Alfred, Giovannie Green and Darren Green, and as composed, produced and engineered by Dernel Green.

The same notes state that vocals were recorded in two places: Studio B in Sint Maarten and The Electric Room in Dominica, with background vocals by Darren Green and Giovannie Green and live bass by Giovannie Green. One track is enough to show the work: writing the material, producing it, then capturing it across two islands.

ThaWizZarD, the cypher mix

For the Trilla-G Up Next Cypher, The Bajan Reporter credits mixing and mastering to ThaWizZarD, whose full name is given as Cecil Joseph Jr., on a production by Shane Massicott of Kwib La Beats.

Mixing a cypher means keeping six different voices readable on one beat: G z Up, Don Lash, Pa Pel, Avatar Omish, Ras B and Pryhmex, plus Trilla-G. Mastering then gives the whole the level and colour of a released track, not a demo.

Studio names inside the credits

Opened credits also name the places: Studio B in Sint Maarten and The Electric Room in Dominica for the Shelly and Signal Band track. On other dossiers on this site, composition also appears in credits, such as the DJ Blax clip with Tamillia, composed by DJ Blax and John Scorp per the DJ Blax post.

This page builds no biography for John Scorp from a single credit line: it keeps the line, attributed to its source, and waits for fuller credits before going further.

Why credits matter

A published credit is evidence: it dates, it names, it connects. The artist pages on this site are built on those lines, and thin dossiers are called thin there.

For engineers, the stake is direct: when a riddim travels from Dominica to Guadeloupe, Martinique or Paris, their names travel in the metadata. Reading them is already a way of making them exist.

Frequently asked questions

Who engineered Where Did You Come From by Shelly and Signal Band?

The published production notes credit Dernel Green as composer, producer and engineer of the track, with vocals recorded at Studio B in Sint Maarten and The Electric Room in Dominica.

Who mixed and mastered the Up Next Cypher?

ThaWizZarD, per The Bajan Reporter, on a production by Shane Massicott of Kwib La Beats.

What is known about John Scorp on the bouyon side?

The DJ Blax post on his clip with Tamillia credits him as a composer alongside DJ Blax. No verified biography is stated here from that single line.
