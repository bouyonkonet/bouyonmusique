Shemmy J: Spoil You, Ou Dou and Bumpa In D Road

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Shemmy J, credited as Shemmy Johannes, sings between two islands. His own pages tie him to Saint Lucia, and nearly all of his bouyon titles run through Dominican producers: “Ou Dou” in 2022 and “Spoil You” in 2023, produced by Litle Boy, then “Bumpa In D Road” in 2026 with Riddim Knockaz Band. The credits of that last title say it in flags: the Riddim Knockaz studio under the Dominica flag, Shemmy J under the Saint Lucia flag.

Ou Dou and Spoil You, with Litle Boy

“Ou Dou”, a duet with Edwin, was published on May 17, 2022 on Shemmy J’s channel under the label Bouyon/Lovers Pump, running 2 minutes 41. It is written by Shemmy J, produced by litleboy767 and recorded, mixed and mastered at Ransum Records. The description frames it as a carnival song for couples, between the fete and the mas.

“Spoil You” followed on March 21, 2023, titled Bouyon Soca 2023, running 3 minutes 13. Writing is credited to Shemmy Johannes, production to Litle Boy, mixing and mastering to ParryJack, recording to Ransum Records and backing vocals to Peter Wildfire Noel, on the DSM Music Group label. The StLuciaCarnival2023 hashtag places the release in the Saint Lucia carnival season. Litle Boy is the Dominican beatmaker profiled on this site as Litleboy LSBeats767.

Bumpa In D Road, with Riddim Knockaz Band

“Bumpa In D Road” was published on February 2, 2026 on the Riddim Knockaz Band channel, labelled Bouyon 2026, running 2 minutes 41; the DistroKid release, under the line 5046775 Records DK, is dated February 3, 2026. The voices are Glenn “Milly” Mills and Shemmy J, writing is shared between Dion Brewster, Glenn Mills and Shemmy J, production goes to Dion Brewster, the riddim and additional production to Arady “Maskman” Moses, and engineering to Ajmal “Skyo” Peters of Bouyon Kitchen Studios.

The recording line is the most telling part of the file: the title was recorded at the Riddim Knockaz studio, marked with the Dominica flag, and at Shemmy J’s end, marked with the Saint Lucia flag. A long distance collaboration between two neighbouring islands that bouyon has linked for a long time.

Around bouyon: LIAR and Saint Lucia soca

“LIAR”, with Litleboy, published on October 22, 2024, opens its description with the flags of Saint Lucia and Dominica; it is produced by Litleboy767, written by Litleboy767 and Shemmy J and released on the OSIDE Ent label. The page states no genre, and the title stays in the text here.

“Ms Grippy”, on the Nasty Riddim, is presented as 2023 Saint Lucia soca, written by Shemmy Johannes and produced by Lashley “Motto” Winter. The riddim note describes a Teamfoxx instrumental made for the Saint Lucia carnival and seasoned with bouyon, Dennery Segment and jab jab: a description of the riddim, not a label on the title, which therefore stays out of the structured data.

Origin, what the pages say

Shemmy J’s tie to Saint Lucia is stated by his own pages and by his partners: the Saint Lucian flag beside his name in the Riddim Knockaz credits, the pair of flags on “LIAR” and the StLuciaCarnival2023 hashtag on “Spoil You”. No opened page gives his birthplace, however, and this site does not write one.

His bouyon file comes down to three titles labelled on their own pages, from 2022 to 2026, all linked to Dominican producers or a Dominican band. It is a good example of the back and forth between Dominica and Saint Lucia that runs through bouyon and bouyon soca.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shemmy J?

Shemmy J is the stage name credited to Shemmy Johannes, writer and performer of “Ou Dou”, “Spoil You” and “Bumpa In D Road”, three titles labelled bouyon on their pages.

Who produced Spoil You?

Litle Boy, according to the description of the video of March 21, 2023, with ParryJack on mixing and mastering and Ransum Records on recording, on the DSM Music Group label.

Who did Shemmy J record Bumpa In D Road with?

With Riddim Knockaz Band: Glenn “Milly” Mills on vocals, Dion Brewster producing and Arady “Maskman” Moses on the riddim. The title came out in February 2026.

Where is Shemmy J from?

His pages and his partners tie him to Saint Lucia, through a flag beside his name and his carnival hashtags. No opened page gives his birthplace.

Is LIAR a bouyon title?

Its page states no genre. The title, produced by Litleboy767, is named here in the text only.
