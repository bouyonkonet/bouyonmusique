Bouyon music: Madam Wob Dwiyet 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026

One week after Samantha Bazil was crowned Miss Wob Dwiyet 2026, Dominica's national dress changed stage generation: the National Madam Wob Dwiyet Pageant was held on Wednesday 28 October 2026 at the Massacre Indoor Facility, inside the official programme of the 48th Independence season.

The counterpart of the Miss, for ages 50 and over

The pageant's official registration form, opened for this page, sets the central rule: the applicant must be fifty years or older. She must have no criminal record and no pending proceedings, in or out of Dominica, and she files a valid police record, a passport size photograph and a full body photograph with her form.

Where the Miss Wob Dwiyet judges contestants aged 18 to 30 on cultural knowledge, talent and traditional movements in the Wob Dwiyet, the Madam addresses the women who have worn this dress for decades and pass it on.

The 28 October date at Massacre

The official programme published by the Office of the Prime Minister places the National Madam Wob Dwiyet Pageant on 28 October at the Massacre Indoor Facility, like the Miss pageant on 21 October. Guide.dm lists the event at 8:00 PM.

The revised Creole season calendar, reported by Writeups 24 as early as 18 September, confirms the same date in the run from 3 October to 4 November, between the World Creole Music Festival and Jounen Kweyol.

The result is not in the press yet

As this page is written, on 29 October, no press source opened for this file names the winner of Madam Wob Dwiyet 2026. Searches today return only the coverage of Samantha Bazil's Miss Wob Dwiyet title.

This page therefore publishes no winner's name: the Madam result will get its own article when the Dominican press publishes it, as the Miss result did on 21 October. Inventing a name would betray exactly what this pageant honours, the exact transmission of a heritage.

Why this pageant matters to the season

The Wob Dwiyet is the national dress of Dominica's women. Tomorrow's National Dress Parade, on 30 October in the streets of Roseau for Jounen Kweyol, will put it in the street, worn by the women and girls of the country.

Between the Miss crowned on 21 October and the Madam held on 28 October, the season gives the dress its two faces: the succession and the memory. That is the chain the Wob Dwiyet pageants protect, at the heart of Heritage Month.
