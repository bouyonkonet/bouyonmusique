Hb War: the Guadeloupe Creole rapper, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Hb War has released Guadeloupean Creole rap since 2021, distributed by DistroKid under the 3125611 Records DK imprint. The writing and composition credits across his catalogue name Olivier Marcel Cayol. The platforms class him as hip-hop and rap, but part of his output carries the word bouyon in the title itself, from “LACRUCRU-ROMÉO-BouyonGwada” (2023) to the Bouyon 2k24 run of 2024. His TikTok account, @crucru971, carries Guadeloupe's 971 code.

A Creole catalogue kept moving since 2021

Hb War's Deezer artist page opens with a mixtape, “Medellin mixtape” (June 28, 2021), then lines up more than thirty singles: “Fanm Sympa” (2022), “HB WAR x WATCH (XXL)” with J2MO, “Nimpho” with Miky Ding La (2024), “FANM AW” with TIDOS, “MoNa LiSa” with DJ BEAW, and releases running into 2026, “Ayin Pou Ayin”, “DÉBOULÉ”, “NO BBL”. The titles are in Guadeloupean Creole and the collaborations stay inside the same circuit, including a cut on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim, “LaCrucru SA KI FO” (2023); see the DJ Taffy profile.

No press article or biography was found for this artist as of October 27, 2026. What the platforms show comes down to three facts: a constant writing credit, Olivier Marcel Cayol; a social account under the crew name, @crucru971, LaCrucru being the name that recurs in his titles; and a measurable audience, since “HBI WAR (SIW DI YO SA)” is his most-searched track on Shazam at 29.8k Shazams. The rest of this profile sticks to the discography.

The Bouyon 2k24 run

The word bouyon enters his titles on April 28, 2023 with “LACRUCRU-ROMÉO-BouyonGwada”, written by Olivier Marcel Cayol and composed by Bryce Beatz: the bouyon being claimed is Guadeloupe's, the bouyon gwada. December 2023 follows with “TCHOUP (BOUYON 2k24)”, which Shazam displays at 157 BPM, a bouyon tempo, while classing the track as hip-hop and rap. Spring 2024 chains “A Tri Bow / Bouyon2k24” (April 6) and “C TALA - bouyon 2k24” with Jackie Beatz (April 13).

The word does not only come from him: on Mjj lc's album “AU DELÀ DES MOTS”, the track “LA CRU CRU / BOUYON /” credits him as a guest, with the genre written into the title by the host artist. This site therefore presents Hb War as a rapper of the Guadeloupe circuit part of whose catalogue claims bouyon by title, while the platforms keep the rap label; both facts are given as they stand. The limits of the file

The verifiable file rests on the platform listings: Deezer for the discography and credits, Shazam for the classing, tempo and audience of his most-searched track, TikTok for the crew account. No birthplace, no interview, no explained stage name in the opened sources.

This profile will be updated if the Guadeloupe circuit press documents the artist, or if he releases new tracks under the bouyon name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Hb War?

A Guadeloupean Creole rapper active since 2021 on DistroKid (3125611 Records DK), whose writing and composition credits name Olivier Marcel Cayol, and whose TikTok account is @crucru971.

What are his bouyon tracks?

“LACRUCRU-ROMÉO-BouyonGwada” (2023), “TCHOUP (BOUYON 2k24)” (December 2023), “A Tri Bow / Bouyon2k24” and “C TALA - bouyon 2k24” with Jackie Beatz (April 2024), plus a bouyon-tagged guest spot with Mjj lc.

Does Hb War make bouyon or rap?

The platforms class his catalogue as hip-hop and rap; his titles carry the word bouyon, and Shazam displays “TCHOUP (BOUYON 2k24)” at 157 BPM. This site gives both facts without deciding for him.

Where is Hb War from?

No opened source names his birthplace. The @crucru971 account, the Guadeloupean Creole titles and the BouyonGwada billing place him in the Guadeloupe circuit, which is the presentation kept here.

What is his most-heard track?

On Shazam, “HBI WAR (SIW DI YO SA)” is his most-searched track, at 29.8k Shazams when checked.
