Ignition Band: Taste De Waist, bouyon, Roseau, Dominica

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Ignition Band is a group of the Dominican bouyon scene whose verified public record holds two traces, and this profile gives both of them, without inventing a third. First trace: in 2020, the “D Riddim Box” mix by Original DJ-Sly, a selection of the season new bouyon coming mostly from Dominica, places tenth “Ignition Band: Taste De Waist”, among Ridge, Triple Kay Band, Signal Band and Asa Bantan. Second trace: the official Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 schedule lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 2:32 PM, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, for the grand finale of the Creole village. No named members, no founding date, no confirmed official page: this profile tells what those two traces prove, the stage context around them, and states its limits in plain sight.

Who Ignition Band is: two verified traces, six years apart

Start with the traces, in their exact form. The published track listing of the “D Riddim Box” mix, subtitled “Best of 2020 Bouyon x Reketeng Mix” and signed Original DJ-Sly, lines up seventy-three tracks and places tenth “Ignition Band: Taste De Waist”. The DJ presents his selection as a collection of the latest bouyon music for 2020, most of it coming from Dominica and neighbouring islands, for promotional use. The title company is that of the Dominican scene of that season: Trilla G opens the mix with “The Prayer”, and Triple Kay Band, Ridge, Keks Mafia, Reo, Carlyn XP, Signal Band with Shelly and Asa Bantan fill the rest of the list.

The second trace arrives six years later, and it changes scale, because it is no longer a DJ tracklist but an official schedule. The Ministry of Tourism and Discover Dominica published the day by day schedule of Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, from 19 to 22 October 2026, for the fifth anniversary of the event, under the theme Tout Biten Domnik. On Thursday 22 October, grand finale day, the schedule lists at 2:32 PM “Ignition and Erwinchy”, between Explosive Band at 1:36 PM and Derick “Rah” Peters at 3:29 PM, on a day that also lines up Reo and Faithii, Trilla G, Asa Bantan and, closing at 7 PM, Shelly and Signal Band.

Between those two traces, and beyond them, the research carried out for this profile in October 2026 found nothing verifiable that belongs to this Dominican group. No biography. No member name. No interview, no label, no artist page on the platforms consulted, no confirmed official account. An American classic rock covers band also carries the name Ignition Band in the sources opened: it is a different act, with no documented link to the Dominican scene, and this profile does not merge the two. The sameAs field of this page therefore stays empty, for lack of an opened and verified link.

What the two traces establish together is already a readable career fact: a group whose track was running in the bouyon mixes of the 2020 season is still, or again, on the official posters of the biggest Creole week in Dominica in October 2026. In a scene where many formations live first as live acts, that continuity between the DJ mix and the ministry stage counts as a lifeline, thin but real.

Taste De Waist and D Riddim Box: the mix as a season archive

To understand the first trace, what a mix like “D Riddim Box” is worth has to be understood. Dominican bouyon lives to the rhythm of carnival seasons: tracks are born for one season, run in parties, on radio and at village fetes, then DJs fix them in mixes published with their full track listing. Those lists become, in effect, the sound archive of the season, the one the diaspora listens to and researchers find again years later. The Original DJ-Sly mix presents itself exactly that way: a collection of the new bouyon of 2020, most of it Dominican.

Appearing tenth in such a list, out of seventy-three tracks, is not a technical detail. It means being kept in the first third of the mix, where a DJ sets the energy of his selection, alongside the names carrying the scene that year. Around “Taste De Waist” stand “Riddim Tonight” by Ridge just before, “Foreigner” by Carlyn XP and Edday just after, then “On De House” by Signal Band with Shelly. The Ignition Band title therefore belongs, by its very place, to the story 2020 left of Dominican bouyon.

The mix title calls for a precision this site makes everywhere. DJ-Sly writes “Reketeng” in his subtitle, as many internet pages do. The official spelling, on this site, is Wrecketeng, and it comes from DJ Cut himself, the founding pioneer of the branch: the form reketeng is only a spelling error circulating online, flagged as such. The branch names the bouyon built on sampled melodies and rap vocals, born in the same Dominican studio ground as bouyon soca. That the 2020 mix marries both labels in its title says something about the state of the scene that season: the branches sit side by side in the same parties and the same lists.

One limit must be stated on this first trace, as this site does for every tracklist: it proves that a track called “Taste De Waist” ran under the name Ignition Band in the 2020 bouyon selection of a Dominican DJ. It gives neither the exact release date of the track, nor its writing or production credits, nor its presence on a streaming platform. No verifiable song page was found for this title during the research, so this profile invents none.

Ignition and Erwinchy: the trace that moves from mixes to the official stage

Ti Vilaj Kweyol is the Creole village of the Dominican independence season, set in the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, with its days of food, crafts, culture and music, from noon to 8 PM. The 2026 edition, the fifth, runs from Monday 19 to Thursday 22 October, under the theme Tout Biten Domnik, everything Dominican, with more than 90 entrepreneurs present according to the official presentation carried by local press, admission at EC$10 and free entry for students in uniform and children aged five and under. A few days later, the World Creole Music Festival takes over at Windsor Park, from 23 to 25 October 2026: it is the densest week of the island music calendar.

The official schedule, published on 19 October 2026 by the Ministry of Tourism and Discover Dominica and carried by Dominica Weekly, gives each day its times to the quarter hour. On Thursday 22 October, the grand finale opens with the Kalinago Dancers, UMOJA and Castle Bruce Primary School, passes through the Waitukubuli Dance Theatre Company then Explosive Band at 1:36 PM, and lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” at 2:32 PM. Derick “Rah” Peters, the founder of WCK, follows at 3:29 PM, then Reo and Faithii, Trilla G and Prymex at 4:26 PM, Asa Bantan at 5:43 PM, and Shelly and Signal Band closing at 7 PM.

The schedule wording pairs Ignition with Erwinchy, an artist also found in the bouyon tracklists of recent seasons, without stating whether it is a duo, a backing band or a shared slot. This profile reproduces the pairing as published, without deciding what the source does not decide. It notes only this: the same afternoon brings together two names the mixes had documented before the posters did, and places them between a stage band, Explosive Band, and the founder of WCK. That is the classic path of Dominican bouyon, from DJ lists to recognition by the cultural institutions of the country.

That scheduling is, to date, the strongest and most recent fact in the record: it connects the name from the 2020 tracklist to a verifiable stage, date, time and place in 2026. For a group with no published biography and no online catalogue found, being on the official programme of the Ti Vilaj Kweyol finale counts as a dated activity fact, delivered by the festival organisation itself.

The stage band: the historical form of Dominican bouyon

The group name itself, Ignition Band, with its word Band, ties it to the historical form of the genre. Bouyon was born in Roseau in the late 1980s, carried by live bands such as WCK, Roots, Stems and Branches and First Serenade, who also appear on the Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 programme. It is first a music of formations: keyboards, bass, drums and voices holding a crowd for hours, at carnival as much as at village fetes. The 2026 poster of the Creole village in fact mixes every generation of that form, from the pioneers to current bands such as Signal Band and Triple Kay International.

The branches that came out of the studios never replaced that form. Bouyon soca, started in Dominica in 2003 by producer Dada according to the first-hand testimony of Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer and a participant in that era, then popularised by Triple Kay, added the logic of producers and riddims. Wrecketeng added that of samples and rap vocals. But the bands stayed the base of the island stages, and a grand finale slot at Ti Vilaj Kweyol is played as a live formation, in front of the family audience of the Creole village, not over a mix backing track.

In that economy, many formations work almost entirely live. They rehearse, they tour the stages of the island, they are booked by fete committees and ministries, and they can hold that role for years without placing a single track on a streaming platform. Their public trace then shrinks to posters, programmes and mix tracklists. The Ignition Band situation, one track in a 2020 mix and one official slot in 2026, fits exactly into that pattern: the group exists in the documents of the scene before it exists in the databases.

This profile therefore refuses two opposite shortcuts. The first would be to invent a biography: a founding year, members, a home village, further titles. None of those elements is published in a verifiable source, so none is printed here. The second would be to treat the group as negligible because it is absent from the platforms. The two traces say the opposite: in Dominica, the stage and the mixes remain the institutions that count, and Ignition Band appears in them twice, six years apart.

What this profile claims, and what it leaves open

Let us sum up the record, because on a thin profile, clarity is worth more than emphasis. Claimed and sourced: an Ignition Band track, “Taste De Waist”, appears tenth in the published track listing of “D Riddim Box”, the 2020 bouyon mix by Original DJ-Sly, presented as a collection of the season new music coming mostly from Dominica. Claimed and sourced: the official Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 schedule lists “Ignition and Erwinchy” on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 2:32 PM at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, on grand finale day.

Open means: members unknown to this record, founding date and place not documented, home base not documented beyond the Roseau posters, repertoire unknown beyond the single tracklist title, label unknown, no official account confirmed, no recording found on the platforms consulted. The single track in this page schema is therefore exactly the 2020 tracklist title, not one more and not one less. If an official page, an interview or a documented discography appears, this profile will be completed, dated and corrected like every page on this site.

In the meantime, listening starts with the documents themselves: the “D Riddim Box” track listing, to hear Ignition Band inside the selection of its 2020 season, then our bouyon soca page and our bouyon playlist for the lineage of the Dominican sound. And for anyone in Roseau in October 2026, the meeting is already written in the programme: Botanic Gardens, Thursday 22 October, 2:32 PM, Ignition and Erwinchy.
