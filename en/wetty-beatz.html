Wetty Beatz: Link Up Riddim, Iz Yourz (2026 Bouyon)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Wetty Beatz, Phillip Dember Bastien by name, is a producer and singer from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines based in Europe. Breakout Artiste of the Vincentian carnival in 2016 per Searchlight, named Cultural Ambassador of his country by Prime Minister Ralph Gonsalves on October 27, 2023 per One News St Vincent, he released in May 2026 the Link Up Riddim and his own title “Iz Yourz”, billed “2026 Bouyon”, the project that also carries Magikal's “Whappa”. His Deezer page lists 44 releases since 2018.

Breakout Artiste 2016, Cultural Ambassador 2023

On July 29, 2016, Searchlight announced that Phillip “Wetty Beatz” Bastien was the Carnival Development Corporation's Breakout Artiste of the year in Saint Vincent. Seven years later, on October 27, 2023, One News St Vincent reported that Prime Minister Ralph Gonsalves, in his Independence Day address at Victoria Park, had named Lornette “Fyah Empress” Nedd and Phillip “Wetty Beatz” Dember Bastien Cultural Ambassadors of SVG, describing him as a Vincentian based in Europe and a leading producer and entertainer.

His sung titles tell the path: “Syé Pa Mélé”, uploaded on April 12, 2019, more than 244,000 views, performed, written, produced and mixed by Phillip Bastien; “Ting Go Nice Again”, published by Julianspromos on May 29, 2020, more than 559,000 views; “Cyah Take It”, in December 2020. For St Vincent's place in bouyon, see the profile of Skinny Fabulous and our page on bouyon soca.

Link Up Riddim and Iz Yourz: the 2026 bouyon billing

On May 25, 2026, Wetty Beatz Records uploaded “Wetty Beatz - Iz Yourz (Link up riddim) 2026 Bouyon”, written by Wetty Beatz, produced by Wetty Beatz Productionz and Foxx. The same day, the “Link Up Riddim” reached Deezer as an EP, label Wetty-Beatz Productionz: “Iz Yourz”, “Splash and Dash”, “Whappa”, “Where di Party Is” and the instrumental. “Whappa”, sung by Magikal, the 2019 Soca Monarch, was past 270,000 views when read.

The Link Up Riddim does not stand alone. The producer's Deezer page lines up riddim projects: Rude Riddim (2023), 16 Bars Riddim (2024), Doomsday, Free Ticket and Most Wanted Riddim (2025), 16 Bars Riddim II and Full Steam Riddim (2026). The Link Up is the one the producer chose to bill bouyon.

Tusty for Rum with Jab King and the rest of the catalogue

With Jab King, the Grenadian singer, Wetty Beatz signed two titles: “Hello” on the Super Saiyan Riddim, billed 2021 Power Soca, uploaded on January 29, 2021, written by Phillip Bastien and David Connell, on a riddim shared with Skinny Fabulous, Lavaman, Triniboi Joocie, Boyzie, Luni Spark and Electrify; and “Tusty for Rum”, published on June 14, 2024, produced by ThaWizzard, mixed by Wetty Beatz Productionz, more than 651,000 views, Deezer single of August 30, 2024.

Wetty Beatz's Deezer artist page lists 44 releases, from “Toe Low” (April 13, 2018) to “Dive” (June 16, 2026), with “Obeyah” (2018), “Bottle Over Head” (2019), “Xox-Yo” (2020), “Rum Love” and “Mas in Style” (2021), “Glory to Soca” (2023), “Piment” (2025) and “Craze” (2026). This profile is written from One News St Vincent, Searchlight and the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Wetty Beatz?

Phillip Dember Bastien, a Europe-based producer and singer from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, 2016 Breakout Artiste per Searchlight and SVG Cultural Ambassador since October 27, 2023 per One News St Vincent.

What is his bouyon title?

Iz Yourz, on his Link Up Riddim, uploaded on May 25, 2026 and billed 2026 Bouyon; the riddim also carries Magikal's Whappa, Splash and Dash and Where di Party Is.

What is his most viewed title?

Tusty for Rum with Jab King (June 2024), more than 651,000 views on October 27, 2026, ahead of Ting Go Nice Again (2020), more than 559,000.

Which riddims has he produced?

Per his Deezer page: Rude Riddim (2023), 16 Bars Riddim (2024), Doomsday, Free Ticket and Most Wanted Riddim (2025), 16 Bars Riddim II, Full Steam Riddim and Link Up Riddim (2026).

Why is he a Cultural Ambassador?

Prime Minister Ralph Gonsalves named him alongside Fyah Empress during his Independence Day address at Victoria Park on October 27, 2023, according to One News St Vincent.
