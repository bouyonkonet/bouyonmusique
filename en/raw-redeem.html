Raw Redeem International: Rah, Gimme Little Bouyon, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Raw Redeem, also credited as Raw Redeem International, is a Dominican bouyon band from the early 2000s. Rah, the voice of WCK, handles vocals and percussion, with Earlson Matthew on keys. Three albums left traces online: “Es Zot La” in 2000, “Gimme Little Bouyon” in 2001 and “Old Skool Session” in 2004, recorded in Roseau.

A band out of the WCK house

The credits posted for “Old Skool Session” give the lineup: Derrick Rah Peters on lead vocals, drums and percussion, Daniel Phillip on bass and backing vocals, Jeoffrey Joseph on keys and vocals, Earlson Matthew on keys and drum programming, Alisha Ducreay on vocals and Delton Alfred on drums.

Several of those names come from the founding band. The A Virtual Dominica guide lists Earlson Matthew and Delton Alfred among WCK's current members, and The Sun in Roseau counts Rah among the people who gave birth to bouyon. Raw Redeem carries the founding generation into the 2000s, with a full lineup in the line of old school bouyon.

Three albums, 2000 to 2004

The first album named is “Es Zot La”, in 2000, with a remix credited to DJ Cut, the pioneer of Wrecketeng. Then came “Gimme Little Bouyon” in 2001, with “Stallion” and “Body Motion”.

“Old Skool Session”, in 2004, was recorded at Bouyon Kitchen Studios on Kennedy Avenue in Roseau, mixed by Earlson Matthew with the band and mastered with Ja Foxx. “Culture Tyme” dates from the same year and was later posted on a channel devoted to bouyon.

Memory online

Most of the catalogue now survives through archive channels. In 2009 the mashupah99 channel posted “Gimme Lil Bouyon” and promised more bands of the era, Bushtown, Efex, First Serenade, RSB, PIK and Triple Kay, under one motto: taking bouyon to the world.

The “More Redeem” medley, billed as a 2001 party mix, is past 15,000 views, well ahead of the other songs. Those modest numbers mostly show that this repertoire still waits for a proper reissue, like a lot of carnival bouyon from that period (see Mas Domnik).

Frequently asked questions

What is Raw Redeem?

A Dominican bouyon band, also credited as Raw Redeem International, active in the early 2000s with Rah on vocals.

Who was in the band?

According to the Old Skool Session credits: Derrick Rah Peters, Daniel Phillip, Jeoffrey Joseph, Earlson Matthew, Alisha Ducreay and Delton Alfred.

What are its albums?

Es Zot La in 2000, Gimme Little Bouyon in 2001 and Old Skool Session in 2004, according to the archive video descriptions.

Where did the band record?

Old Skool Session was recorded at Bouyon Kitchen Studios, 90 Kennedy Avenue, Roseau, Dominica.

What is the link with WCK?

Rah, Earlson Matthew and Delton Alfred are tied to WCK, the band that created bouyon in 1988.
