Dominica: DOMDA urges early action on diabetes

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026

The President of the Dominica Diabetes Association, DOMDA, Antonia Samuel, is calling for stronger action against diabetes in Dominica. Ahead of World Diabetes Day on November 14, she is asking for more prevention, more screening and a national chronic disease registry.

A world day, a clear theme

Dominica joins the international community in observing World Diabetes Awareness Month and World Diabetes Day on November 14. This year's International Diabetes Federation theme is Early Action on Diabetes.

The theme places emphasis on prevention, understanding risk factors, timely diagnosis and targeted health education, above all among primary and secondary school students. For DOMDA, early intervention is essential to protecting lives and improving the long-term health of Dominicans.

DOMDA's figures

DOMDA cites an adult diabetes prevalence rate of 12.9 per cent. According to the figures in its statement, an estimated 7,000 to 10,000 people in Dominica are living with diabetes, while approximately 24.5 per cent of them remain undiagnosed.

Diabetes and hypertension continue to feature among the island's leading causes of death and disability, according to the association's president. The impact extends beyond statistics: it reaches families, workplaces and communities.

Act early, above all with young people

DOMDA remains committed to supporting people already diagnosed with diabetes, while placing greater emphasis on prevention among younger people through 2027. Its initiatives include public education campaigns, free community screenings and peer-support programmes such as Buddy Champions.

The association also aims to reduce complications linked to diabetes, including damage to the eyes and amputations. It encourages people living with diabetes to view their diagnosis as a reason to take proactive steps rather than a barrier to their future, and it urges the public to eat more healthily, stay physically active, monitor their health and support one another.

A national chronic disease registry

Antonia Samuel is appealing to the Government of Dominica to introduce a mandatory National Chronic Disease Registry Policy, building on existing public health measures such as the Sugar-Sweetened Beverage Tax.

The proposed system would be legally supported and applied across primary health centres, to standardise the recording and monitoring of people diagnosed with diabetes and hypertension. According to DOMDA, such a framework could improve continuity of access to essential medication, strengthen screening for complications and help reduce gaps in patient care. The association also offers to work with the Ministry of Health, the Health Promotion Unit and the Ministry of Education.

Frequently asked questions

When is World Diabetes Day?

On November 14, within World Diabetes Awareness Month. The 2026 International Diabetes Federation theme is Early Action on Diabetes.

How many Dominicans live with diabetes?

An estimated 7,000 to 10,000 people, according to the figures cited by DOMDA, with an adult prevalence rate of 12.9 per cent and about 24.5 per cent of cases undiagnosed.

What is DOMDA asking the government for?

A mandatory National Chronic Disease Registry Policy, legally supported and applied in primary health centres, to better track diabetes and hypertension, building on the Sugar-Sweetened Beverage Tax.
