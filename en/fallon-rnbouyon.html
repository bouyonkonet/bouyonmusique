Fallon and RnBouyon: softness wired into bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 25, 2026

RnBouyon is spelled exactly so, and it is the name Fallon gives to her fusion of R&B and bouyon. In 2026, Deezer chose her for its Deezer Next programme, built to support emerging artists for a year. This page gathers what the published sources verify about Fallon and about the branch she embodies.

On this page
  1. What Deezer says about Fallon
  2. RnBouyon, a branch named by its artist
  3. What the sources do not say
  4. Why Fallon matters for bouyon
  5. Frequently asked questions

What Deezer says about Fallon

The Deezer release, dated Paris 4 January 2026 and published on 4 February 2026, presents Fallon at the crossing of her Caribbean heritage and a contemporary sensibility. Deezer names two tracks as audible proof of her writing: Normal and Memories.

The official Deezer blog, The Backstage, carries the same presentation for the Deezer Next 2026 selection: Fallon appears alongside Timar and Heroe, with support announced in the app, on social media and in concert through the year.

RnBouyon, a branch named by its artist

On this site, the RnBouyon page sets the rule of the branch: the name comes from the artists and platforms that use it, not from a manifesto. Fallon is the name Deezer attaches to this fusion, between melodic singing and Caribbean party rhythms.

RnBouyon does not replace the band bouyon born in Dominica: it is a recent branch of it, built for voices, where the R&B melody steps forward without cutting the rhythm engine. That tension between softness and energy is what Deezer highlights in its portrait.

What the sources do not say

No opened source gives Fallon island of origin, her full name, or the release dates of Normal and Memories. Her profile on this site already says it openly: the file is thin and this page does not inflate it.

Deezer presents her with Caribbean roots and inside a selection of artists from France. Until a primary source names her island, this page keeps that wording, attributed to Deezer.

Why Fallon matters for bouyon

When the biggest French streaming platform picks an RnBouyon artist for its development programme, the branch gains a showcase that riddims alone do not offer. Fallon becomes the most visible face of RnBouyon for the French public.

Her path is followed on this site in her artist profile, and the branch in the RnBouyon page. Both pages will be completed when primary sources publish the full dates and credits of her tracks.
