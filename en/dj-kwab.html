DJ KWAB: a producer who posts nothing but bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DJ KWAB is a producer whose YouTube channel fits into one motto, “Long Live Bouyon”, and one visible rule: each of its 35 videos carries the word bouyon in its title. Since October 2024 he has posted riddims there, most offered free on condition of credit, a few marked as sold, and bouyon remixes of well known tracks, from Busta Rhymes to Shenseea. On the vocal side, his clearest credit is the production of Shashabim's “Believe That”, in March 2026. His contact address ends in 971, the Guadeloupe code: it is the only origin clue in the file, and this profile gives it as such.

A channel of bouyon riddims

The channel's first video, “DJ KWAB X RISK - BBC (Bouyon Riddim)”, dates from October 4, 2024. From February 2025 on, DJ KWAB uses a fixed format: a short name in quotes, the words Bouyon Instrumental, the riddim year and the signature Prod. by DJ KWAB. “CHO”, on February 12, 2025, opens the series, followed among others by “Lenmou”, “Chichi”, “Ambians”, “Kòké”, “Soucré sa”, “Doudou” and “Bikini”. The 2026 titles keep the same Creole and party vocabulary: “Chéwi”, “Kann Sik”, “Bokit”, “Brassé”, “Koud’kal”, “Monss”, up to “Zoom”, posted on August 13, 2026.

The model is that of the online beatmaker: listings state that the instrumental is free provided the producer is credited, or that it has been sold, like “Chéwi” or “She Like Dat”. The channel also adds bouyon remixes of international tracks, Busta Rhymes' “Break Ya Neck” in March 2025, Shenseea's “Hit & Run”, “Shake It To The Max (Fly)” in September 2025. For the principle of one instrumental shared by several voices, see the bouyon riddim page, and for the use of instrumentals online, the bouyon instrumental page.

Believe That and the KWAB-DJ credits

On March 27, 2026, the video for Shashabim's “Believe That”, dated Bouyon 2026, credits its production to @KWAB-DJ, a mention that leads straight to the DJ KWAB channel, and the page also carries the tag dj kwab. It is the clearest proof that his riddims leave the channel to become songs.

The same spelling, KWAB-DJ, also appears as a riddim credit on “I Cheating” by NFL Lil Brent, a bouyon track already documented on this site. No opened page states outright that both credits name the same producer: this profile points out the matching name without turning it into a fact.

Limits of the file

No opened page gives a legal name, a biography or a birthplace for DJ KWAB. The only origin signal is the contact address djkwab971 and the keyword instrumental bouyon gwada, and this profile does not treat that as a civil record. The videos are instrumentals: their audience figures, from a few hundred to more than 11,000 views on the listings opened, measure interest from beatmakers and singers, not from a radio audience.

For other producers in the genre, see the bouyon producers page and the bouyon gwada page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ KWAB?

A bouyon riddim producer whose YouTube channel, with the motto Long Live Bouyon, has posted bouyon instrumentals and remixes since October 2024. He produced Shashabim's Believe That in 2026. No public biography was found.

Are DJ KWAB's instrumentals free?

Most are posted as free on condition of crediting Prod. by DJ KWAB; some, such as Chéwi or She Like Dat, are marked as sold. The exact terms are those stated on each listing.

Which song uses a DJ KWAB production?

Shashabim's Believe That, a video posted on March 27, 2026 and dated Bouyon 2026, credits its production to @KWAB-DJ, the handle of the DJ KWAB channel.

Are DJ KWAB and KWAB-DJ the same person?

KWAB-DJ is the YouTube handle of the DJ KWAB channel. The same spelling appears as a riddim credit on NFL Lil Brent's I Cheating, but no opened page confirms that this credit names the same producer.

Where is DJ KWAB from?

His origin is not documented. His contact address ends in 971, the Guadeloupe code, and one keyword on his listings names bouyon gwada: those are clues, not a civil record.
