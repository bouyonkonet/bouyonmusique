Konshens: the Jamaican dancehall star and his bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Konshens is a Jamaican dancehall singer, born Garfield Delano Spence. He is not a bouyon artist: his career was built on “Pon Di Corner”, “Gal Dem a Talk” and “Bruk Off Yuh Back”. He has, however, sung on bouyon twice: in 2022 on the Mele' Riddim by Teamfoxx, then in 2026 on “Stand Up”, with Dominica's Khallion and DeeArr.

A dancehall name, not a bouyon one

Garfield Spence first sang in a duo with his brother Delus, under the name SoJah. Solo, “Pon Di Corner” became a hit in Japan in 2005 and earned him a month long tour there. He launched his Subkonshus label in 2010, released the album “Mental Maintenance” in 2012, then “Bruk Off Yuh Back” in 2016, remixed the next year with Chris Brown.

He has crossed into soca before, all the way to an album called “Soca Virgin” in 2020. That still does not make him a bouyon artist. This profile keeps two songs whose official pages talk about bouyon, and only those.

Manager (Best Whine), on the Mele' Riddim

In June 2022, the producer Teamfoxx released the Mele' Riddim, a 150 bpm riddim built with two Guadeloupe DJ producers, Dj Wiwi and Dj Luchshiy. Konshens sings “Manager (Best Whine)” on it, written under his given name. The riddim's notes say it plainly: this was the first time the Jamaican star voiced a bouyon and dennery rhythm.

The same riddim carries Blackboy, Army Guy and Motto, the Saint Lucia artist. The Teamfoxx upload is titled “2022 Dennery Bouyon Soca”. The song stayed quiet online, under 2,000 views per upload, but it is the first documented meeting between Konshens and bouyon.

Stand Up, with Khallion and DeeArr

On April 27, 2026 came “Stand Up”, in a Raw version and a Radio Edit, on the label of Boogy Rankss, who produced the riddim. Deezer credits four names: Konshens, Khallion, DeeArr and Boogy Rankss. The writing credit reads Garfield Delano Spence, Bertist Jeffers and Donnel Romain.

The video went up the next day on Khallion's channel, shot by Obanga Muzik and edited by Bertist Jeffers, tagged #bouyon. It has passed 34,000 views. It is Konshens' most watched bouyon song, and one of the most visible credits yet for the young Dominica scene.

Frequently asked questions

Is Konshens a bouyon artist?

No. He is a Jamaican dancehall singer. He has, however, sung on two bouyon productions: Manager (Best Whine) in 2022 and Stand Up in 2026.

What is his real name?

Garfield Delano Spence, a name that also appears in the writing credits of Stand Up and Manager (Best Whine).

What is Stand Up?

A single released on April 27, 2026 on Boogy Rankss Entertainment, with Khallion and DeeArr, produced by Boogy Rankss. The video, posted by Khallion, is tagged bouyon.

What is the Mele' Riddim?

A 2022 riddim produced by Teamfoxx with Dj Wiwi and Dj Luchshiy, presented as a bouyon and dennery rhythm. Konshens sings Manager (Best Whine) on it.

Which bouyon artists has he worked with?

Khallion, DeeArr and Boogy Rankss on Stand Up, and the Teamfoxx team with Dj Wiwi and Dj Luchshiy on the Mele' Riddim.
