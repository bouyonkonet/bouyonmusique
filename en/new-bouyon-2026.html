New bouyon 2026: the year in progress

By Smokiller · Published October 2, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

2026 is a full year for bouyon: new collaborations between Dominica and the French Caribbean, the Producer Session series at Bouyon Shattaz, and a World Creole Music Festival in Roseau in October that draws every eye.

The standout releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz

Joli BB, the collaboration of Reo and Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released in October 2026 on the Mauve label. FIX UP by G’z Up. The track Tarif by Koko Bwa with Denmath, whose management approached Bouyon Shattaz for a feature. And the bouyon remixes of Smokiller, including Heaven Baby, available for download on Smokiller.com.

What is coming

The countdown to WCMF 2026, from October 23 to 25 in Roseau, structures the end of the year: Dominican artists play their international visibility there. This page and the bouyon playlist are updated as the releases come out.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I listen to bouyon online?

On the Bouyon Road Mix by Bouyon Shattaz, a playlist on Spotify and YouTube that collects new bouyon releases and carnival classics. You can reach it from this site's bouyon playlist page, which is updated, like this page, as the 2026 releases come out.

How do I get the bouyon playlist?

Follow @bouyonshattaz on Instagram and comment ROAD MIX on a post: the playlist is sent to your DMs. It is the Bouyon Road Mix, which gathers the year's new bouyon releases and carnival classics, and it is also available directly on Spotify and YouTube.

What are the new bouyon releases of 2026?

Among the releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz in 2026: Joli BB, the collaboration of Reo and Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released in October on the Mauve label; FIX UP by G'z Up; Tarif by Koko Bwa with Denmath; and the bouyon remixes of Smokiller, including Heaven Baby, available for download on Smokiller.com.

Who sings Joli BB?

Joli BB is a collaboration between Dominican artist Reo and Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released in October 2026 on the Mauve label. The track illustrates the new collaborations between Dominica and the French Caribbean that mark the 2026 bouyon year. See also the Reo page.

When is WCMF 2026?

The World Creole Music Festival 2026 takes place from October 23 to 25 in Roseau, Dominica. Its countdown structures the end of the bouyon year: Dominican artists play their international visibility there. This page and the bouyon playlist are updated as the year's releases come out.
