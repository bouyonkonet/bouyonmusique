Frequently asked questions

What are the new bouyon releases of 2026?

Among the releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz in 2026: Joli BB, the collaboration of Reo and Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released on October 9 on the Mauve label; FIX UP by G'z Up; Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money, produced by Smokiller; and Smokiller's bouyon remixes, including Heaven Baby, available for download on Smokiller.com.

Who sings Joli BB?

Joli BB is a collaboration between Dominican artist Reo and Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released in October 2026 on the Mauve label. The track illustrates the new collaborations between Dominica and the French Caribbean that mark the 2026 bouyon year. See also the Reo page.

When is WCMF 2026?

The World Creole Music Festival 2026 takes place from October 23 to 25 in Roseau, Dominica. Its countdown structures the end of the bouyon year: Dominican artists play their international visibility there. This page and the bouyon playlist are updated as the year's releases come out.

Where can I listen to bouyon online?

On the Bouyon Road Mix by Bouyon Shattaz, a playlist on Spotify and YouTube that collects new bouyon releases and carnival classics. You can reach it from this site's bouyon playlist page, which is updated, like this page, as the 2026 releases come out.

How do I get the bouyon playlist?

Follow @bouyonshattaz on Instagram and comment ROAD MIX on a post: the playlist is sent to your DMs. It is the Bouyon Road Mix, which gathers the year's new bouyon releases and carnival classics, and it is also available directly on Spotify and YouTube.

Who wrote Joli BB?

Joli BB is written by Maurane Voyer, Dario Yankey and Nicolas Lanclume. Dario Yankey is the real name of Reo, who sings the track with the Martinican singer. Released on October 9, 2026 on the Mauve label, the song runs 2 minutes 43 seconds. See the Maurane Voyer profile.

Which bouyon remix blew up on TikTok in 2026?

Smokiller's bouyon flip of Coi Leray's "Nothing Into Something". Published on September 20, 2026, it showed 854.7K views and 29.9K likes on TikTok according to screenshots Smokiller shared on October 19, 2026. Coi Leray reshared it, and Dominica News Online wrote an article on the story.

What is the Heaven Baby remix?

It is the Bouyon Shattaz remix of Ayra Starr's "Heaven Baby", made by Smokiller and offered as a free M4A download on smokiller.com. It anchors a thirty-day Instagram campaign for the @bouyonshattaz account, live from October 19 to November 16, 2026: the music itself drives the media outlet's growth.

When was Tarif by Koko Bwa released?

Tarif came out in April 2025: it is the first single by Martinican artist Koko Bwa, featuring Denmath, with Boutcha Bwa. The track entered Bouyon Shattaz coverage in October 2026, when Denmath's management proposed a feature that Smokiller accepted. See the Denmath page.

Which other bouyon tracks came out in 2026?

This site's files include "You Can Do What You Want" by DJ MJ with Gwada G, released on January 24, 2026 at 160 BPM and produced by ThaWizzard, "Back Up Your Chat" by Ebony Empress as a remix with Foxy Jael, "Largent" by General Pye with Ebony Empress, presented as Bouyon 2026 and produced by Fr3EzE, and Ebony Empress's "Bounce 2D Riddim".

What is the Producer Session series?

Producer Session is a Bouyon Shattaz series devoted to the producers who make the sound. Its pilot, Producer Session 01, starts from MB On The Track's Rock Bouyon reel published on October 16, 2026, reframed in vertical format with captions in French and English. The draft is in review, awaiting Smokiller's decision.
