Qwann: the hidden name behind Ouba, bouyon, Guadeloupe

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Qwann is a name you meet by reading the small print of modern bouyon, not by reading a biography. No detailed artist page, no press portrait and no verified official account was found during the research for this profile. What does exist is precise and checkable: Qwann is featured on “1er Feu” and on “Uhdahdah #2” by Lucky lukee, alongside Binksdrick, Miimii, Dinho and 1T1, and he is credited as a composer on “Ouba”, the record by 1t1 and Theomaa. This profile tells only what those credits support, explains how a name can matter inside a scene without having a page of its own, and states clearly what the public record does not yet know.

A name with no biography: what the public record actually holds

Start with the state of the record, because it is part of the story. For many bouyon artists of the new generation, you can find a date of birth, a home town, interviews and verified social accounts. For Qwann, none of that was confirmed. The searches carried out for this profile, across search engines, streaming and music identification platforms and the pages of the artists around him, surfaced no published biography under this name, no real name and no birthplace or home base documented by a reliable source.

That gap does not mean Qwann does not exist musically. It means his public existence runs through other people work, and work done with others: collective records, guest credits and one composer credit. In French Antilles bouyon, that mode of existence is common. Singles come out as crew records, names rotate from one title to the next, and an artist can be present in the records everyone dances to before having any page under his own name.

This profile therefore follows a strict rule: every statement comes from a published credit the reader can find again. When a record names Qwann as a feature, this profile says feature. When a platform names him as a composer, this profile says composer credit, without turning that credit into something else. And when a fact is missing, for example his personal origin, this profile writes it plainly instead of deducing it from the Guadeloupe scene of his collaborators. The scene is documented, the personal origin is not: the two are not merged here.

“1er Feu” in 2024: the first collective card

Qwann first solid credit appears in 2024 on “1er Feu”, a Lucky lukee single gathering Binksdrick, Miimii, Qwann and Dinho. Lucky lukee artist page on Shazam lists the record among his 2024 singles, and also ranks it among his most identified tracks on the platform, behind “An Sou”. In other words, Qwann first durable trace is not a solo record: it is a family photo of the new wave, and his name is printed on it in full, on the same level as the other guests.

Look at who holds that poster. Lucky lukee is the collaboration artist of this generation, the one whose whole catalogue runs on rotating crews, as his page on this site tells it: Lucky lukee. Miimii is the voice who would become Miimii KDS. Binksdrick and Dinho are two more recurring names in Guadeloupe collective records, with their own pages, Binksdrick and Dinho, on this site. To be featured on “1er Feu” is therefore to step into the hard core of modern bouyon gwada, the world of short, fast singles built for dancing.

The title itself states the programme: a first fire, a collective ignition. This profile does not claim to know which part of the record belongs to whom, because no published verse by verse credits exist. It keeps the checkable fact: in 2024, on one of Lucky lukee most identified records, Qwann is a credited guest, not a name slipped in quietly. For an artist with no biography, that kind of credit works as a first musical identity card.

“Uhdahdah #2” in 2025: mixes and radio take over

The second credit arrives in 2025 with “Uhdahdah #2”, a Lucky lukee single featuring 1T1 and Qwann, listed as such on Lucky lukee Shazam page and carried among the highlighted tracks around 1t1. The presence of 1t1 changes the scale of the record: 1t1 is the Guadeloupe producer and artist whose records, such as “Bouwéy” with Theomaa, carried bouyon gwada far beyond the Antilles. Sharing a title with him, even in second billing, places Qwann in the main circuit of his scene.

The life of this record can also be read in the mixes. The tracklist of Digga D Sewo Jam mini mix, published on podcast platforms, lists “Uhdahdah” by Lucky Lukee, 1T1 and Qwann at position 51, inside a selection lining up Asa Bantan, Signal Band, Trilla G and Miimii KDS. A broadcast log for the radio station KoolFm Tropikal, readable online, also shows “Uhdahdah” by Lucky lukee with 1T1 and Qwann played on air. Those two traces tell the same reality: Qwann record travelled where bouyon travels first, with DJs and on radio, before any press coverage.

One confusion kept alive by listings has to be cleared here. “Uhdahdah” is not “Uhuu dah dah”. That second record, close in name, is a 1t1 single with GOMKO, Luky and Theomaa, released on 24 March 2023, and Qwann is not credited on it. The two titles share an onomatopoeia and part of the same circle, but their posters are different and this profile does not merge them. The point may look fussy: it is in fact the difference between a verified discography and an inflated one.

“Ouba”: the composer credit the feature listings do not show

Qwann most interesting credit is not a feature. On the Deezer page of the Shatta Bouyon Mix 2026 playlist, the track “Ouba” by 1t1, running 2 minutes 37, lists its composers: 1t1, Theomaa and Qwann. The platforms present “Ouba” as a 1t1 and Theomaa record as performers. Qwann is therefore not on the poster as a guest singer: he is in the writing and composition credits, the place where the melody and the structure of the record are made.

That distinction matters. A feature can be seen, a composer credit has to be looked for. You have to open the full track sheet, read the composer line and compare it with the poster. When you do that work, Qwann appears on one of 1t1 records in rotation, alongside the two names carrying the track. That does not make him the sole author of “Ouba”, and this profile does not claim it: the credit is shared between three named composers. But it does make him a documented participant in the making of a major record of his scene, not just a passing voice.

That credit also casts light on the other two. An artist featured on Lucky lukee crew records in 2024 and 2025, and credited as a composer for 1t1, holds two different positions in the same economy: the voice people invite, and the pen or melody people bring in. Modern bouyon works a lot that way, through crossed contributions that posters sum up badly. Qwann record, thin as it is, draws exactly that profile of a studio and crew man, present in the credits more than in the interviews.

The scene around Qwann: bouyon gwada and its collective records

To place Qwann, you have to place his circuit. His three verified credits put him in the Guadeloupe music world of bouyon gwada, the Antillean branch of the bouyon born in Dominica, now the sound of parties and carnivals across the archipelago, as our bouyon gwada page details. His partners are the central names of that scene: 1t1 on production and vocals, Theomaa beside him, Lucky lukee as crew leader of the collective records, Miimii KDS, Binksdrick and Dinho as recurring voices.

In that economy, an artist value is not measured only by solo records, but by presence in the network: who invites him, who credits him, on which titles his name keeps returning. Measured that way, Qwann public path is short but coherent: 2024, the entry through “1er Feu”; 2025, the confirmation through “Uhdahdah #2” alongside 1t1; and, in parallel, the composition credit on “Ouba”. Three lines, three complementary roles, and no weak or anecdotal title in the set: each time, these are records carried by the headline names of his generation.

This profile does not turn that network into a biography. It does not say where Qwann was born, where he lives, or since when he has been singing, because no open source consulted says so. It credits him with no full alias, no official crew and no label. If Qwann one day releases a record under his name alone, or an official page, the reading of his path will change: his current credits will then become the apprenticeship years of a solo artist. Until then, they form an honest portrait on their own: a name the credits already know, and that the biographies have not caught up with yet.

How to hear Qwann, and what this profile is still waiting for

The most direct listening starts with the three verified records, in credit order. First “1er Feu” by Lucky lukee, to hear Qwann in the 2024 collective format, with Binksdrick, Miimii and Dinho. Then “Uhdahdah #2”, still with Lucky lukee, for the guest pairing with 1t1 in 2025. Finally “Ouba” by 1t1 and Theomaa, knowing this time that Qwann is credited among its composers: listening changes when you know who helped make the record, not only who sings it.

To go wider, our bouyon playlist follows this new wave record by record, and the pages of his partners extend the path: 1t1, Lucky lukee and Miimii KDS each document part of the same network. Any future Qwann news, if his credits keep coming out under other people names before his own, will also pass through those pages.

Finally, this profile states what it needs to be completed: a record released under Qwann name alone, an official artist page on a platform, or a verified account. None of those elements was confirmed during the research, so this page links to no social profile and announces no project. If one of those elements appears, this profile will be completed, dated and corrected like every page on this site. In the meantime, the credits speak, and they already say something rare: in a fast moving scene, Qwann is one of the names that composers and crew leaders write onto their records.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Qwann?

Qwann is an artist in the bouyon gwada circuit known only through his published credits. He is featured on “1er Feu” (2024) and “Uhdahdah #2” (2025) by Lucky lukee, and credited as a composer on “Ouba” by 1t1 and Theomaa. No official biography, real name or personal origin was found during the research for this profile, which states that openly.

Does Qwann have any solo records?

No record released under Qwann’s name alone was found on the platforms consulted for this profile. His three verified credits are collaborations: two features with Lucky lukee and one composition credit with 1t1. If a solo record exists outside the main platforms, it is not documented in the open sources of this file.

Is Qwann only a guest voice on other people’s records?

No, and that is the point most listings miss. On “Ouba” by 1t1 and Theomaa, the Deezer track sheet lists its composers as 1t1, Theomaa and Qwann. Qwann is therefore credited with writing and composition, while not appearing on the poster as a performer. His file holds a studio role, not only features.

Are “Uhdahdah” and “Uhuu dah dah” the same record?

No. “Uhdahdah #2” is a Lucky lukee single featuring 1T1 and Qwann, released in 2025, preceded by a version listed in mixes as “Uhdahdah”. “Uhuu dah dah” is a different single, by 1t1 with GOMKO, Luky and Theomaa, released on 24 March 2023, with no credit for Qwann. The names look alike, the posters and dates keep the two records apart.

Who does Qwann collaborate with?

His verified credits place him in the crew of Lucky lukee, with Binksdrick, Miimii, who became Miimii KDS, and Dinho on “1er Feu”, then with 1t1 on “Uhdahdah #2”, and with 1t1 and Theomaa as fellow composers on “Ouba”. All of them are central names of the collective records of bouyon gwada.

Where should I start listening to Qwann?

With his three verified credits, in order: “1er Feu” and then “Uhdahdah #2” by Lucky lukee, and “Ouba” by 1t1 and Theomaa, where he is a credited composer. Listening changes once you know who helped build the track. To place those records inside their scene, then hear our bouyon playlist.

Who composed “Ouba” by 1t1 and Theomaa?

According to the track’s Deezer sheet, in the Shatta Bouyon Mix 2026 playlist, “Ouba” is composed by 1t1, Theomaa and Qwann. The platforms present this 2 minute 37 track as a 1t1 and Theomaa record on the performing side. The credit is shared between three named composers: Qwann is not its sole author, but a documented participant in its making.

What is Lucky lukee’s “1er Feu”?

“1er Feu” is a 2024 Lucky lukee single that brings together Binksdrick, Miimii, Qwann and Dinho. Lucky lukee’s Shazam artist page ranks it among his most identified songs, behind “An Sou”. It is Qwann’s first solid credit, featured on the same level as the other voices, in a family photo of the new wave of bouyon gwada.

Where has “Uhdahdah” with Qwann been played?

In mixes and on radio. Digga D’s Sewo Jam mini mix, published on podcast platforms, lists “Uhdahdah” by Lucky Lukee, 1T1 and Qwann at position 51, among Asa Bantan, Signal Band, Trilla G and Miimii KDS. A KoolFm Tropikal radio play log also shows the record on air, before any press coverage.
