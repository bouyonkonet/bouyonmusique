Starrin: Stop Call My Name, Mother In Law and bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Starrin, also found spelled Starring, almost always turns up next to Inches and the 4RL crew. Since 2021 the pair have put out songs billed as bouyon, most of them low key. The most played, “Mother In Law”, came out on DJ Taffy's channel with Trema.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

From Bomb to 4RL DogLife

The first trace dates to July 18, 2021: “Bomb”, by Starring with Inches, billed Bouyon 2021 and produced by Muss Buss Productions. The page links to Inches's Instagram, inches4rl, and uses the hashtag 4RoadLinks.

In June 2024, DjeasyPromos carried “4RL DogLife Remix”, by Starring with Inches, tagged bouyon. On Deezer the song came out in October that year under the Inches4RL name, with Starring as guest.

Mother In Law, with Trema

On November 12, 2025, DJ Taffy posted “Mother In Law”, by Trema, Starring and Inches, billed Bouyon 2026. The page gives no credits, but the song has passed 14,000 views, far ahead of anything else the trio has posted.

Deezer dates the single a day earlier, November 11, 2025, again filed under Inches4RL. Three months later, on February 14, 2026, the Inches4RL channel posted “H.I.R.I.T”, another duet with Starrin billed Bouyon 2026.

Stop Call My Name

On June 19, 2026, the Turnt Up Tv channel posted the video for “Stop Call My Name”, billed Bouyon 2026. Starrin and Inches wrote and perform it, and Starrin is the executive producer. The riddim is credited to ThaWizZarD, the recording to 4RL Studios.

Irv “Oozy” Antoine mixed and mastered it, Quecie Linton shot the footage and Dwayne Skerrit edited it. The video is close to 3,000 views. None of the pages opened names Starrin's home island or legal name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Starrin?

A bouyon artist, also credited as Starring, who almost always sings with Inches and the 4RL crew. No source opened gives a legal name or island.

What are Starrin's bouyon songs?

Bomb (Bouyon 2021), 4RL DogLife Remix (2024), Mother In Law with Trema, H.I.R.I.T and Stop Call My Name (Bouyon 2026), all with Inches.

What is Starrin's most watched song?

Mother In Law, by Trema, Starring and Inches, posted on November 12, 2025 by DJ Taffy, at over 14,000 views.

Who produced the riddim for Stop Call My Name?

ThaWizZarD, according to the video credits; the song was recorded at 4RL Studios and mixed by Irv “Oozy” Antoine.

Starrin or Starring?

Both spellings point to the same person: every page pairs the name with Inches and the 4RL circle.
