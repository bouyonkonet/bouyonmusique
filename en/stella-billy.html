Energy, on the Okra Slime Riddim

The full title as published reads “Stella Billy - Energy ( Okra Slime Riddm ) Official Audio [ Barbados Bouyon 2026 ]”, with riddim spelled without its i. On the BimTraveler channel the description gives the credits in three lines: written by Stella Billy, produced by 10Star Records, mixed and mastered by 10Star. The tags at the bottom are stellabilly, bouyon and cropover. On the artist’s own channel the same upload keeps the title word for word and adds the soca and cropover tags.

The Okra Slime Riddim is a shared instrumental, in the bouyon riddim tradition where several voices take turns on one backing. The BimTraveler channel also files Sharkie’s “Okra Slime” and Ian Stylez’s “Crazy Jump” under it; the latter is named here only from the channel’s video listing. Riddim and credits all point to 10Star Records as the common producer of the batch.

What Barbados Bouyon means here

The “Barbados Bouyon 2026” label and the cropover tag, named after Barbados’s big festival season, describe the music and its season. They mark a bouyon made for the Barbadian circuit, where soca and bouyon meet, part of the movement that carries the genre born in Dominica well beyond the island, as our page on the bouyon diaspora tells it.

Those words do not say where Stella Billy was born or lives. None of the opened pages gives that information, and this site infers nothing from them: the profile is about bouyon billed as Barbadian, not about a personal origin.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file is one title, two uploads, one writing credit and one producer. Stella Billy’s channel showed nine videos when opened, and only this one carries the word bouyon in its title. The others are not presented here as bouyon.

If new titles or a biography appear, this page will be updated and dated, for example in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026. Until then, the verified listen is “Energy”, on the Okra Slime Riddim, 2026 season.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Stella Billy?

Stella Billy is the credited artist and writer of “Energy”, on the Okra Slime Riddim, billed as Barbados Bouyon 2026 and produced by 10Star Records. No public biography was found during this research.

Which Stella Billy title is the verified bouyon one?

“Energy”, uploaded on April 6, 2026 on the BimTraveler channel and on April 12, 2026 on the artist’s channel, 2 minutes 42, with Barbados Bouyon 2026 in the title.

Who produced Energy?

The published description credits 10Star Records for production and 10Star for the mix and master.

Where is Stella Billy from?

No opened page names a birthplace or home base. The title bills the music as Barbados bouyon and ties it to Crop Over, and nothing more.

Who else is on the Okra Slime Riddim?

The BimTraveler channel also lists Sharkie with “Okra Slime” and Ian Stylez with “Crazy Jump” on the same riddim.
