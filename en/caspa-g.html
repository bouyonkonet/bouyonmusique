Caspa G: Vincy soca with a jab bouyon turn

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Caspa G is Zavique Morris, from Redemption Sharpes in St Vincent. He made his name in Vincy Mas soca with “Dutty Cup”, “Bad Boy Wine” and then “Rum Diaries” in 2019. In 2025, two of his songs were billed as bouyon: “Tight Wine” on Next Gen Records' Tequila Riddim and “Crashout” on TeamFoxx's Robbery Riddim.

Two bouyon songs in 2025

On May 2, 2025, Next Gen Records posted “Caspa G - Tight Wine [Tequila Riddim] | Bouyon 2025”. Caspa G wrote and performs it; production is by Dré Soundz and JJW Productions, with Josh “JJW Productions” Williams also on the mix. The video's hashtags point to St Vincent and jab jab. On Deezer, the Tequila Riddim album, dated April 29, opens with this track, spelled “Tight Whine”. He shares it with Rum Lawd, Kilogram, 101 Stunna, C4 De Crazyone and Deadly.

A month later, on June 1, TeamFoxx released “Crashout” on the Robbery Riddim, under a title that sums up the blend: “2025 Vincy-Lucian Jab Bouyon Soca”. Production is credited to Lashley Motto Winter, that is Motto, and the mix to Yannick Plante of Minor Productions. Dj Buzz B's riddim mix places “Crashout” right after Grabba Finesse, alongside Slatta, Moralis and Motto himself.

A Vincy Mas name since 2017

In May 2019, Searchlight reported that he had just won the “young trending soca artiste” title at the Vincy Mas launch, judged on crowd response. He was 23 and playing his third carnival. The prize list is worth a smile: ten cases of Hairoun products, two LIAT tickets, a phone, a KFC voucher and a goat.

In July the same paper placed him in Redemption Sharpes, raised in Rilland Hill, and ran through his songs: “You Think Ah Joke” in 2016, “Dutty Cup” in 2017, “Bad Boy Whine” and “Go Hard or Go Home” in 2018. That year he also took the Shell Down Boss title at Black Rave and reached the Soca Monarch final. “My plan is to take the crown,” he said. His biggest online hit is still “Rum Diaries”, produced by Lester “Mack” Iroha, past 445,000 views.

Recent collaborations

Caspa G works with others a lot. Back in 2019 he shared “Shut Down” with Skinny Fabulous on TeamFoxx's Jab Fete Riddim. In 2022 Island Shak Entertainment introduced him as “the Rude Boy Caspa G” on the Ghost Buster Riddim, and in April 2026 “Hello”, co-written with Dat-C DQ, passed 129,000 views on the same label.

On Deezer he released “Tarry” with Problem Child in June 2025, then appeared in 2026 on Problematic Media's Mas Class Riddim and on the Cholonx Riddim. Those recent releases are still billed soca; for bouyon, the two 2025 songs are the ones that count.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Caspa G?

Zavique Morris, a soca singer from Redemption Sharpes, St Vincent and the Grenadines, active at Vincy Mas since 2017.

What are his bouyon songs?

Tight Wine on Next Gen Records' Tequila Riddim (Bouyon 2025) and Crashout on TeamFoxx's Robbery Riddim (Jab Bouyon Soca 2025).

Who produced Crashout?

Lashley Motto Winter, that is Motto, with mixing and mastering by Yannick Plante of Minor Productions.

What is his most watched song?

Rum Diaries, his Vincy Mas 2019 soca song produced by Lester “Mack” Iroha, past 445,000 views.

What titles has he won?

In 2019, per Searchlight, the young trending soca artiste title at the Vincy Mas launch and the Shell Down Boss title at Black Rave.
