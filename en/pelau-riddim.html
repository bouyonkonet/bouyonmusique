Pelau Riddim: Boogy Rankss's 2023 bouyon riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Pelau Riddim is a riddim produced by Boogy Rankss and released in March 2023 on his own label, Boogy Rankss Entertainment. Six vocal tracks came with it, all uploaded the same day to his YouTube channel, with Freezy as executive producer. The videos are billed "SOCA 2023", yet every one of them carries the bouyon hashtag, and the song that travelled furthest was "Balance" by Mata and Bronxx.

A Boogy Rankss riddim, six voices in one day

On March 20, 2023, the Boogy Rankss channel put up six videos at once, each titled "(PELAU RIDDIM) SOCA 2023". The descriptions share one block of credits: riddim produced by Boogy Rankss, executive produced by Freezy, mixed and mastered by Krome Productions. Only "Push Back", sung by Island Prince, names a different engineer, DJ Ky.

On Deezer, the "Pelau Riddim" album is dated March 21, 2023, on the Boogy Rankss Entertainment label, with seven tracks: the six vocals plus the instrumental. The instrumental distributed through DistroKid gives March 18, 2023 as its release date and attaches the name Freezy Madrass to the riddim. None of the sources consulted say which island the singers come from.

Bouyon, soca or dennery?

The riddim doesn't pick a side. The video titles say soca, but each one is tagged #bouyon, and five of the six add #dennerysegment, the name of Saint Lucia's fast genre. "Push Back" swaps that tag for #AntiguaSoca. This is the zone where the three sounds overlap, the one our bouyon vs soca page tries to untangle, alongside our dennery segment page.

DJs read it the same way. On March 29, 2023, DJ RJ Entertainment 758 posted "Bouyon Pelau Riddim Soca Mix 2023", tagged #BouyonSoca. On April 1, DJ Supreme758 followed with his own mix, filed this time under dennery segment, with a link back to the riddim's official playlist.

Balance, the song that carried the riddim

On the producer's channel, "Balance" by Mata and Bronxx had passed 13,000 views when we read it, well ahead of the other songs, which sit between roughly 1,000 and 3,300. The bigger number came from a repost: Jammin' Entertainment uploaded "Mata & Bronxx - Balance | Pelau Riddim 2023" on March 23, 2023, and it has more than 109,000 views.

Next in line on the Boogy Rankss channel is Kisha Kay's "Doh Post Me". Freezy, the executive producer, also sings on two tracks, "Party Party" with Zido and "Breed Dem" with Bronxx, and Zido comes back on "Move From Deh". For how one riddim ends up carrying this many songs, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Pelau Riddim?

Boogy Rankss, according to the March 2023 YouTube descriptions, with Freezy as executive producer and Krome Productions on mixing and mastering (DJ Ky on Push Back).

When did the Pelau Riddim come out?

The six vocal videos went up on March 20, 2023; the Deezer album is dated March 21, 2023, and the DistroKid instrumental lists March 18, 2023.

What label released it?

Boogy Rankss Entertainment, per Deezer and the rights line on the instrumental.

Who sings on the Pelau Riddim?

Mata, Bronxx, Freezy, Zido, Island Prince and Kisha Kay, across six songs: Balance, Breed Dem, Party Party, Move From Deh, Push Back and Doh Post Me.

Is it bouyon or soca?

The videos carry both labels: SOCA 2023 in the title, #bouyon as a hashtag, often next to #dennerysegment. A March 2023 DJ mix calls it bouyon soca.
