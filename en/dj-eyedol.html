DJ Eyedol: bouyon mixtapes from Saint Martin

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

DJ Eyedol, who also goes by “The Magic Touch”, is a DJ on the Saint Martin scene. He plays shatta and dancehall, but his most watched mixes are bouyon: the Bouyon Kings Mixtape in 2022, then Bouyon Cauldron and his live sets in SXM in 2025.

Bouyon Kings Mixtape, 2022

In March 2022 the Guadeloupe label Vadore Concept Groupe released the “Bouyon Kings Mixtape”, with DJ Eyedol on the decks. The idea: 26 songs by different artists, all on the Trouble Beat riddim by Litleboy. The video is past 105,000 views.

The tracklist is a good snapshot of bouyon at the time. It has Tedla with Leyou, “Bandit” by Holly G, “Godo Godo” by Fior 2 Bior, “Pa Fé Chichi” by Anonymous Gyal's, Lünik, Yellow Gaza and Miky Ding La.

Bouyon Cauldron, from SXM

In April 2025 he put out “Bouyon Cauldron Mixtape Vol. 1” on his channel, 42 minutes of nonstop bouyon “straight from SXM / Saint Martin”. It features 1T1, DJ MJ, BRG Hollywood, Reo, Ridge, Triple Kay, Pudaz, Aknose, Kenny G and King Kembe. The mix is past 111,000 views.

He slips in his long version of “Bouwéy”, the 1T1 song with Théomaa. Posted on its own, that extended edit is his most watched video, at more than 177,000 views, well above his full mixes.

Live sets, from District 721 to St Barts

In May 2025 he posted an 18-minute set recorded at District 721 in Saint Martin, with Speyenol on the mic: 1T1, BRG Hollywood, DJ MJ and Jixels in the selection. That set is past 100,000 views too. Another 2025 bouyon set was filmed in St Barts.

In 2026 he took the same pairing to Guadeloupe for a live “Best of Bouyon 2026”, while also putting out shatta and dancehall mixes “from Saint Martin to di world”. For the wider scene, see the bouyon in Saint Martin page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Eyedol?

A DJ on the Saint Martin scene who also goes by The Magic Touch and mixes bouyon, shatta and dancehall.

What is the Bouyon Kings Mixtape?

A 2022 mixtape released by Vadore Concept Groupe: 26 songs on Litleboy's Trouble Beat riddim, mixed by DJ Eyedol.

What is his most watched bouyon mix?

Bouyon Cauldron Mixtape Vol. 1 from April 2025, with more than 111,000 views; his extended edit of Bouwéy is past 177,000.

Where does he play?

His videos show him at District 721 in Saint Martin, in St Barts and, in 2026, in Guadeloupe.

Where is DJ Eyedol from?

His videos place him in Saint Martin, but no page consulted gives his birthplace.
