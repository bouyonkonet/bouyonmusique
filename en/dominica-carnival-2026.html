Dominica Carnival 2026: the guide

Published September 22, 2026

Dominica Carnival, Dominica’s carnival, is the destination for purists: a Creole carnival that has stayed authentic, in the country that invented cadence-lypso and bouyon. This guide sums up what you need to know for 2026.

Why you should watch Roseau

Because the musics the world dances to today, bouyon and its offshoots, are born here. Coming to Mas Domnik or following it online means seeing bouyon at the source, played by the bands and DJs who create the tracks of the year.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mas Domnik?

Mas Domnik is the Creole name of Dominica’s carnival. It keeps its old forms: chanté mas, lapo kabwit drum groups, traditional characters and sensays. Since the 1990s, bouyon has been its music: the tracks of the year are played by the bands, the sound systems and the fetes leading up to the two carnival days.

When is Dominica carnival?

Dominica carnival, Mas Domnik, takes place in Roseau before Lent, with its peak on carnival Monday and Tuesday. J’ouvert opens them at dawn, from 4 in the morning: it is the peak moment for bouyon and soca, when the sound systems and bands play the bouyon of the year.

Why does carnival matter so much for bouyon?

Because the genre was born there and renews itself there every year. Carnival season launches the competition between bouyon tracks: bands and producers release their new songs, the public picks its favorites, and the winners become the classics of the year. That is where artists build their reputation.

Which bouyon artists should I follow in 2026?

The Dominican artists in form, from Reo to Asa Bantan, and the behind-the-scenes told by Bouyon Shattaz. The Bouyon Road Mix gathers the tracks that matter as the season unfolds. Reo and Asa Bantan also play Night 2 of the World Creole Music Festival 2026, on Saturday, October 24 in Roseau.

What is the big bouyon festival?

The World Creole Music Festival is held every October in Roseau, Dominica, at the time of independence. The 2026 edition runs October 23 to 25 at Windsor Park: three nights where Dominica’s bouyon and cadence share the stage with zouk, kompa and guests from the diaspora.
