Dominica Carnival 2026: the guide

Published September 22, 2026

Dominica Carnival, Dominica’s carnival, is the destination for purists: a Creole carnival that has stayed authentic, in the country that invented cadence-lypso and bouyon. This guide sums up what you need to know for 2026.

Why you should watch Roseau

Because the musics the world dances to today, bouyon and its offshoots, are born here. Coming to Mas Domnik or following it online means seeing bouyon at the source, played by the bands and DJs who create the tracks of the year.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mas Domnik?

Mas Domnik is the Creole name of Dominica’s carnival. It keeps its old forms: chanté mas, lapo kabwit drum groups, traditional characters and sensays. Since the 1990s, bouyon has been its music: the tracks of the year are played by the bands, the sound systems and the fetes leading up to the two carnival days.

When is Dominica carnival?

Dominica carnival, Mas Domnik, takes place in Roseau before Lent, with its peak on carnival Monday and Tuesday: in 2026, February 16 and 17. J’ouvert opens them at dawn, from 4 in the morning: it is the peak moment for bouyon and soca, when the sound systems and bands play the bouyon of the year.

Why does carnival matter so much for bouyon?

Because the genre was born there and renews itself there every year. Carnival season launches the competition between bouyon tracks: bands and producers release their new songs, the public picks its favorites, and the winners become the classics of the year. That is where artists build their reputation.

Which bouyon artists should I follow in 2026?

The Dominican artists in form, from Reo to Asa Bantan, and the behind-the-scenes told by Bouyon Shattaz. The Bouyon Road Mix gathers the tracks that matter as the season unfolds. Reo and Asa Bantan also play Night 2 of the World Creole Music Festival 2026, on Saturday, October 24 in Roseau.

What is the big bouyon festival?

The World Creole Music Festival is held every October in Roseau, Dominica, at the time of independence. The 2026 edition runs October 23 to 25 at Windsor Park: three nights where Dominica’s bouyon and cadence share the stage with zouk, kompa and guests from the diaspora.

When was the 2026 carnival season launched?

On November 28, 2025, at The Factory, on Valley Road. That official launch of Mas Domnik 2026, hosted by the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority, programmed two live bands, TK Combo and Riddim Knockaz, plus sets by DJ MJ and MV on the Keys, DJ Taffy and Friends, DJ Sleem, DJ Snow and DJ Flip. See Riddim Knockaz.

Who won Dominica's 2026 Calypso Monarch?

Daddy Chess, real name Chester Letang. At the grand finale on Saturday February 14, 2026 at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, with ten calypsonians on stage, he won with “Beauty and the Beast” and “Roof Leaking”, ahead of Nacheal and Joy, the 2025 winner. It is his second crown, twenty-five years after his 2001 win. See Daddy Chess.

Which bouyon songs were playing during Carnival 2026?

The Bouyon Passport 2026 selection of the DJ Close Connections podcast, published on February 4, 2026, gives a snapshot: “Take Me As I Am” by Trilla G, “La Vie Doux” by Reo and Tehilah, “Dohtish Love” by Reo and Nessa Preppy, “No Love” by Ridge and J Lion, and records by Shelly, TK International, Faithii, Asa Bantan and Signal Band. See Trilla-G.

Who sings the “Bye to de Old Me” remix?

Shelly, Nice and Pudaz, according to Sokah2Soca, republished by Island Vybe on November 11, 2025, which presents it as cut for Carnival 2026. The original is a Signal Band record with Shelly, dated January 27, 2025. The remix credits name Sheldon Alfred, Miguel Hussey and Udi Prince as writers, and Dernel “Dernz” Green for composition and engineering. See Nice.

Who won at the January 2026 Dominica Music Awards?

Signal Band, with six awards, including video of the year for “Bye to the Old Me”, according to Black Island Girl's report. That second edition was held on January 3, 2026 in Portsmouth, in the middle of carnival season. Shelly took Songwriter and Male Artist of the Year, Pudaz was named Bouyon Artist of the Year and DJ MJ DJ of the Year. See Signal Band.

What is Bouyon Day?

It is an annual celebration devoted to bouyon, whose 2026 edition was held in February. According to DOM767, Cornell “Fingers” Phillip, the founding keyboardist of WCK, was a key figure there: he renewed his call for a writers camp to help lift the genre to a global competitive level, as a man who has long called for more structure and composition in bouyon. See Cornell Phillip.
