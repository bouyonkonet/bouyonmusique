Frequently asked questions

Which bouyon playlist should I listen to in 2026?

The reference playlist of this site is the Bouyon Road Mix by Bouyon Shattaz, titled Bouyon Music 2026 - Road Mix on Spotify and also available on YouTube. It brings together new releases from Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique, bouyon soca, the riddims of the moment and carnival classics. Both players are embedded directly on this page.

How do I receive the bouyon playlist by private message?

Follow @bouyonshattaz on Instagram and comment ROAD MIX under a post: the Bouyon Road Mix is sent to you directly by DM. It is the fastest way to never miss a new bouyon release, since the releases that launch a dance arrive first on the Bouyon Shattaz account. More about Bouyon Shattaz.

Which bouyon songs should I start with?

Start with the founding bands, WCK, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade, then move to recent songs: Life Nice by Asa Bantan, Joli BB by Reo with Maurane Voyer, FIX UP by G'z Up, Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money produced by Smokiller, and Mauvais Plan by HYPNO. The annotated selection is on the bouyon songs page.

Does the Road Mix include shatta too?

Yes. The Bouyon Road Mix mixes bouyon, bouyon soca and shatta bouyon, the meeting point between the riddim style of modern Antillean producers and the historic Dominican genre. For anyone looking for a shatta playlist, it is the simplest shortcut between the two worlds, keeping the Dominican origin of the sound at the center. See the shatta playlist page.

Where can I find new bouyon releases in 2026?

The Bouyon Road Mix is kept up to date by Bouyon Shattaz with recent releases: new riddims and new artists from Dominica and the French Antilles. To follow releases day by day, the Instagram account @bouyonshattaz publishes news about the genre in French and English. This year's selection: new bouyon 2026.
