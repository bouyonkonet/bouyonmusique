What is inside the Road Mix

New bouyon releases

The recent releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz: new riddims, new artists from Dominica and the French Antilles.

Bouyon soca

The bouyon and soca fusion that connects Roseau, Castries, Pointe-a-Pitre and Fort-de-France during carnival season.

Carnival classics

The songs that built the legend of the genre, from the WCK era to the anthems of the 1990s and 2000s.

How to receive the playlist by DM

On Instagram, follow @bouyonshattaz and comment ROAD MIX under a post: the Bouyon Road Mix is sent to you directly by private message. It is the fastest way to never miss a new bouyon release.

A playlist for every search

Whether you are searching for bouyon music, music bouyon, bouyon mix, bouyon riddim or a shatta playlist, the logic is the same: start from the origin, Dominica, then follow the sound where it lives today, in Gwada, Martinique, French Guiana and Paris.
