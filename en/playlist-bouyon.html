Which bouyon songs should I start with?

Start with the founding bands, WCK, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade, then move to recent songs: Life Nice by Asa Bantan, Joli BB by Reo with Maurane Voyer, FIX UP by G'z Up, Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money produced by Smokiller, and Mauvais Plan by HYPNO. The annotated selection is on the bouyon songs page.

Does the Road Mix include shatta too?

Yes. The Bouyon Road Mix mixes bouyon, bouyon soca and shatta bouyon, the meeting point between the riddim style of modern Antillean producers and the historic Dominican genre. For anyone looking for a shatta playlist, it is the simplest shortcut between the two worlds, keeping the Dominican origin of the sound at the center. See the shatta playlist page.

Which bouyon classics should I listen to?

The classics come from Dominica's founding bands, WCK, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade, then from the carnival songs of the 1990s and 2000s, when bouyon ruled the island. That is old school bouyon: a live band sound, with long songs built to last a whole night of partying. The Road Mix keeps these carnival classics next to the new releases. See old school bouyon.

Where can I listen to bouyon soca?

In the Bouyon Road Mix, which mixes bouyon, bouyon soca and new releases from the Antilles. To go back to the source, the references single out "Famalay" and "Conch Shell" as the genre's emblematic songs, two productions credited to Dada, the Dominican producer who launched bouyon soca in 2003 according to the testimony of Smokiller. At carnival, these songs travel from island to island. See bouyon soca.

Playlist or bouyon mix: what is the difference?

A bouyon mix chains songs without a break, at the same tempo, so the party never stops: it is the genre's natural format, inherited from carnival sound systems. A playlist like the Bouyon Road Mix plays song by song, but it is designed as a permanent mix of 2026 bouyon, with the rising songs, the classics and the riddims of the moment. See bouyon mix.

Where can I hear bouyon live?

In Dominica first. At Roseau's carnival, Mas Domnik, the songs of the year are played by the bands, the sound systems and at the fetes, all the way to J'ouvert, from 4 in the morning. In October, the World Creole Music Festival brings bouyon, cadence, zouk and kompa together in Roseau: the 2026 edition runs from 23 to 25 October at Windsor Park. See the World Creole Music Festival.

Which bouyon remixes should I listen to?

Those by Smokiller, the home of Bouyon Shattaz remixes: Nothing Into Something and Players by Coi Leray, Vulnerability by Shenseea and Heaven Baby by Ayra Starr. His flip of Nothing Into Something reached 854.7K views on TikTok as of 19 October 2026, and Coi Leray reshared it. The principle: keep the voice of a known hit and rebuild the rhythm as bouyon. See bouyon remix.
