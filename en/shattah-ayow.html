Shattah Ayow: Super Wine and the Super Bouyon Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Shattah Ayow, credited as Elson Gloster for songwriting, is a singer whose releases are billed “Vincy Soca”, the soca of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. His bouyon chapter comes down to one project: the Super Bouyon Riddim, produced by Kross Beatz and released on April 17, 2025, which he executive produced and where he sings “Super Wine”.

A busy soca catalogue

On Deezer his page lists 57 releases. The first ones date from September 2020, “X-Rated”, “Ba Dang Bang” and “Outside”, and the pace never drops after that: a steady stream of singles, plus several riddims released under his name, from the Pelt It Riddim and the Waistline Wuk Riddim in 2022 to the Justice League Riddim in 2023 and the Nightmares Riddim in March 2025.

The credits of “We Ah Vincy”, in May 2022, give his writing name, Elson “Shattah Ayow” Gloster, with production by DJ Shakur and Mega Watts Production. “Sweetest Wine”, the same year, is his most watched page among those read: past 20,000 views on the JulianspromosTV channel, produced by Ziggy Beatzzz. No opened source says where he was born or where he lives.

The Super Bouyon Riddim, his bouyon project

On April 17, 2025, his channel posted five songs under one banner: “SUPER BOUYON RIDDIM | VINCY SOCA 2025”. Every description gives the same credits: produced by Kross Beatz, executive produced by Shattah Ayow, mixed and mastered by Chemical X, artwork by Graflex. The Deezer album, dated the same day, lines up the five vocals with no instrumental.

Shattah Ayow opens the album with “Super Wine”, which he sings alone, two and a half minutes, a little over 3,500 views when read. The other songs go to guests: Flanka with “Want Ah Cat”, DJ 20 with “Pencil”, Ozarie with “For De Iron (Diane)” and D'termine with “Play Wid De Kitty”. The full rundown is on our Super Bouyon Riddim page.

A riddim publisher as much as a singer

The Super Bouyon Riddim is not a one-off: Shattah Ayow often puts out other people's riddims and brings back the same voices. D'termine is already there on “Hold Me Closer” in 2022, Ozarie on “Sweetness” in 2021, and DJ 20 on “Doggy Style” in 2024. A week after the Super Bouyon Riddim he released the Bad Vybz Gone Riddim, on the same 2388561 Records DK label.

For a bouyon reader, that is the point of interest: a Vincentian soca hand naming a whole riddim after the genre. The rest of his catalogue stays filed as soca, like “Need Ah Lickin” on the Silky Riddim in 2025. For how one riddim carries several voices, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shattah Ayow?

A soca singer credited as Elson Gloster for songwriting, whose releases are billed Vincy Soca, and the executive producer of the Super Bouyon Riddim (2025).

What is his bouyon song?

Super Wine, which he sings alone on the Super Bouyon Riddim, uploaded on April 17, 2025.

Who produced the Super Bouyon Riddim?

Kross Beatz, with Shattah Ayow as executive producer and Chemical X on mixing and mastering.

Who sings with him on that riddim?

Flanka, DJ 20, Ozarie and D'termine, one song each.

Where is Shattah Ayow from?

The sources consulted do not say. His releases are tagged Vincy Soca and one of his songs is called We Ah Vincy, but none states where he was born or lives.
