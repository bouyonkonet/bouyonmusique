DJ Boko: the DJ name that keeps returning in 2025 and 2026 bouyon titles

Published October 20, 2026

DJ Boko is a DJ from the French speaking bouyon circuit whose name returns, in 2025 and 2026, in the titles of one family of artists: TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE. Seven verifiable credits name him as a featured artist, from “ON ME” and “WET WET” with TiiTii NBA to “Bay li” and “SOUKWE LI” with DJ SOFTEE, through a collective freestyle and a record listed for 2026 with YSN, Edday and Jixels. This profile gathers those credits that can be checked on the platforms, explains what a DJ feature means in current bouyon, carefully separates this artist from his namesakes outside the Caribbean, then states what the public record does not yet contain.

Who DJ Boko is: a credits name, not yet a biography

DJ Boko has, in the sources verified for this profile, no public biography, no published full name, no confirmed place of origin and no official page this site can link without risk of error. What does exist is more precise and more useful than a rumour: a series of featured credits on dated commercial releases, published by two central artists of recent bouyon, TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE, and indexed in the same way by several independent platforms, Deezer, Shazam and Discogs.

The name is credited sometimes as DJ Boko, sometimes as DJ BOKO in capitals, sometimes as Dj Boko, depending on the typography of each platform and each title. This profile normalises it as DJ Boko in running text, and reproduces the titles as the platforms publish them when it quotes them. That spelling variation is ordinary in bouyon, where titles often carry bracketed tags such as BOUYON 2026 or Bouyon 2025, which date the wave a record belongs to more than they give a release date to the day.

Disambiguation is compulsory here. DJ Boko is also the stage name of DJs with no link to the Caribbean, including a Taiwan based DJ present on YouTube and Instagram with an R and B and hip hop repertoire, and a DJ Mc BoKo based in Bulgaria on SoundClick. This profile borrows nothing from them: no city, no accounts, no catalogue, no path. The DJ Boko on this page is only the one the bouyon credits of TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE name, in 2025 and 2026.

One question remains that credits alone do not settle: the exact role of DJ Boko on each record. In current bouyon, a DJ credited as featured artist may have supplied the riddim, part of the production, a hype voice, a tag or a set presence that is part of the record identity. The platform sheets consulted name the writers and composers record by record, and DJ Boko does not appear there as a credited writer on the records detailed here. This profile therefore presents him as a credited guest artist, without attributing to him a production the published credits do not give him.

The TiiTii NBA credits: ON ME, WET WET and SHE’S A HOE

Three of DJ Boko verified credits can be read with TiiTii NBA, a bouyon artist whose recent discography is documented in detail by Shazam and Deezer. “ON ME (feat. DJ BOKO) (BOUYON 2026)” appears there as a 2025 single, with a duration of 2 minutes 53 according to the Deezer playlist devoted to TiiTii NBA, with Terry Capitolin as writer and Allan Blombou as composer, the composer name spelling varying slightly from page to page. “WET WET (feat. DJ BOKO) (BOUYON 2026)”, also listed as a 2025 single, runs 2 minutes 13, with Terry Capitolin credited as writer and composer.

The third record, “SHE’S A HOE (feat. DJ BOKO) [Bouyon 2025]”, completes the series with the same artist. Shazam lists it among TiiTii NBA 2025 singles, and the Discogs index of 2025 soca releases carries it in the same form. Three features with one artist in one season are not a catalogue accident: they draw a sustained collaboration, where DJ Boko name is part of the formula of the bouyon records TiiTii NBA published for the 2025 and 2026 waves.

The BOUYON 2026 and Bouyon 2025 tags have to be read for what they are: series labels, widely used in this scene, which attach a record to the carnival season and to the playlists of the target year, even though the commercial release can land the previous year. The three records are therefore dated 2025 as singles in platform discographies, while carrying the 2026 tag for two of them. This profile gives both pieces of information, as the sources publish them, instead of choosing one single date that would erase the other.

None of these three records presents DJ Boko as lead artist. TiiTii NBA carries the releases, and Terry Capitolin, his recurring writer name in the Deezer credits, signs the writing. DJ Boko place is that of a recurring guest, regular enough to be named three times, which, in a discography as dense as TiiTii NBA output between 2023 and 2025, is the strongest public signal in his record.

The DJ SOFTEE credits: Bay li, SOUKWE LI, the freestyle and Jéré Sa

The other pole of the record is DJ SOFTEE, a DJ and producer at the centre of the same network, whose album “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”, released in 2025, was opened directly for this profile on Deezer. Two records there name DJ Boko: “Bay li (feat. DJ Boko) (BOUYON 2026)”, duration 2 minutes 38, writers and composers Allan Blonbou and Julian White; and “SOUKWE LI (feat. DJ Boko) (BOUYON 2026)”, duration 2 minutes 10, writer and composer Julian White. The album also gathers Tiitii NBA, DINHO and DJ DIRTEE, which places DJ Boko in the same track listing as the other names of this wave, including Dinho, credited on “SKILLI” on the same album.

Added to that is “LA PEP’HIT FREESTYLE (feat. TIITII NBA, DJ BOKO, LE K and DJ DIRTEE)”, a DJ SOFTEE single released on June 4, 2025 according to Shazam, published under the DJ SOFTEE name, in French, with a recorded tempo of 160 BPM. The collective freestyle format reveals how this scene works: a producer DJ gathers several voices and several DJs on one statement record, and the guest names, including DJ Boko, gain a presence there that goes beyond their own catalogue.

The most recent credit extends the series beyond the usual duo. The Jixels artist page on Shazam lists “Jéré Sa (feat. DJ BOKO)”, a single by YSN, Edday, DJ SOFTEE and Jixels, classified in 2026. DJ SOFTEE remains present in the lead credit, which keeps continuity with the earlier records, but the widening to YSN, Edday and Jixels shows DJ Boko name circulating in a slightly larger circle, still inside recent French speaking bouyon.

In total, seven verifiable records name DJ Boko between 2025 and 2026: three with TiiTii NBA, two on the DJ SOFTEE album, one collective freestyle with DJ SOFTEE, and “Jéré Sa”. That is enough to establish a recurring, dated presence. It is not enough to tell a life, and this profile does not try: it fixes the guest discography, which is the documented fact, and waits for the rest.

The context: guest DJs and new wave bouyon

To place these credits, the origin of the sound has to be recalled. Bouyon was born in Dominica at the end of the 1980s. Its bouyon soca branch was started in Dominica in 2003 by producer Dada, according to the first-hand testimony of Sean “Smokiller” Francis, a participant in the Nursery Krew era, testimony carried on this site bouyon soca page, before being popularised by Triple Kay. The current bouyon of the French Antilles, that of TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE, extends that history with short records, often around two minutes, built for platforms, sound systems and social networks.

In that economy, DJs are not simple broadcasters. They produce, gather the voices, sign collective albums and lend their name as a dancefloor guarantee. The role of a guest DJ such as DJ Boko can be understood inside that system: his name in a title signals a colour, a team, a party circuit. The crossed credits between TiiTii NBA, DJ SOFTEE, DJ DIRTEE, DJ SKAYLONE, DJ LUCHSHIY and DJ SKYCEE, all visible in the TiiTii NBA discography on Shazam, show a scene where DJs form a permanent frame behind the singers.

DJ Boko holds a precise place in that frame: never lead artist in the verified sources, but a repeated guest with the two names that structure part of 2025 and 2026 bouyon. To hear him, start with “ON ME” and “WET WET” with TiiTii NBA, then the DJ SOFTEE album “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”. Our bouyon playlist follows these releases, and our history of bouyon page gives the genre its depth, from Roseau to its current French speaking scenes.

What this profile does not yet know: the limits of the DJ Boko record

This profile ends with its limits, which are substantial. No source verified here gives DJ Boko full name, his island or his city, his DJ path, his party residencies, his official social media or a catalogue released under his name alone. No account is therefore linked in this page schema: linking the account of a namesake from Taiwan or Bulgaria would be a fault, and guessing a Guadeloupe account without confirmation would be another.

Likewise, this profile publishes no listening figure specific to DJ Boko. The platforms consulted give the records, the durations, the single dates and the writers, not a guest share in a record success. Attributing TiiTii NBA or DJ SOFTEE figures to DJ Boko would be false, and this site does that for no guest artist, here or on the Dinho page, whose record has the same collaborations structure.

If a release under the DJ Boko name alone, a confirmed official page or a published biography appear, this profile will be completed and dated. In the meantime, it does its job: establish, record by record, that a DJ named DJ Boko is a recurring and documented guest in French speaking bouyon in 2025 and 2026, with TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE, and refuse everything else, starting with the biographies of namesakes.

Frequently asked questions

On which TiiTii NBA records is DJ Boko credited?

On three singles listed in 2025 in platform discographies: “ON ME (feat. DJ BOKO) (BOUYON 2026)”, “WET WET (feat. DJ BOKO) (BOUYON 2026)” and “SHE’S A HOE (feat. DJ BOKO) [Bouyon 2025]”, according to Shazam and Deezer.

Why do the records carry a BOUYON 2026 tag when they were released in 2025?

Because in this scene, the bracketed tag attaches the record to the season and the playlists of the target year, often the next carnival, while the commercial release lands the previous year. Shazam and Discogs date these singles to 2025, and this profile gives both pieces of information as the platforms publish them.

Did DJ Boko produce the records where he is credited?

The verified sheets do not say so. On the records detailed by Deezer, the named writers and composers are Terry Capitolin, Allan Blonbou or Blombou depending on the page, and Julian White, but not DJ Boko. He appears there as a featured guest artist, and this profile attributes to him no production the published credits do not name.

What is the link between DJ Boko and Dinho?

They belong to the same circuit and the same album: the DJ SOFTEE album “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”, released in 2025, contains “Bay li” and “SOUKWE LI” with DJ Boko as featured artist, and “SKILLI” with DINHO as featured artist alongside Tiitii NBA and QWANN. No verified record credits them together on the same track.

Is DJ Boko the DJ of the same name based in Taiwan?

No, nothing supports that, and this profile separates them explicitly. The Taiwan based DJ Boko, present on YouTube with R and B and hip hop sets, and DJ Mc BoKo from Bulgaria on SoundClick are namesakes with no established link to the bouyon circuit. Only the credits with TiiTii NBA and DJ SOFTEE define the DJ Boko on this page.

Where can I hear the records where DJ Boko is credited?

Start with TiiTii NBA “ON ME” and “WET WET”, then “Bay li” and “SOUKWE LI” on the DJ SOFTEE album “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”, then the freestyle “LA PEP’HIT FREESTYLE” released on June 4, 2025. Our bouyon playlist follows new releases from this scene.
