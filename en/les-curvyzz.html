Les Curvyzz

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Within three months, between October 2023 and January 2024, Les Curvyzz released four titles that all carry the same tag in brackets: “Bouyon 2024”. “Fess”, “Friandise”, “BuzZ” as a freestyle, then “Met Bouche Aw” with Keks Mafia, distributed under the line 2024 Larsonik Music. This profile follows that burst of releases in order, with the data the listings themselves publish, and owns up to what they leave out: no named members, no biography, no origin.

Three months, four Bouyon 2024 titles

The first title, “Fess”, came out on October 15, 2023 on the Curvyzz channel, with the “Bouyon 2024” tag already in the title, like a promise for the season ahead. It runs 2 minutes 03. On December 22, 2023, “Friandise” followed, this time on the DÉGA YOUTH channel, at 2 minutes 29, with the same tag. On January 12, 2024 came “BuzZ”, presented as a freestyle, at 2 minutes 06.

Each description is just a few links: the Instagram account les_curvyzz, the TikTok account les.curvyzz and, under “Friandise” and “BuzZ”, a second TikTok account, larsonik.music. No detailed production credits, no presentation text: the titles speak for themselves, and the bouyon link rests on what they are called.

Met Bouche Aw, with Keks Mafia

On January 19, 2024, Les Curvyzz posted “Met Bouche Aw (Bouyon 2024)”, a collaboration with Keks Mafia. The next day, January 20, the track reached distribution billed “Les CURVYZZ & KEKS MAFIA”, with the line 2024 Larsonik Music, running 2 minutes 34. It is the only one of the four titles whose distribution listing was opened for this profile, and that listing is the one that names the label.

Larsonik Music therefore ties two threads of the file together: the label line on “Met Bouche Aw” and the TikTok account larsonik.music cited under two other titles. The pages say nothing more about Larsonik’s exact role with the group, and neither does this profile. For more titles from the same circuit, see our bouyon playlist.

What the file does not say

The plural name, Les Curvyzz, is the only clue to the shape of the project. No opened page names the members, gives their number or tells the group’s story. None names an island or town of origin either, and this site assigns none, even though the collaboration with Keks Mafia places the group in the bouyon network of the French Caribbean and Dominica.

Four titles tagged bouyon in one season, one named label and one documented partner: that is the verifiable file on October 23, 2026. More recent releases by the group will be added once their listings have been opened and dated.
