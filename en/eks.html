Eks: Tru nuh boi refix, bouyon 2024

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Eks is the third regular voice of the Go Fast crew, alongside Fifin and Wotlis. Four primary pages opened during this research, all titled bouyon 2024, credit him as performer or co writer: one solo title, “Tru nuh boi refix”, produced by DJ Taffy, and three crew collaborations recorded around Riddim Beast Studios and beatmaker Jackie Beatz. None of these pages states his origin or legal name. Eks should not be confused with Keks Mafia, already on this site under a different name.

Tru nuh boi refix, the title in his own name

“Tru nuh boi refix” went up on February 2, 2024 on DJ Taffy’s channel, labelled bouyon 2024 with the Go Fast Music hashtag. It is the only verified title where Eks stands alone at the top of the bill. DJ Taffy produces, Jackie Beatz adds production, Drummer Fridge plays live drums, and both recording and mixing happen at Riddim Beast Studios. The word “refix” in the title points to a reworked version, though the page does not name the original.

The crew titles: Too sort, G Spot, Just Pass Dat

The oldest of the opened titles is “Too sort”, posted on December 26, 2023 on the Jackie Beatz channel and titled bouyon 2024, under the Go Fast banner. It is led by Paw with Eks as guest, written by both, produced by Jackie Beatz, recorded and mixed by YW3 ENT, with live drums by Nikita Christmas, known as Fridge. The page repeats the crew motto, Go Fast Or Go Home.

Next come “G Spot”, on January 16, 2024, billed to Fifin with Eks, written and performed by both, produced by Jackie Beatz and mixed by DJ Taffy, then “Just Pass Dat”, on June 25, 2024, written by Eks, Fifin and Wotlis and produced by Don Carli. Together with the solo title, these three form the verified core of this profile.

Other credits, and the limits of the file

Two more pages on DJ Taffy’s channel name Eks without stating a genre: “Tie Up”, with Wotlis, produced by DJ Taffy with live bass by NattyBill, and “Up is Up (Back door)”, by Smiler G with Eks and Fifin, with no description. They are cited here to complete the picture, but they do not enter the structured data, since their pages carry no bouyon statement.

The “dominica” and “bouyon” tags attached to every DJ Taffy video belong to the channel, not to a statement about Eks. No biography, interview or official page was opened. The file fits into four bouyon titles dated around 2024 and will be completed if more primary sources appear. For context on the genre, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Eks?

Eks is a voice of the Go Fast crew, credited as performer and co writer on 2024 bouyon titles produced by DJ Taffy, Jackie Beatz and Don Carli. No public biography was found.

Is Eks linked to Keks Mafia?

Nothing in the opened sources says so. They are two different names, and this site does not connect them.

Which Eks bouyon titles are verified?

Four: “Too sort” by Paw with Eks, “G Spot” by Fifin with Eks, the solo “Tru nuh boi refix” and “Just Pass Dat” with Fifin and Wotlis, all titled bouyon 2024.

What is Go Fast?

The name of the crew that brings together Eks, Fifin and Wotlis, also used as a release banner (Go Fast Music) in the credits of Riddim Beast Studios and Jackie Beatz.

Where is Eks from?

No opened source says: the origin stays undocumented.
