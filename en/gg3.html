GG3: World Cup Bouyon, a Guadeloupe song for France

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

GG3 is a composer, singer and performer from Guadeloupe. After about ten years in music and a rap album in 2024, he found his first real hit with “World Cup Bouyon”, released on June 11, 2026 as the World Cup began.

Ten years before bouyon

On streaming services GG3 shows up as early as October 2021, as a guest on “Tristesse originale” with 99 and Har2Nok. In March 2024 he released the album “Fè mannèv” on his own label, GG DIAMANT 3, filed as rap: seven tracks, including “Mélange” with Chanceko and “Followers / Lagaba” with Aknose.

In early 2026 the pace picked up, with “Dirty Secret” in January and “Ba-y Adan” in February. RCI presents him as a composer as much as a singer, and that double role is what made the next song possible.

World Cup Bouyon, June 2026

“World Cup Bouyon” came out on June 11, 2026, the week the World Cup started. GG3 told RCI he built the instrumental on his own before singing over it, because he did not feel much buzz around the tournament yet. He also wanted to give a lift to the Guadeloupean players Maxence Lacroix and Marcus Thuram.

Why bouyon? His answer fits in one line: bouyon is what he knows how to do. A Kylian Mbappé fan, he sees the song as support sent from the Antilles. On YouTube the official upload is past 47,000 views.

After the World Cup

On July 9, the day of the quarter-final against Morocco, RCI told his story and noted that he had not heard back from the French team. For him, the song is still his first real success after ten years of work.

He kept going. “Happy Hour” came out in mid-August 2026, then “Touris La” in early October, a duet with General Pye. For the local picture, the bouyon in Guadeloupe page gathers the island's other names.

Frequently asked questions

Who is GG3?

A composer, singer and performer from Guadeloupe, active for about ten years according to RCI.

What is World Cup Bouyon?

A bouyon song released on June 11, 2026 to back the French national team during the World Cup.

Who made the riddim for World Cup Bouyon?

GG3 himself: he told RCI he composed the instrumental before singing on it.

Which players does the song support?

GG3 wanted to cheer on the Guadeloupean players Maxence Lacroix and Marcus Thuram, and he says he is a Kylian Mbappé fan.

Has he released other songs in 2026?

Yes: Dirty Secret, Ba-y Adan, Happy Hour and Touris La with General Pye.
