Tamillia: from The Voice Kids to the DJ Blax bouyon clip

By Smokiller · Published October 25, 2026

Tamillia is a singer from Saint-Martin that France discovered as a child, on television. Ten years later, she appears in a clip alongside DJ Blax, the Paris based producer, on a composition signed by DJ Blax and John Scorp. This page gathers her verified path and explains what her presence means for the bouyon scene.

Saint-Martin, then The Voice Kids in 2016

Tamillia was born on 26 February 2004 in Saint-Martin, in the French Antilles. Her Deezer published biography tells of a child who took her mother microphone at 8, then, at 11, captivated the national audience of The Voice Kids France with Halo by Beyonce.

The archives of season 3 of The Voice Kids confirm the run: Tamillia sang in the team of Matt Pokora, passed the battles and the semi-final, and sang Are We Awake by Tal in the final, on 8 October 2016. She did not win the show, but she left it with a name known across the French speaking Caribbean.

The singles and the 2022 signing

Back in Saint-Martin and Sint-Maarten, Tamillia kept singing across the Caribbean. Her first single Do Me was released on 7 June 2019. On her 18th birthday, in 2022, she signed with The November Society, the label of US music veteran Sundafu Kawah, and travelled to Los Angeles.

What followed is documented by her Deezer biography and platform credits: Murda in 2024, the duet Tout Doucement with Lionel Nidot, the Maladie feature on the Bamby album in March 2025, Love and Affection on 16 April 2025, then Gyal Anthem on 10 July 2025, noticed on TikTok.

The clip with DJ Blax, on the bouyon side

In October 2026, DJ Blax announced a clip with Tamillia, released on YouTube. The DJ Blax post credits composers DJ Blax and John Scorp, and the visual production to Celinemsili. The track title is not in the Instagram post: this page does not guess it and waits for the clip own record.

DJ Blax is a DJ and producer based in Paris, active between France and the Antilles. His meeting with a voice born in Saint-Martin and trained between French television and Caribbean stages draws exactly the road bouyon follows today: from the islands to Paris, and from Paris back to the islands.

What this page keeps

Tamillia is not a bouyon artist by birth: she is a singer from Saint-Martin whose voice arrives in bouyon through production and the clip, like other voices of the region before her. Her full profile and verified recordings are on her artist page on this site.

No view count, no chart position and no clip title is stated here without the source that carries it. When the official clip record is read, this page will be completed with the exact track title.
