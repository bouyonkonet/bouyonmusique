Le Det: one verified single, “Bouwé’y”, and a file that states its limits

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Le Det is a bouyon artist name whose verified public file holds exactly one recording: “Bouwé’y”, a single released on July 4, 2023 on the label ILB PRODUCTION, sung in French, measured at 157 BPM on its song page, and credited to Le Det alone for composition and lyrics. No biography, no interview, no published origin and no second record were found during the research for this profile, carried out across the major platforms and search engines. This profile therefore publishes the complete record page of that single, separates Le Det from Le Dext, the artist with the near identical name documented elsewhere on this site, distinguishes his record from two near namesake titles, the one by BISMOK and the one by 1T1, and states clearly everything the file does not hold. It is a short profile, openly so.

Who Le Det is: one name, one single, almost no archive

The honest starting point, on this file, fits in one sentence. Le Det is the name credited on one recording the platforms document: “Bouwé’y”, released as a single on July 4, 2023. The song page, carried by Shazam from the distributor data, names the label, ILB PRODUCTION, the language, French, the tempo, 157 BPM, and a single credit: composition and lyrics signed Le Det, with no other writer, no guest and no separately credited producer.

Around that single, the void is nearly total, and this profile records it instead of furnishing it. No verifiable artist page was found for this name during the research: no biography, no path, no town, no full name, no press photograph. Searches run across Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Deezer and SoundCloud return no second recording under this name, and the label ILB PRODUCTION appears, in the documents consulted, on this one page only.

The context of the record is therefore read in its own page and in its scene neighbourhood. The single is in French, and the research that isolated it, the wave 11 research of this site devoted to Le Dext, classed it in the bouyon circuit of the French Antilles: it is on that basis that this profile documents it, as a French language bouyon recording of the summer of 2023. No open source publishes the birthplace or residence of Le Det, and this profile assigns him none.

Why devote a page to a single record? Because the record exists, it circulates on the platforms, and its name is confused with that of another artist of the same scene, Le Dext, whose catalog is different. Letting the platforms mix the two names with no page doing the sorting would be a documentation error; publishing the verified page, with its limits displayed, is this site answer.

“Bouwé’y”: the complete page of the 2023 single

According to the song page published by Shazam, “Bouwé’y” was released on July 4, 2023, on the label ILB PRODUCTION, as a single titled “Bouwé’y - Single”. The page states the language of the record, French, and its tempo, 157 BPM: a fast tempo, in the high zone of party bouyon tempos, which places the record on the side of energy rather than ballad.

The writing credit is the simplest there is: the composition and lyrics section of the page names one person only, Le Det, as songwriter. No co writer, no feature, no producer or beatmaker name appears in the opened credits. On a scene where records are often signed by whole sessions, that solitary credit is a fact of the page: the name holding the billing is also, on paper, the name that writes.

The usage traces counted on the page are modest, and this profile gives them for what they are. When the page was opened, the record counted 47 identifications on Shazam: a usage counter of that platform, not a career measure and not a chart. The same page displays, in its genre section, the tag “Christmas” attached to the year 2023: an automatic platform classification, of the same kind counted on other pages of the scene, which this profile reports as a platform tag and not as a description of the record.

What the page does not give must be stated with the same clarity: no commented duration, no documented video clip, no lyrics published in the open sources, and no follow up release. The schema of this page therefore holds one recording, the one the opened document establishes. If a second record appears under this name with its credits, it will be added here, with its source, and the page will be dated again.

Le Det and Le Dext: the separation this profile establishes

The main confusion of the file comes down to a few letters. There is another bouyon artist, Le Dext, with an x, whose profile this site published in its wave 11. His verified file holds three records: “Open That”, released on August 30, 2024 on 5417166 Records DK, in French, at 160 BPM, written by Malik Ismael Paul and Emmanuel Toussaint; “Stick It”, an official audio produced by Tidji, of Blockstudio; and “FAT CAT”, a single co billed with Team Bwè Tou Sa and listed in 2026.

Placed side by side, the two files share nothing but the closeness of the names. Spelling: Le Det without an x, Le Dext with an x. Date: July 2023 against August 2024 for the lead name singles. Label: ILB PRODUCTION against 5417166 Records DK. Tempo: 157 BPM against 160 BPM. Credits: a single writer, Le Det himself, against two named writers, Malik Ismael Paul and Emmanuel Toussaint. On every line of the page, the two artists separate.

That separation is not an invention of this profile: it was made during the wave 11 research, where “Bouwé’y” was explicitly excluded from the discography of Le Dext before his profile was published. This page completes the operation by giving Le Det his own file, built on his own page. The platforms, for their part, keep suggesting the records of one on the pages of the other, and a reader, a DJ or a promoter can be served “Bouwé’y” while searching for Le Dext, and the reverse.

The research crossed other neighbouring names, starting with a Belgian hip hop artist named Le Dé, present in the same search results on an entirely different scene. No source links him to Le Det, and nothing of his file is used here. On a short name built like a nickname, namesakes are the rule: the value of this page is that it was cleared of those neighbours, one by one.

“Bouwé’y”, “Boué’y”, “Bouwéy”: three records, three artists

The title of the Le Det single adds a second layer of confusion, because two other recordings carry a nearly identical title, built on the same Creole expression, and circulate on the same platforms a few months apart. Separating them is necessary so that the July 2023 single is neither inflated nor crushed by its neighbours.

The first neighbour is “Boué’y”, by BISMOK, whose song page was opened for this profile. According to Shazam, that record was released on November 7, 2023, four months after the Le Det one, on the label iMD-DRECORDS, in French, at 162 BPM, with a World genre tag. Its credits differ in everything: BISMOK is the performer, Kurtys Alexis handles vocals and programming, Daryl Clarisse and Ulrich Bienvenu are credited as composers, and Kurtys Alexis signs the lyrics. No name from the Le Det file appears in it, and the page counted 174 Shazam identifications when it was opened.

The second neighbour is the best known of the three: “Bouwéy”, by 1T1 featuring Theomaa, the record that turned this expression into a platform success. Its song page was not opened for this profile; the elements that follow come from the search results consulted the same day: a 2024 release, a place on the EP “CHORD” released on December 13, 2024, a composition credited to 1T1 and Dinho and writing to 1T1 and Théomaa according to the video description, a total close to 967,000 Shazam identifications and a peak at No. 48 on the Shazam Global Chart on March 22, 2025, with seven days spent in the top 50.

The chronology, for its part, is a fact of dates and nothing more: in the documents, the “Bouwé’y” of Le Det, in July 2023, precedes the “Boué’y” of BISMOK, in November 2023, which itself precedes the “Bouwéy” of 1T1, in 2024. This profile records that order and claims neither creative priority, nor influence, nor cover version: the same Creole expression can carry three separate records, and that is exactly what the pages show.

What this profile claims, and what it leaves open

Let the file be summed up, because on a one record file, clarity beats emphasis. Claimed and sourced: Le Det released “Bouwé’y” on July 4, 2023, on ILB PRODUCTION, a record in French at 157 BPM, of which he is the only credited writer. Claimed and sourced: that record is neither the record of Le Dext, nor that of BISMOK, nor that of 1T1, and their pages separate it on every line. Everything else is open.

Open means: identity and path of Le Det not published, origin not published, second record not found, official accounts not verified by opening and therefore not published, the sameAs field of this page staying empty for that precise reason. Publishing this profile under these conditions answers this site mission: document bouyon record by record, with the level of evidence displayed, rather than let a name dissolve into those of its namesakes.

To hear the current of Le Det, start with “Bouwé’y” itself, then read our bouyon gwada page for the Guadeloupean form of the genre, our bouyon soca page for the neighbouring branch, and our bouyon playlist, which follows the current scene of the French Antilles and Dominica. If the Le Det file thickens, this page will grow with it, dated and corrected like every page on this site.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Le Det?

Le Det is a bouyon artist name documented by one verified recording only: the single “Bouwé’y”, released on July 4, 2023 on ILB PRODUCTION. No biography, no interview and no published origin were found during the research for this profile, and it says so openly.

What is the verified record of Le Det?

“Bouwé’y”, a single released on July 4, 2023 on the label ILB PRODUCTION, sung in French and measured at 157 BPM on its page. Le Det is its only credited writer, for composition and lyrics. It is the only record the opened documents assign to this name without a namesake risk.

Are Le Det and Le Dext the same artist?

No source says so, and their pages differ on everything: Le Det, without an x, released “Bouwé’y” on July 4, 2023 on ILB PRODUCTION, at 157 BPM, with a writer credit under his name alone. Le Dext, with an x, released “Open That” on August 30, 2024 on 5417166 Records DK, at 160 BPM, with two other writers, Malik Ismael Paul and Emmanuel Toussaint. The research of this site separated the two names as early as its wave 11.

Are “Bouwé’y”, “Boué’y” and “Bouwéy” the same song?

No: they are three separate recordings carrying nearly identical titles. “Bouwé’y” by Le Det (July 4, 2023, ILB PRODUCTION), “Boué’y” by BISMOK (November 7, 2023, iMD-DRECORDS, written with Daryl Clarisse, Ulrich Bienvenu and Kurtys Alexis) and “Bouwéy” by 1T1 featuring Theomaa (2024). Artists, labels, dates and credits differ on every page.

Where is Le Det from?

His origin is not published in the open sources. The page of his single gives only the language of the record, French, and the research of this site attaches it to the bouyon circuit of the French Antilles. No source publishes his birthplace or his place of residence, and this profile does not guess them.
