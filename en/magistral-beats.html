Magistral Beats: the producer of Célibataire and Bouyon Olympique

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Magistral Beats is a producer who presents himself as a Caribbean producer going “from Guadeloupe to the world”. His name is tied above all to Aknose: he composed “Coulie Coulie” with Kima, which has passed 1.9 million views, then “Bloqué” and “Célibataire” with N’Ken in 2026. His own pages also spell out the genre: “Bouyon Olympique” in 2024, “Magistral Dolibouyon” in 2026, a studio video called We Make Bouyon Music, and a bouyon mix of “Maille Café” with Tency. The published credits on his tracks carry the name Patrice Piquion, on keyboards, drums, flute and bass.

A producer who writes the word bouyon on his own titles

On August 4, 2024, in the middle of the Olympic period, the Magistral Beats channel posted “TEDDY BIEN PWOP - BOUYON OLYMPIQUE”, a 2 minute 14 track that the description sums up in two words, Bouyon Olympique, before stating that it was composed and produced by Magistral Beats, on his own label, Magistral Beats Publisching (spelling as published). Deezer lists the same title, “TEDDY RINER BIEN PWOP”, on the same date. It is the oldest page in his file that states the genre.

Two years later the genre comes back in a track name: “Magistral Dolibouyon”, released on July 15, 2026 through DistroKid, 1 minute 45, on the Magistral Beats Publisching label. In between, on May 4, 2026, the channel posted “We Make Bouyon Music in studio”, four minutes of studio footage whose title says it all. And in August 2026, the first episode of his Top Caribbean Producer series describes him as a Caribbean producer, Guadeloupe flag included, with a mission in one sentence: take Caribbean sounds to the international stage.

Aknose: Coulie Coulie, Bloqué and Célibataire

The most visible partner of Magistral Beats is Aknose. “Coulie Coulie”, by Aknose with Kima and Magistral, came out as a video on June 27, 2025 on the MBI Family channel: composition is credited to Magistral, and flute, drums and keyboards to Piquion Patrice, with Magistral Beats in brackets. The video has passed 1.9 million views. “Bloqué”, posted on April 4, 2026, follows the same pattern: Aknose on vocals, recording, mixing and mastering at Magistral, keyboards and drums by Patrice Piquion, on the joint Magistral Beats Publisching and MBi Family label.

On June 5, 2026 came “Célibataire”, credited to Magistral with Aknose and N’Ken, directed by Jima Kanor, with about 401,000 views on the producer's channel. Magistral Beats is the composer there, and he handles studio, recording, mixing and mastering. None of those three listings uses the word bouyon, so this profile does not enter them in its structured data: they show the workshop, and it is his own titles that carry the label.

Maille Café, Jicypie and a catalogue that goes beyond bouyon

On June 27, 2025, Tency's channel posted “Maille Café Bouyon Mix”, with Tency on vocals, composition shared between Magistral Beats and Kny Factory, and Magistral Beats Publisching as producer. Deezer lists “MAILLE CAFÉ” at the same time, and a month earlier, on May 16, 2025, the single “I KÉ FUÉ”, with Jeancito and Jicypie, a DJ credited among the guests on 1T1's “Mas La Rivé”.

The rest of the catalogue is broad, and this profile says so plainly. The channel lines up trap, afro, shatta and zouk type beats, “Méyè ki Mwen” with T Kimp Gee and Maurane Voyer (piano, drums, synth and bass by Patrice Piquion), “Détestéw” with Keros-n and Don Snoop, “Désidew” with Niko, and “Karma” with Honey Bee’s and Natoxie. Magistral Beats is a versatile Caribbean producer for whom bouyon is one stated register among several, not the only one.

Limits of the file

The name Patrice Piquion is cited here as a published credit, on his track pages and in the artist field of “Magistral Dolibouyon”, with no further biography. No birth date or birthplace is published; the Guadeloupe anchoring comes from his own self presentation. A Guadeloupe La 1ère report title appears in his video list, but the video could not be opened, and this profile draws nothing from it.

For other producers on the same circuit, see the bouyon producers page, the bouyon gwada page and the profile of Magbeatz, not to be confused with Magistral Beats.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Magistral Beats?

A producer and composer who presents himself as a Caribbean producer from Guadeloupe. He composed Coulie Coulie, Bloqué and Célibataire for Aknose, and releases titles such as Bouyon Olympique and Magistral Dolibouyon on his own label, Magistral Beats Publisching.

Which Magistral Beats titles state bouyon?

Bouyon Olympique (Teddy Bien Pwop, August 4, 2024), Magistral Dolibouyon (July 15, 2026) and Maille Café Bouyon Mix with Tency (June 27, 2025). His channel also has a studio video titled We Make Bouyon Music.

Who sings on Célibataire?

Aknose and N’Ken. The video, posted on June 5, 2026 on the Magistral Beats channel, credits Magistral Beats as composer and as the recording, mixing and mastering studio. The page states no genre.

Who is Patrice Piquion?

The name credited on keyboards, drums, flute, synth or bass on several tracks, always followed by Magistral Beats, and listed in the artist field of Magistral Dolibouyon. This profile cites it as a published credit, without drawing any further biography from it.

Are Magistral Beats and Magbeatz the same person?

Nothing suggests so. Magbeatz is another Guadeloupe producer already profiled on this site; the names look alike but no opened source links them.
