Zepek

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Zepek is a voice who claims bouyon in his own titles. On his ZepekOfficiel channel he posted “F*ck 2 Night” in 2024, labelled Bouyon 2024, then a freestyle billed as Bouyon Arabic 2k25. His “Anaconda” remix also circulates under the Bouyon 2024 label. In 2025 his name appears on “Pam Pam (ke lo ke)”, with Djadja and Les p’tits du Rzt, and then on a sequel, “Pam Pam 2”. This profile follows those pages and leaves open the questions they do not settle.

The titles labelled bouyon

“ZEPEK, F*CK 2 NIGHT (BOUYON 2024)” went up on May 3, 2024 on ZepekOfficiel, with a release line dated the day before and the label GG. It is the first of the channel’s three videos, and the most explicit one: genre and year are written into the title.

Summer 2024 brought “Anaconda”, in a remix credited to Anonymous. The DÉGA YOUTH channel posted it on August 2, 2024 as “Zepek, Anaconda (Bouyon 2024)(Remix Anonymous)”, linking the same zepek.gg accounts as the official channel. Three days later ZepekOfficiel posted its original version, tied in the description to the title “A2 Adann Kaz La”. Then, on December 23, 2024, Zepek posted “#FREESTYLE1 MALÉLIVÉ”, a short 1 minute 23 piece billed as Bouyon Arabic 2k25, a label that hints at a blend of sounds, though the page says nothing more.

Pam Pam, with Djadja and Les p’tits du Rzt

On April 18, 2025, “Pam Pam (ke lo ke)” came out, released as audio under Djadja’s name with Les p’tits Rzt and Zepek, and as an official video on the Les p’tits du Rzt channel, where Zepek is billed first. The video credits production to AKH, mix and master to TybiProduction, and the footage to Mighty Production and Thoughtful. The release page adds a credit to Alpha without saying what role it covers.

On July 23, 2025, an artist channel named Zepek Officiel released “Pam Pam 2”, on a project called En Mode Mixtape. None of these pages names bouyon: these titles are part of his story, but stay out of the structured data. This site links the Zepek of Pam Pam to the Zepek of ZepekOfficiel on the strength of the shared name and the sequel released under his name, though no page states it in so many words.

What this file does not settle

No opened page gives a biography, a legal name or an origin for Zepek, and this site assigns him no island. A similar name also exists on YouTube: Zepek Rs, with a different channel and accounts, and a title, “Ochanterie”, labelled Bouyon 2023. Nothing links those accounts to Zepek’s, and the two are not merged here. For other releases from the same period, the site’s bouyon playlist is a good starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Zepek?

Zepek is a bouyon voice who posts on the ZepekOfficiel channel: “F*ck 2 Night” (Bouyon 2024), the “Anaconda” remix (Bouyon 2024) and the “Malélivé” freestyle (Bouyon Arabic 2k25). No public biography was found.

Is Zepek on Pam Pam?

Yes. “Pam Pam (ke lo ke)”, released on April 18, 2025, brings together Djadja, Les p’tits du Rzt and Zepek, produced by AKH. A sequel, “Pam Pam 2”, came out on July 23, 2025 under Zepek’s name. Those pages name no genre.

Which Zepek titles are labelled bouyon?

“F*ck 2 Night (Bouyon 2024)”, “Anaconda (Bouyon 2024)(Remix Anonymous)” and “#FREESTYLE1 Malélivé (Bouyon Arabic 2k25)”.

Are Zepek and Zepek Rs the same person?

Nothing confirms it: the channels and accounts are different. This profile covers Zepek only.

Where is Zepek from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns him no origin.
