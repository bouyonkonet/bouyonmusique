Halibut: Belles Combo's voice and False Hair, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Halibut, born Cletus Abraham, was the lead singer of Belles Combo and one of the big voices of Dominican cadence-lypso. In 2014 he recorded “False Hair”, a bouyon song produced by Dada for carnival. He sang at the World Creole Music Festival one last time in October 2025.

False Hair, the 2014 bouyon song

For Mas Domnik 2014, Dada produced, mixed and mastered “False Hair” on the Madras Riddim, recorded at Cotton Groove Studio in Dominica and released on the Machine Dominik label. The producer's channel calls it a fusion of bouyon and soca and credits it to “Legendary Halibut of Belles Combo”, who also wrote the lyrics.

DJ Socaholic shared it the same week as “a brand new bouyon track” for the 2014 carnival. The producer's upload is past 44,000 views. It is a cadence voice stepping onto a bouyon riddim while keeping his own pen.

Belles Combo, cadence and collaborations

His name stays tied to Belles Combo, a group with recordings from the 1970s online, such as “Simplify Yourself”. On his own, his most watched songs online include “Pawa Bol”, filmed among other places at the Dominica Music Festival, and “Wrong and Strong”, billed as 2015 cadence-lypso.

He also sang with others. “Riches of Dominica”, with First Serenade Band, is close to 75,000 views on the band's channel, and the “De Long Tongue” remix pairs him with Mantius.

The last WCMF and the 2026 tribute

In 2025 he was on the first night bill of the World Creole Music Festival, Friday October 24 at Windsor Park. A video filmed that night shows him on stage with the Midnight Groovers.

At the end of March 2026, DBS Radio wrote of “the late Cletus Halibut Abraham”, one of Dominica's cadence-lypso icons, laid to rest the Saturday before. A tribute concert was held in the Mahaut constituency, and minister Rayburn Blackmoore urged young artists to learn from his songwriting.

Frequently asked questions

Who was Halibut?

The lead singer of Belles Combo, born Cletus Abraham, described by DBS Radio as one of Dominica's cadence-lypso icons.

What is his bouyon song?

False Hair, on the Madras Riddim, produced by Dada for the 2014 carnival and billed as bouyon soca.

Who did he sing with?

First Serenade Band on Riches of Dominica, Mantius on the De Long Tongue remix, and the Midnight Groovers at WCMF 2025.

When did he last play WCMF?

On Friday October 24, 2025, the first night of the 25th edition, at Windsor Park in Roseau.

When did he die?

The sources we opened do not give the date. DBS Radio says he was laid to rest on a Saturday, before its March 30, 2026 report.
