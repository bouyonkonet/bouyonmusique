Bouyon styles

  • Bouyon soca : Bouyon soca: the fusion between Dominica bouyon and Trinidad soca. Origin, sound, territories (Saint Lucia, Antigua, Guadeloupe, Martinique) and songs to listen to.
  • Bouyon gwada : Bouyon gwada: the hardcore branch of bouyon born from collaborations between Dominica and Guadeloupe. History, raw sound and place in the bouyon music of the French Antilles.
  • Shatta bouyon : Shatta bouyon: the modern form of bouyon carried by Antillean producers and the French scene. Riddims, clubs, social media and the link with Dominica, the island where bouyon comes from.
  • Bouyon-muffin : Bouyon-muffin: the branch of bouyon influenced by Jamaican dancehall and ragga. Raggamuffin voices over Dominica bouyon riddims, history and legacy.
  • Reketeng : Reketeng: one of the branches of bouyon born in Dominica, in the family of bouyon fusions, bouyon soca and bouyon-muffin. What you need to know about this style.
  • Jump up : Jump up: in Guadeloupe and Martinique, it is the everyday name for the bouyon music of Dominica. Why this name, and how jump up lives at the Antillean carnival.
  • Old school bouyon : Old school bouyon: the bouyon of the live bands of the 1990s in Dominica, the era of WCK, RSB and First Serenade. The original sound that fans still defend.
  • Gospel bouyon : Gospel bouyon: when the bouyon rhythm of Dominica serves Christian praise in the Caribbean. A real, much searched branch of bouyon music.
  • Modern bouyon : Modern bouyon 2026: digital riddims, shatta bouyon, remixes and producers of the new generation, between Dominica, Guadeloupe, Martinique and Paris.

Bouyon guides

  • What bouyon is : Definition of bouyon: a musical genre created in Dominica in the late 1980s, a mix of jing ping, cadence-lypso, zouk and electronic rhythms. A simple and complete explanation.
  • Origin of bouyon : The origin of bouyon: created in Roseau, Dominica, in the late 1980s by WCK. Why Dominica is the birthplace of bouyon and why the credit must stay there.
  • Meaning of bouyon : Meaning of bouyon: in Antillean Creole, a gumbo soup, a slow-cooked Caribbean dish. Why this name perfectly sums up the bouyon music of Dominica.
  • Bouyon vs soca : Bouyon vs soca: origin (Dominica against Trinidad), rhythms, instruments and spirit. The clear guide to stop confusing bouyon and soca, and to understand bouyon soca.
  • Bouyon vs zouk : Bouyon vs zouk: the zouk of Guadeloupe and Martinique of the 1980s against the bouyon of Dominica. Origins, tempos and roles in Antillean music.
  • Bouyon vs shatta : Bouyon vs shatta: shatta is the modern family of Antillean riddims, bouyon the historic Dominican genre. Their meeting creates shatta bouyon, explained here.
  • Bouyon instruments : The instruments of bouyon: bele drum, lapo kabwit, chakchak, the jing ping accordion, rhythm guitar, keyboards and drum machines. The guide to the sounds of bouyon.
  • Jing ping : Jing ping: the traditional dance music of Dominica, with accordion, lapo kabwit and bamboo flute. One of the two parents of bouyon, with cadence-lypso.
  • Cadence-lypso : Cadence-lypso: the Dominican genre of the 1970s of Exile One and the Grammacks, a fusion of cadence and calypso. With jing ping, it gave birth to bouyon.

  • Asa Bantan : Asa Bantan: bouyon artist from Grand Bay, in Dominica, nicknamed the Bouyon Boss and King of Bouyon. A central figure of Dominican carnival and the bouyon scene.
  • Reo : Reo: Dominican bouyon artist nicknamed the Prince of Bouyon, nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, known for Joli BB with Maurane Voyer. Portrait on Bouyon Musique.
  • Maurane Voyer : Maurane Voyer: singer from Martinique, the voice of Joli BB with the Dominican Reo. A bouyon collaboration between Martinique and Dominica, presented by Bouyon Shattaz.

  • HYPNO : HYPNO (@hypnoticbeatz): bouyon producer followed by Bouyon Shattaz, known for Mauvais Plan, his 160 bpm track that marked the bouyon producer scene.

  • MB On The Track : MB On The Track (@mbonthetrack): bouyon producer who sells custom productions by DM, known for his Rock Bouyon video and Bouyon Shattaz’s Producer Session.
  • Smokiller : Smokiller: the bouyon artist and producer project that flips afro, pop, rap and R and B hits into bouyon versions. Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) for Klass Money, remixes and link with Bouyon Shattaz.
