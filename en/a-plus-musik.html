A-Plus Muzik: the producer name behind Without You

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

A-Plus Muzik is a bouyon producer name: it is under that signature, and under that signature only, that verified sources know it. It is credited as producer of “Without You”, the Bouyon 2025 collective record by Nice, Reo, Trilla-G, Unstoppable Shax and Quan Dawg, a Dominican record that also looks towards the US Virgin Islands. This profile is deliberately different from the other pages on this site: the A-Plus Muzik record holds a single credit, and this page says so from the introduction, then explains why that single credit still deserves a page, who sings on the record, and what is not yet known about the person or structure behind the name.

Who A-Plus Muzik is: a producer name, one verified credit

Start with the fact, because there is only one of them and it needs stating cleanly. In the official description of the “Without You” video, published as a Bouyon 2025 record, one line reads: produced by A-Plus Muzik. The same line gives the handle attached to the name, @aplusmuzik1. That is, to date, the only verified trace of A-Plus Muzik in the sources consulted for this profile: searches on the exact name, on the handle, on bouyon production credits and on the record platforms. No biography, no other production credit, no confirmed official account and no interview was found. This profile therefore invents nothing around that core: no real name, no island of origin, no journey, no catalogue.

That scarcity raises a question the other profiles on this site do not have to ask: is A-Plus Muzik a person, a studio or a label? The sources do not settle it. The credit is worded like a standard production credit, of the kind carried by individual producers on the bouyon scene. But the video description places that credit right before another structure name, Th3rd House Studios, in charge of mixing and mastering, a reminder that in bouyon credits, names of people and names of studios sit side by side without always being distinct. The honest answer is therefore written here: A-Plus Muzik is documented as a producer credit, and nothing more precise is established. If the name holder publishes a presentation, this page will be completed and dated.

The handle published in the credits, @aplusmuzik1, is reported here exactly as printed in the official video description, without building a full address from it and without claiming which platform it belongs to. It is the same discipline this site applies to the handle of Nice, published in the same credits. On a scene where many producers work under a single signature name, with no website, no biography and sometimes no active public account, the production credit is often the first and only piece of identification available. Documenting it exactly, rather than inflating it, is the first step towards anything more existing.

What this profile refuses to do also needs saying. The name A-Plus exists elsewhere in music, carried by established American artists with no documented connection to bouyon or to “Without You”. A full section below is devoted to that disambiguation, because search engines mix those namesakes and a reader could hand the biography of someone else to the producer of “Without You”. Here, the rule is simple: anything that cannot be tied to the “Without You” credit is set aside, even when it is flattering, even when it is famous.

Without You: five voices from Dominica and the Virgin Islands on a Bouyon 2025 record

The record carrying the name of A-Plus Muzik is called “Without You”. It is presented as a Bouyon 2025 record, and its official description credits it to five performers: Unstoppable Shax, Nice, Reo, Trilla-G and Quan Dawg, the last one credited as Quan in the body of the description. The video is by A.Nibbs, and the description links out to Spotify and Apple Music. Around the producer, the published team is therefore complete and readable: five voices in front, A-Plus Muzik on production, Th3rd House Studios on mixing and mastering, A.Nibbs on the visuals. It is a working credit block, of the kind Dominican bouyon publishes more and more cleanly, and it is one of the reasons this site quotes it in full.

The five voices tell the geography of the record. Nice, Reo and Trilla-G belong to the Dominican generation documented on this site: Reo, presented as the Prince of Bouyon by 13th Street Promotions, has his own profile, Reo, and Nice is confirmed there among the Dominican artists of the World Creole Music Festival 2026. Unstoppable Shax appears, according to the published guides of the same festival, on the same closing night as Nice. Quan, for his part, is credited with the handle @quandawg_vi, and the video keywords cite usvi and virgin islands: the record therefore also looks towards the US Virgin Islands, Dominica neighbours in the Caribbean party circuits. This profile reports those elements as published, without assigning each performer an origin the credits do not give.

A collective record of this kind is not catalogue filler. In current Dominican bouyon, these five voice records are generation workshops: the artists test themselves together there, the producers install the sound of a season there, and the bills of the following festivals are prepared there. “Without You” is a clear illustration, since several of its performers turn up afterwards in the published WCMF 2026 programme. For the credited producer, holding the production of such a collective record is a central role, even when he stays invisible elsewhere: it is his sound signature that has to hold five different voices inside a single party record, at bouyon tempo and with bouyon energy.

The rest of the credit block deserves the same attention, because it places the work of A-Plus Muzik inside a chain. Mixing and mastering are entrusted to Th3rd House Studios, credited with its own handle, @theonlyth3rd. Production and finishing are therefore separated in the credits: A-Plus Muzik produces, Th3rd House Studios finalises. That separation, ordinary in structured industries, is still unevenly documented in bouyon, where many records circulate with no published credit at all. That “Without You” publishes both lines, with the handles, makes the record a small model of documentation, and its producer a findable name: that is exactly why Bouyon Shattaz can give him a page.

A-Plus Muzik and bouyon: the producer, the other name behind the record

To understand what a producer credit is worth in bouyon, recall how the genre makes its records. Bouyon was born in Roseau, Dominica, at the end of the 1980s, when WCK fused cadence-lypso and jing ping with electronic keyboards and drum machines. From that birth, the genre is a music of making as much as of stage: someone assembles the ingredients, sets the tempo and builds the machine on which the voices will call the crowd. The producer is not a technician outside bouyon: he is one of the authors of its sound, from the origin onwards.

The branches of the genre confirm that role. Bouyon soca, which Dada started in Dominica in 2003 according to the first hand testimony of Smokiller, and which Triple Kay popularized, rests on riddims built in the studio and shared between several voices. Wrecketeng, the Dominican style of sampled melody and rap voice, is likewise a producer work before it is a singer work. Our page on bouyon soca and the one on wrecketeng tell those lines of descent. In that landscape, a producer name credited on a 2025 collective record sits inside a precise lineage: that of the riddim makers who bring the bouyon seasons into existence, often less visible than the voices they carry.

The closest comparison on this site is Smokiller, Dominican-born producer, founder of Bouyon Shattaz. Smokiller publishes his remixes, his credits and his catalogue on his own site, and his work is documented record by record. A-Plus Muzik stands, for now, at the other end of visibility: one credit, no published catalogue found. Placing the two names in the same paragraph is not about equalling them, but about showing the documentation gap running through the scene: between the producer who documents everything and the one known from a single credit line, there is the silent majority of the makers of current bouyon. This site works to narrow that gap, credit by credit.

That is also why the question of credit sits at the centre of the Bouyon Shattaz project. Bouyon has too often travelled without the names of those who made it, and its producers are the first erased from that history. A page like this one, even a short one, even one full of acknowledged blanks, fixes the verifiable minimum: on “Without You”, a Bouyon 2025 record bringing together five performers from Dominica and the Virgin Islands, production is signed A-Plus Muzik. Anyone who reuses the record, programmes it on radio or places it in a mix now has a source giving that credit cleanly, with its context and its limits.

From Roseau to the mixes: how Without You circulated

After its release, “Without You” circulated where the bouyon of 2025 circulated: in the mixes. The dossier of this site records the record in the tracklist of the Blaqrose Supreme Bouyon Mix 2025 Part 2, credited to Nice, Reo, Trilla-G, Unstoppable Shax and Quan Dawg, and in the tracklist of the DJWillyWonka Factory mix, alongside “Bye to de Old Me” and the Signal Band “Stronger Remix”. Those records are quoted for what they are: published mix tracklist entries, recorded during the research for the Nice profile. They show that the record produced by A-Plus Muzik was picked up by DJs of the season, which is, for a bouyon record, the first form of practical consecration.

That circulation through mixes sheds light on the real role of a collective record producer. A DJ does not keep a record for its credit block: he keeps it because the production holds the dance, chains with the other records of the season and stands comparison with the productions of the established names. That “Without You” appears in lists that also carry Signal Band records says that the production of A-Plus Muzik passed that test of use, at least to the ears of the DJs who built those mixes. This profile does not turn that signal into a chart position or into figures: no verified audience figure for the record is published here, because none was reliably recorded in the open sources.

The path of the performers extends that signal. According to the published World Creole Music Festival 2026 guides, the closing night of the Roseau festival, on Sunday October 25, 2026, brings together notably Shelly and The Signal Band, Unstoppable Shax, Nice and DJ Taffy. Two of the five voices of “Without You” therefore play that night, and the 2025 collective record looks, in hindsight, like a rehearsal for the closing bill. The producer of the record, for his part, appears on no recorded bill: that is the usual fate of producers, present in the credits and absent from the programmes. This page also exists to correct, at its small scale, that asymmetry.

To hear the record in its context, the simplest path runs through the pages of its performers on this site: Nice for the path closest to “Without You”, Reo for the international generation, then our bouyon playlist, which places the collective records of 2025 in the middle of the current genre, from Dominica to the French Antilles. Read also our history of bouyon for the full chain, from WCK to the producers of today.

Do not confuse: the other A-Plus names in music

A reader searching for A-Plus Muzik on a search engine lands first on other artists, and this page has to say so clearly to prevent any wrong attribution. There is an American hip hop artist named A-Plus, a member of the Hieroglyphics and Souls of Mischief collectives, whose career has been documented since the 1990s. There is also an American producer signed PLUSS, also written A+, associated with the EarDrummers label, credited on major American rap and pop records. No source consulted connects either of them to bouyon, to Dominica, to the Virgin Islands or to “Without You”. They are namesakes, and this profile borrows nothing from them: no biography, no catalogue, no figures.

The confusion is all the easier because platforms happily mix close names: A-Plus, A Plus, A+ and A-Plus Muzik can end up neighbours in search results or in automatically generated pages. The only reliable anchor, for the producer at hand, is the exact credit as published: A-Plus Muzik, with a hyphen, an s on Plus and a k on Muzik, followed by the handle @aplusmuzik1, in the official description of the “Without You” video. Any information that cannot be tied to that precise anchor should be treated as belonging to another artist until proven otherwise.

That vigilance is not a documentalist luxury. In bouyon, credits carry a direct value: they decide who gets paid, who gets booked, who gets invited and who can claim a share in the Dominican origin of the genre. Wrongly handing the production of “Without You” to a famous American namesake would erase the real work behind the record. Handing A-Plus Muzik the successes of a namesake would, in reverse, inflate his dossier artificially. In both directions, the error harms the scene this site documents. Disambiguation is therefore part of the profile, on the same level as the credit itself.

If you are A-Plus Muzik, or you work with that name, the simplest way to complete this page is to publish what is missing: a presentation, the list of productions, the official links. Bouyon Shattaz will update this profile with the published sources, as it does for every artist on this site, from founding groups like WCK to the producers of the current season. In the meantime, this page stays what it should be: short, exact, and honest about its limits.

Why A-Plus Muzik matters, and what this profile does not yet know

A-Plus Muzik matters, first, because “Without You” matters in the path of its performers. For Nice, the record is one of the four verified records of his documented catalogue, the one holding the group photograph of his generation. For Reo and Trilla-G, it belongs to the run of collective records building their careers out of Dominica. Behind those five voices there is a production, and that production has a name. Documenting A-Plus Muzik therefore completes the credit block of a record this site already quotes on several pages, and closes a question every attentive reader asks when reading them: who produced this record?

A-Plus Muzik matters, next, as a test case for the method of this site. Faced with a name without a biography, the temptation would be either to ignore it, or to invent a plausible journey. This profile chooses the third way: publish the verified fact, explain its context with facts that are verified too, the genre, the record, the performers, the circulation, and write in plain words what is not known. A reader arriving here from a search on the producer of “Without You” leaves with an exact and complete answer at the current state of the sources, no more, no less. It is the same contract as for the fullest profiles on this site, applied to a thin dossier.

The limits need repeating one last time, without decoration. No real name is known. No geographical origin is established: the record is Dominican and looks towards the Virgin Islands, but nothing says where A-Plus Muzik works. No other record produced under this name was found in the sources consulted, which does not prove that none exists, only that none is publicly documented in a verifiable way to date. No official social account is confirmed, so the sameAs list in the structured data of this page is empty, and the only recording listed is “Without You”. This profile is therefore classed, internally, as a thin profile: shorter than the usual depth of this site, because the facts allow no more.

What comes next will depend on the sources, as always on this site. If A-Plus Muzik produces a new credited record, signs a riddim, publishes an official page or is named in the bouyon press, this profile will be completed and its modification date updated. In the meantime, the recommended listening holds in one record and its neighbourhood: “Without You”, for the production itself, then the records of its performers in our bouyon playlist, from Reo to Asa Bantan, to hear what the producers of Dominica hold together, season after season, since WCK invented the recipe in Roseau.

Frequently asked questions

Who is A-Plus Muzik?

A-Plus Muzik is the producer name credited on “Without You”, the Bouyon 2025 collective record by Nice, Reo, Trilla-G, Unstoppable Shax and Quan Dawg. It is the only verified credit of A-Plus Muzik in the sources consulted, and no source states whether it is a person, a studio or a label.

Who sings on “Without You”, the record produced by A-Plus Muzik?

The official video description credits five performers: Unstoppable Shax, Nice, Reo, Trilla-G and Quan Dawg, credited as Quan in the body of the description. The record is presented as Bouyon 2025, with Spotify and Apple Music links in the description.

Who mixed and mastered “Without You”?

Mixing and mastering on “Without You” are credited to Th3rd House Studios, according to the official video description. Production is signed A-Plus Muzik and the video is by A.Nibbs: the three roles are therefore credited separately.

Is A-Plus Muzik the American rapper A-Plus or the producer PLUSS?

No, nothing connects him to them. The American hip hop artist A-Plus, of the Hieroglyphics and Souls of Mischief collectives, and the American producer PLUSS, also written A+, of the EarDrummers label, are namesakes with no documented link to bouyon or to “Without You”. The producer in this profile is identified only by the A-Plus Muzik credit and the @aplusmuzik1 handle published on the record video.

Do we know the real name and origin of A-Plus Muzik?

No. No verified source consulted for this profile gives a real name, a geographical origin or a biography for A-Plus Muzik. The record he produced is Dominican and its keywords cite the US Virgin Islands, but the origin of the producer himself is not established, and this site does not invent it.

Which other records has A-Plus Muzik produced?

No other record was found in the sources consulted: “Without You” is, to date, the only publicly documented and verifiable production under the name A-Plus Muzik. If further credits are published, this profile will be completed with their sources.
