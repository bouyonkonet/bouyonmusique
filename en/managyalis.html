Managyalis: Ambiance, Are U Feeling and Charo, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Managyalis is a voice heard around the keyboardist and producer Jixels. The clearest title for this site is “AMBIANCE”, shared with Sheyta and released as an official video on the Jixels channel on July 22, 2026 with the hashtag #BOUYON in the title, over a production by Diixly. Around that title, two other recordings carry the name in 2026: “ARE U FEELING”, as part of a group, and “CHARO”, with D.O.C FIX DEM and Virtuose. This profile goes through those three titles credit by credit and also says what the file does not hold: no biography, no legal name and no published island of origin.

AMBIANCE, the title that carries the bouyon label

“AMBIANCE” exists in two forms on YouTube. The first is the official video, posted on July 22, 2026 on the Jixels channel as “MANAGYALIS X SHEYTA - AMBIANCE (CLIP OFFICIEL) #BOUYON”, running 2 minutes 21. Its page gives precise credits: production by Diixly, mixing, mastering and vocal recording by Jixels, direction by G-ISLAND, and Jixels again as executive producer. The second is the distribution version, delivered by DistroKid under the JX PRODZ line, released on July 21, 2026, where the title sits under the name of Jixels with Managyalis and Sheyta as guests.

The video is what brings Managyalis onto this site, because it spells the genre out: the hashtag #BOUYON is part of the video title itself. The distribution version names no genre; it only confirms the date and the split of credits. To place this track within the wider family of bouyon riddims, “AMBIANCE” therefore remains the only piece of the Managyalis file that states bouyon on its own page.

ARE U FEELING and CHARO: two titles without a label

Two months earlier, on May 29, 2026, the Jixels channel released “ARE U FEELING”, an official video of 2 minutes 45 that brings together Lecus, Leseb, Managyalis and Sheyta. The page credits Dinho with the production, Jixels with the piano solo, mixing and mastering, Lucky and Jixels with vocal recording, JX VISION with the direction and Kenz Filmz with the camera for the pool scene. Neither the title nor the description mentions bouyon: this site therefore cites it as a collaboration from the same circle, without presenting it as a verified bouyon title.

“CHARO” shows Managyalis in another setting. The track came out on September 18, 2026 under the D.O.C FIX DEM line, distributed by TuneCore, and its video was posted on October 16, 2026 on the D.O.C FIX DEM channel, with Virtuose. The credits of the distribution version are detailed: Managyalis on vocals, D.O.C FIX DEM on synthesizers, bass, piano, drums and the whole production chain, Virtuose on keyboards and guitar, someone credited as LITLEBOY on bass, and four composer lyricists, METTE Landry, MILON Yann-Yves, CHICATE MOIBERT Manakel and FOSTER marcel xavier. Neither page names the genre, and neither ties any of those legal names to Managyalis in particular: this profile quotes them as published.

Namesakes and the limits of the file

The name Managyalis points to at least two things on YouTube. An automatic “Managyalis - Topic” page carries a title called “Wo De”, released on August 26, 2026, produced by PIZZARO BEATZ and written by Badu-Addo Henry: nothing on that page ties it to the Jixels circle, and this site treats it as another artist with the same name. A channel named MANAGYALIS also relayed “NO PWOBLEM”, a bouyon title by Uglyboy, in November 2025; that is a sign of proximity, not proof of identity, and it is noted as such.

Beyond that, the file is short. No biography, no interview, no attributed legal name and no island of origin were found in the opened pages; the Instagram account quoted in the “CHARO” description was not opened for this profile. This site therefore assigns Managyalis no origin. If new titles come out, they will be added here and tracked in new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Managyalis?

Managyalis is a voice from the Jixels circle, credited on “AMBIANCE” with Sheyta and on “ARE U FEELING”, then on “CHARO” with D.O.C FIX DEM and Virtuose in 2026. No public biography was found during this research.

Which Managyalis title is the verified bouyon one?

“AMBIANCE”, with Sheyta, released as an official video on July 22, 2026 on the Jixels channel under a title carrying the hashtag #BOUYON, over a production by Diixly. It is the only title entered in the structured data of this page.

Is CHARO a bouyon title?

Neither the video nor the distribution version of “CHARO” names a genre. The title is cited here for its credits, released on September 18, 2026 under the D.O.C FIX DEM line, but it is not presented as a verified bouyon title.

Where is Managyalis from?

No page opened during this research names an island, a town or a country for Managyalis. This site therefore assigns no origin.

Is the Managyalis of “Wo De” the same artist?

Nothing supports that. “Wo De”, released on August 26, 2026, is credited to Badu-Addo Henry and PIZZARO BEATZ, with no link to the Jixels circle. This site treats it as a namesake.
