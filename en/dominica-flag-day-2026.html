Dominica bouyon music: Flag Day 2026, Jounen Dwapo

By Smokiller · Published October 31, 2026

On Monday 12 October 2026, Dominica observed Flag Day, called Jounen Dwapo in Kweyol. DOM767 reports that the Cultural Division took its programme into schools and communities, as residents, businesses and organisations marked the national observance.

12 October, across the whole island

Guide.dm places Flag Day on Monday 12 October, islandwide, opening National Emblems Week, from 12 to 16 October. Domnitjen's October calendar describes the expected gesture: the flag encouraged to be put on prominent display, to promote nationalism, islandwide and day long.

The official season programme, published by the Office of the Prime Minister, lists Flag Day and National Emblems Week among the activities of Patriotism and Heritage Month, and Minister Greta Roberts invited Dominicans at home and abroad to display the national flag during the season.

What the flag carries

DOM767, in its reference page on Jounen Dwapo, recalls that the flag was designed by Alwin Bully in 1978, the year of independence, and that it symbolizes the island's lush green landscapes and cultural identity.

At its centre, the Sisserou Parrot symbolizes flight towards greater aspirations. Around it, ten lime green stars represent the equality and unity of the country's ten parishes. That is the reading the schools pass on during Emblems Week.

A day of schools and communities

DOM767 describes the teaching work of the day: schools run activities that teach students the importance of the national emblems, and cultural leaders use it to instil respect for the flag, recalling that its ten stars reflect the strength and unity across the ten parishes.

The day is marked by flag raising ceremonies across schools and communities. Flag Day traditions also include parades, school events and motorcades: DOM767 gives the 2023 motorcade, which started at the Botanical Gardens and travelled through several schools, as its example.

The flag in bouyon season

Flag Day falls in the middle of the month when Dominica prepares to celebrate in music: ten days later, the three nights of the World Creole Music Festival began at Windsor Park, where bouyon holds the stage under the flag of the island where it was born.

The link is not decoration. The independence season mixes emblems and sounds: the flag displayed on 12 October, national dress worn on the Kweyol Fridays, then Jounen Kweyol on 30 October. Bouyon is danced in a country that, in the same month, teaches its children what its colours mean.

Frequently asked questions

When was Flag Day 2026 in Dominica?

On Monday 12 October 2026, islandwide, per Guide.dm and DOM767. The day opened National Emblems Week, from 12 to 16 October.

What does Jounen Dwapo mean?

It is the Kweyol name for Flag Day, the independence celebrations day devoted to national pride and to teaching the significance of the national flag, per DOM767.

Who designed Dominica's flag and what do its stars mean?

Alwin Bully designed it in 1978. Its ten lime green stars represent the equality and unity of the ten parishes, and the Sisserou Parrot at the centre symbolizes flight towards greater aspirations, per DOM767.
