Bouyon songs: the selection to discover the genre

By Smokiller · Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

The classics first

Start with the founding bands, WCK, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade: that is where the genre took its form. Continue with the carnival tracks of the 1990s and 2000s, when bouyon reigned unchallenged over Dominica.

The tracks of the new era

On the recent side: Life Nice by Asa Bantan, identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals. Joli BB by Reo with Maurane Voyer, the Dominica and Martinique bridge. FIX UP by G’z Up, carried by its Ya ya ya hook. Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money, produced by Smokiller. And Mauvais Plan by HYPNO on the producer side. Frequently asked questions

What are the classic bouyon songs?

Bouyon's classics come from Dominica's founding bands: WCK, Roots Stems and Branches and First Serenade, which is where the genre took its form. Next come the carnival tracks of the 1990s and 2000s, the era when bouyon reigned unchallenged over Dominica.

Which recent bouyon songs should I listen to?

Among the recent tracks Bouyon Shattaz follows: Life Nice by Asa Bantan, identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals, Joli BB by Reo with Maurane Voyer, a bridge between Dominica and Martinique, FIX UP by G'z Up and its Ya ya ya hook, Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money, produced by Smokiller, and Mauvais Plan by HYPNO.

Which songs should I start with to discover bouyon?

Start with the classics of Dominica's founding bands, like WCK, to hear the genre's original form, then move on to the tracks of the new era, from Life Nice by Asa Bantan to Joli BB by Reo. Which WCK songs should I listen to?

The catalogue logged for WCK starts with "Culture Shock" and "Dance Floor", on the 1990 album Culture Shock, then "Follow the Leader" in 1991, "Balance Batty" on Tou Cho Tou Flam in 1995, "Pride & Joy" in 2000 and "More Music" in 2003. It is the sound of the band that created bouyon in Roseau in 1988.

Which bouyon songs have gone gold?

Two tracks are documented on this site. "Je l'ai vu", by Guadeloupean trio HollyG with Arendi, released on October 22, 2021, was certified gold single by SNEP on July 10, 2025. "Pow Pow / Allo Doudou", by Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS, was certified Single Or (gold) in 2026, with 15 million equivalent streams in France.

Who produced Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026)?

Smokiller, the artist and producer brand of Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer and the founder of Bouyon Shattaz. Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) is a Klass Money track: an original production credit, separate from the bouyon remixes that made Smokiller known. See the Smokiller page.

Who sings "Ya ya ya"?

It is G'z Up, on FIX UP, also written Fix Up (Ya Ya Ya): the track opens on that shout. A Time Warner Entertainment review dated October 16, 2026 traced it from its origin in a cypher to its release under G'z Up's name, noting his rhythm switches over the keys of producer ThaWizZarD. See G'z Up.

What is Mauvais Plan by HYPNO?

Mauvais Plan, written MAUVAIS PLAN, is the reference track of bouyon producer HYPNO, also written HYPNO!. It is built at 160 BPM, a speed he owns, and was pushed from his producer reel released in October 2026, with a loop designed for social video and dance. See HYPNO.

Which Asa Bantan song should I listen to?

Start with Life Nice, which this site treats as his leading current track: it came out on top of a ranking drawn from a radio panel tracked over fourteen days in our research. Asa Bantan, from Grand Bay, is identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals and widely nicknamed the King of Bouyon. See Asa Bantan.
