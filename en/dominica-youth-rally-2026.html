National Youth Rally 2026: Dominica's young people at Windsor Park on 29 October

By Smokiller · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026

This Thursday 29 October 2026, young people take centre stage in Dominica: the National Youth Rally brings students and youth from across the country to Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. The official programme of the 48th Independence season, published by the Office of the Prime Minister, sums the day up in one line: young people will take centre stage.

A parade of students before the country's leaders

Domnitjen Magazine's October calendar describes the event: contingents of students from schools island-wide parade in front of the heads of state and government, government officials and other spectators, commemorating National Independence Day.

Dominica News, detailing the programme published on 22 October, adds that students and youth from all over the country pay tribute there to those who fought for independence, won from the United Kingdom on 3 November 1978.

The start time: two sources, two times

Guide.dm, in its Independence season playbook, lists the National Youth Rally at 10:00 AM at Windsor Park. The Domnitjen calendar lists 9 a.m., with free entry, at Windsor Park Stadium on Bath Road, Roseau.

This page prints both times instead of merging them: the official programme from the Office of the Prime Minister confirms the 29 October date and the venue but publishes no time. Families should check the exact time with their school.

A heritage month built for the young

The official programme makes October Patriotism and Heritage Month, with particular emphasis on building national pride among children and young people. The Youth Rally is where that intention becomes a parade: the generation that will carry Dominican culture marches into the national stadium.

The Minister for Culture, Greta Roberts, invited Dominicans to support local artistes, musicians, farmers and vendors throughout the season. For bouyon, music born in Dominica and sung in Kweyol, these young people are the audience and the artists of tomorrow.

Between WCMF and Jounen Kweyol

The Youth Rally falls in the busiest week of the season: the World Creole Music Festival has just closed its three nights at Windsor Park, from 23 to 25 October, and the Madam Wob Dwiyet was held yesterday, Wednesday 28 October, at Massacre.

Tomorrow, Friday 30 October, Roseau moves to Jounen Kweyol and the National Dress Parade in the streets of the capital. The Youth Rally is the handover between the festival and Creole Day: the stadium today, the street tomorrow.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the National Youth Rally 2026?

On Thursday 29 October 2026 at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, per the official Independence season programme. Guide.dm lists 10:00 AM; the Domnitjen calendar lists 9 a.m. with free entry.

Who takes part in the National Youth Rally?

Contingents of students from schools island-wide, parading before the heads of state and government and spectators, per Domnitjen Magazine. Dominica News describes students and youth nationwide paying tribute to those who fought for independence.

What surrounds the Youth Rally in the season?

The World Creole Music Festival ran 23 to 25 October, the Madam Wob Dwiyet on 28 October, then Jounen Kweyol and the National Dress Parade arrive on 30 October, ahead of National Day on 3 November.
