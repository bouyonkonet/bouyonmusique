Link Up Riddim: Wetty Beatz's 2026 bouyon project

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Link Up Riddim is a project by Vincentian producer Wetty Beatz, released on May 25, 2026 on his label Wetty-Beatz Productionz. Four singers take a turn: Wetty Beatz himself with “Iz Yourz”, Magikal with “Whappa”, Problem Child with “Splash and Dash” and Nerdy with “Where di Party Is”. All four videos are billed “2026 Bouyon”.

A Saint Vincent riddim billed bouyon

Wetty Beatz, real name Phillip Bastien, comes out of Vincentian soca. With the Link Up Riddim he steps toward bouyon, Dominica's genre, while keeping his own circle around him: three of the four voices are Saint Vincent artists. The EP reached Deezer on May 25, 2026, credited to Wetty Beatz, with six tracks including the instrumental, each about three minutes twenty long.

The same day, the Wetty Beatz Records channel uploaded all four songs, each labelled the same way: “(Link up riddim) 2026 Bouyon”. The credits are identical from page to page. Produced by Wetty Beatz Productionz and Foxx, mixed and mastered by Wetty Beatz Productionz, art and video by the same house. Only the writer changes, since every singer wrote his own lyrics. The pages do not say who Foxx is.

Whappa, the song that carried the riddim

Of the four songs, only one really took off. “Whappa” by Magikal, Derron Rouse, Saint Vincent's 2019 Soca Monarch, was past 271,000 views when read, on an upload shared between Wetty Beatz Records and his own channel. Problem Child's “Splash and Dash” came next with just over 23,000 views, then “Iz Yourz” (7,300) and “Where di Party Is” (3,600).

On June 23, 2026, Magikal put the song back out on MAGIKAL - music with an intro by DJ-Q and a download link. This time the page says “2026 Vincy Soca”. Same riddim, same voice, two labels: the Link Up Riddim sits right on the line between Vincentian soca and bouyon. For more on how a song moves between genres, see our guide to how a riddim becomes a song.

The other voices on the project

Problem Child, who had already crossed into bouyon with Mr Ridge according to his page on our site, delivers “Splash and Dash” here. The song appears twice in the Deezer tracklist, with the same name and the same three minutes twenty; the sources consulted do not explain the duplicate. Wetty Beatz keeps “Iz Yourz” for himself: he wrote and mixed it, and co-produced it with Foxx.

“Where di Party Is” is credited to Nerdy on YouTube and to Imran Nerdy on Deezer, one artist under two spellings. His pages tie his releases to the Saint Lucia carnival season without naming his home island. Other riddims called Link Up exist on the platforms in other genres: this is the only one credited to Wetty Beatz.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Link Up Riddim?

Wetty Beatz Productionz and Foxx, according to the Wetty Beatz Records YouTube pages; mixing and mastering are by Wetty Beatz Productionz.

When was the Link Up Riddim released?

On May 25, 2026, on Deezer (label Wetty-Beatz Productionz) and on YouTube the same day.

Which songs are on the Link Up Riddim?

Iz Yourz (Wetty Beatz), Whappa (Magikal), Splash and Dash (Problem Child), Where di Party Is (Nerdy, or Imran Nerdy on Deezer), plus the instrumental.

Is the Link Up Riddim bouyon or soca?

The four Wetty Beatz Records videos are billed “2026 Bouyon”; the DJ-Q intro version of Whappa that Magikal posted on June 23, 2026 is billed “2026 Vincy Soca”.

What is the riddim's most played song?

Magikal's Whappa, which was past 271,000 YouTube views when read, far ahead of Problem Child's Splash and Dash.
