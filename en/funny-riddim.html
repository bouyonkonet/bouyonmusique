Funny Riddim: DJ Taffy's bouyon Riddim (2023)

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

The Funny Riddim is a bouyon riddim produced by DJ Taffy, known as “Riddim Beast”, at his Riddim Beast Studios. The distributor dates the “Funny Riddim (Special)” instrumental to January 20, 2023, though the first vocals were already out on January 12. It carries two of the biggest bouyon songs of that year: Shanika's “Kay ve yo”, close to 4 million views, and Nice's “Situationship”, past 3 million.

DJ Taffy's riddim, from the Riddim Beast studio

The video credits leave no doubt about who made it. “Produced by DJ Taffy (Riddim Beast) "Funny Riddim"” reads the note under “Bamboo”, and the same signature comes back under Nice, Tidy, Zwady, Ricardo Drue and Shanika. Several songs were recorded, mixed and mastered at Riddim Beast Studio, with live drums by Nikita Fridge Christmas, also credited as Drummer Fridge.

The instrumental, “Funny Riddim (Special)”, is out on the Riddim Beast Studios label. The distributor's YouTube notice dates it January 20, 2023, and the Deezer page February 11, 2023. It runs just under seven minutes, plenty of room for DJs and singers to work with.

Kay ve yo, Situationship, Tolly: the songs that carried it

The first wave came from DJ Taffy's own channel: “S.O.T.T” with Zone Rouge and Gwada G on January 12, 2023, Supalyne's “Burna Boy” with Trixx and Gwada G on February 3, then Nice's “Situationship” on February 10, past 3 million views when read on October 29, 2026. Next came “Bamboo” with Quan, Tidy's “Gwada We Going”, past 436,000 views, and Zwady's “Go Low”.

Then the riddim spread beyond DJ Taffy's own channel. Mr Ridge and PNDRN put out “Tolly” with Killy Killy, co-produced by DJ Taffy and mixed by Dadamanufakture, now past 1.5 million views. Ricardo Drue billed “Last One Standing” as the Antigua Road March 2023. And on July 12, 2023, Shanika released “Kay ve yo”, produced by DJ Taffy and shot by Voodoo Studio: nearly 3.95 million views, the riddim's high mark.

A riddim that keeps coming back

The Funny Riddim did not stop in 2023. On Deezer, Hb War released “LaCrucru SA KI FO” on August 2, with the riddim's name in the title. In January 2024, Nessa Preppy put out “Nasty Things”, which her description places on DJ Taffy's “Funny Things Riddim”, and The Fatha, Klassik Frescobar and WildxFire followed with “Instruction Time”. As late as January 2026, Yollae posted “Home By Me” with the line “Riddim: Funny Riddim”.

Bouyon is on every label: “Bouyon 2023” in the titles from Zwady, Tidy, Fowko and Gwada G, “Bouyon/Soca 2023” for Ricardo Drue, #Bouyon for Nessa Preppy. For more on the producer, see our DJ Taffy page, and for the format itself, the page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Funny Riddim?

DJ Taffy, nicknamed “Riddim Beast”, according to the video credits. The instrumental is out on the Riddim Beast Studios label. “Tolly” was produced by Mr Ridge and co-produced by DJ Taffy.

When did the Funny Riddim come out?

The distributor dates the instrumental January 20, 2023, and Deezer lists February 11, 2023. The first vocal, “S.O.T.T”, has been up since January 12, 2023.

Which artists are on the Funny Riddim?

Zone Rouge and Gwada G, Supalyne and Trixx, Nice, Fowko, Quan, Mr Ridge and PNDRN, Tidy, Ricardo Drue, Zwady, Shanika, Hb War, Nessa Preppy, The Fatha and Klassik Frescobar, then Yollae in 2026.

What is the most watched song on the riddim?

Shanika's “Kay ve yo”, nearly 3.95 million views in October 2026, ahead of Nice's “Situationship” at over 3 million.

Is the Funny Riddim bouyon?

Yes. The videos are labelled “Bouyon 2023” or “Bouyon/Soca 2023”, and Yollae's credits file her song under the bouyon genre, on the Funny Riddim.
