Bouyon music: WCMF 2026 wrap-up, three nights in Roseau

By Smokiller · Published October 26, 2026

The World Creole Music Festival 2026 is over. From Friday 23 to Sunday 25 October, Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau hosted the 26th edition of the festival, inside Dominica 48th Independence season. This page sums up what opened sources document, with no invented figures.

  4. What the wrap-up does not know yet

Three nights, a 26th edition

The festival ran over three nights, from 23 to 25 October 2026, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. Reports published the day after describe it as the 26th edition, organised by the Discover Dominica Authority and Dominica Festivals, at the heart of Creole Heritage Month and the Independence celebrations.

The official programme, announced on 19 August 2026, mixed R and B, soca, dancehall, reggae, bouyon, cadence-lypso, kompa, soukous and amapiano, keeping the Creole foundation that has set the festival apart since its start.

Opening night, Friday 23 October

The opening night report published by Writeups 24 names Colton T for the Dominican energy, Maurane Voyer of Martinique bringing shatta into the festival, the Midnight Groovers led by Chubby for cadence-lypso, and the Haitian group Klass for kompa.

The same night featured Jamaicans Capleton and Skillibeng, plus TK International and Ridge and Pudaz for Dominica. Bouyon singer Blackie Chan also came to Dominica to perform at the festival, and Ne-Yo arrived on the island ahead of his performance, per the same report.

The headliners across three nights

The launch release of 19 August 2026 named Ne-Yo, Machel Montano, Maxi Priest, Alkaline, Skillibeng, Uncle Waffles, Capleton and Fridayy among the major international names, with Klass and Tabou Combo for Haiti, Les Aiglons, Jean-Marc Ferdinand and Edday for Guadeloupe, Maurane Voyer for Martinique and Diblo Dibala for the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

The Dominican share stayed central: Midnight Groovers, TK International, Asa Bantan, Signal Band, Extasy Band, Colton T, Mr Ridge, Pudaz, Reo, Nice, Gwada G and Shaxx were among the home acts confirmed for 2026.

What the wrap-up does not know yet

The organisers have not yet published verified attendance or economic impact figures for the completed 2026 edition. This page therefore states no crowd number for 2026.

The documented tourism context is 2025: government statistics quoted by specialist press put nationwide direct visitor spending at EC dollar 405.5 million in 2025. That background is what makes the festival matter for accommodation, restaurants, transport and local vendors, and it is all the sources allow today.
