Remix bouyon: the hit-converting machine

Published October 4, 2026

The bouyon remix takes a track everyone knows, an Afro, pop, rap or R&B hit, and replays it with the codes of bouyon: raised tempo, keyboard loop, Dominican percussion and carnival energy.

The method

The remixer keeps the voice and the emotion of the original, but rebuilds the whole ground underneath: the rhythmic grid comes from jing ping and lapo kabwit, the bass becomes synthetic and fast, the build-ups imitate a carnival band. The audience arrives for the song, it discovers a genre.

Smokiller, the in-house reference

It is the specialty of Smokiller, the Bouyon Shattaz remix project: its bouyon versions of Afro and pop tracks draw a new audience toward bouyon, then toward the original artists of Dominica. A deliberate gateway strategy to grow the genre.

Frequently asked questions

Who produces bouyon today?

Producers like Smokiller (Bouyon Shattaz), Denmath, HYPNO! and Savion work the bouyon sound across riddims, remixes and original productions.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the instrumental several artists record over. Riddims sit at the heart of bouyon and dancehall production.
