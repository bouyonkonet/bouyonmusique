Remix bouyon: the hit-converting machine

Published October 4, 2026

The bouyon remix takes a track everyone knows, an Afro, pop, rap or R&B hit, and replays it with the codes of bouyon: raised tempo, keyboard loop, Dominican percussion and carnival energy.

The method

The remixer keeps the voice and the emotion of the original, but rebuilds the whole ground underneath: the rhythmic grid comes from jing ping and lapo kabwit, the bass becomes synthetic and fast, the build-ups imitate a carnival band. The audience arrives for the song, it discovers a genre.

Smokiller, the in-house reference

It is the specialty of Smokiller, the Bouyon Shattaz remix project: its bouyon versions of Afro and pop tracks draw a new audience toward bouyon, then toward the original artists of Dominica. A deliberate gateway strategy to grow the genre.

Frequently asked questions

What is a bouyon remix?

A bouyon remix takes a track everyone knows, an Afro, pop, rap or R&B hit, and replays it with the codes of bouyon: raised tempo, keyboard loop, Dominican percussion and carnival energy. The remixer keeps the voice and emotion of the original, but rebuilds the whole rhythmic ground from jing ping and lapo kabwit.

Which bouyon remixes has Smokiller made?

Smokiller has flipped Coi Leray’s Nothing Into Something and Players, Shenseea’s Vulnerability and Ayra Starr’s Heaven Baby into bouyon. His Nothing Into Something flip, published September 20, 2026, reached 854.7K views on TikTok per his own screenshots of October 19, 2026, and Coi Leray reshared it.

Why make bouyon remixes?

Because the remix is a gateway: the audience arrives for a song it already knows and discovers a genre. Smokiller’s bouyon versions of Afro and pop tracks draw a new audience toward bouyon, then toward the original artists of Dominica. It is a deliberate strategy to grow the genre.

Who produces bouyon today?

Producers like Smokiller (Bouyon Shattaz), Denmath, HYPNO! and Savion work the bouyon sound across riddims, remixes and original productions. Denmath, for example, is behind Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim, and HYPNO’s reference track is Mauvais Plan, built at 160 BPM and shared as a producer reel.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the instrumental several artists record over. Riddims sit at the heart of bouyon and dancehall production. They often carry a name, like Denmath’s Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim, and the clips producers dance to on social media have become the first step in launching a bouyon track.
