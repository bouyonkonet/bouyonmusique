Lucky lukee: the connector of the new bouyon wave

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Lucky lukee is a bouyon artist whose name is spelled, on the platforms, with a y in Lucky and a double e in lukee. Our internal roster spelled it Luky Lukee, and this profile flags that difference once, then follows the platform spelling, the one the public sees on Shazam, Deezer and Qobuz. His public story sits almost entirely in his collaborations: with Miimii KDS, with DJ SKYCEE, with 1T1, Binksdrick, Dinho, Qwann and other voices of the new wave. From An Sou in 2023 to WITH SOME BOYZ at the end of 2025, then Bode and Jouvert Morning in 2026, he builds a catalogue of short, dance built singles where his name keeps returning as a thread between crews.

One name, two spellings: what the platforms show

Start with the name, because it already says something about this generation. Streaming and identification platforms spell it Lucky lukee. Shazam gives him an artist page under that spelling, Deezer and Qobuz credit his records under the same form, and that is the name the public searches and finds. This site working list first noted him as Luky Lukee. This profile keeps the platform spelling and says so clearly, so nobody looks for two artists where there is only one.

For the rest, the public archive is thin, and this profile says so instead of hiding it. No détailed official biography, no date of birth and no confirmed birthplace was found during the research. No official social account was vérifiéd well enough to link here, so this page links to no social profile. What is known about Lucky lukee comes from his records: the titles, the writer and composer credits, the release dates and the names that keep appearing next to his.

That kind of credit built profile is common for the new French Antilles bouyon wave. Artists release singles in chains, often as crews, and the discography speaks before the interviews do. Lucky lukee is a clear case: between 2023 and 2026, the platforms list a regular run of singles, almost always shared, drawing a network rather than a closed solo career.

2023 to 2024: An Sou, MOUVMAN and 1er Feu, the first cards

The first record that stands out in identification charts is An Sou, with Miimii and Le Cus, released as a single in 2023. On Shazam it is his most identified track, ahead of the others. That is not a small détail: from the documented start, Lucky lukee moves with Miimii, who would become Miimii KDS, the singer of Se Miimii. The connection is old, it predates the big records of 2025 and 2026, and it explains what followed.

The same year 2023 brought MOUVMAN, an apparently solo single, and Bouncing Ass, with Tichouboy, Nils and Sanno, then Vole y, with Nils and LeJuh. The pattern is already there: an artist who multiplies line ups, moves from one trio to another, and plants his name in several crews at once. It is a local scene strategy, where an artist value is also measured by the doors he opens inside other people records.

In 2024, Everything is nice, with Dj Skycee, marks the public start of the axis with DJ SKYCEE, the producer and DJ who would become his most regular partner. The same year, Come Back puts him in a duo with Tiitii NBA, and above all 1er Feu gathers Binksdrick, Miimii, Qwann and Dinho around him. 1er Feu remains, on Shazam, one of his most identified tracks. The title states the programme: a collective first fire, a family photo of the new wave, with Lucky lukee at the centre of the poster.

WITH SOME BOYZ and Bode: the Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE axis

The record that crystallises his network came out on 12 December 2025: WITH SOME BOYZ, on the Sck Music prod label. The credits published on Deezer and Qobuz are precise: writers Lucky lukee and DJ SKYCEE and Miimii KDS, composers Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE, running time 2 minutes 18. The record is crédited to Lucky lukee, with Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE featured on identification platforms. To date, it is the clearest signature of his positioning: his voice, shared writing, and the production of his close circle.

The direct sequel arrived on 5 June 2026 with Bode, released by Stormwave Entertainment and crédited to DJ SKYCEE, with Miimii KDS and Lucky lukee. The listed writers are Miimii KDS, Lucky lukee, Lucas Gemain and Emilie Afoy, and the composers are DJ SKYCEE and Yanis Batta. The same crew is there, with rotating roles: this time the DJ carries the title, and Lucky lukee steps in as a guest voice. That rotation of roles, from one record to the next, is typical of Guadeloupe bouyon crews, where the same core signs records under different names depending on who carries the track.

Around those two records, the catalogue continues: Uhdahdah #2 with 1T1 and Qwann in 2025, a turn on 1t1 GardaVou with DJ SOFTEE, and on DJ LUCHSHIY Shake it with Les p tits du RZT and Miimii. In 2026, Jouvert Morning gathers Jixels, Jicypie and Cloclo, F**k it puts him with LMS, and Friday Night adds LMS, Le Yo, Le Cus and D.O.C FIX DEM. The word jouvert, naming the early morning opening of carnival, anchors that record in the Antillean festive calendar, where bouyon lives first.

What his catalogue says, and what this profile does not know

Read end to end, Lucky lukee catalogue tells the story of a collaboration artist. Of the fifteen or so records listed by the platforms between 2023 and 2026, almost none is strictly solo, and the same names keep returning: Miimii KDS, DJ SKYCEE, 1T1, Qwann, Le Cus, LMS. That is not a gap, it is a format. Modern bouyon in the French Antilles works in crews, shared riddims and collective records, and Lucky lukee is a visible cog in it, the one who connects the most exposed singer of his generation to her producers.

This profile also states its limits, because they are part of the record. Lucky lukee exact origin is not documented in the sources opened for this page: the scene of his collaborations is Guadeloupe, through Miimii KDS, an artist from Guadeloupe, and the Guadeloupe crews around him, but no personal birthplace is printed here for lack of a source. A record titled ON DOZ appears in Miimii KDS discography listings, but its published credits name Miky Ding La as artist and writer: this profile therefore does not credit it to Lucky lukee, despite listings that place it next to his catalogue. If an official source one day confirms that record or a biography, this page will be completed and dated.

To listen, start with An Sou, the most identified track on Shazam, then WITH SOME BOYZ and Bode for the axis with Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE, and keep an eye on our bouyon playlist, which follows this new wave record by record. Lucky lukee is not a one hit artist: he is a name that keeps coming back, and in bouyon, the names that keep coming back are the ones that count.

Frequently asked questions

How is Lucky lukee's name spelled?

The platforms spell it Lucky lukee, with a y in Lucky and a double e in lukee. This site internal list spelled it Luky Lukee. This profile follows the platform spelling used by Shazam, Deezer and Qobuz, and flags the difference to avoid confusion.

What is Lucky lukee's best known record?

On Shazam, his most identified track is An Sou, with Miimii and Le Cus, released in 2023. Next come 1er Feu, with Binksdrick, Miimii, Qwann and Dinho, then WITH SOME BOYZ, with Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE. The three records share one thing: Miimii, who became Miimii KDS, sings on all of them.

Who wrote and composed WITH SOME BOYZ?

According to the credits published on Deezer and Qobuz, WITH SOME BOYZ, released on 12 December 2025 on Sck Music prod, has writers Lucky lukee and DJ SKYCEE and Miimii KDS, and composers Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE. The record runs 2 minutes 18.

Did Lucky lukee really sing on ON DOZ?

This profile does not confirm it, and that is an answer few listings give. A record titled ON DOZ appears in Miimii KDS discographies, but its published credits name Miky Ding La as artist and writer, with different composers. With no credit naming Lucky lukee, that record is not counted in his discography here.

Who does Lucky lukee collaborate with most often?

With Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE, present from An Sou in 2023 to Bode in 2026, through WITH SOME BOYZ in 2025. The platforms also regularly list 1T1, Qwann, Le Cus and LMS on his records: of the fifteen or so titles listed between 2023 and 2026, almost none is strictly solo.

Where should I start listening to Lucky lukee?

Start with An Sou, his most identified track on Shazam, then 1er Feu, WITH SOME BOYZ and Bode for the axis with Miimii KDS and DJ SKYCEE. To hear the new bouyon wave he moves in, then follow our bouyon playlist.

Who is Lucky lukee?

Lucky lukee is a bouyon artist from the Guadeloupe scene whose public story sits mostly in his collaborations, with Miimii KDS, DJ SKYCEE, 1T1, Qwann or Binksdrick. From An Sou in 2023 to WITH SOME BOYZ at the end of 2025, then Bode and Jouvert Morning in 2026, he releases short, dance built singles, almost always shared.

Who produced Bode with Lucky lukee?

Bode, released on 5 June 2026 by Stormwave Entertainment, is credited to DJ SKYCEE, with Miimii KDS and Lucky lukee as guests. The published composers are DJ SKYCEE and Yanis Batta, and the writers Miimii KDS, Lucky lukee, Lucas Gemain and Emilie Afoy. This time, the DJ carries the title.

What is 1er Feu by Lucky lukee?

1er Feu is a 2024 track that gathers Binksdrick, Miimii, Qwann and Dinho around Lucky lukee. On Shazam it remains one of his most identified tracks, just behind An Sou. The title states the programme: a collective first fire, a family photo of the new wave, with Lucky lukee at the centre of the poster.

What is Jouvert Morning by Lucky lukee?

Jouvert Morning is a 2026 single that gathers Lucky lukee, Jixels, Jicypie and Cloclo. The word jouvert names the early morning opening of carnival, which anchors the record in the Antillean festive calendar where bouyon lives first. The same year he also released F**k it with LMS and Friday Night with LMS, Le Yo, Le Cus and D.O.C FIX DEM.

Has Lucky lukee worked with 1T1?

Yes. He sings on Uhdahdah #2 with 1T1 and Qwann in 2025, and appears on 1t1's GardaVou with DJ SOFTEE, and on DJ LUCHSHIY's Shake it with Les p tits du RZT and Miimii. 1T1 is one of the names that keep returning in his catalogue, alongside Miimii KDS, DJ SKYCEE, Qwann, Le Cus and LMS.

What were Lucky lukee's first songs?

His documented start is 2023: An Sou with Miimii and Le Cus, MOUVMAN, an apparently solo single, Bouncing Ass with Tichouboy, Nils and Sanno, and Vole y with Nils and LeJuh. The pattern is already there: an artist who multiplies line ups and moves from one trio to another, planting his name in several crews at once.

Since when has Lucky lukee worked with Miimii KDS?

Since the documented start of his catalogue: An Sou, his most identified track, released in 2023 with Miimii and Le Cus. Miimii returns on 1er Feu in 2024, then as Miimii KDS on WITH SOME BOYZ in 2025 and Bode in 2026, as writer and composer. That link therefore predates the big records of 2025 and 2026.
