Daly: Big Ting Poppin', Brital with Rah and Lala, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Daly, born Didier Daly, is a Guadeloupean singer who came up through rap and dancehall, born in Pointe-à-Pitre in 1978 according to the French national library. He was one of the first Guadeloupe voices on Dominican bouyon riddims: “Big Ting Poppin'” in 2009, “Brital” with Rah in 2010, “Backshot Hard” with Asa Bantan in 2012. In 2024, “Lala” brought him back to bouyon.

From La Horde noire to bouyon

The record of the Bibliothèque nationale de France describes him as a Guadeloupean rap singer, an actor and a founding member of the group La Horde noire. It lists the 2006 film “Nèg maron” among his acting credits. On Deezer, his catalogue starts with the album “Mista Dal”, dated October 25, 2007.

The video for “Big Ting Poppin'” came out in 2009, produced by Hitmaker Mizik and directed by Olivier Ladal. The song is credited to Daly with Kendel Laurent and Krishna “Dada” Lawrence, the Dominican producer on our Dada page. The upload on his channel is past 720,000 views. Our bouyon gwada page counts him among the first Guadeloupe artists to step into the genre.

The Gwadloup and Dominica connection

For the 2010 carnival he sang “Brital (Ride My Poney)” with Rah, billed in the title as the former leader of WCK. The description calls it a “gwadloup & dominica connection” and credits Dada for the composition, riddim included. It is a direct line between the founding generation in Roseau and Guadeloupe.

In February 2012 his channel posted “Backshot Hard” with Asa Bantan, announced as “new bouyon 2012”. The same channel also carries songs shared with Riddla, such as “Amizé'w”, and the “Respekte-w” remix that gathers several Guadeloupe voices.

Lala and the 2024 return

In January 2024, “Vélo” opened a new run. The song pays tribute to one of Guadeloupe's great gwoka artists and launches two series of ten songs for the 20th anniversary of his first album. The remix with VIM, on drums and conch shells, was released under the bouyon 2024 tag.

On June 20, 2024 came “Lala”, written by Didier Daly and composed with Zipbeatz, then its video tagged bouyon, now past 220,000 views. In between, he also sang “Brilé kalori” with Bilix, a 2017 single on Deezer.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Daly?

Didier Daly, a Guadeloupean singer born in Pointe-à-Pitre in 1978 according to the BnF, who started in rap with the group La Horde noire before moving through dancehall and bouyon.

What is his big bouyon song?

Big Ting Poppin', a 2009 video written with Kendel Laurent and Dada, past 720,000 views on his channel.

Did he sing with WCK?

He sang Brital (Ride My Poney) in 2010 with Rah, billed as WCK's former leader, on a composition by Dada.

What are his recent bouyon songs?

The Vélo remix with VIM, released as bouyon 2024, and Lala, out on June 20, 2024.

Where is Daly from?

Guadeloupe. The BnF record gives Pointe-à-Pitre as his birthplace and calls him a Guadeloupean rap singer.
