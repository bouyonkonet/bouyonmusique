DJ Skycee: the name Miimii KDS calls out on the riddim

Published October 20, 2026

DJ Skycee is a DJ and composer tied to the Stormwave Entertainment label, known to the bouyon audience for “Sé Miimii”, the Miimii KDS record where his DJ name is called out in the song itself: “Skycee wait, surf the riddim”. His verified catalogue holds five records between 2025 and 2026: “Sé Miimii” with Miimii KDS, “Je veux” with Shaimee, Coryy “Get Money”, which he composed, “Caribbean Love” with Kima, and “CARIBBEAN GYAL” with Atox, Billy BYBF and Tito. No published biography of the artist was found: this profile follows his credits, role by role, and states its limits. He must not be confused with DJ Skaytah, producer of “Kay Ka Fet” and “Mass”, covered on a separate page: near identical names, entirely distinct artists.

Who DJ Skycee is: the DJ Sé Miimii calls by name

DJ Skycee enters bouyon through a record that names him. “Sé Miimii”, by Miimii KDS, published on July 11, 2025, credits him as a feature in the very title, “Sé Miimii (feat. DJ SKYCEE)”, and the indexed lyrics of the song cite him twice: “Skycee wait, surf the riddim”, then “Big up Skycee timal” in the bridge. It is the classic format of the DJ in bouyon and shatta: the producer or DJ is not an invisible technician, he is a character in the record, called out, saluted, charged with lifting the rhythm again. For many listeners in the French Antilles, the name Skycee arrived through that call out.

Behind that stage role, the credits show a musician with several distinct functions, and this profile separates them instead of blending them. On “Sé Miimii”, streaming credits list him as a main artist alongside Miimii KDS. On Coryy “Get Money”, the official video description credits him as the composer of the record. On “Je veux”, with Shaimee, he is credited as a performer. Guest DJ, co-artist, composer: three different roles on three records, and that documented versatility defines DJ Skycee better than any slogan.

For the rest, the public record is thin, and this profile says so from the start. No interview, no press article and no artist biography of DJ Skycee was found. No person name appears in the credits consulted for him, unlike other producers whose listings carry a songwriter name. His birthplace is stated by no source. What his own accounts display, by contrast, is checkable: his TikTok bio shows the flags of Guadeloupe and Martinique, and his collaborations place him at the heart of the bouyon and shatta circuit of both islands. This profile holds to those facts.

Do not confuse them: Skycee is not Skaytah, not Skaylone, not a catalogue Sky

The first confusion to clear is the immediate neighbourhood. DJ Skaytah, with a t and an a, is the producer of “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)” and “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, whose credits carry the name Bradley Paulin. DJ Skycee, with a c, is the Stormwave artist of “Sé Miimii”. No record, no label and no account overlaps between the two verified discographies, and this site gives them two distinct pages precisely because search engines and aggregators mix them up. DJ Skaylone, maker of bouyon riddims on YouTube since the late 2010s, is a third artist, with no verified link to either of them.

The second confusion comes from world catalogues. The name Sky, alone or nearly alone, points to dozens of unrelated artists: an American DJ named DJ Skee, fictional characters, producers from other continents. None of that concerns the artist of this page. The only bouyon DJ Skycee is the one behind the accounts @skycee_97 on Instagram and @skycee97 on TikTok, the one named in the official descriptions of the Stormwave videos. The structured data of this page points to those verified handles, and to those alone.

This page therefore also answers a question: how can you be sure you have the right Skycee? The answer rests on three checks, all redone for this profile. The descriptions of the official “Get Money” and “Caribbean Love” videos give his exact socials. His opened accounts display his name, SKYCEE, and his official links. And his five verified records form a closed, coherent set, with no title borrowed from a namesake.

Sé Miimii: the record that made his name, and who really produced it

“Sé Miimii” is the centre of the DJ Skycee catalogue, and it is also the record where the credit question deserves the most care. Miimii KDS, the Guadeloupe artist followed closely by Bouyon Shattaz, is its lead performer. Streaming credits list DJ Skycee as a main artist alongside her, and the published title carries his name as a feature. The lyrics do the rest: the intro calls “DJ Skycee”, the pre-chorus asks him to surf the riddim, the bridge salutes him. The song works as a sung business card.

But a reference profile must also answer the question promotional pages avoid: who produced “Sé Miimii”? The published credits answer clearly. Production and composition are credited to Yannis Batta, also spelled Yanis Batta in other listings of the same catalogue, mixing to Mag, and a lyrics credit to Emilie Afoy, with a Stormwave Entertainment publication credit in 2025. Another credit line also associates the record with MF Music. In other words, the documented role of Skycee on his best known record is that of featured artist and showcased DJ, not that of credited producer. This profile states it without diminishing the record: in bouyon, the DJ who is named and called out on the riddim is an artistic function in its own right, and “Sé Miimii” is a clean example of it.

The circulation of the record confirms its place. “Sé Miimii” appears in independent selections: a soca podcast tracklist lists it as “Miimii KDS x DJ Skycee - Se Miimii”, and a 2026 Antilles mix devoted to bouyon, kompa and shatta in the French Antilles programmes it between Meryl and the Lestef KJF Boyz. A Martinique percussion creator even saluted publicly, in a post that travelled widely, “ton son de bouyon avec DJ Skycee”, your bouyon sound with DJ Skycee, addressed to Miimii. The record therefore lives beyond the platforms: in mixes, in percussion challenges and in the conversation of both islands. For the context of the lead artist, read our Miimii KDS page, and to listen, our bouyon playlist.

Stormwave: the label behind Get Money and Caribbean Love

The other pole of the DJ Skycee catalogue is called Stormwave Entertainment, the label credited on “Sé Miimii” and on his two 2026 videos. “Get Money”, the Coryy single featuring DJ Skycee, is presented by its official description as “le dernier single de @coryy avec @skycee_97”, the latest single by Coryy with Skycee, with this decisive line: “Composé par Skycee”, composed by Skycee. Mixing and mastering are by HYT, the video is directed by ADN and co-directed by Fwame Studio, and the copyright is Stormwave ENT., 2026. It is the clearest composer credit in the Skycee file: on this record he is not only the guest DJ, he is the author of the music.

“Caribbean Love”, released under the name “DJ SKYCEE & Kima”, follows the same circuit. The official video description states that the record, featuring Kima, is “maintenant disponible sur toutes les plateformes”, now available on all platforms, credits Stormwave and Alyssa Gaudens as directors, Alyssa Gaudens on filming and editing, and the copyright Stormwave ENT., 2026. The same description gives his official socials, @skycee_97 on Instagram and @skycee97 on TikTok, which this profile opened and verified. A DJ releases listing gives the record a length of 2 minutes 54. The video text speaks of honouring Caribbean talents and personalities and of pride in representing the Caribbean: that is the positioning of the label, quoted as such, not a biography of the artist.

Stormwave therefore gives DJ Skycee what DJ Skaytah, by comparison, does not show in the sources: a structure. A label that releases, produces the videos, signs the copyrights and connects its artists to each other, from Miimii KDS to Coryy and Kima. This profile knows of Stormwave only what those credits show, and invents neither a full catalogue nor a history for it. But to place Skycee, the fact is enough: he is a label artist, integrated into a small French Antilles stable, where many bouyon DJs work as seasonal independents.

The rest of the verified catalogue: Je veux and CARIBBEAN GYAL

“Je veux”, released as a single under the names Shaimee and DJ SKYCEE, credits him as a performer alongside Shaimee. The other credits on the listing are instructive: keyboards and writing by Yanis Batta, writing also credited to Stacy Deschamps, and recording engineering by Chatelot Oneal. The same working circle as “Sé Miimii” appears again, starting with Yanis Batta, the producer and composer of the Miimii KDS record. This profile knows neither the exact date nor the label of this single beyond its listing, and does not invent them: it keeps the title, the roles and the names the listing publishes.

“CARIBBEAN GYAL”, released on February 6, 2026 on the PlayDiTune label on the “CARNA” EP by Atox and Billy BYBF, credits him as a feature alongside Tito. The song listing, in French, tagged Hip-Hop and Rap by the platform, shows 130 BPM. That tagging recalls a truth of the current circuit: the same artists move between bouyon, shatta and Creole rap, and platform labels follow the record, not the artist. For DJ Skycee, this feature widens the picture: after Miimii KDS bouyon and the Stormwave singles, here he is guesting on an EP by other artists of the same Guadeloupe and Martinique scene.

Five records, then, between 2025 and 2026: a signature record as co-artist, a duet single, a record composed for another artist, a headline record with Kima, and a feature on a collective EP. It is an emerging catalogue, short, entirely recent, and this profile presents it as such. It is enough to establish the function of DJ Skycee in the scene: the DJ that the singers and rappers of the circuit call when they need a name, a hype voice and, on “Get Money”, a composition. To place this generation, our bouyon gwada page tells the Guadeloupe branch of bouyon, a genre born in Dominica, whose soca branch was started in 2003 by Dada according to the testimony of Smokiller.

Guadeloupe and Martinique: what his own accounts show, and what this profile does not know

Where does DJ Skycee come from? The honest answer fits in one line: no published source found says so. This profile therefore refuses the easy deductions. The “97” in his handles, @skycee_97 and @skycee97, is not a postcode and proves no origin. The “972” in his collaborator Coryy handle, cory972__, concerns Coryy, not Skycee. Two verified elements, by contrast, can be published for what they are: the bio of his official TikTok displays the flags of Guadeloupe and Martinique, and his documented collaborations tie him to the artists and the label of that two island circuit. That is a scene belonging, displayed by the artist himself, not a birthplace, and this profile presents it exactly that way.

The other limits can be stated just as clearly. No person name, no birth date, no career path, no verified concert date and no reliable listening figure can be published for DJ Skycee at this time. No account beyond Instagram and TikTok was verified by his own official descriptions, and this page therefore links only those two, opened one by one: @skycee_97 and @skycee97, with their official links out to his link pages. If the artist or his label publishes a biography, an interview or new releases, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site.

To hear DJ Skycee, start with “Sé Miimii” for the role that made his name, then “Get Money” for his composer credit, and “Caribbean Love” for his headline record. Continue with our bouyon playlist and the Miimii KDS page. And if your search meant the producer of “Kay Ka Fet” and “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, DJ Skaytah is the one you need: a different artist, a different page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Skycee?

DJ Skycee is a DJ and composer tied to the Stormwave Entertainment label, active in the bouyon and shatta of the French Antilles. He is known for “Sé Miimii” with Miimii KDS, where he is credited as a feature and called out by name in the lyrics, and for composing Coryy “Get Money”. His verified catalogue holds five records between 2025 and 2026. No published biography of the artist was found.

Who produced Sé Miimii, the Miimii KDS and DJ Skycee record?

The published credits assign the production and composition of “Sé Miimii” to Yannis Batta, the mixing to Mag and a lyrics credit to Emilie Afoy, with a Stormwave Entertainment publication in 2025. The documented role of DJ Skycee on it is that of main artist and showcased DJ: the title credits him as a feature and the lyrics call out to him, “Skycee wait, surf the riddim”. That is the precision promotional pages most often omit.

Are DJ Skycee and DJ Skaytah the same artist?

No. They are two distinct artists with near identical names, and no shared record or credit in the verified sources. DJ Skycee is the Stormwave artist of “Sé Miimii”, “Get Money” and “Caribbean Love”. DJ Skaytah is the producer of “Kay Ka Fet” and “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, whose credits carry the name Bradley Paulin. This site gives them separate pages.

What records has DJ Skycee composed or performed?

Five records are verified: “Sé Miimii” with Miimii KDS, published on July 11, 2025, where he is main artist and feature; “Je veux” with Shaimee, where he is a performer; Coryy “Get Money”, whose official description credits him as composer, on Stormwave ENT.; “Caribbean Love” with Kima, released under his name; and “CARIBBEAN GYAL”, with Atox, Billy BYBF and Tito, released on February 6, 2026 on PlayDiTune.

Where does DJ Skycee come from?

No published source found gives his birthplace or origin, and this site does not deduce it from the “97” in his handles or from the “972” in his collaborator Coryy handle. What is verified: the bio of his official TikTok, @skycee97, displays the flags of Guadeloupe and Martinique, and all his documented collaborations belong to the bouyon and shatta circuit of those two islands and to the Stormwave label.

Where can I follow and hear DJ Skycee?

His verified accounts, given by the official descriptions of his Stormwave videos and opened for this profile, are @skycee_97 on Instagram and @skycee97 on TikTok. His records are on the streaming platforms, starting with “Sé Miimii”, “Get Money” and “Caribbean Love”. For context, hear also our bouyon playlist and read our Miimii KDS page.
