Boogy Rankss: a riddim producer between soca and bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Boogy Rankss is a riddim producer whose label, Boogy Rankss Entertainment, released “Stand Up” in April 2026, the track that brings together Jamaican star Konshens and two voices of Dominica bouyon, Khallion and DeeArr. His own catalogue leans towards soca, but it carries bouyon marks written in plain words: a track named “AYITI BOUYON” in June 2026, and a 2023 riddim, the Pelau Riddim, whose videos carry the bouyon hashtag. This profile puts those verified pieces together, adding nothing about an origin that no source opened documents.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

A producer, a label, a channel

Boogy Rankss appears in the credits in two forms. On one side, a producer name, credited alone with the production of other artists’ tracks. On the other, a label, Boogy Rankss Entertainment, which holds the phonographic rights of his releases distributed by DistroKid. His YouTube channel gathers his riddims in dated series: the Dutty Dutty Riddim in 2022, the Pelau Riddim in 2023, the Mean Girls Riddim in 2024, almost all labelled soca in their titles.

As often in the riddim world, his name sits in the video titles next to the singers’ names, while the descriptions specify his role: that of the riddim producer. This profile therefore presents him first as a producer, not as a singer.

No source opened for this research says where he was born, where he lives or where he works. This profile attributes no island to him.

Stand Up, April 2026: Konshens, Khallion and DeeArr

“Stand Up” came out on 27 April 2026 on the Boogy Rankss Entertainment label, in a Raw version and a Radio Edit. Deezer files it under Konshens and credits Konshens, Khallion, DeeArr and Boogy Rankss as contributors. The YouTube delivery page provided by DistroKid lists the same four names.

The official video, published the next day on the Khallion channel, credits the production to Boogy Rankss, the writing to Garfield Delano Spence, Bertist Jeffers and Donnel Romain, and carries the bouyon hashtag. It is the most visible piece of his record: a track where Jamaican dancehall and Dominica bouyon meet on his production, and under his label.

The Khallion profile on this site tells that track from the Dominican artist’s side. Seen from Boogy Rankss, it says something else: the producer does not only supply a riddim, his label releases the record.

Ayiti Bouyon and Riddim Up, Vol. 1, June 2026

On 22 June 2026, Boogy Rankss Entertainment released “Riddim Up, Vol. 1”, a seven track project signed Boogy Rankss: BBCLAATT, BON BAGUY, SLOW PEP, AYE YI YI, AYITI BOUYON, GRIZZLYYY and WILD WILD WESTTT. Only one carries the word bouyon in its title, “AYITI BOUYON”, which runs 2 minutes 50 on Deezer.

This profile keeps to the published title. The name pairs bouyon with Ayiti, the Creole name of Haiti, but no liner note opened explains the choice of that name or describes how the track is built. The six other tracks of the project do not claim bouyon, and this profile does not present them as such.

The 2023 Pelau Riddim, between soca and bouyon

In March 2023, the Boogy Rankss channel published a series of videos on the Pelau Riddim, which he produced, with Freezy as executive producer and Krome Productions on mixing and mastering. The titles of those videos carry both the SOCA 2023 label and the bouyon hashtag, often with the dennery segment hashtag as well: “Party Party” by Freezy and Zido, “Move From Deh”, “Balance” by Mata and Bronxx, “Breed Dem”, “Push Back” by Island Prince, also tagged Antigua soca, and “Doh Post Me” by Kisha Kay.

That double labelling is the most interesting fact in this record, and this profile reports it as published, without ruling on it. It places the riddim in the fast family where soca, the Saint Lucia dennery segment and bouyon cross, the family our bouyon or soca page tries to untangle.

To listen, start with “Stand Up”, then “AYITI BOUYON” and the Pelau Riddim tracks, and carry on with our bouyon playlist.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Boogy Rankss?

Boogy Rankss is a riddim producer and the name of a label, Boogy Rankss Entertainment. He produced “Stand Up”, with Konshens, Khallion and DeeArr, released on his label in April 2026, and in June 2026 he signed the project “Riddim Up, Vol. 1”, which contains “AYITI BOUYON”.

What is the role of Boogy Rankss on “Stand Up”?

He is credited with the production in the official video published by Khallion, the track came out on his Boogy Rankss Entertainment label, and Deezer counts him among the four contributors with Konshens, Khallion and DeeArr. The writing is signed Garfield Delano Spence, Bertist Jeffers and Donnel Romain.

Is the Pelau Riddim bouyon or soca?

Its March 2023 videos carry both labels: SOCA 2023 in the title and the bouyon hashtag, often with the dennery segment one. This profile reports that double labelling as published, without ruling on it.

Where is Boogy Rankss from?

No source opened for this profile says so. This profile therefore attributes no island or city to him.

Where can I hear Boogy Rankss?

Start with “Stand Up” with Khallion and DeeArr, then “AYITI BOUYON” on “Riddim Up, Vol. 1”, and the Pelau Riddim tracks on his channel. Our bouyon playlist follows the rest of the scene.
