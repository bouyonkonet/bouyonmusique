New Bouyon: one name, three stories

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

New Bouyon is the most quoted term for today's bouyon, and the easiest to confuse. It covers three distinct uses, carried by different artists at different dates. This page separates them cleanly, with their dates, because in bouyon, credit turns on who did what, and when.

On this page
  1. First use: New Bouyon Age, TiiTii NBA's EP (2023)
  2. Second use: Ricky Bishop's New Bouyon concept (2024)
  3. Third use: the generic label for the 2020s
  4. The common root: Wrecketeng techniques

First use: New Bouyon Age, TiiTii NBA's EP (2023)

The first dated use of the term belongs to the Guadeloupe scene: on March 22, 2023, TiiTii NBA released the 'New Bouyon Age' EP on GVE Music. NBA there means New Bouyon Age, the new era of bouyon. The EP, written in part by Terry Capitolin, gathers tracks like 'Bréné Sa', 'Makaveli', 'Kitty Kitty' and 'Poom Poom' with DJ Softee, in fast bouyon measured around 160 BPM.

The same circle also used 'New Bouyon Wave' in 2023, in a TiiTii NBA album and in a D0 Production single bringing together TiiTii NBA, Nils, DJ Softee, Aknose and Theomaa. No source presents New Bouyon Age as a genre manifesto: it is a record title and a scene signature, one year before the next use.

Second use: Ricky Bishop's New Bouyon concept (2024)

In 2024, Ricky Bishop, rapper and sound engineer of Guadeloupean and Malagasy origin based in Bordeaux, launched what his Deezer biography calls New Bouyon: a modern reinvention of traditional bouyon incorporating electro and trap elements. That year he released two EPs, '#newbouyon' and '#sexybouyon', with tracks like 'Anlè Mwen', which Deezer calls a minor hit, then the album '#newbouyonexperience' in 2025.

French press relayed his formula: the magazine Modzik presented him in December 2024 in the 'New Wave du bouyon', alongside Théodora and EzekTheArtist, and quoted his approach, which he names 'audiocrack', a sound he defines as addictive and immersive, with Franglais over bouyon percussion. An honesty note: Bishop presents himself as the concept's introducer, and sources repeat his own claim; no independent outlet crowns him creator of a genre, and TiiTii NBA's use predates his by a year.

Third use: the generic label for the 2020s

The third use is descriptive: in mixes, playlists and encyclopedias, New Bouyon increasingly names contemporary bouyon in general, the 2020s productions blending hip hop, trap, contemporary R&B and dancehall. Smokiller uses it that way in his Bouyon Shattaz mixes, for example in his 'Best New Bouyon Songs' selections.

In that broad use, the term meets RnBouyon: two young labels, born in the 2020s, for productions sharing one technical DNA. This site uses New Bouyon in the generic sense only when context is clear, and always attributes the signed uses to their authors and dates.

The common root: Wrecketeng techniques

Why do these new labels sound familiar to Dominican ears? Because their techniques are not new in Dominica. Sampled melodies, programmed percussion, electronic basslines, vocals influenced by rap and dancehall: that is the grammar of Wrecketeng, practised in Roseau since the late 1990s. Wikipedia puts it in both languages: Wrecketeng opened the way to what is often called New Bouyon today.

Bouyon Shattaz's position is therefore double, and owned: New Bouyon is a useful term for today's bouyon, and its technical roots are Dominican and old. Documenting both, the French and Antillean artists' brands and the method born in Dominica, is exactly this site's work.

Frequently asked questions

Who invented New Bouyon?

No single person invented it: the term has three dated uses. TiiTii NBA's EP “New Bouyon Age”, released on March 22, 2023, Ricky Bishop's New Bouyon concept, launched in 2024 according to his Deezer biography, and a generic use for 2020s bouyon. No independent source names a single inventor of the genre.

What is the difference between New Bouyon and New Bouyon Age?

New Bouyon Age is the title of TiiTii NBA's EP released in 2023 on GVE Music, and NBA in his name stands for New Bouyon Age. New Bouyon, without Age, is the concept carried by Ricky Bishop from 2024, with the EPs “#newbouyon” and “#sexybouyon”. Two artists, two dates, two uses not to be confused.

Is New Bouyon a new genre?

It is a 2020s label for productions fusing bouyon, trap, electro, hip hop and R&B. Its production techniques, sampled melodies, programmed percussion and electronic bass, descend from Dominican Wrecketeng, practised in Roseau since the late 1990s. Wikipedia states in both languages that Wrecketeng paved the way for New Bouyon.

What is the New Bouyon Age EP?

“New Bouyon Age” is TiiTii NBA's EP released on March 22, 2023 on GVE Music, written notably by Terry Capitolin. It gathers tracks such as “Bréné Sa”, “Makaveli”, “Kitty Kitty” and “Poom Poom” with DJ Softee, in fast bouyon measured around 160 BPM. It is the first dated use of the term.

What is New Bouyon Wave?

New Bouyon Wave is a variant used in 2023 by the same Guadeloupean circle: on an album by TiiTii NBA and on a D0 Production single bringing together TiiTii NBA, Nils, DJ Softee, Aknose and Theomaa. No source presents these titles as a genre manifesto: they are record titles and a scene signature.

Which Ricky Bishop records carry the New Bouyon name?

In 2024, Ricky Bishop released two EPs, “#newbouyon” and “#sexybouyon”, with tracks such as “Anlè Mwen”, which Deezer calls a small hit, then the album “#newbouyonexperience” in 2025. His Deezer biography describes New Bouyon as a modern reinvention of traditional bouyon integrating electro and trap. See Ricky Bishop.

What is Ricky Bishop's audiocrack?

Audiocrack is the name Ricky Bishop gives his approach: a sound he defines as addictive and immersive, with Franglais laid over bouyon percussion. Modzik magazine relayed the formula in December 2024, presenting him in the “New Wave du bouyon” alongside Théodora and EzekTheArtist.

Did Ricky Bishop create New Bouyon?

Ricky Bishop presents himself as the introducer of the New Bouyon concept, and the sources repeat his own claim. No independent media outlet crowns him the creator of a genre, and TiiTii NBA's use, with the “New Bouyon Age” EP of March 2023, came a year earlier. This site therefore credits each use to its author and date.

Is New Bouyon from Guadeloupe?

The word, partly: its first dated use belongs to the Guadeloupean scene, with TiiTii NBA in 2023, then Ricky Bishop, of Guadeloupean and Malagasy origin and based in Bordeaux, took it up in 2024. The techniques, however, are Dominican: those of Wrecketeng, practised in Roseau since the late 1990s. See Wrecketeng.

How does Smokiller use the term New Bouyon?

Smokiller uses New Bouyon in its generic sense, to name contemporary bouyon in his mixes for Bouyon Shattaz, for example in his “Best New Bouyon Songs” selections. That is the term's third use, the descriptive one found in mixes, playlists and encyclopedias. This site only uses it that way when the context is clear.
