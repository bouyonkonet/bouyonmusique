Bouyon music: WCMF 2027, dates set for 29 to 31 October

By Smokiller · Published November 1, 2026

The dates landed as the 2026 edition closed. The next World Creole Music Festival will be held from 29 to 31 October 2027, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, per Writeups 24, reporting the organisers confirmation.

On this page
  1. Announced at the 2026 close
  2. Night 3 of 2026, in short
  3. Why these dates matter
  4. What has not been announced yet
  5. Frequently asked questions

Announced at the 2026 close

The 2026 edition ended on Sunday 25 October 2026 at Windsor Park. Writeups 24 reports that the organisers then confirmed the dates of the next edition: from Friday 29 to Sunday 31 October 2027.

The festival is jointly hosted by the Government of Dominica, the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority, to promote the island cultural heritage and attract visitors, per the same source.

Night 3 of 2026, in short

The final night of 2026 started earlier than the first two: gates at 4 p.m., performances from 5 p.m., with a Day 3 ticket at 300 Eastern Caribbean dollars, per Writeups 24.

On stage: Les Aiglons from Guadeloupe, Congolese guitarist Diblo Dibala, Maxi Priest, Uncle Waffles, Fridayy, Machel Montano, plus Shelly and Signal Band, Nice, Gwada G, Shaxx and DJ Taffy, per the Writeups 24 report.

Why these dates matter

The WCMF falls every year on the last weekend of October, in the heart of Creole heritage month and just before Independence Day on 3 November.

For 2027, the 29 to 31 October weekend places the festival in the same window as 2026, with Jounen Kweyol and the national celebrations in its immediate wake.

What has not been announced yet

No 2027 lineup has been published at this stage. Names, prices and ticketing for the 2027 edition are still to come from the organisers.

This page will be updated when Dominica Festivals and the Discover Dominica Authority publish the first names. For now, the date already lets diaspora visitors block their trip.

Frequently asked questions

When is WCMF 2027?

From 29 to 31 October 2027, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, per the organisers cited by Writeups 24 at the close of the 2026 edition.

Who organises the World Creole Music Festival?

The Government of Dominica, the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority, jointly, per Writeups 24.

Is the WCMF 2027 lineup known?

No. At this stage only the dates and venue are confirmed. No names or prices have been published by the organisers yet.
