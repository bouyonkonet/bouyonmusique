DJ Ramses: Bouyon Chocolat and his club singles

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

DJ Ramses is a DJ and producer who has released singles with French language titles since 2023, and one of his titles carries bouyon in its name: “Bouyon Chocolat”, with Stanley Prince, released on August 8, 2025 on NWE Worldwide and Galactique Productions. The title nods to a nursery rhyme, turned into a dance track, and its lyric video presents it as the DJ’s new single. This profile is short because the record is short: one title naming the genre, a discography of singles, and no published origin.

Bouyon Chocolat: the title that brings him here

“Bouyon Chocolat” came out on Deezer on August 8, 2025, a single track of 2 minutes 12, credited to Dj Ramses and Stanley Prince, on NWE Worldwide and Galactique Productions. The lyric video preceded it by two days on the DJ’s channel, on August 6. Its description credits DJ Ramses as writer, DJ Ramses and Stanley Prince as performers, and Sino, Tutuss and DJ Ramses on production, with artwork by Aliamusaa.

The single’s subtitle borrows a line from a well known French nursery rhyme, and the whole title plays in that vein: a familiar jingle turned into a dance track. It is the word bouyon in the title that grounds the primary tie to the genre, and this profile goes no further. Stanley Prince, the guest, has only this one title under his name on Deezer, and no opened page says who he is.

To place this kind of title, our bouyon in France page follows how the word and the rhythm travel through French language productions, and our bouyon playlist gives the references of the genre born in Dominica.

A discography of singles, from Montoya to BBB

The Dj Ramses Deezer page lists a run of singles from “Ma Chérie” in May 2023 and “Si tu veux” in June 2023, then “Winehouse” in August 2024. 2025 is the busiest year: “Snaper les charos” in a DJ Sta$h remix in February, “Montoya” on February 19 and its remix on March 7, “Ohana” in June, “Bouyon Chocolat” in August, “Amina” in November and “Shatta Vay” on December 26.

The video for the “Montoya” remix, with Section Pull-Up, posted on April 9, 2025, is the most visible title on his channel; it credits Sino, Samou and Yanis on production and is directed by Yanis Djam. Sino returns on the production of “Bouyon Chocolat”, which makes him the most constant partner in the record. “Shatta Vay”, for its part, names shatta in its title, a neighbouring genre that our shatta and bouyon page distinguishes from bouyon.

In 2026, the releases continue: “Elle est bonne sa mère” in March, then “Chipies” on August 8 and “BBB” on October 16, those last two under a changed imprint, SOLD OUT Music and Galactique Productions.

The limits of the record

No opened page gives DJ Ramses’ origin, city, civil name or a biography. The French language of his titles and descriptions is not enough to assign him a place, and this site does not do so. Video view counters are not kept as lasting facts.

The name is also common, and this profile covers only the DJ Ramses of NWE Worldwide and Galactique Productions, as shown on the pages cited. If the artist publishes a biography or other titles naming bouyon, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Ramses?

DJ Ramses is a DJ and producer who has released singles since 2023, on NWE Worldwide and Galactique Productions and then SOLD OUT Music. His bouyon title is “Bouyon Chocolat”, with Stanley Prince, released on August 8, 2025. His most visible video is the “Montoya” remix with Section Pull-Up, in April 2025.

Who produced Bouyon Chocolat?

The lyric video description credits Sino, Tutuss and DJ Ramses on production, DJ Ramses as writer, and DJ Ramses and Stanley Prince as performers. The track runs 2 minutes 12 on Deezer.

Where is DJ Ramses from?

His origin is named in none of the pages opened for this research. This site therefore assigns him no country, island or city.

What else has he released?

Among others “Montoya” and its remix in 2025, “Ohana”, “Amina”, “Shatta Vay” in December 2025, then “Elle est bonne sa mère”, “Chipies” and “BBB” in 2026. Only “Bouyon Chocolat” names bouyon among the opened pages.
