VIV Beats: Pablo, a licensed bouyon instrumental, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

VIV Beats is a producer whose verifiable file fits into a single title and a set of usage terms written out in full: “Pablo (Bouyon Instrumental)”, a bouyon instrumental published on YouTube. The page opened during this research offers a commercial licence for purchase, announces a free beats pack to be found through the channel, and sets the free download rules: songs made with the beats may be uploaded to Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, provided the royalties are split and the producer is credited. That is the type beat and riddim maker model, the instrumental as the product. No biography and no origin appear in the opened source: this profile says so and sticks to the published terms.

One instrumental, and the terms that come with it

The opened page is first of all a sales page. Its heading, “Pablo (Bouyon Instrumental) | VIV Beats”, gives the title, the genre and the producer name. Its description opens with the words DOWNLOAD / PURCHASE and BUY COMMERCIAL LICENSE: the instrumental is sold under a commercial licence, like the riddims offered to singers on our bouyon type beat page. A second block announces a DOWNLOAD FREE BEATS PACK, a free pack of beats whose link is to be found on the producer channel. The keyword block on the page, Beats, Riddims, Type, Sounds, Instrumentals, confirms the positioning: VIV Beats sells instrumentals, not finished songs.

The third block is the most precise, and the most useful. Under the heading FREE DOWNLOAD RULES, the page states that songs made with these free beats can be uploaded to Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, on two numbered conditions: a royalty split, ROYALTY SPLIT, and a producer credit, PRODUCER CREDIT. In other words, the beat is free to download but not free of rights: the consideration is written into the page itself, a share of the revenue and the producer name on the release. That is exactly the mechanism our instrumental bouyon page describes, here documented in the producer own terms.

What the page says about the model, and what it does not say

The model is that of the platform beatmaker: one instrumental put forward, a commercial licence for artists who want to release an official track, and a free pack that works as both a lure and a trial catalogue. The singer tests the sound at no cost, then the commercial release triggers the split and the credit. “Pablo” also carries a character title, a short and memorable proper name, designed as a catalogue tag rather than as the title of a sung song: nobody performs on the opened page, and no vocalist is credited there.

For the rest, the page says nothing, and this profile does not fill in the blanks. No precise release date was read on the opened page, no tempo is listed, no other VIV Beats title was verified during this research, and no channel link was opened directly: the page points to the channel for the free pack, without this profile having verified that link. Above all, no origin is named: not Dominica, not Guadeloupe, not Martinique, not any other island. A licensed bouyon instrumental gives its producer no island, and this page leaves the question open, as with the other thin file riddim makers in our bouyon playlist circuit.

The limits of the file, stated

This profile ends with its limits, because they describe exactly the state of the VIV Beats file on October 20, 2026. Single source: one opened YouTube page, carrying a title and licence terms. No biography was found. No origin is named in the source. No second title was verified, and no official link is therefore entered in the structured data of this page, beyond the title itself.

If VIV Beats publishes an artist page, more instrumentals with full pages or general licence terms, this page will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. In the meantime, the listening and the deal fit into one page: “Pablo (Bouyon Instrumental)”, its commercial licences, its free pack, and two written rules, split the royalties and credit the producer. That is little, and it is exactly what the opened source contains.

Frequently asked questions

Who is VIV Beats?

VIV Beats is a producer and riddim maker known through a single verified bouyon instrumental: “Pablo (Bouyon Instrumental)”, published on YouTube with a commercial licence for purchase and a free beats pack. No public biography was found during this research.

Which VIV Beats track is verified?

Only one: “Pablo (Bouyon Instrumental)”, whose primary YouTube page was opened during this research. It is the only recording entered in the structured data of this profile. No other title was verified.

What are the rules for the VIV Beats free beats?

The “Pablo” page states them under the heading FREE DOWNLOAD RULES: songs made with the free beats may be uploaded to Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, provided the royalties are split and the producer is credited. The download is free, the commercial use is conditional.

Can a licence be bought on a VIV Beats instrumental?

Yes, according to the opened page: its description offers a commercial licence for purchase, BUY COMMERCIAL LICENSE, under the words DOWNLOAD / PURCHASE. The prices and the licence details do not appear on the opened page and this profile does not invent them.

Where is VIV Beats from?

His origin is named in no source opened during this research: no island, no town, no country. This site assigns VIV Beats no origin without a source that names it. The question stays open.

Why is this profile so short?

Because the verifiable file is short: one opened instrumental, its licence and free download terms, and no documented biography or origin. This site prefers a short and exact profile to a portrait filled with deductions. It will grow if the producer publishes more primary sources.
