By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Redpunchdrip is a name that surfaced in 2026 in the bouyon released by Knockin Records, the label of producer Xavvoknockin. Five titles released between January and August 2026, all titled bouyon 2026, put him at the top of the bill or in a trio, with Strosey and Devon George, with VIRTUOSE or with the producer C4. His pages regularly carry the hashtag sxmbouyon, which points to the bouyon circuit of Saint Martin, but none says where he comes from: this profile leaves the origin open.

Why Should I?, the entrance

The first verified title is “Why Should I?”, posted on January 14, 2026 by Knockin Records and released to platforms on January 21. Redpunchdrip shares the mic with Strosey and Devon, whom the distribution listing names Devon George. Production is by Xavvoknockin, mixing and mastering by Knockin Records, and the title is available on the major platforms. Six months later, on July 22, 2026, the Gama Production channel posted “Why Did I?”, an answer by the same trio, produced this time by Mushroom with guitar by Jo.

Wah You Like, Don’t Play This Thing and Knock knock

Redpunchdrip also signs alone. “Wah You Like”, shortened to WYL, came out on March 2, 2026 as an official visualizer, produced by Xavvoknockin, and its tags tie it to the Wfyl Riddim. On May 27, 2026, his own channel, Redpunchdrip Music, posted “Don’t Play This Thing”, a title shared with C4, who is credited as producer. The name C4 is carried by several artists, and this site does not tie it to the existing C4 profile without a source that establishes it.

On August 11, 2026, Knockin Records posted “Knock knock”, bringing together Redpunchdrip, VIRTUOSE and Xavvoknockin, the latter also producing. With these five titles, Redpunchdrip ranks among the most active voices in the Knockin catalogue this year, alongside other releases our page on new bouyon in 2026 follows month by month.

A scene signal, not an origin

Nearly all of Redpunchdrip’s pages carry sxmbouyon or sxmbouyon2026 among their hashtags, and the tags on “Wah You Like” speak of Sxm Bouyon. It is a useful marker for placing these releases in the circuit presented on our page about bouyon in Saint Martin. It is not, on its own, proof of the artist’s origin: no opened page gives a birthplace, a home base or a legal name, and no biography was found. The file fits into five bouyon titles from 2026 and will be completed if more primary sources appear.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Redpunchdrip?

A bouyon performer active in 2026, mostly released by Knockin Records and produced by Xavvoknockin. No public biography was found.

Which Redpunchdrip bouyon titles are verified?

Five titles from 2026: “Why Should I?” with Strosey and Devon George, “Wah You Like”, “Don’t Play This Thing” with C4, “Why Did I?” with Devon and Strosey, and “Knock knock” with VIRTUOSE and Xavvoknockin.

Who produces Redpunchdrip?

Xavvoknockin on “Why Should I?”, “Wah You Like” and “Knock knock”, C4 on “Don’t Play This Thing”, and Mushroom on “Why Did I?”.

Is Redpunchdrip from Saint Martin?

His pages use the hashtag sxmbouyon, a signal of the Saint Martin circuit, but none states his origin. This site therefore assigns him no island.
