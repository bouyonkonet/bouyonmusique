Leken SM: Yè Swa, Carnaval Calling Me and Doub 6, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Leken SM has been posting bouyon on his own channel since late 2022, signing in turn as Leken, Leken(TBNS) or LEKEN SM. His most played title is “Yè Swa”, released in October 2025 under the label of DJ Leguste, with OH Yeah producing and featuring. Before that, three videos billed as bouyon mark his path: “Carnaval Calling Me”, “La Règle des 5P” and “Doub 6”. This page follows those titles one by one. It also covers OH Yeah, who is documented by that one song.

Yè Swa, with DJ Leguste and OH Yeah

“YÈ SWA (feat. Leken SM & OH Yeah)” came out on October 17, 2025 on DJ Leguste’s release page, with extra credits to coco kenny and jeremie honore. That page names no genre. The video does: posted on October 31, 2025 by Middle Vision, it carries the tags bouyon, bouyonmusic, riddim and antilles, and presents the song as Leken SM’s new track.

The video credits split the roles clearly. Middle Vision directed, DJ Leguste handled recording and mastering, and production is credited to OH Yeah, who is also the featured name in the title. In the opened sources OH Yeah appears only on this song, so this site presents him here rather than on a separate page with nothing else to say. Middle Vision’s contact address contains the number 971, but that is the video maker’s contact, not a stated origin for the artist.

The 2023 bouyon videos

The first title on the channel, “Carnaval Calling Me”, went up on December 23, 2022 with BOUYON 2023 in the title. Leken signs it as Leken(TBNS) and names TEAM BNS. Production is credited to D0 production and 1T1, recording to Urban MUSIC. The song aims at carnival season, like many end of year bouyon releases.

Then came “La Règle des 5P” on April 21, 2023, produced by DJ Luluk and billed as a bouyon video, and “Doub 6” on October 20, 2023, a pairing with the T BTS channel, again produced by D0 Production and labelled Clip Bouyon 2023. D0 Production thus returns twice in one year behind Leken, which makes it his most regular producer in the opened sources.

A Christmas track, and the limits of the file

On December 23, 2023, Leken posted “Pas fidèle (Kantik nwèl)”, a short 1 minute 29 track produced by DJ Cloclo. The page names no genre, so the title is cited here and kept out of the structured data.

No opened page gives a biography, a legal name or an origin for Leken SM. The signature variants, Leken, Leken(TBNS), LE KEN APB on an Instagram link, and LEKEN SM, all point to the same channel, but their meaning is explained nowhere and this site does not interpret them. For the wider genre context, see the bouyon riddim page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Leken SM?

Leken SM is a bouyon artist who has posted on his own channel since late 2022: “Carnaval Calling Me”, “La Règle des 5P”, “Doub 6”, then “Yè Swa” with DJ Leguste and OH Yeah in 2025. No public biography was found.

Who produced Yè Swa?

The video credits production to OH Yeah, recording and mastering to DJ Leguste, and direction to Middle Vision. The release is published under the DJ LEGUSTE label.

Who is OH Yeah?

OH Yeah is the producer and featured voice on “Yè Swa”. In the opened sources he is documented only by that title, which is why this site presents him on Leken SM’s page.

Who produces Leken most often?

D0 Production, credited on “Carnaval Calling Me”, alongside 1T1, and on “Doub 6”. DJ Luluk and DJ Cloclo each produced one other title.

Where is Leken SM from?

No opened source says. The 971 in Middle Vision’s contact address belongs to the video maker, not the artist. This site therefore assigns him no island.
