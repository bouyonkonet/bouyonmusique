Pinpin OSP: the VGM voice of Gwada bouyon, between hip hop and 160 BPM

Published October 20, 2026

Pinpin OSP is an artist from Guadeloupe whom music databases file between hip hop and bouyon, and whose verified catalogue is built around one house: VGM Product. His documented records tell a short but readable story: “Make it pop” with Edday in November 2023, “On Fwa” with Shanika in 2024, “Don't Play”, “Open Like Dat”, and an official video, “SEE U”, produced by YoJoshii and directed by Mproduction971. This profile follows those verified traces one by one, and also states what the public record does not hold: no published biography, and no source that explains what OSP means.

Who Pinpin OSP is: a Guadeloupean filed under hip hop, picked up by bouyon

Pinpin OSP is an artist from Guadeloupe. The analytics platform Viberate says it most clearly: it files him GLP, for Guadeloupe, under hip hop and alternative hip hop, and it lists him among the artists close to Aknose, LeJuh, Tiitii NBA and Shanika. That filing matters, because it states the man double belonging: trained in the codes of rap, Pinpin OSP works on productions that the scene and his own videos label bouyon, the genre covered by our bouyon gwada page.

The rest of his public identity is thin, and this profile says so from the start. No published biography was found during the research: no press article telling his beginnings, no real name published in the verified sources, no interview located. The name OSP, which he visibly shares with other artists from the same scene, among them Piscine OSP, named among the most prominent bouyon artists in Guadeloupe in a Caraïb Créole News interview, is explained by none of the sources consulted. This profile will therefore not invent a meaning for those three letters.

What stands as an identity card for Pinpin OSP is the credits. One name returns everywhere: Brian Lomba, credited as a songwriter on “On Fwa”, “Make it pop” and “Don't Play”. One structure returns too: VGM Product, label of the two main singles and the recording, mixing and mastering credit on the “SEE U” video. And a network takes shape: Edday, Shanika, producer Luchshiy, composer YasBeats, director Mproduction971, whose 971 is the Guadeloupe code. The portrait of a studio artist, surrounded by a stable team, reads better in those credit lines than in any promotional biography.

His place in the landscape is then easy to understand. Gwada bouyon in the 2020s is a team music: production labels like VGM Product run the same writers and engineers from one record to the next, and singers move from rap to bouyon depending on the production. Pinpin OSP is a pure product of that system, in the noble sense: a performer whose signature is built record after record, in company. Our history of bouyon page follows the genre from its Dominican birth to this Guadeloupe generation.

Make it pop: the duet with Edday that opens the VGM catalogue

Pinpin OSP first documented single is “Make it pop”, released on November 2, 2023 on the VGM Product label, as a duet with Edday. The Shazam listing is precise: French language, a tempo of 160 BPM, the reference tempo of modern bouyon. The writing and composition credits name Edday, Luchshiy, Brian Lomba and Corane Vincent. Luchshiy is a recurring producer on the Gwada scene, and his presence on this record anchors Pinpin OSP inside the bouyon production circuit from his first verified single.

The duet with Edday is itself a scene marker. Edday is one of the active singers of recent Guadeloupe bouyon, heard on Tiitii NBA records and on collaborations with Mr. Vegas. Sharing a single with him, at 160 BPM, on the VGM Product label, places Pinpin OSP straight away in the club bouyon format, far from the laid back hip hop his Viberate filing might suggest. The catalogue therefore speaks louder than the label: from 2023, Pinpin OSP is an artist who sings on bouyon.

The record keeps circulating in platform selections: a Deezer playlist consulted for this profile still lists it among the artist records, with a length of 2 minutes 15. That short format, around two minutes, is the format of current bouyon built for social feeds and party play: one verse, one chorus, and a riddim that never lets go. “Make it pop” sets that grammar from the start, and the records that follow confirm it.

On Fwa: the duet with Shanika, and a date that changes by platform

The most visible record in the catalogue is “On Fwa”, a duet with Shanika, a singer Viberate also files in the Guadeloupe circuit and whom the same Caraïb Créole News interview names among the most prominent bouyon artists in Guadeloupe. The Shazam listing gives a release on June 8, 2024, on the label VGM Product', in French, with Shanika and Brian Lomba credited as songwriters. The duet therefore brings together two voices of the same Gwada generation, on the VGM house, with the house writer in the credits.

This profile flags a detail here: the published release date is not the same everywhere. Shazam gives June 8, 2024. The Shanika discography published by Qobuz gives July 10, 2024 for the same record, under the same VGM Product' label. The two platforms do not agree on the day, and no primary source in the record consulted can settle it. This profile keeps the Shazam date as its main reference, flags the gap, and does not claim a certainty the sources do not give.

Musically, the duet marks the centre of the catalogue. Shanika brings the pop and melodic register, Pinpin OSP the rap and bouyon register, and the record circulates in the selections of both artists. That is the normal working of the Gwada scene: crossed collaborations move audiences from one name to the other. To hear that network in action, our bouyon playlist mixes the voices of this generation with the Dominican classics of the genre.

Don't Play, Open Like Dat, SEE U: the rest of the verified catalogue

Around the two main singles, three more records are documented. “Don't Play” first: a Deezer playlist consulted for this profile credits it to Pinpin OSP, with a length of 2 minutes 13, Brian Lomba and Yovan Andre as writers, and YasBeats, Brian Lomba and Yovan Andre as composers. The same team signature is there: the house writer, a named producer, and a two minute format cut for the dancefloor and the feeds.

“Open Like Dat” next: the VirtualDJ community bouyon chart holds it as a Pinpin OSP record dated 2023, filed under dance. That is a trace of use by DJs, who are the first carriers of Gwada bouyon: a record that enters DJ selections exists in parties before it exists in articles. “My Lolly”, a duet with Totally Spice's, appears in the artist Shazam catalogue. The Viberate analytics platform, for its part, names “Vody” and “Full” as his most popular songs, with “Blend”, “Soar” and “Rise” in the catalogue: those titles are listed here as catalogue entries published by Viberate, without a detailed listing verified record by record.

The “SEE U” video completes the picture, and it is the most recent piece in the record. The official YouTube video description credits Pinpin OSP as author, under the name PINPINOSPOFFICIEL, YoJoshii as producer, Mproduction971 as director, and VGM Product for recording, mixing and mastering. The video keywords are unambiguous: bouyon, Pinpin, VGM. The artist therefore claims the bouyon label himself for this record, with the same technical team as on the singles. It is, to date, the most complete public trace of how he works: a writer performer, a producer, a Guadeloupe director and a label that holds the whole chain.

What this profile does not know, and why Pinpin OSP still matters

The limits first, because they are part of the record. No published biography, no verified real name, no concert date published in the sources consulted, no official account confirmed through an opened and verified link for this page: the sameAs field of this profile therefore stays empty, and the name PINPINOSPOFFICIEL is quoted only as it appears in the credits of his own video. The meaning of the letters OSP is published by none of the sources consulted. Even the dates call for care, as the Shazam and Qobuz gap on “On Fwa” shows.

Why does Pinpin OSP matter, then? Because he shows how current Gwada bouyon is really made. The genre was born in Dominica, with WCK and the Roseau bands, and its full story reads on our history of bouyon page. Its life today, in Guadeloupe, sits inside teams like this one: a production label, a recurring writer, named producers, duets between singers of the same generation, two minute records at 160 BPM. Pinpin OSP is a documented cog in that machine, with a short catalogue that can be traced in full, credit by credit.

What comes next will depend on the next releases. If an album, an interview or a verified official account appears, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on this site. In the meantime, listening starts with “Make it pop” and “On Fwa”, the two fully documented VGM singles, then with the “SEE U” video, and it continues on our bouyon gwada page, where Pinpin OSP takes his place among the Edday, Shanika, Aknose and Tiitii NBA of his generation.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Pinpin OSP?

He is an artist from Guadeloupe, filed under hip hop and alternative hip hop by the Viberate platform, which lists him among the artists close to Aknose, LeJuh, Tiitii NBA and Shanika. His verified catalogue is released mainly on the VGM Product label, with records such as “Make it pop” with Edday and “On Fwa” with Shanika. No published biography was found.

What does OSP mean in Pinpin OSP?

None of the sources consulted for this profile explains it, and this profile will not invent an expansion for those three letters. The tag is shared with other artists from the same Guadeloupe scene, among them Piscine OSP, named among the most prominent bouyon artists in Guadeloupe in a Caraïb Créole News interview. If the artist ever publishes the meaning, this profile will carry it with its source.

What are Pinpin OSP’s verified records?

The records documented by platform listings are “Make it pop” with Edday, released on November 2, 2023 on VGM Product, “On Fwa” with Shanika, released in 2024 on VGM Product’, “Don’t Play”, “Open Like Dat”, dated 2023 in a DJ chart, “My Lolly” with Totally Spice’s, and the “SEE U” video. Viberate also lists “Vody” and “Full” as his most popular songs.

Why does the On Fwa release date change between sites?

Because the platforms do not publish the same date: Shazam gives June 8, 2024, while the Qobuz discography of Shanika gives July 10, 2024, for the same record and the same VGM Product’ label. No primary source consulted can settle it. This profile keeps the Shazam date and flags the gap, instead of choosing in silence.

Who works with Pinpin OSP at VGM Product?

The published credits draw a stable team: Brian Lomba, songwriter on “On Fwa”, “Make it pop” and “Don’t Play”; Luchshiy, composer on “Make it pop”; YasBeats, composer on “Don’t Play”; YoJoshii, producer of the “SEE U” video; and VGM Product, label of the singles and the recording, mixing and mastering credit on “SEE U”.

Where can I hear Pinpin OSP and Gwada bouyon?

Start with “Make it pop” and “On Fwa”, the two fully documented VGM singles, then watch the “SEE U” video. To place Pinpin OSP inside his whole scene, between Guadeloupe and Dominica, where the genre was born, then hear our bouyon playlist, which mixes the new wave with the classics of the genre.

Who produced Pinpin OSP’s “SEE U” video?

According to the official YouTube video description, “SEE U” is produced by YoJoshii, directed by Mproduction971, whose 971 recalls Guadeloupe’s code, and recorded, mixed and mastered at VGM Product. Pinpin OSP is credited there as songwriter under the name PINPINOSPOFFICIEL, and the video keywords leave no doubt: bouyon, Pinpin, VGM.

Who wrote “Make it pop”?

The Shazam credits name four songwriters and composers: Edday, Luchshiy, Brian Lomba and Corane Vincent. The song, a duet with Edday, was released on November 2, 2023 on the VGM Product label, in French and at 160 BPM, the reference tempo of modern bouyon. Luchshiy, a recurring producer of the gwada scene, anchors this first single in the bouyon circuit.

Does Pinpin OSP make rap or bouyon?

Both, according to the sources. Viberate files him under hip hop and alternative hip hop, but his catalogue sings over bouyon: “Make it pop” runs at 160 BPM, and the keywords of his “SEE U” video, chosen by his own camp, start with bouyon. Moving from rap to bouyon depending on the production is typical of 2020s bouyon gwada.

Who is Shanika, his partner on “On Fwa”?

Shanika is a singer whom Viberate files in the Guadeloupe circuit and whom a Caraïb Créole News interview names among the most prominent bouyon artists in Guadeloupe. On “On Fwa”, she brings the pop and melodic register, Pinpin OSP the rap and bouyon register. The writing of the song is credited to Shanika and Brian Lomba, on the VGM Product’ label.

What is Pinpin OSP’s most popular song?

According to the Viberate analytics platform, his most popular songs are “Vody” and “Full”. In the catalogue verified record by record, the most visible is “On Fwa”, his 2024 duet with Shanika on VGM Product, which runs in both artists’ selections. “Open Like Dat” is also kept by the VirtualDJ community bouyon chart.
