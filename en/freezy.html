Freezy: the Saint Lucian voice of the Pelau bouyon riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Freezy, who posts under the channel name Freezy madrass, is a singer from Saint Lucia whose own descriptions file him under Dennery Segment and soca. He meets bouyon through two doors: Boogy Rankss's Pelau Riddim in 2023, where he is executive producer and one of the voices, and “Waistline” with Khallion in 2026. Not to be confused with Fr3EzE, a producer from Dominica.

A Dennery Segment singer

The description of “Controller”, on the Sounami Riddim, uploaded in February 2024, runs three lines: “ST. Lucia”, “Dennery Segment”, “Soca Music”. That is the label Freezy gives himself, and the track was past 21,000 views when read. Dennery Segment is Saint Lucia's fast genre, a close neighbour of bouyon; see also bouyon in Saint Lucia.

His channel goes back at least to 2018, with “Bacchanal” on the Sick Jab Riddim. His most watched videos are “Pave It” with Noahseas on the Wetty Riddim in 2020, past 72,000 views, and “Bike Life”, released in February 2022 on a riddim by Jd Jds. The name Freezy Madrass appears in his links and on his Instagram account.

The Pelau Riddim with Boogy Rankss

The link with Boogy Rankss goes back a while: several titles on Freezy's channel already carry “pro by boogyranks”, such as “Bring A Bottle” and “No Money No Love” with Mighty. On March 20, 2023, Boogy Rankss uploaded the six songs of the Pelau Riddim, all titled “SOCA 2023” with the bouyon hashtag, and every description credits Freezy as executive producer.

Freezy sings on two of them: “Party Party” with Zido and “Breed Dem” with Bronxx, two videos sitting around 1,000 to 1,400 views when read. Zido joins him again on “Move From Deh”, credited to Boogy Rankss and Freezy Madrass on another upload. Mixing and mastering are by Krome Productions.

Waistline with Khallion, and other collaborations

On March 2, 2026, Freezy released “Waistline” with Khallion, produced by Diamond Jay. On Deezer the single runs 2 minutes 34 under the BehsonRecordz and KhallioNation Records labels; on YouTube, the video's keywords name bouyon and Dennery Segment.

Diamond Jay and Behson Recordz were already behind “Pon D Tip” in 2022, shared with Klassik Frescobar. A last word on the name: the Deezer “Freezy” page groups many unrelated namesakes, and the Dominica producer Fr3EzE is a different artist. This profile only keeps titles posted by the Freezy madrass channel or credited to Freezy on the Pelau Riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Freezy?

A singer from Saint Lucia, posting as Freezy madrass, who presents himself as a Dennery Segment and soca artist, and who was executive producer of Boogy Rankss's bouyon Pelau Riddim in 2023.

What are his bouyon titles?

Party Party with Zido and Breed Dem with Bronxx, on the Pelau Riddim (March 2023), plus Waistline with Khallion (March 2026), whose video is tagged bouyon.

What was his role on the Pelau Riddim?

Executive producer, per every YouTube description of March 20, 2023, with Boogy Rankss producing and Krome Productions on the mix.

Are Freezy and Fr3EzE the same person?

No. Fr3EzE, or Boy Freeze, is a producer from Dominica; Freezy is a singer from Saint Lucia.

Who produced Waistline?

Diamond Jay, per the YouTube description; the single came out on March 2, 2026 on BehsonRecordz and KhallioNation Records.
