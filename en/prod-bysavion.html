Savion: the new wave of bouyon producers

@prod.bysavion is a bouyon producer of the new generation. His snippets, published in short format, show how a bouyon riddim is born in 2026: a beat, a loop, and an audience asking for what comes next.

The snippet format, a new showcase

Like many producers today, Savion works in public: snippets of tracks not yet released, reactions in the comments, then the decision to release the song or not. Bouyon Shattaz relays these moments because they show the real making of modern bouyon.
