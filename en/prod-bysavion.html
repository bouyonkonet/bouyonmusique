Savion, bouyon in the age of the snippet

By Smokiller · Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Savion is a bouyon producer who posts under the Instagram account @prod.bysavion. No biography facts are in our records, no origin and no path, and this page invents none of them. His credits, though, do exist: the site's recordings layer attaches to him the Dwivayèz Riddim, a 2020 EP featuring Trilla-G, Zwady & Fireman Lee and Starsha, then the single Bikelife Class with Young Dinero in 2025, and the Real Reel page credits him with production and piano on Tropikini, released on June 19, 2025. In October 2026, Bouyon Shattaz built a clean 11.7-second cut from his unreleased snippet, a short TikTok-format clip, then published the post. Savion stands for the working method of the new generation: show an excerpt, read the reactions, then decide whether to release the track or not.

The snippet as a working method

The snippet, a short excerpt of a track that is not out yet, has become the central tool of bouyon producers. Savion works in that format: a few seconds of unreleased material, posted as a short video, and the audience answers in the comments. That answer is not a simple popularity gauge, it shapes the production itself. An excerpt that catches can become a full track, another can stay in the workshop, be reworked or be dropped. The producer no longer finishes his tracks alone in his studio, he finishes them in conversation with his audience.

That format has its own codes, which the Bouyon Shattaz coverage respects: a clean cut, without the automatic end card of the source platform, so the excerpt looks like a real publication and not a capture. The 11.7-second cut made for Savion’s post follows exactly that logic. Eleven seconds is short, but in the economy of social video it is the time needed to judge a loop, a tempo and an intention.

Our page about the producers and passers puts this format in context: bouyon has always been a music tested in public, from sound systems to social networks. What changes is the speed and the trace: every reaction is written, counted and readable again.

Dwivayèz Riddim: a riddim EP in 2020

The oldest recording attached to Savion is a riddim-format project: the Dwivayèz Riddim, logged as an EP with the year 2020. The listing counts four tracks: Déliway (feat. Trilla-G), Down Deh (feat. Zwady & Fireman Lee), System Failure (feat. Starsha) and Dwivayèz Riddim Soca 2021 (Instrumental). Three tracks sung by different guests, one of them a duo, plus the instrumental version: that is the form this site describes on its bouyon riddim page, a named rhythm on which several artists lay their voices.

The instrumental version is worth a pause. Releasing the bare riddim is, as the bouyon instrumental page recalls, a producer signing his work: the loop, the bass and the percussion without the voice, the tool of DJs and dancers. Its title also carries two details, the word soca and the year 2021, while the EP is dated 2020 on the listing: this page reproduces both as printed, without choosing between them or drawing any conclusion about the project's genre.

Above all, Déliway links Savion to an important Dominican voice. Trilla-G, real name Gael Jno Baptiste, is the Dominican singer this site presents between calypso and bouyon: Dominica Calypso Monarch in 2023, Someone Else honoured at the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, then the album Take Me as I Am in October 2025. The 2020 Déliway comes three years before that crown.

Tropikini for Real Reel: production and piano, June 2025

Savion's most detailed credit sits on another page of this site. Tropikini, the Real Reel single released on June 19, 2025 on No Turkey Music, is produced by Savion according to the official visualizer page on YouTube, and its Shazam listing credits him on piano among the performing artists. The other roles are just as clearly named: Corel Dominique as songwriter, DiYoute as engineer, Yw3 Studio on mixing and mastering. The same listing gives the track in English, with a displayed tempo of 130 BPM.

That credit counts twice. First, it shows Savion in a double role, producer and instrumentalist, which goes beyond the image of a loop maker. Second, it links him to an awarded artist: Real Reel won Rising and Emerging Artist or Band of the Year at the Dominica Music Awards 2025, ahead of Nice, Trixx, Faithii, the Riddim Knockers Band and Trixz Migos. Tropikini then turns up in Blaqrose Supreme's 2026 bouyon freestyle mix. The Real Reel profile itself makes the link to @prod.bysavion.

Bikelife Class and the producer's timeline

The last track on file is Bikelife Class (feat. Young Dinero), released as a single in 2025, the same year as Tropikini. Young Dinero has no page on this site and the listing gives no other credit: this page sticks to the title, the guest and the year.

Put in order, those dates draw a readable path without a biography: a riddim EP in 2020, two releases in 2025, a production for an awarded artist and a single with a guest, then the unreleased snippet covered by Bouyon Shattaz in October 2026. Savion therefore does not arrive on social media without a past: the snippet extends a producer method that already ran through shared riddims and guest voices.

What the Bouyon Shattaz coverage says about his positioning

Bouyon Shattaz follows producers as full artists, not as anonymous beat suppliers. The post built around Savion’s snippet, then the update of its caption under the platform’s search standard, are part of that work: making the makers of the sound findable, with their name, their account and their format. On the Bouyon producers page, Savion therefore stands alongside HYPNO, Denmath, MB On The Track and Smokiller, because audiences increasingly look for tracks by their producer’s name.

Savion is also in the warm group gathered around the Bouyon Shattaz Rights Clinic audit proposal. For a producer who posts unreleased work as excerpts, the rights question is immediate: a public snippet can be taken, reused or claimed, and being able to prove who made a loop becomes basic protection. The programme wants to set those bases straight before releases, not after conflicts.

His snippet's title, however, is not named on this page, for a simple reason: it was tentative and is not verified. Naming an uncertain title would create a false discography, which would then be copied everywhere: this site waits for the release or the confirmation, as with the tracks listed above.

The producer wave, and how to hear Savion

The absence of a public biography is itself information about the era. Many young bouyon producers exist first as an account, a sound and a run of excerpts, before they exist as a story. Savion belongs to that wave, with one difference: dated credits going back to 2020.

For the reader, the right way into this profile is to listen before reading: Real Reel's Tropikini to hear his production and piano, Déliway and the other Dwivayèz Riddim tracks for his riddim work, then the snippet covered by Bouyon Shattaz. The ROAD MIX bouyon playlist then places his world among current releases, and the history of bouyon gives the background: a genre born in Dominica, built from the start by bands and rhythm makers, where the producer was never a secondary role.

What the record does not know yet: his origin, his path, the detailed production credits of the Dwivayèz Riddim and Bikelife Class, and the final title of his snippet. This page will grow with each documented release, without inventing anything.
