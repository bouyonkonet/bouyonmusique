Shashabim: a modern bouyon voice, from Bay Bye to Believe That

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Shashabim is a singer who writes and performs her own tracks and presents her bouyon as “100% Made in Gwada”. In one year, from April 2025 to March 2026, her channel released six titles, five of which state bouyon: “Bay Bye” on a beat by D0 Production, “Pété’ Y Si Yo” with JWD Production, “Double Six”, “Dans ma villa” on MandragonBeat's Circus Riddim, which is close to 282,000 views, and “Believe That”, produced by KWAB-DJ. Her own presentation places her among the women artists who take up bouyon boldly. No legal name or birth date is published.

2025: Bay Bye, Pété’ Y Si Yo and Double Six

The channel's first title, “Bay Bye”, was posted as audio on April 16, 2025. The description presents it as raw bouyon with a glamorous touch, made entirely in Guadeloupe, on a heavy beat by D0 Production. On July 26, 2025 came the video for “Pété’ Y Si Yo”, 3 minutes 34, produced by JWD Production, with Benoit Rieu as co producer and Rayan Evans (Studio Mode) as project lead. The page text explains that the track makes you dance in bouyon mode and presents Shashabim as one of the women artists taking the genre in hand.

On October 24, 2025, “Double Six” is dated Bouyon 2025. Production, mixing and mastering go to D0 Production, recording to JWD Production. The lyrics run on the image of a dominoes game, the double six laid on the table, and the description mentions a bouyon rhythm and the roar of a T-Max. Between those two releases, “Constat”, posted on September 26, 2025, changes register: a tribute to mothers, with a French translation of the lyrics in the description and gwoka and tradition tags. That track does not state bouyon and stays in the text here.

Dans ma villa and the Circus Riddim

Shashabim's most played title is “Dans ma villa”. The audio, posted on December 5, 2025 under the label Bouyon 2026, rides MandragonBeat's Circus Riddim and is close to 282,000 views; the description speaks of a chic, bouyon world and points to Spotify, Deezer and Apple Music. The official video followed on January 9, 2026, with MandragonBeat on production, recording, mixing and mastering, and a phrase that sums up the project: modern bouyon, free attitude.

The method is that of the bouyon riddim: a named instrumental, here the Circus Riddim, over which the artist lays her lyrics. The track also came out in two steps, the audio first and the video a month later.

Believe That and what comes next

On March 27, 2026, “Believe That” is dated Bouyon 2026. Production is credited to KWAB-DJ, the handle of producer DJ KWAB's channel, and recording, mixing and mastering to JWD Production, with direction by Production Kuza. The page lists the copyright and the master rights in Shashabim's name.

In one year, three producers have worked behind her: D0 Production, MandragonBeat and KWAB-DJ, with JWD Production as her regular studio. For other women of the genre, see the women of bouyon page; for the scene, the bouyon gwada page.

Limits of the file

No opened page publishes a legal name, a birth date or a birthplace for Shashabim. Her Guadeloupe anchoring comes from her own presentation (Made in Gwada, the tag Femme guadeloupéenne), and this profile presents it as such. A Toulouse tag also appears on some pages; this profile does not interpret it. Several pages add keywords pointing to other artists: those are search tags, not collaborations, and they are not repeated here.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shashabim?

A singer who writes and performs her own tracks and presents her bouyon as made in Guadeloupe. Since April 2025 her channel has released Bay Bye, Pété’ Y Si Yo, Double Six, Dans ma villa and Believe That, all stated as bouyon. No public biography was found.

What is Shashabim's best known track?

Dans ma villa, on MandragonBeat's Circus Riddim: the audio, posted on December 5, 2025 under the label Bouyon 2026, is close to 282,000 views, and the official video came out on January 9, 2026.

Who produces Shashabim?

D0 Production (Bay Bye, Double Six), JWD Production (Pété’ Y Si Yo, Constat, and the recording of several titles), MandragonBeat (Dans ma villa) and KWAB-DJ (Believe That).

Are all her tracks bouyon?

No. Constat, posted on September 26, 2025, is a tribute to mothers tagged gwoka and tradition, with no bouyon mention. The other five titles on her channel state bouyon.

Where is Shashabim from?

Her own pages present her as from Guadeloupe (Made in Gwada, the tag Femme guadeloupéenne). No page gives a birthplace: this profile sticks to that presentation.
