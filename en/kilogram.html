Kilogram: from Vincy soca to two bouyon riddims

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Kilogram, credited as Keon Romanno Samuel, mostly sings soca, and his own videos have billed his songs “Vincy Soca” since 2019. He has still stepped onto bouyon riddims twice: “Mischief” on DJ Jeanie's Bouyon Drum Riddim in 2021, then “Hardball” on the Tequila Riddim from Next Gen Records in 2025. His biggest hit is a soca song, though, “Nina Nina”, released for the 2026 carnival season.

Mischief, on the Bouyon Drum Riddim

On July 6, 2021, DJ Jeanie posted the four songs of the Bouyon Drum Riddim, recorded, produced, mixed and mastered at Digital Empire Studio. “Mischief” was written and performed by Keon Romanno Samuel, in other words Kilogram. It is also the most watched of the four, past 12,700 plays when we checked.

Two days later, on July 8, the Kilo Gram channel put the song up again as “Mischief, Soca 2021, Bouyon Drum Riddim”, with a plain “Produced by DJ Jeanie”. The two videos sit within a few dozen views of each other. So the singer names the bouyon riddim while keeping the soca label of the year.

Hardball, on the Tequila Riddim

Four years on, Kilogram came back to bouyon with “Hardball”, uploaded on May 2, 2025 by Next Gen Records and billed “Bouyon 2025”. It is one of the six vocals on the Tequila Riddim, next to Rum Lawd, Caspa G, 101 Stunna, C4 De Crazyone and Deadly. Kilogram is credited as writer and performer.

Production is by Next Gen Records, meaning Dre Soundz and JJW Productions, with Josh “JJW Productions” Williams as executive producer and on the mix. The hashtags under the video include #Stvincent, #vincy and #vincysoca2025. Caspa G is a familiar name for him: their duet “Ban This”, on the Hero Riddim, sits on his channel from 2022.

A Vincy soca career, up to Nina Nina

The Kilo Gram channel lines up soca songs year after year: “Wata” and “Rum Trend” in 2019, “Head Numb” in 2020, “Party (We Want To Party)” in 2021, past 74,000 views, then “Rum Play” on the Big Shot Riddim in 2024. In September 2020, “Fuh Quarantine”, on Keem Production's Black Mask Riddim, went past 94,000 views.

On April 24, 2026, Swick B! released “Nina Nina” on the Hard Drive Soca Riddim, billed “Vincy Mas Vincy Soca 2026”. Written by Keon Romanno Samuel and produced by Swick B and Neppy Neptune, it had passed 356,000 views when we read it. One telling detail: it was recorded and mixed by DJ Jeanie, the same producer behind the Bouyon Drum Riddim five years earlier.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kilogram?

A soca singer credited as Keon Romanno Samuel, whose videos carry the Vincy Soca label, and who has sung on two bouyon riddims.

What are his bouyon songs?

Mischief on DJ Jeanie's Bouyon Drum Riddim (July 2021) and Hardball on the Tequila Riddim by Next Gen Records (May 2025).

Who produced Mischief?

DJ Jeanie, who recorded, produced, mixed and mastered the whole Bouyon Drum Riddim at Digital Empire Studio.

What is his most played song?

Nina Nina, on Swick B's Hard Drive Soca Riddim, released in April 2026, with over 356,000 views when we checked.

Which island is Kilogram from?

No source we opened says so plainly. His songs are billed as Vincy soca and Hardball carries the #Stvincent hashtag, but his birthplace is not published.
