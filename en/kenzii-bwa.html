Ken'zii Bwa: Elephant Man Bouyon, the 2012 title

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Ken'zii Bwa has one verified bouyon title, and it is an early one: “Elephant Man Bouyon”, released on January 13, 2012 on BOUTCHA BWA, 2 minutes 08, with more than 105,000 views on YouTube. His Deezer page counts 46 releases and close to 2,500 fans, with a catalogue that, from 2019 on, turns clearly toward shatta. The opened sources give neither an origin nor a biography: this profile describes what the releases show.

Elephant Man Bouyon, 2012

The title came out on January 13, 2012 on the BOUTCHA BWA label, as a single, running 2 minutes 08. On YouTube, the audio version distributed by TuneCore carries the line “℗ 2012 BOUTCHA BWA”; it was posted on the artist’s channel on June 7, 2018 and showed 105,139 views at the time of the check. Deezer files the title under the Dance tag.

The name of the track pairs the name Elephant Man with the word bouyon. No opened page credits Elephant Man on the recording: this site reads the title as a title and attributes no participation to him. What counts here is the word bouyon in the title itself, on an official release.

A catalogue that swings toward shatta

After that 2012 title, the Ken'zii Bwa Deezer page shows a turn. “Mode shatta” came out on February 22, 2019, “Kompa SHATTA” on March 8, 2019 and “Mode Shatta V3” on February 27, 2022. Other titles, such as “Elles veulent du bwa”, “MOZART BWA”, “BANANA” or “Coucoune Pou Nwèl”, carry no genre mention in their listing.

That path, from bouyon to shatta, is not surprising: the two scenes often cross in the Caribbean and in the diaspora, as our page bouyon vs shatta shows. But only “Elephant Man Bouyon” states bouyon, and it is the only title entered in the structured data.

BOUTCHA BWA and the limits of the file

The label on “Elephant Man Bouyon” is called BOUTCHA BWA, and an artist of that name also appears on Deezer. No opened page describes the link between that label, that artist and Ken'zii Bwa, and this site does not turn a similarity of names into an established link.

No opened source names an island, a country or a town for Ken'zii Bwa. The origin therefore stays undocumented. If a biography or an interview appears, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ken'zii Bwa?

Ken'zii Bwa is the artist of “Elephant Man Bouyon”, released in 2012 on BOUTCHA BWA, and of a catalogue of 46 releases on Deezer, largely turned toward shatta since 2019. No biography was found in the opened sources.

What is Ken'zii Bwa’s bouyon title?

“Elephant Man Bouyon”, released on January 13, 2012 on BOUTCHA BWA, running 2 minutes 08. The YouTube version showed 105,139 views at the time of the check.

Does Elephant Man sing on this title?

No opened page credits him. His name is in the title, but this site attributes no participation to him without a published credit.

Does Ken'zii Bwa make shatta?

Yes, judging by the titles on his Deezer page: “Mode shatta” and “Kompa SHATTA” in 2019, then “Mode Shatta V3” in 2022.

Where is Ken'zii Bwa from?

His origin is named in no opened source, and this site assigns him no island.

What is BOUTCHA BWA?

It is the label name given for “Elephant Man Bouyon” on Deezer and on YouTube. The opened sources do not describe that structure further.
