Jordan English: Darkers, bouyon, Barbados

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Jordan English enters this site with “Darkers”, uploaded on April 14, 2025 to his Jordan English TV channel as “Darkers (Barbados Bouyon) - Crop Over 2025”. His description presents it as his first song for the Crop Over 2025 summer and calls the sound “Barbadian Style Bouyon”, produced by DJ Taffy and shot by Coastline Productions. The upload showed about 607,000 views when opened. No biography or birthplace appears on the page.

Darkers, bouyon in the Barbadian style

The page is brief, but exact. The title shows “Barbados Bouyon” in brackets and the season, Crop Over 2025; the description announces Jordan English’s first song for that summer, spells out “Barbadian Style Bouyon”, and gives two credits: production by DJ Taffy and filming by Coastline Productions. The track runs 2 minutes 27.

DJ Taffy’s production matters. The producer, already profiled on this site, cuts bouyon titles for voices from several islands, and “Darkers” shows that crossing: a bouyon circuit producer working on a track built for Crop Over, Barbados’s festival season, where soca and bouyon meet.

An audience beyond the batch

At about 607,000 views when opened, “Darkers” is by far the most played of the Barbadian bouyon titles taken up in this wave. That is a platform count read on one date, not a chart; it only shows that the track found an audience well past a quiet upload.

The page’s tags also name other soca artists and DJs, as is usual for search. Those are not credits, and this profile does not present them as collaborations.

What the file does not say

The page calls the music Barbadian bouyon and ties it to Crop Over. It does not say where Jordan English was born, and this site assigns no birthplace. Of the 41 videos on his channel when opened, only one carries the word bouyon in its title: this one.

This profile will grow if new bouyon releases appear; our page on the bouyon diaspora places this kind of title in the travels of the genre born in Dominica.
