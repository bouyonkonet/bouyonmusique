Kéké: Time with YoungJojo and Le Dext, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Kéké is a beatmaker who also sings, and whose public file fits into four videos on his own channel. The main one is “Time”, a track he composed and shares with YoungJojo and Le Dext, posted on November 14, 2025 with the hashtags bouyon and 2026. The others are riddims, presented as Bouyon Riddim 2025 and sold on his online store. This profile is deliberately short: no biography and no origin were found.

Time, with YoungJojo and Le Dext

“Time” was posted as official audio on November 14, 2025, running 2 minutes 39. The listing credits three artists, Kéké, YoungJojo and Le Dext, a composition by Kéké, recording at DreamBest Empire Studio and cover art by Le Dext. The hashtags place the track in bouyon and in a wide circuit: 767 for Dominica, 971 for Guadeloupe, sxm for Saint Martin, with the Guadeloupe and Dominica flags.

Those tags describe a target audience, not an origin. This profile infers no artist's island from them. Le Dext, already profiled on this site, appears under his accounts og_dxt275 and ledext_971; YoungJojo is quoted as credited.

The 2025 riddims

The rest of the channel is a beatmaker's workshop. “Story Book Riddim Remix”, on September 19, 2025, and “Culture Anthem Remix”, on October 4, 2025, carry the same line, Bouyon Riddim 2025 (Prod. by Kéké), with a contact address and a BeatStars store under the name Keke160. A third remix from the same series is listed but could not be opened. For the principle of a shared riddim, see the bouyon riddim page.

The file stops there on October 23, 2026: four videos, a few hundred views each, one sung track and some instrumentals. That is little, and this profile claims no more; it will be completed if Kéké releases more titles or an account of his path.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kéké?

A beatmaker and singer whose YouTube channel carries Time, with YoungJojo and Le Dext (November 14, 2025, hashtags bouyon and 2026), and riddims presented as Bouyon Riddim 2025. No public biography was found.

Who composed Time?

Kéké, according to the track listing, which also credits recording at DreamBest Empire Studio and the cover art to Le Dext.

Where is Kéké from?

His origin is not documented. The 767, 971 and sxm hashtags and the Guadeloupe and Dominica flags on Time describe a circuit, not a birthplace, and this site assigns Kéké no island.

Why is this profile so short?

Because the verifiable file is short: four videos on one channel, only one of them sung. This site prefers a short and exact profile to a portrait filled with deductions.
