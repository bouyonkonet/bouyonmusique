Modern bouyon: what the bouyon of 2026 looks like

Published September 19, 2026

Modern bouyon is the sum of all the genre’s evolutions since 1988: the Dominican base of WCK, the energy of bouyon gwada, the network form of shatta bouyon and the work of producers who build riddims in their bedrooms between Roseau, Pointe-à-Pitre and Paris.

A digital production

The bouyon of 2026 is born on software: percussion loops inspired by lapo kabwit, sampled keyboards, basses drawn for phones and big systems. Producers publish excerpts, dance on them, read the comments, then release the long version the audience is demanding.

A networked genre

The modern scene works as a network: media like Bouyon Shattaz, bouyon pages of the Antilles, DJs from Gwada and Paris, Dominican artists. It is this network that decides which riddims become carnival songs, and it is the one this site documents page after page.

Frequently asked questions

What is modern bouyon?

It is the bouyon of 2026, the sum of all the genre’s evolutions since 1988: the Dominican base of WCK, the energy of bouyon gwada, the network form of shatta bouyon and the work of producers who build riddims in their bedrooms between Roseau, Pointe-à-Pitre and Paris.

How is modern bouyon produced?

On software: percussion loops inspired by lapo kabwit, sampled keyboards and basses drawn for phones as well as big systems. Producers publish excerpts, dance on them, read the comments, then release the long version the audience is demanding. The network of media, DJs and bouyon pages then decides which riddims become carnival songs.

What is the difference between modern bouyon and shatta bouyon?

Shatta bouyon is one part of modern bouyon: short, hard, danceable riddims made by the new Antillean and French generation for the club and the phone. Modern bouyon is wider: it also brings together the Dominican base of WCK, the energy of bouyon gwada and the whole producer scene.

Who are the producers of modern bouyon?

Among the names Bouyon Shattaz follows: Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer and the founder of the outlet, Denmath, who signs Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim, HYPNO, behind Mauvais Plan, and Savion. Some, like MB On The Track, dance to their own productions: the producer has become a face of the genre. See the bouyon producers page.

What is the difference between modern bouyon and old school bouyon?

Old school bouyon is played by live bands, with long songs designed for a whole night of partying. Modern bouyon is born on software: producers post short excerpts, dance to them, then release the long version the audience demands. Old school fans believe it keeps a better link with jing ping, lapo kabwit and Roseau carnival. See old school bouyon.

Where is modern bouyon made?

Between Roseau, Pointe-à-Pitre and Paris, often in producers' bedrooms. The base stays Dominican, inherited from WCK, but the scene works as a network: outlets like Bouyon Shattaz, bouyon pages of the Antilles, DJs from Gwada and Paris, Dominican artists. That network decides which riddims become carnival songs.

What is New Bouyon?

A name that covers three uses: TiiTii NBA's EP New Bouyon Age, released on March 22, 2023, Ricky Bishop's New Bouyon concept launched in 2024, and a generic label for 2020s bouyon. Its techniques, samples, programmed percussion and electronic basses, descend from the Dominican Wrecketeng of the late 1990s. See New Bouyon.

What is the link between Wrecketeng and modern bouyon?

Wrecketeng, a Dominican style built on sampled melodies and rap vocals, is cited by today's producers as a root of modern bouyon. It set up a logic of transformation: the melody can come from elsewhere, but the treatment, the tempo and the voice make it Dominican. Modern bouyon generalized that method, all the way to RnBouyon. See Wrecketeng.

What is a riddim in modern bouyon?

The shared instrumental that several artists sing over. Riddims are at the heart of bouyon production and often carry a name, like Denmath's Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim. Today, a riddim is launched in a fifteen-second video, the producer dances to it, then the audience demands the long version. See the bouyon riddim page.

Which songs should I play to discover modern bouyon?

Among the recent songs featured on this site: Life Nice by Asa Bantan, Joli BB by Reo with Maurane Voyer, FIX UP by G'z Up, Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money produced by Smokiller and Mauvais Plan by HYPNO. The commented selection, classics included, is on the bouyon songs page.

Is the remix part of modern bouyon?

Yes, it is one of its engines. A bouyon remix takes an afro, pop, rap or R&B hit and replays it with a raised tempo, a keyboard loop and Dominican percussion. Smokiller has flipped Nothing Into Something and Players by Coi Leray, Vulnerability by Shenseea and Heaven Baby by Ayra Starr this way. See the remix bouyon page.
