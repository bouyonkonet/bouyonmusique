Ti locksée: one voice, two verified traces, and a studio for an address

Published October 20, 2026

Ti locksée is a name in French Antillean bouyon whose public file fits inside two verified traces, and this profile gives both of them, adding nothing around them. The first is a duet: in 2022, “Sexuelle Attirance”, track eight of the album “Voyé sa alléé II” by the collective Team Bwè Tou Sa, is credited to MA6MO and Ti locksée. The second is a solo record: “Je t’ai dans la tête”, whose official video page credits Ti Locksée as writer and performer, dannyebtracks as composer, and the GAMMA LAB studio with the recording, the mixing and the mastering. No biography, no published origin and no verifiable artist page were found during the research for this profile: what follows is therefore the story of two recordings and of the circle that produced them, written with its limits displayed.

Who Ti locksée is: a name carried by two credit blocks only

Start with what the documents establish, because it is little, and precisely for that reason it must be established cleanly. The name Ti locksée appears, in the catalogues opened for this profile, in two places. First in the tracklist of a 2022 collective album, in a duet with MA6MO. Then on the page of a video released under his own name, for a record on which he is credited as writer and performer. Two credit blocks, two different seats: guest on a family album in one case, solo artist in the other.

What those two credit blocks share matters more than their rarity: the same workshop. The 2022 album came out on iMD-GAMMA_LAB, the imprint of the GAMMALAB laboratory that produces the collective Team Bwè Tou Sa. The solo video credits GAMMA LAB with the recording, the mixing and the mastering. Ti locksée is therefore not a name fallen from nowhere into a catalogue: it is a voice of the GAMMALAB circuit, the Guadeloupe studio collective whose family this site documents record by record, from Lunik to FLW and Le Dext.

For the rest, the silence is complete, and this profile respects it. No source consulted gives a legal name, an island or town of origin, a start date, an interview or a press photograph. Searches run across the platforms, from Shazam to Spotify, Deezer, Qobuz, Apple Music and SoundCloud, return no artist page under his name, no album, no EP and no further single under a lead billing. On a scene where many voices exist publicly only through their credits, Ti locksée is an extreme case: two lines of credits, and nothing else to quote.

Why publish a page on a file this thin? For the same reason this site publishes the profiles of MA6MO and Laka: listeners who hear these voices on collective albums search for their names, and they deserve an answer that separates fact from rumour. Here the answer fits inside two records and one studio circle. It is short because the file is short, not because the voice counts for less.

“Sexuelle Attirance”: the 2022 duet on “Voyé sa alléé II”

The first trace is the best placed in time. “Voyé sa alléé II”, the second album by the collective Team Bwè Tou Sa, was released on July 8, 2022 on iMD-GAMMA_LAB: that is the date and imprint given by the page of its opening record, “Game Over”, opened for this profile. The album tracklist published by Last.fm counts fourteen tracks, and the eighth is called “Sexuelle Attirance”, credited to two names: MA6MO and Ti locksée.

The shape of the album explains the place of the record. “Voyé sa alléé II” is as much a family photograph as a record: the collective covers with its name tracks carried by its members and associates, “Full Tube” by FLW with Natoxie, “O Top” and “Laisser tomber” by Lünik, “Véyé mwen” with Billy Bybf, “Mamamia” with Team Bodé. In that economy, handing a whole track to a duet neither of whose names is a billed member of the collective signals real closeness: MA6MO and Ti locksée are voices of the house, integrated enough to carry a full cut of the 2022 manifesto album.

On the record itself, this profile stays with what the documents carry. The title, “Sexuelle Attirance”, announces the direct register of bouyon gwada party records, built for dancing and for social feeds. No reliable source opened for this profile publishes the lyrics of the record, its detailed writing credits or its tempo, and no official video of the duet was found: this profile therefore describes no text, no image and no listening figures for this song. It keeps the solid fact: one duet, one track, one dated album, one identified collective.

This duet is also, to date, the only record on which Ti locksée is credited alongside another artist. His partner, MA6MO, has his own profile on this site, itself built on three credits only, this one included: the two files answer each other, two voices of the same documented margin of bouyon, known almost only through other people albums. For a listener discovering the name, “Sexuelle Attirance” is the entry point: it is where the voice of Ti locksée can be heard in its original context, inside the GAMMALAB family.

“Je t’ai dans la tête”: the solo record, and what its video page proves

The second trace changes format: no longer a collective album duet, but a record carried by Ti locksée alone. The official video page of “Je t’ai dans la tête” gives a full credit block, and this profile quotes it as printed: writer and performer, Ti Locksée; composer, dannyebtracks; recording, GAMMA LAB; mixing and mastering, GAMMA LAB; visuals directed by NagolPrD. The description states that the record is available on all platforms. It is, in the whole file, the richest document: it names a writing role, a separate composer, a studio and a video director.

Three readings follow from it, all held by the document. First, Ti locksée writes: the writer and performer credit separates him from simple guest voices who only sing a workshop text. Second, the record remains a house production: even outside a collective album, the recording and finishing are signed GAMMA LAB, the laboratory of the collective. Third, the record was conceived as a release in its own right, with visuals commissioned from a named director and a distribution announced across all platforms, not as a lost cut of a compilation.

The video page also prints, in its description, an Instagram account under the name ti_locksee for the artist, and a gamma__lab account for the studio. This profile reports that mention for what it is, a line of a video credit block, and makes a cautious choice: that account was not opened and verified during this research, so it is not carried in the structured data of this page. Linking an unverified account, on a scene where namesakes and fan accounts abound, would be a fault against the reader.

One limit must be stated with the same clarity: the description opened for this profile gives neither the release date of the record, nor its distribution label, nor its duration, nor its tempo. This profile therefore does not date “Je t’ai dans la tête” and assigns it no imprint. If a complete platform page is found, with a date and distribution credits, it will be added here and the page will be dated again. As it stands, the record stands as proof that a solo path was at least attempted, with the means of the home studio, around the voice heard in the 2022 duet.

The GAMMALAB circle: the scene that explains these two records

A file of two records can only be understood through the scene around them, and that scene, for its part, is richly documented. GAMMALAB is the sound laboratory of the collective Team Bwè Tou Sa: the label of the early days, the engineering behind the records, a name claimed inside the catalogue titles themselves, from “Toufé GAMMALAB4” to “Konfyans Gamma lab 6”. The collective has released three albums, “VOYÉ SA ALLÉÉ” in 2021, “Voyé sa alléé II” in 2022 and “TRAVAILLONS” in 2024, and around thirty releases in which a stable family rotates: a core of writers, Ludmilian Flower, Ludmael Flower and Nicolas Macbeth, and voices such as Lunik, FLW, MA6MO and Le Dext.

Ti locksée sits in the orbit of that family, in the exact place his two credits describe: not a billed member of the collective, whom no list names as such, but a guest voice on an album of the collective and an artist recorded by its studio for his solo record. That position of associate, neither inside nor outside, is one of the ordinary forms of musical work in bouyon gwada: studios rotate the voices of their network, and collective albums serve as a shop window for the whole circle, far beyond the members alone.

The audience of this scene frames the listening of the two records. Audience data for the collective places France first and Guadeloupe second: Guadeloupe bouyon today is heard between the island and the mainland diaspora, at fetes and on the platforms. “Sexuelle Attirance” and “Je t’ai dans la tête” were made for that audience, in the short and direct formats of the GAMMALAB catalogue, even if no listening figures of Ti locksée himself are published under his name.

That reading through the circle has one honest consequence: it places the artist without lending him a biography. Saying that Ti locksée belongs to the Guadeloupe bouyon circuit sums up his two credit blocks, a Guadeloupean album and a Guadeloupean studio. It says neither where he was born, nor where he lives, nor since when he sings, and this profile does not cross that line. Our bouyon playlist lets you hear the whole scene around his two records.

Confusions to avoid, and what this profile leaves open

The name Ti locksée invites two confusions, and this profile settles both. The first is internal to his own file: the two traces do not spell the name in exactly the same way, “Ti locksée” in the 2022 album listing, “Ti Locksée” in the solo video credits. This profile keeps the spelling of this page title, Ti locksée, the one of the duet that brought him into the public catalogue, and flags the variant instead of smoothing it away: on this scene, spellings move from one page to the next, and the credit block, not habit, is what counts.

The second confusion comes from the search engines. The neighbouring name Le Locksé designates another artist of the catalogues: his Shazam page, for the record “R.E.R” released on June 13, 2021 by 7Crew Ltd. on the album “Le Gentil Vaurien”, gives writers named Benjamin Leduc and Thibault Monfroy, in a hip hop and rap register. No source consulted links Le Locksé to Ti locksée, nor to the GAMMALAB circuit, nor to bouyon. This profile therefore merges neither the two names nor their catalogues: the Locksé of mainland rap and the locksée of the 2022 bouyon duet remain two separate files.

What remains is the list of what this profile does not know, given in full: legal name not published, origin not published, path before and after the two records not documented, date of the solo record not established, writing credits of “Sexuelle Attirance” not published in the opened sources, official account not verified by opening and therefore not linked, listening figures of his own not published. Each of those blanks could have been filled with a plausible sentence; this site prefers to display them, as it does for MA6MO and for Laka, rather than invent a notice.

If Ti locksée or his circle one day publish an artist page, a biography or new records, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on bouyonmusique.com. In the meantime, listening takes two stops: “Sexuelle Attirance”, the 2022 duet with MA6MO on “Voyé sa alléé II”, then “Je t’ai dans la tête”, the solo record recorded at GAMMA LAB. Two records, one studio, one voice: it is little, and it is exactly what the public record allows anyone to write.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ti locksée?

Ti locksée is a voice of the Guadeloupe bouyon circuit attached to the GAMMA LAB studio. His verified public file counts two traces: the duet “Sexuelle Attirance” with MA6MO, on the Team Bwè Tou Sa album “Voyé sa alléé II” in 2022, and the solo record “Je t’ai dans la tête”, on which he is credited as writer and performer. No official biography was found during the research for this profile.

What are the verified records of Ti locksée?

Two records: “Sexuelle Attirance”, a duet with MA6MO released as track eight of “Voyé sa alléé II” on July 8, 2022 on iMD-GAMMA_LAB, and “Je t’ai dans la tête”, a solo record composed by dannyebtracks, recorded, mixed and mastered at GAMMA LAB, announced as available on all platforms. No other record was attached to this name on the platforms consulted.

What is the link between Ti locksée and Team Bwè Tou Sa?

The collective Team Bwè Tou Sa releases the album that carries his 2022 duet, and its GAMMALAB laboratory records and finishes his solo record. Ti locksée is therefore documented as a voice close to the collective and its studio, without any source presenting him as a member of the collective.

Has Ti locksée released an album or an EP?

No album, EP or single under a lead billing other than “Je t’ai dans la tête” was found on the platforms consulted for this profile. His verifiable catalogue is made of one duet on a collective album and one solo record. If further releases exist under another spelling, they are not attached to his name in the public catalogues.

Are Ti locksée and Le Locksé the same artist?

No source consulted says so, and the opened documents describe two distinct files. Le Locksé is credited on “R.E.R”, released on June 13, 2021 by 7Crew Ltd., with writers named Benjamin Leduc and Thibault Monfroy, in a hip hop and rap register. Ti locksée appears in the GAMMALAB bouyon circuit. This profile keeps the two names separate.

Where can I hear Ti locksée?

Hear “Sexuelle Attirance” on the Team Bwè Tou Sa album “Voyé sa alléé II”, then “Je t’ai dans la tête” on video and on the platforms. For context, our bouyon playlist and our bouyon gwada page cover the GAMMALAB scene those two records come from.
