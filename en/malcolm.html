Malcolm: MANIGANCES with LaRose, bouyon, Guadeloupe

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Malcolm is a singer, songwriter and composer whom the Medley Concerts channel describes as making modern music from Guadeloupe. He is not a bouyon specialist, and this profile does not present him as one: his own pages also tag his tracks shatta and afrodigital. But two primary pages opened for this research name bouyon in connection with him. The page of his “MANIGANCES” video, with LaRose, carries the word among its hashtags, and the acoustic session recorded by Medley Concerts describes his performance as a mix of bouyon, soul and kompa. In 2026, his singles run through producers of the Gwada circuit, Magbeatz and Skycee, with Krys and then Billy BYBF as guests.

Who Malcolm is: a label singer, a broad label

Malcolm signs his social accounts as malcolmavec2l, Malcolm with two l, and his Deezer discography goes back to a first single, “Allo Mi Amor”, released on September 25, 2020, after an EP titled “Tempérance” in December 2019. Two albums follow, “la nuit nous va si bien, après l’or du soir” in January 2023 and “Demain aura le goût d’hier” in April 2024. It is a singer’s path, not a sound system one: full length records, directed videos, a consistent artistic direction.

From 2025 on, his releases carry the Epic Records and Triple-Double marks, and his video descriptions name a steady team: Triple-Double on executive production, Deuzenn on executive artistic direction, Lock Dog of Maison Quasar on the mix, Bryan Pachaud of CatsOnRacks on mastering. The hashtags he picks for “ANLÈ SA” and “HOT NÈG” are shatta, afrodigital and futurekarnival. The word bouyon only enters that vocabulary with “MANIGANCES”, and that precise point is what earns him a place on this site.

The origin this profile keeps is the one an opened page states: the Medley Concerts session presents Malcolm as a modern music artist from Guadeloupe. No other opened source gives a date or place of birth, or a civil name. This site also keeps this Malcolm apart from other artists of the same name, such as the American singer Malcolm Todd, whom search engines surface under the same name.

MANIGANCES and FUTURE KARNIVAL: the word bouyon on his own page

“MANIGANCES”, with LaRose, came out on Deezer on July 1, 2025 on Epic, and its official video was posted on Malcolm’s channel on July 10, 2025. The credited composers are Hypnotic Beatz and Ray Da Prince, and the video is directed by Plug. In the description, Malcolm lines up his hashtags: his name, LaRose’s, the title, then bouyon, afrodigital and futurekarnival. It is the only video page on his channel, among those opened, where the genre is named, and the guest, LaRose, already has her own profile on this site.

The track then joined “FUTURE KARNIVAL”, released on October 3, 2025 on Epic, which Deezer files as an album and which the “SENKLÒD” video page calls an EP. It holds eight tracks: “SENKLÒD”, “ANLÈ SA”, “FLORA COQUEREL”, “MANIGANCES”, “TOUT ÇA C’EST MOI”, “HOT NÈG”, “CORPS À CORPS” and “PIMPON”. The project name states its idea, a carnival projected into the future, and the labels on its videos show that it draws on several Caribbean sources at once, shatta first, bouyon on “MANIGANCES”.

It would therefore be wrong to call “FUTURE KARNIVAL” a bouyon record. What the opened pages establish is narrower and firmer: one track of the project is presented by the artist himself under the bouyon label, beside other labels, and that track is a duet with LaRose.

2026: CHALÈ, SAFE PLACE and the producers of the circuit

In 2026, Malcolm’s production moves clearly closer to names the Guadeloupe bouyon circuit knows. “CHALÈ”, with Krys, came out on April 8, 2026 on Triple-Double, produced by Magbeatz and Skycee, with Magbeatz on mix and master. The video, directed by Plusdegrain, gathers a large family of extras credited one by one.

“SAFE PLACE (TAKE MY MONEY)”, with Billy BYBF, followed on June 10, 2026, produced by ATOX and Magbeatz, mixed and mastered at HYT REC of Maison Quasar, and filmed by Yanné Ramakers on a boxing ring set, with boxers and ring girls in the credits. Neither of these two video pages names a genre. The tie of these tracks to bouyon therefore rests on their producers and guests, already profiled on this site, and not on a label.

The rest of the year keeps the same pace of singles: “PAPI CHULO” on May 15, “Miss B” on July 10, then “TOULOULOU” with Jahlys on September 16, 2026, on Triple-Double. None of those three pages was opened with a genre statement, and this profile assigns them none.

Medley Concerts: bouyon, soul and kompa, acoustic

On September 2, 2026, the Medley Concerts channel, an acoustic concert platform devoted to emerging Caribbean artists, published a session by Malcolm. He sings “Corps à corps”, “Safe Place” and “Senklod”, accompanied on guitar by Josué Genteuil. The session notes describe him as a singer, songwriter and composer of modern music from Guadeloupe, and call his performance a mix of bouyon, soul and kompa.

It is an interesting reading, because it strips bouyon of what usually defines it, the fast tempo and the keyboards, and keeps only the vocal line and the manner. It remains Medley Concerts’ reading, and this profile quotes it as such. To hear Gwada bouyon in its dance form, our bouyon gwada page and our bouyon playlist give the context.

The limits of the record are clear. Two opened pages name bouyon, one written by the artist, the other by a session platform. No biography, no interview and no official account were opened. Video view counts were not used as indicators. Any confirmed element will complete this profile with its date.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Malcolm?

Malcolm is a singer, songwriter and composer whom the Medley Concerts channel presents as a modern music artist from Guadeloupe. His releases since 2025 carry the Epic Records and Triple-Double marks, including the “FUTURE KARNIVAL” EP, released on October 3, 2025, and in 2026 the singles “CHALÈ” with Krys and “SAFE PLACE (TAKE MY MONEY)” with Billy BYBF.

Does Malcolm make bouyon?

In part, and this profile says so precisely. His own video page for “MANIGANCES”, with LaRose, carries the bouyon hashtag, and the Medley Concerts session of September 2026 describes his performance as a mix of bouyon, soul and kompa. His other videos are tagged shatta and afrodigital. He is therefore not presented here as a specialist of the genre.

Who produces Malcolm’s 2026 tracks?

“CHALÈ”, with Krys, is produced by Magbeatz and Skycee, with Magbeatz on mix and master. “SAFE PLACE (TAKE MY MONEY)”, with Billy BYBF, is produced by ATOX and Magbeatz and mixed at HYT REC of Maison Quasar. Both tracks come out on Triple-Double Production.

What is on FUTURE KARNIVAL?

Eight tracks, according to Deezer: “SENKLÒD”, “ANLÈ SA”, “FLORA COQUEREL”, “MANIGANCES” with LaRose, “TOUT ÇA C’EST MOI”, “HOT NÈG”, “CORPS À CORPS” and “PIMPON”. The project came out on Epic on October 3, 2025. Only “MANIGANCES” carries the bouyon label on its video page.

Where can I hear Malcolm?

Start with “MANIGANCES” with LaRose, then “CHALÈ” with Krys and “SAFE PLACE (TAKE MY MONEY)” with Billy BYBF. The Medley Concerts session shows another side, acoustic. Our bouyon playlist gives the context of the Gwada circuit.
