Lunik: the Team Bwe Tou Sa voice, bouyon, Guadeloupe

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Lunik, whom the platforms also spell Lünik with an umlaut, is a Guadeloupe bouyon voice linked to Team Bwe Tou Sa and the GAMMALAB label. Behind the name, the published credits give a writer name: Ludmael FLOWER. From Chichi in 2020 to CHOKOLA in 2021, from Laisser tomber in 2022 to Jumping Jack with TiiTii NBA in 2023, his catalogue is short, collective and rooted in a single crew. This profile tells it without inventing it: no published official biography, no confirmed birthplace, only records, credits and a network, read closely. He is not to be confused with the Swiss pop band Lunik, formed in 1997, which carries the same name with no connection at all.

One name, an umlaut, and another Lunik not to confuse him with

The first job with Lunik is sorting homonyms. The bouyon platforms spell his name sometimes Lunik, sometimes Lünik with an umlaut on the u. Qobuz credits CHOKOLA to Lunik, Shazam credits Chichi and Laisser tomber to Lünik, and bouyon playlists use both forms for the same artist. This profile keeps Lunik as the main name, the one in this page address, and flags the umlaut form so searches land in the same place.

The other sorting job concerns Switzerland. A Swiss pop band named Lunik, formed in 1997 around singer Jaël, released albums such as Rumour, Ahead and Weather and fills most reference pages under that name. It has no connection with the bouyon artist in this profile. Confusing them would distort everything: neither the dates, nor the labels, nor the crews overlap. When a search for Lunik lands on Swiss pop, it has simply landed on the homonym.

Once that sorting is done, the Guadeloupe file is thin but coherent. No official biography, no press portrait and no verified social account was confirmed during the research, so this page links to no social profile and prints neither an age nor a birthplace. The credits of his records, however, repeatedly name Ludmael FLOWER as writer, performer and voice, and a second name from the same family, Ulrich Flower, as programmer and producer on Laisser tomber. Once again, this artist is approached through his credits.

Chichi, CHOKOLA and Voye sa allee: the Team Bwe Tou Sa years

The oldest record documented here is Chichi, released on 22 October 2020 on the Lünik label, in French, with Ludmael FLOWER credited as songwriter. On Shazam, the record passes 24,000 identifications, a notable figure for an artist with no public biography, and it remains the most searched calling card under his name. Shazam tags it reggae, a platform label which, for this scene, covers bouyon and its neighbouring forms rather than reggae in the strict Jamaican sense.

On 23 January 2021, Qobuz credits CHOKOLA to Lunik, released by GAMMALAB, the Team Bwe Tou Sa label. Bouyon playlists credit the record to Lünik, Team Bwe Tou Sa and M.D.S, and the Team Shazam page places it among its most identified tracks. CHOKOLA is therefore a crew record as much as a solo record: Lunik carries the voice, the Team carries the frame, and GAMMALAB carries the release. It is the centre of gravity of his catalogue.

The same logic runs through the Team Bwe Tou Sa Voye sa allee albums. The first volume, released on 29 July 2021 on T-BTS, contains Tik tak, with Dj Dirtee and Lunik. The second, Voye sa allee II, released on 8 July 2022 by iMD-GAMMA_LAB, contains Laisser tomber, whose detailed credits name Ludmael FLOWER on voice and lyrics, Ulrich Flower on programming and production, and Léonard Gueguen as composer. Two crew albums, two appearances, and a name that returns inside a collective rather than as a permanent headline.

Jumping Jack, Tu veux and HOT: releases from inside the circle

In 2023, Lunik stepped outside his own Team for one record that matters: Jumping Jack, on TiiTii NBA album New Bouyon Wave, credited to TiiTii NBA with Lünik featured. The published credits name Terry Capitolin and Ludmael FLOWER as writers. The record runs 2 minutes 03 and places Lunik inside the 2023 bouyon project of one of the most exposed artists on the Guadeloupe scene, an exposure his own, very collective catalogue did not give him alone.

Reference bouyon playlists keep two other traces. Tu veux is credited to Billy Bybf and Lünik, and CHOKOLA appears there among the records that define the recent bouyon sound. For its part, the Team Bwe Tou Sa Shazam page lists HOT #Oups 5, credited to Lünik, FLW, See V and Team Bwe Tou Sa, a line up that sums up how he works: four names, one crew, one short dance record.

One last clue comes from the Team video credits. The description of the official T-BTS clip Laisse-moi passer with YU MEI, produced by CamRich and mastered by GAMMALAB, credits photography under a handle containing lunik_tbts. This profile cites it as a circle clue, not as a verified official account: it shows that, in the Team credits, the name Lunik is associated with the initials TBTS, those of Team Bwe Tou Sa. That fits everything else in the file, and that is all that can honestly be said about it.

What this profile knows, and what it does not know yet

What is known about Lunik fits in one sentence: he is the voice, credited under the name Ludmael FLOWER, of a small set of Guadeloupe bouyon records released between 2020 and 2023, at the heart of Team Bwe Tou Sa and GAMMALAB, with one noted outing alongside TiiTii NBA. Chichi, CHOKOLA, Tik tak, Laisser tomber, Jumping Jack, Tu veux and HOT #Oups 5 form a short catalogue, checkable record by record on the platforms, with no solo album announced in the sources consulted.

What is not known is just as clear, and this profile would rather say it than fill in the blanks. No birthplace, no date, no path before 2020 and no published interview was confirmed. No verified official link is therefore given here, and the umlaut form, Lünik, remains one of two platform spellings. If the artist or his crew publish a biography or a confirmable official account, this page will be completed, dated and corrected like every other page on this site.

To listen, start with Chichi, the most identified record, then CHOKOLA for Team Bwe Tou Sa, and Jumping Jack for his meeting with TiiTii NBA. Our bouyon playlist places those records inside the recent bouyon sound, alongside Billy Bybf and FLW, two names from his immediate circle. Lunik is an artist of credits and crew: a small catalogue, but a readable place inside one of the crews that made Guadeloupe bouyon in the 2020s.

Frequently asked questions

Are Lunik and Lünik the same artist?

Yes, in bouyon the platforms use both spellings for the same artist. Qobuz writes Lunik for CHOKOLA, Shazam writes Lünik for Chichi and Laisser tomber, and bouyon playlists use both forms. This profile keeps Lunik as the main name and flags the umlaut form.

Is Lunik connected to the Swiss band Lunik?

No, and that is the most common confusion. The Swiss band Lunik, formed in 1997 around Jaël, is a pop and trip hop band with no connection to the Guadeloupe bouyon artist in this profile. Neither the labels, nor the crews, nor the dates overlap.

Who is behind the name Lunik?

The published credits name Ludmael FLOWER as the songwriter of Chichi, as voice and lyricist on Laisser tomber, and as co-writer of Jumping Jack with Terry Capitolin. On Laisser tomber, Ulrich Flower is credited with programming and production. Ludmael Flower also co-wrote “KJF 2” by LESTEF KJF BOYZ, released with Lunik in 2022.

What is Lunik's best known record?

Chichi, released on 22 October 2020, is his most identified track on Shazam, with more than 24,000 identifications listed. CHOKOLA, with Team Bwe Tou Sa and M.D.S, is the other record that returns in reference bouyon playlists, and Jumping Jack, with TiiTii NBA, remains his most notable outing beyond his own crew.

Is Lunik a member of Team Bwe Tou Sa?

His catalogue places him at the heart of that crew: CHOKOLA was released by GAMMALAB, the Team label, Tik tak and Laisser tomber appear on the Team Voye sa allee albums, and Team video credits associate his name with the initials TBTS. No official biography states it in full, so this profile presents it as a link documented by credits, not as an official announcement.

Where should I start listening to Lunik?

Start with Chichi, his most identified record, then CHOKOLA for Team Bwè Tou Sa, Laisser tomber for its detailed credits and Jumping Jack for his meeting with TiiTii NBA. To hear the recent bouyon sound those records belong to, then follow our bouyon playlist.

Who is Lunik?

Lunik, also spelled Lünik, is a Guadeloupe bouyon voice linked to Team Bwè Tou Sa and the GAMMALAB label. The published credits name Ludmael FLOWER behind the name. His short, collective catalogue runs from Chichi in 2020 to Jumping Jack with TiiTii NBA in 2023, through CHOKOLA, Tik tak and Laisser tomber.

What is CHOKOLA by Lunik?

CHOKOLA is the Lunik record released on 23 January 2021 by GAMMALAB according to Qobuz, credited in bouyon playlists to Lünik, Team Bwè Tou Sa and M.D.S. It is the centre of gravity of his catalogue: Lunik carries the voice, the Team carries the frame, GAMMALAB the release. The Team Bwè Tou Sa page ranks it among its most identified records.

Who produced Laisser tomber by Lünik?

Ulrich Flower, credited with programming and production. Laisser tomber appears on the Team Bwè Tou Sa album Voyé sa alléé II, released on 8 July 2022 by iMD-GAMMA_LAB. Ludmael FLOWER is credited on voice and lyrics, and Léonard Gueguen as composer. It is the record with the most detailed credits in the file.

What is Jumping Jack with Lünik?

Jumping Jack is a 2023 track from TiiTii NBA's album New Bouyon Wave, featuring Lünik. The published credits name Terry Capitolin and Ludmael FLOWER as writers, and the record runs 2 minutes 03. It is Lunik's most notable outing beyond his own crew, alongside one of the most exposed artists on the Guadeloupe scene.

Has Lunik worked with LESTEF KJF BOYZ?

Yes. According to the LESTEF KJF BOYZ profile, “KJF 2” was released on 13 January 2022 with Lunik, written by Stephan Cornano and Ludmael Flower. The group is also a regular partner of Team Bwè Tou Sa, with whom it recorded “50/50”, a record on which Ludmael Flower is among the credited writers.

Is Lunik reggae or bouyon?

Bouyon. Shazam files Chichi under reggae, but that is a platform label which, for this scene, covers bouyon and its neighbouring forms rather than reggae in the strict Jamaican sense. Lunik's whole catalogue belongs to the bouyon gwada of Team Bwè Tou Sa and GAMMALAB.

Which artists has Lunik worked with?

His records connect him to Team Bwè Tou Sa and M.D.S on CHOKOLA, Dj Dirtee on Tik tak, TiiTii NBA on Jumping Jack, Billy Bybf on Tu veux, FLW and See V on HOT #Oups 5, and LESTEF KJF BOYZ on “KJF 2”. Almost all of these names belong to the same Guadeloupe circle.
