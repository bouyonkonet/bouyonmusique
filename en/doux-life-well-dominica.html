Doux Life Well: a wellness retreat in Dominica

By Smokiller · Published November 11, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Kimberly King-Lestrade, from the DDA to wellness

Kimberly King-Lestrade is the former Head of Marketing at the Discover Dominica Authority, the body that promotes Dominica as a destination. She is now launching her own venture, Doux.Life.Well, a wellness experience built on nature, culture and intentional living.

Her own line sums up the project: "Achievement without restoration is not sustainable. Doux.Life.Well is my answer, an invitation to pause, reconnect, and rediscover the sweetness of life." The very name of the project, Doux, carries the French word for sweetness that Dominica knows well, the sweetness of life.

The retreat of 13 to 16 November 2026

The inaugural retreat runs from 13 to 16 November 2026. Activities are centred around Jungle Bay, in Soufriere, and Sabifi River Resort, in Castle Bruce, two places that show the two faces of the island: the south coast and the east coast.

The full package, called the Full Doux Experience, lasts three nights and four days. It includes accommodation at Jungle Bay, meals, wellness workshops, cultural activities and ground transportation. Full packages start at US$1,192 per person, based on double occupancy.

Two open days: Doux Restore and Doux Nature

Two days can be lived on their own. Doux Restore is a single-day programme on 14 November at Jungle Bay. Doux Nature takes place on 15 November at Sabifi River Resort, in Castle Bruce.

These two days let visitors taste the project without taking the full four days. They keep the same idea: wellness passes through Dominican nature, the culture of the island, and time taken for oneself.

Wellness, a new chapter for Dominican tourism

Dominica is known as the Nature Isle, with its rainforests, rivers, waterfalls and mountainous interior. Doux.Life.Well adds a layer to that image: the island is not only visited, it is lived, and rest is part of the journey.

The launch falls right in independence season, days after the November celebrations. It shows that Dominican tourism is not limited to festivals and hikes: wellness, culture and nature form a single product, carried by Dominicans who know their island.
