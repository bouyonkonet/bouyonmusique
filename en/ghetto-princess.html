Ghetto Princess: Bouyon En Nou Sal, Never Stop Di Bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Ghetto Princess is a voice from the first wave of bouyon gwada. In March 2013 two songs came out a few days apart: “Bouyon En Nou Sal” with Doc J, then “Never Stop Di Bouyon” with Suppa and Ti Chô, now past 155,000 views. The name came back in 2014 with “Mal Yo Fè Zot”, and in 2023 on a LsBeats riddim.

March 2013, two songs in one week

On March 7, 2013, Doc J's channel posted “Bouyon En Nou Sal”, credited to Ghetto Princess featuring Doc J, with a download link and the artist's official Facebook page. Three days later the King Bouyon channel put up “Never Stop Di Bouyon”, by Suppa with Ti Chô and Ghetto Princess.

The description of that second song links to the Vadore Concept site and to the Facebook pages of Suppa and Ghetto Princess. This is the circle of early 2010s Guadeloupe hardcore bouyon that our bouyon gwada page describes.

With Yellow Gaza, Doc J and the King Bouyon channel

On the same King Bouyon channel, “Putri”, with Yellow Gaza, is close to 120,000 views. Solo songs sit there too, such as “Haterz” and “Just En Wine”. “Pas Coder Nou”, with Doc J, circulates on another channel with more than 24,000 views.

In May 2014, DJ ToOBY Lùdo posted “Mal Yo Fè Zot” under the bouyon 2014 tag. On the ReelxOfficiel channel, the video “Ghetto Princess R.I.P Amansha” is past 50,000 views.

A return in 2023

Ten years on, the name returned with “F*ck An Ex”, released in September 2023 by VGM Product' as bouyon 2023. Production is credited to “LsBeats / LitleBoy”, the two names of the Dominican producer on our Litleboy LSBeats767 page, with recording, mix and master by VGM Product'.

The sources we opened give no legal name, home island or birth date. The path is read through the credits instead: a foot in 2013 hardcore bouyon with Doc J, Suppa and Yellow Gaza, and a recent song on a riddim from Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ghetto Princess?

A voice of 2010s bouyon gwada, heard with Doc J, Suppa and Yellow Gaza. The sources we opened do not give a legal name.

What is the most watched song?

Never Stop Di Bouyon, by Suppa with Ti Chô and Ghetto Princess, posted in March 2013 and watched more than 155,000 times.

Which songs with Doc J?

Bouyon En Nou Sal, posted in March 2013 on Doc J's channel, and Pas Coder Nou.

Are there recent releases?

Yes, F*ck An Ex, released in September 2023 as bouyon 2023, produced by LsBeats and LitleBoy.

Where is Ghetto Princess from?

The sources we opened do not say. The songs are shared with Guadeloupe bouyon names and the Vadore Concept circle.
