Explosive Band: a name on the poster, almost nothing in the archive

Published October 20, 2026

Explosive Band is a group from Dominica whose name appears, in plain print, in the official programme of Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026, the Creole village at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau: Thursday October 22, 2026, grand finale day, at 1:36 PM. That is, for now, nearly everything the public record holds, and this profile says so on its first page. No named members, no catalogue found on the platforms, no verified official account: Explosive Band is a textbook case of the Dominican stage band that exists live, in front of an audience, before it exists in databases. This profile gathers what is verified, explains the context of that billing, and clearly names what remains unknown.

What is verified: an official slot, a finale day, an exact time

The central fact of this profile fits in one programme line, but that line is official. The Ministry of Tourism and Discover Dominica published the day by day programme of the fifth edition of Ti Vilaj Kweyol, the major Creole gathering of Dominica Independence season, held at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau from Monday October 19 to Thursday October 22, 2026, from noon to 8 PM, under the theme Tout Biten Domnik, everything Dominican. In that programme, Thursday October 22 is billed as the grand finale, and Explosive Band appears in it at 1:36 PM.

The company around that slot says something about the level of the day. Before the band, the finale lines up the Kalinago Dancers, UMOJA, Castle Bruce Primary School and the Waitukubuli Dance Theatre Company. After it come Ignition and Erwinchy, then Derick “Rah” Peters, then the Reo and Faithii block with Trilla G and Prymex at 4:26 PM, then Asa Bantan at 5:43 PM, and the closing by Shelly and Signal Band at 7 PM. Explosive Band therefore plays in the afternoon of a day that ends with two of the biggest names in current bouyon. A group unknown to the platforms, but not a group outside the circuit: it is programmed, at a fixed time, by the official festival organisation.

The festival context matters too. Ti Vilaj Kweyol brings together, according to the official presentation carried by local press, more than 90 entrepreneurs, food, craft and local product stalls, and a programme mixing school groups, cultural groups, traditional dances and bouyon headliners. Admission is EC$10, free for students in uniform and children aged five and under. It is a family and national event as much as a music festival, and appearing in it is part of the normal path of a working Dominican band.

The stage band: the historical form of bouyon

To understand what a name like Explosive Band is doing on this poster, remember how bouyon was born. The genre appeared in Roseau in the late 1980s, carried by live bands such as WCK, Roots, Stems and Branches and First Serenade, who also appear on the Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 programme. Bouyon is first a band music: keyboards, bass, drums, horns or voices holding a crowd for hours, at carnival as much as at village fetes. The bouyon soca branch, started in Dominica in 2003 by producer Dada according to the signed testimony of Smokiller, added the logic of producers and riddims, but it never replaced the bands.

In that economy, many formations work almost entirely live. They rehearse, they tour the stages of the island and the diaspora, they are booked by fete committees and ministries, and they can fill that role for years without placing a single track on a streaming platform. Their public trace then shrinks to posters, programmes and phone videos. That is exactly the situation documented here: the official programme proves the band activity on October 22, 2026, and nothing in the sources opened allows more to be said about its repertoire.

This profile therefore refuses two opposite shortcuts. The first would be to invent a biography: a founding year, members, a home village, song titles. None of those elements is published in a verifiable source, so none is printed here. The second would be to treat the band as negligible because it is absent from the platforms. The official poster says the opposite: in Dominica, the stage remains an institution, and a grand finale slot at Ti Vilaj Kweyol counts as a career fact, even without an online catalogue.

The X Plosive question: two spellings, no confirmation

The record holds an ambiguity that this profile exposes instead of hiding. The first official announcement of the Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 artists, the list of twenty names confirmed by the Government of Dominica, carried a band spelled X Plosive, alongside Shelly and Signal Band, TK International, Extasy, Reo, Faithii and Riddim Knockaz. The day by day programme published afterwards for the same edition names, instead, Explosive Band, at 1:36 PM on finale day. The tags of the same press coverage carry both forms.

Are they the same band, its name written in full in the final programme? That is plausible, and it would even be the simplest explanation: Caribbean band names often travel in several spellings, shortened on lists and complete on posters. But no source opened confirms it explicitly, and this site does not merge two names on plausibility. This profile therefore covers Explosive Band, the spelling of the official day by day programme, and flags the X Plosive form as a possible variant, not an established one.

The distinction has a practical consequence for readers: the searches run for this profile across streaming platforms and social networks, under both spellings, brought up no recording, no artist page and no verifiable official account tied to a Dominican band of that name. If the band publishes its music or makes its channels official, this profile will be completed with the verified links and the real discography, dated like every correction on this site.

What this profile does not know, and how to follow the band

The honest state of the record, on October 20, 2026, is the following. Verified name: Explosive Band, in the official Ti Vilaj Kweyol 2026 programme. Nature: a band, programmed as a live formation in the middle of a day of bands and dance companies. Verified place and date: Botanic Gardens, Roseau, Dominica, Thursday October 22, 2026 at 1:36 PM. Everything else is open: members, instrumentation, repertoire, seniority, exact base on the island, recordings, social media. No verified recording therefore exists to attach to the band in this page structured data, and the track list there is deliberately empty rather than filled with guesses.

To hear the sound this band belongs to, the shortest path remains band bouyon itself: our bouyon soca page for the producer branch, our bouyon playlist for listening, and our history of bouyon page for the band lineage from WCK to Signal Band. Explosive Band belongs to that lineage through the stage, while waiting to belong to it through the archive as well.

This profile will be updated as soon as a verifiable source adds a fact: a published recording, a named lineup, a confirmed official account or a new poster. In the meantime, it stays what it should be: the written trace, dated and sourced, of a Dominican band that the official programme of the island biggest Creole week placed on the grand finale stage, and that the databases have not caught up with yet.
