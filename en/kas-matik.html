Kas Matik: PACE, bouyon, Barbados

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Kas Matik is first of all a producer: their YouTube channel, Kas Matik Music, lines up trap and dancehall instrumentals, riddims and remixes. One title there carries bouyon: “PACE”, posted on June 10, 2026 with the tag #CropOver2026 and the label *Bajan Bouyon* in its title. The page states that the track is written, produced and performed by Kasime J Elcock, and that Kas Matik Music owns all of it. That is the only verified link between Kas Matik and the genre, and this profile rests on it.

PACE, the bouyon title

“PACE” runs 3 minutes 12. Its page is short and precise: a Kas Matik Music production, a single name for writing, production and performance, Kasime J Elcock, and a line claiming full ownership of the piece. The hashtags place the track alongside soca, calypso, carnival, bouyon and Crop Over. The title itself picks a sharper label: *Bajan Bouyon*.

That label is interesting because it names a variant rather than the genre alone. It points to a bouyon made for the Crop Over season, the Barbados carnival, and for its audience. It is not an isolated case: other 2026 releases carry Barbados Bouyon or Bajan Bouyon billings, a sign that the genre born in Dominica is finding a place in the carnival programming of neighbouring islands. For the instrumentals that travel this way, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

A producer with many styles

The rest of the channel shows a beatmaker’s profile: “RAGE BAIT 2026”, a trap instrumental, “'HERE I AM' Lost Island Riddim”, a remix of “Barbies” by Valiant and a remix of “For A Fact” by SimxSantana produced by Kas Matik, a 2022 instrumental called “KINGDOM” billed dancehall, Trinibad and Bajan, and a song, “LET IT GO”. None of those titles carries a bouyon mention, and this profile does not present them as bouyon.

The move into bouyon with “PACE” therefore reads as a foray by a producer used to other sounds. That is an observation about the channel, not a career story: no interview or biography was found.

What the file does not say

The “PACE” page names no country, island or town for Kas Matik. The word Bajan in the track label and in the title of a 2022 instrumental is a circuit signal, not a stated origin, so this site assigns no island. The name Kasime J Elcock is quoted as it appears in the credits.

If Kas Matik publishes further bouyon titles or an official introduction, this page will be revised and dated, for example in our tracking of new bouyon in 2026. In the meantime, the verified listening fits into one title: “PACE”, Bajan Bouyon, Crop Over 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Kas Matik?

A producer and performer whose YouTube channel Kas Matik Music posts instrumentals, riddims and remixes. The track “PACE” is credited there for writing, production and performance to Kasime J Elcock.

What is their bouyon title?

“PACE”, posted on June 10, 2026, 3 minutes 12, billed #CropOver2026 and *Bajan Bouyon*. It is the only title on the channel that states bouyon.

What does Bajan Bouyon mean?

It is the track’s label: a bouyon tied to the audience and season of Barbados, Bajan meaning Barbadian. This site reads it as a style and season marker, not as a biography.

Where is Kas Matik from?

No opened page says. This site therefore assigns no island.

Are their other productions bouyon?

Nothing says so. Their other uploads are billed trap, dancehall, Trinibad or riddim, with no bouyon mention.
