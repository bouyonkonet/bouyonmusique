Dominica bouyon music: Thea LaFond, UWI honorary doctorate

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026

The Olympic champion is now a doctor. Thea LaFond-Gadson received an honorary Doctor of Laws from the University of the West Indies, at its Five Islands Campus in Antigua and Barbuda, in October 2026, per Dominica News.

An honour for sport and for women

The honorary Doctor of Laws recognises her contribution to Caribbean sport, her leadership and her advocacy for women in sport, per Dominica News. UWI Chancellor Dr Dodridge D. Miller presented the degree at the Five Islands Campus graduation ceremony, per Writeups 24.

She was introduced by the university s public orator, Makeda Fleming-Challenger, who traced her journey from an early interest in ballet to the top of Caribbean athletics, per Writeups 24.

The jump that changed Dominica s history

Thea LaFond wrote Dominica into Olympic history at Paris 2024: gold in the women s triple jump with 15.02 metres, a national record, and the country s first ever Olympic medal, per Dominica News.

Writeups 24 recalls that she competed in Paris with fractured cartilage in her knee and had surgery two days after the gold. In the same year 2024 she had already won the world indoor title.

A complete set in 2026

Her Commonwealth Games path shows a steady climb: bronze in 2018, silver in 2022, gold in 2026, per Writeups 24. The October 2026 doctorate therefore arrived a few months after she completed her Commonwealth medal collection.

The university also praised her five years as a teacher for students with special needs and her push for more investment, visibility and recognition for women s sport across the Caribbean, per Writeups 24.

The speech: setbacks that make you stronger

In her acceptance speech, reported by Dominica News, she remembered her late uncle, former President of Dominica Dr Nicholas J.O. Liverpool, an inspiration in her personal and professional life.

Addressing the 2026 graduating class, she used her Rio 2016 Games as an example: every setback strengthened her determination, to make not only her family proud, but the whole nation, per Dominica News. Fleming-Challenger summed up the journey as the fruit of tenacity, perseverance and Caribbean pride.

Frequently asked questions

What title did Thea LaFond receive from UWI?

An honorary Doctor of Laws (LLD), presented by the University of the West Indies at its Five Islands Campus in Antigua and Barbuda in October 2026, for her contribution to sport, per Dominica News.

Why is Thea LaFond historic for Dominica?

She won the first Olympic medal in Dominica s history: gold in the women s triple jump at Paris 2024, with a national record of 15.02 metres, per Dominica News.

What did she win at the Commonwealth Games?

Bronze in 2018, silver in 2022 and gold in 2026, a complete collection, per Writeups 24.
