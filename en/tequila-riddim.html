Tequila Riddim: Next Gen Records' 2025 bouyon riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Tequila Riddim is a bouyon riddim produced by Next Gen Records, meaning the pair Dré Soundz and JJW Productions. The six track album is dated April 29, 2025 on Deezer, and all six videos went up together on the label's YouTube channel on May 2, each one tagged “Bouyon 2025”. The most watched of the set is “No Hole” by Rum Lawd.

Six voices, one block of credits

All six YouTube descriptions carry the same credits. Production: Next Gen Records, with Dré Soundz and JJW Productions. Executive producer: Josh 'JJW Productions' Williams, who also mixed and mastered. Cover art is by WildWolf Designz and the visuals by Dré Soundz. On “In De Back”, “Attitude” and “Hoe Stick”, the credits add recording by ObeeLayne and No Porch Records.

On Deezer, the “Tequila Riddim” album came out on the Next Generation Records label on April 29, 2025. It holds the six vocal tracks and no instrumental. The running times are tight: “No Hole” is the shortest at 2:18, “Attitude” the longest at 2:48, and the other four sit around 2:36.

Who sings on the Tequila Riddim

Each singer is credited as writer and performer of their song. Rum Lawd has “No Hole”, Caspa G “Tight Wine” (spelled “Tight Whine” on Deezer), Kilogram “Hardball”, 101 Stunna “Hoe Stick”, C4 De Crazyone “In De Back” and Deadly “Attitude”. None of the pages we opened say which island the six come from, so this page doesn't guess.

The numbers are small. When read on October 28, 2026, “No Hole” had 1,953 views, ahead of “Tight Wine” at 1,191 and “Hardball” at 1,161; the other three sat between 700 and 760. Rum Lawd had another bouyon song out in 2025, “Whale Fu See” with OG Park on the Whale Oil Riddim.

A label between soca and bouyon, and a name already taken

Next Gen Records doesn't only release bouyon. On its channel, the 2025 Love Maps Riddim and the 2024 Nasty Chant Riddim are labelled bouyon, while Whole Mood, Dingolay, Payphone and Space Alien are billed as soca. The Tequila Riddim sits firmly in the first group. For where the line falls between the two, see our bouyon vs soca page.

Mind the namesake: Deezer also lists a “Tequila Riddim” on the Stainless Music label, released November 16, 2010, with Beenie Man, Busy Signal and Ice Cold. That one is a Jamaican dancehall riddim with no link to the Next Gen Records release. For how the format works, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Tequila Riddim?

Next Gen Records, that is Dré Soundz and JJW Productions, with Josh 'JJW Productions' Williams as executive producer and on mixing and mastering, according to the YouTube descriptions.

When did the Tequila Riddim come out?

The Deezer album is dated April 29, 2025, on the Next Generation Records label, and the six videos have been on the Next Gen Records channel since May 2, 2025.

Which artists are on the Tequila Riddim?

Rum Lawd, Caspa G, Kilogram, 101 Stunna, C4 De Crazyone and Deadly, one song each.

Is the Tequila Riddim bouyon?

Yes. All six official videos are titled “Bouyon 2025”, while the same label files other riddims under soca.

Is it the same Tequila Riddim as the Beenie Man one?

No. The 2010 Tequila Riddim on Stainless Music is a dancehall riddim. The Next Gen Records one is from 2025 and is bouyon.
