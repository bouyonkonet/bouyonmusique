Bouyon in French Guiana: the Creole sound of the continent

Published September 11, 2026

French Guiana, a French territory of South America with a strong Creole culture, is part of bouyon’s natural audience. Tracks from Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique circulate there through the networks, radio and parties.

An audience connected to the Antilles

The Guyanese audience follows the same bouyon coverage as that of Gwada and Martinique: the same riddims, the same artists, the same dances. For Dominican artists, French Guiana completes the francophone triangle of the Caribbean and France.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
