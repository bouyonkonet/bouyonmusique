Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora, from New York to London, Toronto and Montreal. On the South American continent, French Guiana is also part of its natural audience, carried by its strong Creole culture.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s: it was in Roseau, the island's capital, that the band WCK created it in 1988, mixing jing ping, cadence-lypso and electronic rhythms. Bouyon remains the sound of Roseau carnival today.

Is bouyon listened to in French Guiana?

Yes. French Guiana, a French territory of South America with a strong Creole culture, is part of bouyon's natural audience. Tracks from Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique circulate there through the networks, radio and parties, and the Guyanese audience follows the same riddims, artists and dances as the Antilles.

What is bouyon?

Bouyon is a music genre born in Dominica in the late 1980s, pioneered by the band WCK. Its name means soup in Kweyol: it mixes jing ping, cadence-lypso, zouk and electronic rhythms. Its fast tempo made it the soundtrack of carnival, in Dominica as well as in Guadeloupe and Martinique. See the history of bouyon.

Which bouyon singer has performed in French Guiana?

Shanika, a singer from Guadeloupe in the current bouyon and shatta wave. According to her biography published by Music Story, her track "Kay ve yo", released on May 27, 2023 and produced by DJ Taffy according to the video credits, drew particular attention and let her perform in several countries, including France, Germany and French Guiana.

Is bouyon gwada played in French Guiana?

Yes, this site documents one example. Jixels, a bouyon keyboardist and producer rooted in Guadeloupe, captioned on TikTok a show at the festival de la bouffe in Kourou, French Guiana, with the hashtags bouyon, 973 for French Guiana and 971 for Guadeloupe. The bouyon gwada circuit therefore reaches all the way to French Guiana.

What does 973 mean in bouyon?

973 is French Guiana's dialing code. Some bouyon artists tag their videos with the codes 971 for Guadeloupe, 767 for Dominica, 972 for Martinique and 973 for French Guiana: a way of speaking to the whole arc of French-speaking and Creole-speaking bouyon, from Guadeloupe to French Guiana by way of Dominica and Martinique.

Where can I listen to bouyon in French Guiana?

On the Bouyon Road Mix by Bouyon Shattaz, on Spotify and YouTube, which gathers new releases from Dominica, Guadeloupe and Martinique, bouyon soca and carnival classics. The playlist's logic is simple: start from the origin, Dominica, then follow the sound where it lives, in Gwada, Martinique, French Guiana and Paris. See the bouyon playlist.

Why does French Guiana matter to Dominican artists?

Because it completes the francophone triangle of the Caribbean and France. A French territory of South America with a strong Creole culture, French Guiana follows the same bouyon news as Guadeloupe and Martinique: the same riddims, the same artists, the same dances. Dominican tracks circulate there through the networks, radio and parties.

Does the Guyanese diaspora listen to bouyon in France?

Yes. Paris and France's big cities concentrate a Guadeloupean, Martinican, Guyanese and Dominican diaspora that keeps bouyon nights and the networks alive. For this audience, bouyon is a direct link to home. The presence of these Antillean fans is one of the reasons France has become the genre's big market. See bouyon in France.
