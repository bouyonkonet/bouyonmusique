KTB

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

KTB enters this site through a single title, but a clearly labelled one: “Come Ova (Bouyon 2026)”, published on April 9, 2026 on his KTB Music channel. The description credits him under his full name, Klyvert “KTB” Browne, as writer and performer, with Dj Shil producing and Joshy Beatz on the mix. The rest of his visible catalogue sits under the Vincy Soca banner, the soca of Saint Vincent, and that contrast is what makes the profile worth having: an artist from the soca circuit who cuts a bouyon record and claims it as one.

Come Ova, the bouyon title

“Come Ova” was released on April 9, 2026, as a video on the artist’s channel and as a DistroKid release under the KTB Music line. The video runs 3 minutes 08 and carries Bouyon 2026 in its title. The credits are complete: written and performed by Klyvert “KTB” Browne, backing vocals by Ishatta Da Silva, produced by Dj Shil, recorded at JC Productions, mixed and mastered by Joshy Beatz, credited as Kemmuel “Joshy Beatz” Simmons on the distributed version.

The video tags put bouyon first, followed by a list of islands from Saint Vincent to Dominica, through Grenada, Saint Lucia, Trinidad, Martinique and Saint Martin: an address to the whole Caribbean audience rather than a statement of origin. A radio version, “Come Ova (Clean Version)”, followed on April 25, 2026 with the same credits.

A catalogue rooted in Vincy Soca

The other releases on the KTB Music channel present themselves as soca. “Madness”, published on February 1, 2025, is labelled Vincy Soca 2025, written and performed by Klyvert “KTB” Browne and produced by Next Gen Records. “Sideman”, with Jahlil “VYPER” Williams, published on May 23, 2024 and produced by DJ Jeanie, carries the hashtags Stvincent and vincysoca2024; its riddim is by Jackie Beatz, a name that turns up often on bouyon productions from Dominica.

Those titles state no bouyon tie and are named here in the text only. They do show that “Come Ova” is a deliberate step sideways, from an artist of the soca circuit toward the sound born in Dominica, a crossing that bouyon soca has known since its beginnings.

Origin and the limits of the file

The channel name, KTBMusicVC, the Vincy Soca label in his titles and the Stvincent hashtag tie KTB to the Saint Vincent circuit. These are signals taken from his own pages, not a biography: no opened page gives his birthplace or home, and this site does not invent them.

One more point stays open. A Topic page under the name KTB lists “Mend Your Heart (feat. Demetra Stanton)”, released in 2022 on Beat Boutique with Italian composers. Nothing links that title to Klyvert Browne, and KTB is a short enough name to be shared: that title is excluded from this profile until identity is established.

Frequently asked questions

Who is KTB?

KTB is the stage name credited to Klyvert “KTB” Browne, writer and performer of “Come Ova (Bouyon 2026)”. His other visible releases are presented as Vincy Soca.

Who produced Come Ova?

Dj Shil, according to the video description. Recording is credited to JC Productions, and mixing and mastering to Joshy Beatz, Kemmuel Simmons on the distributed version.

When was Come Ova released?

On April 9, 2026, as a video on the KTB Music channel and as a DistroKid release. A radio version followed on April 25, 2026.

Where is KTB from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. His channel name, KTBMusicVC, his Vincy Soca titles and the Stvincent hashtag tie him to the Saint Vincent circuit, as signals.

Is Mend Your Heart by this KTB?

Nothing establishes it. That 2022 title on Beat Boutique carries credits with no visible link to Klyvert Browne, and it is excluded from this profile.
