DeBouyon Riddim

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The DeBouyon Riddim, also written De Bouyon Riddim, is a bouyon riddim produced by Cornell Phillip and Brent Samuel for the 2019 season. The album came out on Deezer on February 18, 2019, on Imperial Publishing, with six vocal tracks and the instrumental. Cornell Phillip is the lead artist throughout, and each song brings in one or two singers from Dominica.

A riddim from WCK's keyboard player

Cornell Phillip, known as Fingers, is the founding keyboard player of WCK. On the DeBouyon Riddim he shares production with Brent Samuel, according to the seven official audio uploads posted to his YouTube channel on February 21, 2019. Mixing and mastering were done at Drews Crib by Andrew Denny, and Kester Labad made the artwork.

Deezer dates the "De Bouyon Riddim" album February 18, 2019, under Imperial Publishing, which also appears as publisher in the YouTube credits. The tracks are short: most run about two and a half minutes, with "Reverse" the longest at 3 minutes 35. Every video is tagged "Bouyon 2019", so there's no doubt about where the project sits.

Six songs, Dominican voices

Each track is credited to Cornell Phillip with a guest. Shelly sings "Reverse", which he wrote; Mr. Benji carries "Bouyon 2 D World", written by Wayne Benjamin and Kendel Laurent; and Colton T writes and sings "Big Iron". Calypso monarch Daddy Chess takes "Bumpa Fete".

Carlyn XP is the only one to appear twice: as the sole guest on "Breaking Loose", written by Clement JnoBaptiste, then with Melodee on "Illegal Whine", written by Kurt Savarin. The writing credit on "Bumpa Fete" also names Carlyn Xavier Phillip. Shelly, Colton T, Carlyn XP and Daddy Chess are all presented on our pages as Dominican artists.

More a DJ riddim than a view counter

The numbers on Cornell Phillip's channel are modest. When we checked on October 28, 2026, "Bouyon 2 D World" and "Reverse" sat around 1,800 views, "Big Iron" around 1,200, and the other three between 600 and 900. The riddim mostly travelled through mixes.

DJ Hothead, Dj CashMoney 767 and DjeasyPromos each posted a DeBouyon Riddim mix in 2019. Shilo Scotland went further and blended it with another riddim of the same season in "Champion Riddim meets DeBouyon Riddim Mix": see our page on the Champion Riddim, and for the format itself, the one on the bouyon riddim.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the DeBouyon Riddim?

Cornell Phillip and Brent Samuel, according to the official audio descriptions. Andrew Denny mixed and mastered it at Drews Crib.

When did it come out?

The Deezer album is dated February 18, 2019; the official audios have been on YouTube since February 21, 2019.

What label released it?

Imperial Publishing, per Deezer and the publisher line in the YouTube credits.

Who sings on the DeBouyon Riddim?

Shelly, Mr. Benji, Colton T, Carlyn XP (twice, once with Melodee) and Daddy Chess, each as a guest of Cornell Phillip.

De Bouyon or DeBouyon?

Both spellings are used: Deezer has "De Bouyon Riddim", Cornell Phillip's YouTube titles have "DeBouyon Riddim". It is the same project.
