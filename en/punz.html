Punz: the Bouyon Bad Riddim with Nessa Preppy and Mafi

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Punz, also credited as DJ Pun or DeeJay Punz, is a DJ and producer whose label is called Punz Entertainment. In January 2025 he published the “Bouyon Bad Riddim”, a project on which two titles carry his name next to the voice: “Feel It” with Nessa Preppy, more than 342,000 views when this profile was written, and “Pokey” with Mafi. In August 2025, “Bumpa Move” with Jamaica's Skillibeng passed 550,000 views, and his “Bouyon Mix Vol. 01” with Selectah Renzo neared 670,000.

Feel It with Nessa Preppy: the Bouyon Bad Riddim

On January 18, 2025, Nessa Preppy's channel uploaded “Nessa Preppy x Punz - Feel It (Official Music Video) | Bouyon Bad Riddim”. The description is precise: performed by Nessa Preppy and Dj Pun, off the “Bouyon Bad Riddim” project. On Deezer, “Feel It” was released on January 23, 2025 on the Punz Entertainment label, with Nessa Preppy and Punz as contributors, 2 minutes 36. The video was past 342,000 views on October 27, 2026.

Nessa Preppy, a singer from Trinidad and Tobago, is one of the soca voices that return most often on riddims billed bouyon since 2025; this site also finds her on DJ Kalli's “Back Hurt”. For the setting, see our page on bouyon soca.

Pokey with Mafi: the project's second title

On January 26, 2025, the Mafi Official channel published “Mafi x Punz - Pokey (Official Music Video) | Bouyon Bad Riddim”, performed by Mafi and Dj Punz, off the same project, with the tags Trinidad, Guyana and Jamaica; the video was past 83,000 views when read. On Deezer, “Pokey” is dated January 27, 2025, label Punz Entertainment, 2 minutes 25.

Two titles, two singers, one riddim and one label: the Bouyon Bad Riddim is a producer's project in the sense bouyon and soca give the word, one instrumental shared by several voices. That the producer bills his riddim bouyon from Trinidad's soca circuit says how far the genre reached in 2025.

Bumpa Move with Skillibeng and the mixes with Selectah Renzo

On August 28, 2025, Skillibeng's channel published “Punz - Bumpa Move (Official Video) ft. Skillibeng”, produced by Punz Entertainment, more than 552,000 views when read: the producer of the Bouyon Bad Riddim signs here a title under his own name with one of the most streamed Jamaican dancehall artists of the moment. The title is distributed on the platforms by the label.

On his own channel, Punz Entertainment, he also mixes: “DeeJay Punz & Selectah Renzo - Bouyon Mix Vol. 01”, uploaded on June 3, 2025, 43 minutes, was nearing 668,000 views, and the pair's “Strictly Gyal Chune” volumes pass a million for some. This profile is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026; none says where Punz is from, and this site assigns him no island.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Punz?

A DJ and producer, also credited as DJ Pun or DeeJay Punz, head of the Punz Entertainment label, maker of the Bouyon Bad Riddim project (January 2025).

What is the Bouyon Bad Riddim?

A riddim project published in January 2025 on Punz Entertainment, of which Feel It with Nessa Preppy (January 23) and Pokey with Mafi (January 27) are the two titles opened for this profile.

What is his most viewed title?

Bumpa Move with Skillibeng (August 2025), more than 552,000 views on October 27, 2026, ahead of Feel It with Nessa Preppy, more than 342,000.

Does Punz publish mixes?

Yes: DeeJay Punz & Selectah Renzo - Bouyon Mix Vol. 01 (June 3, 2025) was nearing 668,000 views, and the pair also publishes the Strictly Gyal Chune series.

Where is Punz from?

No opened page says. His titles' pages carry Trinidad, Guyana and Jamaica tags; this profile assigns him no origin.
