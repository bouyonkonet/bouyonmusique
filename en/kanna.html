KANNA: the Dpv Lion bouyon songs, from 2012 to Universe

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

KANNA has been releasing music through Dpv Lion Pwod, both a YouTube channel and a label, for more than ten years. The word bouyon shows up in KANNA's titles as early as 2012 and keeps coming back: music videos, numbered freestyles, then two full projects released on Deezer in 2021 and 2022.

Sa Ka Pété Sec and the first videos

The oldest record is also the most watched. On December 2, 2012, Dpv Lion Pwod uploaded “Sa Ka Pété Sec”, presented as a “clip vidéo bouyon” by KANNA. It now has more than 12,800 views. In April 2015 came “Marinade Bouyon”, an official video on a production by SND Prod, again under the Dpv Lion banner.

The channel does not carry only bouyon. It also holds “On a compris” from 2018, close to 10,000 views, “Freedom” with Bludjah, “Fondation” with Sounda and several songs shared with Frédy di Lion, among them “Gwadeloup” in October 2020. None of these descriptions says where KANNA comes from. The video tags only mention 971 and Sainte Rose.

The freestyles and Fémé Guél Aw

On the same channel, KANNA put out a run of numbered freestyles. “Bouilli Bouyon” is number 2, “Bouyon Ayin Bay” number 5, “Princess' Bouyon” number 8 and “An Kay Féy” number 9. They are short clips with a few hundred plays each, but they show a steady work rate.

Several of those songs also exist as studio versions. The single “Bouyon -Ayin Bay” reached Deezer on December 26, 2020, on Dpv Lion. The next day “Fémé Guél Aw”, billed “Bouyon”, landed on YouTube through DistroKid, again credited to Dpv Lion.

Polyvalente Mixtape and Universe

On December 28, 2021, Dpv Lion released the “Polyvalente Mixtape”, ten tracks. Four carry the word bouyon: “Bouilli bouyon”, “Bouyon nextyear”, “Bouyon fèmé guelaw” and “Bouyon ayen bay”. The rest includes “Guadeloupe”, with a guest credited as Freddy, and “Je garde le sourire” with Sounda.

A little over a month later, on February 1, 2022, came “Universe”, eight tracks, including “Soucey bien bouyon”, “An dot cou bouyon” and “Bavè bouyon”. A warning about Deezer: its KANNA page lumps together several artists with the same name, from Tamil singles to a jazz EP and an indie rock record. Only the Dpv Lion releases belong to this KANNA.

Frequently asked questions

Who is KANNA?

An artist released by Dpv Lion Pwod who has put out songs billed as bouyon since 2012. No source we opened gives the artist's origin or real name.

What is KANNA's most watched bouyon song?

“Sa Ka Pété Sec”, a bouyon music video uploaded by Dpv Lion Pwod in December 2012, with more than 12,800 views.

What projects has KANNA released?

The single Bouyon -Ayin Bay (2020), the Polyvalente Mixtape (2021) and the album Universe (2022), all on Dpv Lion.

Who does KANNA work with?

Frédy di Lion on Gwadeloup and Cannabis, Sounda on Fondation and Je garde le sourire, Bludjah on Freedom. SND Prod handled the production of Marinade Bouyon.

Why is KANNA's Deezer page confusing?

Deezer files several different artists under the name KANNA. The bouyon songs are the ones released on the Dpv Lion label.
