Bouyon dance: Miimii KDS, 6 o clock and its three composers

By Smokiller · Published October 26, 2026

Miimii KDS is an artist from Guadeloupe. On 31 August 2026 she released 6 o clock, a 2 minute 04 single on KDS Records. This page gathers the verified credits of the track and what regional press reported about it, with no lyrics and no invented figures.

On this page
  1. The track credits
  2. The video, between pool and stage
  3. Where 6 o clock sits in her catalogue
  4. What this page keeps
  5. Frequently asked questions

The track credits

The Deezer single page gives the full chain: 6 o clock, released 31 August 2026, running 2 minutes 04, on KDS Records, lyrics written by Miimii KDS, music composed by Joshii, TitoWeGlobal and Jervon Henderson.

The YouTube page for the track, provided to YouTube by Universal Music Group, confirms the same date and the same three composers, with Miimii KDS as lyricist. Two independent distribution sources therefore tell the same credit line.

The video, between pool and stage

13th Street Promotions, which covered the release, describes a video directed by JX Vision and Middle Vision: Miimii KDS gets ready with her squad for a night out, between a pool party and highlights from one of her recent live performances.

The same article presents the track as getting ready for an unforgettable night, carried by its production. This page keeps that description as its source wording, without inventing a lyric story.

Where 6 o clock sits in her catalogue

The Deezer discography of Miimii KDS places 6 o clock after Se Miimii, released 11 July 2025, and alongside her 2026 collaborations, including Allo Doudou with Niska and Litleboy LSBeats767.

The track confirms a method already visible in her catalogue: short formats, around two minutes, built for dance and for social platforms, with composer teams drawn from the bouyon and shatta scene.

What this page keeps

6 o clock is a single documented by its metadata: date, label, length, lyricist and three composers are confirmed by two distribution pages. Her full profile and other verified tracks are on her artist page on this site.

No lyrics are reproduced here, no stream count is stated, and the artist island is given as Guadeloupe, with no hometown town, in line with opened sources.
