Fucha Kid: Antigua soca and the Bouyon Soca Fete album

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Fucha Kid, also known as Feds Boss, is a soca singer tied to Antigua's carnival, credited as a writer under the name Karl Edwards. Bouyon has its own corner in his catalog: songs with Mr Gwada since 2018, then a full album, Bouyon Soca Fete, in 2024.

A singer from Antigua's carnival

As early as 2014 his songs were going around with labels like “Antigua Soca 2014” or “Groovy Soca 2014 for Antigua Carnival”. In 2019, Antigua's Carnival Festivals Commission posted “Drinks On Me” itself, on the Kusada Riddim, noting that the song was written by Karl “Fucha Kid” Edwards and recorded at Federation Family Studio.

That studio is still his base. Its channel carries “Farmer” with Dr Ec, on the Farmyard Riddim, billed “Antigua Soca 2026”, at more than 539,000 views. “Show Off”, posted in July 2026, went further and is past one million views. Both of those hits are soca, not bouyon.

Money Moves with Mr Gwada

His link to bouyon starts with Mr Gwada. In January 2018, DjeasyPromos posted “Make Money (Money Moves)”, by Mr Gwada with Fucha Kid, billed “2018 bouyon”, then the official video in July, labeled “2018 Bouyon”. The tags pair Antigua with Dominica.

The two met again on “Who Fool Dem”, which Djeasy's channel files under dancehall instead. Fucha Kid moves between styles a lot: “Work”, his 2022 video with more than 38,000 views, is described as a dancehall and reggae song, produced with Oshane Campbell.

The Bouyon Soca Fete album

In April 2024 he released “Bring Out De Wine” with Kenne Blessin. On June 2 it became the first track of “Bouyon Soca Fete”, an album on his own label, Fucha Kid Music. Eight songs, each with a different guest, plus two radio edits. Benz Mr Gwada sings on “Cat”, whose YouTube track is past 1,800 views.

The guest list maps out his circle: Shattakeem on “Biscuit Jam”, Kiyode Erasto on “Spread Out (Woosh)”, Redd Gyal on “Fire Truck”, Famus Face on “Plenty Iron”, Gangsta Ferrella on “Dash” and Barcus on “De Yeng Yeng Boss”. Every track runs about two minutes and fifteen seconds, a short format built for the party.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Fucha Kid?

A soca singer tied to Antigua's carnival, also known as Feds Boss, credited as a writer under the name Karl Edwards. He has released several bouyon songs.

What are his bouyon songs?

Make Money (Money Moves) with Mr Gwada in 2018, then the 2024 album Bouyon Soca Fete, with Bring Out De Wine, Cat, Biscuit Jam and Dash.

Who sings with him on Bouyon Soca Fete?

Kenne Blessin, Benz Mr Gwada, Shattakeem, Kiyode Erasto, Redd Gyal, Famus Face, Gangsta Ferrella and Barcus, one guest per track.

What is his most watched song?

Show Off, a soca song posted by Federation Family Studio in July 2026, at more than one million views. Farmer with Dr Ec is past 539,000.

Where does he record?

At Federation Family Studio, named in the credits of Drinks On Me in 2019 and Farmer in 2026. His releases come out on his label, Fucha Kid Music.
