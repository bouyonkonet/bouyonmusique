Bouyon Shattaz: the media behind this site

BouyonMusique.com is published by Bouyon Shattaz, the media outlet that documents bouyon every day: releases, artists, producers, events and the history of the genre, with one clear line, bouyon was born in Dominica and must benefit Dominica.

What Bouyon Shattaz does

On Instagram, @bouyonshattaz publishes bouyon news in English and French. The Bouyon Road Mix distributes the genre’s playlist on Spotify and YouTube. The Smokiller project produces the bouyon remixes that open the genre to new audiences. And the Bouyon Konet network connects the bouyon pages of the Antilles to coordinate artists’ releases.

Why a site in French

Because the audience searching for music bouyon, bouyon danse and playlist shatta is francophone, and it deserves a guide that tells the genre from its origin. This site is Bouyon Shattaz’s French-language library: it will grow with the scene.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it called bouyon?

Bouyon means soup in Kweyol: the genre stirs Dominica's traditional musics together with electronic production.

Is bouyon part of Creole culture?

Yes. Bouyon was born in Dominica's villages and carnival and soundtracks Creole celebrations across the French Caribbean.
