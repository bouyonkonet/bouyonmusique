What Bouyon Shattaz does

On Instagram, @bouyonshattaz publishes bouyon news in English and French. The Bouyon Road Mix distributes the genre’s playlist on Spotify and YouTube. The Smokiller project produces the bouyon remixes that open the genre to new audiences. And the Bouyon Konet network connects the bouyon pages of the Antilles to coordinate artists’ releases.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bouyon Shattaz?

Bouyon Shattaz is the media outlet that publishes BouyonMusique.com and documents bouyon every day: releases, artists, producers, events and the history of the genre. Its line is clear: bouyon was born in Dominica and must benefit Dominica. It is active on Instagram, Spotify and YouTube.

What is the Bouyon Road Mix?

The Bouyon Road Mix is Bouyon Shattaz's bouyon playlist, distributed on Spotify and YouTube. It collects new bouyon releases and carnival classics, and also blends bouyon soca and shatta bouyon while keeping the sound's Dominican origin at the center. To get it by direct message, follow @bouyonshattaz on Instagram and comment ROAD MIX.

What is the Bouyon Konet network?

Bouyon Konet is the network that connects the bouyon pages of the Antilles to coordinate artists' releases. It is part of Bouyon Shattaz's work, alongside the @bouyonshattaz Instagram account, the Bouyon Road Mix playlist and the bouyon remixes of the Smokiller project, which open the genre to new audiences.

Why is it called bouyon?

Bouyon means soup in kwéyòl, Dominica's Creole language: everything mixes and nothing is lost. The name describes the recipe of the genre WCK created in Roseau in 1988, jing ping and cadence-lypso stirred together with keyboards and drum machines. That origin is what Bouyon Shattaz documents. See meaning of bouyon.

Is bouyon part of Creole culture?

Yes. Bouyon was born in Dominica's villages and carnival, it is sung in kwéyòl and English, and it soundtracks Creole celebrations across the French Caribbean. It is for that Creole audience, from Dominica to the French Antilles and their diaspora, that Bouyon Shattaz publishes in French and English.

Who founded Bouyon Shattaz?

Bouyon Shattaz was founded by Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer. Under his Smokiller artist brand, he turns afrobeats, pop, rap and R&B hits into bouyon versions and produces for other artists, such as Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money. Bouyon Shattaz is his media platform and his distribution and rights project. See Smokiller.

What is the Bouyon Shattaz Rights Clinic?

It is an audit carried out by Bouyon Shattaz with Dominica's artists and bands: checking their contact routes, protecting their credits and leaving them in control of their name, their works and their agreements. Artists such as Colton T and Shelly are part of its audit cohort, with a verified contact route.

What is Bouyon Shattaz radio servicing?

It is the supply of Bouyon Shattaz rotation packs to Caribbean radio stations, run by Smokiller. Stations receive tracks in usable formats, including the WAV remixes from the smokiller.com hub, for their programming and their DJs. The goal: that a bouyon remix ends up played in Roseau, in the Antilles and across the region.

How does Bouyon Shattaz cover bouyon producers?

As full artists. In October 2026, Bouyon Shattaz published a clean 11.7-second cut of Savion's unreleased snippet and an edit of HYPNO's producer reel for Mauvais Plan, then updated their captions to the platform's search standard, so that these makers of the sound become findable.

Why is BouyonMusique.com in French?

Because the audience searching for music bouyon, bouyon danse and playlist shatta is French-speaking, and it deserves a guide that tells the genre from its Dominican origin. This site is Bouyon Shattaz's French-language library, with an English edition, and it grows with the scene, artist by artist and credit by credit.
