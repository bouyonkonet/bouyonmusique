Soca Ras: Antigua Carnival bouyon, 2017 to 2021

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Soca Ras is a singer who has released bouyon for Antigua Carnival since 2017. Nearly every one of his songs carries the bouyon tag, and one of them, “Egg Plant”, was posted by the official Antigua Carnival body. His home island is not stated anywhere.

Bouyon jab in Antigua, 2017

The oldest song found is “Push It Back”, posted in July 2017 and billed “Antigua bouyon jab 2017”. The same year brought “Bouyon Request”, a title that leaves no doubt. From the start he worked the Antigua summer season, where bouyon from Dominica sits next to local soca.

In 2018, “Hurricane Irma”, on the Bomb Riddim, took the name of the storm that hit Barbuda and Saint Martin in the autumn of 2017. It is his most watched video among those found, at close to 6,900 views. Most of his songs travel through the DjeasyPromos channel, which focuses on bouyon and soca.

Egg Plant and Hooking Me, Carnival 2019

In May 2019, Antigua's Carnival Festivals Commission posted “Egg Plant” under the tag “Bouyon 2019” on its channel, which works as a showcase for carnival songs. For a singer on that circuit, it is a kind of official recognition.

July brought “Hooking Me”, billed “Bouyon Jazz” and “Antigua Carnival 2019”. The same year he released “Lets Get It On” and “She is Your Ex”, on the Kite Frame Riddim. Four bouyon songs dated 2019 make it his busiest year.

Dou Dou, Karma and Oh Holy Night

In July 2021, “Dou Dou” put him on a riddim by Litleboy, one of the most sought-after bouyon composers. “Karma” is also billed as bouyon, with no year in its title.

He even tried Christmas bouyon with “Oh Holy Night”, a duet with Derisia that came with a video. His run shows how bouyon settled into Antigua Carnival well before the big recent hits; the bouyon in Antigua page gives the rest of the context.
