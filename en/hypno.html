HYPNO: the producer who makes bouyon dance

HYPNO, @hypnoticbeatz, is a bouyon producer whose track Mauvais Plan, built at 160 bpm, has become a reference of the producer scene followed by Bouyon Shattaz.

Mauvais Plan, 160 bpm

With Mauvais Plan, HYPNO delivered a concentrate of modern producer bouyon: a fast tempo worn with pride, a loop that never lets go, and a format designed for the networks as much as for dance. The track is part of Bouyon Shattaz’s reference producer content.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the key bouyon artists?

WCK created the genre, Asa Bantan is widely called the King of Bouyon, and the new generation includes Reo, Ridge, Signal Band and Shelly, while producers like Smokiller export the sound.

Who publishes these artist pages?

BouyonMusique.com is published by Bouyon Shattaz, the platform of Dominican-born producer Smokiller, with facts verified artist by artist.
