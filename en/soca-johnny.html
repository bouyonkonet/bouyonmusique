Soca Johnny: from Bouyon Monarch to London soca

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Soca Johnny is a singer born in London and raised in Dominica, a name on the UK soca scene. He links the two: he sang at Dominica's first Bouyon Monarch in 2016, was back in the final in 2017, and since 2014 has released songs billed as bouyon, often tagged “Dominica/UK”.

Two Bouyon Monarchs, 2016 and 2017

On January 30, 2016, Soca Johnny took the stage at the national stadium in Roseau for the Bouyon Monarch final, the first edition of the contest. He sang “Gyal Tonight”, posted a few days earlier as “Bouyon 2016”. Video of his set went up on his own channel in February.

In February 2017, Soca News named him among the finalists of the second edition at Carnival City, against reigning monarch Carlyn XP and names such as Fetty Mark. The final placings do not appear in the sources we checked. For the setting, see our Dominica Carnival page.

Between Dominica and London

According to his Soca News profile, he was born in London, moved to Dominica very young and grew up in a village listening to WCK, First Serenade and Buju Banton. Back in the UK at 17, he joined Mangrove Steel Orchestra in West London, where he got his nickname. He started out in dancehall before turning to soca and bouyon.

UK Soca Scene points to his first local hit, “Drunk Tonight”, around 2008, then to 2010: with producer Soca Child and the song “Madness”, he became the UK's first Road March King and won the UK Soca Monarch.

His bouyon songs

In 2014, “I Like That” came out on Club Caribbean Music and Flipside Entertainment, tagged bouyon 2014. He wrote it himself; Sheldon Alfred, Terry Victor and DJ Flip produced it, and DJ Flip mixed it in Dominica. Then came “Ca Ka Fete” in 2017 and “BMA” for the 2019 season, billed as “Dominica/ UK”.

Also in 2019, “Drunk Already”, with DJ Junez on the Mad Fellas Riddim, was posted by JulianspromosTV as “2019 Bouyon” and is past 4,800 views. The numbers are modest, but they show a London singer writing for a Dominican crowd.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Soca Johnny?

A singer born in London and raised in Dominica, active in UK soca and in bouyon.

Did he enter the Bouyon Monarch?

Yes. He sang at the 2016 final at the national stadium and was among the 2017 finalists.

What did he sing in 2016?

Gyal Tonight, released in January 2016 as Bouyon 2016.

What are his UK hits?

Drunk Tonight around 2008, then Madness, which won him the UK Soca Monarch and the UK Road March in 2010.

What is his most watched bouyon song?

Drunk Already with DJ Junez, on the Mad Fellas Riddim, past 4,800 views on JulianspromosTV.
