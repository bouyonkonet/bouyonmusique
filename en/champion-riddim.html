Champion Riddim: R.B.M's bouyon for Dominica Mas 2019

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Champion Riddim is a Dominican bouyon compilation released on February 14, 2019 by Nature Island Records under the name R.B.M. Five vocal tracks and an instrumental, arranged by Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green, with Carlyn XP, Colton T, Raii, Shelly, King Dice, Shadowflow and Daddy Chess on the mic. R.B.M Productions called it its contribution to Carnival 2019.

A compilation built for Carnival 2019

On February 4, 2019, the R.B.M Productions channel posted a short promo. The text under it announced a “Bouyon Riddim Compilation” as the team's contribution to Carnival 2019, featuring “some of Dominicas best artists” such as Carlyn XP, Shelly, King Dice, Shadowflow and Colton T. The stated goal was to take Dominica and its culture to the world. Ten days later, on February 14, the EP landed on Deezer under the name R.B.M, on the Nature Island Records label.

The credits posted under each song describe a tight crew. Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green arranged the riddim. Dernel Green mixed everything at Dernz Music Studios, Cornell Phillip mastered it at Imperial Publishing, and R.B.M Productions is credited as producer. Several pages also list Rhum Boyz Music Group as exclusive producer. One oddity: Deezer files the EP under reggae and ska, while every YouTube page bills it “2019 Bouyon”. For context, see our page on bouyon in Dominica and our guide to how a riddim becomes a song.

Five voices, five recording rooms

Each singer cut vocals somewhere different. Colton T recorded “Champion”, which he wrote himself as Colton E Thomas, at ICM Records. Carlyn XP recorded the opener, “Bouyon Army”, at Imperial Publishing with Cornell Phillip; Michelle Henderson and Carlyn Xavier-Phillip share the writing credit, with backing vocals from Jason “Froggy” Joseph and Mekhel Lee. Raii, Raheem Henderson, wrote “Cya Hold Me Back” and recorded it at Dernz.

“Cya Stop De Sewo” puts three voices together: Shelly (Sheldon Alfred, one of the three arrangers), King Dice (Dennison Joseph) and Shadowflow (Wayne Robinson), recorded at Sonic Art Studios. Daddy Chess, Chester Letang, closes with “Voltage Up”, recorded at Music Box Studios by Kendel “Killer” Laurent. So the Swinging Stars veteran sits on the same riddim as the generation of Colton T and Shelly.

How the riddim travelled

R.B.M Productions uploaded “Champion” and “Cya Hold Me Back” on release day, February 14, 2019. The next day Colton T posted his own lyric video on ColtonTMusic, and DjeasyPromos put out a riddim mix billed “Bouyon 2019”. On February 16, JulianspromosTV carried “Bouyon Army”, “Champion”, “Cya Stop De Sewo” and “Voltage Up”. When read, “Bouyon Army” there was past 4,500 views, “Cya Stop De Sewo” 3,600 and “Champion” 3,500, while the DjeasyPromos mix had reached 8,000.

Those are small numbers, but the line-up shows what the project was aiming for: a Bouyon Monarch, a former Soca Monarch and newer names on one three-minute instrumental. The EP is credited to R.B.M; the sources consulted do not spell out the initials or say who is behind them. Watch out for namesakes too: several dancehall riddims share the name and have nothing to do with this Dominican release.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Champion Riddim?

R.B.M Productions, per the YouTube credits; arranged by Sheldon Alfred, Hadley Joseph and Dernel Green, mixed by Dernel Green, mastered by Cornell Phillip at Imperial Publishing, with Rhum Boyz Music Group as exclusive producer.

When was the Champion Riddim released?

On February 14, 2019 on Deezer, through Nature Island Records, after a promo posted by R.B.M Productions on February 4, 2019.

Which songs are on the Champion Riddim?

Bouyon Army (Carlyn XP), Champion (Colton T), Cya Hold Me Back (Raii), Cya Stop De Sewo (Shelly, King Dice and Shadowflow), Voltage Up (Daddy Chess), plus the instrumental.

Is it a bouyon riddim?

Yes. The promo calls it a bouyon riddim compilation and every YouTube page is tagged “2019 Bouyon”, even though Deezer lists the EP as reggae and ska.

Is this the dancehall Champion Riddim?

No. This Champion Riddim is R.B.M's Dominican release on Nature Island Records; other riddims with the same name exist in dancehall and are unrelated.
