Litleboy LSBeats767: Pow Pow / Allo Doudou, SNEP Gold Single

By Smokiller · Published September 30, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026

Litleboy LSBeats767 is an artist and beatmaker credited on 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou', alongside Niska and Miimii KDS. The track passed 15,000,000 equivalent streams in France and was certified Gold Single by SNEP in 2026, according to SNEP's own certification post. That, for now, is the central fact of his dossier, and it is a considerable one: a credit on a French gold record for a bouyon track. The 767 in his name is Dominica's telephone area code. This page presents what that credit proves, without inventing the biography our dossier does not contain.

Who is Litleboy LSBeats767: one name, several credits

Litleboy LSBeats767 is presented on this site as an artist and beatmaker, and his credits show both trades: producer and composer for others, singer and lead artist on his own tracks. The published writing credits of his tracks, gathered for the profile of Quan, name Foster Marcel Xavier, or Foster Xavier depending on the listing. Two of his singles, "Leaving" and "Fell For You", came out under the label name Lsbeats767.

His name circulates under several spellings, and this profile gathers them without merging them at random: Lsbeatz litleboy in the 2016 composition credits, Litleboy or Litle Boy in DJ tracklists and awards lists, litleboy lsbeats767 in the title of a 2026 remix. No open source gives a town, a date of birth or a biography, and this page invents none.

A credit on 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou'

On 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou', the name Litleboy LSBeats767 appears alongside two very different profiles: Niska, a major name in French language rap, and Miimii KDS, a Guadeloupean singer on the rise. Being credited on such a track means taking part in a meeting between mainstream French rap and Antillean bouyon. His role is quoted as published: we do not detail a production split that the source does not detail.

The site's recordings log completes that credit. "Allo Doudou" and "Pow Pow" also appear there as 2026 releases, and a version titled "Allo Doudou (feat. litleboy lsbeats767) (Spain Remix)", released in 2026 under Miimii KDS's name, credits him as a featured artist. The track kept living after its release, with his name in the credits.

15 million equivalent streams and the SNEP Gold Single

The fact is established by the most direct source possible: the certification post from SNEP, the French body that certifies sales and streams. 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' passed 15,000,000 equivalent streams in France, the Gold Single threshold, and the certification was published in 2026. Equivalent streams are the official measure that converts listening into a sales equivalent: a figure verified by a certifying body, not a platform counter quoted without context.

For bouyon, this certification is an event, and the bouyon goes global page lists it among the proofs that the sound converts on the French market, beyond social platforms. Bouyon Shattaz covered it in October 2026 with a gold scheme reel, using the visual language of certifications to say, in one image, that the genre had just won a form of recognition in France that can be counted and archived.

Before the gold: composer of 'Sa Ka Débodé' in 2016

The oldest documented credit goes back ten years before the gold record. According to Shazam, "Sa Ka Débodé", by Bilix featuring Mr Boka, came out on January 12, 2016 on the Ôréveil label, at 160 BPM. The composition credits name two composers, Dj Weez and Lsbeatz litleboy, with Franck Offranc credited for the lyrics. The DJ Weez profile identifies this Lsbeatz litleboy as the producer known on this site as Litleboy LSBeats767.

That credit changes the reading of his path. Before being a name in the credits of a track certified in France, Litleboy LSBeats767 was one of the two composers of a song Bilix still cites as the one that made him known, and that Viberate ranks among his most played tracks. The gold record's beatmaker has a decade of craft behind him.

His tracks, from 'Yo baw gwopwel' to 'Fell For You'

The site's recordings log, checked on Shazam, follows his releases as lead artist. In 2022, "Yo baw gwopwel" came out as a single, then "Pa ka tan / Man allu looking for", with Quan as featured artist and co-writer, on October 18, 2022 on 3238844 Records DK2, at 159 BPM according to Shazam; its video, published as Litleboy x Quan, Pa ka tan, Bouyon 2023, kept it alive for another season. In 2023 came two singles, "SYA-SYB" and "You".

"Leaving" came out on December 10, 2024 on Lsbeats767, written by Foster Xavier, Quan Denis and Tafari Maffei, the writer name of DJ Taffy; its video is published under the name Quan Dan x Litleboy x Taffy. In 2025 followed "Let Me See", with Freddy as featured artist, and "Fell For You", again with Quan, on vocals and writing. Seven singles in four years, four of them shared with Quan or Freddy: the catalogue of a producer who sings, and who builds with a stable circle.

'Someone Else' and the duo with Quan

The track that took this circle to regional scale is "Someone Else". Released in 2024 on the Summer Of Carnivals compilation, on DSM Music and Monk Music according to the Qobuz credits, it brings together Litleboy as lead artist, with Quan and Trilla G featured, and it is written by Foster Marcel Xavier, Gael Jno Baptiste and Quan Denis. At the 2025 Dominica Music Awards, presented on January 3, 2026, it won Collaboration of the Year, according to the winners list published by Soca News, and it also received Soca Collaboration of the Year at the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards.

Around that track, DJ tracklists show the duo at work: "Kay Ka Fet", credited depending on the list to Quan alone or to Litleboy and Quan; "Your Friend", credited to Quan and Litle Boy with Freddy featured; and "Nasty Freestyle Medley", where Litleboy shares the rhythm with Gwada G, Trixx, Freddy and Niice. The Dreamer World session published on YouTube also presents "Early Morning Freestyle" by Quan Dan, with Litleboy and Mr Keyz.

Awards, bills and the Dominica scene

The official lists of 2025 confirm his place. At the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, litleboy was among the nine nominees for Bouyon Artist of the Year, with Asa Bantan, Kenny G, Mr. Ridge, Quan, Shelly Alfred, Trilla G, Triple K and Pudaz; the title went to Mr. Ridge. At the 2025 Dominica Music Awards, Litle Boy was a finalist for Producer of the Year, alongside DJ Taffy and Tha Wizzard among others.

On stage, Litle Boy was on the bill of Bouyon Day, Bouyon in de Streets, on Hanover Street in Roseau on February 25, 2025, according to the Discover Dominica Authority release, then in May 2025 on the bill of the Strictly the Best Festival, Bouyon Edition, in Saint Martin. Those public traces place him in Dominica's bouyon scene, which gives the 767 in his name, the island's telephone area code, an anchoring those traces make consistent, without turning it into a biography.

Why this profile matters, and where to listen

Litleboy LSBeats767 links two stories that are often told apart: the Dominican bouyon of tracklists, national awards and shared tracks, and the bouyon certified in France. The same name composed "Sa Ka Débodé" in 2016, led "Someone Else" in 2024 and sits in the credits of a Gold Single in 2026. The names in a credits list deserve to be written correctly, starting with his.
