Who Jasse is: a feature name, and nothing else published

Let us start with the exact inventory, because the whole file fits inside it. Jasse is credited on a single verified record, a record by another artist. “Not A Word” is a record by Khallion, a bouyon artist from Dominica documented on this site, and its full heading, as the platform publishes it, is “Khallion - Not A Word ft Jasse & Gwada G | (Bouyon 2026)”. In that heading, Jasse appears after the letters ft, the English abbreviation of featuring, which names the guest artists. That is the only function the source attributes to him: a guest voice on another artist record, with no other named post.

The name appears a second time on the same page, and the place matters. The keyword block of the video, the search listing the channel files in order to be found, lines up the names of the Khallion circuit, Reo, Ridge, Shelly, Signal Band, Trilla G, Litle Boy and others, then the titles “stand up” and “not a word”, and ends with the word jasse, in last position. That presence confirms that the channel associates the name of Jasse with this record in its own metadata, but a keyword is not a credit: it says neither what Jasse sings, nor for how long, nor in which post of the making he worked. This profile flags it as support for the feature billing, and nothing more.

No source consulted documents a record where Jasse is the lead artist, nor a second verified feature under this name, nor an artist page under his name on any platform. No biography, no interview and no official account confirmed by direct opening was found during this research, carried out on October 20, 2026. Searching on the name Jasse alone also returns namesakes with no connection, including a visual artist documented under a neighbouring name, whom no element ties to the Khallion record: this profile merges none of those names with his, and the Jasse of this page is exactly and only the guest of “Not A Word”.

The most precise fact in the file therefore lies in the company. A feature never reads alone: it also says who does the inviting. Here, the invitation comes from an artist in the middle of a regional expansion year, under the banner of his own label, and Jasse shares the guest line with Gwada G, an established name of the current Dominican scene. To be one of the two guests on such a record, on a page that publishes nothing else about him, draws an exact position: a voice the circuit knows well enough to bill in a bouyon 2026 heading, and which the public archives do not yet document beyond that. This profile holds both halves of that sentence together, without inflating the first or hiding the second.

For a record with two features, the page is therefore one of total sobriety, and the file of Jasse inherits that sobriety: everything that concerns him fits into the heading line and the closing keyword.

Khallion and Gwada G: the two names that frame the feature

To place Jasse, the two artists who share his heading must be placed, since their circuit gives the feature its context. Khallion is a bouyon artist from Dominica, whose published credits give the full name, Bertist “Khallion” Jeffers. His catalogue holds the album “777 Di Bouyon Rockstar” in 2025, a run of singles, and in 2026 the single “Stand Up”, shared with Jamaican dancehall star Konshens, DeeArr and Boogy Rankss. The same year, he stands among the Dominican bouyon artistes nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards, and his releases appear under his KhallioNation Records banner. “Not A Word” belongs to that sequence: a season record by an artist building, record after record, a house under his own name.

Gwada G, the second guest, is also a bouyon artist from Dominica, and this site presents him that way on the basis of the official World Creole Music Festival 2026 documents, which file him among the homegrown Dominica artistes, despite a name that evokes Guadeloupe. His file, unlike that of Jasse, is that of an installed name of the current scene, present in the bills of the Roseau festival. The guest line of “Not A Word” therefore brings together two opposite profiles: a documented name of the scene, and a name for which this record is the first opened trace. That contrast is information in itself: it shows that the Khallion page works as a showcase where the next generation stands beside the established names, a constant practice of bouyon season headings.

The grammar of the heading sums up that mechanism. The name of the lead artist opens the line, his label signs the description, and the guests appear in the order in which the channel displays them, Jasse first, Gwada G next. No source says who sings which verse on “Not A Word”, nor whether Jasse and Gwada G share a verse, a chorus or separate passages, and this profile splits nothing that the page does not split. What is established is simpler and stronger: in 2026, on a bouyon record released by one of the most exposed Dominican artists of the year, the first guest name the listener reads is that of Jasse.

Bouyon 2026, and the limits of the file, stated

The context of the genre recalls what a bouyon 2026 heading line is worth. Bouyon was born in Dominica at the end of the 1980s, and bouyon soca was started in Dominica in 2003 by Dada, according to the first hand testimony of Smokiller, a Dominican-born producer and a participant in that era. A generation later, the bouyon season runs on the single filed by vintage, published on the pages of the artists and their personal labels, and features are its ordinary currency of exchange: an established name invites one or two others, and the shared heading circulates the publics. “Not A Word” is a clean specimen of that system, and the presence of Jasse in its heading is his documented place in the season.

This profile ends with its limits, because they form half of its information. No solo record by Jasse was verified on the platforms consulted during this research, carried out on October 20, 2026: no single under his name, no artist page of his own, no album, no second feature confirmed by a primary page. No biography, no official photograph and no social media account confirmed by direct opening was found, so the structured data of this page links to no external account. His personal origin is published by no source: no island, no town and no country is stated here, and the Dominica of this page is that, documented, of the two other artists in the heading, Khallion and Gwada G, not an origin lent to their guest.

The verified scope therefore fits into one record and one billing. The record: “Not A Word”, by Khallion, billed bouyon 2026, published under the line © KhallioNation Records, documented by its YouTube page opened in full. The billing: Jasse as a feature in the heading, alongside Gwada G, and the word jasse closing the keyword block of the same page. The mix signal, “Vivre La Vie” with Tallyboy in the tracklist of the Miami carnival mix by DJ JEL, is published separately, as a signal, with its exact status. Anything circulating elsewhere about Jasse without a source, an age, an origin, a solo discography or a full identity, should be read with the caution that scope imposes.

The listening, while waiting for what comes next, fits into one record, and this profile owns that. “Not A Word” is heard as a page of the bouyon 2026 season of Khallion, and as the only verified trace of his first guest. The paths of the two other names continue on their pages, Khallion, from “777 Di Bouyon Rockstar” to “Stand Up”, and Gwada G. If Jasse one day publishes a record under his own name, an artist page or a biography, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. In the meantime, it publishes what the page gives, no more and no less: a guest name, a shared title, and a season, 2026.
