Pow Pow / Allo Doudou: the gold single from Niska, Litleboy and Miimii KDS

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026

Bouyon has won a gold record in France. Pow Pow / Allo Doudou, the joint title from Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS, was certified a gold single by SNEP on October 8, 2026, three months after its release, with over 15 million equivalent streams in France.

A gold single in three months

The certification is listed on SNEP's certifications page: singles, Pow Pow / Allo Doudou, Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS, Charo Inc / Warner Music France, Gold, release date June 26, 2026, certification date October 8, 2026, time to gold three months. The title therefore passed the gold threshold in a single quarter, a rare performance for a bouyon-driven track.

The threshold passed represents over 15 million equivalent streams in France, according to the certification report. That is the figure that triggered the certification, and the one SNEP recorded for the single.

Two songs in one video

The story of the title is double. Niska first put online, at the end of June, a single video chaining two distinct songs. A few days later, the two titles came out separately on streaming platforms: Pow Pow as a duo, and Allo Doudou with singer Miimii KDS.

It is the pairing of the two tracks, counted as a single by SNEP, that has just been certified gold. On Litleboy's page, the combined track alone passes 870,000 streams, a count specific to that page and not an overall total.

Three artists, three scenes

Niska, from Sarcelles, is one of the major voices of French rap, with a first solo mixtape, Charo Life, in 2015, then the albums Commando in 2017 and Mr Sal in 2019. Litleboy LSBeats767 is credited on the title under that name only, and Miimii KDS is the Guadeloupean bouyon singer who carries Allo Doudou.

The meeting therefore brings together rap from Sarcelles, bouyon production and the voice of an artist from Guadeloupe. That combination made the title a bridge between French rap and the bouyon current, born in Dominica and very present in the French Antilles, carried by fast and festive rhythms.

Bouyon wins in France

The certification puts bouyon on the map of French certifications, alongside the rap and pop titles of the moment. The title shows that a sound born in Dominica can travel through Guadeloupe and France and come back certified, with figures measured by SNEP and not estimated.

For bouyon, the gold record for Pow Pow / Allo Doudou is a step: after the certifications of artists from the Gwada scene, it is a title built on the bouyon sound that enters the official accounts of French music.

Frequently asked questions

When was Pow Pow / Allo Doudou certified?

On October 8, 2026, according to SNEP's certifications page. The single came out on June 26, 2026 and reached gold in three months, with over 15 million equivalent streams in France.

Why are there two titles in one single?

Because Niska first published, at the end of June, a video chaining two distinct songs, then the two titles came out separately: Pow Pow as a duo and Allo Doudou with Miimii KDS. SNEP counted the pairing of the two tracks as a single.

Who are the three artists on the title?

Niska, rapper from Sarcelles, Litleboy LSBeats767, credited under that name, and Miimii KDS, bouyon singer from Guadeloupe. The title was released by Charo Inc and Warner Music France.
