Whale Oil Riddim: Boss Raw Records, bouyon and soca 2025

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Whale Oil Riddim is a Boss Raw Records project released on April 13, 2025, produced, mixed and mastered by Rajiv "Suhrawh" Sutherland. It holds three vocal tracks and an instrumental. Its way into bouyon is OG Park and Rum Lawd's "Whale Fu See", which the official video bills as both Bouyon 2025 and Vincy Soca 2025, and which was past 565,000 views when read.

Suhrawh and Boss Raw Records

On Deezer the riddim is four tracks dated April 13, 2025 on the Boss Raw Records label: "Shellings", "Renk It Up", "Whale Fu See" and the instrumental. The instrumental's listing, distributed by ONErpm, confirms the date and names the producer, Suhrawh, full name Rajiv Amron Sutherland. Executive production goes to Rasheid "Rawsheid" Sutherland for Boss Raw Records, who also did the artwork and animation for the videos.

Suhrawh is also spelled Suh Raw depending on the page, and he does more than produce: he sings "Shellings" with G.E.O and shares the writing on "Whale Fu See". The video titles place the project in Saint Vincent soca, with the tags Vincy Soca and SVG. See also our page on the bouyon riddim.

Whale Fu See, the bouyon track

Boss Raw Records uploaded "Whale Fu See" on release day, April 13, 2025, under a title with two labels: Bouyon 2025 and Vincy Soca 2025. The vocals are by OG Park, credited Omar Phillips, and Rum Lawd, credited Enrique Matthews; five people share the writing, with Rasheid Sutherland, Giovanni "GEO" Collins and Suhrawh. Deezer also lists it as a standalone single dated April 14.

It is by far the most watched song on the riddim: over 565,000 views on the official video when read, while the others top out in the tens of thousands. JulianspromosTV carried the same song as Soca 2025 only, a reminder that the bouyon label depends on which page presents it.

Shellings, Renk It Up and a cover

On May 9, 2025, two more songs reached YouTube. G.E.O posted "Shellings" with Suh Raw on his GEO 784 channel, titled Vincy Soca 2025, and JulianspromosTV uploaded "Renk It Up" by Skinny Fabulous, the Saint Vincent soca star, written by Gamal Doyle himself and produced by Suhrawh. The M.A.D. One Music mix, published June 5, 2025, runs through these three songs and repeats the April 13 date.

A fourth vocal exists outside the release: "Thickaz Girl Bend Down Pause", posted on May 18, 2025 by Tender Boss on his own channel with the Whale Oil Riddim tag. His description is upfront about it: a soca remix, not an original, since he does not own the riddim. This page sticks to the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 28, 2026; a bass-boosted upload credited to Godlar, seen on a repost channel, is left out.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Whale Oil Riddim?

Rajiv "Suhrawh" Sutherland, also written Suh Raw, who mixed and mastered it too; Rasheid Sutherland is executive producer for Boss Raw Records.

When was the Whale Oil Riddim released?

April 13, 2025, the date of the Deezer EP and of the instrumental's listing, on Boss Raw Records.

Is the Whale Oil Riddim bouyon?

The project is mostly billed as Vincy Soca 2025; only the official video of Whale Fu See, by OG Park and Rum Lawd, also carries the Bouyon 2025 label.

Which songs are on the Whale Oil Riddim?

Shellings by G.E.O and Suh Raw, Renk It Up by Skinny Fabulous, Whale Fu See by OG Park and Rum Lawd, and the instrumental; Tender Boss voiced an unofficial remix.

What is the most played song?

Whale Fu See: over 565,000 views on the Boss Raw Records video when read.
