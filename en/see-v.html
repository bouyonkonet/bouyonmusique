See V: He Baby and HOT #Oups 5 with FLW and Team Bwè, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

See V is a voice that the Team Bwè Tou Sa audience heard on two titles two years apart, each time next to FLW: “He Baby” in February 2024, on a LAZA beat, then “HOT #Oups 5” in April 2026, with Lünik. Both listings published by the team’s official channel carry the word bouyon in their hashtags. This profile goes through those two releases credit by credit and stops where the sources stop: no biography, no legal name and no origin are published for See V.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

He Baby, February 2024

On February 16, 2024, the T BTS Officiel channel posted the official audio of “He Baby”, billed as an FLW (T-BTS) title with See V. The description announces FLW’s new single featuring See V, coming soon to the platforms, and splits the roles into three lines: vocals by FLW and See V, beat by LAZA, mix by DJ Spïcky. The text opens on an end of week joke about Friday, and the hashtags file the track under bouyon 2024. It runs 1 minute 55.

Three months later, on May 9, 2024, a “Hé Baby (Remix)” appeared on FLW’s automatic channel, distributed by TuneCore under the line 2024 Team Bwè Tou Sa. That listing credits FLW alone and names neither See V nor a genre. This profile mentions it so readers do not mistake it for the original, but does not count it among See V’s verified titles.

HOT #Oups 5, April 2026

The second title arrived on April 16, 2026 in distribution, then on April 17 as official audio on the team channel: “HOT #Oups 5”, by Lünik, FLW and See V. The beat is credited to iceboyismad139 and the mix to GAMMALAB, the studio found across the team’s catalogue. The description points to the three voices’ accounts and files the track under the hashtags bouyon, nouveauté and teambwètousa. The distributed version carries the line 2026 Team Bwè Tou Sa and runs 2 minutes 05.

The distribution listing also publishes a roll of named credits: three names on vocals, one recording engineer and four writers and composers. It does not say which legal name matches which stage name. This profile therefore cites that roll as the credit list of the track, without drawing an identity for See V from it.

The limits of the file

The handles published for See V, SEE_V971 on YouTube and sev_97170 on Instagram, contain digits that suggest a French Caribbean area code, and the “HOT #Oups 5” listing carries a guadeloupe hashtag. Those are signals, not statements: no page says where See V was born or lives, so this site assigns no island. The setting is Team Bwè Tou Sa, documented on its own page and on our page about bouyon in Guadeloupe.

Two verified titles, two regular partners and one collective: that is the whole file on October 23, 2026. If See V releases a solo title or an official presentation, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is See V?

See V is a voice associated with Team Bwè Tou Sa, credited with FLW on “He Baby” in 2024 and with Lünik and FLW on “HOT #Oups 5” in 2026. No public biography was found.

Who produced He Baby?

The official audio description credits LAZA for the beat and DJ Spïcky for the mix. The track went online on February 16, 2024 on the T BTS Officiel channel, with the hashtag bouyon 2024.

Is the Hé Baby remix a See V title?

The remix listing published on May 9, 2024 credits FLW alone. This site therefore does not count it among See V’s verified titles.

When did HOT #Oups 5 come out?

On April 16, 2026 in distribution, under the line 2026 Team Bwè Tou Sa, then on April 17, 2026 as official audio. The beat is credited to iceboyismad139 and the mix to GAMMALAB.

Where is See V from?

No opened source says. The digits in the social handles are signals, not proof, and this site assigns no island.
