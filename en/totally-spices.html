Totally Spice's: Chien, bouyon, Guadeloupe

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Totally Spice's is a women bouyon group from Guadeloupe. Its public record holds three verified tracks, “Chien”, “Bouè'y Bay” and “6 Dwèt”, two voices named in the credits, Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina, and one consecration few groups on the scene can claim: a place at the Eurockéennes festival in Belfort in July 2025, on the Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon stage, alongside Holly G, with the festival director himself presenting both acts as the emblematic women groups of the bouyon scene. This profile publishes those traces one by one, with their exact credits, states what the record does not hold, and warns the reader about a trap search engines set on this name: another, unrelated artist circulates under an almost identical name.

A group, two named voices: what the credits say about Totally Spice's

Totally Spice's is a group, not a solo artist: that is the first thing the credits establish, and the one automated listings most often miss. On “6 Dwèt”, the listing published by Shazam names two singers, Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina, both credited on vocals and on lyrics. On “Chien”, the credits name Carcheline Cereme on vocals and lyrics. The official Eurockéennes 2025 playlist published by Qobuz confirms the same pair of voices and writers for the group track it selects: Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina, lyricists and singers.

The rest of the team can be read in the same credits. Composition and programming on the first two tracks return to Daryl Clarisse, and production to D0 Production, the Guadeloupe producer studio whose name precedes the group name in the presentation of “Bouè'y Bay”: “D0 production Presents Totally Spice's”. On “6 Dwèt”, production and arrangement are signed Yoan Courcet. The group therefore works the way the Gwada scene formations of today work: voices in front, a producer behind, and credits published track by track, which allow persons to be named instead of a biography being guessed.

Around the tracks, the credits also draw the Guadeloupe production circuit the group was born into: a producer studio, D0 Production, presenting the group as its signature; a video studio, Play Studio 971, whose name carries the Guadeloupe postcode; a recording studio, Urban Music; and a named publisher, Yapasdesouci.comm. That infrastructure of small identified structures is the one of Gwada bouyon today, and the fact that the group tracks document it cleanly, instead of circulating without credits, is part of what makes this record readable and verifiable, track after track.

That method has a limit, and this profile states it at once: no source consulted publishes a biography of the group, neither a formation date, nor the path of the two singers before the first tracks, nor an official member list beyond the credited voices. Totally Spice's exists publicly through its tracks and its stages, and it is through them that this profile tells it. To place the group inside the women scene of bouyon, our women of bouyon page gives the general context.

Chien: the founding track, between the 2023 video and the 2024 single

The first documented track of the group is “Chien”. Its official video, published under the title “D0Production Feat. Totally Spice's, Chien”, was directed by Play Studio 971 and is dated 2023 in its own description: instrumental by D0 Production, recording at Urban Music, publishing Yapasdesouci.comm, and thanks to the Drift Riderz and the Gwada Hot Boyz, the crews of Guadeloupe motorcycle and party culture. The track then came out as a single on February 6, 2024, under the iMD-DRECORDS label, according to the Shazam listing, which gives a tempo of 162 beats per minute and more than 25,000 recognitions shown on the track page.

The credits of “Chien” fix the group formula: lyrics and vocals by Carcheline Cereme, composition and programming by Daryl Clarisse, production by D0 Production. The length, 2 minutes 07 in the Deezer catalogue, is that of the current bouyon format: short, fast, built for clips, mixes and parties. The track also lives its life as a mix track: the published tracklist of the DJ T-Sia mix “Vaïbz Party #2”, a Shatta, Bouyon and Soca selection from Carnaval 2024, places “Chien” at the twentieth minute, among the Antillean scene tracks of the moment. Being selected by a DJ who tours between Martinique, Saint Lucia, Guadeloupe, Saint Martin and Miami means existing inside the real circuit of the genre.

“Chien” is therefore the entry point of the group, and its most documented track: a video, a single date, a label, a tempo, full credits and a trace of circulation in mixes. It is also the track the bouyon playlists of the platforms select, where it sits alongside the productions of the same Guadeloupe season. For the listener, the whole rest of the record reads from there: a group of women voices, a producer sound, and a track that travelled further than most first singles.

Bouè'y Bay and 6 Dwèt: the collaborations that widen the circle

The second track of the record, “Bouè'y Bay”, pairs the group with TiiTii NBA: the full billing, as published by the TiiTii NBA artist page on Shazam, is “Bouè'y Bay (feat. TiiTii NBA) [D0 production Presents Totally Spice's]”, and the track ranks among the most recognised on his page, with 33,100 Shazams shown. In the Deezer catalogue, “Bouè'y Bay” runs 2 minutes 05, with Carcheline Cereme on lyrics and Daryl Clarisse on composition: the same team as “Chien”, widened to a male guest from the same scene.

The third track changes the scale of collaboration. “6 Dwèt”, released on February 15, 2025 under the Dreamtracks label, is billed “LESTEF KJF & Totally Spice's”, with DJ Luchshiy as a feature, according to the Shazam listing: French language, 160 beats per minute. The credits tell the meeting: Naima Dimina and Carcheline Cereme on vocals and lyrics, Stephane Cornano on vocals and composition, Corane Vincent on programming and composition, Yoan Courcet on arrangement and production. It is on this track that the second voice of the group, Naima Dimina, appears by name in the public credits, which confirms the reading of Totally Spice's as a duo of voices rather than as a singer surrounded by backing vocalists.

These two collaborations draw the circulation method of Gwada bouyon: a presentation track with TiiTii NBA, a collective track with LESTEF KJF and a DJ feature, and between the two, the original production team kept in place. The group has released no album in the sources consulted, and this profile invents none: its verified catalogue is a catalogue of singles and collaborations, which is the normal form of the scene at this stage. Eurockéennes 2025: Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon, the festival step

The consecration of the group plays out on stage, and it is documented by the festival itself. At the Eurockéennes de Belfort 2025, one of the major French summer festivals, the Friday July 4, 2025 programme includes a stage named Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon, bringing together Holly G and Totally Spice's. The festival presentation describes this stage as an introduction of festivalgoers to bouyon, a dance step and a musical style of Antillean origin, and writes, about both acts: “Even 7,000 km from their Guadeloupe bases, count on Holly G and Totally Spice's to raise the temperature and the bpm to 160.”

The director general of the festival, Jean-Paul Roland, asked about the highlights of the 2025 edition, places the bouyon scene at the same level as the international headliners of the bill, Justice, Damso or DJ Snake: he says he puts the accent on the new urban musics with “the figures of the Bouyon scene, come from the Antilles, among them two emblematic women groups, Totally Spice's and Holly G”. The word emblematic is his, and this profile quotes it as a programmer statement, not as an official title: it nevertheless states the position the group held, in the summer of 2025, in the eyes of the major French festivals, that of a women and Guadeloupe face of bouyon.

The official festival playlist published by Qobuz extends that presence through a group track released in 2025 on Dreamtracks, produced by Yoan Courcet, composed by Allan Blonbou, with Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina on lyrics and vocals. The playlist page does not let the title of that track be read in the accessible detail, and this profile therefore does not guess it: it keeps the presence, the label, the year and the credits, which are enough to establish that the group had, at festival time, a current release on the same label as “6 Dwèt”.

That festival step matters in the history of the genre. Bouyon was born in Dominica, and its history of bouyon page tells the journey from Roseau to the French Antilles. That a full stage of a festival of this size be devoted to bouyon, and entrusted to two women formations from Guadeloupe, marks the moment the genre stops being a matter of specialist parties and enters the general programming of major French events. Totally Spice's is one of the names attached to that moment, with only three tracks to its count: proof that in bouyon today, the stage can come before the catalogue.

Marseille 2026 and the party circuit: the journey continues

The festival journey extends the following summer. The published programme of L'Été Marseillais 2026, the free festival of the City of Marseille, announces for Saturday July 25, 2026 an urban music night on the Scène sur l'eau, with Scorpio Qveen, Jungeli, and “Holly G & Totally Spice's” reunited once again. The Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon formula therefore plays again a year later, in another major city and in front of a municipal festival audience: the two Guadeloupe acts have become, for French programmers, the reference women bill of bouyon.

Alongside the festivals, the group also holds the circuit of specialist parties. The Shotgun ticketing platform announces, for Saturday October 31, 2026, a “Halloween Spices” night organised by Good Life événements at Jam Session in Melesse, in Brittany, with an “exclusive Totally Spice's show” on the bill and a bouyon, shatta, afrobeats and coupé-décalé programme. The contrast is instructive: the same group plays generalist festival stages in summer and targeted Afro-Caribbean parties in autumn, the two circuits between which bouyon circulates in France.

Taken together, these dates state the real position of the group in 2026: few tracks, but stages that many more prolific formations have not obtained, and a clear image for programmers, that of the women voices of Gwada bouyon. Our bouyon Guadeloupe page places this trajectory inside the scene of the island, and our women of bouyon page connects it to the other women formations of the genre, from Dominica to the French Antilles.

The name trap, and what this profile does not yet know

Finally, this profile must flag a confusion that search engines maintain on this name, because it distorts most automated listings. Certain ticketing sites publish, under the name “Totally Spice”, a generated biography describing a hyperpop, glitchcore and digicore artist, with tracks called “BOOM” and “C.L.E.A.N”. No source consulted connects that text to the bouyon group from Guadeloupe, to its singers or to its verified tracks: neither “Chien”, nor “Bouè'y Bay”, nor “6 Dwèt” appear in it, and neither Carcheline Cereme nor Naima Dimina is named there. This profile therefore treats that text as the listing of another, unrelated artist that aggregators merged with the group listing, and uses no element of it.

On the side of the verified record, the limits are clear and this profile states them: no published biography of the group, no formation date, no documented individual path for Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina outside the credits quoted, no album or EP in the catalogues consulted, and no official account of the group verified by direct opening during this research, so the structured data of this page links to no social network. If the group publishes an album, a biography or a verifiable official account, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on this site.

What remains is the essential, which is already considerable for a three track record: a founding single circulating in the Carnaval mixes, two collaborations that properly name its two voices, and two summers in a row on the bills of major French festivals, alongside Holly G, under a formula that became a brand, Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon. Totally Spice's is the Guadeloupe demonstration that a bouyon group can exist first through the stage and through the exact credit of its tracks, even before it has a long catalogue to tell.

Frequently asked questions

Who are Totally Spice's?

Totally Spice's is a women’s bouyon group from Guadeloupe, carried by two voices named in the credits, Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina. Its file holds three verified tracks, “Chien”, “Bouè'y Bay” and “6 Dwèt”, and a rare consecration: the Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon stage of the Eurockéennes de Belfort, on July 4, 2025, alongside Holly G.

How is the name spelled: Totally Spice's, Totally Spices or Totally Spice?

The form published by the credits and the festivals is Totally Spice's, with an apostrophe and a final s. The “Chien” video also carries the hashtag Totallyspices, without the apostrophe. The form Totally Spice, with no s, is however that of a biography generated by ticketing sites for a hyperpop artist unrelated to the Guadeloupe group: that is the search trap to avoid.

Where are Totally Spice's from?

From Guadeloupe. The Eurockéennes 2025 presentation speaks of their “Guadeloupe bases”, 7,000 kilometres from Belfort, and the group’s credits map the island’s production circuit: the producing studio D0 Production, the video studio Play Studio 971, recording at Urban Music. The festival director presents them as a figure of the bouyon scene come from the Antilles.

Who are the singers of Totally Spice's?

The published credits name two voices: Carcheline Cereme and Naima Dimina, credited on vocals and lyrics on “6 Dwèt” and on the group track selected by the official Eurockéennes 2025 playlist. On “Chien”, Carcheline Cereme is credited on vocals and lyrics. No source consulted publishes any other named member or a biography of the group.

What are the verified tracks of Totally Spice's?

Three tracks are documented by the platforms: “Chien”, a single released on February 6, 2024 on iMD-DRECORDS, at 162 BPM; “Bouè'y Bay”, with TiiTii NBA as a feature, presented by D0 Production; and “6 Dwèt”, with LESTEF KJF and DJ Luchshiy, released on February 15, 2025 on Dreamtracks, at 160 BPM. No album or EP appears in the catalogues consulted.

Who produces the tracks of Totally Spice's?

The first two tracks, “Chien” and “Bouè'y Bay”, are produced by D0 Production, with Daryl Clarisse on composition and programming. On “6 Dwèt”, production and arrangement are signed Yoan Courcet, who also produces the group track selected by the Eurockéennes 2025 playlist, composed by Allan Blonbou, on Dreamtracks.

Who directed the Totally Spice's “Chien” video?

Play Studio 971, according to the description of the official video “D0Production Feat. Totally Spice's, Chien”, dated 2023. The instrumental is by D0 Production, the recording by Urban Music and the publishing by Yapasdesouci.comm, with thanks to Drift Riderz and Gwada Hot Boyz. The track then came out as a single on February 6, 2024, and DJ T-Sia’s “Vaïbz Party #2” mix places it at the twentieth minute.

Did Totally Spice's play the Eurockéennes?

Yes. The official Eurockéennes de Belfort 2025 programme places Totally Spice's and Holly G on Friday July 4, 2025 on the Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon stage, devoted to bouyon. The festival director, Jean-Paul Roland, presented both acts as the emblematic women’s groups of the bouyon scene come from the Antilles, in his own words.

What is the link between Totally Spice's and Holly G?

The two women’s groups from Guadeloupe have shared bills since 2025: the Bad Gyal Bouyon Lé Bon stage of the Eurockéennes on July 4, 2025, then the L’Été Marseillais night of July 25, 2026, billed “Holly G & Totally Spice's”. No recorded collaboration between them is documented: their published link is a stage link. See our Holly G profile.

Where can I see Totally Spice's live in 2026?

Two dates are published. On Saturday July 25, 2026, the group plays with Holly G on the Scène sur l’eau of L’Été Marseillais, the free festival of the City of Marseille, with Scorpio Qveen and Jungeli. On Saturday October 31, 2026, the “Halloween Spices” night by Good Life événements announces an exclusive show at the Jam Session in Melesse, Brittany.

Is Totally Spice's the hyperpop artist described on some ticketing sites?

No, nothing demonstrates it. Those sites publish under the name “Totally Spice” a generated biography speaking of hyperpop, glitchcore and tracks called “BOOM” and “C.L.E.A.N”, with no link to the singers, producers or verified tracks of the Guadeloupe group. This profile treats that text as the listing of another artist, merged by mistake by the aggregators.

Where can I hear Totally Spice's and the women of Guadeloupe bouyon?

Start with “Chien”, then “Bouè'y Bay” with TiiTii NBA and “6 Dwèt” with LESTEF KJF and DJ Luchshiy.
