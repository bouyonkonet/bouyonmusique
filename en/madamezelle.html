MadameZelle

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

MadameZelle is an artist name whose Bandcamp page gives New York as its location and files its titles under bouyon and RnBouyon. Two tracks appear there: “MadameZelle”, dated January 1, 2025, and “Tipsy Riddim”, released on January 2, 2026 and produced by Smokiller. On YouTube, that second track is posted as “Tipsy - [MadameZelle] [Bouyon 2026]”: “Tipsy” is the title, and MadameZelle the artist. This site states up front that Smokiller, producer of Tipsy, also signs the pages of bouyonmusique.com.

Scene: 🇺🇸 United States · All islands

Tipsy, and a mix up to correct

The title first appeared in the tracklist of Smokiller’s “Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1”, presented on this site on the Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1 page, as “Tipsy - [MadameZelle]”. Earlier research waves read Tipsy as the artist name and MadameZelle as the title. The pages opened for this profile show the reverse: the video is posted by MadameZelle’s channel, and MadameZelle’s Bandcamp page carries the track as “Tipsy Riddim”.

The YouTube video, posted on January 4, 2026 and running 1 minute 46, is an excerpt: the description points to the Bandcamp page for the full version and credits production to Smokiller. Its title carries the hashtags bouyonriddim, bouyonmusic and bouyonshattaz. On Bandcamp, “Tipsy Riddim” is dated January 2, 2026, runs 1 minute 43, carries the tags bouyon, rnbouyon, world and New York, and opens with the words Bouyon 2026, followed by a long presentation of the project as a bouyon album rooted in Dominica’s drum driven sound.

The title MadameZelle, 2025

The second track on the page carries the project’s name: “MadameZelle”, dated January 1, 2025, running 3 minutes 17, with the same tags, bouyon, rnbouyon, world and New York. Its credits name Michèle Charlemagne among the contributors. No page says whether that name is the project’s voice: this profile cites it as a credit without equating it with the artist.

The short bio on the Bandcamp page presents MadameZelle as a figure of the Caribbean woman, powerful and sovereign, without filter. It is an artist statement, summarized here rather than quoted, and not a biography: it gives no birthplace, no date and no career history.

What the location says, and what it does not

The Bandcamp page gives New York, New York as its location, and the New York tag recurs on both tracks. This site reports it as the profile’s stated location, not as an origin or a birthplace. A link with the bouyon diaspora is plausible, but no page puts it in words, so this profile does not present it as fact.

The verifiable file therefore holds two tracks, a Bandcamp page, a YouTube channel and a producer, Smokiller, who is Dominican-born. This profile will be expanded if MadameZelle releases more titles or a biography.

Frequently asked questions

Who is MadameZelle?

MadameZelle is an artist name whose Bandcamp page, located in New York, publishes two tracks tagged bouyon and rnbouyon: MadameZelle (2025) and Tipsy Riddim (2026).

Is Tipsy an artist?

No, according to the opened pages: Tipsy is the track title, released as Tipsy Riddim on Bandcamp and posted as “Tipsy - [MadameZelle] [Bouyon 2026]” on YouTube. The artist is MadameZelle.

Who produced Tipsy?

Smokiller, credited as producer on Bandcamp and on YouTube. Smokiller also signs the pages of this site, and the link is stated openly.

Where is MadameZelle from?

The Bandcamp page gives New York, New York as its location. No opened page gives a birthplace or an origin, and this site assigns none.

What is RnBouyon?

It is the tag, present on both MadameZelle tracks, for an R&B leaning bouyon, presented on this site’s RnBouyon page.
