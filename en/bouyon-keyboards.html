The keyboard men: Ridge, Earlson Matthew and the bouyon keyboard

By Smokiller · Published October 24, 2026

In bouyon, the voice takes the light, but the melody comes from the keyboards. Synth lines carry the riddims, hold the breaks and give the crowd the notes it sings. This page presents two names documented in the site files: Ridge and Earlson Matthew, and explains the place of the keyboard in the sound.

The keyboard, backbone of bouyon

Since WCK and cadence-lypso, the keyboard sits at the centre of the Dominica sound: it plays the melody, the chords and the calls that structure a party track. The site pages on Cornell Phillip, founding WCK keyboardist, and on the Roseau bands show that lineage line by line.

In modern bouyon, the keyboardist is often also the arranger: he builds the loop, places the rises and prepares the breaks where the singer brings the crowd back in.

Ridge, the name the bills know

Ridge is documented on this site by his full profile and his verified recordings. He is among the names the World Creole Music Festival guides list for night 1 of the 2026 edition, alongside the bands and voices of Dominica.

His file shows him at the crossing of keyboard playing and production, like many musicians of his generation in Roseau: the same musician plays on stage and builds in the studio.

Earlson Matthew, the credits file

Earlson Matthew is documented on this site as a credits file: his profile, built on opened sources, gathers his verified contributions track by track. The profile attributes to him no band role and no alias that the sources do not carry, and says so openly.

That kind of file matters for bouyon history: keyboardists and arrangers are often credited in small print in the metadata, and gathering their credits is the only way to make their share visible.

Hearing the keyboards

To hear this work, the Ridge and Earlson Matthew profiles on this site list their verified recordings, and the bouyon riddim page explains how a keyboard loop becomes a full riddim. The keyboard men are one of the series this site keeps documenting, credit by credit.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the known bouyon keyboardists?

The verified site files document Ridge and Earlson Matthew, and the older lineage of Cornell Phillip, founding keyboardist of WCK. Their profiles list their verified recordings and credits.

What is the role of the keyboard in bouyon?

The keyboard carries the melody, the chords and the calls that structure a party track. In modern bouyon, the keyboardist is often also arranger and producer.

Does Earlson Matthew have a band confirmed by the sources?

No. His profile on this site is a file of verified credits and says openly that no band role or alias is attributed without a source.
