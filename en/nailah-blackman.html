Nailah Blackman: Trinidad soca and How To Love, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Nailah Blackman is a soca singer and songwriter from Trinidad and Tobago, granddaughter of Ras Shorty I, the man credited with inventing soca. She is not a bouyon artist. Her verified bouyon song is “How To Love”, sung in 2025 with Dominica's Khallion and DeeArr, and co written by her.

A soca voice, not a bouyon one

Born in December 1997, Nailah Blackman grew up in a family of musicians: her grandfather Garfield Blackman, known as Lord Shorty and later Ras Shorty I, and her mother Abbi Blackman, a calypso singer. The Trinidad and Tobago Guardian says the family moved often before settling in Rio Claro.

Her career took off in 2017 with “Workout”, sung with the band Kes. Then came “Badishh” with Shenseea in 2018, the EP “The Reel” in 2019 and the album “Teknique” in 2022. In 2023 she won the Trinidad Road March with Skinny Fabulous on “Come Home”, whose video is past 11 million views.

How To Love, with Khallion and DeeArr

On July 7, 2025, Khallion released the album “777 Di Bouyon Rockstar” on KhallioNation Records. Track seven, “How To Love”, features Nailah Blackman and DeeArr. The song is written by Bertist Jeffers, Donnel Romain and Nailah Blackman, produced by Shako Production, with live bass by Keith Goddard of WCK.

The audio went up on July 10 on Khallion's channel, titled “Bouyon 2025”. It has passed 76,000 views. It is the first verified trace of the singer on a Dominica bouyon production.

The video, and where soca picks up

The official video came out on November 26, 2025. It was shot by Matt Tederick and Kendy Boniface, directed by Donnel “DeeArr” Romain and Matt Tederick, and edited by Khallion himself. It has passed 107,000 views.

The same season she sang “Try Me” with Trilla-G, a voice from the bouyon scene. The pages opened present that song as soca 2025, so this profile keeps it outside bouyon. For the line between the two genres, see our bouyon vs soca page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nailah Blackman a bouyon artist?

No. She is a soca singer from Trinidad and Tobago. She does sing on How To Love, a bouyon song by Khallion released in 2025.

What is How To Love?

Track seven of Khallion's album 777 Di Bouyon Rockstar, out on July 7, 2025, with Nailah Blackman and DeeArr. She co wrote it, and Shako Production produced it.

How many views does How To Love have?

Over 76,000 for the audio and over 107,000 for the official video, both on Khallion's channel.

Who is her grandfather?

Garfield Blackman, known as Lord Shorty and Ras Shorty I, who is credited with inventing soca.

Is Try Me with Trilla-G a bouyon song?

Not according to the pages opened, which present it as soca 2025. This profile does not count it among her bouyon songs.
