Kross Vybez: Arden P's bouyon band, from 2007 to No Pain

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Kross Vybez is a bouyon band whose live videos go back to 2007. Its dated songs run from “Hustle”, tagged bouyon 2009, to “No Pain”, released as bouyon 2014 and tagged Dominica. On the band's own channel, “Play My Song” is sung, written and produced by Arden Panthier, known today as Arden P.

A stage band from 2007

The oldest videos are concerts: “Kross Vybez Band live 2007”, posted by a studio in Nevis, and another 2007 recording on the mashupah99 archive channel. Then come a 2010 medley and live clips such as “Zero Tolerance” or “Boom”, dated 2013.

So Kross Vybez shows up on stage first, before its dated releases. Our Xs Groove page names Kross Vybez in the list of bands of that generation, with Ncore, Esclav and Signal.

From Hustle to No Pain

A channel devoted to bouyon reposted “Hustle” under the bouyon 2009 tag, then a 2010 song and “P.D.P”. In January 2014, DjeasyPromos put up “No Pain” as bouyon 2014, tagged Dominica, and the video is past 5,000 views.

The band's channel carries “No Pain” too, along with “Work It”, “Gyah”, “Come Back”, “Under The Weather”, “Bad Bumper”, a song with Asher Thomas and a 2015 live recording.

Play My Song and Arden P

On January 20, 2015, “Play My Song” came out under the name Kross Vybez Band. The description credits Arden P on vocals and Arden Panthier for writing, composition, production with Brent Samuel and Rodney Thomas, and the mix. It was recorded at Yow studio.

The same channel posts “Am I Wrong” under the Arden P name. That name, and the Yow brand, return in the newer bouyon releases on our Arden.P page. For the wider era, see the history of bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kross Vybez?

A bouyon band whose live videos go back to 2007, with songs such as Hustle, No Pain and Play My Song.

What is the link with Arden P?

Play My Song, released by the band in 2015, is sung by Arden P and written, composed and co-produced by Arden Panthier.

What is its most watched song?

No Pain, posted by DjeasyPromos in January 2014 as bouyon 2014, with more than 5,000 views.

Where is Kross Vybez from?

The sources we opened do not say. The 2014 video is tagged Dominica, and a 2007 live clip was posted by a studio in Nevis.

Is the band still active?

The latest dated video found is a 2015 live recording. The sources we opened do not mention a split.
