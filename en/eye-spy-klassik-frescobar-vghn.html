Eye Spy, Klassik Frescobar and VGHN

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Eye Spy was released on 16 January 2026. It is the first collaboration between Klassik Frescobar and VGHN, an exchange between Jamaica and Grenada, grounded in the rhythmic backbone of bouyon. The instrumental is by DJ Kalli and Boogy Rankss, and the track samples the anime Inuyasha.

On this page
  1. A Jamaica-Grenada meeting
  2. How the track was made
  3. Dansa and the road of Frescobar
  4. Frequently asked questions

A Jamaica-Grenada meeting

Klassik Frescobar was born Kamaal Burrowes. He is originally from Jamaica and based in Miami. VGHN is described as one of the notable voices of Grenada. The record is presented as an exchange between the two islands, a meeting of Jamaica and Grenada on a bouyon sound.

Both artists already have their pages on this site: Klassik Frescobar, a profile from the world of soca and bouyon, and VGHN, a voice from Grenada. Eye Spy is their first collaboration, and the first shared track of the two artists.

How the track was made

The instrumental was handled by DJ Kalli and Boogy Rankss. The track includes a sample from the anime Inuyasha. The process, as told by the 24hip-hop article, was fast: after connecting with the producers in New York, Frescobar mapped out the concept and structure in a short session, then the track was sent to VGHN, who completed his part within a day.

That making-of account comes from the 24hip-hop article, published on 24 April 2026. It is reported as such: it is the version of the artists and their producers, not an independent observation from this page.

Dansa and the road of Frescobar

Before Eye Spy, Klassik Frescobar had been noticed with Dansa, nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards in the Best Soca and Dennery Song of the Year categories, according to the same article. The article adds that Frescobar has held the top streaming soca record for over a year: that is the wording of the article, not a figure verified by this page.

For Eye Spy itself, the article gives no label, no distributor, no chart position and no stream count. This page invents none of them. The track stands on its meeting: two islands, two voices, one bouyon rhythm.
