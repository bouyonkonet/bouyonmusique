Mr Delly: the voice of How De Band Playing, between WCK and Roseau

Published October 20, 2026

Mr Delly, whose full name is Delton Alfred, is a bouyon singer from Dominica. His most fully documented record is “How De Band Playing”, a 2023 bouyon written and composed by Earlson “Likkle Man” Matthew and performed by Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred, according to the record official credits. In February 2025, Dominica tourism authority, the Discover Dominica Authority, billed him as Delly on the Bouyon Day lineup in Roseau, alongside the leading figures of the genre. This profile gathers those verified pieces, the WCK roster that lists a Delton Alfred, the 2020 Bouyon Unit record, and honestly flags what is not corroborated.

Who Mr Delly is: a full name, at last, and a short record

Unlike many voices of recent bouyon, Mr Delly has a full name published in an official credit. The page of the record “How De Band Playing” credits the performance to Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred. This profile therefore keeps the name Delton Alfred, with that precise source: the performance credit of the 2023 record. No published biography, no interview and no verified official profile of the singer was found during this research, and no streaming platform artist page under this name was confirmed. The record is short, and this profile says so up front, as it does on every page of this series.

One confusion has to be cleared here, because it waits for any reader who searches the name Alfred in bouyon. International press published a long portrait of another Alfred, Shelly Alfred, the Signal Band singer, a guitarist turned singer and writer trained in architecture. That portrait is not about Delton Alfred. The two men share a family name and work the same Dominican scene, and nothing in the sources opened allows them to be merged, or Shelly path to be transferred to Mr Delly. This profile therefore does not do it: no calypso school story, no junior monarch crown, no Signal Band album in these lines, because none of that is documented for Delton Alfred.

What connects Mr Delly to the history of the genre rests on two further pieces. First, the community site A Virtual Dominica, on its page about bouyon in Dominica, lists the current members of WCK, the founding band of bouyon, and that roster includes Delton Alfred, alongside Earlson Matthew among others. That site is an older community source, so this profile reports the roster as published there, without turning it into a verified 2026 contract. Second, the official Bouyon Day 2025 lineup bills a Delly among the icons of bouyon. Two different pieces, two different scales: a founding band roster, and a state lineup for the festival of the genre.

“How De Band Playing”: the 2023 record, full credits

The central record of the Mr Delly file is “How De Band Playing”, released as a bouyon of 2023 and presented as Mr Delly x Likkle Man. Its official description explains the saying that gives it its title: there is an old saying, “How the band, is so you have to dance.” The text spells out the meaning: many things in life are beyond our control, and sometimes it makes no sense to swim against the tide. The record applies that expression to various life situations. It is a proverb bouyon, in the Dominican tradition of records that carry street wisdom, and its author is named: written and composed by Earlson “Likkle Man” Matthew, performed by Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred.

The full credits of the record also tell a way of working. The bass is played by Elijah JeanJacques, the guitar by Joel Francis, the drums by Nikita “Fridge” Christmas, and the additional vocals bring together Erneil “Shabba” Paul, Dorelle Diolen, Serena Alexander, Syan Davis, Deanna Emmanuel and Michael “Bully / Ti Mal” John. Recording and mixing are credited to Sonic Art Studios. In other words, this 2023 bouyon is a musicians record, played by a band, not a lone producer instrumental: named bass, guitar and drums, a full backing chorus, a named studio. In a genre often reduced to its machines, that complete credit is a reminder that Dominica still makes bouyon in live formation.

The link between writer and performer gives the record its particular weight. Earlson “Likkle Man” Matthew, the writer and composer, also appears on the WCK roster published by A Virtual Dominica, alongside Delton Alfred. The 2023 record therefore brings together, if those two published sources are followed, two musicians listed in the founding band of bouyon: one writes, the other sings. The official keywords of the record tie it to bouyon, bouyon soca, Dominica and Dominica Carnival 2023. This profile does not turn that bundle into a grand claim: it simply notes that “How De Band Playing” is not an isolated record from nowhere, but a record anchored, through its credits, in the historic circle of the genre.

Bouyon Unit and “Bouyon 101”: the 2020 trace

The second verified trace of Delly is older and shorter. The reference mix by Original DJ-Sly, “D’ Riddim Box”, devoted to the best of bouyon in 2020, lists at position 73 a record titled “Bouyon 101”, credited to Bouyon Unit, a formation billed Delly x Skinny x Whizzkidd. This kind of list, published by a scene DJ in the description of his mix, is a field source: it attests that a record circulated in the bouyon selections of 2020 under that name and with those three voices. It gives neither a separate release date nor a label, and this profile invents neither.

“Bouyon 101” still deserves a reading. A record named like an introductory course, carried by a unit of three singers rather than by a soloist, says something about the way Dominican bouyon passes itself on: through crews, through answering voices, through manifesto records that sum up the genre for those who are arriving. The name Bouyon Unit itself claims that collective logic. Delly appears there as a crew member before appearing, three years later, as the lead voice of a proverb record signed by Likkle Man.

Between those two traces, 2020 and 2023, the public record delivers nothing else verified for Mr Delly: no intermediate single documented on the platforms opened, no official video found under his name alone. That silence is part of the profile of live formation singers: their work happens on stage, in band and in carnival season, where platforms keep few traces. This profile therefore prefers two solidly credited records to a catalogue rebuilt from memory.

Bouyon Day 2025: Delly on the official Roseau lineup

The most recent piece of the file is official. On 24 February 2025, the Discover Dominica Authority announced Bouyon Day 2025: on Tuesday 25 February 2025, the free event Bouyon in de Streets would take over Hanover Street, in Roseau, from 6 PM to midnight. The release presents a lineup of bouyon icons, and the name Delly appears on it, alongside Asa Bantan, Litle Boy, Quan, Reo, Freddy, Clint H, Najee, Likkle Man, Skinny Bantan, Rah, Kenny G and TK International, with Unstoppable Shax and DJ Snow. For a singer whose written file is this thin, a place on that list is a document in itself: the authority that organises the national festival of the genre counts him among the names the Roseau public comes to hear.

The release also restates the frame this site defends everywhere: bouyon was born in Dominica in the late 1980s, it combines traditional rhythms of the island such as jing ping and lapo kabwit, it absorbed cadence-lypso, soca and zouk, and the band WCK is credited there as pioneer of the sound. Bouyon Day is the state celebration of that history, in the middle of the Dominica Carnival season. That Delly is billed there in 2025, two years after the record with Likkle Man, shows an artist active on the capital stage, inside the official circuit of the genre, and not an archive name.

That lineup must be read with the same rigour as the rest of the file. It bills a Delly, with no full name, and it is the bundle of sources, the full 2023 credit in the name of Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred, the WCK roster in the name of Delton Alfred, that lets this profile attach that lineup line to Mr Delly. No recording and no review of his performance that night was found during this research, and this profile therefore does not describe a concert it has not read. It keeps the verifiable fact: the name appears on the official Bouyon Day 2025 release.

What is not corroborated, and what this profile refuses to invent

One claim circulates in a blog list of modern Dominican musicians: it presents a 1990s group named Delly and Sye, said to be formed in the 1990s, with records such as “Twaka” and “Red Devil” described as bouyon classics. During the research for this profile, no other source opened corroborated that group, those titles, or any link between them and Delton Alfred. That claim is therefore flagged here for what it is: a single, uncorroborated claim, which this profile does not repeat as fact. The titles “Twaka” and “Red Devil” do not appear in the verified discography of this page, and they do not enter its structured data.

The rest of the limits are of the same order. No date of birth, no precise home community, no musical training and no interview with Delton Alfred was found. No verified official account was confirmed, and this page links to no social profile. The WCK roster that lists Delton Alfred comes from a community site whose update date is not established: it is cited as a published roster, not as the line-up of the band at the hour these lines appear.

Until better material arrives, listening and reading start from the solid pieces: “How De Band Playing”, the 2023 proverb bouyon written by Likkle Man and sung by Delton Alfred, “Bouyon 101” by Bouyon Unit in the selections of 2020, then our WCK, bouyon soca pages and our bouyon playlist. If verifiable material reaches this site, releases, credits, official links or archives of the Delly and Sye group, this profile will be completed, dated and corrected like every page on the site. A Roseau singer billed at Bouyon Day deserves better than a copied legend: he deserves an exact file, even a short one.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Mr Delly?

Mr Delly is the stage name of Delton Alfred, a bouyon singer from Dominica. The official credit of “How De Band Playing” (2023) names the performer Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred. A published WCK roster also lists a Delton Alfred among its members, and the official Bouyon Day 2025 lineup in Roseau bills a Delly among the icons of bouyon.

What is the best documented Mr Delly record?

“How De Band Playing”, a 2023 bouyon presented as Mr Delly x Likkle Man. Its official description explains the saying “How the band, is so you have to dance” and credits the record as written and composed by Earlson “Likkle Man” Matthew and performed by Delton “Mr. Delly” Alfred, with named bass, guitar, drums and additional vocals, recorded and mixed at Sonic Art Studios.

Are Mr Delly and Shelly of Signal Band the same person?

No, and this profile exists to clear up that confusion. Shelly Alfred, the Signal Band singer portrayed by international press, is a different artist. Nothing in the sources opened allows his path, his junior calypso crowns or the Signal Band albums to be attributed to Delton “Mr Delly” Alfred, and this profile does not do so.

It is a bouyon record listed at position 73 of the “D’ Riddim Box” mix by Original DJ-Sly, devoted to the best of bouyon in 2020, and credited to Bouyon Unit, a formation billed Delly x Skinny x Whizzkidd. The mix list attests to the circulation of the record in 2020, without giving a label or a separate release date.

Did the 1990s group Delly and Sye exist?

A single blog list claims it, with titles named “Twaka” and “Red Devil”, but no other source opened during this research corroborates the group, those titles, or their link to Delton Alfred. This profile therefore flags the claim as uncorroborated and does not present it as fact.

Where can Mr Delly be seen and heard in current bouyon?

The official Discover Dominica Authority release for Bouyon Day 2025 bills Delly at Bouyon in de Streets, Hanover Street in Roseau, on 25 February 2025. For listening, start with “How De Band Playing”, then read our WCK and bouyon soca pages and hear our bouyon playlist.
