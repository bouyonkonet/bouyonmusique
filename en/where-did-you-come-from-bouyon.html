Where Did You Come From: the tender bouyon of Shelly and Signal Band

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Where Did You Come From is a bouyon track performed by Shelly and Signal Band, whose official video also features Shane St. Claire. Island Vybe presents it as bouyon that speaks to the heart as much as to the dance. This page follows the published credits of the song and the video, with no lyrics reproduced.

A bouyon presented as tender and playful

In its presentation of the track, Island Vybe describes a bouyon that is playful and tender at once, able to speak across generations without compromising its Dominican identity.

That reading matters because it shows the other side of Signal Band bouyon: next to the Carnival and fete tracks, a song built on melody and feeling, with the same care over credits.

The song credits

Per the production notes published by Island Vybe and repeated in the official video description, the song was written by Sheldon Alfred, Giovannie Green and Darren Green, and composed, produced and engineered by Dernel Green.

Background vocals are by Darren Green and Giovannie Green, live bass guitar by Giovannie Green, and graphics by Darren Green. Vocals were recorded at Studio B in Sint Maarten and The Electric Room in Dominica, with the origin stated as Dominica.

The video and Shane St. Claire

The official video, published on YouTube, presents the track as Shelly and Signal Band featuring Shane St. Claire. Its description credits direction to Dylan Lowe, shooting and editing to GloSho Multimedia, and the filming location to the InterContinental Dominica Cabrits Resort and Spa.

This page therefore separates the two layers: the song is a Shelly and Signal Band track, and the video adds the presence of Shane St. Claire and its own image credits.

Why the credits matter here

The track carries a complete, checkable chain: three named writers, a named producer and engineer, named musicians and precise recording locations, split between Sint Maarten and Dominica.

For a genre often documented through uncredited clips, that complete sheet makes Where Did You Come From an example of traceable bouyon, from the studio to the video.
