DJ Luchshiy: the producer behind Xmas Freestyle and Chabin

Published October 20, 2026

DJ Luchshiy is a bouyon producer whose published catalogue starts in 2023 on his LUCHSHIY AND BRO label. His reference records are XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN, with Lestef and Edday, released on 22 December 2023, and his credits name, published by Shazam on record after record, is Corane Vincent. Around that core, the platforms show a network producer: he produced Reo Dj luchshiy wifi freestyle in 2025, he appears on LESTEF KJF 6 Dwet, on DJ SOFTEE BOUNCE, and his records play inside bouyon mixes from the French Antilles and Dominica scene. This profile follows those credits one by one, carefully separates this artist from the namesakes the word Luchshiy pulls into search results, and states clearly where the public record stops.

Who DJ Luchshiy is: a credits producer, not a press name

DJ Luchshiy has no published interview, no press biography and no verified official page found during the research for this profile. Like many bouyon producers of his generation, his public existence rests almost entirely on his releases: dated singles, labels, tempos and credits that the platforms publish and that any reader can check. This profile therefore works from that material, the only honest kind available, and cites it record by record instead of replacing it with an invented story.

One name runs through those credits: Corane Vincent. He is a songwriter on KKM, a songwriter on XMAS FREESTYLE, a songwriter on CHABIN, and he is credited with programming and composition on 6 Dwet, where DJ Luchshiy appears as a feature. He also appears among the composers of Make it pop, by Pinpin OSP and Edday, where the name Luchshiy is also credited. The most careful reading, and the one this profile keeps, is that Corane Vincent is the published credits name behind DJ Luchshiy. It is printed here only in that capacity, with no biographical detail added.

The name itself needs a clarification. Luchshiy is a common Russian word meaning the best, and search engines mix Russian language artists with no connection to bouyon under that word. This profile keeps none of those namesakes: every fact cited here is tied either to the LUCHSHIY AND BRO label, to the Corane Vincent credits, or to the collaborations with Lestef, Edday, Reo and the artists of that scene. If a record does not pass that filter, it does not enter this profile.

The scene place used here is Guadeloupe, and this profile states what it rests on: the collaborator circle documented in the credits and listings, Lestef and his group LESTEF KJF BOYZ, Edday, Aknose, Pinpin OSP, is the Guadeloupe bouyon circle of these years, and the mixes where his records appear blend that scene with the Dominica scene. No birth town is published in our sources, so none is printed here: Guadeloupe names the scene, not a birth.

XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN: the December 2023 double record

The centre of the catalogue is an end of year project. On 22 December 2023, DJ Luchshiy released on his LUCHSHIY AND BRO label a set titled Xmas Freestyle, with Edday and LeStef. Two records from that set are documented separately by the platforms: XMAS FREESTYLE, with LESTEF and EDDAY, credited to Corane Vincent as songwriter and to Luchshiy And Bro as producer, and CHABIN, with the same guests, also released on 22 December 2023, also at 160 BPM, also written by Corane Vincent. The XMAS FREESTYLE listing showed 28,400 Shazams in the checked listing, the CHABIN listing 8,703: dated figures, cited here as a snapshot from this research, not as a ranking.

Another detail is found on another listing for the same project. The compilation Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1, presented by Int'l Stephen and released by DSM Music and Monk Music, carries Xmas Freestyle as a single track of 28 minutes and 54 seconds, with Edday and LeStef featured, Corane Vincent as writer and Luchshiy And Bro as producer. In other words, the Christmas freestyle is not an ordinary single: it is a long form piece, almost a session, which then travelled both as separate platform tracks and as one block inside a carnival compilation from the soca and bouyon scene.

That double life says a lot about the job. The same recording can be a streaming single, a DJ tool inside a compilation, and a mix moment inside scene podcasts. The mixtape tracklists checked during this research show exactly that: Chabin returns in the form Chabin Black Toy Raw, followed by a track called Mons, in a bouyon podcast selection, alongside Signal Band, Trilla-G and 1T1. DJ Luchshiy records therefore also live inside the raw and mix economy, where bouyon travels fastest.

For listening, start with CHABIN and XMAS FREESTYLE in their platform versions, then look for the long compilation version if you want to hear the session format. Our bouyon playlist places these records inside the flow of bouyon releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz.

KKM and the documented beginnings: April 2023

The first dated record in the checked catalogue is KKM, with AKNOSE, released on 24 April 2023 on LUCHSHIY AND BRO, in English according to the published listing, at 160 BPM, with writing credits for Corane Vincent and Gael Rampath. Eight months before the Christmas project, the formula is already there: a label carrying the producer name, a guest voice, the cruising tempo of modern bouyon, and the same writer name that returns everywhere afterwards.

The choice of feature is not neutral. Aknose is one of the rising voices of the Guadeloupe bouyon scene of these years, and opening a published catalogue with him means entering, from the start, the network that matters for this audience. What follows confirms that reading: almost every DJ Luchshiy record is a meeting record, with Lestef, Edday, Reo, Jixel or DJ SOFTEE, and almost none is a solo record. The producer builds his place by connecting the voices of his scene to his riddims.

On 2 November 2023, another piece of the puzzle appears under a different headline name: Make it pop, by Pinpin OSP and Edday, released on VGM PRODUCT, credited in writing and composition to Edday, Brian Lomba, Corane Vincent and Luchshiy. That double credit, Luchshiy and Corane Vincent listed separately on the same record, is one of the items that link the stage name to the credits name: the platforms themselves treat the two names as two entries for the same production and writing work.

By the end of 2023, the picture is therefore already complete: a first single in April, a writing credit in November, then the Xmas Freestyle project in December that gives the producer his reference records. It is a fast start, run entirely through a network, and documented entirely by the platforms for lack of any press coverage.

2024 and 2025: BOUNCE, 6 Dwet and the Reo wifi freestyle

In 2024, the DJ Luchshiy name appears on carnival compilation territory. The Beatport releases page linked to his name places him in the artist field of the season compilation, alongside the soca and bouyon names of Dominica and the Caribbean, from Skinny Fabulous to Signal Band and Trilla-G. The same year, on 29 November 2024, came BOUNCE, by DJ SOFTEE with DJ Luchshiy featured, on the DJ SOFTEE label, at 160 BPM, with a writing credit for Julian White. A DJ to DJ record, in the most direct format of the genre: one word, one tempo, one function, make the floor bounce.

2025 chains two major collaborations within one week. On 15 February 2025 came 6 Dwet, by LESTEF KJF and Totally Spice's with DJ Luchshiy featured, on the Dreamtracks label, in French, at 160 BPM. The published credits are precise: Naima Dimina and Carcheline Cereme on lyrics, Corane Vincent on programming and composition with Stephane Cornano, Yoan Courcet on arrangement and production. The DJ Luchshiy credits name is therefore, on this record, inside the machine itself, on programming, while his stage name is on the cover.

On 22 February 2025, Reo released Dj luchshiy wifi freestyle, with Edday, Jixel and Dj luchshiy, on Reo music Ent., in English according to the listing. The credits give DJ LUCHSHIY as producer, Edday and Dario Sebastien Yankey as songwriters, and Jixel on piano among the performing artists. The song title carries the producer name, and the headline artist is Reo, a major figure of Dominican bouyon: it is the reverse pattern of the usual single, where the producer becomes the frame and the singer becomes the guest of his freestyle format. Scene mixtape tracklists add another shared record, You Mad, credited to Reo, DJ Luchshiy, Jixel and Edday in the Sewo Jam selection.

The 2025 path continues in the LESTEF KJF BOYZ catalogue, whose artist page on the platforms lists NO COME BACK (WHATCHA SAY), with DJ Luchshiy, as a 2025 single, then TAKE UR TIME, with DJ Luchshiy, as a 2026 single. No Come Back also plays in bouyon podcasts, credited to Lestef and Dj Luchshiy. The collaboration born on the December 2023 Christmas project had therefore become, two years later, a lasting axis of both catalogues.

The Luchshiy network: Lestef, Edday, Reo and the riddim question

Reading the DJ Luchshiy catalogue means reading a map. At the centre, a duo of voices returns constantly: Lestef, singer of LESTEF KJF BOYZ, and Edday. They are on XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN in 2023, on the Reo wifi freestyle in 2025, and Lestef extends the link with NO COME BACK and TAKE UR TIME. Around them orbit Aknose, Pinpin OSP, Riddla, whose record Mi Boss LA, on the Summer Of Carnivals compilation, credits DJ Luchshiy as a feature with Corane Vincent as a writer, and the producers DJ SOFTEE and Yoan Courcet.

That network working is the normal form of current bouyon, and it has to be understood to understand the place of a producer like DJ Luchshiy. Bouyon was born in Dominica, it grew in Guadeloupe and Martinique as party and carnival music, and its producer branch, the branch of riddims and freestyles, moves through short repeated collaborations rather than albums. A producer earns his reputation there by supplying the frame on which voices from several scenes, here Guadeloupe and Dominica, can meet. The Reo wifi freestyle, produced by DJ Luchshiy, is exactly that kind of meeting. For the full context, read our history of bouyon page and our bouyon soca page.

Tempo tells the same belonging story. KKM, CHABIN, BOUNCE and 6 Dwet are all listed at 160 BPM, the signature tempo of modern bouyon, the one that lets DJs chain these records with the whole current repertoire inside mixes. The tracklists checked for this profile, Sewo Jam, the Blaqrose Supreme and DJ Close Connections podcasts, show his records in exactly that use: mid mix, between records by Signal Band, Trilla-G, Reo and Miimii KDS.

Finally, note what this network does not show: no lasting outside label structure, no credited management, no documented tour. LUCHSHIY AND BRO released the early records, then the collaborations pass through the labels of others, Dreamtracks, Reo music Ent. or DJ SOFTEE. It is the complete independent model, with its strength, speed, and its weakness, the near total absence of written archives.

What this profile knows, and what it does not know

The verified facts of this profile fit in a few lines. DJ Luchshiy has released music since April 2023, first on his LUCHSHIY AND BRO label. His published credits name is Corane Vincent, writer, composer or programmer on the majority of the checked records. His reference records are XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN, with Lestef and Edday, released on 22 December 2023. He produced Reo Dj luchshiy wifi freestyle in February 2025, he appears on 6 Dwet, BOUNCE, NO COME BACK and TAKE UR TIME, and at least twelve verified records carry his artist name, his credits name or his production.

What the file does not hold is just as clear. No official biography, no interview, no birth date or town and no verified official account were found, and the Russian language namesakes the word Luchshiy attracts were systematically excluded from this research. This profile therefore links no social account in its structured data, and it prints Corane Vincent only as a credits name, never as an identity completed with invented details.

If the producer publishes an official page, gives an interview or has his profiles verified, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on this site. In the meantime, the recommended listening follows the chronology: KKM for the beginnings, CHABIN and XMAS FREESTYLE for the reference project, the long Summer Of Carnivals compilation version for the session format, 6 Dwet and the Reo wifi freestyle for the 2025 collaborations. Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Luchshiy?

DJ Luchshiy is a bouyon producer active since 2023 on his LUCHSHIY AND BRO label. His credits name, published by Shazam on most of his records, is Corane Vincent. His main collaborations are Lestef, Edday and Reo, and his reference records are XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN, released on 22 December 2023.

What are DJ Luchshiy best known records?

XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN, with Lestef and Edday, released on 22 December 2023 on LUCHSHIY AND BRO. In the listings checked during this research, the XMAS FREESTYLE listing showed 28,400 Shazams and the CHABIN listing 8,703. The catalogue also includes KKM with AKNOSE, BOUNCE with DJ SOFTEE and 6 Dwet with LESTEF KJF.

Why does Xmas Freestyle run 28 minutes on one compilation?

Because the Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1 compilation, released by DSM Music and Monk Music, carries Xmas Freestyle as a single track of 28 minutes and 54 seconds, with Edday and LeStef featured and Luchshiy And Bro as producer. On the platforms, the same December 2023 project travels as separate tracks, including XMAS FREESTYLE and CHABIN. The project therefore has two forms: a long session for compilations and DJs, and singles for streaming.

What is the link between DJ Luchshiy and Reo?

DJ Luchshiy produced Dj luchshiy wifi freestyle, released by Reo on 22 February 2025 on Reo music Ent., with Edday, Jixel and Dj luchshiy. The credits give DJ LUCHSHIY as producer and Jixel on piano. Scene mixtape tracklists also credit a shared record, You Mad, to Reo, DJ Luchshiy, Jixel and Edday.

Is DJ Luchshiy a Russian artist?

No, not the one in this profile. Luchshiy is a common Russian word meaning the best, and search engines mix in Russian language artists with no connection to bouyon. The DJ Luchshiy in this profile is the one from the LUCHSHIY AND BRO label, the Corane Vincent credits and the collaborations with Lestef, Edday and Reo. No fact from the namesakes is used here.

Where can I hear DJ Luchshiy?
