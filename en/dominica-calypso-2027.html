Dominica calypso 2027: Trilla-G among

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Twelve calypsonians survived the elimination round and move on to the quarter-finals of Dominica calypso, on 2 January 2027. Former Calypso Monarch Trilla-G is one of the twelve qualifiers, according to Dominica 1, reporting the Dominica News Online report. The other eleven names have not yet been published in the opened sources, and this article does not invent them.

Twelve through to the quarters

Twelve calypsonians survived an elimination round and moved on to the quarter-finals, according to a Dominica News Online report carried by Dominica 1 on 8 November 2026. The quarter-finals are scheduled for 2 January 2027. Dominica 1 confirmed the next day, 9 November, that former Calypso Monarch Trilla-G is one of the twelve chosen.

The venue for the quarters is given in no opened source, and this article does not invent one. No opened source says the quarters are part of Mas Domnik 2027 either. The 2027 calypso season is under way, and the full list of the twelve qualifiers is still to be published.

Trilla-G, the only confirmed name

Trilla-G, born Gael Jno Baptiste in Goodwill, just outside Roseau, is the only qualifier named in the opened sources so far. He was Dominica's Calypso King in 2023, and he said it himself in his Billboard interview: calypso is national, and he won. He says calypso taught him how to perform properly on stage, manage his diction, polish his songwriting and flex his wit and humour.

Calypso therefore shaped the bouyon artist Trilla-G became. His track Someone Else, released in summer 2024 with Quan and Litleboy, won soca collaboration of the year at the 2025 Caribbean Music Awards, and his debut album Take Me as I Am was named Billboard's No. 4 Best Caribbean Album of 2025. The other eleven qualifiers will be added when a press source names them.

The 2027 season under way

The 2027 eliminations are already visible online: a compilation video of the Dominica Calypso Eliminations 2027 circulates on YouTube, with individual clips, including Shanice's Say Her Name. The song titles are known only from the titles of those videos, not from a press article, and this article does not present them as confirmed entries.

Trilla-G reaches the quarters on a strong run. His project Up Next Cypher, Vol. 1, released on 2 October 2026 through Still Trill Entertainment, reached the No. 1 spot on Apple Music's Top Songs chart in Dominica, with 83,000 YouTube views in one week, and a Volume 2 is confirmed. The rest of the season, the quarters on 2 January, then the semi-finals and the final, will be followed here when Dominican press publishes the results.
