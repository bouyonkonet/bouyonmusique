Bouyon music 2026: WCMF night 1 guide, Friday October 23

By Smokiller · Published October 21, 2026

The first night of the World Creole Music Festival 2026 takes place on Friday October 23 at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, Dominica. It is the opening night of the festival's 26th edition, in the heart of Independence season. For bouyon and cadence, night 1 sets the scene: Dominican institutions TK International and Midnight Groovers share the bill with Capleton's reggae, Skillibeng's dancehall and Klass's kompa.

The night 1 programme: a reggae, dancehall and kompa opening

According to the official Discover Dominica Authority release of August 19, 2026 and the published festival guides, Friday October 23 brings together Capleton, the Jamaican reggae veteran, Skillibeng, the Jamaican dancehall star, and Klass, the Haitian kompa band. Dominica News, in its article on the festival preparations, describes the same opening: Capleton and Skillibeng as the international heads, with TK International and Midnight Groovers as the local pillars of the night.

Station509, in its October 7, 2026 article on the Haitian presence at the festival, confirms the same start to the weekend: Klass opens the Haitian contingent on Friday October 23, on the same night as Capleton and the Midnight Groovers. Three opened sources therefore tell the same night 1 story, which makes this programme the verified base of this guide.

TK International and Midnight Groovers: Dominica opens at home

For a bouyon reader, the heart of night 1 is Dominican. TK International, the Triple Kay band, and the Midnight Groovers, guardians of cadence-lypso, play at home, in front of the Windsor Park crowd. The festival was born to put Dominica's Creole music on the same stage as guests from the region, and opening night keeps that role: before the global headliners of the following nights, Roseau first hears its own bands.

Published festival guides add other names this site follows to the Friday night: Ridge, Pudaz and Colton T for Dominica, and Maurane Voyer, the singer from Martinique, among the Creole guests. This site's World Creole Music Festival page, built on the official communication, also places TK International, the Midnight Groovers, Ridge and Maurane Voyer in night 1. The exact running order is not published in the sources opened for this guide, and it is not invented here.

Why night 1 matters for bouyon

Bouyon was born in Dominica in the late 1980s, and WCMF remains its biggest home showcase. An opening night where the Midnight Groovers' cadence-lypso and TK International's energy come before the reggae and dancehall guests recalls the full chain of the Dominican sound: from the cadence roots to today's bouyon. The new generation of bouyon artists, like Reo and Trilla-G, are expected on the following nights, but night 1 shows where the sound comes from.

Across its three nights, the festival covers R and B, soca, dancehall, reggae, bouyon, cadence-lypso, kompa, soukous and amapiano, according to the official release. Night 1 concentrates the reggae, dancehall and kompa part of that spectrum, with the Dominican Creole base opening the ball.

Planning night 1: what this guide knows and does not know

This guide keeps only facts confirmed by several opened sources: the date, Friday October 23, 2026, the venue, Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, the festival's 26th edition and the artists named above. Artist by artist set times, per night ticket prices and travel times are not repeated here, because the opened sources do not confirm them in agreement.

Frequently asked questions

When is WCMF 2026 night 1?

On Friday October 23, 2026, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, Dominica. It is the opening night of the 26th edition of the World Creole Music Festival.

Which artists play WCMF 2026 night 1?

Opened sources agree on Capleton, Skillibeng, Klass, TK International and Midnight Groovers. Published guides add Ridge, Pudaz, Colton T and Maurane Voyer for the Friday.

Is WCMF night 1 a bouyon night?

In part: the base of the night is Dominican, with TK International and the Midnight Groovers, while the international heads of the night are Capleton's reggae, Skillibeng's dancehall and Klass's kompa.
