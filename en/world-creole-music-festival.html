World Creole Music Festival: Dominica’s Creole showcase

By Smokiller · Published September 5, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

The World Creole Music Festival, the WCMF, is Dominica’s great musical gathering: three nights of Creole music in Roseau, every year in October, at the time of the island’s independence celebrations.

Three nights, all the Creole musics

The WCMF brings the musics of the Creole world together on one stage: bouyon and cadence from Dominica, zouk from the French Antilles, kompa from Haiti and guests from the diaspora. For bouyon, it is the biggest international showcase at home.

Bouyon at home

Playing at the WCMF, for a bouyon artist, means playing at home in front of the audience that saw the genre be born. Bouyon Shattaz covers the festival as a strategic moment: that is where the visibility of Dominican artists and the meetings with partners from the Antilles and France are played out. 2026 edition: from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25 at Windsor Park, Roseau.

Frequently asked questions

What is the World Creole Music Festival?

The World Creole Music Festival, or WCMF, is Dominica’s great musical gathering: three nights of Creole music in Roseau, every year in October. It brings together on one stage Dominica’s bouyon and cadence, zouk from the French Antilles, kompa from Haiti and guests from the diaspora.

Where does the World Creole Music Festival take place?

In Roseau, the capital of Dominica, at the Windsor Park stadium. The festival runs there every year for three consecutive nights during the independence celebrations, October being Dominica’s independence month, which makes it a national celebration as much as a festival.

When does WCMF 2026 take place?

The 2026 edition is scheduled from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25, 2026 at Windsor Park, Roseau. On the bouyon side, Night 1 features TK International, Ridge and Midnight Groovers among others, Night 2 Reo and Asa Bantan, and Night 3 Shelly with Signal Band, per official communication and published guides.

Why does WCMF matter for bouyon?

Because it is bouyon’s biggest international showcase at home: artists play in front of the audience that saw the genre be born. Bouyon Shattaz covers the festival as a strategic moment, where the visibility of Dominican artists and the meetings with partners from the Antilles and France are played out.

How long has the World Creole Music Festival existed?

Since 1997. The festival was created that year at Windsor Park in Roseau, and it is nicknamed the festival that never sleeps. It celebrated its twenty-fifth edition from October 24 to 26, 2025, under the theme of the global echoes of the Nature Island, with WCK and First Serenade, pioneer bouyon bands, on the bill. See WCMF in context.

What is the theme of WCMF 2026?

“Pulsating Rhythms”, according to the published festival guides, notably writeups24. The 2026 edition runs at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, from October 23 to 25, during Independence season, dates and venue confirmed by the Sweet TNT Magazine complete guide. Bouyon Shattaz followed the festival with a countdown devoted to five Dominican acts, TK International among them.

Who plays Night 1 of WCMF 2026?

According to the published guides, notably writeups24, Night 1 on Friday October 23, 2026 brings together Capleton, Skillibeng, Klass, Maurane Voyer, TK International, Midnight Groovers, Pudaz, Ridge, DJ MJ and MV on the Keys, Colton T and Ezra D' Fun Machine. For bouyon, it is the night of the historic bands and the solo generation, with a singer from Martinique. See DJ MJ.

Who plays Night 2 of WCMF 2026?

According to the published guides, notably writeups24 and Sweet TNT, Night 2 on Saturday October 24, 2026 brings together Ne-Yo, Alkaline, Tabou Combo, Asa Bantan, V'ghn, Trilla G, Full Blown, Extasy Band, Reo, Edday, DYP, Imran Nerdy, Sedale, Shemmy J, Tally Boy, Hollywood HP and Andrew. On the Dominican bouyon side, Asa Bantan, Trilla G and Reo share the same night. See Asa Bantan.

Who plays the closing night of WCMF 2026?

According to the published guides, Night 3 on Sunday October 25, 2026 brings together Machel Montano, Uncle Waffles, Fridayy, Maxi Priest, Diblo Dibala, Les Aiglons, Jean Marc Ferdinand, Shelly and The Signal Band, Gwada G, Unstoppable Shax, Nice and DJ Taffy. Dominican bouyon closes the festival there alongside Trinidad soca. See DJ Taffy.

Which bouyon band has never missed WCMF?

TK International, also known as Triple Kay. According to the band's official site, it has played the World Creole Music Festival since its debut on that stage in 2002 and has not missed an edition since 2005. Soca News confirms the band are crowd favourites at WCMF. In 2026, it plays Night 1, on Friday October 23. See TK International.

Who played WCMF 2025?

For the twenty-fifth edition, the official programme announced on July 4, 2025 placed on the second night, Saturday October 25, Vybz Kartel, Spice, Asa Bantan, Gordon Henderson, Joe Dwet File, Kenny G, WCK, First Serenade, Ophelia, Pudaz, Quan, Ridge, Shanika, Ebony Empress, DJ MJ, Kes The Band and Faithii. See the Faithii profile.

What is Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl?

It is the Creole village of the Dominican independence season, set in the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, with days of food, culture and music, from noon to 8 PM. The 2026 edition, the fifth, runs from Monday 19 to Thursday 22 October, a few days before WCMF. Its programme mixes cultural groups, schools, historic bands such as Midnight Groovers and Swinging Stars, and the current bouyon scene.
