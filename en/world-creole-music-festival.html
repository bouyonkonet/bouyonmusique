World Creole Music Festival: Dominica’s Creole showcase

Published September 5, 2026

The World Creole Music Festival, the WCMF, is Dominica’s great musical gathering: three nights of Creole music in Roseau, every year in October, at the time of the island’s independence celebrations.

Three nights, all the Creole musics

The WCMF brings the musics of the Creole world together on one stage: bouyon and cadence from Dominica, zouk from the French Antilles, kompa from Haiti and guests from the diaspora. For bouyon, it is the biggest international showcase at home.

Bouyon at home

Playing at the WCMF, for a bouyon artist, means playing at home in front of the audience that saw the genre be born. Bouyon Shattaz covers the festival as a strategic moment: that is where the visibility of Dominican artists and the meetings with partners from the Antilles and France are played out. 2026 edition: from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25 at Windsor Park, Roseau.

Frequently asked questions

What is the World Creole Music Festival?

The World Creole Music Festival, or WCMF, is Dominica’s great musical gathering: three nights of Creole music in Roseau, every year in October. It brings together on one stage Dominica’s bouyon and cadence, zouk from the French Antilles, kompa from Haiti and guests from the diaspora.

Where does the World Creole Music Festival take place?

In Roseau, the capital of Dominica, at the Windsor Park stadium. The festival runs there for three consecutive nights during the independence celebrations, October being Dominica’s independence month, which makes it a national celebration as much as a festival.

When does WCMF 2026 take place?

The 2026 edition is scheduled from Friday, October 23 to Sunday, October 25, 2026 at Windsor Park, Roseau. On the bouyon side, Night 1 features TK International, Ridge and Midnight Groovers among others, Night 2 Reo and Asa Bantan, and Night 3 Shelly with Signal Band, per official communication and published guides.

Why does WCMF matter for bouyon?

Because it is bouyon’s biggest international showcase at home: artists play in front of the audience that saw the genre be born.
