Dominica Independence 2026: the 48th, from 19 September to 4 November

By Smokiller · Published November 4, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

On 3 November 2026 Dominica marks 48 years of independence, gained from the United Kingdom on 3 November 1978. The official season programme, announced by Culture Minister Greta Roberts and published by the Office of the Prime Minister, runs from 19 September to 4 November 2026 under the theme Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future.

A theme in three beats

The official theme, Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future, is presented by Minister Greta Roberts as the full story of the nation: reflect on the journey so far, preserve the legacy handed down and build a stronger future for the generations to follow.

The season opened on 19 and 20 September with the National Days of Prayer and Thanksgiving, then the Official Opening Ceremony on 25 September and the walk-through parade on 26 September at the Botanic Gardens, per the Office of the Prime Minister programme.

October, Patriotism and Heritage Month

October is observed as Patriotism and Heritage Month. The official programme places Heritage Day in Paix Bouche on 18 October, Ti Vilaj Kweyol from 19 to 22 October at the Botanic Gardens, the Miss Wob Dwiyet pageant on 21 October and the Madam Wob Dwiyet pageant on 28 October at Massacre, and the World Creole Music Festival from 23 to 25 October at Windsor Park.

That is the frame in which bouyon holds its place as a season sound: the Creole festival is its musical showcase, between cadence-lypso, kompa, zouk and Dominican bouyon.

The final week, from Youth Rally to National Day

The home stretch starts on Thursday 29 October with the National Youth Rally at Windsor Park, followed by Jounen Kweyol and the National Dress Parade on Friday 30 October, then the Cultural Extravaganza and Eat Fish Day on Saturday 31 October, per the official programme.

The National Cultural Gala is held at the Botanic Gardens on Sunday 1 November. On Tuesday 3 November, the National Day Ceremony at Windsor Park includes, per Dominica News, a military parade, the Prime Minister National Address and an awards ceremony honouring citizens for their work and dedication.

On 4 November, the season closes by working

The celebrations conclude on Wednesday 4 November with the National Day of Community Service. The official programme combines it this year with community beautification and practical infrastructure projects across the country.

Dominica News describes the day as citizens painting, planting, organising and decorating public infrastructure. The Minister invited Dominicans at home and abroad to display the flag, wear national and cultural attire and support local artistes, musicians, farmers, vendors and small businesses throughout the season.

Frequently asked questions

When does Dominica celebrate Independence in 2026?

On Tuesday 3 November 2026, for the 48th time: independence from the United Kingdom dates to 3 November 1978. The National Day Ceremony is held at Windsor Park in Roseau.

What is the theme of the 2026 Independence season?

Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future, announced by Culture Minister Greta Roberts in the official programme published by the Office of the Prime Minister.

What are the last dates of the 2026 programme?

National Youth Rally on 29 October, Jounen Kweyol on 30 October, Cultural Extravaganza and Eat Fish Day on 31 October, National Cultural Gala on 1 November, National Day on 3 November and the National Day of Community Service on 4 November.
