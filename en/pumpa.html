Pumpa: Virgin Islands soca meets bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Pumpa is a soca singer from the US Virgin Islands, known well beyond his island since “The A List” in 2022. In July 2025 he cut “Savage” on the Kryptic Riddim, a bouyon riddim produced in Dominica by Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence, who lists him among its regional headliners. In April 2026 a bouyon take on “The A List” also surfaced on the WYFL Warm Up Riddim.

A Virgin Islands soca name

Pumpa's catalogue goes back a while. In October 2014 JulianspromosTV was already carrying “VI Up”, billed “2015 Virgin Islands Soca Music”, with the island right there in the title. The real turning point is “The A List”: the single reached Deezer on November 17, 2022, the official audio has passed 1.9 million views, and the video, posted in September 2023 on his PUMPA MUSIC channel, is close to 2.9 million. The production credit goes to the handle @thedjavalanche, and the mix, a telling detail for what came next, is credited to Dadamanufakture.

In February 2025 Pumpa shared “Her Mudda” with Machel Montano, on the Her Mudda Riddim produced by Stadic and Jonny Blaze for the Monk Music label. The video, on Machel's channel, was past 630,000 views when read. That is Trinidad soca, not bouyon, but it shows how much exposure he brought when he stepped onto a Dominican riddim.

Savage on the Kryptic Riddim (2025)

On July 4, 2025, the dadamanufakture channel put the whole Kryptic Riddim online in one batch, “Pumpa - SAVAGE” included. The press text files the project under bouyon and names Pumpa, tagged USVI, first among the voices from other islands, next to Fadda Fox, Sackie and Zan, and Shanna Raymond. On the Dominican side you find Faithii, Pudaz and Zwady.

The song is written by Denis Liburd and produced, mixed and mastered by Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence. On the Deezer album of July 6, 2025, “Savage” is the longest cut on the riddim at 2:49. The YouTube video had a little over 4,500 views when read, far behind “Shake Something”, the riddim's big hit.

The A List goes bouyon, and the 2026 releases

On April 13, 2026, the DJ RyanFlossy channel posted “Pumpa - The A List (WYFL Warm Up Riddim) (Bouyon 2026)”, produced by XavvoKnockin, tagged remix and bouyonriddim. It is a reworking of the hit over a bouyon riddim, still under the radar with just over 600 views when read.

The rest of his 2026 stays on the soca side. “Walk & Wine”, on 1st Klase Records' Happy Life Riddim, came out on April 3 with Dennis Liburd credited for the lyrics, and “3pc Combo”, which compares a woman to a KFC three-piece meal, followed in September. One caution on Deezer: the artist page under the name Pumpa mixes several namesakes, an Italian children's album among them, so its counts are not used here.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Pumpa from?

The US Virgin Islands: the Kryptic Riddim press text introduces him as “Pumpa (USVI)”, and his 2014 and 2015 songs are billed Virgin Islands soca.

What is his bouyon song?

“Savage”, on Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence's Kryptic Riddim, uploaded July 4, 2025 and released on Deezer on July 6. A bouyon version of “The A List” also exists on the WYFL Warm Up Riddim (April 2026).

What is his biggest hit?

“The A List” (November 2022): over 1.9 million views for the audio on JulianspromosTV and nearly 2.9 million for the video on his own channel.

Has he sung with Machel Montano?

Yes, on “Her Mudda” (February 2025), produced by Stadic and Jonny Blaze for Monk Music, a soca song.

Who wrote Savage?

The YouTube page credits the writing to Denis Liburd and the production, mixing and mastering to Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence.
