Shawn Storm: one bouyon remix with Ebony Empress

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Shawn Storm is a singjay whom the Ebony Empress release page presents as a Jamaican dancehall star, and whose label Hapilos gives his civil name, Shawn Campbell. His verified link with bouyon rests on one track: the remix of “Kokayoko” by Ebony Empress, released in July 2026 and presented by the Dominican singer as a blend of bouyon and dancehall. This profile is deliberately short, in proportion to that record.

Who Shawn Storm is

The Hapilos artist page sums up a Jamaican dancehall path: introduced to the business in 2007 by Vybz Kartel, Shawn Campbell, known as Shawn Storm, released mixtapes from 2009, had a first hit in 2008 with “Ghetto Yutes Be Wise”, then broke through with “My Life”, a 2010 track that reached, according to that page, number one in Jamaica in January 2011 and held it for five weeks.

No source opened for this profile gives his place or date of birth. The Jamaican dancehall star description is that of the Ebony Empress release page, and this profile cites it as such.

Kokayoko (Remix) with Ebony Empress, July 2026

The “Kokayoko” remix came out on Deezer on 17 July 2026, on the Ebony Empress label, crediting Ebony Empress and Shawn Storm, with a running time of 3 minutes 23. The video published on 29 July 2026 on the channel the two artists share describes the track as a blend of bouyon and dancehall, and carries the Bouyon and Dancehall hashtags.

The credits are clear. The writing goes to Everlyne Leandra Walters, a name matching the birth name documented on our Ebony Empress profile, and to Shawn Campbell, the name Hapilos gives for Shawn Storm: he writes his own part. Production is credited to ThawizZard, the published spelling of the producer this site profiles as ThaWizZarD. Recording is credited to Xavvo2knockin and Kwashwana Records, mixing and mastering to Xavvo and Felix Studio; this profile reproduces those spellings without merging them with other names.

One detail of the title deserves a plain statement: the video carries the Bouyon 2027 label although the remix came out in July 2026. It is the season label chosen by the channel, looking ahead to the next carnival, not a release date.

The limits of the record

The “Kokayoko” remix is the only verified official release tying Shawn Storm to bouyon. A video on the LRB MUSIC channel, published in September 2025 under the title of a bouyon remix of “Shot e rum”, declares itself for promotional use only: it is not a release by the artist, and this profile does not count it.

To listen, start with the “Kokayoko” remix, then the original and the rest of the Ebony Empress catalogue. Our dancehall and bouyon page tells what connects the two genres.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shawn Storm?

Shawn Storm is a Jamaican singjay, Shawn Campbell by name according to the Hapilos label, known for “My Life”, number one in Jamaica in January 2011 according to the same page. In bouyon, he is the guest of Ebony Empress on the “Kokayoko” remix, released in July 2026.

Why is the “Kokayoko” remix marked Bouyon 2027?

That is the label written into the video title by the channel, while the remix came out on 17 July 2026 on Deezer and the video dates from 29 July 2026. This profile reads it as a season label, not a date.

Who produced the “Kokayoko” remix?

The official video credits production to ThawizZard, recording to Xavvo2knockin and Kwashwana Records, and mixing and mastering to Xavvo and Felix Studio. The writing is signed Everlyne Leandra Walters and Shawn Campbell.
