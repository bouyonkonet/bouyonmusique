Hxnter: bouyon riddim producer (2025-2027)

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Hxnter, credited as Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt on a Big Red Productions page, is a producer whose channel posts bouyon and nothing else. His YouTube channel lines up 67 instrumentals, all billed bouyon, split between original riddims and remixes of pop hits. At least three acts have recorded on his productions: Sully Jacob, ShaSha with Big Red HD, and very probably Vibwoy.

A riddim channel, season after season

The Hxnter channel (@HxnterBeatz) works like a catalogue. Every title opens with the season, “Bouyon 2025”, “Bouyon 2026” or already “Bouyon 2027”, then the riddim's name: Caution, Uncharted, Emerald, Pressure, Destiny, Waveline, Tough, Punch. When read, it held 30 instrumentals for 2025, 27 for 2026 and 5 for 2027, and the riddims are sold through a link in each description.

The most viewed of those we checked, the Caution Riddim posted on October 20, 2025, was past 4,800 views; Hxnter shares the credit with a second producer, @joshii748. The Uncharted Riddim of January 16, 2026 lists his contacts, an Instagram account hxnter.pb and an hxnterbeatz email address. Alongside the riddims, a dozen remixes turn pop hits into bouyon, from Soulja Boy's “Crank That” to Ariana Grande's “Bloodline”, plus a few live keys sessions.

Productions for other voices

On June 21, 2025, Sully Jacob posted “Où est mon argent? (Bouyon 2025) #RUNDAT” with the line “Prod : @hxnter.pb”, the same handle as on Hxnter's channel. The mix is by @dernz.wav, and Sully Jacob handled the recording and the cover art himself.

The fullest credit is on “Put Something On Me (Bouyon 2026)”, posted on May 21, 2026 by Big Red Productions: the page reads “Produced by Reyshaun "Hxnter" Hunt” for this duet between ShaSha and Big Red HD, mixed by Judah “Diyoute” Jno. Baptiste. With more than 10,800 views when read, it is the most watched of the pages that name him.

Hxnter, hunter, Hunter: who is who

On Vibwoy's “Mommy (Bouyon 2026)”, posted on November 5, 2025, the description only says “Prod by hunter”. The Hxnter channel itself uses “hunter bouyon beats” among its keywords, which makes the match likely, but the Vibwoy page gives no handle and no full name.

He should not be mixed up with Hunter, a soca singer whose channel carries “Warn You” with Blaka Dan, produced by Litleboy, and “Feter” with Denise Belfon. No page opened for this profile names Hxnter's home island; the word “dominica” shows up only in one riddim's keywords. Profile drawn from the YouTube pages opened on October 29, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Hxnter?

A bouyon riddim producer, credited as Reyshaun “Hxnter” Hunt on Put Something On Me; his YouTube channel holds 67 bouyon instrumentals.

What is his most viewed riddim?

Among those checked, the Caution Riddim (Bouyon 2025), posted on October 20, 2025 and co-produced with @joshii748, past 4,800 views when read.

Which artists has he produced for?

Sully Jacob (Où est mon argent?, 2025) and ShaSha with Big Red HD (Put Something On Me, 2026); Vibwoy's Mommy is credited to “hunter”.

Are Hxnter and the singer Hunter the same person?

No. The Hunter on Warn You, with Blaka Dan, is a soca singer, and that song was produced by Litleboy.

Where is Hxnter from?

No opened page says: neither his channel nor the credits on his productions name an island.
