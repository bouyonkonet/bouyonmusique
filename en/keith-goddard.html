Keith Goddard: WCK bassist, 2026 bouyon lifetime award

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Keith Goddard is the bassist of WCK, the Dominican band tied to the birth of bouyon. His name runs through the band's credits from 2006 to 2025, and on January 3, 2026 the Dominica Music Awards gave him the lifetime achievement award for bouyon.

An award for bouyon's bass

On January 3, 2026, at the Dominica Music Awards run by Creole Heartbeat at the InterContinental in Portsmouth, seven lifetime achievement awards were given in the name of the Office of the Prime Minister. Soca News lists the line “Keith Goddard : Bouyon”. DBS Radio puts it as bass guitarist Keith Goddard, honoured for bouyon music.

He was the only person honoured that night for bouyon, next to Nasio Fontaine for reggae, Ian Jackson for calypso and Fitzroy Williams for cadence-lypso. The same night, Signal Band and Shelly took most of the year's awards.

In WCK, from Calling to 2020

In 2024, when WIC News covered the first bouyon category at the Caribbean Music Awards, the article traced the genre to WCK, with Derek “Rah” Peters on vocals, Cornell Phillip on keys and Keith Goddard on bass. The credits of “All Die Hards”, from the 2006 album Calling, put him on bass, with Earlson Matthew on keyboards.

He also plays on the band members' solo work. In February 2020 he played bass on two Clint H songs, “Bouyon To The World (Remix)” and the “Clint H x WCK Ultimate Road Mix 2020 Bouyon”, with Monelle Mondesire on guitar.

Sound of De Nature Isle, 2025

On October 3, 2025, WCK released “Sound of De Nature Isle”, presented as the band going back to its bouyon roots. It was composed by Irvine “Smokey” Phillip and sung by Cornell Phillip and Elisha Benoit. Keith Goddard is on bass, with Martindale Olive and Monelle Mondesire on guitars.

The credits add a jing ping band, accordion, David Benjamin on conch shell and gwaj, and bèlè and djembé drums. Under all of it, the bass line is still his, close to twenty years after Calling. For context, see our Dominica Carnival page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Keith Goddard?

The bassist of WCK, the Dominican band tied to the birth of bouyon.

What award did he receive?

The lifetime achievement award for bouyon at the Dominica Music Awards on January 3, 2026.

Which songs does he play on?

WCK's All Die Hards (2006), two Clint H songs in 2020 and WCK's Sound of De Nature Isle in 2025.

Does he sing?

The credits we checked always list him on bass, never on vocals.

Who does he play with in WCK?

The credits name Derek Rah Peters, Cornell Phillip, Earlson Matthew, Monelle Mondesire and Elisha Benoit, depending on the period.
