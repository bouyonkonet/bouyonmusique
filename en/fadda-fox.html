Fadda Fox: a Barbados Crop Over voice on bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Fadda Fox is a soca singer from Barbados and a Crop Over regular for more than ten years. His name crosses bouyon twice: in 2022 with “No Stoosh Ting”, shared with Hollywood HP and billed “2022 Bouyon”, and in July 2025 with “Bess Jam”, a duet with Faithii on the Kryptic Riddim produced in Dominica by Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence.

From Ducking to Crop Over

“Ducking” is still his calling card. The official audio, posted by JulianspromosTV in June 2014, was past 3 million views when read; Studio B produced it and Jason “Shaft” Bishop wrote it. Years of Barbados soca followed: “Dirty Habits” on the Hand Grenade Riddim in 2017, “Carnival Memories” on the Adrift Riddim in 2022, billed as a Crop Over song, and two albums on Deezer, “Sweet Spot” (2022) and “Nostalgia” (2025).

His collaborations often leave the island. He sang “Stampede” with Dominica's Asa Bantan in 2019 and appears on Motto's “Shell Dong Dat”, posted by TeamFoxx in 2020 and watched more than 3 million times. Those are two direct links to the fast neighbouring scenes, Dominican bouyon and Saint Lucian Dennery Segment.

No Stoosh Ting, billed bouyon (2022)

On June 14, 2022, JulianspromosTV posted “Fadda Fox x Hollywood HP - No Stoosh Ting”, with “2022 Bouyon” in the title and Bouyon among the tags. Fadda Fox, Hollywood HP and Elmo Norville wrote it, and Elmo Norville also handled production, mixing and mastering.

It had a little over 6,100 views when read, modest next to his soca hits. Still, it shows bouyon was part of the Barbadian toolkit well before the 2026 “Barbados Bouyon” wave carried by riddims such as the Okra Slime Riddim.

Bess Jam with Faithii, and Crop Over 2026

On July 4, 2025, the dadamanufakture channel posted “Fadda Fox x Faithii (Bouyon Barbie) - Bess Jam” on the Kryptic Riddim. The press text files the riddim under bouyon and lists “Fadda Fox (Barbados)” among the guests from other islands. Nicholas Sealy and Faith Matthew wrote the song, and Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence produced, mixed and mastered it. It opens the Deezer album of July 6, 2025 and was past 23,000 YouTube views when read. See also the profile of Faithii.

In May 2026 Fadda Fox was back at Crop Over with “Crop Over I Love You”, shared with Nikita, produced by Boogy Rankss and recorded at Decibel Studios. It is billed as Crop Over soca rather than bouyon, but it ties him once more to a producer who works a lot in bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Fadda Fox from?

Barbados: the Kryptic Riddim press text lists him as “Fadda Fox (Barbados)”, and much of his catalogue is billed Crop Over.

What are his bouyon songs?

“No Stoosh Ting” with Hollywood HP (June 2022, billed 2022 Bouyon) and “Bess Jam” with Faithii on Dada's Kryptic Riddim (July 2025).

Who produced Bess Jam?

Krishna 'Dada' Lawrence, who also mixed and mastered it; the writing is credited to Nicholas Sealy and Faith Matthew.

What is his biggest hit?

“Ducking” (2014), produced by Studio B, past 3 million views for the official audio on JulianspromosTV.

Has he worked with Boogy Rankss?

Yes: Boogy Rankss produced “Crop Over I Love You” (May 2026), sung by Fadda Fox and Nikita.
