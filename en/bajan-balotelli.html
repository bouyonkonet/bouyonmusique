Bajan Balotelli: three Bouyon 2026 titles for Crop Over

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Bajan Balotelli enters this site through their own YouTube channel, which holds three titles and nothing else: “LOLLIPOP” and “Far A'way”, posted on July 2, 2026, then “Cou Cou Stick (Unstick yah back)”, posted on July 10, 2026. All three carry Bouyon 2026 in their titles, and two of them also name Crop Over 2026, the big carnival season of Barbados. It is a young and modest file: no page gives a writing, production or label credit, and none states an origin. This profile keeps to what those three pages show.

Three titles in eight days

Bajan Balotelli’s channel comes down to three uploads, all from July 2026. On July 2 came “LOLLIPOP”, presented as official audio and billed Bouyon 2026 (2 minutes 04), and “Far A'way”, also official audio, billed both crop over 2026 and Bouyon 2026 (2 minutes 54). On July 10 followed “Cou Cou Stick (Unstick yah back)”, billed Crop Over 2026 and Bouyon 2026 (2 minutes 30). The hashtags on the first two pages follow the same line: bouyonmusic on both, cropover on “LOLLIPOP”.

The timing says something about the approach. Three titles in eight days, right in the run up to the main Crop Over days, look like a first wave aimed at the road and the fetes. That is a reading of the calendar, not a statement from the artist: none of the pages explains the project. The verified fact stays simple, three titles and three times the word bouyon in the title, the kind of release we follow in new bouyon in 2026.

What the name and the titles say

“Bajan” is the everyday word for things from Barbados, and Crop Over is the Barbadian carnival season. The artist name and the titles therefore clearly place these releases in the Crop Over 2026 circuit. This site still draws no origin from that: a stage name and a target season are not a stated birthplace or home base, and none of the three pages names an island for the artist.

“Cou Cou Stick” refers to the stick used to turn cou cou, the national dish of Barbados, and the subtitle “Unstick yah back” plays on loosening the back, a dance image. Here too, the title does the talking, not a biography. The movement is a familiar one: bouyon, born in Dominica, travels to the neighbouring islands, where artists take it up for their own carnival seasons, as our page on bouyon soca shows.

The limits of the file

The file is thin and this profile says so. Three pages, three running times, three upload dates, and view counts still under one hundred per title when the pages were opened. No page credits a writer, producer, riddim or label, and no biography was found. This site does not fill those blanks with guesses.

If Bajan Balotelli publishes credits, an official page or further releases, this page will be revised and dated. In the meantime, the verified listening fits into three titles: “LOLLIPOP”, “Far A'way” and “Cou Cou Stick (Unstick yah back)”, all three billed Bouyon 2026 on their channel.
