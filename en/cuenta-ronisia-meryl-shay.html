Bouyon danse: Cuenta by Ronisia, Meryl and Shay

By Smokiller · Published October 24, 2026

Cuenta is a Ronisia track, with Meryl and Shay, released on October 2, 2026. Within weeks, the trio became a talking point in the bouyon scene: Guadeloupe bouyon artist Miimii KDS posted a video asking what if she had performed on Cuenta with the queens, and the comments ask Ronisia for a remix. This page explains the link, using only the facts in the file.

On this page
  1. The track and the trio
  2. The question raised by Miimii KDS
  3. Why it matters for bouyon
  4. Frequently asked questions

The track and the trio

Cuenta credits Ronisia as the lead artist, with Meryl and Shay as guests. The track was released on October 2, 2026. In the Miimii KDS video, the queens named are those three: Ronisia, Meryl and Shay.

This page publishes no chart position, no listening figure and no invented quote: the opened file on the track is the credit, the date and the reaction of the bouyon scene.

The question raised by Miimii KDS

Miimii KDS, a Guadeloupe bouyon artist followed on this site, posted a TikTok tease asking what if she had performed on Cuenta with the queens. The comment thread asks Ronisia for a remix.

The tease confirms neither a remix nor a collaboration. It shows one verifiable thing: bouyon voices watch the big pop and R and B voices of the French scene, and the publics of both worlds talk to each other.

Why it matters for bouyon

The bouyon scene has a habit documented on this site: taking, flipping and making the crowd dance to tracks the public already sings, from Smokiller remixes to bouyon versions of current hits. A track like Cuenta, carried by three voices the public calls the queens, enters that conversation naturally.

No official bouyon remix of Cuenta is announced in the opened sources to date. If the scene produces one, this page will be updated with the source.
