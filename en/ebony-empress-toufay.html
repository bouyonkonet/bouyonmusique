Bouyon songs: Ebony Empress, Toufay, the official video

By Smokiller · Published November 3, 2026

A new song from one of the women voices of bouyon. Ebony Empress has presented the official video for Toufay, written by Everlyne Walters and produced by McGyver, per the credits published under the official video.

On this page
  1. The song credits, in black and white
  2. An artist who bills herself Da Goddess of Bouyon
  3. The women of bouyon hold the new season
  4. Frequently asked questions

The song credits, in black and white

The official video description gives the full credits for Toufay: written by Everlyne Walters, produced by McGyver, mixed and mastered by McGyver, with cover art by Everlyne Walters. The song is announced as available on all digital platforms, with Spotify and Apple Music links in the description.

The video carries Bouyon 2027 in its title: Ebony Empress positions Toufay as a song of the next bouyon season, just as the 2026 season closes.

An artist who bills herself Da Goddess of Bouyon

In the video presentation, Ebony Empress presents herself as Da Goddess of Bouyon. That is her billed title, her artist brand: this page reports it as her own presentation, not as an award.

Her record is documented: she was a nominee at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026 in the Best Bouyon Artist category, a title that went that year to Mr Ridge, and her full profile can be read on her artist page on this site.

The women of bouyon hold the new season

Toufay arrives in a period when women voices stand at the front of the bouyon scene, from Shanika and her Kay ve yo to Nice and Situationship on DJ Taffy Funny Riddim. A new Ebony Empress video, produced by McGyver, adds one more song to that line.

To follow the new releases, the Bouyon Music 2026 playlist gathers the bouyon songs of the season, and the women of bouyon chapter traces the place of women singers in the history of the genre.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced Ebony Empress Toufay?

McGyver, who also mixed and mastered the song. Writing and cover art are by Everlyne Walters, per the credits published under the official video.

Where can I hear Toufay?

The song is announced as available on all digital platforms, with Spotify and Apple Music links in the official video description.

Was Ebony Empress recognised at the Caribbean Music Awards?

She was a nominee at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026 in the Best Bouyon Artist category. The category title went to Mr Ridge.
