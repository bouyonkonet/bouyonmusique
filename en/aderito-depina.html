Kareka Gostozo: a bouyon declared in words

The opened page for “Kareka Gostozo” is, for a thin file, unusually complete. The heading gives the artist, the title, the format, official video, and the genre, bouyon. The release notes repeat the genre in a separate field: type of music, bouyon. The track is therefore not attached to the genre by an algorithm or by outside commentary: the artist team declares it itself, twice, in the primary page of the title. Depina is credited there with lyrics and melody, the classic writer performer double post of independent releases.

The rest of the credit roll fits into three clean lines. Music production is signed CMBeatz. Mixing and mastering are signed CMstudio, a name that extends the same prefix, without any opened source stating the link between the two signatures. The video is filmed by ItsPopo, and its direction is credited to 10stars and to Depina himself: the artist therefore co directs the video of his own title. The notes close with thanks to the participants in the video and with the usual invitation to follow the artist on his social media, without publishing any account address in the opened text. Our bouyon playlist follows this kind of declared release, where the genre is claimed in the page before the listening even starts.

The Cabo Verde circuit, read in the keywords

The origin of this bouyon cannot be read in a biography: it can be read in the keyword block the video publishes for its referencing. It carries “caboverde” as one word, “cabo verde artists playlist” in full, and three geographies of the Cabo Verde diaspora: Brockton and New Bedford, the two Massachusetts cities known for their Cabo Verde communities, and Portugal. Around them, the block lines up names of the Cabo Verde scene and its diaspora, Djodje, Gil Semedo and Soraia Ramos among them, as well as the producer of the title, CMBeatz. Those names are referencing keywords, not collaborations: this profile converts them neither into features nor into endorsements.

What those keywords establish is a circuit, not a birthplace. They say whose listening, and from where, the video is seeking: the Cabo Verde audience, in the archipelago and in the North American and Portuguese diaspora. They say neither where Depina was born nor where he lives, and this profile settles neither. Bouyon, born in Dominica, now circulates through whole diasporas, as our bouyon and diaspora page traces: “Kareka Gostozo” is a documented case of it, a bouyon title addressed to the Cabo Verde circuit, with lyrics and a melody signed by its performer.

The limits of the file, stated

The verifiable file on Depina fits into a single opened page, and this profile says so like the other thin files on this site. No biography was found during this research: no date or place of birth, no path, no interview. No other Depina title was verified on the platforms consulted, and the “Kareka Gostozo” video publishes in its opened text no precise release date and no label name. No official account confirmed by direct opening was retained, so no external link is entered in the structured data of this page.

The exact scope is the following: one title, “Kareka Gostozo”, a genre declared twice, bouyon, a complete credit roll, lyrics and melody for Depina, production for CMBeatz, mixing and mastering for CMstudio, filming for ItsPopo and co direction for 10stars and Depina, and a keyword block that anchors the release in the Cabo Verde circuit, from Brockton and New Bedford to Portugal. If Depina publishes further titles or a biography, this page will be completed and dated. In the meantime, it fixes the trace of a Cabo Verde bouyon declared as such by its own credit roll, on October 20, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Aderito Depina?

Aderito Depina is the artist of record on “Kareka Gostozo”, an official video published on YouTube whose notes state the type of music: bouyon. He is credited there with lyrics and melody and co directs the video with 10stars. No public biography was found during this research.

Which Aderito Depina track is verified?

Only one, whose primary page was opened: “Kareka Gostozo”. Its notes credit Depina with lyrics and melody, CMBeatz with music production, CMstudio with mixing and mastering, ItsPopo with filming and 10stars and Depina with direction. No other title was verified.

Where is Aderito Depina from?

No opened source gives his place of birth or residence. The keyword block of his video anchors the release in the Cabo Verde circuit: caboverde, Brockton, New Bedford and Portugal appear there. This profile documents that diaspora circuit, without assigning Depina a birthplace or a town the sources do not name.

Who produced Kareka Gostozo?

CMBeatz, credited as music producer in the notes of the video. Mixing and mastering are credited to CMstudio. No opened source states the link between the two signatures, and this profile does not assume one.

Is Kareka Gostozo a bouyon track?

Yes, according to its own primary page, opened during this research: the heading of the video ends with the word bouyon, and the release notes fill a separate field, type of music: bouyon. The genre is therefore declared by the artist team, not deduced by this site.

Why is this profile short?

Because the verifiable file fits into a single page, complete on credits but with no biography, no precise date and no other verified title. This site prefers a short and exact profile to a portrait filled with deductions. It will grow if the artist publishes more primary sources.
