Magikal: Soca Monarch 2019, Whappa (Bouyon 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Magikal, Derron Rouse by his legal name, is a soca singer from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. On July 7, 2019, aged 21, he was crowned Vincy Mas Soca Monarch with “Bloody Hell Crazy”, ahead of the veteran Fireman, according to iWitness News. In May 2026 he released “Whappa” on Wetty Beatz's Link Up Riddim, a title billed “2026 Bouyon” that was past 270,000 views when this profile was written: the voice of the Vincentian Soca Monarch on the genre born in Dominica.

Soca Monarch 2019: Bloody Hell Crazy dethrones Fireman

iWitness News tells the night of July 6 to 7, 2019 at Victoria Park, Kingstown: at the end of an eight-hour show, around 5 a.m., a 21-year-old artist, Derron Rouse, known as Magikal, a Campden Park resident, was crowned Soca Monarch 2019 on his first entry. His title “Bloody Hell Crazy”, inspired by a British cousin describing Vincentian revellers as “bloody hell crazy”, scored 425 points, seven more than Delroy “Fireman” Hooper, who was going for an eleventh crown. Vilroy “Tuffa” Jack came third with “Liver”.

The same article names his other titles of the time, “Crazy Wine”, “Mad and Cray”, “Can't Stop We” and “Running”, his producer Super K, and about eight years of writing soca before that title. “Wah Deh”, a video published on May 31, 2022 on his MAGIKAL - music channel, more than 237,000 views, carries the same stamp: composed by Derron “Magikal” Rouse, produced by Super-K production and Mr Vik, with vocal production by Peter “Wildfire” Noel and a mix by Parry Jack. The single reached Deezer on May 30, 2022 on the MAGIKAL MUSIC label.

Whappa on the Link Up Riddim: the step toward bouyon

On May 25, 2026, the Wetty Beatz Records channel uploaded “Magikal - Whappa (Link up riddim) 2026 Bouyon”. The page credits writing to Magikal, production to Wetty Beatz Productionz and Foxx, mixing and mastering to Wetty Beatz Productionz, and carries the keywords vincy soca, vincy carnival and wetty beatz. The title was past 270,000 views on October 27, 2026.

On Deezer, “Link Up Riddim” is a Wetty Beatz project dated the same day, label Wetty-Beatz Productionz, with five tracks: “Iz yourz”, “Splash and Dash”, “Whappa”, “Where di Party Is” and the instrumental. The Vincentian producer thus signs a riddim billed bouyon for the 2026 season and places the 2019 Soca Monarch on it. For how bouyon travels between St Vincent and Dominica, see our page on bouyon soca.

From Leave We Alone to Head Chip: a Vincentian catalogue

Between the 2019 title and “Whappa”, the opened pages document a steady catalogue. “Leave We Alone”, published on October 29, 2021 on the Chips & Dip Riddim, composed by Derron “Magikal” Rouse and produced by Xpert Productions. “Inside”, on the Smirk Riddim, billed Grenada/Vincy Soca 2020. “Head Chip (Bad We Bad)”, a single from May 20, 2026 on Island Shak Entertainment. And “Friday”, the NDP campaign song for the 2025 election, uploaded on November 5, 2025 by IT PROVE INT.

This profile cites only the releases whose credits point to Magikal of St Vincent: the Deezer artist page under that name also mixes in Spanish-language titles by another act of the same name, which we leave aside. It is written from iWitness News and the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Magikal?

Derron Rouse, a soca singer from Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, a Campden Park resident, crowned Soca Monarch 2019 at 21 with Bloody Hell Crazy according to iWitness News.

What is his bouyon title?

Whappa, on Wetty Beatz's Link Up Riddim, uploaded on May 25, 2026 and billed 2026 Bouyon; the Deezer project Link Up Riddim also holds Iz yourz, Splash and Dash and Where di Party Is.

How did he win Soca Monarch 2019?

With 425 points for Bloody Hell Crazy, seven more than Delroy Fireman Hooper, at Victoria Park on the night of July 6 to 7, 2019, on his first entry.

What are his other known titles?

Wah Deh (2022, more than 237,000 views), Leave We Alone (Chips & Dip Riddim, 2021), Inside (Smirk Riddim, 2020) and Head Chip (Bad We Bad) (2026).

Is Magikal a bouyon artist?

He is a Vincentian soca singer; this site presents him for Whappa, his 2026 title billed bouyon and produced by Wetty Beatz.
