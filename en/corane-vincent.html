Corane Vincent: the writing name that keeps returning in bouyon gwada credits

Published October 20, 2026

Corane Vincent is a name that exists, in the public sources opened for this profile, only in the credits. No artist page, no interview, no biography: only writing lines, record after record, in the bouyon gwada circle of DJ Luchshiy. Between April 2023 and February 2025, the pages opened for this file credit the name as songwriter of “KKM”, “XMAS FREESTYLE” and “CHABIN” for DJ Luchshiy, co-writer of “Make it pop” for Pinpin OSP and Edday, co-writer of “Mi Boss LA” for Riddla, then composer and programming on “6 Dwèt” for Lestef KJF and Totally Spice’s. This profile follows those six credits one by one, explains what their different roles tell, and states openly, including in its questions and answers, that the civil identity behind the name is documented nowhere in the sources consulted.

A name that exists only in the credits: writer, composer, programming

Let us start with what this profile will not do: it will not invent a person behind Corane Vincent. No source consulted gives an age, a place, a background, a face or an official account for this name. It has no artist page on the platforms opened for this research, where the singers and producers of its circle do have one. What exists, and it is already a great deal for a backstage name, are six verifiable credits on song pages opened one by one: five Shazam pages and one Qobuz album. This file is therefore a credits file, owned as such, in the line of the pages this site devotes to bouyon writers, from Brian Lomba to the Letin sisters.

The name appears under three distinct roles, and the distinction matters. On four records, it is credited Songwriter, in the composition and lyrics section: “KKM”, “XMAS FREESTYLE”, “CHABIN” and “Make it pop”. On the compilation album opened on Qobuz, the same role is printed Writer, on “Xmas Freestyle” and on “Mi Boss LA”. And on “6 Dwèt”, in 2025, the credit changes in nature: Corane Vincent first appears as Programming, among the performing artists of the page, then as Composer, in the composition section. A writer of words or melodies on other people records, then composer and programmer on the most recent record: the path readable in the credits alone runs from writing towards making the sound.

The circle in which these credits turn is identifiable and coherent. At the centre, DJ Luchshiy, bouyon gwada producer, and his banner LUCHSHIY AND BRO, which releases three of the records in the file. Around him, names this site already documents: Edday and Lestef, the voices of the Luchshiy Christmas records; Pinpin OSP, whose “Make it pop” is one of the reference records; Riddla, the voice of “Mi Boss LA”; and Brian Lomba, co-writer of the same “Make it pop”. Corane Vincent is the writing thread that links those pages together: follow the name, and you cross almost the whole first Luchshiy circle, which is exactly why it deserves a page, even without a biography.

A word on tempo, because it also links this file to the rest of the scene. Four of the five Shazam pages opened give 160 BPM: “KKM”, “CHABIN”, “Make it pop” and “6 Dwèt”. The fifth, “XMAS FREESTYLE”, leaves the field empty. 160 BPM is the cruising tempo of the current bouyon single, from the Dominican pages of Trixzmigos to those of the Luchshiy circle: the Corane Vincent credits sit at the centre of that format, record after record, with no documented exception.

“KKM”: the earliest dated credit in the file, April 2023

The oldest dated credit in this file is “KKM”, by DJ Luchshiy featuring Aknose, released on April 24, 2023 on LUCHSHIY AND BRO, in English, at 160 BPM. The Shazam page opened for this profile credits two writers, and two only: Corane Vincent and Gael Rampath. The page showed 638 identifications when it was opened, a modest figure that tells the real size of this layer of the scene: records that matter for the credits and for the mixes, far more than for public counters.

The record belongs to the first DJ Luchshiy catalogue, the one where the LUCHSHIY AND BRO banner sets its formula: a producer as lead name, guest voices in rotation, and recurring writers backstage. Aknose, the guest voice of “KKM”, has his own page on this site, Aknose, and the producer path is read on the DJ Luchshiy page. In that formula, the name of Corane Vincent holds the least visible and most durable place: the writer, who stays on the page when the voices change from one single to the next.

Gael Rampath, the co-writer of “KKM”, does not reappear in any other credit in this file. Corane Vincent writing partnerships therefore change from record to record: alone, with Luchshiy And Bro producing, on the Christmas records; with Brian Lomba and Edday on “Make it pop”; with Wesley Boulogne on “Mi Boss LA”. This is not a fixed team, it is a circle writer, called record by record, and that is one more reason not to turn this name into a character: the sources show a function inside a network, not a narrated career.

The 2023 Christmas set: “XMAS FREESTYLE” and “CHABIN”

On December 22, 2023, LUCHSHIY AND BRO released the DJ Luchshiy Christmas set with Lestef and Edday, from which the Shazam catalogue holds two records for this file. “XMAS FREESTYLE”, the lead record of the set, showed 33,611 identifications when its page was opened, by far the highest counter of the six Corane Vincent credits. Its page credits DJ Luchshiy as performer, Luchshiy And Bro as producer, and a single name on the writing: Corane Vincent, Songwriter. On the biggest record in the file by identifications, the writing is therefore signed by one credit, which makes the trace as clean as that of a sole producer on a production page.

The second record, “CHABIN”, carries the same date, the same banner and the same feature, Lestef and Edday, at 160 BPM, with 8,771 identifications shown when opened. Its writing credit is identical: Corane Vincent, Songwriter, sole name in the composition and lyrics section. Two records from the same set, two pages, one sole writer: the DJ Luchshiy Christmas set of 2023 is, in document terms, the most concentrated writing work under the Corane Vincent name, and the simplest entry point into its file.

The set kept circulating after its release. The compilation album “Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1”, presented by Int’l Stephen and released on DSM Music and Monk Music in 2024, carries “Xmas Freestyle” as track 28, with the same Corane Vincent writer credit, Edday and LeStef as features, and Luchshiy And Bro producing, in the Qobuz page opened for this profile. The duration printed on the compilation, 28 minutes and 54 seconds, does not match the format of an ordinary single: this profile reports it as the duration of the compilation track, without merging it with that of the original record, which the pages consulted do not give. What the reissue proves, without ambiguity, is the second life of bouyon records in carnival compilations, where a Christmas freestyle ends up, a year later, in an international soca selection of 53 tracks.

“Make it pop”: the bridge to Pinpin OSP, November 2023

Between the two Luchshiy sets, the file passes through another name of the circle. “Make it pop”, by Pinpin OSP and Edday, was released on 2 November 2023 on VGM PRODUCT, in French, at 160 BPM, with 344 identifications shown when its page was opened. The composition and lyrics section of the page opened for this profile lines up four names: Edday, Songwriter; Luchshiy, Composer; Brian Lomba, Songwriter; and Corane Vincent, Songwriter. It is the most collective credit in the file, and the only one where the name stands next to Brian Lomba, the other recurring writer of the circle, whose path this site traces on the Brian Lomba page.

The structure of the credit deserves a slow reading, because it shows how this scene shares the work. Luchshiy is composer here, not the producer of the page: the composition of the record is his, while three writers share the writing, including the two circle voices and names, Edday and Brian Lomba, and Corane Vincent. The record is in French, where “KKM” and the Christmas records are filed in English: the Luchshiy circle writes in both languages of its audience, and its recurring writer is credited on both sides of that line.

The path of the lead artist is read on our Pinpin OSP page, and that of the associated voice on the Edday page. Hold the date here: November 2023 falls between “KKM” in April and the Christmas set in December. In eight months, the Corane Vincent name is therefore credited on three releases and two banners of the same circle, LUCHSHIY AND BRO and VGM PRODUCT, which makes 2023 its densest documented year, before the 2025 return under a new role.

“Mi Boss LA”: the Riddla credit, known through the compilation

The sixth credit in the file is the only one that does not come from a single page. “Mi Boss LA”, by Riddla, stands as track 31 of the “Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1” compilation opened on Qobuz for this profile, with a duration of 2 minutes and 3 seconds. The track page gives the full split: Riddla, MainArtist; Wesley Boulogne, Writer; Corane Vincent, Writer; DJ Luchshiy, FeaturedArtist; and Grand-mère Baptiste Prod as producer. The Corane Vincent name therefore reappears as co-writer, this time alongside Wesley Boulogne, for the voice of Riddla, with Luchshiy present as a feature rather than in production.

This credit calls for a particular honesty, and this profile practices it. The compilation is dated 2024 on DSM Music and Monk Music, but no original single release page for “Mi Boss LA” was opened during this research: no first release date, no original label, and no counter are claimed here. What is verified is the presence of the record, with this precise writing credit, inside an international carnival compilation of 53 tracks, alongside Problem Child, Shelly and Signal Band, TK International and Lil Rick. For a writer with no artist page, standing in the writing credits of such a selection is a real trace of circulation, and it is exactly at that level, no higher and no lower, that this profile presents it.

The path of the voice of the record is read on our Riddla page. In the economy of the file, “Mi Boss LA” plays a precise role: it proves that the Corane Vincent name is not limited to LUCHSHIY AND BRO releases. Between VGM PRODUCT for “Make it pop”, the DSM Music and Monk Music compilation for this record, and Dreamtracks for “6 Dwèt” in 2025, the writer circulates between at least three publishing structures, always from the same position, the writing line.

“6 Dwèt”: the role changes in 2025, and the limits of the file

The most recent credit in the file is also the most different. “6 Dwèt”, by Lestef KJF and Totally Spice’s featuring DJ Luchshiy, was released on February 15, 2025 on Dreamtracks, in French, at 160 BPM, with 5,706 identifications shown when its page was opened. Corane Vincent appears twice on it, under two roles that are new in this file: Programming, in the list of performing artists of the page, alongside the voices of Naima Dimina, Carcheline Cereme and Stephane Cornano; and Composer, in the composition section, alongside Stephane Cornano, while Yoan Courcet is credited as arranger, producer and engineer, and the lyrics belong to Naima Dimina and Carcheline Cereme.

The difference with the 2023 credits is clear, and this profile underlines it without over interpreting it. On “KKM”, “CHABIN” and “Make it pop”, Corane Vincent wrote, and others built: Luchshiy composed or produced, Luchshiy And Bro produced. On “6 Dwèt”, the name moves to the building side, programming and composition, while the writing of the lyrics belongs to two other names. Nothing in the sources says whether this is a change of trade, a one off role on this record, or a credit correction: this profile records the page line, the date, and the contrast with the four writer credits before it, and stops there. The paths of the voices of the record are read on our Lestef KJF and DJ Luchshiy pages.

The limits close this file as they opened it. Corane Vincent has, in the sources consulted, no biography, no origin, no artist page, no interview, and no verifiable official account: the sameAs list in the structured data of this page is empty, deliberately. Even its territory is claimed only through the circle: the records are those of the Guadeloupe bouyon gwada this site documents around DJ Luchshiy, and the title of this page places the file in Guadeloupe on that basis, that of the scene, without pretending to know the writer personal origin. If one day an artist page, an interview or an official presentation gives a face to this writing name, this profile will be completed and dated. In the meantime, listening starts with “XMAS FREESTYLE” and “CHABIN”, continues with “KKM” and “Make it pop”, then with “Mi Boss LA” and “6 Dwèt”, and the context is read in our bouyon playlist and on our bouyon soca page, the form started in Dominica in 2003 by Dada according to the first hand testimony of Dominican-born Smokiller, and popularized by Triple Kay.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Corane Vincent?

A writing and composing name in the bouyon gwada circle of DJ Luchshiy, documented only through its credits: songwriter of “KKM”, “XMAS FREESTYLE” and “CHABIN” in 2023, co-writer of “Make it pop” and “Mi Boss LA”, then composer and programming on “6 Dwèt” in 2025. No source consulted gives a civil identity, an origin or a biography, and this profile therefore publishes only the credits, opened page by page.

What is the earliest verified Corane Vincent credit?

The oldest dated in this file is “KKM”, by DJ Luchshiy featuring Aknose, released on April 24, 2023 on LUCHSHIY AND BRO, at 160 BPM. The Shazam page of the record credits two writers: Corane Vincent and Gael Rampath. This profile says “the oldest in the file”, not the first of a career, which no source allows to date.

On which DJ Luchshiy records is Corane Vincent credited?

Three records opened for this profile: “KKM” in April 2023, co-written with Gael Rampath; “XMAS FREESTYLE” and “CHABIN”, released on December 22, 2023 in the Christmas set with Lestef and Edday, where the name is the sole credited writer and Luchshiy And Bro produces. “XMAS FREESTYLE” showed 33,611 identifications on its page, the highest counter in the file.

What is the role on Pinpin OSP “Make it pop”?

Songwriter, a shared role. The record, released on 2 November 2023 on VGM PRODUCT, in French, credits Edday, Brian Lomba and Corane Vincent as songwriters, and Luchshiy as composer. It is the only credit in the file where Corane Vincent writes alongside Brian Lomba, the other recurring writer of the same circle.

What does “Mi Boss LA” add to the file?

A writing credit outside the LUCHSHIY AND BRO banner. The Riddla record, featuring DJ Luchshiy and produced by Grand-mère Baptiste Prod, stands on the 2024 compilation Summer Of Carnivals, Vol. 1, where the Qobuz page credits Wesley Boulogne and Corane Vincent as writers. No original single page was opened during this research, and this profile claims neither its first release date nor its original label.

Why is so little known about Corane Vincent?

Because the name exists publicly only in the credits. No artist page, no interview, and no official account verified by direct opening were found during this research, and the sameAs list of this page is therefore empty. Its role, though, changed across the pages: writer in 2023, then composer and programming on “6 Dwèt” in 2025. This profile publishes those credit facts, states the limit in full, and will be completed if an official source appears.
