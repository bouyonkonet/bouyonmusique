King Low Rider: Land Diver, bouyon, Antigua

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

King Low Rider is the stage name credited to Rudolph Albert in the descriptions on his own YouTube channel. Four of his videos carry the word bouyon in their titles, including “Land Diver”, billed as Antigua Carnival bouyon for 2026, and “Bounce”, which lists additional production by Mr. Ridge, already profiled on this site. The credited studio, Black Pineapple, is shown with the Antigua flag: a circuit signal, and this profile does not turn it into a birthplace.

Four bouyon titles on one channel

“Land Diver (Antigua Carnival BOUYON 2026)” runs 2 minutes 36. Its description credits the writing to Rudolph Albert, aka King Low Rider, the recording to Black Pineapple Studio and the production to TBG Music. The upload date reported for this video is January 17, 2024, while the title on screen says 2026: this profile reports both as they stand and assumes no reason for the gap.

“Bounce”, uploaded February 17, 2026 and tagged bouyon 2026, is the best documented: production by Black Pineapple, additional production by Mr. Ridge, recorded at Black Pineapple Studio, written by King Low Rider and Sheldon, mixed and mastered at King Heart Beat Studio, video by Akeeba Martin. Two more titles complete the list: “Handy Jam”, with Kiss Da Hand, labelled 2025 BOUYON and uploaded February 1, 2025, and “The Toy”, labelled Bouyon soca 2025, presented by Klahsik Promotion, recorded by Force 1 Studio, written by Rudolph Albert, with an upload date reported as April 9, 2022.

Antigua, as the page puts it

The word Antigua appears twice: in the title of “Land Diver”, aimed at Antigua Carnival, and next to the Black Pineapple studio name, with the island’s flag. Both place the music on the Antiguan carnival circuit. Neither says where the artist was born, and no opened page does, so this site assigns him no island. The verified context for the genre on the island is on our page about bouyon in Antigua.

Mr. Ridge on “Bounce” is the only credit linking King Low Rider to a bouyon artist already profiled here. It is noted as a credit, with no wider collaboration inferred from it.

The limits of the file

The file is four videos from his channel and the listing of its 38 uploads. No primary biography, no streaming platform page and no interview was opened. The gaps between title years and upload dates are unexplained in the sources, and this profile leaves them that way.

New releases carrying the word bouyon will be added here and in new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is King Low Rider?

The stage name credited to Rudolph Albert in the descriptions on his YouTube channel, where four videos carry the word bouyon in their titles.

Which bouyon titles are verified?

“Land Diver (Antigua Carnival BOUYON 2026)”, “Bounce” (bouyon 2026), “Handy Jam” with Kiss Da Hand (2025 BOUYON) and “The Toy” (Bouyon soca 2025).

Who produced Bounce?

Black Pineapple, with additional production by Mr. Ridge, according to the description. Writing is credited to King Low Rider and Sheldon.

Where is King Low Rider from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. Antigua Carnival in a title and the Antigua flag next to the studio are circuit signals, not a stated origin.

Why do some dates not match the titles?

The upload dates reported for “Land Diver” (2024) and “The Toy” (2022) come before the year shown in their titles. The sources do not explain it, and this site assumes nothing.
