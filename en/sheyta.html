Sheyta

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Sheyta is a voice whose name comes back, between 2025 and 2026, on three titles explicitly filed under the bouyon label: “VENDREDI”, released in April 2025 by the VGM Product’ house with BOUYON 2025 in the title, “VODY 22”, a group video released in December 2025 on the Jixels channel, and “AMBIANCE”, with Managyalis, in July 2026. Sheyta also runs a YouTube channel, which carries “KANIBAL” with Ugly Boy. This profile follows those titles one by one and leaves blank what the pages do not say: biography, legal name and origin.

Island: not stated in the sources · All islands

VENDREDI, the first labelled title

The oldest verified Sheyta title is “VENDREDI”, posted on April 12, 2025 on the channel of VGM Product’, the production and recording house that also works with Pinpin OSP. The video runs 2 minutes 29 and its full title, “SHEYTA - VENDREDI (BOUYON 2025)”, carries the genre and the year. The description is empty: no production credit, no beatmaker name and no platform release date appear there, and this profile adds none.

A month later, on May 23, 2025, the SHEYTA_OG channel released “KANIBAL”, presented in the description as the second title by the pairing of Sheyta and Ugly Boy, with the Instagram and TikTok accounts of both voices. The page does not mention bouyon: the title is therefore named here for the timeline, without entering the structured data. It does show a partnership that continues a few months later on “VODY 22”.

VODY 22 and AMBIANCE, in the Jixels orbit

“VODY 22” came out as an official video on December 17, 2025 on the Jixels channel, with the hashtag #BOUYON in the title. Four voices are credited, Lecus, Leseb, Sheyta and Uglyboy, over a production by Qwann; Yas Beats handles mixing, mastering and vocal recording, Mighty Production the direction, and Jixels appears as executive producer. The video runs 2 minutes 33.

“AMBIANCE” followed in July 2026: an official video posted on July 22 as “MANAGYALIS X SHEYTA - AMBIANCE (CLIP OFFICIEL) #BOUYON”, over a production by Diixly, with Jixels on mixing, mastering and studio work, and G-ISLAND directing. The distribution version, released on July 21, 2026 under the JX PRODZ line, files the title under the name of Jixels with Managyalis and Sheyta as guests. In between, on May 29, 2026, Sheyta also appears on “ARE U FEELING”, produced by Dinho, whose page does not name the genre.

What the file does not say

The opened pages give credits, dates and genre labels, but none publishes a Sheyta biography, legal name, birth date or place of origin. The accounts quoted in the “KANIBAL” description were not opened for this profile. This site therefore assigns Sheyta no island, even though every verified title goes through the Jixels circle or through VGM Product’.

The verifiable file thus comes down to three declared bouyon titles, “VENDREDI”, “VODY 22” and “AMBIANCE”, and two unlabelled collaborations, “KANIBAL” and “ARE U FEELING”. To hear the genre more broadly, the site’s bouyon playlist remains the starting point, and upcoming releases will be tracked in new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sheyta?

Sheyta is a voice credited on several bouyon titles between 2025 and 2026, released by VGM Product’ and on the Jixels channel, who runs a YouTube channel under the name SHEYTA_OG. No public biography was found.

Which Sheyta bouyon titles are verified?

“VENDREDI”, titled BOUYON 2025 and released on April 12, 2025; “VODY 22”, a video tagged #BOUYON from December 17, 2025 with Lecus, Leseb and Uglyboy; and “AMBIANCE”, with Managyalis, a video tagged #BOUYON from July 22, 2026.

Who produced the Sheyta titles?

According to the opened pages, Qwann produced “VODY 22”, Diixly produced “AMBIANCE” and Dinho produced “ARE U FEELING”. The “VENDREDI” page gives no production credit.

Who is Ugly Boy, the Sheyta partner?

The voice credited with Sheyta on “KANIBAL” in May 2025, spelled Uglyboy on “VODY 22”. This site gives that artist a separate profile.

Where is Sheyta from?

No opened source names an island, a town or a country for Sheyta. This site therefore assigns no origin.
