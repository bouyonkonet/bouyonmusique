Vibwoy: Love You Vibwoy, a whole mixtape billed bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Vibwoy, Devonte Luke according to the writing credits of his titles, is a singer who bills nearly all his releases bouyon: “Warzone” and “Try” in 2025, “Mommy”, “Papa Met”, “Like Me” and “Watch Me Funny” billed Bouyon 2026, “Party” with Dastivo billed Bouyon 2027. On February 6, 2026, he gathered that vein on an album titled “Love You Vibwoy (Bouyon Mixtape)”, published on his own label, Vibwoy Music. His Deezer page lists 13 releases since 2024.

Mommy, Papa Met, Like Me: the titles billed Bouyon 2026

On November 5, 2025, Vibwoy Music published “Mommy (Bouyon 2026) (Lyric Video)”, produced by Hunter and written by Devonte Demoi Luke, a birthday title addressed to his mother. On December 12, 2025 came “Papa Met (Bouyon 2026)”, produced by Zagho Beats, mixed and mastered by Bouyon Kitchen Studios, more than 2,100 views when read. On May 7, 2026, “Like Me (Bouyon 2026)”, produced by Zagho Beats and mixed by YW Free Studio, completed the run, with “Watch Me Funny”.

Hunter, producer of “Mommy”, is also credited on Sully Jacob's “Où est mon argent?” under the name Hxnter. Zagho Beats and Bouyon Kitchen Studios, on the other hand, appear on no other page of this site: Vibwoy works with his own team.

Love You Vibwoy: a mixtape that states its genre

On Deezer, “Love You Vibwoy (Bouyon Mixtape)” is an album dated February 6, 2026, label Vibwoy Music, whose tracks include “Light It Up”, “Somebody”, “WDW”, “Papa Met”, “Limere Freestyle” and “Mommy”. The subtitle does the filing: the mixtape presents itself as bouyon. On August 28, 2026, “Party (feat. Dastivo)”, billed Bouyon 2027 on YouTube, was announced as a cut from “Love You Vibwoy 2”, due October 2.

Vibwoy's Deezer artist page lists 13 releases: “UME” (album, February 2, 2024), “Xtacy” (2024), “The World Is Yours” (album, February 7, 2025), “Light It Up”, “Treat Me Better” and “Koshay” with Guwapgawd (2025), the EP “O.S.O.S” (April 17, 2026), “Grind On Me”, “Party”, “Wasted Time” and “Shake It” with Syanne and 6LO (October 16, 2026). For the format, see our page on bouyon mixtapes.

What the pages say, and what they do not

His videos' tags cite Dominica, its carnival, its independence day, the World Creole Music Festival, but also Miami, St Croix, St Thomas, Tortola, Saint Lucia, Antigua and Jamaica: distribution keywords, not a statement of origin. No page opened for this profile says where Vibwoy is from, and this site therefore assigns him no island.

Views remain modest, from a few hundred to two thousand per title, but the regularity of the releases and the album billed bouyon justify the profile. It is written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026 and will be extended when an official page or an interview documents his path.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Vibwoy?

Devonte Luke according to the credits of his titles, a singer whose Vibwoy Music channel has published titles billed bouyon since 2025 and whose album Love You Vibwoy (Bouyon Mixtape) came out on February 6, 2026 on his Vibwoy Music label.

What are his bouyon titles?

Warzone and Try (Bouyon 2025), Mommy, Papa Met, Like Me and Watch Me Funny (Bouyon 2026), Party with Dastivo (Bouyon 2027), and the tracks of the mixtape Love You Vibwoy.

Who produces his titles?

Hunter on Mommy, Zagho Beats on Papa Met and Like Me, with mixes by Bouyon Kitchen Studios and YW Free Studio, according to his YouTube pages.

What is Love You Vibwoy?

An album subtitled Bouyon Mixtape, dated February 6, 2026 on Deezer, with Light It Up, Somebody, WDW, Papa Met, Limere Freestyle and Mommy; a volume 2 is announced on the Party page.

Where is Vibwoy from?

No opened page says: his tags cite Dominica as much as Miami or St Croix. This profile assigns him no origin.
