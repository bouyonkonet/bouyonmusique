National Cultural Gala 2026: today, Sunday 1 November at the Botanic Gardens

By Smokiller · Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

It is today. The National Cultural Gala of the 48th Independence season takes place this Sunday 1 November 2026 at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, at 3 p.m. per Guide.dm, in the official programme published by the Office of the Prime Minister.

On this page
  1. Today s appointment
  2. Jing ping, bele and quadrille on stage
  3. The E.O. LeBlanc Trophy

Today s appointment

The official season programme, announced by Culture Minister Greta Roberts, places the National Cultural Gala at the Botanic Gardens this Sunday 1 November, just ahead of the National Day ceremony on Tuesday 3 November at Windsor Park.

Guide.dm gives the time: 3 p.m., at the Botanical Gardens. Dominica News describes a day filled with cultural music, dance and old folk tales.

Jing ping, bele and quadrille on stage

DOM767 presents the gala as one of the cornerstone events of the Independence celebrations: traditional music, dance and art forms like jing ping, bele and quadrille.

Cultural groups from different communities across the island showcase dance, drumming, storytelling and traditional music there, per DOM767.

The E.O. LeBlanc Trophy

The gala also includes awards, among them the E.O. LeBlanc Trophy for outstanding cultural contributions, and lifetime achievement awards for people who have dedicated their lives to preserving Dominican artistic traditions, per DOM767.

The gala was originally conceived by former Premier Edward Oliver LeBlanc in 1965, per DOM767. The 2026 winners will be known after tonight: a report will follow when Dominican press publishes the names.

The final stretch of the season

The gala closes the densest week of the season: Youth Rally on 29 October, Jounen Kweyol on 30 October, Cultural Extravaganza and Eat Fish Day on 31 October, the gala on 1 November.

Still to come: the National Day ceremony on 3 November at Windsor Park, with military parade, the Prime Minister address and the national awards, then Community Service Day on 4 November, which closes the season.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the National Cultural Gala 2026?

Today, Sunday 1 November 2026, at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, at 3 p.m. per Guide.dm, in the official Independence season programme.

What can you see at the National Cultural Gala?

Traditional music and dance performances, including jing ping, bele and quadrille, by cultural groups from across the island, per DOM767.

What award is presented at the gala?

The E.O. LeBlanc Trophy for outstanding cultural contributions, plus lifetime achievement awards for Dominican tradition keepers, per DOM767. The 2026 winners will be announced after tonight.
