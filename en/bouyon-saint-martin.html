Bouyon in Saint Martin: small island, sharp producers

Published September 10, 2026

Saint Martin counts in the new geography of bouyon thanks to its producers, foremost among them Denmath, a beatmaker originally from SXM, a specialist in bouyon, kompa and shatta, known for his riddims Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim.

A bridge between the Caribbean and France

Based between Nantes and Paris, Denmath illustrates the path of the new generation of producers: Caribbean roots, a studio in France, collaborations across all the Antilles. See his dedicated page: Denmath.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s: it was in Roseau, the island's capital, that the band WCK created it in 1988, mixing jing ping, cadence-lypso and electronic rhythms. Bouyon remains the sound of Roseau carnival today.

Which bouyon producers come from Saint Martin?

The main name is Denmath, whose legal name is Mathurin DENMATH Denis: a producer, sound engineer and artistic director from Saint Martin, of Haitian heritage, based between Nantes and Paris. A specialist in bouyon, kompa and shatta, he is known for his riddims Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim. See his page Denmath.

Which bouyon singer grew up in Saint Martin?

Tamillia, full name Tamillia Chance, grew up in Saint Martin in the island's French and Dutch cultures and was on The Voice Kids France at 14. She is the voice of Don't Care, DJ Blax's single released on October 16, 2026, whose video was shot in St Maarten, the Dutch side of the island. See DJ Blax.
