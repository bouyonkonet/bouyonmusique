Bouyon music / Bouyon artists / Green Apple

Green Apple: Pretty Ways, Tonight (Ayo)

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Green Apple appears in 2025 alongside Khallion, first as a feature on “Bend Down”, then as a duet partner on “Ride Or Die”. In 2026 the artist moves to lead billing with “Pretty Ways”, billed Bouyon 2026, then “Tonight (Ayo)”, billed Bouyon 2027 and posted on October 17, 2026. Every one of these tracks is posted on Khallion’s channel under KhallioNation Records, most of them produced by ShakoProduction. The artist’s accounts carry the suffix 767, Dominica’s area code, but no opened page gives an origin, a legal name or a biography.

From features to lead billing

“Bend Down”, posted on May 9, 2025, is the first track in the record: an official Khallion video with Green Apple, running 2 minutes 53, produced by ShakoProduction, tagged bouyon and 2025bouyon, and linking to the album “777 Di Bouyon Rockstar”. “Ride Or Die”, from July 7, 2025, running 3 minutes 08, is billed bouyon in its title and links to the same album.

In 2026 Green Apple’s place grows. “They Hating On Us”, from February 21, brings together Khallion, Top Boy and Green Apple on a track billed Bouyon 2026. “Arch Your Back”, from March 29, is an official Khallion and Green Apple video, produced by DMitriBts and directed by Khallion and Reo, with the hashtag bouyon.

Pretty Ways and Tonight (Ayo)

“Pretty Ways”, posted on February 12, 2026, running 2 minutes 19, is Green Apple’s first lead title in the record, billed Bouyon 2026. The page links to a streaming page in the artist’s name and to the artist’s accounts, under the name greenapple.official767.

“Tonight (Ayo)”, posted on October 17, 2026, running 2 minutes 55, is already billed Bouyon 2027, following the circuit habit of dating a track by the carnival season it aims at. Writing is credited to the artist’s account, production to ShakoProduction, and one of the tags names Dominica.

The credits, and their limits

The duets with Khallion carry a recurring writing credit: Bertist Jeffers, Khallion’s legal name, with Karrel Simon, joined by Lee Mills on “They Hating On Us”. No page says Karrel Simon is Green Apple’s name, so this profile quotes the credit as it stands, with no biographical conclusion.

The description of “They Hating On Us” is worth noting: it defends soca and bouyon together and presents bouyon as a movement born in Dominica. That is the label’s position, reported as such, and it matches the history told on our bouyon in Dominica page. For what comes next, see our new bouyon 2026 page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Green Apple?

Green Apple is a bouyon artist name released by KhallioNation Records, Khallion’s label. After duets with Khallion in 2025, the artist released “Pretty Ways” (Bouyon 2026) and “Tonight (Ayo)” (Bouyon 2027) as lead.

Which tracks did Green Apple make with Khallion?

“Bend Down” and “Ride Or Die” in 2025, “They Hating On Us” with Top Boy and “Arch Your Back” in 2026, all posted on Khallion’s channel.

Who produces Green Apple?

ShakoProduction is credited as producer on “Bend Down”, “Ride Or Die”, “They Hating On Us” and “Tonight (Ayo)”. “Arch Your Back” is produced by DMitriBts.

Where is Green Apple from?

No opened page says. The artist’s accounts carry the suffix 767 and one tag on “Tonight (Ayo)” names Dominica: these are signals, and this site does not present them as a confirmed origin.
