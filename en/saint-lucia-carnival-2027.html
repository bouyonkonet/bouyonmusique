Saint Lucia Carnival 2027

By Smokiller · Published November 11, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Saint Lucia Carnival 2027 runs from 1 to 20 July 2027, with the Parade of the Bands on Monday 19 and Tuesday 20 July, in Castries. The dates and the parade days are confirmed, but no theme and no official programme have been published yet. This is the July carnival of the Antilles, the carnival of Dennery Segment, Saint Lucia's Kweyol genre and a direct cousin of bouyon.

The dates: 1 to 20 July 2027

The carnival season runs from 1 to 20 July 2027. The Parade of the Bands, the heart of the carnival, takes place on Monday 19 and Tuesday 20 July in Castries, in the classic pattern of Carnival Monday and Tuesday. Both days do fall on a Monday and a Tuesday in 2027.

One public holidays list gives Carnival on Friday 16 July 2027. That date does not match the Monday and Tuesday parade days confirmed by several guides, and we do not publish it as a parade day. It may reflect a different observance or an error, and it is unverified.

No theme, no programme yet

No 2027 theme for Saint Lucia appears in the verified sources. One guide mentions the theme Legendary 70, but that belongs to Antigua Carnival 2027, not Saint Lucia, and it must not be borrowed here.

The dates and parade days are confirmed, but the exact venues, times and full programme have not yet been published. The only official activity under way concerns the bands: on 21 October 2026, the Saint Lucia Carnival Bands Association invited new bands to apply for the 2027 National Parade of the Bands, with a deadline of 30 November 2026.

Dennery Segment sets the tone

No bouyon artist, stage or 2027 bouyon booking has been announced for Saint Lucia Carnival. What is verified is the neighbouring sound: on 25 October 2026, the official Carnival Saint Lucia page recalled that Dennery Segment, the island's Kweyol genre, takes over the streets every year during Lucian Carnival.

The first 2027 Dennery Segment releases are already arriving, such as Soak Di Salt Fish (Let Me Bite It) by Exodus AKA Mr. X, billed as Saint Lucia Soca 2027 and Dennery Segment. For bouyon fans, Saint Lucia Carnival is therefore best read through its Kweyol cousin, in a carnival circuit shared between the islands.

A July carnival, not a February one

Saint Lucia moved its Carnival from February to July in 1999. The event builds through Panorama, the Calypso Monarch, the Soca Monarch and the election of the King and Queen of the Bands, before J'ouvert at dawn on the Monday.

This carnival must not be confused with Dominica's Mas Domnik, which remains a February carnival. The two islands share the Kweyol language and carnival sounds, but their calendars differ. Saint Lucia Carnival 2027 is the July date on the circuit, and the bouyon pages of this site will follow its programme when it comes out.
