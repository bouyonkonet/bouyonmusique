Th3rd: Carnival is Woman, Congratulations (Bouyon 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Th3rd, Leo Sibilly III according to the credits of his titles, is a singer, writer and sound engineer working under the Th3rdHouse name. Bouyon brought him to notice next to Trilla-G: “Carnival is Woman”, billed Bouyon 2025 and presented as a Dominica x Virgin Islands link up, then “Congratulations”, billed Bouyon 2026, whose vocals he engineered himself. His Deezer page lists 41 releases, from “Shotta in Love” in 2019 to “Gyal Alone” in September 2026.

Carnival is Woman: the link up with Trilla-G

“Th3rd x Trilla-G - Carnival is Woman” was uploaded on December 6, 2024 on Trilla-G's channel, which presents it as “Bouyon 2025” and a link up between Dominica and the Virgin Islands, flags included. The page credits production to Shaquille Narcisse (Nova Elite Audio), live bass to Dernel Green, writing to Adrian Burke, Gael Jno Baptiste, Anthony Nibbs, Lionel Blucher and Leo Sibilly III, mixing to Dernel Green, Jordan Duverney and Th3rd House, mastering to Dernel Green. The title was past 88,000 views when read.

On Deezer, “Carnival Is Woman (feat. Trilla-G)” was released on December 4, 2024 on the Th3rdHouse label, 2 minutes 45, under Th3rd's name. It is therefore a Th3rd release inviting Trilla-G, not the other way round: this site's page on bouyon in Dominica tells the circuit this duo belongs to.

Congratulations, Bumpa, Toxic Love: three bouyon titles in three months

On February 7, 2026, Trilla-G published “Congratulations ft. Th3rd (Bouyon 2026)”, more than 74,000 views when read. The page credits production to Andel “Fr3EzE” Horsford and Dernel Green, arrangement and live guitars to Dernel and Giovannie Green, and the vocals to Th3rd himself, “vocals engineered by Th3rd (Th3rdHouse)”. A few weeks earlier, on December 18, 2025, DeeArr Music uploaded “DeeArr ft. Th3rd - Bumpa (2026 Bouyon)”, written and performed by Donnel “DeeArr” Romain and Leo “Th3rd” Sibily, produced by BAPS, mixed and mastered by Bertist “Khallion” Jeffers.

In between, Trilla-G's album “Take Me As I Am: The Album” (Still Trill Ent., October 22, 2025) carries “Toxic Love (feat. Th3rd)”. In three months Th3rd thus sits on three bouyon releases with three different producers, Fr3EzE, Baps and Dernel Green, and two artists of this site, Trilla-G and DeeArr, with Khallion on the mix.

Th3rdHouse: 41 releases, from dancehall to bouyon

Th3rd's Deezer artist page lists 41 releases between “Shotta in Love” (July 5, 2019) and “Gyal Alone (feat. Boogy Rankss)” (September 25, 2026), including three albums: “Change of Heart” (December 21, 2018), “Deh Ville” (June 11, 2020) and “Coming Home” (March 27, 2024). The label is his own, Th3rdHouse, and the same name appears on the mix of his titles: Th3rd records, mixes and publishes his own catalogue.

“Whine Down (feat. Boogy Rankss)”, a video published on September 29, 2025 on his channel, written by Leo Sibilly III and Adrian Burke, produced by Boogy Rankss, mixed and mastered by Th3rd House, carries bouyon, soca and carnival among its keywords: the page itself files the title under those genres. Boogy Rankss is also the co-producer of “Eye Spy” for DJ Kalli. This profile is written from the pages opened on October 27, 2026; Th3rd's origin is not spelled out on them, and this site does not infer it from a flag in a video description.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Th3rd?

Leo Sibilly III according to the credits of his titles, a singer, writer and sound engineer of the Th3rdHouse label, with 41 releases on Deezer since 2019 and three bouyon titles with Trilla-G and DeeArr between October 2025 and February 2026.

What are his bouyon titles?

Carnival is Woman with Trilla-G (Bouyon 2025, December 4, 2024), Toxic Love on Trilla-G's album Take Me As I Am (October 22, 2025), Bumpa with DeeArr (2026 Bouyon, December 18, 2025) and Congratulations with Trilla-G (Bouyon 2026, February 7, 2026).

What is Th3rdHouse?

The label of his Deezer releases and the name credited for mixing and mastering on Whine Down, Carnival is Woman and Congratulations.

Is Th3rd from Dominica?

The Carnival is Woman page presents the title as a Dominica x Virgin Islands link up without saying where each artist is from. This profile states no origin.

What is his latest release?

Gyal Alone (feat. Boogy Rankss), a single dated September 25, 2026 on his Deezer page.
