Yawiar: from Bouyon café to the Bouyon Story compilations

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Yawiar is a singer whose songs come out on the label What time is hit. Active on YouTube since 2015, he has turned out bouyon tracks since 2020, often in Creole, and in 2025 he gathered his circle on two compilations called “Bouyon Story”.

From roots beginnings to Baw baw baw bouyon

In 2015 his channel posted “Black man”, subtitled “An vi”, on a riddim by ObieDaz The BeatOven and filed under “roots gangsta music”. The tone shifted in 2020. “Maddley Bouyon” came out in March, “Baw baw baw bouyon” in September, and the genre has stayed with him since.

The album “Sickness of Their Chains”, from June 2021, still mixes styles. It holds “Bouyon café”, sung with Awawak, which remains one of his most played songs on YouTube with more than 2,000 views. “Bouyon whatta” and “Bouyon Black Pannta” followed in 2022.

What time is hit and Awawak

What time is hit is both the label behind his releases and a video channel. That is where “She Like a Bad Man” appeared in March 2024, again with Awawak, described as “bouyon 2024 dirty side” on a riddim by Litle Boy. The pair turns up often in his catalogue.

His highest view count does not come from straight bouyon, though. The Christmas version of “Ça boit ? ou ça aboie ?” is past 5,500 views. A March 2025 single is even named after the pesticide chlordecone.

The two Bouyon Story volumes

In June 2025, the compilation “Bouyon story” opened the door to other voices from the label: Djawest, Diko, Awawak, Gwadaking, Ayssi. Yawiar contributes “Viniw”, “Humide” and “Actrice”, all three tagged bouyon.

The second volume came in July with “An wout”, “Bouyon volan” and “Fly ansanm”, next to songs by Little Di Lion and Durty ary. In September 2026 he released “Yo wont” with Tchiking, a song that also exists in a bouyon version.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Yawiar?

A singer whose songs come out on the label What time is hit and has made bouyon, often in Creole, since 2020.

What are his bouyon songs?

Maddley Bouyon, Baw baw baw bouyon, Bouyon café, Bouyon whatta, She Like a Bad Man, An wout, Viniw and Humide, among others.

What is Bouyon Story?

Two compilations released in June and July 2025 on What time is hit, with Yawiar, Awawak, Djawest and other label artists.

Who does he sing with most?

Awawak, who appears on Bouyon café, She Like a Bad Man and both Bouyon Story volumes.

Where is Yawiar from?

His releases do not say, even though many of his titles are in Creole.
