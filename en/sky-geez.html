Sky Geez: Jéré $A, Han Han and the 2023 to 2024 bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Sky Geez has been releasing music since January 2021, first as freestyles and singles, then as songs billed bouyon from 2023 on. The two clearest ones carry the genre in the title: “Jéré $A (Bouyon 2023)” and “Han Han (Bouyon 2024)”. No source we opened says where the artist is from.

From One Man Gang to Jéré $A

The Sky Geez catalogue on Deezer starts on January 13, 2021, with four songs on the same day: “Dame de pique”, “One Man Gang (Freestyle 1)”, “Sanm vou” and “Decoupe”. None of those titles mentions bouyon yet.

It arrives on February 5, 2023, with “Jéré $A (Bouyon 2023)”, distributed by DistroKid under the 4673096 Records DK imprint, alongside another single, “Vrai”. The audio upload has passed 16,000 views on YouTube. Do not mix it up with “Jéré Sa”, the 2026 single by YSN, Edday, DJ Softee and Jixels: that is a different song.

The $ky EP and a Super Smash summer

At the end of April 2023 came the “$ky” EP, six tracks including “Takeoff” and “CV” with Grax. The “Takeoff” video, posted by the Planète Smash channel, credits Sky Geez as artist and writer, Super Smash Filmz as director and Super Smash Music as the label.

The same label put “Mi Chalè” on the “Block Party Summer” compilation, released on August 1, 2023. The audio has passed 880,000 views, by far the highest Sky Geez number, though no listing names the genre of the song. The “Dans Le Bloc” session filmed for it is tagged bouyon and shatta.

Han Han and the recent singles

On November 1, 2024, Sky Geez posted the video for “Han Han (Bouyon 2024)” on the artist's own channel, with the note “me revoilà avec un nouveau titre Bouyon” (back again with a new bouyon song) and the hashtag #itsbouyontimebaby. The riddim is by RTC Beatz, the mix by DJ Leguste and the visuals by Smashfilmz. It has passed 23,000 views, more than any other official Sky Geez video checked for this profile.

Between the two bouyon songs the singles kept coming: “Hey Bitch” in August 2023, “Bad & Boujee” in January 2024, “Kay vé missié” in June 2024. On YouTube, Sky Geez also turns up with Sassoow on “Hum Hum Hum” and with Djocka on “Non Stop”. The latest single listed on Deezer, “Pray (Pay)”, is from July 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sky Geez?

A name from the bouyon scene, releasing since 2021, with two songs billed bouyon: Jéré $A in 2023 and Han Han in 2024. The sources we opened say nothing about the artist's origin.

What are the Sky Geez bouyon songs?

Jéré $A (Bouyon 2023), released in February 2023, and Han Han (Bouyon 2024), a video posted in November 2024.

What is the most played Sky Geez song?

Mi Chalè, on the Super Smash compilation Block Party Summer, with more than 880,000 views for the audio. The listing does not name its genre.

Who worked on Han Han?

RTC Beatz on the riddim, DJ Leguste on the mix and Smashfilmz on the video.

Is Jéré $A the same song as the DJ Boko Jéré Sa?

No. The Sky Geez Jéré $A is from 2023. The Jéré Sa with DJ Boko is a 2026 single by YSN, Edday, DJ Softee and Jixels.
