Bouyon music 2026: WCMF eve guide, kickoff

By Smokiller · Published October 22, 2026

It is Thursday October 22, 2026, the eve of the first night of the World Creole Music Festival. Tomorrow, Friday October 23, the festival's 26th edition opens at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, Dominica, for three nights of Creole music in the heart of Independence season. This page collects what the eve makes it possible to verify: the kickoff time, the forecast weather, access to the island and the experiences around the stadium.

On this page
  1. The kickoff: Friday October 23 at 8 p.m.
  2. The weekend weather: heat, with a possible shower on Saturday night
  3. Getting to Roseau: the weekend's air access
  4. VIP and VVIP: the 2026 premium experience
  5. Frequently asked questions

The kickoff: Friday October 23 at 8 p.m.

Festival Coordinator Kareem Guiste gave the time that organises the whole eve: the teams want everyone to arrive safe and on time, he told BuzzDA, so the festival can kick off at 8 p.m. on October 23. More than 30 acts are expected, and the festival teams are coordinating accommodation, flights and arrival schedules while Roseau fills up.

The Discover Dominica Authority is running a simple campaign in parallel, summed up by Kareem Guiste: come for the vibe and stay for a while. The festival opens Independence season, and night 1, with Capleton, Skillibeng, Klass, TK International and Midnight Groovers, launches the weekend before the big bouyon night on Saturday and the closing night on Sunday.

The weekend weather: heat, with a possible shower on Saturday night

The forecasts read this Thursday October 22 on weather.com for Roseau point to a hot and mostly dry weekend: Friday October 23 partly cloudy, high 88 degrees Fahrenheit, rain chance 20 percent day and night; Saturday October 24 partly cloudy, high 89 degrees, with showers forecast at night and a rain chance of 40 percent; Sunday October 25 partly cloudy, high 89 degrees and a rain chance of 20 percent. The UV index is forecast extreme, at 11, on all three days, and no weather alerts are in force.

Other services do not tell exactly the same story: some see possible patchy rain on Friday and isolated thunderstorms on Saturday. The honest eve reading is therefore this: strong heat by day, warm nights around 79 degrees, and a real possibility of a brief shower, above all on Saturday night. A light rain cover for cameras and microphones, and water for the daytime, remain the two useful precautions.

Getting to Roseau: the weekend's air access

The festival's access work focuses on regional links: the team visited partners in St. Martin, St. Kitts and Anguilla, according to BuzzDA. On the new route side, Writeups24 reports that Contour Airlines opened a nonstop Trinidad to Dominica service on October 5, 2026, twice a week, with Piarco departures on Tuesday and Friday at 8:30 a.m. arriving at 9:50 a.m., which puts a Friday morning flight within reach of festivalgoers from Trinidad.

For visitors already on the ground, the eve is also lived outside the stadium: the last day of Ti Vilaj Kweyol takes place today at the Botanic Gardens, walking distance from Windsor Park, before the festival takes over tomorrow night.

VIP and VVIP: the 2026 premium experience

The 2026 edition adds a hospitality layer around the musical programme: the VIP and VVIP experiences are produced by Rize and Whine, from Dominica, and the Tipsy Music Festival, from Barbados, according to Sweet TNT Magazine and The Caribbean Edit. Both tiers give access to dedicated parking and entrance, a premium viewing area and a premium bar; the VVIP tier adds live chef stations and a strengthened hospitality service, among other features.

The musical programme itself does not change: three nights of R and B, soca, dancehall, reggae, bouyon, cadence-lypso, kompa, soukous and amapiano, according to the official release. This site's guides detail each night: night 1 on Friday October 23, night 2 on Saturday October 24 and night 3 on Sunday October 25.

Frequently asked questions

What time does WCMF 2026 start?

Kickoff is set for 8 p.m. on Friday October 23, 2026, according to Festival Coordinator Kareem Guiste, quoted by BuzzDA. The festival is held at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, Dominica.

What weather is forecast for WCMF 2026?

Forecasts read on October 22 on weather.com point to a hot weekend, 88 to 89 degrees Fahrenheit, partly cloudy, with a 20 percent rain chance on Friday and Sunday and possible showers on Saturday night at a 40 percent chance. Weather services differ on the detail, so light rain protection stays advisable.

What is on in Roseau on the eve of WCMF 2026?

The last day of Ti Vilaj Kweyol takes place on Thursday October 22 at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, from noon to 8 p.m., with Asa Bantan and Shelly with Signal Band headlining the finale, walking distance from Windsor Park.
