Que (Que767): Brace You and Hard Fuxk, bouyon 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Que, who releases music as Que767 on Deezer, has two known bouyon songs so far, as writer and as voice. “Brace You”, with TooYung, came out at the end of October 2025 under the Bouyon 2026 tag. “Hard Fuxk”, co-written with Dboss, followed in September 2026 with a video billed Bouyon 2027. Both go through the same team, Bad Producer Ent.

Brace You with TooYung

The single “Brace You (feat. TooYung)” reached Deezer on October 26, 2025 on Knockin Records, credited to Que767 with TooYung as guest; it is still the only release on that artist page. Four days later the DbossRiddimz channel posted the official audio as “Que X TooYung - Brace You [Bouyon 2026]”.

The description spells out the team: production by Bad Producer Ent. and Knockin Records, riddim by Dbossriddimz and Noface Beatz, mix and master by XavvoKnockin. The lyrics are by Que and TooYung. The page had just over 1,300 views when read.

Hard Fuxk with Dboss

On September 4, 2026, the DJTaffy Tha Riddim Beast channel uploaded “Dboss & Que - Hard Fuxk [Official Music Video] (Bouyon 2027)”. The song is produced by Bad Producer Ent., the riddim is by Prodbyblxze, and Yw3 Ent (DiYoute) handled recording, mixing and mastering. The video was shot by Eternal Media and OfficialStickmanmedia.

The credits give the lyrics to Dboss and Que, while the performance line names only Dboss; the video title shows both names. Past 6,700 views when read, it is Que's most watched page.

What is known, and what is not

The two songs share one circle: Bad Producer Ent. on production, Dboss on riddim or lyrics, and Noface Beatz, credited on “Brace You” and tagged as @nofacebeatz767 on “Hard Fuxk”. The number 767, also found in the name Que767, is Dominica's dialling code, and the keywords on both videos mention the island.

Still, no page opened gives a biography, a real name or a home island, and we found no personal channel under the name. This profile is short on purpose; it rests on the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 29, 2026.
