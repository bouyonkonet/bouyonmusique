TMX Official: Bouyon Zouk, WOY and shatta remixes

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

TMX, who releases music as TMX Official, is a DJ and producer known for shatta, ragga and afro remixes. Bouyon has a smaller but real place in his catalogue: “Bouyon Zouk” at the end of 2023, then “WOY (Bouyon)” on the album “Carnavibes” in 2024.

Remixes that travel

His first release goes back to March 2020 with “Tarrafro”. Ragga reworks followed, such as “Home T” and “Coca”, then a long run of shatta versions. On his channel, the shatta remix of “Talk To Me Nuh”, by Vybz Kartel and Shenseea, posted with a “Feat TMX” credit, is close to half a million views.

In August 2026, his shatta remix of “Slick”, credited “Prod : TMX” and made for TikTok, neared a million views within weeks. He also releases songs under his own name, such as “Fé Roulé” with Mister Ramsy, Abdoul and LA2B, released in July 2024.

Bouyon Zouk and Carnavibes

On December 3, 2023 he released the single “Bouyon Zouk”. The title spells out the blend, and the YouTube upload is past 3,300 views. It was his first release to carry bouyon in its name.

Six months later, the album “Carnavibes” brought back “Bouyon Zouk” and added “WOY (Bouyon)”. The rest of the record, seven tracks in all, leans afro: “Afro Boucan”, “Afro Warning”, “Joga Mindu” and a piano version of “AYAN”.

Behind the decks

In February 2026, DJ MG invited him onto the first episode of “DJ MG & Friends”, a set filmed on Réunion Island and billed as dancehall, shatta, bouyon and afro. The video is past 31,000 views. TMX works in his own mash-ups, including “Bam Bam x Ton Mec”.

The joint set closes on “Fé Roulé”. Since then he has kept releasing singles at a steady pace, “Body” in September 2026, then “Malaxa” and “Lébon” in October.

Frequently asked questions

Who is TMX Official?

A DJ and producer, also called TMX or DJ TMX, known for shatta and afro remixes and for a few bouyon songs.

What are his bouyon songs?

Bouyon Zouk, released in December 2023, and WOY (Bouyon), on the Carnavibes album from June 2024.

What is his most watched track?

His shatta remix of Slick, posted in August 2026, is close to a million views.

Has he played DJ sets?

Yes, he is the guest on the first episode of DJ MG & Friends, a set filmed on Réunion Island in 2026.

Where is TMX from?

His releases do not say. The set with DJ MG was filmed on Réunion, which does not tell us where he is from.
