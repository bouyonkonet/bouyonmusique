Pslbeat

Published October 20, 2026

Pslbeat is a riddim maker whose name so far fits into two titles, and two titles that say the same thing: bouyon. The first, “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”, was released as a single on February 8, 2024 on his own label, Pslbeat, with a songwriter credit to PETER Pascal and a listed tempo of 155 BPM on its Shazam page. The second, “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026”, is presented on YouTube as a 2026 riddim available on BeatStars. This profile is deliberately short and honest: no biography, no interview and no verified origin were found. The French titles suggest the French Caribbean circuit, but this site assigns no island to an artist without an opened source that names it.

Two riddims, and nothing else public for now

The public file on Pslbeat fits into two pages opened during this research, and this profile claims nothing more. The first is the Shazam page for “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”: a single, released on February 8, 2024, issued on the Pslbeat label, so self released at least in its declared form. The page gives a tempo of 155 BPM, in the usual zone of the bouyon riddim, and a single writing credit, PETER Pascal, songwriter of the track. Whether PETER Pascal is Pslbeat full name is a plausible reading, but no opened source says so: this profile quotes the credit as it stands, without turning it into biography.

The second page is the YouTube video “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026 PSLBEAT”. Its description is short and commercial: the riddim is available on BeatStars, with a purchase link, and an invitation to contact the artist for more information. That is the classic model of the riddim beatmaker: the instrumental is the product, offered to singers and DJs who might voice it, rather than a finished song carried by a vocal. No full artist page, no official account confirmed by direct opening and no interview were found to complete this portrait.

What the two titles say about positioning

The two titles share a naming choice that amounts to positioning. “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)” puts the word bouyon in brackets, as a genre tag readable by platforms and by DJs searching for a riddim by style. “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026” does the opposite: bouyon becomes a taste, a flavour claimed in the title itself, in French, with the year as a vintage. In both cases Pslbeat does not hide his category: he sells a sound, instrumental bouyon, at 155 BPM for the first documented title.

The language of the titles is the only circuit clue this profile has, and it is treated as a clue, not as proof. The French of “GOÛT BOUYON” and the form of “PITAYU” bring these riddims close to the French speaking audience that consumes bouyon in Guadeloupe, Martinique and the diaspora, the same audience our bouyon playlist addresses. But a French title gives neither a birth island nor a home town: until an opened source names Pslbeat origin, this page leaves it blank, rather than filling it in by deduction.

The limits of the file, stated

This profile ends with its limits, because they are the most useful information it can give about Pslbeat today. No biography was found. No origin is named in the opened sources: not Dominica, not Guadeloupe, not Martinique, not any other island. No title other than the two riddims documented here was verified on the platforms consulted, and no official link is therefore entered in the structured data of this page, beyond the two releases themselves.

If Pslbeat publishes an artist page, a biography or a new riddim with full credits, this page will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. In the meantime, the listening fits into two instrumentals: “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”, the 2024 single, and “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026”, the riddim offered on BeatStars. Two short titles, one tempo, one songwriter credit, and an origin question left open: that is little, and it is exactly what the verifiable file contains on October 20, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Pslbeat?

Pslbeat is a riddim maker known through two verified bouyon riddims: “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”, released as a single on February 8, 2024 on the Pslbeat label, and “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026”, presented on YouTube and available on BeatStars. No public biography was found during this research.

Where is Pslbeat from?

His origin is named in none of the sources opened during this research. The French titles of his riddims suggest the French Caribbean circuit, but this site assigns Pslbeat no island without a source that names it. The question stays open and the page will be completed if a source settles it.

Which Pslbeat tracks are verified?

Two tracks, whose primary pages were opened: “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”, single of February 8, 2024, label Pslbeat, 155 BPM, songwriter credit to PETER Pascal; and “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026”, a riddim presented on YouTube with a BeatStars link. No other title was verified.

Who is PETER Pascal?

PETER Pascal is the name credited as songwriter on the Shazam page for “PITAYU RIDDIM (Bouyon)”. No opened source states whether it is Pslbeat full name or a collaborator. This profile quotes the credit as it stands, without drawing a biographical conclusion from it.

Where can a Pslbeat riddim be bought or used?

The description of the YouTube video “GOÛT BOUYON RIDDIM 2026 PSLBEAT” states that the riddim is available on BeatStars, with a purchase link, and invites contact with the artist for more information. That is the only contact route documented in the opened sources.

Why is this profile so short?

Because the verifiable file is short: two opened releases, one songwriter credit, one tempo, and no documented biography or origin. This site prefers a short and exact profile to a portrait filled with deductions. It will grow if the artist publishes more primary sources.
