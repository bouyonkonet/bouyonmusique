The name misunderstanding, and why it must be corrected

The Gwada G case is a standing lesson for Bouyon Shattaz: names travel, but credit must stay accurate. A stage name can suggest another island, a style, a nickname or a personal story without defining an artist's nationality. When a site, a poster or a media outlet takes that name and deduces a Guadeloupean origin, the error then repeats everywhere, in search engines and festival introductions alike.

The source that settles it here is official. The media release from the Discover Dominica Authority, the body behind the WCMF, lists Gwada G among Dominica's homegrown acts. Homegrown means from home, formed on the ground, presented as a talent of the island. Against a source at that level, a deduction drawn from a stage name carries no weight. This page therefore follows the official source and calls Gwada G Dominican, full stop.

This is not a vocabulary detail. The origin thesis Bouyon Shattaz defends is that Dominica created bouyon and must receive its credit, bookings and revenue. If even Dominican artists get relabelled toward neighbouring islands because of their names, the leak of credit becomes automatic. Correcting the record on Gwada G, calmly and with the source, is exactly the work this site exists to do.

An artist of Dominica's new generation

A full interview was billed for September 30, 2026 on the Love Bouyon Music YouTube channel. New generation does not mean without roots. In Dominica, young bouyon artists grow up with the island's classics, carnival seasons, sound systems and tracks that circulate from phone to phone before they ever see a clean release. Being spotted by Bouyon Talks and held in the festival's official communication places Gwada G in the group of young names that the scene's institutions and media are already watching closely.

His documented profile rests on three verifiable pillars: the official DDA source, the Love Bouyon billing and his published WCMF 2026 booking. What his path says about bouyon today

Gwada G shows how Dominican bouyon works in 2026. A specialised platform like Love Bouyon spots and interviews, the festival's official authority classifies and books, guides publish the nights, and the public often discovers the name before there is a full discography to hear. In that system, the quality of information becomes almost as important as the music itself: a wrong origin label can follow an artist for years.

He also shows the permanent closeness between Dominica and Guadeloupe in this music. The two islands share a Creole language, audiences, producers and stages, to the point where a Dominican name can sound Guadeloupean and the reverse. That closeness is a strength for bouyon's circulation, as long as it does not erase origins. Bouyon was born in Dominica: saying so for each artist, with the right source, maintains the memory that lets the island benefit from its own creation.

What comes next for Gwada G will need documenting like the beginning: through tracks when they are published with their credits, stages when organisers announce them and interviews when they appear. Until then, this profile gives the reader exactly what is established, no more and no less, plus a clear way in: WCMF 2026 Night 3, the @gwada_g account and the playlists where the new generation of Dominican bouyon can be discovered in music.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Gwada G?

Gwada G is a bouyon artist from Dominica, presented by Love Bouyon as a voice of the new Dominican generation. The Discover Dominica Authority release counts him among the island's homegrown acts for WCMF 2026, where he plays Night 3, on October 25. His name travels mostly through collaborations, with DJ MJ, DJ Taffy or BRG Hollywood.

Is Gwada G from Guadeloupe?

No. Despite a name that evokes Guadeloupe, Gwada G is an artist from Dominica: the official Discover Dominica Authority media release for WCMF 2026 lists him among the island's homegrown acts. A stage name can point to another island without defining an artist's origin, and the official source outweighs any guess drawn from a pseudonym.

What is Gwada G's real name?

The only full name published in this site's verified sources is a songwriting credit: on the published page for “You Can Do What You Want”, by DJ MJ, the writing is shared between Paul DJMJ Jolly and Kurt Gwada G Douglas. This site reports it exactly as printed, as a published credit, without adding any biography.

What are Gwada G's songs?

His tracks verified on the platforms are mostly features: “On your back” with DJ Taffy (2023), “Plap Plap !” with Nice and Freddy (2023), “WTHelly Bouyon” with BRG Hollywood (2025) and “You Can Do What You Want” with DJ MJ (2026). Other titles, such as “What Name” with Kenny G and Dova, circulate in bouyon mix tracklists.

Who produces Gwada G's tracks?

The published credits name several producers. “You Can Do What You Want”, with DJ MJ, is produced by ThaWizzard, with Mv On De Keys on piano. “WTHelly Bouyon”, with BRG Hollywood, is produced by Xavvoknockin and Xavern Labega, recorded by Knockin Records. “On your back” came out in 2023 in DJ Taffy's catalogue, on the Riddim Beast Studios label.

Who has Gwada G collaborated with?

His documented collaborations draw a real network: DJ MJ and MV on the Keys, DJ Taffy, BRG Hollywood, Kenny G and Dova on “What Name”, Erwinchy on “Make A Woman Cum”, and Litleboy, Trixx, Freddy and Niice on the “Nasty Freestyle Medley”. Gwada G moves mostly through duets and shared tracks. See also Dova's profile.

Who sings on “You Can Do What You Want” with DJ MJ?

Gwada G. According to its Shazam page, “You Can Do What You Want”, released on January 24, 2026 on the DJMJ ANYWAY label at 160 BPM, credits vocals to Paul DJMJ Jolly, vocal harmonies to Gwada G and piano to Mv On De Keys, with the writing shared between the two artists. See DJ MJ's profile.
