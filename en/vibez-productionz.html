Vibez Productionz: Start D Party and bouyon riddims

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Vibez Productionz is a production outfit that releases its riddims as group projects, several singers on one instrumental. Its credits cluster around the Saint Lucia carnival season, in soca at first, then in bouyon in 2024 with Imran Nerdy's “Start D Party” and the Jeremie Street Riddim.

A riddim workshop

The Vibez Productionz catalog on Deezer reads like a carnival calendar: Sa Ka Fèt Riddim and Merry Makers Riddim in spring 2023, Caye Manjé Riddim in June, French Toast Riddim in December. Each project gathers several voices on one instrumental, and the series runs all the way to the Goodie Pack Riddim of April 2026.

On “Bwa Bandé”, sung by Motto on the Caye Manjé Riddim, the description names the producer as Miguel “Migz” Joseph of Vibez Productionz. The same nickname shows up in the handle credited on “Start D Party”. The “Bwa Bandé” video is past 20,000 views, and the video for “Balance”, with Imran Nerdy and Ricky T on the same riddim, is past 200,000.

Start D Party, the turn to bouyon

In March 2024, Imran Nerdy released “Start D Party”, billed Imran Nerdy X Vibez Productionz with the tag “2024 Bouyon”. The description calls it an EDM and bouyon fusion for St. Lucia Carnival 2024, produced by Vibez Production, mixed and mastered by Yannik Plante.

It is their most watched collaboration: more than 234,000 views on the singer's channel. The song then came out as a Deezer single in May 2024, on the Vibez Productionz label, with both names credited side by side.

Jeremie Street Riddim and the team around it

On May 21, 2024, Vibez Productionz presented the Jeremie Street Riddim, five songs and an instrumental: “I Like What I Like” by DYP, “More Rum” by Sedale, Mata and Bronxx, “Cool Down” by Imran Nerdy, “Side Work” by Jiggy and “Money Never Fight” by Orion. “Cool Down” is billed “Bouyon 2024” and is past 15,000 views, tagged #Bouyon, #Dominica and #Gwada.

The same names come back from one project to the next. Yannick Plante mixes and masters most of the songs, Zack Popo plays guitar and Dean Benn bass on the Jeremie Street Riddim, and Claude Jonas plays bass on “Bwa Bandé”. On the Ti Marguerite Riddim, Ezra D' Funmachine's “Sélibwé” is past 10,000 views, with Stephen Mathurin on keys.

Frequently asked questions

What is Vibez Productionz?

A production outfit that releases group riddim projects tied to the Saint Lucia carnival season, in soca and, from 2024, in bouyon.

What is its best known bouyon song?

Start D Party with Imran Nerdy, billed as bouyon 2024, with more than 234,000 views on YouTube.

Who is behind Vibez Productionz?

The credits for Bwa Bandé, with Motto, name Miguel “Migz” Joseph of Vibez Productionz as producer.

Who is on the Jeremie Street Riddim?

DYP, Sedale with Mata and Bronxx, Imran Nerdy, Jiggy and Orion, on a project released May 21, 2024.

Who mixes its productions?

Yannick Plante mixes and masters most of the songs checked, from Bwa Bandé to Start D Party and the Jeremie Street Riddim.
