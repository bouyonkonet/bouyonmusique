Bouyon in Dominica: where it all began

Published September 16, 2026

Dominica is the country of bouyon. It was in Roseau, its capital, that WCK created the genre in 1988, and it is in its villages and its carnival that the sound continues to live in its raw state.

Roseau and carnival

Dominica’s carnival, Mas Domnik, is the home of bouyon: live bands and sound systems play the tracks there that will become the classics of the year. Grand Bay, the village of Asa Bantan, the Bouyon Boss, is one of the strongholds of this culture.

A small island, a world invention

With fewer than 70,000 inhabitants in its modern history, Dominica invented two genres that have traveled the whole world over: cadence-lypso and then bouyon. Defending that origin is the heart of Bouyon Shattaz’s work and of this site.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
