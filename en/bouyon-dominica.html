Bouyon in Dominica: where it all began

Published September 16, 2026

Dominica is the country of bouyon. It was in Roseau, its capital, that WCK created the genre in 1988, and it is in its villages and its carnival that the sound continues to live in its raw state.

Roseau and carnival

Dominica’s carnival, Mas Domnik, is the home of bouyon: live bands and sound systems play the tracks there that will become the classics of the year. Grand Bay, the village of Asa Bantan, identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals, is one of the strongholds of this culture.

A small island, a world invention

With fewer than 70,000 inhabitants in its modern history, Dominica invented two genres that have traveled the whole world over: cadence-lypso and then bouyon. Defending that origin is the heart of Bouyon Shattaz’s work and of this site.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique, where it is also called jump up, and present in France, Saint Lucia, Antigua, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora, from New York to London, Toronto and Montreal. Its center of gravity remains Dominica, where it has been the music of carnival since the 1990s.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s: it was in Roseau, the island's capital, that the band WCK created it in 1988. Bouyon is still the sound of Roseau carnival, Mas Domnik, where live bands and sound systems launch the tracks that become each year's classics.

Who created bouyon in Dominica?

Bouyon was created by WCK (Windward Caribbean Kulture), a band formed in Roseau, the capital of Dominica, in 1988. WCK fused cadence-lypso and jing ping with electronic keyboards and drum machines, and gave birth to the fast carnival sound now called bouyon.

Where can you hear bouyon in Dominica?

The best place is Dominica's carnival, Mas Domnik, in Roseau: live bands and sound systems play the tracks there that become the classics of the year. Grand Bay, the village of Asa Bantan, identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals, is also one of the strongholds of bouyon culture on the island.

Which music genres did Dominica invent?

Dominica invented two genres that have traveled the whole world: cadence-lypso, then bouyon, created by WCK in 1988. That is remarkable for an island with fewer than 70,000 inhabitants in its modern history, and it is why this site defends the Dominican origin of bouyon.

Is bouyon from Dominica or Guadeloupe?

Dominica. Bouyon was created in Roseau in 1988 by WCK, then crossed the sea to Guadeloupe thanks to the shared Creole language. Guadeloupe danced it as jump up and drew its own branch from it, bouyon gwada. TIITII NBA, a Guadeloupean artist, says in substance that bouyon belongs to Dominica and that Guadeloupe is its second chapter.

When is Dominica's carnival?

Dominica's carnival, Mas Domnik, is held every year in Roseau before Lent, with its high points on Carnival Monday and Tuesday, preceded by J'ouvert at dawn, a peak moment for bouyon and soca. The parties and sound systems of the previous weeks launch the year's bouyon tracks.

What is the World Creole Music Festival?

It is Dominica's big music event: three nights of Creole music at Windsor Park stadium, in Roseau, every year in October, the month of Dominica's independence. The 2026 edition runs from October 23 to 25, with Asa Bantan and Reo among others on Saturday the 24th, according to the published guides. See the World Creole Music Festival.

Who are Dominica's bouyon artists today?

Among the names this site follows: Asa Bantan, from Grand Bay, widely called the King of Bouyon, Reo, presented as the Prince of Bouyon by 13th Street Promotions, Ridge, Shelly, Signal Band and Trilla-G. The genre also keeps its founding bands: WCK, Roots, Stems and Branches (RSB) and First Serenade. See the bouyon artists of Dominica.

Which bouyon styles were born in Dominica?

Several. In the 1990s, when bouyon vocals opened up to Jamaican dancehall and ragga, bouyon-muffin and Wrecketeng were born, the latter built on sampled melodies and rap vocals. In 2003, Dada launched bouyon soca in Dominica, according to the account of Sean "Smokiller" Francis. Bouyon gwada was born from collaborations with Guadeloupe.

Reketeng or Wrecketeng: which spelling is correct?

Wrecketeng. It is the official spelling, handed down by DJ Cut, the pioneer of the style in Dominica. The form "reketeng", very widespread online, is a misspelling. Wrecketeng is the Dominican practice of rebuilding existing recordings over bouyon instrumentals, in the line of bouyon-muffin, and it is cited as a root of modern bouyon.

What language is bouyon sung in, in Dominica?

In Kwéyòl, Dominica's Creole language, mixed with English. This language carries the calls, the jokes, the social commentary and the energy of chanté mas, like the language of carnival, whose very name, Mas Domnik, is Creole. Dominican Kwéyòl is close to the neighboring Creoles, which helped bouyon travel. See bouyon and Kwéyòl.
