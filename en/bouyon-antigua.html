Bouyon in Antigua: spreading toward the Leewards

Published October 2, 2026

Antigua is one of the islands where bouyon and its fusion with soca have taken root for good. Its carnival and its sound systems relay the sounds of Dominica toward the Leeward Islands.

An audience of connoisseurs

Like Saint Lucia, Antigua helped shape bouyon soca by testing it against the codes of soca and local parties. This shared history is a reminder that bouyon is a Caribbean genre, with a clear center of gravity: Dominica.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
