Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique, where audiences also call it jump up. It is present in France, Saint Lucia, Antigua, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora, from New York to London, Toronto and Montreal. In the Leeward Islands, Antigua is one of the relays of the Dominican sound.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born in Roseau in the late 1980s, when the band WCK, formed in 1988, fused cadence-lypso with jing ping and traditional drums such as the lapo kabwit. It remains the sound of Roseau carnival, Mas Domnik.

Is bouyon popular in Antigua?

Yes. Antigua is one of the islands where bouyon and its fusion with soca have taken root for good. Its carnival and sound systems relay the sounds of Dominica toward the Leeward Islands, and bouyon and soca have circulated there since the 1980s and 1990s, as in Saint Lucia.

What role did Antigua play in bouyon soca?

Like Saint Lucia, Antigua helped shape bouyon soca by testing it against the codes of soca and local parties. The style itself was launched in Dominica by Dada in 2003, according to Smokiller's testimony, before it developed on these islands. See the bouyon soca page.

What is bouyon soca?

Bouyon soca is the most popular fusion born around bouyon: the fast rhythms and keyboards of Dominican bouyon meet the energy and codes of Trinidad soca. Built for the road, the bands and the sound systems, it travels from island to island during carnival season, all the way to Antigua.

Which bouyon song was a hit in Antigua?

“Whistle While You Work” by Triple Kay is the record this site documents on that point. Presented by the band as a bouyon 2024 release, it is described by its official site as topping several regional charts, in Antigua among others. The official credits name Kendel “Killa” Laurent as riddim producer. See the Killa Laurent profile.

Who produced Romel's “Forks Up”?

Kendel “Killa” Laurent, a producer from Dominica, according to the official credits. This 2019 soca record by Romel, a soca artist from Antigua, was produced and mixed by Killa Laurent at Music Box Studio, in Dominica, with mastering by Cornel “Fingers” Philip of Imperial Publishing. The credit shows a Dominican studio serving an Antigua voice, inside the regional carnival circuit.

Does Triple Kay play Antigua carnival?

Yes, according to the band's official site. TK International, also known as Triple Kay, a bouyon band from Dominica, lists the carnivals of Antigua, St. Thomas, St. Croix and St. Martin among its regular stages, alongside Toronto Caribana and Notting Hill Carnival. It is one of the routes by which Dominican bouyon reaches Antigua. See TK International.

Which other Dominica artists have performed in Antigua?

Daddy Chess, among others. His profile published on ItzCaribbean has him performing across the Caribbean, from the Virgin Islands to St. Lucia, Trinidad, Barbados and Antigua. Born Chester Letang, the voice of the Swinging Stars Orchestra, he is a calypso and soca artist who shares the carnival stages of bouyon. See Daddy Chess.

Is Tizzy, the Antigua singer, a bouyon artist?

No. Tizzy, real name Tanzania Sebastian, is a soca singer from Antigua, a voice of the El A Kru band, known for “Expose”. Her name is spelled with an i and she has no bouyon profile. She should not be confused with Tazzy, nor with Tazzo Thaboss, an artist from Dominica: these are three distinct names. See the Tazzy page.
