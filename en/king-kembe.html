King Kembe: Sint Maarten bouyon, from Just Horn Him to Loca

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

King Kembe, also billed as DJ King Kembe, is a DJ and singer who represents Sint Maarten. First known for island party songs, he put out bouyon as early as 2019 and since 2025 has stacked up collaborations with the Knockin Records circle and Dominican voices.

Island: 🇲🇫🇸🇽 Saint Martin / Sint Maarten · All islands

Sint Maarten party songs

In 2017, “OK Good”, with the Tolly Boys on a D'Mitri production, passed 278,000 views. “Dance Like Maggie”, with King Rumer and YoungOne, went higher still, and “GEBE Wuk Up”, on the El Pepe Riddim, is past 180,000. His name also turns up with Ciio on “Get on Bad” and with Wizito on “She A Bawl For Di Chook”.

In July 2018, The Daily Herald listed him among the three acts Sint Maarten sent to the first Power Soca Monarch in Statia, alongside Junior Lion and Ebony Empress. Junior Lion took the crown; King Kembe's placing is not given.

Just Horn Him and Bless Me

The first song billed bouyon on his channel dates from January 2019: “Just Horn Him”, on the Simon Say Riddim, tagged “Bouyon [Sint-Maarten]”. Production is credited to Lashley “Motto” Winter, also known as Motto.

At the end of 2024 he came back with “Bless Me”, billed as bouyon 2025. He wrote it with the engineer Dizzel Beuno, recorded it at Great Music Studio in SXM, and the riddim comes from Fr3EzE. He has also worked with DJ Luchshiy on “Its Pouring (In Di Wata)”.

The 2025 and 2026 collaborations

In June 2025 he guested on “Up” by General Pye, produced by Xavvoknockin. At the end of January 2026, “No Feelings” paired him with Faithii, Dominica's Bouyon Barbie; the credits read “Genre: Bouyon” and “Culture: Sint Maarten x Dominica”. It is his most watched bouyon song, with more than 14,000 views.

March brought “Work Out” with Th3rd, its credits pairing Sint Maarten with the US Virgin Islands, then “Loca” with Tweny Montana at the end of September. He still DJs: his J'ouvert 2026 mix for Dutty Dawgs Splash Commandos is past 36,000 views, and DJ Eyedol names him in the Bouyon Cauldron mixtape. More context on the bouyon in Saint Martin page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is King Kembe?

A DJ and singer who represents Sint Maarten, active since at least 2017 and in bouyon since 2019.

What is his first bouyon song?

Just Horn Him, on the Simon Say Riddim, posted in January 2019 and produced by Lashley Motto Winter.

Who has he sung bouyon with?

General Pye on Up, Faithii on No Feelings, Th3rd on Work Out and Tweny Montana on Loca.

Who produces his recent songs?

Xavvoknockin, or Xav Knockin, on Up, No Feelings and Work Out; Fr3EzE on Bless Me. Dizzel Beuno is credited as engineer.

Has he entered a competition?

Yes, The Daily Herald wrote in 2018 that he represented Sint Maarten at the first Power Soca Monarch in Statia.
