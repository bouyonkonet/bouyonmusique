Bouyon artists: the pioneers and the new wave

From the band that invented the genre in 1988 to the producers making today's riddims, here are the names that matter in bouyon music.

The pioneers of Dominica

WCK (Windward Caribbean Kulture)

The band that created bouyon. Formed in 1988 in Roseau, Dominica, WCK fused cadence-lypso and jing ping with electronic keyboards to invent an entire genre.

Roots, Stems and Branches (RSB)

One of the original groups of Dominican bouyon, a player in the first wave that established the genre as the sound of Dominica's carnival.

First Serenade

Another founding name of bouyon, counted among the groups that defined the original sound at the end of the 1980s.

The figures of modern bouyon

Asa Bantan

Artist from Grand Bay, Dominica, nicknamed King of Bouyon and identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals. A central figure of Dominican carnival and of today's bouyon scene.

Reo

Dominican artist nicknamed the Prince of Bouyon, nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026. He represents the new generation pushing bouyon toward the French Antilles, as on Joli BB with Martinican singer Maurane Voyer.

Smokiller

Bouyon artist and producer, the engine behind the Bouyon Shattaz bouyon remixes, flipping Afro, pop and rap songs into bouyon versions, like Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) for Klass Money.

The producers behind the sound

Modern bouyon is also made in the studio, by producers that Bouyon Shattaz follows daily: HYPNO (@hypnoticbeatz), Savion (@prod.bysavion), Denmath (producer from Saint-Martin based between Nantes and Paris, behind the riddims Tropical Riddim and Harley Riddim), and MB On The Track. Their riddims feed the bouyon dance and the playlists of the moment.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the most famous bouyon artists?

On the pioneer side, the band WCK, which created the genre in 1988, with Roots, Stems and Branches (RSB) and First Serenade. On the modern side, Asa Bantan, nicknamed King of Bouyon and identified as the Bouyon Boss by Dominica Festivals, and Reo, presented as the Prince of Bouyon. Behind them, producers like Smokiller, HYPNO, Savion, Denmath and MB On The Track. The essentials: the best of bouyon.

Which band invented bouyon?

Bouyon was invented by WCK, Windward Caribbean Kulture, a band formed in 1988 in Roseau, Dominica. WCK fused cadence-lypso and jing ping with electronic keyboards and drum machines. In the 1990s, its bouyon became the music of Roseau carnival and of village fetes. Full profile on the WCK page.

Why is Asa Bantan called the King of Bouyon?

Asa Bantan, whose real name is Asa Edwards, comes from Grand Bay, Dominica. King of Bouyon is a widely used nickname for his central place in modern Dominican bouyon, and Dominica Festivals has identified him as the Bouyon Boss. A pillar of Mas Domnik carnival, he took Life Nice to the top of a tracked radio panel. See the Asa Bantan page.

Who is Reo, the Prince of Bouyon?

Reo is a Dominican bouyon artist, real name Dario Yankey, presented as the Prince of Bouyon by 13th Street Promotions. Nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, he released Joli BB on 9 October 2026 with Martinican singer Maurane Voyer. He embodies the new generation building its career with the French Antilles. See the Reo page.

Which producers make the sound of modern bouyon?

Bouyon Shattaz follows HYPNO, who claims Mauvais Plan at 160 bpm, Savion, Denmath, a producer from Saint-Martin based between Nantes and Paris (Tropical Riddim, Harley Riddim), MB On The Track, who dances on his own productions, and Smokiller, the home of bouyon remixes. These producers often launch their riddims on video. See the bouyon producers page.

Who are the bouyon artists from Dominica?

The Dominican roster starts with WCK, creator of the genre in 1988, and the cadence-lypso heritage of Midnight Groovers. Then come Asa Bantan, widely called the King of Bouyon, Reo and Ridge, nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, where Ridge won Bouyon Artist of the Year, the bands Signal Band and TK International, the voices of Shelly and Colton T, and the next wave with BONNA and Ras B. The full roster: artists from Dominica.

Which bouyon artists are from Guadeloupe?

Among the Guadeloupean profiles followed by Bouyon Shattaz: Miimii KDS, a breakout artist credited on the gold single "Pow Pow / Allo Doudou", TIITII NBA, from Morne-à-l'Eau, whose name stands for New Bouyon Age, and Ricky Bishop, of Guadeloupean and Malagasy origin, based in Bordeaux, who presents himself as the creator of the New Bouyon concept. Guadeloupe received bouyon from Dominica before turning it into bouyon gwada. See artists from Guadeloupe.

Which bouyon artists are from Martinique?

The reference Martinican profile is Maurane Voyer, a singer from Martinique, author of the album "BBW" in 2022 and the voice of "Joli BB" with Dominican artist Reo, released on 9 October 2026. This site also flags Martinican artist Koko Bwa and his song "Tarif", with Denmath and Boutcha Bwa, released in April 2025. The hub only publishes verified profiles. See artists from Martinique.

Which female bouyon singers should I know?

Maurane Voyer, from Martinique, the voice of "Joli BB" with Reo. Miimii KDS, from Guadeloupe, viral with "Sé Miimii" and credited on a SNEP gold single. Tamillia, who grew up in Saint-Martin, on "Don't Care" by DJ Blax, and Ebony Empress, the voice of the Bounce 2D Riddim. See women of bouyon.

Which producer started bouyon soca?

Dada, a producer from Dominica. According to the first-hand testimony of Sean "Smokiller" Francis, who was in the studios with Dada and the Nursery Krew, Dada coined the term bouyon soca and launched the style in Dominica in 2003. The style took off when Triple Kay and other bands adopted it. The press also credits him with "Famalay" and "Conch Shell". See Dada.

Who are the pioneers of Wrecketeng?

The Dominican DJ and producer DJ Cut, of Bucktown Sounds, is recognized among the early pioneers of Wrecketeng, with the song "Party" featuring Joanne. The Bushtown Clan then popularized the style, and the Nursery Krew Inc. developed it in the early 2000s through original recordings, with artists like Burn Brain Jolly, Charlie Black, Watty and Smokiller. See DJ Cut.

Reketeng or Wrecketeng: which spelling is right?

Wrecketeng. According to Sean "Smokiller" Francis, founder of Bouyon Shattaz and a participant in the Nursery Krew era, this official spelling comes from DJ Cut himself, the pioneer of the style. The form "reketeng", widespread online and in many mix titles, is a misspelling that became common. This site writes Wrecketeng everywhere, including for the historical practice. See the Wrecketeng page.

Who is Smokiller, the producer behind the bouyon remixes?

Smokiller is the artist and producer brand of Sean "Smokiller" Francis, born in Dominica and founder of Bouyon Shattaz. He flips Afro, pop, rap and R and B hits into bouyon versions, like Nothing Into Something by Coi Leray, which the artist reshared, or Heaven Baby by Ayra Starr, and he produced Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) for Klass Money. See Smokiller.

Which bouyon artists have gone gold in France?

Among the songs documented on this site: "Kongolese sous BBL" by Theodora, the genre's first gold certification in December 2024 according to the reference article on bouyon, then "Je l'ai vu" by the Guadeloupean trio HollyG with Arendi, certified in July 2025. "Pow Pow / Allo Doudou", by Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS, is a SNEP Single Or at 15 million stream equivalents. See bouyon in France.

Is Gwada G from Guadeloupe?

No. Despite his name, Gwada G is a Dominican bouyon artist: the official media release of the Discover Dominica Authority for WCMF 2026 lists him among Dominica's homegrown artists. His case shows why this site checks every origin instead of guessing it from a stage name, in a genre that travels between islands with similar names. See Gwada G.
