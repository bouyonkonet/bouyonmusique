Clint H: a WCK voice in bouyon, from 2006 to 2020

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Clint H is a singer on Dominica's bouyon scene, one of WCK's lead voices on the 2006 album Calling. He later turns up on his own as a 2017 Bouyon Monarch finalist, then in 2020 with “Bouyon To The World” and a road mix cut with his band.

Singing in WCK

The credits of “All Die Hards”, from WCK's 2006 album Calling, reposted by Love Bouyon Music in 2018, list three lead voices: Derek “Rah” Peters, Clint H and Neijel Jno-Baptiste. They also name Keith Goddard on bass, Pellam Jno-Baptiste on drums and Earlson Matthew on keyboards and drum machine.

The same album has “Kallalou”, which the same channel files under “Throwback Bouyon”. An older track, “Who You Rolling Wid”, is dated 2004 in one upload. These are the earliest traces of his voice in the sources we checked.

2017 Bouyon Monarch finalist

In February 2017, Soca News published the finalists for the second Bouyon Monarch at Carnival City in Roseau. Clint H is on the list, with Soca Johnny, Fetty Mark and reigning monarch Carlyn XP. The final placings do not appear in the sources we checked.

The next year he sang “Code Red”, posted as “2018 Bouyon” on the Mal Kase Riddim, a riddim produced by Earlson Matthew. For the setting of these contests, see our Dominica Carnival page.

Bouyon To The World, 2020

On February 20, 2020, two releases landed on the same day. “Bouyon To The World (Remix)”, produced by Alex Shillingford and Clint H, keeps the home sounds: Keith Goddard's bass and David “Galleh” Benjamin on conch shell. On his own channel, the “Clint H x WCK Ultimate Road Mix 2020 Bouyon” was written with iDada, over TewaBeatz riddims, with WCK on backing vocals.

The same season also gave “Jouvert” with WCK and a take on “Time” over an Effex Band refix. None of these is past 2,000 views on YouTube: Clint H's music lives mostly on the road at carnival.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Clint H?

A singer on Dominica's bouyon scene, a WCK lead voice on the 2006 album Calling.

Which band did he sing with?

WCK, alongside Derek Rah Peters and Neijel Jno-Baptiste according to the All Die Hards credits.

Did he enter the Bouyon Monarch?

Yes. Soca News lists him among the 2017 finalists.

What did he release in 2020?

Bouyon To The World (Remix), the Clint H x WCK Ultimate Road Mix 2020 and Jouvert with WCK.

Who plays bass on his songs?

Keith Goddard, WCK's bassist, credited on All Die Hards in 2006 and on his 2020 songs.
