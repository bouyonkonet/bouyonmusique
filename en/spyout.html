Spyout

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Spyout appears on this site through a single track, posted not on his own channel but on his producer's: “Spyout, Shake It! | Billie Jean Riddim | Soca/Bouyon 2026”, uploaded by JC Productions on October 16, 2026, running 2 minutes 24. The listing names the artist and the song, credits arrangement, mixing and mastering to JC Productions, and files the release under Vincentian soca through its hashtags. That is all the file holds: no biography, no writing credit, no birthplace.

A track on a riddim drawn from a classic

“Shake It!” sits on the Billie Jean Riddim, a JC Productions instrumental. Ten days earlier, on October 6, 2026, the same channel posted “Michael Jackson, Billie Jean (Remix) | Bouyon/Soca 2026”, a producer remix of the same length, 2 minutes 24, with the same arrangement, mixing and mastering credits. The riddim name therefore points to that remix, and Spyout is the voice on top of it. It is the classic bouyon riddim logic: one instrumental, several songs.

The remix itself is not a Spyout track and does not enter the structured data of this page. It is cited here only because it explains the riddim name and the Soca/Bouyon label the producer applies to his October 2026 releases.

Soca and bouyon on the same label

The title reads “Soca/Bouyon 2026”, two genres side by side, with no ranking. The description says a little more about the circuit: its hashtags include #spyout, #vincysoca2026, #Stvincent and #vincy, and the JC Productions handles carry the number 784, the area code of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. Those signals belong to the producer's page: they place the release within the Vincentian circuit, they do not say where Spyout was born.

This double label is common on the islands around Dominica, where bouyon soca, a name coined by Dada in 2003, has settled into the carnival seasons. The bouyon vs soca page sets out what separates the two.

The limits of the file

On October 23, 2026, Spyout's file is one listing posted by his producer a week earlier. No artist channel, streaming page or biography was found under that name during this research, and no writer is credited on the listing.
