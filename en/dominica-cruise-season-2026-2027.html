Dominica's 2026-27 cruise season

By Smokiller · Published November 4, 2026 · Updated November 4, 2026

Dominica's 2026-27 cruise season opened on Thursday 8 October 2026, with the arrival of MV Rhapsody of the Seas at the Roseau Cruise Ship Berth, with about 2,100 passengers aboard, from San Juan, Puerto Rico, on a seven-night Southern Caribbean round trip. The season runs through July 2027, with 267 calls and 475,521 passengers expected.

An opening on 8 October

The Rhapsody of the Seas opened the season on 8 October 2026, at the Roseau Cruise Ship Berth. The ship came from San Juan, Puerto Rico, with about 2,100 passengers aboard, for a seven-night Southern Caribbean cruise.

The schedule published by the Discover Dominica Authority runs from September 2026 to July 2027. The last scheduled call is MV Disney Fantasy, on 15 July 2027, with a capacity of 4,000 passengers. The stated target is one million cruise passengers a year by 2030.

267 calls, three ports

Most calls are at the Roseau Cruise Ship Berth. Woodbridge Bay Port, one mile north of Roseau, also receives ships, such as MV Majestic Princess, with more than 4,200 passengers. The Cabrits Cruise Ship Berth, near Portsmouth, about 30 miles from Roseau, receives boutique and luxury liners such as SY Sea Cloud and Le Dumont d'Urville.

The largest vessel of the season is MSC World Europa, due to dock nine times between December and April. Peak traffic is expected between 28 December 2026 and 19 February 2027, with more than 9,600 passengers carried by MV Queen Mary 2 and MV Arvia. About 80 percent of passenger concentration falls between December and March.

A record season in sight

According to Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit, in his address of 3 November, the past cruise season was the strongest in fifteen years, and last year Dominica welcomed more stay-over visitors than ever before. The minister attributes to cruise growth a contribution to GDP growth of 3.1 percent and more than 3,000 jobs secured.

The two Dominica News reports do not give the same comparison with the previous season: one speaks of 23 percent growth, the other of nearly 50,000 more passengers. Both figures are reported here as they were published, and no single growth rate is used.

The season under way in November

The season is under way in November: the Rhapsody of the Seas called on 5 November, ms Zuiderdam on 6 November, and Mein Schiff 5 is due today, 7 November, from 7am to 7pm, followed by Mein Schiff 2 on 8 November, according to the schedules published by CruiseMapper.

These November calls open the densest period of the season, which peaks between late December and mid-February, when the largest ships follow one another at Roseau and Woodbridge Bay.
