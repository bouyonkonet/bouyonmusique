Subance: Granny, Saint Lucian bouyon from 2019

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Subance is a singer from Saint Lucia, a voice of creole soca and dennery segment. His biggest hit is still “Back Bend (Remix)” with Uncle Ellis. In 2019 he stepped into bouyon with “Granny”, next to Keks Mafia on a DJ Taffy riddim, and in 2024 he played the World Creole Music Festival.

Granny, on the Coconut Riddim

In February 2019, Jammin' Entertainment released “Granny”, written and sung by Subance and Keks Mafia, labelled “Coconut Riddim, Bouyon 2019”. The riddim is by DJ Taffy, a Roseau bouyon figure, and the mix is by Jason Darius at Jammin' Entertainment in Saint Lucia.

Keks Mafia's channel put up its own upload the same day, titled “Granny Bouyon 2019”, now past 25,000 views. It is his clearest bouyon song: a Saint Lucian singer, a Dominican riddim, and a home label holding it together.

Back Bend, Bay Pat and creole soca

Outside bouyon, Subance is above all the voice of “Back Bend (Remix)” with Uncle Ellis. Written by Subance and Dion Gomez and produced by Simeon “DRC” Theodore, the video posted by JulianspromosTV at the end of 2017 is past 12.8 million views, and the official audio adds more than 5 million.

Much of his catalogue runs through Jammin': “Bay Pat” on the Bay Pat Riddim in 2019, past 2 million views, “Nasty” as dennery segment in 2022, “Ki Sa” in 2024. In 2014, “Fini Wom” on the Ca Ca le Riddim came out for Creole Day, the occasion covered on our Jounen Kwéyòl page.

At the 2024 World Creole Music Festival

The official 2024 programme, released on October 7, placed “Umpa & Subance”, performers from Saint Lucia, on Saturday October 26. That night's bill also had Asa Bantan, Mr. Ridge and Pudaz, Kassav, and Damian and Stephen Marley.

Fan videos show his set at Windsor Park, among them “Umpa and Subance Live at Dominica's World Creole Music Festival 2024”. For the link between the two islands, see our bouyon and Saint Lucia page and the World Creole Music Festival page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Subance?

A singer from Saint Lucia, known for creole soca and dennery segment, and for Back Bend (Remix) with Uncle Ellis.

What is his bouyon song?

Granny, with Keks Mafia, on DJ Taffy's Coconut Riddim, billed Bouyon 2019.

What is his most watched song?

Back Bend (Remix) with Uncle Ellis, more than 12.8 million views for the video posted by JulianspromosTV.

Has he played WCMF?

Yes, on Saturday October 26, 2024, with Umpa, according to Dominica's official programme.

Which label does he work with?

Many of his songs come out on Jammin' Entertainment in Saint Lucia, including Granny, Bay Pat, Nasty and Ki Sa.
