Frequently asked questions

What is the World Creole Music Festival?

The World Creole Music Festival, or WCMF, is Dominica's great Creole music festival: three nights of concerts in Roseau, every year in October, at the time of the island's independence celebrations. Bouyon, cadence, zouk and kompa share the Windsor Park stage. Overview on the World Creole Music Festival page.

Why is the WCMF strategic for bouyon?

Because it is the gathering where collaborations, deals and recognition are decided for bouyon artists. Around the three nights of concerts revolve the media, the partners from the French Antilles and France, and the conversations that decide the following year's releases. For a Dominican artist, it also means playing at home.

When is WCMF 2026?

The 2026 edition of the World Creole Music Festival runs from Friday October 23 to Sunday October 25, 2026, at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica. Bouyon Shattaz sees it as the moment to launch its services for artists: rights clinic, content and distribution network.

Why is it called bouyon?

Bouyon means soup in Kweyol, a slow-cooked dish that blends its ingredients. The name sums up the genre: since its creation by WCK in 1988, it has stirred Dominica's traditional musics, like jing ping and cadence-lypso, together with electronic production. See the meaning of bouyon page.

Is bouyon part of Creole culture?

Yes. Bouyon was born in Dominica's villages and carnival, it is sung in kwéyòl and English, and it soundtracks Creole celebrations across the French Caribbean. At the World Creole Music Festival, it shares the stage with cadence, zouk and kompa, the other musics of the Creole world.

How long has the World Creole Music Festival existed?

The World Creole Music Festival was created in 1997 and is held at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica. Nicknamed the festival that never sleeps, it celebrated its twenty-fifth edition from October 24 to 26, 2025, under the theme Global Echoes of the Nature Island, launched by the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority.

What is The Bouyon Assembly?

The Bouyon Assembly is an all-bouyon segment created for the 25th edition of WCMF, scheduled on Night 2, Saturday October 25, 2025. It brought together Rohie, Ridge, Pudaz, Kenny G, Quan, DJ MJ, Little Boy and Ebony Empress for Dominica, with Jixels and Shanika from Guadeloupe: an assembly of the next generation on the island's biggest stage.

Who opens WCMF 2026?

The first night, Friday October 23, 2026, brings together on the bouyon and Creole side TK International, the Midnight Groovers, Ridge, Colton T, Pudaz and Maurane Voyer, with Capleton, Skillibeng and Klass as headliners, according to official communication and published guides. DJ MJ is also billed as a duo with keyboardist MV on the Keys.

What is Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl?

Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl is the Creole village of Dominica's Independence season, set up at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau in the days before WCMF. Its fifth edition runs from October 19 to 22, 2026, with live music, bouyon, quadrille, cultural showcases and more than ninety exhibitors. Shelly and the Signal Band, TK International and Reo are among the first confirmed artists.

How does Bouyon Shattaz cover WCMF?

As a strategic moment, where the visibility of Dominican artists and partnerships with the French Caribbean are decided. For the 2026 edition, the network devoted a three-post countdown series to the festival. Smokiller also helped shape Creole Conversations, beachside conversations during festival week, without owning the event.
