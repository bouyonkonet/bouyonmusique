The WCMF, a strategic stage for bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 6, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Every October, the World Creole Music Festival turns Roseau into the world capital of Creole music. For bouyon artists, it is the gathering where collaborations, deals and recognition are decided.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it called bouyon?

Bouyon means soup in Kweyol, a slow-cooked dish that blends its ingredients. The name sums up the genre: since its creation by WCK in 1988, it has stirred Dominica's traditional musics, like jing ping and cadence-lypso, together with electronic production. See the meaning of bouyon page.

Is bouyon part of Creole culture?

Yes. Bouyon was born in Dominica's villages and carnival and soundtracks Creole celebrations across the French Caribbean. At the World Creole Music Festival, it shares the stage with cadence, zouk and kompa, the other musics of the Creole world.

What is the World Creole Music Festival?

The World Creole Music Festival, or WCMF, is Dominica's great Creole music festival: three nights of concerts in Roseau, every year in October, at the time of the island's independence celebrations. Bouyon, cadence, zouk and kompa share the Windsor Park stage. Overview on the World Creole Music Festival page.

Why is the WCMF strategic for bouyon?

Because it is the gathering where collaborations, deals and recognition are decided for bouyon artists. Around the three nights of concerts revolve the media, the partners from the French Antilles and France, and the conversations that decide the following year's releases. For a Dominican artist, it also means playing at home.

When is WCMF 2026?

The 2026 edition of the World Creole Music Festival runs from Friday October 23 to Sunday October 25, 2026, at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica. Bouyon Shattaz sees it as the moment to launch its services for artists: rights clinic, content and distribution network.
