Rum Lawd: Whale Fu See with OG Park and No Hole, bouyon 2025

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Rum Lawd, also written RumLawd or RumLawD depending on the page, is credited as Enrique Matthews. Two of his 2025 titles carry the bouyon label: “Whale Fu See”, shared with OG Park on the Boss Raw Records Whale Oil Riddim, and “No Hole”, on the Next Gen Records Tequila Riddim. The rest of his visible catalogue presents itself as Vincy Soca, the soca of Saint Vincent. This profile follows the two bouyon titles and leaves the rest in the text.

Whale Fu See, with OG Park

“Whale Fu See” was released on April 13, 2025 on the Boss Raw Records channel as “OG Park & Rum Lawd - Whale Fu See (Whale Oil Riddim) | Bouyon 2025 | Vincy Soca 2025”, running 3 minutes 27. Enrique “RumLawd” Matthews appears among five writers, with Rasheid Sutherland, Giovanni “GEO” Collins, Rajiv “Suhrawh” Sutherland and Omar “OG Park” Phillips. Suhrawh produces, mixes and masters; Rasheid Sutherland is executive producer.

The ONErpm release, filed under the riddim name, carries the same date. The video hashtags put RumLawd and VincyMas, the Saint Vincent carnival, up front, which places the title in that circuit while giving it the bouyon label.

No Hole, on the Tequila Riddim

“No Hole” was published on May 2, 2025 on the Next Gen Records channel, labelled Bouyon 2025, running 2 minutes 19. The description credits writing and performance to RumLawD, production to Next Gen Records, that is Dré Soundz and JJW Productions, executive production to Josh “JJW Productions” Williams, and mixing and mastering to JJW Productions. Cover art is by WildWolf Designz, visuals by Dré Soundz.

It is the most personal title in his bouyon file: one voice, on a named riddim, with the genre label in the title. The page hashtags, led by Stvincent and vincysoca2025, are a reminder that it came out in the Vincentian soca season.

Soca, origin and limits

On his own channel, Rum Lawd published “Blackman Don’t Cheat” on March 15, 2026, with Riverside Boyz, presented as Vincy soca 2026, written by Enrique Matthews and Biggie, produced by Joshy Beatz and recorded at Country Boyz Studio. That title states no bouyon tie and stays in the text here.

The Vincy Soca labels, the Stvincent hashtag and the number 784, the Saint Vincent dialling code, in his Instagram name tie Rum Lawd to that circuit. These are signals, not a biography: no opened page gives his birthplace, and this site assigns none.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Rum Lawd?

Rum Lawd, also written RumLawd or RumLawD, is the stage name credited to Enrique Matthews. Two of his 2025 titles are labelled bouyon: “Whale Fu See” with OG Park and “No Hole”.

Which Rum Lawd titles are verified bouyon?

“Whale Fu See” (Whale Oil Riddim, Boss Raw Records, April 13, 2025) and “No Hole” (Tequila Riddim, Next Gen Records, May 2, 2025), both titled Bouyon 2025.

Who produced No Hole?

Next Gen Records, that is Dré Soundz and JJW Productions, with Josh “JJW Productions” Williams as executive producer and JJW Productions on mixing and mastering.

Where is Rum Lawd from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. His Vincy Soca titles, the Stvincent hashtag and the 784 in his Instagram name tie him to the Saint Vincent circuit, as signals.

Is Blackman Don’t Cheat a bouyon title?

Its page presents it as Vincy soca 2026 and does not mention bouyon. It is named here in the text only.
