Arden.P: Bouyon Is Us, Akunamatata and the 2026 bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Arden.P, credited as a writer under the name Arden Panthier, has been singing bouyon since at least 2014. The artist writes, mixes, masters and films a good share of the songs, and has lined up the 2026 season with a run of tracks billed “Bouyon 2026”, led by “Bouyon Is Us”, the most watched at close to 21,000 views.

From Look Boom Boom to the Burna Boy remix

The oldest trace goes back to February 2014: “Look Boom Boom”, by Cocotet with Arden P, posted by the weekendz Hits channel under the title “Bouyon 2014”. In 2017, “Docking D Police”, shared with Hott and Kaka, passed 12,000 views on another channel.

On March 29, 2024, the Wizzard House Recordz label put out two singles on Deezer the same day, “Burna Boy (Bouyon Remix)” and “Pas Ni Konbine”. On YouTube, WizZarD HouSe RecorDs states that the remix was “written & performed by Arden "Arden.P" Panthier” and mixed and mastered in house. Under the name DJ Arden P Yow, the Arden.P channel also keeps mixes recorded in 2023, including a “Live Bouyon mix”.

Akunamatata and Bouyon Is Us

“Akunamatata” opened the 2026 campaign as early as October 5, 2025, on the artist's own channel, marked “Bouyon 2026”. Arden Panthier wrote it, co-produced it with Precky, mixed, mastered, filmed and edited it. The single reached Deezer on November 4 under the Yow inc imprint.

On February 24, 2026, came “Bouyon Is Us”, with Mr. Precky, who also produced it. Arden Panthier wrote, mixed and mastered it and filmed the video with SHATTA. At close to 21,000 views, it is the most watched Arden.P song among those checked.

The Turnt Up Tv series

In 2026, the Turnt Up Tv channel posted a run of songs billed “Bouyon 2026”. “Wood I Have” came out on March 19 on a riddim by Kross Beats, recorded at Arden P Studios, with more than 6,000 views. “Big One” followed on June 8 on a Savion Beats riddim, mixed with Qulture Studios. “Doh Say That” rounds out the series.

On June 14, Turnt Up Tv also uploaded “El Chapo”, recorded live at Galisbay in St. Martin. For bookings the channel lists two numbers, one with the 767 Dominica area code and one with the 721 Sint Maarten code. No source, however, says where Arden.P is from.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Arden.P?

A bouyon artist credited as a writer under the name Arden Panthier, active since at least 2014, who often writes, mixes and films the songs.

What is the most watched Arden.P song?

Bouyon Is Us, with Mr. Precky, posted on February 24, 2026, at close to 21,000 views.

What are the Arden.P bouyon 2026 songs?

Akunamatata, Bouyon Is Us, Wood I Have, Big One and Doh Say That, all billed “Bouyon 2026”.

Who supplies the riddims?

Precky and Mr. Precky on Akunamatata and Bouyon Is Us, Kross Beats on Wood I Have, Savion Beats on Big One.

Is Arden.P also a DJ?

The Arden.P channel carries 2023 mixes signed DJ Arden P Yow, including a Live Bouyon mix.
