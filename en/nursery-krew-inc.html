Nursery Krew Inc: the crew that took bouyon Wrecketeng

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Nursery Krew Inc, shortened to NKI, is a Dominican crew from the early 2000s. According to the English Wikipedia page on bouyon, the crew pushed Wrecketeng forward by moving from DJ and remix practice into original recordings. The names associated with the crew are Burn Brain Jolly, Charlie Black, Watty and Smokiller, and the recordings named are “Peanuts”, “Sorti Desandre” and the NKI Medley. This profile says what the sources support and no more: the public record on Nursery Krew Inc is thin, and that is one more reason to write it carefully.

Who Nursery Krew Inc was: a crew, not a classic live band

Nursery Krew Inc enters the bouyon story at a precise moment: the early 2000s, in Dominica. It is neither a live band from the founding era, like WCK, nor a solo artist from the current generation. It is a crew, a collective of artists and producers gathered around studio work. That form matters in this history, because Wrecketeng was born in DJ practice, sampling and production, not on the carnival stages of the big bands.

The wording of the sources is careful, and this profile keeps it that way: according to the English Wikipedia page on bouyon, Nursery Krew Inc “further developed” Wrecketeng, “through original recordings and music production”. The crew is therefore not presented as the inventor of the style. It is presented as the team that, at a given moment, gave the style a recorded and original form, with named works that could circulate beyond the dances where the style was played.

That distinction between inventing and developing is the heart of the subject. Wrecketeng existed before NKI, in the work of DJ Cut and the Bushtown Clan. But a style that lives only in DJ sets and remixes stays fragile: it depends on parties, tapes and memories. By recording original titles, a crew like Nursery Krew Inc gives the style works with names, a place in a discography, and a way to be passed on. That passage is what this page documents.

This profile also states clearly what it does not contain, because no verified source provides it: no exact formation date, no hometown named in the sources, no official and stable membership list, and no verified official accounts on social media or streaming platforms. When the record is thin, this site says so instead of filling the gaps with invented detail. The rest of this page follows the sources step by step, and names them.

The context: from bouyon-muffin to Wrecketeng

To understand Nursery Krew Inc, you have to climb one generation up the chain. Bouyon was born in Dominica in the late 1980s with WCK. In the 1990s, a first branch pushed the voice toward raggamuffin, hip hop and dancehall: bouyon-muffin, whose key figure is Skinny Banton. Ragga influenced voices chanted and toasted over bouyon rhythms. Wrecketeng grew directly in the wake of that branch, as our Wrecketeng page explains.

In its first form, Wrecketeng is a DJ practice: take existing dancehall and hip hop recordings, strip away their rhythm base, and rebuild the track over a bouyon instrumental, with fast percussion, heavy basslines and repetitive patterns. Older versions of Wikipedia describe this work as close to dub: the source recording is stripped down, the vocal or melodic elements are kept, and the bouyon foundation left standing, drum and bass pushed forward, is sometimes called a riddim.

DJ Cut, of Bucktown Sounds, is recognized as the pioneer of this approach, and the Bushtown Clan played an important role in developing and popularizing it in Dominica. The French Wikipedia page adds a third pioneer crew name, Klockerz Krew. Nursery Krew Inc arrives in that lineage of collectives in the early 2000s: after the remix pioneers, a crew working the same inheritance, but signing original recordings instead of reconstructions of existing songs.

One word on spelling, because it is part of the story. The official spelling used by this site is Wrecketeng: it comes from DJ Cut himself, the pioneer of the style. The spelling “reketeng”, widespread on the internet, is the common misspelling, the one used by older references and many articles. The French Wikipedia page writes “Rekenteng”, another variant. This site writes Wrecketeng, and mentions the other spellings only to flag them as variants or as the widespread error.

The contrast with the live bands of the same period helps place the Krew. Wikipedia notes that Wrecketeng developed alongside the rise of live bouyon bands, including Triple Kay International, formed in Laudat and one of Dominica’s prominent bouyon groups during the 2000s. Its approach, that of an energetic stage band, differed from the sample based production associated with Wrecketeng. Yet both paths grew modern bouyon together: the stage on one side, the studio on the other, and Nursery Krew Inc firmly on the studio side.

Who was in the Krew: the four associated names

The question everyone asks about a crew is the lineup. For Nursery Krew Inc, the public reference source, the English Wikipedia page on bouyon, associates four artists and producers with the crew: Burn Brain Jolly, Charlie Black, Watty and Smokiller. This profile presents them exactly that way: names associated with the crew according to Wikipedia, not an official fixed formation, because no verified source publishes an official membership list for the group.

Burn Brain Jolly holds a separate place in that list. He is the only one of the four names that the Wrecketeng revival album announced by Smokiller in December 2025 acknowledges by name for his influence, together with the Nursery Krew Inc recordings themselves. In other words, when the generation reviving the style in 2025 looks back, Burn Brain Jolly is the Krew name it cites as a marker. No detailed public biography of Burn Brain Jolly was found for this profile: his name survives in the sources through the Krew and through that 2025 acknowledgment.

Charlie Black is the second associated name. He reappears in the 2025 project as well: he is among the announced contributors to Smokiller’s Wrecketeng album, alongside DJ Cut, Klass Money, Keith Goddard, O Banga and Mopo Neck. The return of the same name, from the early 2000s crew to the 2025 album, draws the continuity the project claims: the veterans of the style’s first era gathered on one record, twenty years later.

Watty is the third name Wikipedia associates with the crew. He is also, to be honest about it, the one the public record is quietest about: no independent source detailing his role, his own titles or his path was found during the research for this page. Smokiller, finally, is the fourth name. Dominican-born, he is today the figure who links Nursery Krew Inc directly to the present, since he produced the 2025 album that acknowledges the Krew’s recordings. His full profile is on our Smokiller page.

The recordings: Peanuts, Sorti Desandre, the NKI Medley and the Dada produced titles

Three recordings carry the Nursery Krew Inc name in the Wikipedia source: “Peanuts”, “Sorti Desandre” and the NKI Medley. The source wording is precise and this profile respects it: those recordings contributed to the style’s development and its growing recognition within Dominica. No release year is given by Wikipedia for these titles, and this profile invents none. They are presented as what they are in the record: the Krew’s named works, reported by Wikipedia, with no dates published in the source.

A second source, independent of Wikipedia, confirms and extends that list. The biography of Krishna Lawrence, known as Dada, published on Showcase Your Music, says he brought out a group called Nursery Krew Inc and names its songs: “Peanuts”, “Calaloo” and “Don’t F**k Around”, the last presented as a dancehall song, all produced by Dada. “Peanuts” therefore appears in both sources, which makes it the Krew’s best attested title. “Calaloo” and “Don’t F**k Around” rest on Dada’s biography alone, and this profile attributes them to that source.

The same biography gives the measure of the group’s impact in its era, in its own words: Nursery Krew Inc, it says, took Dominica by storm, and the group rose faster than any other group in the country before it. Those are claims from an artist biography, an interested source, and this profile reports them as such. But they line up with the plain fact Wikipedia keeps: the Krew’s recordings grew the recognition of Wrecketeng within Dominica.

The NKI Medley deserves a note of its own. A medley, in crew and sound system culture, is a run of titles or excerpts presented as a single piece: the showcase of a collective, a demonstration of its catalogue and its voices in one recording. That the source keeps a medley under the Krew’s name says something about its nature: Nursery Krew Inc worked as a stable of voices and productions, not as a one song group. To hear the bouyon of today that descends from this lineage, our bouyon playlist is the site’s starting point.

The role of Dada: the producer behind the Krew

The biography published on Showcase Your Music gives Nursery Krew Inc a human point of origin that Wikipedia does not: a producer. According to that biography, Krishna “Dada” Lawrence, who grew up around the members of the Bushtown Clan and who worked with Triple Kay International, then brought out the group Nursery Krew Inc and produced its named songs, “Peanuts”, “Calaloo” and “Don’t F**k Around”. The source verb is “brought out”: he put the group forward and presented it to the public.

That link changes how the Krew reads. Nursery Krew Inc no longer appears as a collective from nowhere, but as the project of a young producer formed in the orbit of the Bushtown Clan, the pioneer Wrecketeng crew. The lineage becomes concrete: the Bushtown Clan inspires and forms, Dada produces and launches, and the Krew records. It is the same chain as the whole genre, where styles pass from hand to hand through studios and crews rather than through institutions.

Dada later holds his own place in bouyon history. According to the first-hand testimony of Smokiller, bouyon soca was started by Dada in 2003 in Dominica, then popularized by Triple Kay, the band Dada also produced for. This profile does not develop that chapter, which belongs to Dada’s page, but it flags it: the man who brought out Nursery Krew Inc is, in Smokiller’s first-hand account, the same man who opened another major branch of the genre.

The measure of the sources has to be kept: Dada’s role with the Krew rests on his biography published on an artist platform, a presentation text written from his side. No independent press source found either confirms or contradicts it. This profile therefore presents it attributed, as it should: according to Dada’s biography. That is also why Dada’s name does not appear in Wikipedia’s associated list, which names Burn Brain Jolly, Charlie Black, Watty and Smokiller: the two sources do not tell exactly the same layer of the story, one the stage, the other the production.

The same biography fills in the circle Dada grew up in: it names Kendel “Loosin” Knight, Kerry “Syco Fury” Severin, Nigel “Jonny Blaze” Anthony and Steven “Bofa” Lander as the artists who formed the Bushtown Clan, the dancehall, soca, hip hop and bouyon group that motivated him to dig deeper into music, and it names Cornell “Fingers” Phillip of WCK among his production mentors. The Krew’s launch therefore sits inside a documented local network of crews and mentors, not in a vacuum.

2025: the acknowledgment, and why Nursery Krew Inc matters

For a long time, Nursery Krew Inc existed in the public record only through a few lines. The name’s return comes from the project reviving the whole style. On December 3, 2025, Dominica News Online reported Smokiller’s announcement of a collaborative Wrecketeng album, produced by Smokiller, with announced contributions from DJ Cut, Klass Money, Charlie Black, Keith Goddard, O Banga and Mopo Neck. According to that report, repeated by Wikipedia, the project acknowledged the influence of Burn Brain Jolly and the Nursery Krew Inc recordings, and aimed to introduce the history of Wrecketeng to a younger generation.

That acknowledgment is the reason this page exists. When a 2025 album takes the trouble to name an early 2000s crew and its recordings, it says the memory of the genre runs through those names. Without the NKI link, the Wrecketeng story would jump straight from DJ Cut’s remixes to the labels of the 2020s, and it would miss the step where the style became a music of original recordings, with its own titles and its own voices.

The French Wikipedia page writes the crew’s name as “Nursery Crew Ink”. This profile flags that variant for what it is: the spelling of the French page, while the English page, Dada’s biography and the name under which the crew is cited in the 2025 project all write Nursery Krew Inc. On a subject where the record is thin, even the spelling of the name asks for care: the correct name is established by crossing sources, not by copying the first one found.

The French Wikipedia page tells the same lineage from its side: the pioneer groups and crews named in its Rekenteng section are the Bushtown Clan, Klockerz Krew and the crew it spells “Nursery Crew Ink”. Two language traditions of the same encyclopedia therefore place the Krew in the pioneer line of the style, even where they disagree on the spelling of its name. For a crew with no official page of its own, that double presence is part of the record.

Why Nursery Krew Inc matters, in the end, comes down to three verified points. The crew further developed Wrecketeng through original recordings in the early 2000s, according to Wikipedia. Its named titles, “Peanuts” first among them, are attested by two distinct sources. And in 2025, the project documenting the style’s history acknowledges it by name, together with Burn Brain Jolly. To place the Krew in the full trajectory of the genre, read our history of bouyon, then the Wrecketeng page: Nursery Krew Inc is the studio link between the remix pioneers and what came next.

To follow this history in order, the site path is simple: start with the early and the , pass through the Krew on this page, then through the page, the Krew associate who became the producer of the 2025 return. Each step adds a layer: the remix, the original recording, then the historical documentation. Nursery Krew Inc is the middle step, the one without which the other two do not connect.

What is the difference between Nursery Krew Inc and Triple Kay?

Nursery Krew Inc is a studio crew, on the production side associated with Wrecketeng. Both paths grew modern bouyon in parallel, and one producer links them: Dada, who worked with Triple Kay and brought out the Krew.

The style became popular when Triple Kay and other bands adopted it. The producer who brought out the Krew therefore opened another major branch. See bouyon soca.
