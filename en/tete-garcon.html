Tete Garcon: No Sleep and the 2017 Bouyon Monarch

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Tete Garcon, also spelled Tet Garcon, is a bouyon voice listed among the finalists of Dominica's Bouyon Monarch in 2017. That year the name turns up on “Ghetto Life”, “Trouble Me” with Infinite Band and “No Sleep”, then in 2021 on “Take It Away” with Jem Jem.

2017 Bouyon Monarch finalist

In February 2017, Soca News published the eleven finalists for the second Bouyon Monarch at Carnival City in Roseau. “Tet Garcon” is on the list, next to reigning monarch Carlyn XP and Fetty Mark. The final placings do not appear in the sources we checked.

A few days later, on February 20, 2017, a channel posted “Ghetto Life”, tagged “Bouyon mix 2017”, with a short note: a brand new song from Tete Garcon for the carnival season. For the setting, see our Dominica Carnival page.

A busy 2017

In July 2017, Infinite Band released “Trouble Me” featuring Tet Garcon, tagged “Bouyon 2017”. August brought the video for “No Sleep”, also “Bouyon 2017”, now past 4,600 views: the most watched video under the name on YouTube.

The same season the name also appears around Lega-C Band: “Pound D Alarm”, with Shirvy and Jem Jem, and “This Could Be Us”, where Lega-C Band and Henrii credit a “Tete G”. The following year, “Stack Up” was posted as “2k18 Bouyon”.

Take It Away, 2021

On February 20, 2021, Outta Music posted “Take It Away”, written by Jem Jem and Tet Garcon and tagged “Bouyon 2021”. The riddim is by Tewa Beats, it was recorded at Akai Friendly and Dernel Green mixed it.

It is the last dated release we found under the name. The sources we checked give no real name, age or home island, and this profile assumes none.

Who is Tete Garcon?

A bouyon voice, also spelled Tet Garcon, and a 2017 finalist in Dominica's Bouyon Monarch.

Which bands does the name appear with?
