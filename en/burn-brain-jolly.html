Burn Brain Jolly: Babawhoolay, Unstable Minds, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Burn Brain Jolly is a bouyon voice tied to producer Ridge and his More Sewo and Cross D Bridge channels. His most watched song, “Babawhoolay”, produced by Ridge, is past 735,000 views since January 2022. In January 2023 came “Unstable Minds”, an album made over six months with Ridge, which holds “Electric” and his version of “Nasty Business”.

Babawhoolay and Ridge's workshop

“Babawhoolay” went up on January 27, 2022 on the More Sewo channel. The upload credits Burn Brain Jolly as the artist, Ridge on production, the Cross D Bridge studio and Nikita Christmas on drums. The same drummer comes back on nearly all his videos, which gives his songs a live band feel rather than a pure machine one.

He also turns up on group songs from the camp. The platform listings put “Cover Up”, with Pudaz and Ridge, past two million views, and “Backerz”, where he is credited as BB Jolly, past 470,000. A song like “My Name Is Jolly” works as a calling card: that is the short form of his stage name.

Unstable Minds, the 2023 album

On January 13, 2023, More Sewo posted “Unstable Minds” in full. The description calls it a project Burn Brain Jolly and Ridge worked on for six months, and a culmination of his life through bouyon music. The thanks list includes Watty, Dada, Paris and Kenny G. Watty and Dada are two names our Nursery Krew Inc page ties to that crew, which also lists a Burn Brain Jolly; no page opened here confirms they are the same man.

Three days later the songs came out one by one. “Electric” is past 336,000 views. On Deezer the album is dated October 22, 2023 on More Sewo, with Ridge as main artist and Jolly featured on all twelve songs, from “Kettle” to “Police Beating Them”. Under his own name, Deezer also lists the EP “AMS”, out October 31, 2023.

Nasty Business, by the dates

The name “Nasty Business” has travelled widely in bouyon since 2023, and the pages let us date the steps. The first trace is a Ridge solo song, written by Coleridge Bell, which its distribution listing dates to May 1, 2021. Its video on More Sewo, posted August 24, 2022, is past 694,000 views. Burn Brain Jolly's version came out January 16, 2023 on “Unstable Minds”, where Deezer credits him as featured.

After that, the name became a label. On February 10, 2023, “Situationship” by Nice, produced by DJ Taffy, was released tagged “Nasty business 2023” and is past three million views; “Wake Me Up” by Gwada G and Nice followed in August. More Sewo posted songs by Sukie and Kenny G under the heading “Nasty Business Riddim”. DJs then turned it into a mix theme from 2024 to 2026. None of these pages names who started the wave.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Burn Brain Jolly?

A bouyon voice from producer Ridge's More Sewo camp, known for Babawhoolay and the 2023 album Unstable Minds. He is also credited as Jolly or BB Jolly.

What is his most watched song?

Babawhoolay, produced by Ridge and posted on January 27, 2022 on More Sewo, past 735,000 views. On a group song, Cover Up with Pudaz and Ridge is past two million.

Did he create Nasty Business?

The pages consulted credit the original song to Ridge, written by Coleridge Bell and released May 1, 2021. Jolly's version dates from January 2023, on Unstable Minds. The name then became a tag for bouyon songs and mixes.

Is he the same artist as Sukie Burn Brain?

No. Sukie Burn Brain is Sukie's full stage name. Burn Brain Jolly releases under his own name, with Jolly as the short form.

Where is Burn Brain Jolly from?

No page consulted says. His songs come out on Ridge's channels, More Sewo and Cross D Bridge, which tag them as Dominica bouyon.
