Up Next Cypher: the bouyon next wave takes the mic at Trilla-G

By Smokiller · Published October 26, 2026

Trilla-G, the Dominican artist whose full name is Gael Jno. Baptiste, launched the Up Next Cypher with his Still Trill Entertainment label, a platform built to give the next generation of Dominican bouyon artists a chance to be heard, seen and celebrated. Volume 1 is documented by The Bajan Reporter.

  2. The six voices of volume 1

The cypher principle

The format is the classic cypher: several artists take turns on one beat, each showing their talent and personality. Trilla-G told The Bajan Reporter the idea had been on his mind for some time, and most of the volume 1 artists had already reached out to him about collaborating or using his platform.

The project became real on 28 September 2026, when Trilla-G brought those young artists together around the mic.

The six voices of volume 1

Volume 1 brings together G z Up (Phillip Martin), Don Lash (Cadeem McPherson), Pa Pel (Loic Arius), Avatar Omish (Omari Dayl James), Ras B (Bayna Bellot) and Pryhmex (Ajahlie Ettienne), alongside Trilla-G himself.

Several of those names already have a page on this site, built from opened sources that are sometimes thin. The cypher gives them a shared, dated and credited record, which matters in a dossier.

The credits behind the mic

The Bajan Reporter credits production to Shane Massicott of Kwib La Beats, and mixing and mastering to ThaWizZarD, whose full name is given as Cecil Joseph Jr.

A good cypher rests on that chain: one beat that leaves room for the voices, then a mix that keeps every pass readable. The published credits let readers follow that chain name by name, and this page adds nothing to them.

Why this format matters for bouyon

Trilla-G presents the Up Next Cypher as a chance to introduce new voices to audiences and to encourage emerging artists to use every platform available to them.

For a scene where many young artists first exist only as social media clips, a filmed, credited cypher relayed by regional press builds a first verifiable discography. Later volumes will tell whether the platform keeps its promise.

What is the Up Next Cypher?

A platform launched by Trilla-G and Still Trill Entertainment to spotlight the next generation of Dominican bouyon artists, in cypher format: several voices take turns on one beat.

Who is on volume 1 of the Up Next Cypher?

G z Up, Don Lash, Pa Pel, Avatar Omish, Ras B and Pryhmex, alongside Trilla-G, per The Bajan Reporter.

Who produced and mixed the Up Next Cypher?
