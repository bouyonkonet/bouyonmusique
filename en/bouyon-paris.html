Bouyon in Paris: the capital of the Antillean sound

Paris is home to the largest Antillean diaspora in Europe, and bouyon has found a scene there: dedicated nights, specialist DJs, producers making the riddims of the moment and an audience that demands every new release.

The Paris circuit

A bouyon track in 2026 often follows the same circuit: born in Dominica or in Gwada, tested by the DJs of Paris, pushed on the networks, then certified on the French market. Understanding Paris means understanding the bouyon economy of today.

The role of Parisian and Antillean media

Specialist media and pages, from Paris to the Antilles, decide visibility. The network that Bouyon Shattaz brings together aims to keep Dominican artists in control of this circuit, from the interview to the meme.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
