By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

NoFace Beatz is a bouyon riddim producer. With DbossRiddimz he signs the riddim of Que and TooYung's “Brace You”, billed Bouyon 2026.

WYFL: the bouyon riddim that travels

On January 14, 2026, NoFace Beatz II uploaded “WYFL (Wah You Feel Like) Bouyon Riddim”, with a lease link, the Instagram handle nofacebeatz767 and the tag #bouyon2026. The page warns that the instrumental contains an uncleared sample and that the producer is not responsible for copyright claims if the buyer distributes the recording commercially: a rare transparency on riddim pages.

The riddim has been used. This site's Bouyon Music 2026 playlist holds “Stylo G, Djmac, Crash Dummy - Mukky [OTR Freestyle] | WYFL Riddim”, published by Off The Record: a freestyle on the WYFL Riddim with Stylo G, the UK-based Jamaican singer. For what a riddim shared by several voices means, see our page on the bouyon riddim.

Been Waiting, Reserve, Still Love You: a catalogue of instrumentals

“Been Waiting Bouyon Riddim (Bouyon 2026)”, published on April 5, 2026, gives its technical details: key of B major, 160 beats per minute. That tempo says the genre: bouyon is played fast, and the page puts a number on it. “Reserve Bouyon Riddim”, uploaded on October 15, 2026, is already billed Bouyon 2027, for the carnival season to come.

The 2025 pages complete the catalogue: “Bouyon Riddim Instrumental 2025 - Still Love You”, from September 6, 2025, with a WhatsApp number in +1 767, and “Bouyon Riddim Warmup 2025 - Easy Peazy”. On Deezer, the NoFace Beatz name also appears on “Zeroo Cities, NoFace Beatz”, a track from Constant Musiq's EP “Dash Him Weh”.

With DbossRiddimz: from the 2022 Warmup to Brace You

The collaboration with DbossRiddimz is documented since May 14, 2022: “Bouyon Riddim Warmup 2022 || DbossRiddimz x NoFace Beatz”, riddim produced by DbossRiddimz and mixed by NoFace Beatz, more than 25,000 views. On October 30, 2025, the DbossRiddimz channel published “Que x TooYung - Brace You (Official Audio) [Bouyon 2026]”: produced by Bad Producer Ent. and Knockin Records, riddim by Dbossriddimz and Noface Beatz, mixing and mastering by XavvoKnockin, lyrics by Que and TooYung.

No opened page spells out where NoFace Beatz is from; his Instagram handle and number carry the 767 code. This profile, written from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026, places him in the Dominican circuit without stating an origin, and will be extended when sung titles on his riddims are published with credits.
