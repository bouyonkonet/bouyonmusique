Music bouyon: Dominica cable car, 6.6 km to the Boiling Lake

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026

On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, European Union Ambassador Fiona Ramsey visited the Dominica Cable Car project site to follow the progress of construction, according to Writeups 24. The 6.6 kilometre cable car will connect Roseau Valley with the Boiling Lake area, and it will cut a six-hour walk to 20 minutes.

A site visit on November 3

As part of the visit, Project Manager Victor Costa explained the development to Ramsey and her delegation. They also toured the station building and the equipment being used for the project, according to Writeups 24.

The visit comes at a time when the European Union is increasing its work across the Caribbean. Ramsey has served as Ambassador to Barbados since September 2025 and is also Ambassador Designate to the Eastern Caribbean.

6.6 km, 65 cabins, 1,000 passengers an hour

The project is expected to include up to 65 cabins, each of which can accommodate 10 passengers, and a capacity reportedly as high as 1,000 passengers per hour. Facilities planned for the stations include hospitality and retail spaces, giving visitors a chance to enjoy local products and services.

This project was founded by ABL Holdings along with the Austrian company Doppelmayr. It was initially announced with an estimated cost of US$54 million. According to the Ministry of Tourism, 210 people have already been hired as part of the project.

New access to the national park

The project will also improve access to popular natural sites such as Boiling Lake and Trafalgar Falls, while passing through Morne Trois Pitons National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, according to Writeups 24.

The construction updates published in August 2026 gave an overview of the progress at several points along the route: the tower was under construction, finishing and landscaping were being carried out at the lower station, and construction at the upper station was also advancing. No opening date has been published in the sources opened for this article.

Jobs and cruise passengers

The project is expected to create jobs and support the tourism industry. It is also expected to attract more cruise passengers, who are seeking to explore the island, and to support local businesses like hotels, taxis, tour guides, and vendors, who will benefit from passenger spending.

For music and culture lovers, the stake is direct: easier access to the Boiling Lake and Trafalgar Falls makes Dominica more attractive for visitors who also come for the World Creole Music Festival and the independence season, and every extra visitor is another chance for the island artists and craftspeople.

Frequently asked questions

Where will the Dominica cable car run?

Over 6.6 kilometres between Roseau Valley and the Boiling Lake area, passing through Morne Trois Pitons National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, according to Writeups 24.

How long will the cable car take to the Boiling Lake?

About 20 minutes, against a six-hour walk today, according to Writeups 24.

When will the Dominica cable car open?

No opening date has been published in the sources opened for this article. The latest construction update, from August 2026, had the tower under construction and work under way at both stations.
