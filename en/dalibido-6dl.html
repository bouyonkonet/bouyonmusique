Dalibido 6DL: a French rapper and a single named Bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Dalibido 6DL is a French rapper whose catalogue runs from November 2020 to April 2025: thirteen releases, singles and one EP, across the Jeunes Foux Ambiteux records, POLEED, Emergence and then TNEKET.FR imprints. The writing credit, constant from track to track, names Sid-Ali Kebiri. On April 1, 2025, he released a single titled “Bouyon”. Deezer classes the track, like his whole catalogue, as rap and hip-hop: this profile documents the title as it appears, without making him a Caribbean bouyon artist.

A rap catalogue, from 2020 to 2025

The Deezer artist page opens in November 2020 with “No signal 7” and “Opium”, followed by “Club” (December 2020) and “Brume” (February 2021), on Jeunes Foux Ambiteux records. “Besson” followed on POLEED in 2021, then “Jouvence” and “Scalpel” on Emergence in 2022. The same year, he guests for Nell Widmer on “Décroche”, per the Qobuz listing of her album “Humains après tout”. From 2023 the imprint becomes TNEKET.FR: “Tneket Love”, “Tneket Boyz”, “Tneket Girl”, “Tneket Doute”, gathered around a “Tneket” EP in 2024.

The stage trail reads in Lille: the Lille La Nuit agenda, for the tenth anniversary of the Le Flow venue, presents Dalibido 6DL running an urban-music karaoke with Baptiste Wery, both billed as rappers. No biography of the artist was found in the opened sources; neither his birthplace nor the meaning of the name 6DL is documented.

The Bouyon single, a word from elsewhere

“Bouyon” was released on April 1, 2025 on TNEKET.FR. The single's listing gives it at 2 minutes 30, written by Sid-Ali Kebiri and Sam Vano, composed by Maxence Evangelista and Sam Vano. The word is the one from the genre born in Dominica, widely taken up by French rap and urban pop as a party name. Deezer files the single under Rap/Hip Hop, like the rest of the catalogue.

This site therefore files the track for what the sources say it is: a French rap single named “Bouyon”, kept here because the word appears in it and readers searching it should find the distinction written out. For the Caribbean genre itself and its scene in France, see bouyon in France.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file rests on the platform listings and one Lille cultural agenda: a complete discography, constant credits, one guest appearance and one stage date. No biography, no interview, no detailed label page found as of October 27, 2026.

This profile will be updated if the artist documents his path, or if he releases more tracks under the bouyon name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Dalibido 6DL?

A French rapper active from 2020 to 2025, whose writing credits name Sid-Ali Kebiri, released by Jeunes Foux Ambiteux records, POLEED, Emergence and then TNEKET.FR.

What is his track named Bouyon?

“Bouyon”, a single released on April 1, 2025 on TNEKET.FR, written by Sid-Ali Kebiri and Sam Vano, composed by Maxence Evangelista and Sam Vano.

Does Dalibido 6DL make Caribbean bouyon?

Nothing in the opened sources ties him to the Dominica genre: Deezer classes his catalogue, the “Bouyon” single included, as rap and hip-hop. This profile documents the title without reclassing it.

Where is Dalibido 6DL from?

No opened source names his birthplace. One documented stage date places him in Lille, at the Le Flow venue, where a cultural agenda bills him as a rapper.

What are his other tracks?

“No signal 7”, “Opium”, “Club”, “Brume”, “Besson”, “Jouvence”, “Scalpel”, then the Tneket run: “Tneket Love”, “Tneket Boyz”, “Tneket Girl”, “Tneket Doute” and the “Tneket” EP.
