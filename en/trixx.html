Trixx: the rising name, between mixes, a festival and a national nomination

Published October 20, 2026

Trixx is a rising name in the bouyon of Dominica, and his public file fits in a few strong lines: a feature on Signal Band “Make Her Bawl”, two records with Beevo traced in DJ tracklists, a place on the bill of the first Bouyon Festival in St. Maarten in May 2025, and a finalist nomination in the Rising Artist/Band of the Year category at the Dominica Music Awards 2025. This profile gathers those verified facts, then answers the question everybody asks: how do Trixx and the group Trixz Migos relate? The answer sits in the official lists, and this profile gives it without arranging it.

Who Trixx is: a collaborations name, spotted by the DJs

Trixx appears in the public bouyon file as a collaborations artist. No published biography, no interview found, no filled out artist page on the major platforms at the time of this research: his trace runs through mix tracklists, festival bills and nomination lists. That is the typical profile of the emerging Dominican name of recent seasons, whose reputation is built first in DJ selections and on regional stages, before the official pages.

That trace, thin as it is, is coherent. The same names keep coming back around him: Beevo, with whom he records two titles; Freddy, present on two of his traced records; Signal Band and Shelly, the band and the voice at the top of current bouyon, who credit him on one of their season records. An artist does not always choose his tracklist neighbours, but when the neighbours are those, his place in the scene reads clearly: Trixx works in the first circle of the rising bouyon generation.

This profile follows the site rule: print nothing that is not in a source. There is therefore no age here, no home village, no real name, because no source consulted publishes them. There are, instead, dated records, a festival bill, a weekly chart and a national nomination, which is enough to tell honestly where Trixx stands in bouyon today.

The verified records: “Make Her Bawl”, “Doe do me Dat”, “Fall In De Trap”

Trixx most visible record is a feature at the top of the scene. The Island Vybe weekly chart Dominican Bouyon and Soca Top Trending Songs, for the week ending April 26, 2025, programmes “Make Her Bawl” with the full credit: Signal Band, Shelly, DJ Taffy and Trixx. Signal Band is the dominant band of current bouyon, Shelly its lead voice, DJ Taffy one of its regular producers; standing as the fourth name on that credit, in a chart of the trending bouyon and soca records in Dominica, is the strongest line in the Trixx file.

The other two traced records show him in a smaller team. The DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY pre-carnival selection programmes “Doe do me Dat”, credited to Trixx and Beevo, among the names carrying Dominican bouyon right now, from Ridge to Reo. The Dj Close Connections podcast programmes “Fall In De Trap”, credited to Trixx, Freddy and Beevo. Two different mixes, two different records, and the same partner, Beevo: the pattern draws a regular collaboration rather than a chance meeting.

A fourth recording completes the list, traced in the Bouyon Passport 2026 mix tracklist: a “Nasty Freestyle” medley bringing together Litleboy, Gwada G, Trixx and Freddy. The medley format, where several voices trade over one riddim, is a classic carnival season exercise; standing in it alongside Litleboy, a central producer and artist of the new wave, confirms Trixx place in the current pool. Those four records form the verified discography of this profile: four collaborations, and no solo record documented to date in the sources consulted.

St. Maarten, May 2025: on the bill of the first Bouyon Festival

In May 2025, Trixx took a step the tracklists cannot give: the stage, abroad, on a historic bill. The first Bouyon Festival, organised in St. Maarten by Two Brothers Entertainment, is told by the Black Island Girl Multimedia outlet as the first edition of a festival devoted entirely to the genre outside Dominica. The published bill lines up Pudaz, Litle Boy, Quandan, Freddy, Reo, Nice, Kenny G, Trilla G, Trixx, Unstoppable Shax, Khallion and Edday, with bands such as T.G. Band and DJs from St. Maarten and beyond; Mr Ridge, a leading figure of the genre, closed the night.

Trixx place on that bill reads two ways. First, it confirms that his name travels beyond the mixes: a promoter building the region first bouyon festival programmes him among the artists he brings over. Second, it sets him in the photograph of a generation: most names on that bill belong to the current Dominican wave, the one exporting bouyon to St. Maarten, Guadeloupe and the diaspora. The report notes that the crowd danced in the rain and asked for a next edition the following year; Trixx was one of the artists of that first time.

That kind of line counts double in a profile like this one. A nomination can come from a list, a record from a studio; a festival bill proves an artist can hold a stage in front of an audience that paid for the genre. For Trixx, it is, with the Dominica Music Awards nomination, one of the two facts that set him apart from the mass of names that pass through tracklists without ever leaving them.

Dominica Music Awards 2025: a Rising finalist, in a strong category

The second strong line of the file is national. The Dominica Music Awards 2025, the second edition of the awards organised by Creole Heartbeat, published their official finalists list, carried by Black Island Girl Multimedia: in the Rising Artist/Band of the Year category, the finalists are Nice, Trixx, Faithii (Bouyon Barbie Faithii), Riddim Knockers Band, Real Reel and Trixz Migos. The ceremony was held on Saturday January 3, at the InterContinental Dominica Cabrits Resort and Spa in Portsmouth, and the winners report published by Soca News gives the category, under the name Rising and Emerging Artist/Band of the Year, to Real Reel.

Trixx therefore did not win, and this profile says so plainly: he was a finalist, in a category that rewards exactly the names in the act of breaking through, against the eventual winner Real Reel and names such as Nice and Faithii who fill the Dominican bouyon news. For an artist with no album, no documented solo record and no published biography, a national finalist place is an official marker: Dominica awards, which cover 24 categories and every trade of the island music business, wrote his name into the short list of the next wave.

That nomination also sets the spring 2025 records back in context. “Make Her Bawl” was turning in the charts in April 2025, the St. Maarten Bouyon Festival followed in May: the year end nomination crowns a season in which Trixx moved from the tracklists to the bills, then from the bills to the official lists. That is exactly the path the Rising category was created to spot.

Trixx and Trixz Migos: what the sources say, and what they do not say

One question remains, the one every reader meeting both names asks: is Trixx a member of Trixz Migos? Here is exactly what the sources consulted allow to be written. Trixz Migos is a bouyon group from Dominica whose best known record, “What She Like” with Hussey, passed a million views on YouTube and was a Collaboration of the Year finalist at the same Dominica Music Awards 2025. The group has its own separate profile on this site, under its own name.

In the official Dominica Music Awards 2025 finalists list, Trixx and Trixz Migos appear as two distinct entries in the same Rising Artist/Band of the Year category. Dominica awards therefore treated them as two separate acts: a solo artist named Trixx, and a group named Trixz Migos. No source consulted during the research for this profile, neither press, tracklists nor awards lists, presents Trixx as a member of the group, and none presents the group as his project. This profile therefore writes what the sources support: two close names, two separate entries, and no documented membership link.

The confusion is understandable, and spelling plays its part. The group appears across sources as Trixz Migos, Trixzmigos, TrixZ Migos and even Trixmigos in the Island Vybe chart, and one mix credits that same “What She Like” under the form Trixx Migos, with two x letters. Those tracklist variants blur the line between the group name and the solo artist name. The reading rule on this site is simple: the group records, such as “What She Like” and “Toxic Love”, belong to the group profile; the records credited to Trixx alone or with Beevo, Freddy and Signal Band belong to this profile. If an official source one day establishes a membership link between the two, this profile will be corrected and dated.

Hearing Trixx, and what this profile does not yet know

To hear Trixx, start with “Make Her Bawl”, the Signal Band record where he is featured, then “Doe do me Dat” and “Fall In De Trap”, his two records with Beevo, and the “Nasty Freestyle” medley with Litleboy, Gwada G and Freddy. Our bouyon playlist sets those records back inside the flow of Dominican new releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz, where the names of his generation cross from one record to the next.

Finally, this profile states its limits. No solo Trixx record is documented in the sources consulted; no biography, no real name and no verified official account was confirmed during this research, so the structured data on this page links to no social profile. No membership link with Trixz Migos is stated, for lack of a source, and the likeness of the names is treated as what it is: a likeness, documented with its spelling variants.

The file then fits in one sentence: four verified collaborations, a bill at the historic St. Maarten Bouyon Festival, and a Rising finalist place at the Dominica Music Awards 2025. For an emerging name, that is a short but real file, made of lines every reader can check. What comes next, a solo record, an artist page, a biography, belongs to Trixx; this profile will be updated when it appears.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Trixx?

Trixx is an emerging bouyon artist from Dominica. His verified public file includes a feature on Signal Band “Make Her Bawl”, records with Beevo and Freddy traced in DJ tracklists, a place on the bill of the first Bouyon Festival in St. Maarten in May 2025, and a finalist nomination at the Dominica Music Awards 2025.

Is Trixx a member of Trixz Migos?

No source consulted says so. In the official Dominica Music Awards 2025 finalists list, Trixx and Trixz Migos appear as two distinct entries in the same category, Rising Artist/Band of the Year: the awards therefore treat them as two separate acts. The group records, such as “What She Like” with Hussey, are documented on the group profile, not here. The confusion also comes from the variable spellings of the group name in tracklists, up to a “Trixx Migos” form.

What are the verified Trixx records?

Four collaborations: “Make Her Bawl”, with Signal Band, Shelly and DJ Taffy, charted in Island Vybe Dominican Bouyon and Soca Top Trending Songs for the week ending April 26, 2025; “Doe do me Dat”, with Beevo, in the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY selection; “Fall In De Trap”, with Freddy and Beevo, in the Dj Close Connections podcast; and a “Nasty Freestyle” medley with Litleboy, Gwada G and Freddy, in the Bouyon Passport 2026 mix. No solo record is documented to date.

Did Trixx win at the Dominica Music Awards 2025?

No: he was a finalist in the Rising Artist/Band of the Year category, whose winner, announced at the January 3 ceremony at the InterContinental Dominica Cabrits Resort and Spa in Portsmouth, is Real Reel, according to the Soca News report. The other finalists were Nice, Faithii, Riddim Knockers Band and Trixz Migos.

Where has Trixx performed live?

At the first Bouyon Festival, organised by Two Brothers Entertainment in St. Maarten in May 2025, according to the Black Island Girl Multimedia report. The bill also included Pudaz, Litle Boy, Freddy, Reo, Nice, Kenny G, Trilla G, Khallion and Edday, and Mr Ridge closed the night. It is the main documented live performance by Trixx in the sources consulted.

Where can I hear Trixx?

Start from Signal Band “Make Her Bawl”, where he is credited with Shelly and DJ Taffy, then look for “Doe do me Dat” and “Fall In De Trap” in the selections of DJs from the Dominican scene, such as the DJWILLYWONKA FACTORY and Dj Close Connections.
