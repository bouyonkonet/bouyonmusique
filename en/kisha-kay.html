Kisha Kay: Saint Lucia, Doh Post Me and bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Kisha Kay, first credited simply as Kisha, is a singer from Saint Lucia who moves between Dennery Segment, soca and bouyon. Her clearest bouyon outing is “Doh Post Me”, on Boogy Rankss's Pelau Riddim in 2023. In 2026 her channel billed “Done” as her winning performance at the Saint Lucia Groovy Soca Monarch.

TPM and Dennery Segment

The song that made her name is “TPM (The People Man)”, posted in February 2018 by NGM Eventz under the line “Saint Lucia 2018”. Kisha sings it with Mata over the Sick Jab Riddim, produced by Wmglab and Motto Productions. The writing is credited to Kisha Joseph, Lyleton “Mata” Cornwallis and Marley “Mongstar” James, and the video was past 1.4 million views when read.

The song soon crossed genre lines: in 2019, remixes by DJ Flip Tha Boss were circulating under the label “Bouyon 2019”. Her base is still Dennery Segment, as “Lick It” with Jardel on Krome Productions' Vaxx Riddim showed in 2022. For how the two scenes meet, see our page on bouyon in Saint Lucia.

Doh Post Me on the Pelau Riddim (2023)

On March 20, 2023, Boogy Rankss posted “Kisha Kay & Boogy Rankss - Doh Post Me (Pelau Riddim)”, with #Bouyon and #DennerySegment right in the title. The Pelau Riddim is produced by Boogy Rankss, with Freezy as executive producer and a mix by Krome Productions. The album reached Deezer the next day, and “Doh Post Me (feat. Kisha Kay)” is its first track.

The video had about 3,300 views when read. In August 2023 the Stratosphere Muzic label reposted the official audio and tied it to its “Ultimate Soca | Lucian Soca 2023” compilation, a sign the song also travels as a Saint Lucian scene record.

Show Me, Done and the 2026 Groovy Soca Monarch

2026 is her strongest year online. “Show Me”, from the FEELS: The Audio Collective project, came out in March on the TeamFoxx channel, produced by Lashley “Motto” Winter, better known as Motto. “Done”, released on May 1, is produced by Darence “Hitty Lance”, mixed by Yannick Plante and written by Kisha Joseph with Sedale “Rico” Simei and Sherwinn Dupes Brice; it was past 64,000 views when read.

In late July her channel posted the live version of “Done” as her “2026 Groovy Soca Monarch winning performance (St Lucia)”. In September, “Horn for Horn”, on Team Foxx's By A Bar Riddim, was already pitched at the 2027 season. These recent songs are billed soca, not bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Kisha Kay from?

Saint Lucia: her channel bills “Done” as her winning performance at the 2026 Saint Lucia Groovy Soca Monarch, and “TPM” was billed “Saint Lucia 2018”.

What is her bouyon song?

“Doh Post Me”, on Boogy Rankss's Pelau Riddim, posted on March 20, 2023 with the hashtags #Bouyon and #DennerySegment.

Are Kisha and Kisha Kay the same artist?

Yes, going by the credits: “TPM” (2018, as Kisha) and “Done” (2026, as Kisha Kay) are both written by Kisha Joseph, and her Kisha Kay Music channel also carries “Low Key”, signed Kisha.

What is her biggest hit?

“TPM (The People Man)” with Mata (2018), on the Sick Jab Riddim, past 1.4 million views on NGM Eventz.

Has she worked with Motto?

Yes: Lashley “Motto” Winter produced “Show Me” in 2026 under the Teamfoxx name, and the credits of “TPM” (2018) already name Motto Productions.
