Asa Bantan at WCMF 2026: the Night 2 aircraft entrance

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026 · Updated November 7, 2026

Some entrances stay in a festival memory. On Saturday October 24, on Night 2 of the World Creole Music Festival 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau, Asa Bantan made his arrival aboard an aircraft prop that was pulled through the crowd, turning his journey to the stage into part of the entertainment itself, per the WorlMag review.

An aircraft pulled through the crowd

The entrance created an immediate spectacle, WorlMag reports. Technical difficulties with the microphone meant he could not properly address the patrons while making his way off the aircraft and towards the stage, but the visual impact had already done much of the work.

Once onstage, Asa Bantan took the audience through a performance that moved between emotion and unrestrained energy. He paid tribute to loved ones who had passed away, giving the crowd a moment to reflect, before turning the atmosphere back up: he soaked the crowd and encouraged the audience to let loose, in the participatory style that makes bouyon such a powerful festival experience.

A night opened by culture, carried by konpa

The evening began with cultural presentations from the Karenia Cultural Group and the Flamboyance Dancers, WorlMag recalls. Extasy Band then opened the musical programme with Dominica interpretation of konpa, with special guests Luc Leandre, Pudaz, Paris and Khadija Joseph, per WIC News coverage cited by WorlMag.

Tabou Combo brought Haitian konpa royalty to Roseau, inside the long musical relationship between Dominica, Haiti and the French Caribbean. Full Blown, from Trinidad and Tobago, also performed songs they had written for other artists, spotlighting the songwriting work behind familiar Caribbean hits.

Ne-Yo, Alkaline and the Dennery Segment

Ne-Yo delivered a catalogue of R and B classics, including So Sick, Because of You, Miss Independent and Closer, and the review notes that he also wrote Irreplaceable for Beyonce. Alkaline then connected with Dominica dancehall fans, whose appetite for the genre remains strong alongside bouyon, per WorlMag.

The Saint Lucia Dennery Segment showcase brought together DYP, Hollywood HP, Shemmy J, Sedale, TallyBoy, Andrew, Nerdy and Ezra the Fun Machine, and its standout moment was the appearance of Dominican legend Derick Rah Peters to perform with DYP, per the review.

To sunrise: Edday, Reo and Trilla-G

Edday kept the momentum going into the early hours, then Reo raised the energy again with dancers in Dominican colours. Reo revealed his suit and welcomed Maurane Voyer, from Martinique, to perform their song Joli BB, with Trinidadian social media personality Certified Samson also featuring in his set, per WorlMag.

Trilla-G followed and kept the sunrise crowd moving. By the end of the night, the WorlMag account is clear: the international names drew the attention, but it was Dominica bouyon artists who held the stadium until morning.

How did Asa Bantan enter at WCMF 2026?

Aboard an aircraft prop pulled through the Windsor Park crowd on Saturday October 24, during Night 2, per the WorlMag review. Microphone difficulties marked the entrance without cancelling its visual impact.

What did Asa Bantan do during his set?

He paid tribute to loved ones who had passed away, then turned the energy back up, soaking the crowd and encouraging the audience to participate, per WorlMag.

Who else played Night 2 of WCMF 2026?

Extasy Band, Tabou Combo, Full Blown, Ne-Yo, Alkaline, the Saint Lucia Dennery Segment showcase, Edday, Reo with Maurane Voyer, and Trilla-G, per the WorlMag review.
