Redz: Head Gone Bad, Ohh Baby Why and his bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Redz sings on the line between Vincentian soca and bouyon. His YouTube pages carry Vincy Soca hashtags, and his label, HGB Records, is shown with the Saint Vincent flag. Nearly all his production runs through the same person, Little Boy.

Scene: 🇻🇨 Saint Vincent and the Grenadines · All islands

Head Gone Bad, on the Ponza Bouyon Riddim

On June 9, 2023, Redz posted “Head Gone Bad”, with Farry and Emthon, on the Ponza Bouyon Riddim. The song is filed as Vincy Soca 2023, but the riddim carries the bouyon name. The three singers wrote it, Little Boy produced and Parry Jack mixed. At over 62,000 views, it is his most played song.

In 2024, DjeasyPromos carried two songs billed Bouyon 2024: “One Hundred”, with Mr. Nayee, on February 3, also produced by Little Boy, then “Come And Check Me” on April 23. The same channel posted songs from an EP called Isle Of Sewo, including “Love To Sewo” and “Val Again” with Knutkase.

Tek Wuk and Ohh Baby Why

In spring 2025, Redz put two songs on his own channel, each billed Bouyon 2025. “Tek Wuk” came out on April 21, written by Christian Charles, produced by Little Boy and mixed by DJ Jeanie. “Ohh Baby Why” followed on May 16, written and sung by Redz, with cover art by Dawson Nanton.

On Deezer, “Ohh Baby Why” has been out since November 15, 2024, on the HGB label: the video came six months after the single. Both songs sit around 13,000 views each. In June 2026, “Flingstone” went back to the Vincy Soca tag.

Another Redz in Saint Lucia?

In June 2026 a Redz appeared on “Lie To Me” by Jayanti, billed Bouyon 2026 and presented as bouyon from Saint Lucia. That song is produced by ShakoProduction, not Little Boy.

No page opened says this is the same artist. This profile therefore sticks to songs posted on Redz's channel or credited to Little Boy, and gives neither a birthplace nor a legal name.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Redz?

A soca and bouyon artist released by HGB Records, whose pages carry Vincy Soca hashtags. No source gives his legal name.

What are his bouyon songs?

One Hundred with Mr. Nayee and Come And Check Me (Bouyon 2024), Tek Wuk and Ohh Baby Why (Bouyon 2025), and Head Gone Bad on the Ponza Bouyon Riddim.

Who produces his music?

Little Boy, on Head Gone Bad, One Hundred, Tek Wuk and Ohh Baby Why. DJ Jeanie and Parry Jack share the mixing.

What is his most watched song?

Head Gone Bad, with Farry and Emthon, posted on June 9, 2023, at over 62,000 views.

Is he the Redz on Lie To Me with Jayanti?

That is not established. Lie To Me is presented as bouyon from Saint Lucia, with a different production team, and no source links the two.
