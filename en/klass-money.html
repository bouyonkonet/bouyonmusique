Klass Money

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Klass Money is an artist released by his label Route Change Music Group, credited on the official listings under the writer name Jefferson Scotland. His verified bouyon title is “Throw Dat”, released on February 13, 2026, produced by Smokiller and billed as bouyon 2026 on two distribution pages. In October 2026 he also released “Drop Down” with Krys, with no genre label. The opened pages give no stated origin; a few third party channel titles point to Dominica, and this profile cites them as signals.

Throw Dat, the 2026 bouyon title

The official listing for “Throw Dat”, provided to YouTube by Route Change Music Group, gives a release date of February 13, 2026, a running time of 2 minutes 55 and a 2026 copyright in the label’s name. It credits Smokiller as producer and Jefferson Scotland as writer and composer, with ASCAP and BMI as publishers. The genre is spelled out on two distribution pages.

On February 17, 2026, the promotion channel JulianspromosTV posted the track as “Klass Money - Throw Dat (Official Audio) | Bouyon 2026”, with a description repeating the artist, the title, Smokiller’s production and the words Bouyon 2026. Two days later, the KMR SOUNDS channel posted the same track, titled “(Bouyon 2026 Official Audio) | Produced by Smokiller”, with a write up describing it as a bouyon title built for the carnival road. This site notes that Smokiller, the track’s producer, also signs the pages of bouyonmusique.com, so the reader has the full picture.

Drop Down with Krys, October 2026

On October 17, 2026, Route Change Music Group released “Drop Down”, distributed by TuneCore, running 2 minutes 47. The listing credits rap to Klass Money and Krys, production to Shako, and writing to Jefferson Scotland and Pedro Pirbakas, the legal name under which Krys is credited, as his profile on this site already notes.

The “Drop Down” page states no genre. The title is therefore named here as a Klass Money release with a bouyon artist already profiled, but it does not enter the structured data of this page, which is kept for tracks whose page states bouyon.

An older career, and signals of origin

Klass Money is not a new name. On June 19, 2018, the Dj CashMoney 767 channel posted “Klass Money- Rude Whine FT King Dice (Dominica) [SOCA]”, produced by DLL Productionz: a title filed under soca, not bouyon. YouTube search results also show a Route Change Music Group channel with videos such as “Put In Work”, “I’ll Be Gone” with Laudie J and “Today” with Supa Sam, and a Cbn4News item from September 2016 announcing a video shot on “the Nature Isle”, Dominica’s nickname. Those videos were seen only as listings, with no stated genre.

The word Dominica in a DJ channel title, the island’s nickname in a news title and the DominicaVibes hashtag on a distribution page all point the same way, but none of those pages says where Klass Money was born or comes from. This profile therefore stays with the signals and states no origin. To place Throw Dat in its season, see new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Klass Money?

Klass Money is an artist released by Route Change Music Group, credited as writer under the name Jefferson Scotland on the official listings for Throw Dat and Drop Down.

What is Klass Money’s bouyon title?

“Throw Dat”, released on February 13, 2026, produced by Smokiller, and billed as Bouyon 2026 on the JulianspromosTV and KMR SOUNDS pages.

Is Drop Down with Krys a bouyon title?

Its official listing states no genre. This site cites it as an October 2026 release without filing it under bouyon.

Is Klass Money from Dominica?

Several third party pages point to Dominica, but none states it as his origin. This site therefore assigns him no island.

Why is Smokiller mentioned?

Smokiller is credited as producer of Throw Dat. Since he also signs the pages of this site, the link is stated openly.
