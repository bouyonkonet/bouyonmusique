TK International: Triple Kay, an established name in live bouyon

Published September 11, 2026

TK International, also known as Triple Kay Band and Triple Kay International, is a bouyon band from Dominica. It is one of the established band names of the Dominican scene, in the tradition of the live-band bouyon era that followed WCK. On Friday October 23, 2026, TK International plays Night 1 of the World Creole Music Festival, and Bouyon Shattaz named it among the five Dominican acts of its festival countdown.

One band, three names, one address

TK International, Triple Kay Band, Triple Kay International: the band circulates under several forms of its name, and this profile brings them together so the public does not get lost. The TK initials point to Triple Kay, and it is under that full name that the band keeps its public presences: @triplekayband on Instagram, triplekayinternational on Facebook, @tkinternational1 on TikTok and the website triplekayinternational.com.

That plurality of names is common among long-running Caribbean bands, whose identity was built on stage before it was built online. What matters is that the public routes converge: a listener who discovers TK International on a festival bill can find the band, follow it and hear its music without changing name on the way.

The band is one of the established names of Dominica bouyon band scene. Established here means one precise thing: a lasting presence in a landscape where live formations have carried the island party since the era that followed WCK, the band that created the genre in 1988. Our history of bouyon tells that band era, of which TK International is a current representative.

The live band tradition after WCK

After the creation of bouyon by WCK, Dominica lived through a live-band era in which formations answered each other from fete to fete and carnival to carnival. TK International stands in that tradition: the band as a local institution, with its loyal audience, its stage sound identity and its ability to hold a whole evening, not just one track.

That tradition explains the place of bands in bouyon culture. A track may be born in the studio, but the stage consecrates it: the crowd reaction, holding the tempo over time and the exchange between musicians and audience are part of the music itself. Established bands like TK International are the keepers of that collective craft.

For an audience in Guadeloupe or Martinique used to more recent forms of the genre, discovering TK International is a way to hear bouyon in its historic band form, still active. The Dominica artists hub lets you place the band among the island other formations, from the founders to the 2026 acts.

Understanding bouyon before the name

To read the story of TK International properly, bouyon first has to be put back at its source. The genre was created in Dominica, and its full story is told on our history of bouyon page. That reminder matters, because bouyon now travels through Guadeloupe, Martinique, France and the diaspora, sometimes far from the island that created it. Keeping the names of Dominican artists at the centre of the story keeps the map of the genre readable for new listeners.

Bouyon is band music, street music and party music as much as it is listening music. A track can live in a playlist in the morning, in a fete in the evening and on a big stage the following month. That is why a profile like this one is not limited to a list of songs: it is also about the stage, the audience and the place the island holds inside its own creation.

This page therefore follows a simple rule: verified facts only, titles attributed when they are a matter of reputation, and no invented numbers. When something is not confirmed, it is not written. Frequently asked questions

Are TK International and Triple Kay Band the same band?

Yes. TK International is also known as Triple Kay Band and Triple Kay International: the initials TK stand for Triple Kay. Its public accounts use those forms, @triplekayband on Instagram, triplekayinternational on Facebook and @tkinternational1 on TikTok, and mixes also cite it under the short form Triple K.

Where is TK International from?

TK International is a bouyon band from Dominica, founded by three friends from the Laudat community according to its official website. Its early years mixed cadence-lypso, Haitian compas and zouk, before the switch to bouyon in the mid-2000s. The band claims more than twenty-five years on the road and more than nine albums.

Who are the members of Triple Kay?

According to the official website, Triple Kay has nine musicians: three frontline singers, David "Sonic Tazzy" George, Wayne "Mr Benji N'O" Benjamin and Lennan "Ticky" Matthew, then Kendel "Killa" Laurent on lead keyboards and production, Jeoffrey "JJ Yout" Joseph on second keyboards, Jason "Froggy" Joseph on drums, Ordel "Zoubs" Hamilton on guitars and Daniel "Brush Head" Phillip on bass.

Who produces Triple Kay's music?

Kendel "Killa" Laurent produces all of the band's music, according to its official website, while Jeoffrey "JJ Yout" Joseph writes much of the lyrics. As lead keyboardist, Killa Laurent is the constant of the Triple Kay sound, from the album “Big Ting” in 2000 to the singles of the streaming era. See Killa Laurent.

What are TK International's best songs?

Reference titles include “Sewo”, named song of the year in 2005, “Please”, People's Road March 2008, “Nous Konnet Vive”, official carnival theme in 2014, “Lock De Gate”, People's Road March 2015, “Still Standing”, the 2018 anthem, and “Heat”, People's Bouyon Road March 2019. The listed recent catalogue runs from “Chocoloco” (2021) to “Long Spoon” (2025).

How did Triple Kay switch to bouyon?

Through the arrival of Benji. According to the band's official website, Benji produced and sang “Log On”, came back in 2004 as a guest to produce the track “Sewo”, then became a full member in 2005: the repertoire, rooted in cadence-lypso, compas and zouk, then swung to the fast bouyon he was already known for. See Benji.

Did Triple Kay create bouyon soca?

No, Triple Kay popularised it. According to the first-hand testimony of Sean "Smokiller" Francis, bouyon soca was launched by Dada in Dominica in 2003, then grew when Triple Kay and other bands took it up in their own version of bouyon. The Laudat band is therefore one of the branch's historic carriers. See bouyon soca.

Why is Triple Kay called the Bouyon MVPs?

Bouyon MVPs is the nickname fans and the regional press give the band, according to its official website: a reported nickname, not an official title. It sums up Triple Kay's place, which eMAGE DM describes as an ensemble that dominated the bouyon scene for more than a decade, through Road Marches, anthems and official carnival themes.

When does TK International play WCMF 2026?

The band is a Night 1 act at WCMF 2026, on Friday October 23, 2026, per Bouyon Shattaz sprint records and published festival guides. It is a habit: according to its official website, Triple Kay has played the World Creole Music Festival since its first appearance in 2002 and has not missed an edition since 2005.

How can I follow TK International?

Through its verified public routes: Instagram @triplekayband, Facebook triplekayinternational, TikTok @tkinternational1 and the website triplekayinternational.com. These channels belong to the band, and they let programmers, media and the public follow it directly, at the Dominican source, without an uncertain middleman.
