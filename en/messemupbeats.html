Two bouyon titles on one day

On January 29, 2026, MessEmUpBeats released two singles distributed by DistroKid, both under the MessedUpRecords copyright. “Get It Sexyy (Bouyon 2026)” runs 2 minutes 42 and “Friend Do (Bouyon)” 2 minutes 43. On YouTube, both automatic pages credit the artist under three spellings, Messemupbeats, messem and messemup, which reads as aliases of one name rather than three people. On Deezer, the artist page lists them among twelve releases, and the page for “Get It Sexyy (Bouyon 2026)” files the album under Rap/Hip Hop: the platform has no bouyon box, so the title itself does the labelling.

The same day also brought “Ever Since U Left (New Jack Swing)”, a third title that announces its genre in brackets. That habit says something about the method: MessEmUpBeats names the style in the title, as DJ circuits do when a track has to be found by its genre. For bouyon, the method yields two verified titles, and they are the only two entered in the structured data of this page. For the genre itself, see our page what is bouyon.

A producer and remixer catalogue

The Deezer page shows a producer profile more than a singer. It holds MessedUp remixes, “Which One”, “March Madness”, “Skrilla”, “Tight Up Skirt”, “Throat” and “Like Dat”, several of them gathered on compilations titled RolledUpRemixes, Vol. 1 and Vol. 3. It also holds a run of feature credits on the album “Super Sonics” by the rapper Bobby Brugal. None of those titles states a bouyon tie: they are named here to describe the catalogue, not presented as bouyon.

YouTube search listings also show, on his MessemUpBeats channel, mixes whose titles name bouyon: “Bouyon Mix 2025 Vol 3”, “Bouyon Mix 2025 VOL 2”, a “Bouwey Mix 2025” and a “BabyMama Radio Vol.1” show that pairs Miami Bass, Jersey Club, Juke and bouyon. Those titles were seen only in result listings, not opened as release pages, and a mix is not a recording by the artist: they point to a DJ who plays the genre, nothing more. To hear the genre in a selection, see our bouyon playlist.

What the file does not say

No opened page names a country, an island or a city for MessEmUpBeats, and none publishes a biography. The names around his catalogue, Bobby Brugal, New Jack Swing or Miami Bass, are style signals, not proof of origin, and this site does not turn a genre into a passport. The origin therefore stays undocumented.

On October 23, 2026, the verifiable file comes down to two bouyon titles dated January 29, 2026, an in house label, MessedUpRecords, and a catalogue of remixes and collaborations outside bouyon. If the artist publishes a biography or further bouyon titles, this page will be completed, for example in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is MessEmUpBeats?

MessEmUpBeats is a producer, remixer and DJ whose Deezer page lists twelve releases, including two bouyon labelled titles released on January 29, 2026 on MessedUpRecords. No public biography was found during this research.

Which are his verified bouyon titles?

“Get It Sexyy (Bouyon 2026)”, 2 minutes 42, and “Friend Do (Bouyon)”, 2 minutes 43, both released on January 29, 2026 through DistroKid under the MessedUpRecords copyright. They are the only two titles entered in the structured data of this page.

Where is MessEmUpBeats from?

His origin is named in none of the opened sources. This site therefore assigns him no country and no island.

Does he also make bouyon mixes?

YouTube search listings show mixes on his channel titled Bouyon Mix 2025 Vol 2 and Vol 3, among others. Those titles were seen in listings only: they point to a DJ who plays the genre, not to recordings by the artist.

What is in the rest of his catalogue?

MessedUp remixes (Which One, March Madness, Skrilla, Tight Up Skirt, Throat, Like Dat), the title Ever Since U Left (New Jack Swing) and feature credits on the Bobby Brugal album Super Sonics. None of those titles states a bouyon tie.
