Mr. Vegas: the dancehall star and his bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Mr. Vegas is a Jamaican dancehall singer, born Clifford Smith in Kingston. He is not a bouyon artist: his career was built on Heads High, Pull Up and Sweet Jamaica. Since 2023, though, he has kept releasing bouyon songs, from a whole EP on his MV Music label to duets with Shanika, Edday and Litleboy.

A dancehall name, not a bouyon one

Clifford Smith belongs to the history of Jamaican dancehall. “Hot Gal Today” reached the US charts in 2000, “Pull Up” made the Billboard Hot 100 in 2004, and “Sweet Jamaica” was named Song of the Year at the International Reggae and World Music Awards in 2013. He recorded for Greensleeves and VP before putting music out on his own label, MV Music.

This profile does not count him among bouyon artists. It keeps only what his releases show: from late 2023, Mr. Vegas puts the word bouyon in his titles, sings on riddims by producers from the scene and shares the mic with artists from the bouyon scene.

Kay Ve Yo and Bouyon All Da Way

The first documented contact dates from September 2023. Shanika posted the remix of “Kay Ve Yo” featuring Mr Vegas, on the Funny Riddim by DJ Taffy, tagged #bouyon. The video has passed 100,000 views. In January 2024 the two met again on “Hot Gal”, this time produced by Craig “Leftside” Parks and presented as shatta and dancehall.

In December 2023 he released “Bouyon All the Way” with Edday, produced by Jervon Henderson, Cornell Phillip and himself. The song opens the “Bouyon All Da Way” EP, out on January 26, 2024 on MV Music, along with “Bouyon Dugu”, “Dagga Dagga”, “Follow Me” and “Shake It”. The credits on “Bouyon Dugu” list Clifford Smith next to Eliezer Vanerot, Edday's writer name.

Cyan Dun Bouyon and Wap Wap

In February 2025, Mr. Vegas put out “Cyan Dun” with DJ Michel Miles on Metis' Prod. On January 30, 2026 came “Cyan Dun Bouyon (Remix)”: DJ Michel Miles produced and mastered it, Kendoyll Simpson did the remix and the mix, and Mr. Vegas kept the vocal.

In September 2026 he guested for Litleboy on “Wap Wap”, released on Islanda Records. The MyuzikBox channel posted it with the credit “Genre: Bouyon” and filed it among its 2027 songs. The distributor's upload has passed 20,000 views.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mr. Vegas a bouyon artist?

No. He is a Jamaican dancehall singer, born in Kingston. He has, however, released several bouyon songs since 2023, including a whole EP.

What is his real name?

Clifford Smith, a name that also appears in the writing credits of Bouyon Dugu.

What is the Bouyon All Da Way EP?

A five track EP released on January 26, 2024 on his MV Music label: Bouyon All the Way with Edday, Bouyon Dugu, Dagga Dagga, Follow Me and Shake It.

What is his most watched bouyon song?

The Kay Ve Yo remix with Shanika, on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim, past 100,000 views on Shanika's channel.

Which bouyon artists has he worked with?

Shanika on Kay Ve Yo and Hot Gal, Edday on Bouyon All the Way, DJ Michel Miles and Kendoyll Simpson on Cyan Dun Bouyon, and Litleboy on Wap Wap.
