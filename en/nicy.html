Nicy: Tout Jod La, Bel Koukoun, Meritey, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Nicy is a bouyon voice who has sung since 2011 with the same small circle: Miky Ding La and Keros-N. His first album, “Pa kouwi”, came out in October 2011 on Jutsu. Then came “Tout Jod La” in 2013, “Bel Koukoun” with Miky Ding La in 2022 and “Meritey” in 2023, all presented as bouyon.

Early days with Jutsu

On Deezer his catalogue starts with the single “Tan la ka defilé”, dated January 11, 2011. The album “Pa kouwi” followed on October 15, 2011, under the Jutsu production line; the listing for “Bouwéy” credits him as lyricist. The same year “Hot Pussy”, sung with Miky, Babou and Keros-N, went around as jump up 2011, one of the names bouyon goes by. One copy is past 92,000 views.

In March 2013 “Tout Jod La” came out as a video and is past 69,000 views. The description points to his official site and pages, and its tags read bouyon, gwada and Jutsu. In December 2012, “Kal Ki Nom Ay” with Miky Ding'La had already been posted as bouyon, past 118,000 views according to the platform listings.

Bel Koukoun and Meritey

On November 10, 2022, Miky Ding La posted “Bel Koukoun” with Nicy, on a Snowbeat riddim produced by Pandemuzik. The video is past 108,000 views and carries the tags bouyon and Guadeloupe. Our Miky Ding La page also lists “Traka”, in a remix, with Keros-N and Nicy.

On March 24, 2023, Nicy posted “Meritey” on his channel, with Miky Ding La, Archy and Keros-N, produced by DjBeaw. He billed it as a “petite surprise”. The video is past 66,000 views and is tagged bouyon.

Le Nice and Quarantenaire

The pace has not dropped. Deezer lists the album “Mister A l'aise” on January 6, 2024, “Han Han” with Keros-N on April 8, 2026, then the album “Le Nice” on June 19, 2026. He is also on “Pa Vinn”, a Miky Ding La song.

On July 22, 2026 came “Quarantenaire”, credited to Shaba Squall, Nicy, Keros-N and Miky Ding La. As a guest, he sings “Catholique” with Larose. Fifteen years after “Pa kouwi”, the same core keeps coming back.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Nicy?

A bouyon singer active since 2011, close to Miky Ding La and Keros-N, whose first album Pa kouwi came out on Jutsu in October 2011.

What are his known bouyon songs?

Tout Jod La in 2013, Kal Ki Nom Ay and Bel Koukoun with Miky Ding La, and Meritey in 2023 with Miky Ding La, Archy and Keros-N.

Who does he sing with most?

Miky Ding La and Keros-N, from Hot Pussy in 2011 to Quarantenaire in 2026, along with Archy and Shaba Squall.

What is his latest album?

Le Nice, dated June 19, 2026 on Deezer, after Mister A l'aise in January 2024.

Where is Nicy from?

The pages consulted do not say. His videos carry the tags gwada and Guadeloupe, and he sings with Miky Ding La, a Guadeloupe artist.
