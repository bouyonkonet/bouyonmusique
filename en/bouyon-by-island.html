Bouyon artists by island

Bouyon was born in Dominica in 1988 and now runs through the whole Lesser Antilles. This page files every artist profile on the site under its island, using only what each profile states.

Listen while you browse: the Bouyon Music 2026 playlist on YouTube, the newest bouyon songs, updated every month.

Two kinds of link: from means the profile states where the artist comes from; scene means the profile places the artist in that island's circuit or on releases billed for it, without stating a birthplace. Artists whose profile states neither are listed at the end, not guessed.
