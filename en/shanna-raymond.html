Shanna Raymond: from soca to bouyon, Bam Bam (2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Shanna Raymond describes herself as a singer, songwriter, actress and model, between Jamaica and Saint Kitts. Known first for her soca, from “Bend Yuh Back” to “Gypsy”, she has two bouyon titles: “Hustle Hard”, written for Dada's Kryptic Riddim, mixed in Dominica, in 2025, and “Bam Bam”, released in October 2026 on the SHANNA Entertainment label.

Jamaica, St Kitts and soca

The descriptions on her early videos, in 2016, fit on one line: “Singer | Songwriter | Actress | Model | (Jamaica / Saint Kitts)”. That year she posted her set at the St Kitts Music Festival, and later the 2017 edition too. The booking number under those videos starts with 869, the area code of Saint Kitts and Nevis.

Her soca found listeners fast: “Bend Yuh Back”, billed “2017 Soca”, was past 458,000 views on JulianspromosTV when read, and “Harmony” with Hi Light past 178,000. The album “Shanna's World Deluxe Edition”, out on January 20, 2020, gathers thirteen tracks, among them “International Girl”, “Gypsy” and “Queen a De Ring”. She also tried dancehall with “Boasy” in 2023.

Hustle Hard on the Kryptic Riddim

The first of her two bouyon songs arrived on July 4, 2025 on Dada's channel. “Hustle Hard” was written by Shannakay Raymond and composed with Krishna “Dada” Lawrence, who produced, mixed and mastered it. The press text for the Kryptic Riddim presents a project built squarely on bouyon and lists her among the regional guests as “Jamaica/St Kitts”.

The riddim reached Deezer on July 6, 2025 on Dadamanufakture, with “Hustle Hard” fifth of nine tracks. She sits there next to Pumpa from the US Virgin Islands, Fadda Fox from Barbados, and local voices such as Faithii. The whole project was mixed at the Dadamufakture studio in Dominica.

Bam Bam, October 2026

On October 24, 2026, Shanna Raymond posted “Bam Bam” herself, with a title that leaves no doubt: “Bouyon Produced by Eugene Faneyt”. She wrote it alone; Steady Productions recorded it and Scratchi Bamma mixed and mastered it. JulianspromosTV carried it two days later under the tag “Bouyon 2027”, and the single reached Deezer on October 27 on the SHANNA Entertainment label.

The same week brought “I Need You”, this time filed as “Soca 2027”, a sign that bouyon is one register among several for her. At the time of writing, the two “Bam Bam” pages totalled just over 2,100 views. Profile drawn from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 29, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shanna Raymond?

A singer and songwriter who presents herself as between Jamaica and Saint Kitts, known for her soca (Bend Yuh Back, Gypsy, Harmony with Hi Light).

What are her bouyon titles?

Hustle Hard on Dada's Kryptic Riddim (July 2025) and Bam Bam (October 2026), produced by Eugene Faneyt.

Who produced Hustle Hard?

Krishna “Dada” Lawrence, who also mixed and mastered it and composed it with Shannakay Raymond.

Which label released Bam Bam?

SHANNA Entertainment, on Deezer on October 27, 2026; recorded by Steady Productions, mixed by Scratchi Bamma.

Has she played the St Kitts Music Festival?

Yes, she posted her 2016 and 2017 sets on her YouTube channel.
