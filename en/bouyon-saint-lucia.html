Bouyon in Saint Lucia: the neighbor that created bouyon soca

Published October 2, 2026

Saint Lucia is the Creole-speaking island closest to Dominica. It is largely there, among producers who love Trinidad soca as much as Dominican bouyon, that the bouyon soca fusion took shape.

Two carnivals that answer each other

The audiences of Roseau and Castries share language, history and a taste for partying. Tracks travel from one carnival to the other, and bouyon soca has become their shared musical language.

Frequently asked questions

Where is bouyon popular?

Born in Dominica, bouyon is now central in Guadeloupe and Martinique and present in France, Saint Lucia, Saint Martin and the Caribbean diaspora.

Which country created bouyon?

Dominica. The genre was born there in the late 1980s and remains the sound of Roseau carnival.
