Bouyon mix: an hour of bouyon without lifting your foot

Published October 10, 2026

The natural format of bouyon is the mix: the tracks chain at the same tempo, the party never stops. A good bouyon mix blends new releases from Dominica, bouyon soca and carnival classics.

What makes a good bouyon mix

An order designed like a night out: the tracks that build, the classics that make people sing, the riddims of the moment, and no drop in energy. The DJs of Gwada and Paris have made a specialty of these chains, inherited from carnival sound systems.

What is a bouyon mix?

A bouyon mix is a continuous chain of bouyon tracks at the same tempo, so the party never stops: the mix is the natural format of the genre. A good bouyon mix blends new releases from Dominica, bouyon soca and carnival classics, in an order designed like a night out.
