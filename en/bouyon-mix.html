Bouyon mix: an hour of bouyon without lifting your foot

Published October 10, 2026

The natural format of bouyon is the mix: the tracks chain at the same tempo, the party never stops. A good bouyon mix blends new releases from Dominica, bouyon soca and carnival classics.

Frequently asked questions

What is a bouyon mix?

A bouyon mix is a continuous chain of bouyon tracks at the same tempo, so the party never stops: the mix is the natural format of the genre. A good bouyon mix blends new releases from Dominica, bouyon soca and carnival classics, in an order designed like a night out.

What makes a good bouyon mix?

A good bouyon mix follows an order designed like a night out: the tracks that build, the classics that make people sing, the riddims of the moment, and no drop in energy. The DJs of Gwada and Paris have made a specialty of these chains, inherited from carnival sound systems.

Which bouyon mix podcasts should I listen to?

Several shows publish their bouyon mixes with full tracklists. The Blaqrose Supreme podcast runs its Bouyon Mix series and its Bouyon Freestyle Mix 2026, Digga D presents Sewo Jam, a mix show devoted to bouyon and soca, DJ Close Connections published Bouyon Passport 2026 in February 2026, and the Jamz Podcast offers a Soca/Bouyon 2025-26 Mix.

What is Sewo Jam?

Sewo Jam is Digga D's mix, presented in the Soca Podcast. The 2023-2024 edition presents itself as a tribute to Dominica, where bouyon pride reigns, and its tracklist lines up Trilla G, Shelly, Asa Bantan, Triple Kay International, Signal Band, First Serenade Band and Carlyn XP. Its published tracklists, track by track, serve as a sound archive of the season.

What is the Bouyon Shattaz bouyon mix?

Smokiller made the Bouyon Music 2026 Mix Vol. 1 for Bouyon Shattaz, a 41-minute non-stop mix published on YouTube with a timestamped tracklist. Its description highlights the best new bouyon tracks from Dominica for 2026. Alongside it, the Bouyon Road Mix works as a permanent mix, kept up to date on Spotify and YouTube.

Which tracks keep coming back in recent bouyon mixes?

Among the tracks found in several tracklists: "Make Her Bawl" by Shelly, Trixx and DJ Taffy, played at 27:02 in Blaqrose Supreme's Bouyon Mix 2025 and listed in Bouyon Passport 2026, and "Leave Me Alone" by 1T1 with Dinho, Lejuh, Theomaa and Binksdrick, placed at 5:58 in Bouyon Mix 2025 Pt 2. See also DJ Taffy.

What BPM should I mix bouyon at?

Most recent bouyon tracks are listed around 160 BPM, the signature tempo of modern bouyon, which lets DJs chain new releases with the whole current repertoire. Some party tools sit lower: "KPT", labelled bouyon warm up 2025, is listed at 127 BPM. So the DJ keeps an ear on the energy as much as on the number.

Why do bouyon mix titles say "reketeng"?

Because that misspelling spread across the internet, right into many online mix titles. The official spelling is Wrecketeng: according to Sean "Smokiller" Francis, it comes from DJ Cut, pioneer of this sample-based bouyon style, which rebuilt dancehall and hip hop recordings over bouyon instrumentals. See the Wrecketeng page.

Why do bouyon mix tracklists matter?

Because much of Dominica's new music travels through mixes first. A DJ's tracklist often becomes the most complete public record of who recorded what, with whom, at a given moment. It has its limits too: names are sometimes misspelled, like "Binkdrick" for Binksdrick, which sends listeners searching for a name that does not exist on the platforms.
