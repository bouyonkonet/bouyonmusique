Le Cus: VODY 22, Holiday and An Sou, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Le Cus, also written LECUS or LeCus depending on the page, is a voice that keeps coming back in the videos published on the Jixels channel. Three of his titles are presented as bouyon on their own page: “VODY 22”, a December 2025 video with Leseb, Sheyta and Uglyboy, “HOLIDAY”, a February 2026 video with Yoshy and Lejo, and “AN SOU”, a 2023 video with Miimii and Lucky 11. Five further collaborations fill out the file. No biography, no origin and no official account were found: this profile sticks to the credits.

Three bouyon titles, three teams

The oldest verified title is “AN SOU”, posted on October 27, 2023 on the Lucky 11 channel. The description credits Le Cus, Miimii and Lucky Luke, recording at Fana MF boyz, a mix by Elmoh, direction by Mighty Production and production by MF Music and Gomkomedia, with the hashtag Bouyon2023. It is the earliest trace, and it puts Le Cus in a circle that was already active well before the 2025 and 2026 run of videos.

Two videos from the Jixels channel follow. “VODY 22”, posted on December 17, 2025, brings together LECUS, Leseb, Sheyta and Uglyboy on a Qwann production, mixed and recorded by Yas Beats, directed by Mighty Production and produced by Jixels, with #BOUYON in the title. “HOLIDAY”, posted on February 12, 2026, pairs Le Cus with the duo Yoshy and Lejo on a Skylight production, with the same mix engineer, direction by Middle Vision and, again, #BOUYON in the title. Those two videos make Le Cus a mainstay of the studio’s group releases.

The other collaborations

Five more opened pages show Le Cus with no genre statement. “Kiss Me”, an October 2024 video with Lady Spicy, Jixels and LGC, produced and mixed by Jixels. “INDECISION RIDDIM”, posted on January 2, 2026, which pairs “HOLIDAY” with Jixels’ own “BRING ME”. “ARE U FEELING”, a May 2026 video with Leseb, Managyalis and Sheyta, on a Dinho production with Jixels on piano. “BAY LI”, by DJ Skylight and DJ Screem with Le Cus and Leseb, released on June 19, 2026 on SKYLIGHT STUDIO. And “Friday Night”, by Lucky 11 with LMS, Le Yo, Le Cus and D.O.C Fix Dem, released on August 21, 2026, with D.O.C and Skycee on the beats.

These titles are named to map the network, not to pad the bouyon file: only the three pages that state the genre enter the structured data of this profile. The network itself is clear: Jixels, Skylight, Yas Beats, Mighty Production, Lucky 11 and their guests recur from one video to the next.

What the file does not say

No opened page gives a legal name, a birthplace or an island for Le Cus. The Guadeloupe phone number printed under “Kiss Me” is the booking contact for Jixels, not information about Le Cus, so this site does not use it to assign him an origin. The scene context is covered on our page about bouyon in Guadeloupe, without any source tying Le Cus to it.

The spelling also varies: LECUS in the title of “VODY 22”, LE CUS in its description, LeCus on “Kiss Me” and in the “Friday Night” credits. Those variants come from the same channels and the same teams, and this profile treats them as one artist. It will be updated if a biography or an official page appears.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Le Cus?

Le Cus is a bouyon voice heard across the Jixels channel videos, also written LECUS or LeCus. Three of his titles are presented as bouyon on their own page: VODY 22, HOLIDAY and AN SOU. No public biography was found.

Which of his titles are verified bouyon?

“AN SOU” with Miimii and Lucky 11 (video of October 27, 2023, #Bouyon2023), “VODY 22” with Leseb, Sheyta and Uglyboy (December 17, 2025, prod Qwann, #BOUYON) and “HOLIDAY” with Yoshy and Lejo (February 12, 2026, prod Skylight, #BOUYON).

Who does he work with?

With Jixels, who produces the videos on his channel, with the beatmakers Qwann, Skylight and Dinho, the mix engineer Yas Beats, the directors Mighty Production and Middle Vision, and with Leseb, Sheyta, Uglyboy, Yoshy, Lejo, Managyalis, Miimii, Lucky 11, LGC and Lady Spicy.

Where is Le Cus from?

No opened source says. This site therefore assigns him no island.

Are LECUS, Le Cus and LeCus the same artist?

Yes, according to the opened pages: the same channel writes LECUS in one title and LE CUS in the description of the same video, and LeCus on another video from the same studio.
