Mona Lisa, a duet with JJW

“Mona Lisa” is shared from its title onward: Detal and JJW appear side by side. The credits spell out each role. The writing is by JJW and Detal, the beat comes from JJW Productions, and both recording and production are credited to ChapMove Records, which also holds the rights shown for 2025. Of the 60 uploads on the channel checked during this research, it is the only one that carries the word bouyon.

The JJW Productions name is not isolated on this circuit: it also appears in the production credits of D'yonté's tracks on Next Gen Records, alongside Dré Soundz. That overlap of credits sketches a small scene of producers who readily label their releases bouyon, without this site drawing any conclusion about the personal ties between the artists.

What the page says about place

The “Mona Lisa” description gives two booking numbers with the 784 area code, the code of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, and a string of hashtags that opens with #chapmove, #dirtydetal, #Stvincent and #vincysoca before listing a long run of Caribbean territories. The first hashtags and the numbers place the release within the Vincentian circuit; the long list that follows mostly serves the video's reach and says nothing about the artist's origin.

This site therefore presents “Mona Lisa” as a Saint Vincent circuit release, which is what the page shows, without naming a birthplace for Detal. For how this kind of track moves between the two labels, see bouyon vs soca.

The limits of the file

The verifiable file on October 23, 2026 is a single YouTube listing on the artist's channel: one track, clear credits, a label, contacts. No biography, no interview, no streaming artist page found during this research.

This profile will be updated if Detal releases more tracks billed as bouyon, like the pages followed in new bouyon in 2026.
