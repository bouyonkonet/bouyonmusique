DJ Skaytah: Kay Ka Fet, Mass and carnival bouyon, Guadeloupe

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

DJ Skaytah is a DJ and producer active on the French speaking bouyon and shatta scene. His verified catalogue is a short, precise list: “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Real Shatta Kartel”, “Raggaboday (Instrumental)” and “It’s You”, plus a featured appearance on Dexterman “Ti Moun a Moun La”. His songwriting credits carry one name, Bradley Paulin. Beyond that, no published biography and no interview were found: this profile states what is verified, record by record, and states plainly what the public record does not support. Above all, he must not be confused with DJ Skycee, covered on a separate page of this site: the names look alike, the catalogues have nothing in common.

Who DJ Skaytah is: a stage name, a credits name, almost no biography

DJ Skaytah presents himself through records, not through a story. On the platforms, the name appears as producer and performer of bouyon and shatta in French and Creole. His song listings agree on one rare and useful point: a person name behind the stage name. “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Real Shatta Kartel” and “It’s You” credit Bradley Paulin as songwriter, “Raggaboday (Instrumental)” credits him as composer and programmer, and “Batman 2” also credits DJ Skaytah himself as producer. This profile therefore keeps the exact wording the sources support: the official credits of DJ Skaytah carry the name Bradley Paulin. It does not turn that credits name into a full legal identity, because no published source presents it that way.

No interview, no press article and no artist biography of DJ Skaytah was found during the research for this profile. His birthplace and his home base are stated by no reliable source consulted, and this profile does not invent them. What the sources show is a working geography: records that salute Guadeloupe, a carnival anthem picked up in soca and dennery sets, and listed dates in Bordeaux and Montreal, on the French Caribbean party circuit of France and the diaspora. The distinction matters: an artist can work for a Guadeloupe audience and play Bordeaux without this site being able to write where he comes from.

His verified accounts draw the same outline. His business Instagram, @djskaytah, lists as its official website his video “MASS 2 (WELCOME TO GUADELOUPE) #CARNAVAL2026”. His TikTok, @deejayskaytah, gives his Instagram and Snapchat as DjSkaytah in its bio and links to the same video. The description of that video also gives his YouTube channel, @djskaytah, and a Soundcloud account under the name skaytah. Those links, opened and checked one by one, are the ones the structured data of this page keeps, and no others.

Do not confuse them: Skaytah, Skycee, Skaylone, and the automatically generated profiles

The name DJ Skaytah lives in a dangerous neighbourhood. DJ Skycee, a near identical spelling, is a different artist, tied to the Stormwave label and to Miimii KDS record “Sé Miimii”: no record, no credit and no account overlaps with DJ Skaytah, and this site gives them two separate pages. DJ Skaylone, a third neighbouring name, is the maker of bouyon riddims published on YouTube since the late 2010s: again, nothing verified connects him to DJ Skaytah. A reader searching “sky” and “bouyon” meets three different artists, and search engines happily mix them up.

The second trap is the automatic profile. One music analytics page devoted to DJ SKAYTAH claims, in plainly generic copy, that he played Coachella, Lollapalooza and Bonnaroo, plus the BET Hip Hop Awards, with classics called “Get Low” and “Turn Up the Bass”. None of it matches his verified catalogue: none of those titles exists among his releases, and none of those festivals appears in any primary source. This profile cites that page only to disown it: it is the kind of copy produced without research, and a serious record must discard it instead of copying it. The same caution applies to the audience rankings of such platforms, which rest on no publicly checkable data for this artist.

The rule of this site is therefore simple, and it holds across the bouyon catalogue: a fact enters a profile when a primary source or a credits listing carries it. For DJ Skaytah, those sources are the Shazam and Apple Music listings of his records, his own opened and verified accounts, his official “Mass 2” video, and the pages of the events that bill him. Everything else, including the ready made biographies of aggregators, stays out.

The verified catalogue, record by record, from 2023 to 2025

The first verified trace of the name is a guest appearance. On March 25, 2023, Dexterman released “Ti Moun a Moun La”, featuring Haterz B and DJ Skaytah, on the UpFull Music label, on the album “2ème QUART TEMPS”. The song listing shows a tempo of 144 BPM. To this day it is the only documented sung collaboration of DJ Skaytah, and it already places him in the Creole production circuit where bouyon meets dancehall.

The year 2024 holds most of his solo catalogue. On January 12, 2024 came “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)”, the title track of an EP released by 3070122 Records DK2, in French, with DJ Skaytah credited as producer and a video titled “DJ SKAYTAH - KAY KA FET #1 (2024) BOUYON”. On February 3, 2024 followed “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, on the same label, in French, at 127 BPM, with Bradley Paulin credited as songwriter. The title states its programme: a salute to Guadeloupe, built for the carnival season, which became his signature to the point of calling for a sequel, “Mass 2”, for carnival 2026. The same period brought “Real Shatta Kartel”, a single whose writing is credited to Bradley Paulin, and whose title claims the other side of his sound, shatta.

Two further records complete the picture, without a release day this profile can guarantee. “Raggaboday (Instrumental)” is a 160 BPM instrumental, composed and programmed by Bradley Paulin, whose title sticks the word raboday, the Haitian electronic branch, onto the DJ world: a producer wink, not a documented lineage. “It’s You”, released as a single, credits Bradley Paulin as songwriter. Finally, on February 5, 2025, “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)” came out as a single on ATC, at 160 BPM, the high tempo of party bouyon, with Bradley Paulin on writing and DJ Skaytah on production. The “2024” kept in a 2025 release title suggests a track from the 2024 season issued later as a single: this profile notes the gap without inventing an explanation.

Read together, these records draw a short but coherent catalogue: seven verified recordings between 2023 and 2025, in French and Creole, split between carnival bouyon, shatta and one instrumental. It is the catalogue of a seasonal producer, built for carnival weeks and parties rather than for albums. To hear this side of the genre, our bouyon playlist and our bouyon gwada page place these records inside the Guadeloupe branch of bouyon, a genre born in Dominica.

Carnival as home ground: Mass, Mass 2 and the Montpel anthem

Carnival is the centre of gravity of DJ Skaytah. “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)” salutes the island at the height of its street season. Its sequel, “MASS 2 (WELCOME TO GUADELOUPE)”, is presented by an official video stamped #CARNAVAL2026, with this message in the description: “Bon carnaval a tous ! Bientot sur les plateformes !”, happy carnival to everyone, coming soon to the platforms. At the time that video serves as the official link of his Instagram, “Mass 2” is therefore a carnival record presented on video before its platform release: this profile describes it as such, and invents neither a streaming date nor listening figures for it.

Another clue shows that his carnival records travel beyond his own audience. The “Dennery Frenzy” edition of the Soca Podcast, a mix devoted to Saint Lucia dennery, lists in its tracklist a “Montpel Carnival Anthem” credited to DJ Skaytah, among records by Motto, Freezy and Blackboy. Dennery is the Saint Lucian cousin of bouyon, and that presence says something precise: DJ Skaytah carnival production is played by soca DJs in sets that mix the fast branches of the Caribbean. This profile keeps that listing as a trace of circulation, not as an extra catalogue record, because no release listing for this “Montpel Carnival Anthem” was found.

That position clarifies DJ Skaytah place in the genre. Bouyon was born in Dominica in the late 1980s, and its soca branch, bouyon soca, was started in Dominica in 2003 by producer Dada, according to the first hand testimony of Smokiller, published on our bouyon soca page. DJ Skaytah belongs to a later and different generation: the solo producers of the French Antilles and their diaspora, who deliver each season short anthems in Creole, calibrated for the street, the trucks and the parties. His work cannot be understood without that carnival calendar, from Guadeloupe to the rooms of Bordeaux and Montreal.

The live circuit and the networks: Bordeaux, Montreal, TikTok

Two event listings, checked on their official pages, show where DJ Skaytah plays. In Bordeaux, the party “La Addictive - Caribbean Lifestyle”, organised by Caera Dreams at Levrette Cafe, 6-8 rue de Merignac, on Sunday April 12, 2026 from 4 pm to 10 pm, bills him on the decks with Rimka, in a line up advertised as Rap, Dancehall, Bouyon, Afro and Shatta. In Montreal, the party “SUGAR CRUSH”, organised by Tropikal 97 at 680 rue Sainte-Catherine Ouest, bills him with Dj Magma, on a Saturday October 10, from 10.30 pm to 3 am. Two cities, two Caribbean diasporas, and the same format: the Caribbean party where bouyon holds a prime slot in the mix.

Those two dates rule out two opposite mistakes. The first would be to make him a Bordeaux or Montreal artist: a date proves a visit, not an address, and this profile assigns him no city of residence. The second would be to inflate these listings into a tour: they are two documented club nights, nothing more, and that is already useful information for an audience looking for where to hear this sound live. No other verified date was found for this profile.

Online, his presence is that of a working DJ. TikTok, where his @deejayskaytah account visibly serves as his main showcase with its live streams, Instagram as a business account, YouTube for the carnival videos, Soundcloud named in his own descriptions. This profile publishes no follower counts: those numbers change every week and say nothing about the music. It keeps only the verified handles, @djskaytah on Instagram and @deejayskaytah on TikTok, because they let the reader find the artist without landing on a namesake.

What this profile does not know, and why it says so

This profile ends with its limits, because they are part of the record. The birthplace, biography, beginnings and influences of DJ Skaytah are documented by no published source found. His credits name, Bradley Paulin, is the only person name the official listings carry, and it is quoted as such. No lasting label can be assigned to him beyond the per record mentions: 3070122 Records DK2 for the 2024 releases, ATC for “Batman 2”, UpFull Music for the Dexterman feature. No verified listening figure, chart placing or award can be published.

Those limits carry a value of their own. Bouyon and shatta in the French Antilles live largely outside written media: seasonal producers there are known to DJs, promoters and carnival audiences long before they exist in print. An honest profile is worth more than an invented biography, and that is the bet of this page: seven verified records, two verified dates, verified accounts, and openly admitted blanks. If the artist publishes a biography, an interview or new releases, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site.

To start listening, begin with “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)” and “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, continue with “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)” for the high tempo, then connect those records to our bouyon playlist and our bouyon gwada page. And if your search meant the other, near identical name, the one behind “Sé Miimii”, go to the DJ Skycee page: it is not the same artist.

Frequently asked questions

Who is DJ Skaytah?

DJ Skaytah is a DJ and producer active in French speaking bouyon and shatta. His verified catalogue includes “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Real Shatta Kartel”, “Raggaboday (Instrumental)” and “It’s You”. The official songwriting credits of those records carry the name Bradley Paulin. No published biography of the artist was found.

Is Bradley Paulin DJ Skaytah's real name?

Bradley Paulin is the name carried by DJ Skaytah's official credits, and this site presents it only as a credits name. He is songwriter on “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, “Real Shatta Kartel” and “It’s You”, and composer and programmer on “Raggaboday (Instrumental)”. No published source presents it as the artist's full legal identity.

Are DJ Skaytah and DJ Skycee the same artist?

No. Despite near identical names, they are two distinct artists, with no shared record or credit in the verified sources. DJ Skaytah is the producer of “Kay Ka Fet” and “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”. DJ Skycee is the DJ tied to the Stormwave label, known for “Sé Miimii” with Miimii KDS. This site gives them separate pages to end the confusion search engines maintain.

Is DJ Skaytah the same as DJ Skaylone?

No. DJ Skaylone, a third neighbouring name, is the author of bouyon riddims published on YouTube since the late 2010s, and no verified element links him to DJ Skaytah. Skaytah, Skycee and Skaylone are three different artists that search engines readily mix up. The DJ Skaytah on this page is the one behind “Kay Ka Fet”, “Mass” and the Bradley Paulin credits.

What are the verified bouyon records of DJ Skaytah?

Platform listings verify “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)”, released on January 12, 2024 on 3070122 Records DK2, “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”, released on February 3, 2024 on the same label, and “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, a single released on February 5, 2025 on ATC. Add “Real Shatta Kartel”, “Raggaboday (Instrumental)”, “It’s You” and the Dexterman feature “Ti Moun a Moun La”.

What is DJ Skaytah's Kay Ka Fet?

“Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)” is the lead track of an EP released on January 12, 2024 on 3070122 Records DK2, in French, with DJ Skaytah credited as producer. It comes with a video titled “DJ SKAYTAH - KAY KA FET #1 (2024) BOUYON”. It is the first record of 2024, the year that holds most of his own catalogue.

Who produced Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)?

DJ Skaytah himself: the “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)” listing credits him as producer, with Bradley Paulin as songwriter. The record came out as a single on February 5, 2025 on ATC, at 160 BPM, the high tempo of party bouyon. The “2024” kept in the title of a 2025 release suggests a 2024 season track later issued as a single.

What is “Mass 2 (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”?

It is the announced sequel to his carnival record “Mass (Welcome To Guadeloupe)”. Its official video, stamped #CARNAVAL2026, wishes everyone a happy carnival and announces “Bientot sur les plateformes”, coming soon to the platforms. It is also the official link placed on his Instagram profile @djskaytah. This site describes it as a carnival video presentation, with no verified streaming release date.

Does DJ Skaytah make bouyon or shatta?

Both. His carnival records are tagged bouyon, like “Kay Ka Fet, Vol. 1 (Bouyon 2024)” and “Batman 2 (Bouyon 2024)”, while “Real Shatta Kartel” claims in its title the other side of his sound, shatta. His 2023 feature for Dexterman, “Ti Moun a Moun La”, also places him in the Creole productions where bouyon meets dancehall.

What is DJ Skaytah's Montpel Carnival Anthem?

It is a record signed DJ Skaytah listed in the “Dennery Frenzy” programme of the Soca Podcast, a mix devoted to Saint Lucia dennery, the Saint Lucian cousin of bouyon, among records by Motto, Freezy and Blackboy. This site keeps it as a trace of his carnival production travelling through soca DJ sets, not as a catalogue release, since no release listing was found.

Did DJ Skaytah play Coachella?

Nothing shows that. A music analytics page claims, in plainly generic copy, that he played Coachella, Lollapalooza, Bonnaroo and the BET Hip Hop Awards with tracks called “Get Low” and “Turn Up the Bass”. None of those tracks exists in his verified catalogue and none of those festivals appears in a primary source: it is automatic copy, set aside here.

Where has DJ Skaytah been billed live?

Two verified event pages bill him: “La Addictive - Caribbean Lifestyle” at Levrette Cafe in Bordeaux, on Sunday April 12, 2026, with Rimka, in a Rap, Dancehall, Bouyon, Afro and Shatta line up, and “SUGAR CRUSH” at 680 rue Sainte-Catherine Ouest in Montreal, with Dj Magma. Those dates prove visits on the Caribbean party circuit, not a place of residence.
