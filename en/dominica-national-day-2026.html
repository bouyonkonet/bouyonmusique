Dominica bouyon music: National Day 2026, the 3 November

By Smokiller · Published October 30, 2026

On Tuesday 3 November 2026, Dominica's Independence season reaches its summit: the National Day Observance Ceremony takes place at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. The Office of the Prime Minister confirmed the time in its 29 October release: the ceremony commences at 5:30 p.m., for the 48th anniversary of independence.

48 years after 3 November 1978

Dominica News recalls the marker: Dominica achieved full independence from the United Kingdom on 3 November 1978. The 2026 edition carries the season's official theme, Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future.

The official programme published by the Office of the Prime Minister runs the season from 19 September to 4 November 2026. The 3 November ceremony is its official high point, between the National Cultural Gala on 1 November at the Botanic Gardens and the community service day on 4 November that closes the season.

The running order: military parade, address and awards

Dominica News describes the ceremony: a military parade, the Prime Minister's National Address and an award ceremony honouring some of the nation's most respectable citizens for their hard work and dedication.

Guide.dm lists the event at Windsor Park Sports Stadium as the National Day Observance Ceremony. This year's honours are already known: seventeen Dominicans will be recognised, in the list published by the Office of the Prime Minister on 29 October and detailed in our article on the 2026 independence awards.

The stadium of the season

Windsor Park has carried the whole season: the World Creole Music Festival played its three nights there from 23 to 25 October, the National Youth Rally gathered the country's students there on 29 October, and the 3 November ceremony brings the whole nation back, this time in official dress rather than fete dress.

The Minister for Culture, Greta Roberts, invited Dominicans at home and abroad to take full part in the celebrations: display the national flag, wear national and cultural attire, and support local artistes, musicians, farmers and vendors.

What follows the ceremony

The season does not stop on the evening of 3 November: the next day, Wednesday 4 November, the National Day of Community Service closes the programme, with community beautification and practical infrastructure projects across the country, per the official programme.

Between Jounen Kweyol today, 30 October, the Cultural Extravaganza and Eat Fish Day tomorrow, 31 October, the Gala on 1 November and this ceremony, the final stretch of the season chains five events in five days.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the National Day 2026 ceremony?

On Tuesday 3 November 2026 at 5:30 p.m. at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, per the Office of the Prime Minister release published on 29 October.

What does the 3 November ceremony include?

A military parade, the Prime Minister's National Address and the presentation of national awards to seventeen Dominicans, per Dominica News and the official 2026 awards release.

What is the theme of the 48th independence?

Our Journey, Our Legacy, Our Future, per the official season programme published by the Office of the Prime Minister. Dominica gained its independence from the United Kingdom on 3 November 1978.
