LK (Louse King): PORK NF, an Antigua Bouyon 2026 title

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

LK is the artist credited on “PORK NF (Antigua Bouyon 2026)”, the only video on the Louse King YouTube channel, uploaded May 22, 2026. The page itself labels the track as Antigua bouyon, credits the beat to Dj Kwab and the mix to Gotti Island Trap. That is the whole verifiable file: one title, short credits, and no biography.

One title, short credits

“PORK NF” runs 2 minutes 54. The description names three contributors: LK as artist, Dj Kwab as beat maker, and Gotti Island Trap for mixing and mastering. The page tags pair Bouyon, Soca and Antigua, and its hashtags point to bouyon music and the bouyon riddim. The channel name, Louse King, and the LK credit sit on the same page; this profile uses both, without claiming what the initials stand for beyond that pairing.

The title itself is slang the page does not explain, and this site does not explain it either.

Antigua Bouyon, a circuit label

“Antigua Bouyon 2026” places the track on the Antigua circuit, like other titles of the same season that claim an island bouyon. The label gives no birthplace or home base for LK, and no other opened page does: the origin stays undocumented. The context for the genre on the island is gathered on our page about bouyon in Antigua.

The limits of the file

One video, a channel listing that holds only that one, no streaming platform page opened, no interview: this profile is short because the file is. It will grow if LK publishes more titles or an official presentation, and is tracked in new bouyon in 2026.
