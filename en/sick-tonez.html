Sick Tonez

By Smokiller · Published July 20, 2026 · Updated July 26, 2026

Sick Tonez is a singer from Sint Maarten, the Dutch side of the island of Saint Martin. After freestyles and group songs, he released five bouyon singles between February and June 2026, most of them on riddims by Joshii G or YoJoshii.

Straight Outta Sint Maarten

In March 2021 he posted a live vlog on his channel called “Sick Tonez Straight Outta Sint Maarten”, filmed at a show. Five years later, the description of “My Ways” says the same thing in three words: “Made In Sint Maarten”. The #sintmaarten hashtag also runs under his 2026 videos.

Before bouyon, he shows up on group songs. “Bluff”, with Pharaoh G and Billy Hush, is past 5,300 views, one of his most watched videos. “Mad Man Shit” puts him with Twon Jahova, Big A and Pharaoh G, and a “Freestyle” official video is close to 1,500 views.

Cheater and Do You Love Me?, winter 2026

The turn came on February 1, 2026 with “Cheater”, shared with Jah2Chill and billed “Bouyon 2026”. Jah2Chill's channel holds the most watched upload, with more than 1,400 views. The distribution credits list Joshii G next to the two singers.

Two weeks later he released “Do You Love Me?” for Valentine's Day. The official video says he wrote the lyrics and sings them with Kali, that Joshii G made the riddim and that Young Pierre mixed and mastered it. March brought “Stand Up”, with Dt the Artist, again billed “Bouyon 2026”.

Dont Let Me Down, My Ways and Ride

In April, “Dont Let Me Down” shifted the mood a little: it is billed “Groovy Bouyon 2026”. The JoshiiMadeIt channel posted its video in May. At the end of June, “My Ways” closed the run, with the riddim, mix and master credited to YoJoshii.

He also turns up on other people's songs. In late March 2026, Le Dext released the single “Ride”, with Sick Tonez as a guest. All five of his 2026 singles came out under the same distribution label, which makes this year his first real bouyon season.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sick Tonez?

A singer from Sint Maarten, the Dutch side of Saint Martin, who has released bouyon singles since February 2026.

What are his bouyon songs?

Cheater with Jah2Chill, Do You Love Me?, Stand Up with Dt the Artist, Dont Let Me Down and My Ways, all billed as bouyon 2026.

Who makes his riddims?

Joshii G is credited on Cheater and Do You Love Me?, and YoJoshii on My Ways, where he also handles the mix and master. Young Pierre mixed Do You Love Me?.

What is his most watched bouyon song?

Cheater, in the upload on Jah2Chill's channel, with more than 1,400 views.

Has he worked with Le Dext?

Yes, he is a guest on Ride, a Le Dext single released in late March 2026.
