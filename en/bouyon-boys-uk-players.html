The Bouyon Boys welcome two UK-based players

By Smokiller · Published November 11, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Two UK-based players are joining Dominica's senior men's football team, the Bouyon Boys. Jayden Carbon, a striker, and Malachi Sharpe, a former Manchester United Under-21 player, have been called up ahead of the final two CONCACAF Nations League fixtures. The team nickname, Bouyon Boys, ties Dominican football directly to bouyon, Dominica's carnival music and dance.

Jayden Carbon and Malachi Sharpe

Jayden Carbon is presented as a striker. Malachi Sharpe is presented as a former Manchester United Under-21 player. Both players are based in the United Kingdom and are joining Dominica's senior men's squad.

The news was published by Dominica 1 on 11 November 2026. The same outlet published it on 10 November under a different headline, so it is one story, not two separate call-ups. No statement from the coach accompanies the announcement in the published text.

Two final Nations League fixtures

The call-ups come ahead of the final two CONCACAF Nations League fixtures. Dominica therefore closes its campaign in the competition with two reinforcements from England.

It should be said plainly: the dates, venues and opponents of the two fixtures are not in the published text, nor is the full squad list. This site's Bouyon Boyz Nations League page tells the team's story in the competition, without inventing the details the sources do not give.

Why the Bouyon Boys

The nickname Bouyon Boys ties the team to bouyon, Dominica's carnival music and dance. The published text says so explicitly: the nickname connects the squad to the sound that carries the island's identity, the same sound bouyon artists export across the Caribbean.

It is not the first time football and bouyon cross paths on this site. Bouyon gives the team its name, and the team gives bouyon new visibility among supporters who follow the Nations League. The two UK-based players arrive in a team whose very name is already a celebration.

What is not known yet

No opened source gives the dates and venues of the two final fixtures, the opponents, or the full list of called-up players. Those details will come from official announcements by the federation or by CONCACAF.

The same caution applies to the two players: their current clubs in the United Kingdom are not named in the published text, and Malachi Sharpe is presented only through his past with Manchester United Under-21s. We publish only what the source gives, and this page will be updated when the official details come out.
