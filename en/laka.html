Laka: the host of “BAISE-MOI”, and a file of two verified traces

Published October 20, 2026

Laka is the lead name on “BAISE-MOI”, a collective bouyon session released on November 22, 2023 on the label LAKA: a record in French, at 160 BPM, lining up eight guests, Riko, Le Bluff, Tgx, Billy Bybf, PIXOU, Le daah, WAYSEE and Team Bwè Tou Sa, and thirteen credited writers. A second trace exists: the song page lists, among the top songs of Laka, a “Manigans” by Ti Toto on which Laka appears as a guest. No biography, no published origin, no verifiable artist page and no other lead name record were found during the research for this profile. This profile publishes the two traces with their exact credits, reads the session through its writers, separates this Laka from his known namesakes, and states everything the file does not hold.

Who Laka is: the name that hosts the session

What the documents establish comes down to one role: Laka is the lead artist, the name holding the billing, of a collective recording, “BAISE-MOI”, released as a single on November 22, 2023. The label published on the song page carries the same name as the artist, LAKA: in the state of the documents, the release therefore appears under the own banner of the name that hosts it, the ordinary pattern of independent releases on this scene.

That host role is the heart of the file. Eight guest names sing on the record, thirteen writers sign it, and the name of Laka is what holds the whole together: that is how the collective sessions of Antilles bouyon work, where one name gathers the voices of a circuit for the length of one record. The opened documents describe neither the individual part of Laka in the record, nor his full name, nor his path: they document him as the name that convenes the session, by the place of that name in the credit, and nothing more.

Around that record, the research found nothing else under a lead name: no album, no EP, no second single, no interview, and no artist page opened and verified on the platforms consulted, from Shazam to Spotify, Deezer, Qobuz, Apple Music, YouTube and SoundCloud. Searches on this name return first to namesakes of other scenes, treated in a section of this profile. The Laka file is therefore a session file: one record as lead name, one guest appearance, and a network of collaborators which, for its part, is well documented.

Why publish this profile? Because “BAISE-MOI” is a document of the Guadeloupe bouyon scene of 2023, because eight artists are credited behind this name on it, and because readers searching for Laka deserve a page that distinguishes this artist from his namesakes instead of merging them. The right answer to a thin file is neither invention nor silence: it is the publication of the facts, with their limits displayed.

“BAISE-MOI”: the complete page of the 2023 session

According to the song page published by Shazam and opened for this profile, “BAISE-MOI” was released on November 22, 2023, on the label LAKA, as a single. The page states the language of the record, French, and its tempo, 160 BPM, the fast tempo of the party records of this scene. When the page was opened, the record counted 433 identifications on the platform: a usage counter of Shazam itself, which this profile reports as such, without converting it into a chart position.

The full title published by the page lines up eight guest credits: Riko, Le bluff, Tgx, Billy Bybf, PIXOU, Le daah, WAYSEE and Team Bwè Tou Sa. Several of those names have their own page on this site: Le Bluff, whose profile counts this session among his four verified traces of 2023, Billy Bybf, and the collective Team Bwè Tou Sa, whose central place in the writing of the record the next section shows. The other guest names, Riko, Tgx, PIXOU, Le daah and WAYSEE, are cited here exactly as the page prints them.

The format is that of the collective session, the gathering of the voices of one circuit on a single record, carried by a host name. The page displays, in its genre section, the tag “Christmas” attached to the session: an automatic platform classification, counted identically on other pages of the same scene, which this profile reports as a platform tag and not as a description of the record.

What the page does not give must be stated as well: no separate production credit is published on it beyond the thirteen writers of the composition and lyrics section, no video clip is documented in the open sources for this profile, and no follow up, neither remix nor second session under this name, was found. The record stands as the central and almost only piece of the file: it is the only document where the name of Laka holds the billing.

Thirteen writers: the Team Bwè Tou Sa core at the heart of the writing

The composition and lyrics section of the “BAISE-MOI” page names thirteen writers, and this profile cites them exactly as the page prints them: Marvin MAYOUTE, Queency Treyvor SOLVAR, Emmanuel PEREC, kerane landre, Yohan corneille, Fabien AURELA, Ludmilian FLOWER, Malric Lambert, Nicolas MACBETH, Daryl Biabiany, Terry BAPTISTE, ryan auguste and Ulrich Flower. Thirteen names for a session record: in this format, each voice generally writes its own part, and the writers list follows the list of participants and their studio circle.

Three of those names tell the workshop story of the record. The song page of “Game Over”, by Team Bwè Tou Sa, opened for this profile, gives the credits of the collective on that record released on July 8, 2022 on iMD-GAMMA_LAB, on the album “Voyé sa alléé II”, in French and at 144 BPM: Nicolas MACBETH is credited there with vocals and lyrics, Ludmilian FLOWER with vocals and lyrics, Terry BAPTISTE as composer, and Luce Rodrigue with programming and production. The first three are exactly three of the thirteen writers of “BAISE-MOI”.

The reading the documents allow is therefore precise: “BAISE-MOI” is a session of the Team Bwè Tou Sa circle, hosted under the name of Laka. The collective appears on it both as a guest in the title and as a writing core in the credits, alongside other names of its network, such as kerane landre, whose spelling matches the Keran Landre credited among the writers of “VIN TCHEK NOU”, the record of the Lewismelo album “Premières Notes” documented on this site in the Le Bluff profile. It is that network, and not a biography, that places Laka in the Guadeloupe bouyon scene.

One point of the page must be noted with the same precision: the name of Laka does not appear, as such, in the published list of thirteen writers. One of those names may be his in civil form, or none of them: no open source makes the link, and this profile does not guess it. It publishes the list as the page gives it, and the role of Laka as the title billing gives it: lead artist and host name of the session.

“Manigans”: the second trace, and its exact limit

The second verified trace of the name is found on the same page. The top songs section of Laka, on the “BAISE-MOI” page opened for this profile, lists a second title: “Manigans”, by Ti Toto, featuring Laka. Laka appears there in the reverse role of “BAISE-MOI”: a guest on the record of another artist, instead of the host of his own session.

On that record, the limit of the file must be stated without detour. Despite targeted and repeated searches, no song page of its own for “Manigans” was found or opened: neither a release date, nor a label, nor a tempo, nor writing credits are verified for this title. This profile therefore claims nothing beyond what the opened document establishes: the presence of “Manigans (feat. Laka)” in the list of titles associated with Laka on the “BAISE-MOI” page.

It is on that basis, and on that basis only, that “Manigans” enters the recordings list of this page schema: the rule of this site is that a recording appears there if an opened document shows it, and the opened “BAISE-MOI” page shows it. If a page of its own for the record is found, its credits will be added here and the page will be dated again. No other record, as lead name or as guest, was attached to Laka during the research.

The namesakes: what this profile does not merge

The name Laka is carried by several artists with no relation to each other, and that is the second trap of the file after its thinness. The most visible Laka on the web is a Bosnian singer, Elvir Laković, from Goražde and the Bosnian entrant at Eurovision 2008: searches on this single name return to him first. He has no documented link with the bouyon session of 2023, and nothing of his file is used here.

The same searches cross other bearers of the name or of neighbouring names: a kompa singer named Laurent Laka, associated with the Kompalaka world, a Liki Laka name in African music, and many projects named Laïka on other scenes. None of those artists is the artist of “BAISE-MOI” according to the opened documents, and this profile merges neither their biographies, nor their catalogs, nor their origins with the present file.

The proof chain of this profile is deliberately narrow: the opened “BAISE-MOI” page, its list of associated titles where “Manigans” appears, and the opened “Game Over” page, which establishes the writing core of Team Bwè Tou Sa. Everything that places Laka comes from those documents and the network they name: the bouyon scene of the French Antilles, and more precisely the Guadeloupe circle of Team Bwè Tou Sa, Billy Bybf and Le Bluff. No personal origin beyond that network is claimed.

What this profile claims, and what it leaves open

Let the file be summed up. Claimed and sourced: Laka is the lead artist of “BAISE-MOI”, a single in French at 160 BPM released on November 22, 2023 on the label LAKA, with eight credited guests and thirteen named writers. Claimed and sourced: three of those writers, Nicolas MACBETH, Ludmilian FLOWER and Terry BAPTISTE, form the documented writing core of Team Bwè Tou Sa, a guest collective of the record. Claimed and sourced: a “Manigans” by Ti Toto featuring Laka appears among the titles associated with Laka on the opened page of the single.

Open means: identity, origin and path of Laka not published, individual part in the session not described by the sources, a page of its own for “Manigans” not found, official accounts not verified by opening and therefore not published, the sameAs field of this page staying empty for that reason. Publishing the file under these conditions is better than the two symmetrical errors: inventing a biography, or letting the name be confused with that of the Bosnian singer the search engines return first.

To hear the current of Laka, start with “BAISE-MOI” itself, then with the records of the collective at the heart of the session on our Team Bwè Tou Sa page, and with our bouyon playlist, which follows the current Guadeloupe and Antilles scene. Our bouyon gwada page describes the Guadeloupean form of the genre in which this session sits. If the Laka file thickens, this page will grow with it, dated and corrected like every page on this site.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Laka?

Laka is the lead name on “BAISE-MOI”, a collective bouyon session released on November 22, 2023 on the label LAKA, with eight guests and thirteen credited writers. No public biography documents his full name, his origin or his path, and this profile says so openly.

What is “BAISE-MOI”?

“BAISE-MOI” is a single by Laka released on November 22, 2023, in French, at 160 BPM, crediting eight guests: Riko, Le bluff, Tgx, Billy Bybf, PIXOU, Le daah, WAYSEE and Team Bwè Tou Sa. Its page counted 433 Shazam identifications when it was opened for this profile.

What are the verified records of Laka?

Two traces: “BAISE-MOI”, as lead artist, and “Manigans”, a record by Ti Toto on which Laka appears as a guest, listed among the top songs of Laka on the opened “BAISE-MOI” page. No page of its own for “Manigans” was found: neither its date, nor its label, nor its credits are verified.

What is the link between Laka and Team Bwè Tou Sa?

Team Bwè Tou Sa is a guest on “BAISE-MOI”, and its documented writing core, Nicolas MACBETH, Ludmilian FLOWER and Terry BAPTISTE, credited on the collective record “Game Over” in 2022, appears among the thirteen writers of the Laka record. The session is therefore documented as a record of the collective circle, hosted under the name of Laka.

Is Laka the Bosnian singer from Eurovision?

No. The most visible Laka on the web is Elvir Laković, a Bosnian singer from Goražde, the Bosnian entrant at Eurovision 2008. He is a namesake with no documented link to the artist of “BAISE-MOI”, whom the sources consulted place only in the bouyon network of the French Antilles, without publishing his personal origin.

Where can I hear Laka and the bouyon of his scene?

Hear “BAISE-MOI” on the platforms, then the records of Team Bwè Tou Sa and Le Bluff, and our bouyon playlist, which follows the current Guadeloupe and Antilles scene.
