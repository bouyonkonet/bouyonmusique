Okra Slime Riddim: 10Star's Barbados bouyon riddim (2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 28, 2026

The Okra Slime Riddim is a 10Star Records riddim that surfaced in spring 2026 and was billed, song after song, as "Barbados Bouyon". It takes its name from Sharkie's "Okra Slime", whose visualizer went up on March 24, 2026. Within ten weeks at least six more songs landed on it, from Quan De Artist to Ian Stylez, nearly all produced, mixed or overseen by 10Star.

A 10Star Records riddim, opened by Sharkie

It starts on March 24, 2026. That day the BimTraveler channel posted "Sharkie X 10Star - Okra Slime", a visualizer tagged "2026 Bouyon" with the hashtag bouyonriddim, and the single "Okra Slime (feat. Sharkie)" reached Deezer the same day on the 10Star Music Prod label. The official audio followed on April 5, now labelled "Barbados Bouyon 2026". The credits are short: written by 10Star and Jamal Beckles, recorded, produced, mixed and mastered by 10Star Records.

So the riddim is 10Star's work, a name that shows up in three spellings across the pages (10Star, 10Star Records, 10 Star Records). No page we opened gives the person behind the name, or a release date for the riddim as a whole: all we have are the upload dates of the songs. See also our page on the bouyon riddim and the profile of Sharkie.

April 2026: the series fills out

On April 2, Quan De Artist, the Barbadian singer Jaquan Headley, put out "Do It Like That" on his own channel, recorded and produced by 10Star Records; Deezer dates it April 3, label 10Star Music Prod / QDAent. On April 5, Walkes released "No Talk", which he billed as soca 2026, with 10 Star Records as executive producer and on the mix. The next day BimTraveler posted Stella Billy's "Energy", written by the singer and produced by 10Star Records.

On April 18, Fiddy ii uploaded "All Kinda Gal", co-written with Stephan "C4" Hope, again produced by 10 Star Records and carrying both Barbados Bouyon and Soca 2026 in its title. On April 29 the 246Mystery channel added "How We Looking (Good)", a trio track by Gaza, Scoobz and Dwayne3Dot, filed as Barbados Soca 2026. Double labelling is the norm on this riddim: bouyon in the title, soca in the hashtags, often both at once.

Up to Crop Over: Crazy Jump, and what we don't know

The latest song we found is from June 4, 2026: Ian Stylez's "Crazy Jump" on BimTraveler, titled "Bouyon Crop Over 2026", with no credits in the description. Crop Over, Barbados' big summer festival, also appears in the hashtags of Sharkie, Stella Billy, Quan De Artist and Fiddy ii. The riddim was plainly aimed at that season.

View counts are modest, from a few hundred to just over 2,000 per song when read, Quan De Artist's being the highest. An eighth song, "Mr Never Sober" by $yco and JK The Boss, circulates on an edits channel and is left out here because no artist page carries it. This page is built from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 28, 2026; a singer's origin is given only where a source publishes it.

Frequently asked questions

Who produced the Okra Slime Riddim?

10Star Records, credited as producer on Sharkie's Okra Slime, Quan De Artist's Do It Like That, Stella Billy's Energy and Fiddy ii's All Kinda Gal; the person behind 10Star is not named.

When was the Okra Slime Riddim released?

No release date for the full riddim is published. The first song, Sharkie's Okra Slime, came out on March 24, 2026 as a visualizer and a Deezer single; the latest one found, Ian Stylez's Crazy Jump, on June 4, 2026.

Is the Okra Slime Riddim bouyon?

The songs present it that way: Barbados Bouyon 2026 for Sharkie, Stella Billy and Quan De Artist, Bouyon Crop Over 2026 for Ian Stylez. Several also carry a soca label.

Which artists are on the Okra Slime Riddim?

Sharkie, Quan De Artist, Walkes, Stella Billy, Fiddy ii, the trio Gaza, Scoobz and Dwayne3Dot, and Ian Stylez.

Why does Crop Over come up?

The CropOver hashtag is on the pages of Sharkie, Stella Billy, Quan De Artist and Fiddy ii, and Ian Stylez puts it in his title: the riddim targets the 2026 Barbados Crop Over season.
