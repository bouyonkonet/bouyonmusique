Bouyon and R&B

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Bouyon is a fast, percussive sound, made for dancing. R&B is soft, melodic singing, made for voices. The two have met inside bouyon since its beginnings, and a new generation says so out loud: Fallon calls her universe RnBouyon, a fusion of R&B and bouyon, and Theodora took a bouyon track to the first gold single of the genre in France.

Fallon names RnBouyon

Fallon is a singer who places her music between R&B and bouyon. She was selected for Deezer Next 2026, the Deezer programme that highlights artists to watch, announced on 4 January 2026. The Deezer newsroom, on 4 February 2026, puts it plainly: she calls her universe RnBouyon, a fusion of R&B and bouyon, blending softness and energy to create a style in her own image, with authentic songwriting already heard in the tracks Normal and Memories.

In an interview, Fallon says her music lives between R&B and bouyon. It comes from her love of singing R&B, but also from rhythm, energy and movement, and she wanted to pay tribute to her Caribbean culture through bouyon. On Normal, she says, the balance between R&B and bouyon feels the most natural. RnBouyon is therefore not a journalist word: it is the name the artist herself gives to her sound.

Theodora, the proof from the charts

Theodora, a French-Congolese singer, took bouyon to success on the French charts. Her track Kongolese sous BBL was released on 27 September 2024, produced by Jeez Suave, with an instrumental that is a bouyon version of the track FNG, released in February 2024. The track appears on her mixtape Bad Boy Lovestory, released on 1 November 2024.

In December 2024, Kongolese sous BBL became the first bouyon song certified a gold single in France. The track reached No. 10 on the SNEP singles chart, after climbing from No. 113 to No. 35 in early November 2024. Her universe blends shatta, bouyon, R&B, pop and electronic music. The success of the track also opened a debate in the Caribbean community about the use of bouyon by a non-Antillean artist: a real discussion, reported as such, not a judgement from this page.

The R&B root of Wrecketeng

The link between R&B and bouyon is older than RnBouyon. Wrecketeng, the practice born in Dominica that turns international tracks into bouyon, takes well-known vocals, often R&B or hip-hop voices, and lays them over a fast bouyon base. DJ Cut, Bushtown Clan and Nursery Krew popularised this way of working, with flips of Jay-Z Big Pimpin and Chamillionaire Ridin Dirty cited as examples by the account of the genre.

That account is the genre own story, not a truth established by an independent source: the page that carries it flags its own insufficient sources. It remains useful because it describes a real practice documented elsewhere on this site: Wrecketeng removes the rhythm section of a track and lays the vocal over bouyon. Bouyon, for its part, has always kept a place for smooth voices: an analysis picked up by the press describes the genre as the alliance of pounding, syncopated percussion and melodic singers, in the line of the crooners.

Maurane Voyer, an R&B voice in bouyon

Maurane Voyer, a Martinican singer, is another example of an R&B voice working in bouyon. Her repertoire is described as zouk, R&B and creole pop, and her duo Joli BB with Reo, released on 9 October 2026, is a bouyon track. The meeting is natural: bouyon gives the rhythm and the energy, the R&B voice gives the melody and the softness.

The RnBouyon of Fallon, the gold single of Theodora and the voice of Maurane Voyer draw the same map: R&B is not a guest in bouyon, it is one of its ingredients, and the 2026 generation claims it as such.

Frequently asked questions

What is RnBouyon?

RnBouyon is the name the singer Fallon gives to her universe: a fusion of R&B and bouyon, blending softness and energy. She was selected for Deezer Next 2026, and her tracks Normal and Memories are the first cited examples of this style.

What is the first bouyon gold single in France?

Kongolese sous BBL by Theodora, released on 27 September 2024, became the first bouyon song certified a gold single in France, in December 2024. The track reached No. 10 on the SNEP singles chart.

Has R&B always been part of bouyon?

Yes, through the voice and through the flip. Wrecketeng lays well-known vocals, often R&B or hip-hop, over a fast bouyon base, and bouyon has always kept a place for melodic singers. The RnBouyon of Fallon gives a name to that meeting in 2026.
