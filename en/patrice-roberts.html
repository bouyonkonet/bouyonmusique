Patrice Roberts: Trinidad soca and her bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Patrice Roberts is a soca singer from Trinidad, born in 1986 and raised in Toco, a fishing village on the island. She is not a bouyon artist: her name was made at the Road March and in duets with Machel Montano. Two of her songs do run through bouyon, though: “Dutty Bad” in 2019, then “Taking Over” with Asa Bantan in 2023.

A soca voice, not a bouyon one

Patrice Roberts started singing at eight and also plays steelpan. She broke through in 2005 with “The Islands”, sung with her second cousin Bunji Garlin. The next year she won the 2006 Road March with “Band of the Year”, alongside Machel Montano.

The duo with Montano keeps coming back, up to “Like Yuh Self” in 2023, whose video is close to 10 million views. This profile does not count her among bouyon artists. It keeps only the two songs whose official pages talk about bouyon.

Dutty Bad, on the Simon Say Riddim

In January 2019, the Teamfoxx team released the Simon Say Riddim, which it described as a bouyon sounding riddim. Its notes even explain the genre: music born in Dominica, with conch shell, accordion, kick, snare and live bass.

Patrice Roberts sings “Dutty Bad” on it, written by Jovan James and produced by Lashley “Motto” Winter for Teamfoxx. The same riddim carries Motto, from Saint Lucia, and Asa Bantan, billed as the Dominican king of bouyon. The Teamfoxx upload is labelled “Soca Bouyon 2019”.

Taking Over, with Asa Bantan

On February 3, 2023, Asa Bantan released “Taking Over” with Patrice Roberts on the Dark Knight Riddim, produced by Kendel “Killer” Laurent. The two singers wrote it, Dizzell Bueno mixed it and Scratch Master mastered it. Ricardo Drue is co executive producer, and Wildfire sings backing vocals.

On Asa Bantan's channel the audio is filed as bouyon 2023 and soca 2023, under North Island Records and Bouyon Boss Entertainment. It has passed 28,000 views. Soca promo channels file it as soca, which says a lot about where the song sits, right on the border of the two genres. See our bouyon vs soca page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Patrice Roberts a bouyon artist?

No. She is a soca singer from Trinidad. She has, however, sung on two bouyon productions: Dutty Bad in 2019 and Taking Over with Asa Bantan in 2023.

Where is Patrice Roberts from?

Trinidad. She grew up in Toco, a fishing village, and the Teamfoxx notes call her “Trinidad's very own”.

What is Taking Over?

A 2023 song sung with Asa Bantan on the Dark Knight Riddim by Kendel “Killer” Laurent, filed as bouyon 2023 on Asa Bantan's channel.

What is the Simon Say Riddim?

A 2019 Teamfoxx riddim, presented as a bouyon sounding riddim, with Patrice Roberts, Motto and Asa Bantan.

How is she related to Bunji Garlin?

He is her second cousin. They sang The Islands together in 2005, the song that made her known in Trinidad.
