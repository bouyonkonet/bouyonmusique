Dj Dirtee: from Kay teka fe to Touch and Go, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Dj Dirtee is a DJ and producer from the French speaking bouyon scene whose name can almost only be read in platform credits. After a full search, his verifiable public record comes down to a run of precise releases: a production credit in 2020 on Skynix “Kay téka fè” with Gucci C, appearances in 2021 with Team Bwè Tou Sa and on his own single “I ja lè”, an English language record in 2024, “Touch and Go”, with Trixzmigos and Fetty Mark, then two collaborations in 2025 inside the circle of DJ Softee and Tiitii NBA. No biography, no interview and no verified official account were found. This profile therefore publishes the credits, in order, carefully separates this artist from his American and British namesakes, and states plainly everything the record does not establish.

Who Dj Dirtee is: a producer only the credits can tell

Dj Dirtee has no presentation page in the press consulted during this research, and that is the first fact of this profile. Where the singers of the scene have videos, interviews and documented social profiles, this DJ producer only exists publicly where platforms print credits: as producer of another artist record, as featured artist on a collective album, as lead artist of two singles, and as a guest name inside the long collective titles of recent Guadeloupe bouyon. Rebuilding his path therefore means reading those credits as a register, record by record, without adding anything between the lines.

The name itself is a namesake trap, and this profile rules the namesakes out before anything else. Dj Dirtee is not DJ Dirtee $keet, the stage name of bassist Nicholas Largen of the Jacksonville punk band Whole Wheat Bread, documented by Wikipedia and by Consequence magazine: that American musician, active in the Florida underground rap scene in the early 2000s, has no established link with bouyon. He is not the British breakbeat DJ who signed as Dirtee and published his biography on the Nubreaks forum in 2010, after years of London internet radio. He is not the hip hop manager DJ Dirrty, also called JDirrt, profiled by The Source in 2013 alongside Trinidad James. No fact from those three careers is used here, and the spelling matters: the bouyon producer writes Dirtee, with one r and a double e at the end, exactly as the platforms print his credits.

What remains, once the namesakes are set aside, still draws a coherent territory. His credited collaborators belong to the French and Creole speaking bouyon scene: Gucci C, who returns on three records, Team Bwè Tou Sa and Lunik, then Trixzmigos and Fetty Mark on the English speaking side, and finally DJ Softee, Tiitii NBA, DJ Boko and Le K. Two names return behind the scenes and serve as the thread of this profile: Steeve Fleret, credited as songwriter on “I ja lè”, on the megamix that bears the Dj Dirtee name and as songwriter and producer of “LA PEP'HIT FREESTYLE”, and Julian White, writer of the DJ Softee records Dj Dirtee appears on. This profile does not turn those recurrences into a biography: it reads them for what they are, the trace of one working circle over several years.

2020 and 2021: production first, then the first records under his name

The first verified credit in the record is a production credit, and it is dated July 15, 2020. The record “Kay téka fè”, performed by Skynix with Gucci C and released by the label iMD-TQTMP, credits Dylan Kézia Frair as composer and Dj Dirtee as producer, in the production and engineering section of its Shazam page. The published tempo is 160 BPM, the cruising speed of modern bouyon. That back room role matters for reading what follows: Dj Dirtee enters the public record as the man behind another artist record, before he appears in front of one.

The year 2021 shows him in two other hats. On July 29, 2021, the Team Bwè Tou Sa album “VOYÉ SA ALLÉÉ”, released by T-BTS, includes “Tik tak”, which credits him as a featured artist with Lunik, at 155 BPM and in French. On November 9, 2021, he released under his own name “I ja lè”, with Gucci C, on 1899862 Records DK, the same label number that would return on “Touch and Go” three years later. The record page credits Steeve Fleret as songwriter. Its tempo is listed at 80 BPM, exactly half the usual tempo of the scene, which platforms often do with fast records: this profile quotes the published figure without correcting it on its own authority.

A third record from this period calls for particular care, and this profile states it. The Shazam page of “Moto”, by Tytys with Gucci C, from the album “Mr Chodé”, attaches an official video whose full title is “TYTYS x GUCCI C - MOTO (Feat.DJ DIRTEE)”. The Dj Dirtee name therefore appears in the video title, but not in the track title as the platform lists it. This profile holds to the exact fact: a published video credit associates Dj Dirtee with “Moto”, alongside the same two names, Tytys and Gucci C. It does not count it as a Dj Dirtee single and does not place it in the recording list of its schema, for lack of a platform credit at the same level as the others.

Touch and Go and Wuk up di riddim: the English speaking side of the record

The most complete record in the file arrives on February 7, 2024. “Touch & Go”, released by Dj Dirtee as lead artist with Trixzmigos and Fetty Mark, again on 1899862 Records DK, is an English language single at 160 BPM. The reciprocal credit confirms that this is no platform error: the Shazam artist page of Trixzmigos, a group from the Dominican scene, lists among its most streamed songs “Touch and go (feat. Fetty mark & Dj dirtee)”. The same recording is therefore filed on both sides, with the French speaking producer and with the English speaking group, which makes it the documented junction point between the two halves of bouyon, that of the French Antilles and that of English speaking Dominica.

The other English speaking trace is more fragile and this profile presents it as such. A community chart on the VirtualDJ platform lists “Dj Dirtee Wuk up di riddim Bouyon”, dated 2023 and filed under hip hop and rap. No streaming platform page consulted documents that title with a date, a label or credits comparable to the other records in this profile. It is therefore quoted here as a DJ chart trace, with its exact title, and it appears in the recording list of the schema on that basis only: a title DJs programme and chart, whose official page remains to be established. In a scene where riddims often travel as files before they travel as metadata, that difference in status is part of the fact.

Those two English speaking traces matter because they shift the portrait. Read only through his French records, Dj Dirtee would be a producer from the Guadeloupe workshop. “Touch and Go” shows him working with voices from Dominica, in the language of original bouyon, born in Dominica and told on our history of bouyon page. Bouyon soca, the branch started in Dominica in 2003 by producer Dada according to the signed testimony of Smokiller and popularised by Triple Kay, taught the scene these crossings between islands and between languages. Dj Dirtee, at his documented scale, works exactly on that seam.

2024 and 2025: the DJ Softee circle, Tiitii NBA and the collective freestyles

In 2024, the Dj Dirtee name appears in a title that is not his, and the nuance is one of the most instructive facts in the file. On April 4, 2024, LAPEPHIT released “Dj Dirtee (No limit Megamix)”, on MJ BEATS-RECORD, inside the album “No Limit Riddim”, in French, with Steeve Fleret credited as songwriter. A megamix that bears a DJ name in its title belongs to a known practice of sound system scenes: the name travels as a signature or a tribute inside another artist title. This profile therefore does not count that record as a Dj Dirtee recording, and it says so, because the name inside a title and the author of the title are two different things, especially for DJs.

The year 2025, on the other hand, brings him back in full credit, twice, in the same circle. The Deezer page of DJ Softee lists the EP “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”, whose sixth track is “Supalova”, with Tiitii NBA and DJ Dirtee, tagged bouyon warm up 2026 and credited in writing to Julian White. On June 4, 2025, DJ Softee released the single “LA PEP'HIT FREESTYLE”, a long format of 9 minutes 42 according to Deezer, which credits Tiitii NBA, DJ Boko, Le K and DJ Dirtee as featured artists, at 160 BPM, with Steeve Fleret and Julian White in writing and Steeve Fleret in production according to Shazam. The format speaks for itself: a collective freestyle of nearly ten minutes, where the DJs and voices of one team take turns, is the workshop form of current Guadeloupe bouyon, and Dj Dirtee appears there in full name, at the same rank as the others.

Taken together, those 2025 credits confirm the position the 2020 credits announced. Dj Dirtee works in the extended team of DJ Softee and Tiitii NBA, one of the most active names in French speaking bouyon, without the public record allowing anything more to be said about the nature of that work: production, voice, selection or studio presence are not separated by the pages consulted, and this profile does not choose in their place. What it can say is dated and sourced: between 2020 and 2025, the name returns, with recurring labels and writers, always at bouyon tempos, always in Creole and French company, with one documented English speaking crossing in 2024.

What this profile does not know, and why this page exists

The limits of the file have to be named one by one. No source consulted gives Dj Dirtee full name, his island of birth, his town, his age or the start of his activity before the 2020 credit. No interview, no press article and no published artist biography were found during this research. No official social or platform account was verified by direct opening as his, so the schema of this page contains no external link: linking the wrong account, among the many Dirtees of the world, would be worse than linking none.

This page exists in spite of those blanks, and even because of them. Modern bouyon is a music of producers and credits: its records live on platforms with pages that are sometimes complete, sometimes reduced to a name and a tempo. A DJ producer like Dj Dirtee can work for five years in a scene, produce for Skynix, sing with Gucci C, cross Trixzmigos and appear on the freestyles of DJ Softee, without ever receiving a line of biography. Gathering his verified credits, ruling out his namesakes and refusing the records that are not his is already writing his history with the only honest materials available.

To hear the sound in which these credits make sense, our bouyon playlist gathers bouyon, bouyon soca and new releases followed by Bouyon Shattaz, and our bouyon soca page tells the producer branch of the genre. If Dj Dirtee or his team confirm an identity, an origin or official accounts, this profile will be completed and dated like every page on this site.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Dj Dirtee?

Dj Dirtee is a DJ and producer from the French speaking bouyon scene, documented only by his platform credits: producer of Skynix’s “Kay téka fè” in 2020, lead artist of “I ja lè” in 2021 and of “Touch and Go” in 2024, then featured on DJ Softee’s “Supalova” and “LA PEP'HIT FREESTYLE” in 2025. The record consulted establishes neither his full name nor his origin.

Is Dj Dirtee the former punk bassist DJ Dirtee $keet?

No. DJ Dirtee $keet is the stage name of Nicholas Largen, bassist of the Jacksonville, Florida punk band Whole Wheat Bread, documented by Wikipedia and Consequence. The British breakbeat DJ named Dirtee, active on London internet radio and the Nubreaks forum, and the hip hop manager DJ Dirrty, called JDirrt, profiled by The Source in 2013, are yet other people. None of their facts belong in this profile.

What is the earliest known Dj Dirtee credit?

A production credit: “Kay téka fè”, by Skynix with Gucci C, released on July 15, 2020 on the label iMD-TQTMP and composed by Dylan Kézia Frair, credits Dj Dirtee with production and engineering on its Shazam page, at 160 BPM. He therefore enters the public record as the man behind someone else’s track, before appearing under his own name.

Is “Dj Dirtee (No limit Megamix)” a Dj Dirtee record?

No, not according to the published credits. The record, released on April 4, 2024 on MJ BEATS-RECORD inside the album “No Limit Riddim”, is credited to LAPEPHIT, with Steeve Fleret as songwriter. The Dj Dirtee name appears in the megamix title, which documents the circulation of his name in the scene, but this profile does not count it among his recordings.

Who did Dj Dirtee collaborate with in 2025?

With the DJ Softee circle. Deezer lists “Supalova”, with Tiitii NBA and DJ Dirtee, on the DJ Softee EP “Chodong Route Empire C.R.E”, and the single “LA PEP'HIT FREESTYLE”, released on June 4, 2025, credits Tiitii NBA, DJ Boko, Le K and DJ Dirtee, with Steeve Fleret and Julian White in writing.

Where can I hear the bouyon of the Dj Dirtee scene?

Start with his verified records, “I ja lè”, “Touch and Go”, “Supalova” and “LA PEP'HIT FREESTYLE”, all published on the streaming platforms, then hear our bouyon playlist, which gathers bouyon, bouyon soca and new releases from Dominica and the French Antilles followed by Bouyon Shattaz.

What is Dj Dirtee’s Touch and Go?

It is his most complete record: an English language single released on February 7, 2024 on 1899862 Records DK, at 160 BPM, with Trixzmigos and Fetty Mark. The Shazam page of Trixzmigos, a group from the Dominica scene, lists the same track in return. “Touch and Go” thus links French Antilles bouyon and the bouyon of English speaking Dominica.

What is I ja lè, Dj Dirtee’s single with Gucci C?

“I ja lè” is a track released under Dj Dirtee’s name on November 9, 2021, with Gucci C, on 1899862 Records DK, the label that would release “Touch and Go” in 2024. Its sheet credits Steeve Fleret as songwriter. The listed tempo, 80 BPM, is half the scene’s usual tempo, a common platform notation for fast tracks.

What is the link between Dj Dirtee and Gucci C?

Gucci C is his most frequent collaborator: he sings on Skynix’s “Kay téka fè”, produced by Dj Dirtee in 2020, on “I ja lè”, Dj Dirtee’s 2021 single, and on Tytys’s “Moto”, whose official video carries the mention “Feat. DJ DIRTEE”. Three tracks that sketch the same workshop over several years.

Is Dj Dirtee on Tytys’s Moto?

In the video title, yes: the Shazam page of “Moto”, by Tytys with Gucci C, from the album “Mr Chodé”, links an official video titled “TYTYS x GUCCI C - MOTO (Feat.DJ DIRTEE)”. The track as the platform lists it does not name him. This profile therefore keeps a video credit, without counting “Moto” among his recordings.

Who is Steeve Fleret, the name that keeps coming up with Dj Dirtee?

Steeve Fleret is a songwriter and producer credited several times around Dj Dirtee: writer of “I ja lè” in 2021, writer of LAPEPHIT’s “Dj Dirtee (No limit Megamix)” in 2024, then writer and producer of DJ Softee’s “LA PEP’HIT FREESTYLE” in 2025, according to Shazam. He is the common thread of the record.

Did Dj Dirtee work with Team Bwè Tou Sa?

Yes. Team Bwè Tou Sa’s album “VOYÉ SA ALLÉÉ”, released by T-BTS on July 29, 2021, includes “Tik tak”, which credits Dj Dirtee as a featured artist with Lunik, in French and at 155 BPM according to Shazam. It is his first appearance as a featured artist, one year after his production credit on “Kay téka fè”.

Is Wuk up di riddim a Dj Dirtee track?

It is a DJ chart trace, not a documented release. A VirtualDJ community chart lists “Dj Dirtee Wuk up di riddim Bouyon”, dated 2023 and filed under hip hop and rap. No streaming platform sheet consulted gives it a release date, a label or credits comparable to the other tracks in the record.
