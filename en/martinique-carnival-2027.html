Martinique Carnival 2027

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

Martinique Carnival 2027 runs Sunday 7 to Wednesday 10 February, in Fort-de-France. Four Fat Days, from Fat Sunday to Ash Wednesday, with the Vaval parade, the burlesque weddings, the great red parade of the red devils and the burning of Vaval to close the festival. The season opens Sunday 3 January 2027, and the edition runs under the theme An Zafe Nou.

The Fat Days, 7 to 10 February

The 2027 Fat Days run Sunday 7 to Wednesday 10 February, in Fort-de-France. Sunday 7 February, Fat Sunday, opens the festival with the parade of Vaval, the Carnival King, followed by the queens and mini-queens, the walking groups and the first bradjaks, old cars repainted in bright colours. Monday 8 February, Fat Monday, brings the burlesque weddings, where the roles are reversed between brides and grooms, in white.

Tuesday 9 February, Shrove Tuesday, is the day of the great red parade, with the red devils and the floats. Wednesday 10 February, Ash Wednesday, sees residents parade in black and white for the mourning of Vaval, burned in the evening, and Lent begins. Saturday 6 February, the eve of the Fat Days, is the day of the parade of the carnival queens and mini-queens, parading in Fort-de-France. Some guides count the Fat Days from Saturday 6 to Wednesday 10 February; the official Fat Days remain Sunday 7 to Wednesday 10, with Saturday 6 as the queens day.

The theme An Zafe Nou

The 2027 edition runs under the theme An Zafe Nou. The theme was chosen by a public vote and announced in late August 2026 by the City of Fort-de-France, which runs the programme in the capital. The visual direction of the edition is inspired by the Maretet epi Chapo, the traditional Martinican headwear.

It should be said plainly: two wordings circulate. Some guides present Maretet epi Chapo as the theme itself, while the most detailed pages give An Zafe Nou as the theme and Maretet epi Chapo as the visual direction. We follow the second reading, the best documented. The 2027 Vaval, the effigy of the Carnival King, had not been unveiled by late October 2026, so this page describes and names no effigy for this year.

The season, from 3 January to Mi-Careme

The carnival season opens Sunday 3 January 2027, the first Sunday of January, Epiphany, with pre-carnival parades in the communes every weekend in January, notably in Le Lamentin, Riviere-Pilote and Sainte-Marie. The great parades of the Fat Days pass along boulevard General-de-Gaulle and La Savane, in central Fort-de-France.

Mi-Careme falls on Thursday 4 March 2027. Martinique school carnival holidays run Saturday 6 to Monday 22 February 2027. Before the Fat Days, pre-carnival parties are announced, such as Carnivalicious Red and Rubis on Friday 29 January 2027 and Jouvert x Bliss on Saturday 30 January 2027. These are private parties listed by event agendas, not the official programme: no official hour-by-hour parade programme for the Fat Days had been published by 10 November 2026.

Carnival, home of Martinican bouyon

Martinique is a bouyon island. Maurane Voyer, a Martinican singer, released Joli BB with Reo on 9 October 2026, a bouyon duo between Martinique and Dominica. Miimii KDS, an artist from neighbouring Guadeloupe, belongs to the same French bouyon scene that runs through the carnivals of the French Antilles.

Guadeloupe Carnival 2027 runs over the same period, with Fat Days from 7 to 10 February 2027 as well. For bouyon fans, the two carnivals form a single French season: the same weeks, the same Fat Days, and streets where bouyon, zouk and traditional music share the stage.

Frequently asked questions

When is Martinique Carnival 2027?

The Fat Days run Sunday 7 to Wednesday 10 February 2027, in Fort-de-France: Fat Sunday on the 7th, Fat Monday on the 8th, Shrove Tuesday on the 9th and Ash Wednesday on the 10th. The season opens Sunday 3 January 2027 and Mi-Careme falls on Thursday 4 March 2027.

What is the theme of Martinique Carnival 2027?

The Fort-de-France theme is An Zafe Nou, chosen by a public vote and announced in late August 2026 by the City of Fort-de-France. The visual direction is inspired by the Maretet epi Chapo. Some guides present Maretet epi Chapo as the theme; An Zafe Nou is the best documented wording.

Is the official programme published?

No, not by 10 November 2026: no official hour-by-hour parade programme for the Fat Days had been published. Pre-carnival parties listed by agendas, such as Carnivalicious Red and Rubis on 29 January, are private events, not the official programme.
