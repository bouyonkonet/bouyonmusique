Dominica Calypso 2027: the twelve quarter-finalists

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026

The twelve quarter-finalists of Dominica calypso 2027 are known. The Dominica Calypso Association announced them after the eliminations of Saturday 7 November 2026. Trilla-G, a former Calypso Monarch turned bouyon star, is among them. The quarter-finals are scheduled for 2 January 2027.

  2. The return of Trilla-G

The twelve names

The twelve quarter-finalists are Madame Everglow, Myka, Shadow Flow, Vigilante, Yonce, Haxey, Shanice, Soul Puss, Bingo, Mighty Omee, Chris B and Trilla-G. Three reserves are named: Liberator, Ace and Danyan. This list completes the first announcement, which named only Trilla-G among the twelve.

The eliminations took place on Saturday 7 November 2026. The list of twelve was published on 8 November 2026. No quarter-final venue is published to date, and no semi-final date is published either.

The return of Trilla-G

Trilla-G won the calypso crown in 2023. Since then, the star has established himself on the bouyon scene in Dominica, with tracks such as Fix Up, led by Gz Up with Trilla-G as the source, and I Like Outside. His return to calypso competition is therefore a return to his roots, not a first appearance.

Calypso remains the historic gateway to carnival in Dominica, and bouyon came out of it. To see a bouyon star return to calypso is to see the two scenes standing together, as they do every year at Mas Domnik.

The road to the title

The quarter-finals are scheduled for 2 January 2027. The rest of the calendar, semi-final and final, is not published by 10 November 2026. Mas Domnik 2027, the carnival of Dominica, is the natural setting for this competition, but no official link between the quarter-finals and a carnival date is published to date.

This page will be updated when the Calypso Association publishes the rest of the calendar.
