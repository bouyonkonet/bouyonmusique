A bouyon title for the 2027 season

The video title states both genres at once: Bouyon/Soca, and a year, 2027, that aims at the next carnival season rather than the release date. The published credits are detailed: production Fr3EzE, writing Dwayne “Funky Fresh” Mendes and Devon Martin, background vocals by Trini Baby, executive production Stephen Phillip and Devon Martin, mix and master Jordan “J-Breazy” Ramdaya, distribution Monk Music Group and DSM Music Group. The video is directed by Deyondre Louis, with Shelton Saltibus as co director.

On Deezer, “Let's Get It On” appears from September 11, 2026, running 2 minutes 44, on the label DSM Music / Monk Music, filed by the platform under reggae and dancehall. As often, the platform category does not tell the real genre: the artist page is what sets the bouyon label. Among the 121 videos listed on his channel, it is the only title that carries it.

Fr3EzE, the link to the bouyon scene

The name that ties this title to bouyon, beyond the label, is the producer's. Fr3EzE is already profiled on this site for his work with artists of the scene, and his presence on a Lyrikal single shows the path the genre is taking in 2026: bouyon producers called in by established soca voices, who keep their audience and take on the tempo. To understand that relationship, see our pages bouyon vs soca and bouyon soca, a term coined by Dada in Dominica in 2003.

This profile does not retrace Lyrikal's whole soca career: no page opened for this research documents it in detail, and this site does not fill gaps from memory. It keeps to the verified bouyon title and its credits.

Origin and limits

The only indication of origin comes from the artist himself: the line Trinidad/United States in his presentation on the YouTube page. It is quoted as such, with no birthplace added. The background vocals by Trini Baby and the carnival aimed at by the 2027 label are context signals, not further proof.

On October 23, 2026, Lyrikal's bouyon file comes down to one title, published on two platforms with an identified bouyon producer. If further bouyon titles come out, they will be added here and in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Lyrikal?

A soca singer, songwriter and performer who presents himself, on his YouTube page, with the line Trinidad/United States. He enters this site through “Let's Get it On”, labelled Bouyon/Soca 2027.

Which is his verified bouyon title?

“Let's Get it On”, posted on his channel on September 24, 2026 with the label Bouyon/Soca 2027, and listed on Deezer from September 11, 2026 on DSM Music / Monk Music. It is the only title entered in the structured data of this page.

Who produced Let's Get it On?

Fr3EzE, a producer from the bouyon scene. The writing is credited to Dwayne “Funky Fresh” Mendes and Devon Martin, the mix and master to Jordan “J-Breazy” Ramdaya.

Where is Lyrikal from?

His YouTube page presents him with the line Trinidad/United States. No opened source gives a birthplace, and this site adds none.

Why 2027 in the title?

The label aims at the next carnival season, a common practice in soca. The release itself dates from September 2026.
