ORGIXx: Guel Aw Gwo and 2023 bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

ORGIXx has a short catalog: four singles released between July and November 2023, all on the PBDrec label. The third, “Guel Aw Gwo”, carries “Bouyon 2023” in its title and is by far the most played of the four.

Four singles over one summer and autumn

It all happens in 2023. On July 20 a first song came out under the artist's own name, “ORGIXx (Gran Madanm)”. Ten days later came “Mank De Suk”. Both run under three minutes, a tight format kept for the rest of the year.

The Deezer catalog credits nobody but the artist on these releases, with no guest and no named producer. The titles are in Creole, and the label is the same on every one, PBDrec. In November, “Ni Fess Ni Tête” closed the year, and nothing new has appeared since.

Guel Aw Gwo, the bouyon song

“Guel Aw Gwo” came out in mid August 2023. Deezer dates it August 16, the YouTube page generated through DistroKid August 19. Both carry the same full title, “GUEL AW GWO (BOUYON 2023)”, with the bracket placing the song squarely in the genre and the season.

It is the only one of the four releases tagged bouyon, and it is the one that found listeners. The audio upload on the artist's auto-generated YouTube channel is past 67,000 views, while the artist has only a few dozen fans on Deezer. The song runs 2 minutes 42.

What the pages say, and what they leave out

The sources opened say nothing about the basics. None gives an island or a country, a legal name or a birth date. No official video turns up in searches either: the artist's presence online runs through streaming platforms and an auto-generated YouTube channel.

So this profile sticks to what is published: a name spelled ORGIXx, with two x at the end, four singles in 2023 on PBDrec, and one bouyon song that traveled well beyond that small fan base. If more releases or clearer credits appear, this page will be updated.
