Mad General: a Martinique scene deejay on bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Mad General is a dancehall deejay seen on Martinique sound systems as early as 2014, with his own outfit, Madness Record. He has crossed into bouyon several times: “My Thing”, billed “2016 bouyon”, a turn on a Christmas Bouyon Riddim that same year, then “Down Di Road” on J2MO's High Lights Riddim in 2021.

Madness Record and the sound systems

In January 2014, a video on the Rachel Thiam Esmerasta Martinique channel shows him at a South Clash in Anse Figuier, alongside Djo Black, LT and Titi Dédonia. Three months later the same channel gave him a reel of extracts, introduced as “an artist who is tearing it up in Martinique”. None of these videos says where he was born, though.

On his Mad General Official channel, nearly every title from 2014 to 2016 carries the Madness Record tag, often with the riddim's name: Platin & Cassav Riddim, Red Boy Riddim, the Bruk Back Riddim by G6 Productions. “Feel Di Vybz” is close to 47,000 views. In October 2016, “Bend Down”, with Dj Jo of Webadub, passed 66,000 views on the DJ's channel.

His bouyon songs

On February 4, 2016, Mad General uploaded “My Thing”, flagged as an exclusive and billed “2016 bouyon”. That December he shared “Monté”, a group song on a Christmas Bouyon Riddim by DJ Pasko and DJ Beaw, with Sytème, Vybz Monia and Nia Styl; he had already released “Back Shot” with Nia Styl in 2014.

On September 27, 2021, Up Full Media Group released the High Lights Riddim by J2MO. Mad General opens the track list with “Down Di Road”, written by Christophe DeMocrite and Mac Kenzie Pernock. He is also on the “Mega Mix High Lights Riddim”, next to Mikty and Miky Ding La, which J2MO's channel tags #bouyon.

See Me So and the recent releases

In October 2020 he put out the video for “See Me So”, directed by Hassan Koufail, with the company Marvoni and the label BML. Deezer dates the single to October 12, 2020, on the 2361986 Records DK label. February 2022 brought “Gwo Fè Nwè”, a Creole song distributed by Upfull Music, the same outfit that had released the High Lights Riddim.

In the years since, he has turned up on riddim compilations, such as “Do” on Riddim Box No. 1: DTC Riddim in June 2024. July 2025 brought “I'm Coming”, a seven track album with “Your Man”, “Informers” and “Good Good Good”. The Deezer page under his name gathers other releases too, but only those that match his channel or his labels are cited here.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Mad General?

A dancehall deejay active in Martinique from 2014, running Madness Record, who has also sung on several bouyon riddims.

What are his bouyon songs?

My Thing (2016, billed 2016 bouyon), Monté on a Christmas Bouyon Riddim (2016), Down Di Road on J2MO's High Lights Riddim (2021) and that riddim's megamix.

What is Madness Record?

The name he attaches to most of his 2014 to 2016 songs on his YouTube channel, also printed under Bend Down with Dj Jo of Webadub.

Who wrote Down Di Road?

The Up Full Media Group credits name Christophe DeMocrite, composer of the whole riddim, and Mac Kenzie Pernock.

Where is Mad General from?

The 2014 videos present him as an artist making his mark in Martinique, but no source we opened gives his island of birth.
