Bouyon music: Madam Wob Dwiyet 2026, Hazel-Ann Anthony

By Smokiller · Published November 1, 2026

The result awaited since 28 October is official. Hazel-Ann Anthony, representing Mahaut, was crowned Madam Wob Dwiyet 2026 on Wednesday 28 October 2026, at the Massacre Indoor Facility, per Writeups 24, reporting the announcement by the Cultural Division and the National Cultural Council.

The full placings

Edmay Titre of Good Hope was named first runner-up. Gloria Merrifield of Grand Bay placed second, and Simonne Joseph-Toussaint of Portsmouth third, per Writeups 24.

The seven contestants were Martha Dangleban (Grand Bay), Hazel-Ann Anthony (Mahaut), Edmay Titre (Good Hope), Simonne Joseph-Toussaint (Portsmouth), Gloria Merrifield (Grand Bay), Cynthia Talbert (Pichelin) and Carol Jno Jules (Imperial Road).

Special and judged awards

Hazel-Ann Anthony also won the People s Choice Award and the Best Talent award. Simonne Joseph-Toussaint received Madam Photogenic and Best in Wob Dwiyet.

Edmay Titre won Best in Creole Contemporary Wear. The Cultural Division thanked all seven contestants for their elegance, confidence and cultural pride, at a pageant dedicated to Dominican womanhood and tradition.

A pageant at the heart of Creole season

Madam Wob Dwiyet is the madam counterpart to Miss Wob Dwiyet, won on 21 October 2026 by Samantha Bazil of Wesley, on the same Massacre stage.

Both pageants carry the same traditional dress, the wob dwiyet, and hold their place in the official programme of the 48th Independence season, between Ti Vilaj Kweyol and Jounen Kweyol.

What the title passes on

The Cultural Division, quoted by Writeups 24, praised performances celebrating the richness of Dominican cultural heritage, and recalled that beauty is timeless and age is celebrated.

With this result, the traditional dress pageant season is complete for 2026: Miss Wob Dwiyet on 21 October, Madam Wob Dwiyet on 28 October, then the National Dress Parade on 30 October in Roseau.
