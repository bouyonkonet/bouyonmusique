Soca Inferno

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Soca Inferno carries soca in his name, and most of the tracks on his YouTube channel present themselves as soca. Two pages still tie him clearly to bouyon. In May 2020 he posted “Close to me bouyon remix 2020”, with a four word description: rap fused into bouyon. And in December 2025 he sang on, produced and recorded at his studio, Inferedsoundz Studio, DPENG’s “Secret Bay”, billed Bouyon 2026. In between, he mixed and mastered DPENG’s “Move”. No opened page gives his origin or his legal name.

Two bouyon pages in a soca catalogue

The Soca Inferno channel has been posting since at least 2018. Almost every title there sits under soca: “Mix Up” for Antigua carnival 2018, “Caribbean Gem”, billed soca in 2023, “So Bad” in 2024, “Rum and Soca Music” in 2025. None of those pages mentions bouyon, and this profile does not present them as bouyon tracks.

The exception is “Close to me bouyon remix 2020”, posted on May 15, 2020, running 3 minutes 11. The title announces a bouyon remix, and the description sums it up: rap fused into bouyon. The page does not name the original work or the credits, and this site does not guess them.

Secret Bay, the voice and the studio

On “Secret Bay”, posted by DPENG on December 25, 2025, Soca Inferno has a wide role. The credits name him as second singer, voice of the chorus and backing vocals, and producer, with recording at Inferedsoundz Studio and beats by Joshii. The track, 2 minutes 29, is billed Bouyon 2026 and tied by a hashtag to the Storybook riddim.

The partnership goes back at least to February 2025: the page for DPENG’s “Move” credits Soca Inferno with mixing and mastering. A 2023 page on his own channel already names a studio with a close name, Inferedsound studio. This profile notes the similar names without claiming it is the same place.

What the record does not say

No opened page gives a birthplace, a legal name or a biography. The “Mix Up” page speaks of blending two cultures, with the words wadadli and rivanica, and the title targets Antigua carnival 2018: these are circuit signals, reported as such, not an origin. The “little boy production” credit on “Caribbean Gem” is not linked to any artist profiled on this site.

Soca Inferno’s bouyon record therefore comes down to two tracks and a mixing credit. That is little, but it is verified. For context, see also our page on bouyon in Antigua and our new bouyon 2026 tracker.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Soca Inferno?

Soca Inferno is a singer and producer who mostly posts soca on his YouTube channel. He sings on and produced “Secret Bay” with DPENG, billed Bouyon 2026, and he posted a bouyon remix of “Close to me” in 2020.

Which of his tracks are verified bouyon?

“Close to me bouyon remix 2020”, on his own channel, and DPENG’s “Secret Bay”, where he is credited for vocals, chorus, backing vocals and production. He is also credited with mixing and mastering DPENG’s “Move”.

Where is Soca Inferno from?

No opened page says. One of his tracks targets Antigua carnival 2018, but a carnival title is not an origin, and this site assigns Soca Inferno no island.

What is Inferedsoundz Studio?

It is the studio where “Secret Bay” was recorded, according to the published credits. The opened pages give neither its address nor its owner.
