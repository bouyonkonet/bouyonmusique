FLW: GENKIDAMA and BIG BAG, bouyon, French Caribbean

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

FLW is a bouyon artist who signs FLW (T-BTS), the mention of his collective, Team Bwe Tou Sa. His most visible track is “GENKIDAMA”, released as an official video with beatmaker Diixly, and his verified catalogue holds four tracks between 2025 and 2026: “GENKIDAMA”, “BIG BAG”, “RAKITA” and “HOT #Oups 5”. This profile documents them one by one, with their credits as published on the platforms, then states clearly what the public record does not hold: no real name, no documented island of origin, no published artist biography. FLW is a name of the new French Caribbean bouyon wave, presented here at the exact height of his verified traces.

Who FLW is: a collective artist, four verified credits

FLW presents himself, in every verified release, inside a collective: he signs FLW (T-BTS), and the official video of his main track is published under the Team Bwe Tou Sa banner, with the team hashtag in the video title itself. T-BTS is no ornament: it is also the name of the label that released, as early as 2021, the album “VOYE SA ALLEE” by Team Bwe Tou Sa, according to the collective Qobuz catalogue. Artist and team therefore share the same signature, and it is in that form, FLW (T-BTS), that the platforms and the video credit him.

The public, verified discography of FLW fits in four lines, all recent. In 2025, he appears on “RAKITA”, a Team Bwe Tou Sa single with Lunik. In 2026, he is the lead artist of “BIG BAG”, with Team Bwe Tou Sa and DJ T’CHABS and DJ LARAFALE as featured names, and he appears on “HOT #Oups 5”, with Lunik, See V and Team Bwe Tou Sa. At the centre stands “GENKIDAMA”, the track released as an official video, covered in the next section. Four tracks, no album under his name, no track earlier than 2025 found by this research: in the public record, FLW is an emerging artist of the 2025 and 2026 bouyon wave.

This profile applies to FLW the rule of this site for emerging names: only credits published and opened during the research are written, in their exact form. No platform artist page publishes, as of today and to our verified knowledge, a biography of FLW, his real name or his origin. The collective catalogues credit, as Team Bwe Tou Sa composers, names such as Ludmael FLOWER and Ludmilian FLOWER; no published source connects those names personally to FLW, so this profile makes no such connection. If an official biography appears, this page will be completed and dated.

GENKIDAMA: the signature track, with Diixly on the beat

“GENKIDAMA” is the track by which FLW is most identifiable. The official video presents it in the form FLW (T-BTS) X Diixly, and the video description announces the track as available on all platforms, with a DistroKid hyperfollow link. That link, truncated in the text fetched by this research, is not reproduced here: this site only publishes addresses it has verified by opening them to the end. The video hashtags frame the track without ambiguity: bouyon, Bouyon2026, Caribbean music, new release.

The published credits of the video draw the full team behind the track: direction by Dylan Farah, sound engineer Gammalab, beatmaker Diixly. Gammalab is a recurring name in the Team Bwe Tou Sa catalogue, present as label and studio on collective releases since 2020, and its presence here ties “GENKIDAMA” to the usual workshop of the team. FLW holds the lead artist role: his voice and his name carry the track, with Diixly signing the rhythm as a named collaborator from the video title onward.

The track name belongs to the popular culture of its generation: Genkidama refers, in the manga imaginary shared by young Antillean audiences, to the energy sphere of Dragon Ball. This profile notes that reference as a reading of the title, without lending FLW a statement no source publishes about the choice of the name. The video keywords, by contrast, are factual: bouyon 2026, bouyon Guadeloupe, bouyon Martinique, bouyon Dominica appear there as search terms for the clip. They describe the market the track aims at, the French Antilles and their diaspora, and not the personal origin of FLW, which nothing documents.

BIG BAG, RAKITA, HOT #Oups 5: the discography line by line

“BIG BAG” is the track where FLW moves, in the catalogues, from credited member to lead artist. The Team Bwe Tou Sa artist page on Shazam lists it as “BIG BAG (feat. DJ T’CHABS & DJ LARAFALE)”, a single by FLW and Team Bwe Tou Sa, in 2026, and Qobuz dates the release to 20 January 2026, released by Team Bwe Tou Sa. The presence of two DJs as featured names, DJ T’CHABS and DJ LARAFALE, is a trait of how French Caribbean bouyon is now made: the track is built from its release for mixes and parties, with the names of the DJs who will carry it written into its own credit.

“RAKITA” is the first verified appearance of FLW in a catalogue. Shazam lists it as a 2025 single by Team Bwe Tou Sa, FLW and Lunik, and Qobuz dates the release to 11 September 2025. Lunik is a recurring collaborator of the collective, present as early as “CHOKOLA” in 2021, and the “RAKITA” trio shows the method of the team: a collective track where every voice is credited by name, rather than a solo track. It is in that format that FLW enters the public record, a year before his own single.

“HOT #Oups 5” extends the same method in 2026: Shazam lists the single under the names of Lunik, FLW, See V and Team Bwe Tou Sa. The number in the title points to a series, the #Oups releases, inside which this fifth volume pairs FLW with the usual voices of the collective and with See V. Together, these four tracks form the whole verified discography of FLW as of 20 October 2026: one lead track with an official video, “GENKIDAMA”, one single under his name, “BIG BAG”, and two collective tracks, “RAKITA” and “HOT #Oups 5”. No other track is added here, because no other was found with a published, checkable credit.

The context: Team Bwe Tou Sa and collective bouyon in the French Caribbean

To understand the place of FLW, look at the team that carries him. Team Bwe Tou Sa has published since 2020 a dense catalogue, the albums “VOYE SA ALLEE” and “Voye sa allee II”, the album “TRAVAILLONS” in 2024, and a run of singles in 2025 and 2026, released under its own labels, T-BTS, GAMMALAB and Team Bwe Tou Sa. Audience tracking services place its public mainly in France, then in Guadeloupe, and the team appears on the bill of the Karukera One Love festival in Torcy, near Paris: that is the circuit of the French Antilles and their diaspora, where bouyon and shatta share the parties.

That collective bouyon has its own grammar, different from the live band bouyon of 1990s Dominica told on our history of bouyon page. Here, tracks are short, credits are shared between collective voices, beatmakers and DJs, and the team signature, Team Bwe Tou Sa or T-BTS, works as a mark of manufacture. FLW is an exact product of that grammar: a short three letter name, always published with his team mention, collaboration tracks before the track under his own name, and an official video as the centrepiece. His documented path, from “RAKITA” to “BIG BAG”, follows the usual slope of the collective: enter through the team tracks, then sign your own.

The genre itself keeps its roots written in plain sight: bouyon was born in Dominica, and its soca branch was started in Dominica in 2003 by Dada, according to the testimony of Smokiller published on this site. The French Caribbean collective wave, of which FLW is part, is one of the forms that Dominican sound takes in 2026 as it circulates between Guadeloupe, Martinique and the mainland diaspora. Our bouyon playlist and our bouyon gwada page follow that circulation track by track.

What this profile does not know: the limits of the FLW record

This profile ends with its limits, because they are part of the record. Not present in the sources opened for this research, and therefore not written here: the real name of FLW, his island or country of origin, his age, his path before “RAKITA”, his verified official accounts and his personal listening figures. None of these elements is guessed, deduced from the keywords of a video or borrowed from a namesake: FLW is also, outside music, the initials of organisations unrelated to this artist, and this site does not merge those entries with the Team Bwe Tou Sa artist.

The recording list in this page schema reflects exactly the verified state of the catalogue: four tracks, “GENKIDAMA”, “BIG BAG”, “RAKITA” and “HOT #Oups 5”. Where the established artist profiles on this site list eight to fifteen tracks, this one lists four, because four is the true number as of 20 October 2026. If FLW publishes an album, a biography or new tracks, this profile will be expanded and dated, like every page on this site.

In the meantime, listening begins with “GENKIDAMA”, the official video with Diixly, continues with “BIG BAG”, the single under his name of 20 January 2026, then with the collective tracks “RAKITA” and “HOT #Oups 5”, before placing the whole in this site bouyon playlist. FLW appears there for what he is in the public record: the T-BTS voice of a French Caribbean collective, documented by four tracks, no more, no less.

Frequently asked questions

Who is FLW?

No biography, no real name and no origin are published in the sources opened for this profile.

What does T-BTS mean in FLW (T-BTS)?

T-BTS is the short form of Team Bwe Tou Sa, the collective of FLW. It is also the name of the label that released the Team Bwe Tou Sa album “VOYE SA ALLEE” in 2021, according to the collective Qobuz catalogue. FLW therefore carries, in his own artist name, the signature of his team and of its in house label.

What is Team Bwe Tou Sa?

Team Bwe Tou Sa is the bouyon collective FLW belongs to. What are the verified tracks of FLW?
