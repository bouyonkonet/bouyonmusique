BONNA: the new bouyon voice from La Plaine

Published September 28, 2026

BONNA is a Dominican bouyon artist from La Plaine, Dominica. His Instagram, @bonna_nasa, is a Dominica based creator account that showed 312 followers and 24 posts at our October 17, 2026 check. His bio reads 'str8 outta lapwet', La Plaine in Creole, with a 'nasa crew' reference. He is signed to the label More Sewo, and his bio carries the label hashtag. His track 'Not Real' arrived as a 30 second performance clip filmed outdoors in Dominica, full of energy from the first second. He is a genuine new talent case: as of our October 2026 research, the track had no indexed press footprint at all.

La Plaine, 'str8 outta lapwet'

BONNA's bio does work that many official presentations never do: it says where he is from, in his own language. 'Str8 outta lapwet' names La Plaine, on Dominica's east coast. For a bouyon artist, announcing your village or district is not a folk detail. It is a signature. The island's music moves through communities, local crews and audiences who back their own first, before watching them head to Roseau and then to neighbouring islands.

The 'nasa crew' reference, echoed in his handle @bonna_nasa, points the same way: BONNA presents himself as part of a collective, not as a product made alone in an office. We present these elements for what they are, parts of his own public presentation on his account. They do not replace a biography, but they give the right frame: a young artist rooted in La Plaine, building his image with his crew and his territory.

That rooting matters for what came next, because the producer search run with Bouyon Shattaz in October 2026 starts exactly there: finding sounds worthy of an artist who represents a specific place in Dominica. A producer who understands that starting identity will work differently from a producer who only sees one more new bouyon name on a list.

Signed to More Sewo, with a manager

BONNA is signed to the label More Sewo, MORESEWO MUSIC. His Instagram bio carries #moresewo, and Bouyon Shattaz deals with him through his manager. More Sewo is presented as a Guadeloupe based visual and audio entertainment brand, created in 2007 by Arturo Bellot. A Dominican artist signed to a Guadeloupe based structure illustrates the real geography of bouyon today: the music is born in Dominica, while structures and audiences circulate between the Creole islands.

Having a manager changes the nature of every conversation. For Bouyon Shattaz, it means the serious subjects, producers, collaborations, beat use terms and releases, go through the manager and the label, not through an improvised direct message to the artist. That precision is written here because it protects everyone: the artist, his label and the producers being approached. A bouyon project that wants to last must respect the chain of people who represent the artist.

That structure also explains this profile's caution. We publish no fee, no term and no release plan as if it were confirmed. What is public and verified is enough: BONNA has a label, a manager, a track in circulation as a clip and a team actively looking for the right producers for what comes next. For a new artist, that is already a more advanced organisation than most profiles this site documents.

'Not Real': 30 seconds, maximum energy

'Not Real' reached Bouyon Shattaz as a 30 second performance clip, filmed outdoors in Dominica. The clip is full of energy from the first second: no slow introduction, no build up, the artist is already inside the track when the picture starts. That short format has become a real calling card in bouyon, because it must convince a promoter, a producer or a public in less time than a radio chorus.

Bouyon Shattaz selected a 'NEW TALENT' style edit of the clip for its coverage. The choice of format states the intention: presenting BONNA as a discovery to follow, not as an established star. In October 2026, our research found no indexed press footprint for 'Not Real'. That absence is not a criticism. It is the definition of new talent in the literal sense, an artist whose documentation starts now, and of which this page is one of the first structured written traces.

A content note, stated neutrally: the clip shows BONNA rolling and smoking on camera, the kind of raw street presentation that is part of his image. Flagging it lets readers and promoters know what the video contains before sharing it, without judging the artist on that element alone. In bouyon, a raw street image is part of the language of many young artists, and describing it honestly is better than discovering it by surprise.

October 2026: the producer search and the Denmath lead

In October 2026, BONNA's team contacted Bouyon Shattaz looking for producers for him. That is the moment this profile stops being only a discovery and becomes a connection project. The Guadeloupe side producer Denmath was green-lit by his management as a first collaboration candidate. Denmath is a producer, sound engineer and artistic director, with bouyon among his stated specialities in the Bouyon Shattaz dossier.

The shape emerging is exactly the pipeline Bouyon Shattaz wants to build: a Dominica artist, a French side producer, and a conversation where the artist's label and manager are present from the start. No beat sent without a frame, no release promised without an agreement. The collaboration is described here as what it is at this stage, a green-lit lead to begin with, not an announced track. Any other wording would be invention.

For BONNA, the stake is clear. A new bouyon artist needs productions that carry his voice without drowning it, and that can travel to audiences in Guadeloupe, Martinique and France without losing the La Plaine accent. For Denmath and producers at that level, an artist like BONNA represents the most precious raw material in current bouyon: fresh, local energy, already organised with a label and a manager ready to talk properly.

Why BONNA deserves a page right now

Some sites wait until an artist is already big before giving him a page. Bouyon Shattaz does the opposite when the dossier, even a thin one, is verifiable: documenting the beginning fixes the credits, label, place and first tracks before approximate rewrites set in. If BONNA grows, this page will serve as a reliable starting point. If an element needs correcting, the source based structure makes correction possible without rewriting a legend.

His case also connects several threads of this site. Dominica as origin, on artists from Dominica. Circulation toward French audiences, in the bouyon playlist. And the stage where new names must eventually prove themselves, the WCMF and the island's stages. BONNA is not announced here on any bill that our dossier does not contain. We say only that this is the natural road for a rising Dominican artist, and that his team is already working on the producer step.

Hear 'Not Real', look at where he comes from and remember the name. Bouyon careers that matter often start like this: a short clip in circulation, a team getting organised, a producer answering present. What comes next for BONNA will be written with tracks, credits and agreements that can be cited. This page will update at that pace, the pace of facts, so that the name out of La Plaine is documented as seriously as the headliners.
