Bouyon producers: the architects of the sound

By Smokiller · Published September 19, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

Modern bouyon is decided more and more in the studio. Behind every riddim that makes Gwada, Martinique or Paris dance, there is a producer who chose the tempo, the loop and the bass. Here are the ones Bouyon Shattaz follows.

The names Bouyon Shattaz follows

HYPNO and his Mauvais Plan at 160 bpm. Savion and his snippets of upcoming tracks. Denmath, from Saint-Martin, and his Tropical and Harley riddims. MB On The Track, who dances on his own productions and sells custom work. Smokiller, the home of bouyon remixes. Each one has their page on this site, in the artists section.

Why the producer became the star

The format changed: a riddim launches in a fifteen-second video, the producer dances on it, the audience demands the long version. The producer is no longer hidden behind the artist: they have become a face of the genre, with their own audience.

Frequently asked questions

Who produces bouyon today?

In Dominica, producers such as Dada, Killa Laurent, DJ Taffy and Fr3EzE sign the scene's riddims and records. Bouyon Shattaz also follows the wave of social media producers: Smokiller, Denmath, HYPNO!, Savion and MB On The Track, across riddims, remixes and original productions. Each one has a page on this site, in the artists section.

What is a bouyon riddim?

A riddim is the shared instrumental that several artists record over, a culture inherited from the Jamaican and Dominican riddim tradition. Riddims sit at the heart of bouyon and dancehall production: today a riddim often launches in a fifteen-second video, before the audience demands the long version. See the bouyon riddim page.

Why did producers become bouyon stars?

Because the format changed: a riddim launches in a fifteen-second video, the producer dances on it and the audience demands the long version. The producer is no longer hidden behind the artist: they have become a face of the genre with their own audience, like MB On The Track, who dances on his own productions.

Who is Smokiller?

Smokiller is a Dominica-born artist and producer and the founder of Bouyon Shattaz. The home of bouyon remixes, he has flipped Coi Leray's Nothing Into Something and Ayra Starr's Heaven Baby, and produced Throw Dat (Bouyon 2026) by Klass Money. See his Smokiller page.

Where is producer Denmath from?

Denmath, whose legal name is Mathurin DENMATH Denis, is a producer, sound engineer and artistic director from Saint-Martin, of Haitian heritage, based between Nantes and Paris. Bouyon Shattaz follows his Tropical and Harley riddims and the track Tarif by Koko Bwa, on which he collaborates.

Who produced “Famalay”?

Dominican producer Dada, according to the press and the official credits. The Trinidad site Ebuzztt presents him as the producer behind “Famalay”, by Machel Montano, Skinny Fabulous and Bunji Garlin, the 2019 Road March of Trinidad and Tobago, and the credits name DadaMuzic and DSM on production. It is the same Dada who launched bouyon soca in Dominica in 2003. See Dada.

Who produces Triple Kay's records?

Kendel “Killa” Laurent, the band's lead keyboardist and producer. Triple Kay's official site sums it up: Kendel produces all of their music. His catalogue runs from the albums “Big Ting” (2000) to “Bubblin” (2012) and covers the band's Road March titles, such as “Please”, “Lock De Gate” and “Roll dat Boom Boom”. See Killa Laurent.

Who is DJ Taffy, the Riddim Beast?

DJ Taffy is a bouyon DJ and producer from Dominica, known as the Riddim Beast. His label is Riddim Beast Studios and his songwriting credits published by Shazam carry the name Tafari Maffei. He was a Producer of the Year finalist at the Dominica Music Awards 2025 and plays Night 3 of WCMF 2026 according to the published guides. See DJ Taffy.

Boy Freeze or Fr3EzE: how is this producer's name spelled?

The verified producer is spelled Fr3EzE, with a digit and capitals, and pronounced Freeze. No source consulted uses the exact spelling Boy Freeze. Fr3EzE, a producer from Dominica, produced “Mood” by TriSzy, handled additional programming on the “Bye to the Old Me” remix and is credited on production of Trilla-G's album “Take Me as I Am”. See Fr3EzE.

Who created the bouyon keyboard sound?

Cornell “Fingers” Phillip, the founding keyboardist of WCK, the band that created bouyon in Dominica at the end of the 1980s. DOM767 presents him as widely regarded as the Godfather of Bouyon Music. In the family house in Goodwill, the jing ping accordion became a keyboard, and a whole genre came out of that move. See Cornell Phillip.

Which bouyon producers work in the French Antilles?

Several names documented on this site: DJ Leguste, linked to Guadeloupe in public credits, releases singles on his own label, nearly all at 160 BPM; J2MO, known as The Beat Cooker, has been active in credits since 2015 on the bouyon and shatta circuit; DJ Skaytah credits his compositions to the name Bradley Paulin. See DJ Leguste.
