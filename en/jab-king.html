Jab King: Grenada jab on Gasolina (bouyon soca 2026)

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Jab King is a singer from Grenada whom Complex listed in 2023 among the Grenadian soca artists to know, for “High” and “Chopping The Line”, a mas-band title that reached Notting Hill Carnival. His name states the genre, the jab jab of Spicemas, and his videos have been billed Grenada Soca since 2019. On April 13, 2026, “Gasolina” came out billed “Grenada bouyon soca 2026”: jab and bouyon in the same title. “Tusty for Rum”, his duet with Wetty Beatz, was past 651,000 views when this profile was written.

Gasolina: jab takes the bouyon billing

On April 13, 2026, the JAB KING OFFICIAL channel uploaded “Jab King - Gasolina (Grenada bouyon soca 2026)”, more than 18,000 views when read. The page credits production to WizZarD HouSe RecorDs, mixing and mastering to Can'tstop Records, and the video to Jab King himself with BDV Frost. On Deezer, “Gasolina” is a single dated April 12, 2026, 2 minutes 48.

The production credit points to Tha Wizzard, the Dominican engineer present on a large share of the titles documented by this site; he already produced “Tusty for Rum” in 2024. The “bouyon soca” billing matches what our page on bouyon soca describes: a Grenadian genre borrowing bouyon's label and tempo.

Tusty for Rum, Chopping The Line, Addicted: the titles that count

“Tusty for Rum”, published on June 14, 2024 with Wetty Beatz, is his most viewed title, more than 651,000 views: written by David Connell and Phillip Bastien, produced by ThaWizzard, mixed and mastered by Wetty Beatz Productionz; on Deezer the single is dated August 30, 2024, label Wetty Beatz Productionz / Jab King Music / Thawizzard. The same pair had signed “Hello” on the Super Saiyan Riddim, billed 2021 Power Soca, in January 2021.

“Start To Move Like Jab” (Genie Riddim, 2019 Grenada Soca), published in February 2019 by Stick To The Plan Entertainment, was past 526,000 views; “Chopping The Line” (Foo Foo Riddim, Grenada Soca 2023), produced by Impulse Productions, more than 470,000; “Addicted” (Alien Head Riddim, Grenada Soca 2024), produced by Travis World, more than 276,000. The pages credit David Connell for the writing of several of these titles.

Twenty-nine releases, from the Jab Nation Riddim to the Lapo Kabwit Riddim

Jab King's Deezer artist page lists 29 releases, from “Jab Nation Riddim” (July 7, 2017) to “Get Some Oil” (June 24, 2026). Riddims are many: Glass Bottle, Black and Dutty, Ahdobay (2018), Quater, Lapo Kabwit (July 19, 2019), Project Zero (album, June 25, 2025). A riddim named Lapo Kabwit, after the Dominican drum, says in its own way how close jab sits to bouyon's roots.

The recent singles, “Jab RnB”, “Show Killer”, “Take Horn”, “Room for Everybody” (2025), “Smell D Rum”, “Lewwe Go” (2026), and “Bro Code” with Litleboy (2024), complete the picture. This profile is written from Complex and the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Jab King?

A jab and soca singer from Grenada, named by Complex (2023) among the Grenadian artists to know for High and Chopping The Line; 29 releases on Deezer since 2017.

What is his bouyon title?

Gasolina, uploaded on April 13, 2026 and billed Grenada bouyon soca 2026, produced by WizZarD HouSe RecorDs and mixed by Can'tstop Records.

What is his most viewed title?

Tusty for Rum with Wetty Beatz (June 2024), more than 651,000 views on October 27, 2026, produced by ThaWizzard.

What is jab jab?

The masquerade tradition of Grenada's carnival, Spicemas, whose name Jab King carries; his titles Start To Move Like Jab (2019) and Chopping The Line (2023) are the most viewed examples.

Has Jab King worked with Dominican producers?

Yes: ThaWizzard produced Tusty for Rum (2024) and WizZarD HouSe RecorDs is credited on Gasolina (2026); Bro Code (2024) features Litleboy.
