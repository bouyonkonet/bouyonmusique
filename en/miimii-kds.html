Emilie Afoy, an artist from Guadeloupe born in 2007

Behind the name Miimii KDS is Emilie Afoy, born in 2007 and from Guadeloupe. Her age is part of what stands out in her story: she belongs to a generation that grew up with the phone as its first studio, social media as its first stage and bouyon as everyday music. This page does not print a hometown, because sources disagree on that point. What is established, and what matters for the music, is the Guadeloupe grounding, claimed in her identity and in her audience alike.

Her name travels in one clear and stable form: @_miimiikds, on Instagram and on TikTok. The Instagram account is verified and stood at about 86K followers at our October 2026 check. The #bouyon4life line in her bio is not a graphic detail: it places bouyon at the centre of her public image, before the wider French public discovered her through a certified record. For a young artist, displaying the genre that clearly is choosing a musical camp and a culture.

That Guadeloupe identity places her in a wider story told on our history of bouyon page: the genre was born in Dominica, and Guadeloupe then became a major land of bouyon dance, production and new faces. Miimii KDS does not represent the Dominican source, she represents the Guadeloupe wave that keeps the genre alive today, with its own codes, its own networks and its youth. That precise position is what makes her path worth following.

Sé Miimii, the viral track that broke her through in 2025

Miimii KDS broke through in 2025 with 'Sé Miimii'. The track is described as a viral bouyon track, and its very title works as a signature: the artist introduces herself in the title, and the audience remembers the name at the same time as the chorus. In a genre where many songs travel through riddims and parties, attaching your own name to a track that circulates is a decisive step for an artist emerging at scale.

The success of 'Sé Miimii' should be read with its ground: Guadeloupe, short form social video, and sharing between the islands and the diaspora. A viral bouyon track does not live on streaming alone, it lives when it is replayed, danced to, quoted and put back into party playlists. To extend the listening, our bouyon playlist places the key tracks of the recent wave in a wider set, where 'Sé Miimii' holds its place as an entry point into Miimii KDS and her work.

After a breakthrough track, the question is always the same: can the artist exist beyond the moment that revealed her? For Miimii KDS, the answer runs through the collaborations and credits that follow, in particular 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' and her appearance on Meryl's album. Those steps show an artist whom other established names accept on their records, which changes her status: she is no longer only the maker of a track that circulated, she becomes a voice the scene calls on.

Pow Pow / Allo Doudou: Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and the SNEP Single Or

Miimii KDS is credited on 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' alongside Niska and Litleboy LSBeats767. That credit places her on a record carried by a major name for French audiences, while keeping the link to bouyon production through the presence of Litleboy LSBeats767. For a young artist from Guadeloupe, appearing on such a record, with her name in the credits, is professional recognition as much as exposure to a far wider audience.

The track crossed 15,000,000 equivalent streams in France and was certified Single Or, gold, by SNEP in 2026. Those two elements are the public markers in the file: a listening threshold measured in France and an official certification from SNEP, the body that issues music certifications in France. They should be read for what they are: proof that a record with a Guadeloupe bouyon artist in the credits reached, in France, a level that can be counted and certified.

That certification matters for the whole genre as well. Bouyon comes from Dominica, lives strongly in Guadeloupe and Martinique, and has long looked for clear recognition in French numbers. Seeing Miimii KDS's name attached to a Single Or in 2026 gives the young Guadeloupe wave a concrete argument, beyond party impressions or views on one network. It is a catalogue fact, dated and verifiable, and it will stay attached to her name whatever comes next in her career.

A central face of the young Guadeloupe bouyon wave

Calling Miimii KDS a central face of the young Guadeloupe bouyon wave sums up the whole file without forcing any part of it. A viral breakthrough in 2025 with 'Sé Miimii', a credit on a record certified Single Or in France in 2026, a place on Meryl's 'La Dame' album in 2025, a verified audience on Instagram and TikTok under the same name, and a stated motto, #bouyon4life: every piece comes from the artist's public record and points in the same direction.

For a reader arriving from France, the advised path is simple: hear 'Sé Miimii' for the signature, 'Pow Pow / Allo Doudou' for the certified record, find her place in 'La Dame', then follow her activity on TikTok, where the 'Cuenta' moment was born. Miimii KDS's next step will be played out over time: turning a breakthrough and strong credits into a recognisable personal catalogue. That is the challenge for the whole young wave, and it is why Bouyon Shattaz keeps her name at the centre of its 2026 coverage. At her age, with those first markers already set, she starts from a point many bouyon artists never had in France: a track of her own that circulated, a Single Or credit, and an audience already asking for her on the next remix. To keep that path readable, remember four names and four dates: 'Sé Miimii' in 2025 for the breakthrough, 'La Dame' in 2025 for the album appearance, the SNEP Single Or in 2026 for the French certification, and the 'Cuenta' tease in October 2026 for the audience expectation. That short chronology, drawn entirely from her public file, is enough to place Miimii KDS without adding promises about what comes next or turning a fan expectation into an announcement.

Miimii KDS stands exactly at that meeting point. Following her does not ask anyone to choose between the street history of bouyon and its new streaming life: her public record holds both, from a Guadeloupe breakthrough track to a gold certified credit in France.

That is why her name belongs in any serious 2026 round up of the genre's young faces, alongside the Dominican artists who carry the source.
