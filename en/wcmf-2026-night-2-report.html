WCMF 2026 night 2: the report from Saturday 24 October at Windsor Park

By Smokiller · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026

Night two of the World Creole Music Festival 2026 was played on Saturday 24 October at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau. The report published by Writeups 24 on 26 October leads with Ne-Yo, Haitian band Tabou Combo and several Caribbean acts. This page follows that report, name by name, adding no running order and no crowd figures.

On this page
  1. Extasy Band opens the night with kompa
  2. Tabou Combo, DYP and Full Blown
  3. Ne-Yo headlines on Saturday
  4. What this report covers, and what it does not
  5. Frequently asked questions

Extasy Band opens the night with kompa

Per the report, Dominican band Extasy Band opened the show with its kompa style, welcoming guests Luc Leandre, Pudaz, Paris and Khadija Joseph.

Dominica Festivals presents Extasy Band as the only kompa band in the English-speaking Caribbean, and the report carries that line as a quote from the organisers, not as a fact established by this page.

Tabou Combo, DYP and Full Blown

Haitian band Tabou Combo delivered a strong performance of konpa rhythms, in line with its long-standing presence in Caribbean music, per the same report.

Saint Lucian artist DYP appeared with Shemmy, Hollywood HP, Sedale, Tallyboy, Nerdy, Andrew and Ezra. Full Blown, from Trinidad and Tobago, also performed a collection of well-known Caribbean music at the stadium.

Ne-Yo headlines on Saturday

Ne-Yo was the main performer of the night, singing his world famous R and B songs to the Dominican audience, who sang along with him, per the report.

Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit welcomed him in a post on his official Facebook page, quoted by the report, saying what a pleasure it was to welcome him to Dominica and see the festival audience singing along.

What this report covers, and what it does not

The 26 October report focuses on the performances it names: Extasy Band, Tabou Combo, DYP and his guests, Full Blown and Ne-Yo. It publishes no full running order, no set times and no attendance figures.

The announced Saturday programme also included other names, among them Alkaline and Asa Bantan. This page therefore separates the announced programme, already documented on the night 2 guide page, from the performances reported after the fact by the press.

Frequently asked questions

Who played WCMF 2026 night 2 according to the press?

The Writeups 24 report published on 26 October 2026 names Extasy Band, Tabou Combo, DYP with Shemmy, Hollywood HP, Sedale, Tallyboy, Nerdy, Andrew and Ezra, Full Blown and Ne-Yo.

Who headlined Saturday 24 October 2026?

Ne-Yo, the American R and B singer, presented by the report as the main performer of the second night, at Windsor Park Sports Stadium, Roseau.

Did Extasy Band open night 2?

Yes, per the report: Extasy Band opened the show with its kompa style and guests Luc Leandre, Pudaz, Paris and Khadija Joseph.
