Bouyon music: Dominica ports, Skerrit opens

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026

Prime Minister Roosevelt Skerrit wants to attract private investment into Dominica ports. He announced it in his National Day address on November 3, 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau, with a stated aim: to reduce the island dependence on Citizenship by Investment revenue and borrowing, according to Writeups 24.

What is proposed

The plan covers the cruise facilities, the cargo port and the ferry terminals. Under the proposed arrangements, private investors would provide funding and technical expertise, while the government would provide land and other assets to support the developments.

Several potential investors have already shown strong interest in developing the facilities, according to the Prime Minister. The plan fits into the preparations for the country 50 years of independence, in 2028.

Consultations by November 18

Skerrit has directed the ministries concerned to consult stakeholders by November 18, allowing port workers, vendors, businesses and other interested groups a chance to understand the proposals and share their opinions, according to Writeups 24.

The consultation is therefore the next concrete step in the file. No final decision is announced in the source opened for this article: the government is opening the discussion before deciding.

A United States delegation expected

The Prime Minister also announced that a delegation from the United States International Development Finance Corporation, the DFC, is expected to visit Dominica later in November. The visit follows recent meetings in Washington and would focus on possible areas of investment, including major infrastructure projects.

The DFC is the United States development finance institution, and its visit signals that the ports file goes beyond the local frame: it sits inside a search for partners for the island big projects.

The plane and the airport in the same address

In the same address, Skerrit also spoke about air connectivity. American Airlines is preparing to introduce a larger aircraft on its Dominica route, while the government plans to finalise an Open Skies Agreement with the United States by the end of 2026, according to Writeups 24.

On the international airport at Wesley, the Prime Minister confirmed that more than 400 Dominicans are currently employed on the site, with completion expected in the first quarter of 2028. Dominicans interested in providing tourism services or operating businesses at the new airport were encouraged to submit their expressions of interest to the Ministry of Tourism.
