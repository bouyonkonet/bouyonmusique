Bouyon music 2026: WCMF opening night, Friday October 23

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

It is Friday October 23, 2026, opening day of the World Creole Music Festival. Tonight the festival 26th edition opens at Windsor Park Sports Stadium in Roseau, Dominica, for three nights of Creole music in the heart of Independence season. This page collects what the day itself makes it possible to verify: the night 1 programme, the kickoff time already announced and the weather read this morning.

Night 1: reggae, dancehall, kompa and bouyon

Published guides agree on the core of night 1: Capleton for Jamaican reggae, Skillibeng for dancehall, Klass for Haitian kompa, and on the Dominica side TK International and Midnight Groovers, two names from cadence-lypso and bouyon. Writeups24 and the Purely Dominica guides add Maurane Voyer, Pudaz, Ridge, DJ MJ and MV on the Keys, Colton T and Ezra D Fun Machine to the same night.

Station509 confirms the logic of the evening: Klass opens the Haitian contingent on Friday October 23, on the same night as Capleton and Midnight Groovers. Night 1 launches the weekend before Saturday night 2, the big bouyon night, and Sunday night 3.

Kickoff announced for 8 p.m.

Festival Coordinator Kareem Guiste had given the time that organises the day: kick off the festival at 8 p.m. on October 23, so everyone can arrive on time. The official ticket platform listing carried by local calendars also shows night 1 at 8 p.m. at Windsor Park.

More than 30 acts are expected across the three nights, according to the festival communication. No hour by hour running order is published consistently by the opened sources, so this page publishes no invented running order.

Opening day weather, read this morning

Forecasts read this Friday October 23 morning on weather.com for Roseau point to a partly cloudy day, high 88 degrees Fahrenheit, a low rain chance by day around 10 percent, and possible showers late at night. The east wind blows around 13 miles per hour, the UV index rises to 11, extreme, at midday, and no weather alert is in force.

Other services see possible patchy rain this Friday. The honest opening day reading therefore stays the same as the eve: strong heat by day, a warm night, and light rain protection for cameras and microphones.

And after tonight

Night 2, Saturday October 24, is presented by the guides as the biggest night of the festival, with Ne-Yo, Alkaline, Tabou Combo and, on the bouyon side, Asa Bantan, widely called the King of Bouyon, Trilla G and Reo. Night 3, Sunday October 25, closes with Machel Montano, Uncle Waffles, Fridayy, Maxi Priest and Shelly with Signal Band.

The detailed guides for each night are already on this site: WCMF 2026 night 1, night 2 and night 3, plus the eve guide published on October 22.

Published guides agree on Capleton, Skillibeng, Klass, TK International and Midnight Groovers, with Maurane Voyer, Pudaz, Ridge, DJ MJ and MV on the Keys, Colton T and Ezra D Fun Machine also listed for the same night.
