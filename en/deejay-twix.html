One remix credit, repeated on the page

The opened page is short and built entirely around the credit. Its full heading, “Valiant - Time Capsule (bouyon remix) prod by deejay twix”, gives in order the original artist, Valiant, the title, “Time Capsule”, the nature of the version in brackets, bouyon remix, and the name of the remix producer, deejay twix. Its description repeats the same line in lower case, “valiant - time capsule bouyon remix prod by dj twix”, with the short variant dj twix for the same name. The hashtags on the page, bouyon, bouyonriddim, remix, valiant and deejaytwix, file the video in the remix section of the bouyon riddim.

The division of roles is therefore readable without ambiguity, and this profile respects it strictly. “Time Capsule” is Valiant track: he is its artist of record, and nothing on the opened page makes him the subject of a bouyon profile on this site. Deejay Twix steps in on the version: he produced the remix that dresses the track in bouyon. That is the work our bouyon remix page describes, taking an existing track and rewiring it to the tempo and the codes of the genre, and it is for that credit alone that Deejay Twix appears here.

What the credit says, and what it does not say

The credit states a circuit remixer positioning: the bouyon version of an already released track, published on YouTube with the remixer name in the heading itself, so that DJs and listeners searching for the track also find his version. The double signature, deejay twix in the heading and dj twix in the description, shows a working name used in two forms on the same page. This profile keeps the long form, Deejay Twix, the one in the heading, and flags the variant rather than smoothing it away.

For the rest, the page says nothing. No precise release date was read on the opened page, no tempo, no studio, no official link and no other Deejay Twix remix or track were verified during this research. No origin is named: not Dominica, not Guadeloupe, not Martinique, not any other island, and the remixed artist gives the remixer no origin either. This profile therefore leaves the file at its real size: one name, one credit, one remixed track, two genre tags, like the thin files in our bouyon playlist circuit.

The limits of the file, stated

No other Deejay Twix track was verified, and no official link is therefore entered in the structured data of this page, beyond the remix itself, entered under the name of its bouyon version.

If Deejay Twix publishes more remixes with full credits, an artist page or a verifiable channel page, this page will be completed and dated, like every page on this site. Frequently asked questions

Who is Deejay Twix?

Deejay Twix is a remixer credited as producer of the bouyon remix of “Time Capsule” by Valiant, on the YouTube page opened during this research. The credit appears in the heading and in the description of the video. No public biography was found.

Which Deejay Twix track is verified?

A single credit: “Time Capsule (bouyon remix)”, the bouyon version of the Valiant track, produced by deejay twix according to its opened YouTube page. It is the only recording entered in the structured data of this profile, under the name of the remixed version.

Is Deejay Twix the artist of Time Capsule?

No. The original artist of “Time Capsule” is Valiant, named at the head of the page heading. Deejay Twix is credited only as producer of the bouyon remix, and this profile presents him for that role alone. Valiant is not the subject of this page.

Why does the name appear in two forms?

Because the opened page uses both of them: deejay twix in the video heading and dj twix in its description. This profile keeps the long form, Deejay Twix, and flags the short variant instead of choosing in silence.

Where is Deejay Twix from?

His origin is named in no source opened during this research: no island, no town, no country. This site assigns Deejay Twix no origin without a source that names it. The question stays open.

Why is this profile so short?

Because the verifiable file is short: one opened page, one remix credit repeated twice, bouyon and bouyonriddim tags, and no documented biography or origin. This site prefers a short and exact profile to a portrait filled with deductions. It will grow if the remixer publishes more primary sources.
