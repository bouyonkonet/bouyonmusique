Esclav: the bouyon band behind No Ugly Ting and Viway

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Esclav is a Dominican bouyon band whose dated songs start in the late 2000s with “No Ugly Ting”, “Sortir Descande” and “Viway”. In 2025 the band put its catalogue online on its own label, Esclav Inc.: three albums released the same day, billed as underground bouyon classics.

A new school name of the 2000s

The oldest dated traces are “No Ugly Ting”, tagged bouyon 2007, and “Sortir Descande”, tagged bouyon 2008. In February 2010 the Numusiczone channel posted “Viway” and introduced it as bouyon from the Esclav band of Dominica.

The name also appears in the list of new school bands quoted on our Xs Groove page, next to Triple K International, Ncore and Signal. On archive channels, one mix brings together Triple Kay, Esclav and Skinny Banton, three names from the same Dominican generation.

Slave To Master, Slave For Music, Revolution

On March 24, 2025, Esclav released three albums on Deezer, “Slave To Master”, “Slave For Music” and “Revolution”, on the Esclav Inc. label. On YouTube every song went up as official audio tagged bouyon. The credits name Falco, DJ Midian and Koko Killer on vocals, Carllian James, Midian Pacquette and Kelis Mitchel as lyricists, and Yohann Ferrol on music.

Guests show up too: Bambii on “Viway” and “Heat”, and Mr Benji N2O, the voice of Triple Kay, on “One Nation”. Mastering went to Joseph “Foxx” Royer of Blackkingz Studio.

Archiving for the next generation

In April 2025 the channel started a series, “Esclav: Unmastered”. The first episode, “The Catalog”, explains that going back to the early recordings began as preservation and turned into a look at the roots of the sound and the lost influence of the pioneers.

New releases keep coming: “Anxiety” in May 2025, then “Name On It” with Malcom Meckert in June, written by Carllian “Falco” James, who describes it as a bridge between reggae heritage and today's bouyon energy. For the wider story of that era, see the history of bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

What is Esclav?

A Dominican bouyon band, active from the late 2000s, with songs such as No Ugly Ting, Sortir Descande and Viway.

Who sings in Esclav?

The 2025 credits name Falco, DJ Midian and Koko Killer. The full lineup and founding year are not given.

What albums has the band released?

Slave To Master, Slave For Music and Revolution, put online on March 24, 2025 on the Esclav Inc. label.

Who has Esclav worked with?

Mr Benji N2O of Triple Kay on One Nation, Bambii on Viway and Heat, and Malcom Meckert on Name On It.

Why did these albums come out in 2025?

The band says it started revisiting its early recordings to preserve them, and presents them as underground classics.
