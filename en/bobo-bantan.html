Bobo Bantan: My Bouyon Girl

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Bobo Bantan first appears as a feature with Mr Gwada, on two tracks billed Bouyon 2026 and posted in 2025, “Big Chain” and “Ben Ur Back”, both on riddims by J2mo. In 2026 his own channel takes over: a live performance of “Money Makers” on New Year’s Eve, then “My Bouyon Girl”, which carries the genre in its title, and “360°”. The writing credit on “My Bouyon Girl” gives the name Kurt Williams. His pages show the flag and the name of Dominica, and the hashtag Atlanta, without ever giving a birthplace. One useful note: the word Bantan is also in Asa Bantan’s name, but no source links the two artists, and this site assumes no link between them.

Two features with Mr Gwada

“Big Chain”, posted on June 24, 2025 on the MR GWADA BOUYON NATION767 channel, running 2 minutes 50, is billed BOUYON 2026. The credits give the writing to Kernel Stevens, Mr Gwada’s legal name, and Kurt Williams, the video direction to O Banga, the riddim to J2mo and the mix to Di youte Judah. The description adds that the song was inspired by 20 Strong Atlanta, without saying more.

“Ben Ur Back”, from September 1, 2025, running 3 minutes 13, is also billed Bouyon 2026 and sits on J2mo’s Layou River Riddim, also called Demon Riddim. Both pages link to Mr Gwada’s album “Bouyon Blacksheep Legend 2026” on Bandcamp.

My Bouyon Girl and Bobo Bantan’s channel

On January 2, 2026, Bobo Bantan’s channel posted “Money Makers” live, a full New Year’s Eve performance, billed bouyon, with the hashtags BouyonMusic, DominicaMusic and Atlanta. The next track, “My Bouyon Girl”, came out as an official visualizer on April 30, 2026, then as a distributed release on May 6, 2026 through United Masters. The distributor page credits production to The Beastbox and writing and composition to Kurt Williams; the artist’s page credits recording, mixing and mastering to O Banga and speaks of bouyon and dancehall.

“360°”, posted on October 11, 2026, running 3 minutes 08, is produced by O Banga and tagged bouyon and Dominica. Another track on the channel, “We Outside Tonight”, from September 2026, also produced by O Banga, says nothing about its genre: it is named here and kept out of the structured data. O Banga thus returns at every stage, from directing the “Big Chain” video to producing “360°”.

What the record does not say

The name Kurt Williams appears as writer and composer on “My Bouyon Girl” and as co writer on both tracks with Mr Gwada: this profile notes that consistency without drawing a biography from it. The flag and name of Dominica, on several pages, and the hashtag Atlanta, on the New Year’s Eve live, are signals, not civil record facts: no page says where Bobo Bantan was born or where he lives.

No opened source links Bobo Bantan to Asa Bantan. The word the two stage names share establishes neither kinship nor artistic lineage, and this site does not present one as linked to the other. Upcoming releases can be followed on our new bouyon 2026 page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Bobo Bantan?

Bobo Bantan is a bouyon artist posting on his own channel since 2026 (“Money Makers” live, “My Bouyon Girl”, “360°”) after two features with Mr Gwada in 2025. The writing credit on “My Bouyon Girl” gives the name Kurt Williams.

Is Bobo Bantan related to Asa Bantan?

No opened source says so. The two stage names share the word Bantan, but that establishes no link, and this site assumes none.

Which tracks did he make with Mr Gwada?

“Big Chain” and “Ben Ur Back”, both billed Bouyon 2026, written by Kernel Stevens and Kurt Williams on J2mo riddims, and tied to Mr Gwada’s album “Bouyon Blacksheep Legend 2026”.

Where is Bobo Bantan from?

No opened page gives his birthplace. His pages show the flag and name of Dominica and an Atlanta hashtag: this site reports those signals without presenting them as a confirmed origin.

Who produces Bobo Bantan?

The Beastbox is credited as producer of “My Bouyon Girl”, and O Banga as producer of “360°” and “We Outside Tonight”, as well as for mixing “My Bouyon Girl” and directing the “Big Chain” video.
