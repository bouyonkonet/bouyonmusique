Bouyon music: Skillibeng at WCMF 2026, dancehall on Night 1

By Smokiller · Published November 2, 2026

Night 1 of the World Creole Music Festival 2026 put Jamaican dancehall at the top of the bill. Skillibeng played on Friday 23 October 2026 at Windsor Park, Roseau, on the same stage as Capleton, per DancehallMag.

Two generations of dancehall on one stage

The pairing covers a lot of ground in a single evening, writes DancehallMag: Capleton, a fixture of reggae and dancehall since the late 1980s, known to fans as The Fireman, and Skillibeng, carried by the newer wave.

Skillibeng broke through with Whap Whap and the Crocodile Teeth remix featuring Nicki Minaj, recalls DancehallMag. Capleton s return to Dominica, twenty years after his last stage on the island, has its own dedicated page, Capleton at WCMF 2026.

Dominica and the neighbouring islands on the same night

The night was not only about the guests. Colton T played to his home crowd, and Dominica Festivals, quoted by DancehallMag, praised the energy, rhythm and unmistakable Dominican vibes he brought to Night 1.

Maurane Voyer of Martinique brought shatta to Windsor Park, Midnight Groovers held the cadence-lypso with frontman Chubby, and Klass covered Haitian kompa. TK International and Ridge and Pudaz rounded out the night s bill, per DancehallMag.

The festival s pitch, in the organisers words

The festival is organised by the Government of Dominica, the Ministry of Tourism and the Discover Dominica Authority. The organisers present a programme spanning R and B, soca, dancehall, reggae, bouyon, cadence-lypso, kompa, soukous and amapiano, per DancehallMag.

For many fans, the draw is seeing Jamaican dancehall share a stage with Dominican bouyon and Haitian kompa. DancehallMag also raises the open question of how much room homegrown and French Caribbean acts get next to the bigger imports.

The rest of the weekend

The 26th edition of the festival continued on Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October 2026 at Windsor Park, with Ne-Yo, Machel Montano, Maxi Priest, Alkaline, Fridayy and Uncle Waffles spread across the three nights, per DancehallMag.

No set list for Skillibeng s set was published in the source opened for this page: this page keeps only what DancehallMag verified, the concert, the date, the stage and the company.
