Zone Rouge: the bouyon group of Owl, Savage and Trixx

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Zone Rouge is a bouyon group that the credits present as three voices: Trixx, Owl, also written De Owl, and Savage. The name has been around since 2017, with “S.H” on the Sook Pak Riddim. Its biggest hit came in January 2023: “S.O.T.T”, with Gwada G on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim, past 286,000 views.

Early days, from S.H to the stage

On November 18, 2017, DjeasyPromos posted “S.H”, on the Sook Pak Riddim, billed “2018 bouyon”. The same theme returns in “She Want A Man With A S.H”, with Reo, billed “Bouyon 2017” and also carried by the WizZarD HouSe RecorDs channel. In January 2018, 365 Music Promo uploaded “Party”, “2018 Bouyon”, which went past 15,000 views.

The group shows up live too. In November 2019, the Dominica 767 channel posted “Live CITP 2019”, tagged #Bouyon and #Dominica. Other videos catch them at 767 Hideaway for a Color 4 Color night, or at Breakfast Fete 2022 with “Banana Boy”. None of these pages gives the members' island of birth, though.

De Streets For Real and the ThaWizzard circle

On October 18, 2022, Zone Rouge posted “De Streets For Real” on its own channel, marked “Bouyon Music 2022”. The upload credits the three voices, De Owl, Savage and Trixx, and gives production to WizZarD HouSe RecorDs, the studio of ThaWizZarD. The video for the same song is past 28,000 views.

ThaWizzard's channel holds several more of the group's songs, such as “Not In Time”, “Crowd Pleaser”, “Fete Land” and “Rouge Life”. Zone Rouge also guests for Asa Bantan on “Play Mass”, close to 58,000 views.

With Gwada G and DJ Taffy

On January 12, 2023, DJ Taffy opened the Funny Riddim with “S.O.T.T”, subtitled “Hard Like Brad”. The upload spells out the roles: Zone Rouge for Trixx, Owl and Savage, and Gwada G for the “hard like brad” part. Recorded at Riddim Beast Studio with Nikita Fridge Christmas on drums, it was past 286,000 views when we read it on October 29, 2026.

The pairing with Gwada G carried on that year. “Superstars”, posted on April 15, 2023, is written and performed by Zone Rouge and Gwada G, again produced by DJ Taffy, and has passed 65,000 views. “Take It”, on the Rude Boy Riddim, went further, past 84,000, and “Hard And Bad” is close to 34,000.

Frequently asked questions

Who is in Zone Rouge?

The credits on S.O.T.T and De Streets For Real name three voices: Trixx, Owl (or De Owl) and Savage.

What is their most played song?

S.O.T.T (Hard Like Brad), with Gwada G on DJ Taffy's Funny Riddim, released January 12, 2023, past 286,000 views.

What were their first bouyon songs?

S.H on the Sook Pak Riddim, posted in November 2017, She Want A Man With A S.H with Reo, then Party in January 2018.

Who produces Zone Rouge?

DJ Taffy on S.O.T.T, Superstars and Take It, and WizZarD HouSe RecorDs, ThaWizzard's studio, on De Streets For Real.

Is Zone Rouge from Dominica?

Their live videos are posted by the Dominica 767 channel with the #Dominica tag, but no source we opened states where the members come from.
