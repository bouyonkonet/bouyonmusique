Frequently asked questions

When is the World Creole Music Festival 2026?

WCMF 2026 runs from Friday October 23 to Sunday October 25, 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica. Its three nights bring together bouyon, cadence, zouk and kompa: it is the annual summit of the bouyon scene and its biggest international showcase at home.

Did bouyon go global in 2026?

The crossover is documented: Smokiller's bouyon flip of Coi Leray's Nothing Into Something had 854,700 TikTok views as of October 19, 2026, and Pow Pow / Allo Doudou by Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS was certified Single Or (gold) by SNEP in France. What does "bouyon music" mean?

Bouyon music is the English name for bouyon, the genre created in 1988 in Roseau, Dominica, by the band WCK. The word bouyon means soup in kwéyòl: a mix where everything blends. In 2026, French-speaking audiences keep it alive in Guadeloupe, Martinique, French Guiana and Paris. Full definition: what is bouyon.

Where can I see bouyon live in 2026?

In Dominica first. Ti Vilaj Kwéyòl, the Creole village of Independence, runs from October 19 to 22, 2026 at the Botanic Gardens in Roseau, with the current bouyon scene on the bill, then the World Creole Music Festival takes over Windsor Park from October 23 to 25. The rest of the year, the Mas Domnik carnival remains bouyon's home.

Which producers shaped bouyon in 2026?

Smokiller, whose bouyon flip of Coi Leray was reshared by the artist, HYPNO and his Mauvais Plan built at 160 BPM, MB On The Track and his Rock Bouyon reel of October 16, and Denmath, whose beats are now framed by a written agreement with Bouyon Shattaz. See the bouyon producers.

Which bouyon artists mattered in 2026?

The published list of bouyon nominees at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026 gives a good snapshot: 1T1, Asa Bantan, BRG Hollywood, Dirty Dawg Pudaz, Mr Ridge, Reo, Shelly and Signal Band, and Trilla G, and Mr Ridge won bouyon artist of the year, according to results published in September 2026. On the French Caribbean side, Miimii KDS earned a Single Or (gold) with "Pow Pow / Allo Doudou".

Is New Bouyon the bouyon of 2026?

Partly. In its generic use, New Bouyon names the contemporary bouyon of the 2020s, and Smokiller uses it in his Best New Bouyon Songs selections for Bouyon Shattaz. The term also has two signed uses: TiiTii NBA's New Bouyon Age EP in 2023 and the concept Ricky Bishop launched in 2024. See New Bouyon.

Keep a listening note for your 2026 discoveries

Choose two available recordings. Save each full title, artist, version and official link. Listen once for the voice, then return to the bass entrance and rhythmic changes. Write down what makes you want to hear the recording again.

If a live performance by the same artist is available, compare it with the studio recording. A longer introduction, a repeated chorus or a response from the crowd describes that performance; it is not a rule for the whole genre.

Try the exercise with the songs guide, find the performer in the artist directory, or open the Road Mix.
