Bouyon Music 2026: all of the year’s bouyon

Published September 24, 2026

Bouyon music is the English term for the genre, and its year 2026 is playing out in English as much as in French: it is in Gwada, in Martinique, in French Guiana and in Paris that the French-speaking audience keeps alive the bouyon born in Dominica, and the English-speaking Caribbean and diaspora are right there with them.

The state of bouyon in 2026

A networked scene: Dominican artists collaborate with the French Caribbean, producers launch their riddims on video, specialized media like Bouyon Shattaz and its French-speaking partners relay every release, and the Roseau WCMF serves as the annual summit.

What are the new bouyon releases of 2026?

Among the releases Bouyon Shattaz followed in 2026: Joli BB by Reo with Martinican singer Maurane Voyer, released in October on the Mauve label, FIX UP by G'z Up, the track Tarif by Koko Bwa with Denmath, and Smokiller's bouyon remixes, including Heaven Baby. The full list is on the new bouyon 2026 page.

When is the World Creole Music Festival 2026?

WCMF 2026 runs from Friday October 23 to Sunday October 25, 2026 at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica. Its three nights bring together bouyon, cadence, zouk and kompa: it is the annual summit of the bouyon scene and its biggest international showcase at home.

Did bouyon go global in 2026?

The crossover is documented: Smokiller's bouyon flip of Coi Leray's Nothing Into Something had 854,700 TikTok views as of October 19, 2026, and Pow Pow / Allo Doudou by Niska, Litleboy LSBeats767 and Miimii KDS was certified Single Or (gold) by SNEP in France. Details on the bouyon goes global page.
