Umpa: No Reply with Mr Ridge and Dova, a stop in bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Umpa is first of all a Dennery Segment voice: that is the label on almost all of his opened pages. He enters this site through one title, “No Reply”, shared with Mr Ridge and Dova, produced by Cross D Bridge Studio and released on June 12, 2024. The page for that title on the More Sewo channel files it explicitly under bouyon. This profile is deliberately short: one title, clear credits, and an origin the sources do not state.

No Reply, the bouyon title

“No Reply” was published on June 12, 2024 on the More Sewo channel as “Mr Ridge, Umpa feat Dova - No Reply”, running 2 minutes 43. The description credits production to Cross D Bridge Studio, Mr Ridge’s studio, and the page tags name the genre outright: bouyon music, dominica bouyon, bouyon soca. The TuneCore release, credited to Ridge & Umpa under the line 2024 Ridge, carries the same date.

It is the only title opened in this research where Umpa’s name is tied to bouyon by the page itself. It brings three voices together: Mr Ridge, the Dominican keyboardist and producer already profiled on this site, Dova, another name from the Dominica circuit, and Umpa.

The rest of the path, on the Dennery Segment side

The other opened pages present Umpa as a Dennery Segment artist. “Baguy”, on G6 Prod’s Dynamite Riddim, was published in 2019 with the label Lucian Dennery Soca. “Pain Killer”, with Mikado, on Jammin’s Tiki Twaka Riddim, is filed as Dennery Segment 2025. “Chaleur”, with Teamfoxx, on the By A Bar Riddim, is presented in August 2026 as Saint Lucia Dennery Segment for Jounen Kwéyòl.

Those titles state no bouyon tie and stay in the text here. The Saint Lucia mentions come from the titles given by the channels that publish them: they are circuit signals, to be read alongside the page on bouyon and Saint Lucia, not a biography. No opened page gives Umpa’s birthplace.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Umpa?

Umpa is an artist whose opened titles are mostly presented as Dennery Segment. He is on this site for “No Reply”, a bouyon title shared with Mr Ridge and Dova.

Who produced No Reply?

Cross D Bridge Studio, according to the description of the video published on the More Sewo channel on June 12, 2024. The distributed version is credited to Ridge & Umpa.

Why is No Reply filed as bouyon?

Because the title’s page says so: its tags include bouyon music, dominica bouyon and bouyon soca.

Where is Umpa from?

No opened page says. Several of his titles are presented by their publishers as Saint Lucia Dennery Segment, which remains a circuit signal, not proof of origin.
