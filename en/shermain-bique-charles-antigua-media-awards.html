Shermain Bique-Charles, four awards in Antigua

By Smokiller · Published November 10, 2026 · Updated November 11, 2026

Dominican journalist Shermain Bique-Charles won four awards at the Antigua and Barbuda Legacy Ball and Media Awards on Friday 6 November 2026. Best News Reporter, Best Journalist and Current Affairs, Best Programming Director and Best News Editor: she was the only winner to take home four awards, and her outlet, Antigua.news, was the only media house to win in four categories.

Four awards in one evening

The four awards are: Best News Reporter; Best Journalist and Current Affairs; Best Programming Director; and Best News Editor. The ceremony, the 2026 Legacy Ball and Media Awards, was held on Friday 6 November 2026 in Antigua and Barbuda.

Shermain Bique-Charles is the managing director of Antigua.news. Her outlet had received six nominations and was the only media house to win in four categories. She said the award is really for the people, for the followers and the fans, for those who continue to support the online newspaper and especially for those who trust the platform for accurate and honest information.

From Kairi FM to Antigua.news

Her career began in Dominica, at Kairi FM. She packed Alvin Knight CDs there, then hosted his Saturday Top 25 Countdown, then the Mid-Morning Show at the age of 18, before becoming news director. She also worked in print, at The Chronicle in Dominica, and she received the DBS Flamboyant Award for Best Journalist.

She then moved to Antigua and Barbuda, where she has worked for more than eight years. She teamed up with Ambassador Dr. Dario Item to manage Antigua.news. She also runs BiQ Vision Media and she is Public Relations Officer in Antigua Ministry of Social and Urban Transformation, a ministry with about thirteen departments.

A career between two islands

Shermain Bique-Charles story is that of a Dominican who built a career away from her island without cutting her roots. She learned the craft at Dominican radio, in the years when Kairi FM was training the voices of Dominica, then she built an online outlet in Antigua, in a bigger and more competitive media landscape. Her four awards reward reporting, current affairs, programming and editing at once: four different crafts, in a single evening.

The Dominican press welcomed the news on 7 November 2026. The exact category names and the outlet name, Antigua.news, come from the detailed account published by BuzzDA. Another account published the same day did not give the category names: this page therefore sticks to the facts given by the most complete source.

A win for the readers

In her reaction, Shermain Bique-Charles put her readers first: this award is really for the people, for the followers and the fans, for those who continue to support the online newspaper and especially for those who trust the platform for accurate and honest information. It is a line that sums up her career: a journalist trained at Dominican radio, now running an online outlet in Antigua, and recognised by her peers in four crafts at once.

For Dominica, it is also a diaspora win: one of its journalists, who left to work in Antigua more than eight years ago, is back in the news of her home island with four awards. Her story shows that voices trained in Dominica carry far, and that Dominican journalism, from Kairi FM to Antigua.news, knows how to earn its place in the Caribbean.
