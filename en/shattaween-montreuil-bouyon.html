Shattaween: Dominican bouyon dances Halloween in Montreuil

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

For Halloween 2026, the Shattaween party settles at Le Chinois, a concert venue and club in Montreuil, Seine-Saint-Denis, on Saturday 31 October. SortiraParis, which announces the event, places Dominican bouyon in the programme, alongside shatta, dancehall and konpa. This page follows that announcement, adding no names and no times that were not published.

A party by Le Bal Tropical de Paname

Shattaween, billed as an Afro and Caribbean Halloween Party, is a night created by Le Bal Tropical de Paname, per SortiraParis. It runs on Saturday 31 October 2026, from 11:30 p.m. to 5 a.m., at Le Chinois in Montreuil.

The organisers announce DJ sets until dawn, guests encouraged to come in costume, and a Halloween set design of pumpkins, masks, candles and smoke, per the same announcement.

Bouyon in the announced programme

The musical programme blends shatta, dancehall, bouyon, konpa and afrobeats, with room also for ndombolo, batida, amapiano and Afro-electro across the night, per SortiraParis.

The announcement presents shatta as closely tied to Martinique, bouyon as Dominican and konpa as Haitian. That musical geography, in a single night a few metro stops from Paris, sums up how Caribbean sounds now circulate in Ile-de-France.

The announced DJs

At the decks, the organisers announce Cucurucho Groovalizacion, Ghost Nebula, Spark972 and Groovalizacion, per SortiraParis.

This page gives no ticket price and no booking link, which the opened announcement does not detail here. For up-to-date practical information, the event announcement and the venue are the places to check.

A signal for bouyon in the Paris region

A general Halloween party, not a bouyon festival, programming bouyon at the same level as shatta and dancehall shows that the Dominican sound is part of the normal vocabulary of Afro-Caribbean nights in Paris in 2026.

That is a diffusion signal, not a ranking, and this page presents it as such, from a single verified announcement.
