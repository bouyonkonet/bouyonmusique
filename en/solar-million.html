Solar Million: Bouyon 2099 and the album Otherworld

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Solar Million is an artist whose label carries the same name. The title “Bouyon 2099”, a single from August 1, 2025 on Deezer, took off with the lyric video of April 23, 2026, tagged #bouyon and #bouyonmusic, more than 25,000 views when this profile was written. The next day, the album “Otherworld” gathered the title with “Genie”, “Long Ago”, “Dreamer”, “Play In The Dark”, “F.T.B” and “Twirl”. Only one of the seven titles carries the genre's name, and it is the one that found its audience.

Bouyon 2099: a title named after the genre

On April 23, 2026, the Solar Million channel uploaded “Bouyon 2099 (Official Lyric Video)”, with the tags #solarmillion, #bouyon2099, #otherworld, #bouyon and #bouyonmusic. The title already existed as a single: Deezer dates it August 1, 2025, label Solar Million, 1 minute 54. The video was past 25,200 views on October 27, 2026, far ahead of the channel's other titles.

The name states the project: a bouyon projected into 2099, a short title in the format of platform singles. This site presents it for what it is, a title claiming the genre in its name and tags, without lending it a role in the Dominican scene that the opened pages do not document.

Otherworld: the album of April 24, 2026

On Deezer, “Otherworld” is an album dated April 24, 2026, label Solar Million, seven tracks: “Genie” (2 min 02), “Long Ago” (2 min), “Bouyon 2099” (1 min 54), “Dreamer” (1 min 49), “Play In The Dark” (1 min 37), “F.T.B” (1 min 25) and “Twirl” (2 min 04). “Dreamer” and “Twirl” had come out as singles on September 19 and October 17, 2025.

The official audios published the same day on YouTube give the measure: “Genie” close to 6,800 views, “Long Ago” 5,600, “Dreamer” 3,200, “Twirl” 3,100, “Play In The Dark” 2,000. The gap with “Bouyon 2099” shows that the word bouyon in a title draws a specific audience, the one our page on new bouyon 2026 follows.

What the pages do not say

Solar Million's Deezer artist page lists four releases, all from 2025 and 2026, and no opened page gives a biography, a legal name or a place: this site assigns no island. Production credits are not filled in on the opened pages.

This profile is therefore short and stays so on purpose: it documents one title billed bouyon and the album carrying it, from the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026, and will be extended if the artist publishes an official page or further bouyon titles.
