Bermixx

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Bermixx releases, under his own name and on his own label, a run of short mixes that carry the genre in their titles. On his Deezer artist page, at least eight releases between September 2024 and May 2026 name bouyon, from “3 Minutes de Bouyon” to “Se Mimii ki dèyè sa (Bouyon)”, and two of them together pass 650,000 views on YouTube. The rest of the catalogue leans clearly toward shatta. No opened page gives an origin, a birthplace or a biography: this profile sticks to the releases.

A run of bouyon mixes in short format

The Bermixx Deezer artist page shows 60 releases, almost all singles, and every one opened during this research carries Bermixx as its label. The bouyon run starts on September 12, 2024 with “3 Minutes de Bouyon”, which actually runs 9 minutes 29, then continues with “Le Mix Bouyon Percutant” on December 20, 2024 (2 minutes 23), “BOUYON MIX” on February 9, 2025 (2 minutes 12, with a featured credit to Berson seide), “Le Mix Bouyon” on July 27, 2025 (2 minutes), “Bouyon Du Moment” on September 2, 2025, “The Purge Bouyon Mix” on October 4, 2025 (2 minutes 06), “Se Mimii ki dèyè sa (Bouyon)” on February 17, 2026 (3 minutes) and “Le Mix Ecrase Le Bouyon” on May 10, 2026.

That format, a few minutes of blended sound released as a title of its own, looks more like DJ work than a singer’s work. The opened listings do not say which songs or riddims these mixes contain, so this profile names no artist inside them. For the practice itself, our page on the bouyon mix describes it, and the bouyon playlist gathers titles by identified artists.

What YouTube shows

Two of these releases were opened on YouTube, distributed by TuneCore under the line “℗ Bermixx”. “Le Mix Bouyon” showed 410,693 views at the time of the check, and “Le Mix Bouyon Percutant” 240,756 views. For two minute mixes released without a video, those figures point to a real audience, without saying where it sits.

On Deezer, the bouyon releases carry the genre tag Dance, a generic platform category that says nothing more. The one Creole phrase in a title, “Se Mimii ki dèyè sa”, is a language clue, not proof of origin.

A mostly shatta catalogue, an undocumented origin

Most Bermixx titles belong to shatta: “Le Shatta Des Filles”, “Shatta Turbo”, “Shatta Anthem”, “La Purge Du Shatta” on October 10, 2026, and many more. The same page also carries “MIX SPECIAL 973 GUYANE”, a raboday remix of “M Pap Abandone” and “Men Bon RARA”. The 973 points to French Guiana and raboday and rara to Haiti, but these are repertoire signals, not an address: none of these titles says where Bermixx comes from.

This site therefore assigns Bermixx no territory. The verified fact is simpler: a mix producer who, since 2024, has regularly released bouyon titled releases on his own label, next to an even larger flow of shatta mixes. If a biography or an interview appears, this page will be completed and dated.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Bermixx?

Bermixx is the artist and label name behind a run of mixes released on the platforms, 60 releases on his Deezer page, mostly shatta, at least eight of which carry the word bouyon in their title between 2024 and 2026. No biography was found in the opened sources.

Which Bermixx bouyon titles are verified?

“3 Minutes de Bouyon” (September 12, 2024), “Le Mix Bouyon Percutant” (December 20, 2024), “BOUYON MIX” (February 9, 2025), “Le Mix Bouyon” (July 27, 2025), “Bouyon Du Moment” (September 2, 2025), “The Purge Bouyon Mix” (October 4, 2025), “Se Mimii ki dèyè sa (Bouyon)” (February 17, 2026) and “Le Mix Ecrase Le Bouyon” (May 10, 2026).

Which Bermixx bouyon title is the most played?

Among the opened pages, “Le Mix Bouyon”, released on July 27, 2025, showed 410,693 views on YouTube at the time of the check, ahead of “Le Mix Bouyon Percutant” with 240,756 views.

Where is Bermixx from?

No opened source says. The catalogue includes a mix titled 973 Guyane and raboday and rara titles, but these are repertoire signals, not a stated origin. This site assigns Bermixx no island and no country.

Does Bermixx mostly make bouyon?

No. The larger share of his catalogue carries the word shatta. Bouyon forms a regular but minority run of short mixes within it.

Who is Berson seide?

It is the name credited as featured on “BOUYON MIX”, released on February 9, 2025. No other information about that name appears in the opened pages.
