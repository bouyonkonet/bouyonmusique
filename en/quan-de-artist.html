Quan De Artist: Tune of the Crop 2023, Barbados Bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 27, 2026

Quan De Artist, Jaquan Headley by his legal name, is a singer from Barbados. In August 2023, aged 21, he won the Tune of the Crop, the most played title on the Grand Kadooment road, with “Waistline”, according to Barbados Today and CBC. Since then his pages regularly bill “Barbados Bouyon”: “Booty Clap” on the Energetic Riddim (Bouyon 2024), “Don't Mix Me” (Crop Over 2025), produced by Litleboy, and “Do It Like That” on the Okra Slime Riddim (Barbados Bouyon 2026). His Deezer page lists 57 releases since 2021.

Waistline, Tune of the Crop 2023

On August 7, 2023, Barbados Today reported the Grand Kadooment result: Quan De Artist's “Waistline” was the most played title on the road, 11 plays at the judging points, ahead of Chief Diin's “Yuh Know Da” (7) and Leadpipe and Jus Jay's “Brain Freeze” (6). Two days later, CBC introduced him: Jaquan Headley, 21, about eight years in music across several genres, writer of all his own lyrics, not to be confused with former Pic O De Crop Monarch Quon.

“Waistline (Official Audio) PTP Riddim Crop Over 2023”, uploaded on April 19, 2023 on his QuanDeArtistMusic channel, was past 847,000 views when this profile was written; the single is dated June 30, 2023 on Deezer, next to the EP “Waistline Crop Over Episode 2023”. This site does not confuse him either with Quan, the Dominican singer: two artists, two islands.

Booty Clap, Don't Mix Me, Do It Like That: the Barbados Bouyon billing

On November 12, 2023, “Booty Clap (Energetic Riddim) Bouyon 2024” came out on YouTube and Deezer, recorded at EarCandy Studio, written by Quan De Artist. On May 16, 2025, “Don't Mix Me”, billed “Crop Over 2025 [Barbados Bouyon]”, produced by LsBeats767, the label of Litleboy, recorded and mixed by DonWrita, was past 27,000 views; the single is dated May 18, 2025 on Deezer.

On April 2, 2026, “Do It Like That (Okra Slime Riddim) [Barbados Bouyon 2026]” carried the run onto the Bajan riddim this site already documents with Sharkie, Stella Billy and Fiddy ii. A Dominican producer on a Crop Over title, a Bajan riddim billed bouyon: the circuit described by our page on bouyon soca.

Fifty-seven releases in five years

Quan De Artist's Deezer artist page lists 57 releases between “Forever Hungry Ep” (April 1, 2021) and “Top Shotta” (October 19, 2026). Crop Over titles sit next to dancehall and riddims: “Brace It” and “All Out (Nutty Buddy Riddim)” (2023), “Rifle Rise”, “So Nice (Wild Hornz Riddim)”, the Brawling Riddim (EP, 2024), “Bounce”, “Risk It”, “Show It Off (Jack Hammer Riddim)”, “My Side” with Boogy Rankss (2025), “Bring A Bottle (Island Glow Riddim)”, “Jump Out”, “Fully Loaded” and “Run Up” (2026).

Boogy Rankss, guest on “My Side”, links this catalogue to those of DJ Kalli and Th3rd. This profile is written from Barbados Today, CBC and the YouTube and Deezer pages opened on October 27, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Quan De Artist?

Jaquan Headley, a singer from Barbados, winner of the 2023 Tune of the Crop with Waistline per Barbados Today and CBC, 57 releases on Deezer since 2021.

What are his bouyon titles?

Booty Clap (Energetic Riddim, Bouyon 2024), Don't Mix Me (Crop Over 2025, Barbados Bouyon) and Do It Like That (Okra Slime Riddim, Barbados Bouyon 2026).

What is the Tune of the Crop?

The most played title on the Grand Kadooment road, the final parade of Barbados' Crop Over; in 2023 Waistline was played 11 times at the judging points according to Barbados Today.

Who produced Don't Mix Me?

LsBeats767 / Litleboy, with recording, mixing and mastering by DonWrita, according to the YouTube page of May 16, 2025.

Is Quan De Artist the same as Quan?

No: Quan De Artist is Barbadian and Quan is Dominican; CBC also notes he is not to be confused with former Pic O De Crop Monarch Quon.
