Money Shaun: the voice on She Want That Ah, bouyon, Dominica

By Smokiller · Published October 20, 2026

Money Shaun is a bouyon voice whose name travels the world with almost no archive behind it. He is known through two documented records: “She Want That Ah”, released under the name of Mr Ridge with Money Shaun as the featured artist, produced by Cross D Bridge Studio and More Sewo, and “She Stupid”, with Ridge and 1T1. Those two records have been played for years in bouyon and soca DJ mixes, from Dominica to Miami. This profile tells exactly what the public record allows to be told, no more, no less: no official biography, no confirmed hometown, no solo artist page on the major platforms. Just credits, tracklists, and a name DJs keep programming.

Who Money Shaun is: a name known through features

Money Shaun is a bouyon artist whose public trace lives almost entirely in other people credits. Where most artists on this site have pages on Deezer, Spotify or Shazam, videos under their own name and identifiable social accounts, Money Shaun has, at the end of the research carried out for this profile, no confirmed solo artist page on the major platforms, no video published under his name alone and no official biography. His name appears as a feature, that is, as the guest voice on the records of another artist, Ridge, also released under the name Mr Ridge.

That feature status is not a formality. In Dominica bouyon, many voices live exactly like that: a singer lays a vocal on a producer riddim, the record goes out under the producer name, and the voice becomes famous at fetes without ever building a catalogue of its own. Ridge is, according to the profile this site devotes to him, a Dominican bouyon keyboardist and producer, known on Instagram as @crossdbridge. The studio credited on the Money Shaun record, Cross D Bridge Studio, carries the same name as that handle: everything indicates that “She Want That Ah” was born inside Ridge workshop, with Money Shaun as the guest voice.

With no biography available, this profile refuses the usual shortcuts. No source consulted gives Money Shaun real name, nor his birth date, nor his town. The keywords of the official “She Want That Ah” video even mix the generic labels of the genre, Dominica bouyon, Guadeloupe music, bouyon soca, without assigning a precise origin to the artist. This profile therefore places Money Shaun where the documents place him: inside the Dominican bouyon circuit of Ridge, Cross D Bridge Studio and the label More Sewo, without inventing a personal address for him.

Why publish a profile this short? Because search engines and fans ask the question, and an honest answer is worth more than a gap filled by generated texts inventing careers. Money Shaun exists in the public record through two real records, played by real DJs in front of real audiences. That is little, and it is documented. This profile is the first on this site built almost entirely on DJ mix tracklists, and it owns that particular material: proof by programming.

She Want That Ah: the record that toured the mixes

“She Want That Ah” is the central record of the file. Its reference publication is the YouTube video titled “Mr Ridge feat. Money Shaun, She want that ah”. The official description credits the production to Cross D Bridge Studio and to More Sewo, and attaches a MediaFire download link, the direct distribution method bouyon producers used to service DJs before the platforms took over. The record is therefore designed, in its very form of release, as a DJ tool: a record made to be downloaded, programmed and replayed.

More Sewo is not an unknown name to readers of this site. It is the label BONNA, the bouyon artist from La Plaine in Dominica, is signed to, and its presence in the production credit connects “She Want That Ah” to the same professional network that structures part of current bouyon between Dominica and Guadeloupe. For Money Shaun, that credit is the only documented industry attachment: no personal label, no published management, just this co-production between Ridge studio and a label that matters in the genre.

The real career of the record then reads in the tracklists. The DJ Close Connections podcast programmes it in “Soca Passport (Bouyon & French TakeOver)”, a mix presented as the best releases from Dominica, Saint Lucia, Martinique and Saint Kitts and Nevis, in the form “Mr Ridge & Money Shaun, She Want That Ah”. Digga D “Sewo Jam 2023-2024” mix, presented as an homage to Dominica, places it at track 30. DJ Selecta Beast plays it inside a soca and bouyon set. Deejblaze “Miami Carnival Soca Mix 2024” lists it at track 29. DJ T-Sia programmes it at 18:05 in her “Shatta, Bouyon & Soca, Best of Carnaval 2024” mix. The Soca Podcast and the A.O.N Dynasty podcast pick it up as well.

That list is not padding: it is the lived discography of the record. Between 2023 and 2025, “She Want That Ah” was programmed by DJs of podcasts, carnival mixes and soca sets across at least three broadcast worlds, the Caribbean, the European diaspora and Miami. For a record with no campaign, no official artist video and no dedicated platform page, that is a remarkable longevity, and it says something about bouyon itself: it is a DJ music before it is an algorithm music, and a record can live there for years on the strength of sets alone. Find the spirit of that circuit in our bouyon playlist.

She Stupid: the second trace, with Ridge and 1T1

The second documented Money Shaun record is called “She Stupid”. The DJ Close Connections podcast tracklists bill it as “Ridge, Money Shaun & 1t1, She Stupid”, and the same billing returns in the “Soca Passport (Island Hoppin' 2024)” list. There the record sits alongside the Dominican bouyon repertoire of the moment: Ridge and J Lion, Asa Bantan, Signal Band, Trilla-G and Miimii KDS. No full platform listing for “She Stupid” was found during the research for this profile: no duration, no published writer credits, no readable official release date. The record exists in the public file only through its life in mixes.

The presence of 1T1 on this record is a valuable network clue. 1T1 is a central producer of current Guadeloupe bouyon, and his meeting with Ridge and Money Shaun on a single record shows, once again, the permanent circulation between Dominica and Guadeloupe that makes the bouyon of today: riddims, voices and records cross the Caribbean Sea in both directions, and features are the natural form of that circulation.

Should we look for more Money Shaun records? The research for this profile covered the streaming platforms, the mix archives and the search engines, in French and in English, under the forms Money Shaun and Mr Ridge feat. Money Shaun. No other credited record appeared in a checkable way. It is possible that records circulate under neighbouring spellings or inside mixes without published tracklists, but this site publishes only what can be reread. The documented Money Shaun catalogue therefore counts two records, and this profile presents them as such, without inflating the number.

Those two records share one trait that sketches, in negative, the role of Money Shaun: they are Ridge records. “She Want That Ah” is released under the name of Mr Ridge with Money Shaun featured, and “She Stupid” brings together Ridge, Money Shaun and 1T1. Money Shaun is therefore, in the checkable file, the voice associated with Ridge, the one the producer calls when the record needs that timbre and that energy. Many bouyon careers started exactly like that, in the shadow of a producer, before a first record under their own name. For Money Shaun, that first solo record is not documented to this day.

A DJ circuit rather than a platform circuit

The Money Shaun case sheds light on how bouyon really works, better than many fuller profiles. The genre was born in Dominica at the end of the 1980s with WCK, it was passed on through sound systems, fetes and carnivals, and part of its repertoire still lives outside the platforms: files shared between DJs, direct download links, mixes published on podcast hosts, records known by their place in a set rather than by their cover art. “She Want That Ah”, serviced through a MediaFire link and carried from mix to mix, belongs entirely to that economy.

That economy has its strengths and its blind spots. Its strength: a record can be adopted by DJs in several countries with no intermediary, and “She Want That Ah” was, from the Caribbean to Miami. Its blind spot: the guest artist can stay invisible while his record travels. Money Shaun has no artist page to claim, no catalogue to monetise cleanly, no profile where the fans of the mixes can find him. That is exactly the credit and rights problem Bouyon Shattaz documents across this site, from the history of bouyon soca to the producer profiles. The case here is pushed to the extreme: a name known to DJs, unknown to platforms.

It must also be said what tracklists cannot establish. They prove that the records exist and that they are played. They say nothing about the voice itself, nor about listening counts, nor about income. This profile therefore does not rank Money Shaun, lends him no audience and no figures, and does not turn two features into a full career. It records a real presence in bouyon and soca DJ programming between 2023 and 2025, and documents it with the names of the mixes where anyone can check it.

For the listener discovering the name here, the listening path is simple: “She Want That Ah” first, in the official Mr Ridge video or in any of the mixes named above, then “She Stupid” inside the DJ Close Connections sets. Two records, a few minutes of music, and one question left open: who is the voice behind the name, and will a record under his own name ever come? Our bouyon playlist will follow the answer if it arrives.

The limits of the file: what this profile does not know

This profile ends with the honest list of its unknowns, longer than for most artists on this site. Full name: unknown to the public record. Birth date and place: unknown. Town of residence: unknown. Path before 2023: unknown. Verified social accounts: none confirmed, so no link is published and the page schema sameAs field is empty. Artist page on Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Shazam or Audiomack: none found under this name during the research. Catalogue: two documented records, both in collaboration with Ridge.

Every unknown on that list is also information. It says that Money Shaun belongs to the part of bouyon that does not pass through platform offices and forms, the part of neighbourhood studios, features between producers and records that live at fetes. Documenting that part of the genre, with the same rigour as the stars, is part of the work of this site: bouyon is not only its most visible names, and its full history includes its feature voices.

If Money Shaun, Ridge, Cross D Bridge Studio or More Sewo publish a biography, a solo record, an artist page or official accounts, this profile will be completed, corrected and dated, like every page on this site. The DJs who programme his records and hold credits the research did not find are a source too: in bouyon, the archive is often on DJ hard drives before it is on the platforms.

In the meantime, the file fits in one sentence: Money Shaun is the guest voice of Mr Ridge “She Want That Ah”, produced by Cross D Bridge Studio and More Sewo, and of “She Stupid” with Ridge and 1T1, two records bouyon and soca DJs have been programming since 2023 far beyond Dominica. That is little paper for a name that made so many sets dance. It is, for now, all the checkable paper that exists, and this site prefers to say so rather than print a false one.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Money Shaun?

Money Shaun is a bouyon voice known through his features with the Dominican producer Ridge, also released under the name Mr Ridge. No official biography and no solo artist page were found on the major platforms during the research for this profile: his name is documented by two records, She Want That Ah and She Stupid, and by their repeated presence in bouyon and soca DJ mixes.

Is She Want That Ah a Mr Ridge record or a Money Shaun record?

Both, but not in the same role. The official publication, on YouTube, is titled Mr Ridge feat. Money Shaun: the record is released under the name of Mr Ridge, with Money Shaun as the featured artist, and the production is credited to Cross D Bridge Studio and More Sewo. Mixes then shorten it to Mr Ridge & Money Shaun, but the correct form remains the one of the original publication.

Which Money Shaun records are documented?

Two checkable records: She Want That Ah, by Mr Ridge with Money Shaun featured, produced by Cross D Bridge Studio and More Sewo, and She Stupid, which brings together Ridge, Money Shaun and 1T1 and which the DJ Close Connections podcast tracklists name, notably in Soca Passport (Island Hoppin' 2024). No solo record and no other credited feature was found in a checkable way.

Who produced She Want That Ah?

The official video description credits the production to Cross D Bridge Studio and to More Sewo. Cross D Bridge is also the name under which Ridge, the lead artist of the record, is known on Instagram (@crossdbridge), which ties the studio to Ridge himself. More Sewo is the label BONNA, the bouyon artist from La Plaine in Dominica, is also signed to.

Where is Money Shaun from?

The public record does not say, and this profile does not invent it: no source consulted gives his town or his country of origin. What is documented is his circuit: a Dominican producer, Ridge, a studio, Cross D Bridge Studio, a label, More Sewo, and records filed by DJs inside Dominica bouyon selections. His personal origin remains to be confirmed by the artist or his circle.

Where can I hear Money Shaun?

Through the official She Want That Ah video released under the name of Mr Ridge, then through the mixes that programme his two records: DJ Close Connections’ Soca Passport, Digga D’s Sewo Jam 2023-2024, Deejblaze’s Miami Carnival Soca Mix 2024 or DJ T-Sia’s Carnaval 2024 mix. To hear the wider bouyon scene, follow our bouyon playlist as well.

What is She Stupid with Money Shaun?

She Stupid is Money Shaun’s second documented record, billed as Ridge, Money Shaun & 1t1 in the DJ Close Connections podcast tracklists and in the Soca Passport (Island Hoppin' 2024) listing. There it sits among the Dominica bouyon repertoire of the moment, including Asa Bantan, Signal Band and Trilla-G. It exists in the public record through its life in mixes, with no complete platform listing found.

Who is Mr Ridge, Money Shaun’s partner?

Mr Ridge, artist name Ridge, is a Dominican bouyon artist, keyboardist and producer, known on Instagram as @crossdbridge. He won Bouyon Artist of the Year at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026, according to the results published in September 2026. Both of Money Shaun’s documented records are Ridge records, which makes Money Shaun the voice associated with that producer.

What is the link between Money Shaun and 1T1?

They meet on She Stupid, billed as Ridge, Money Shaun & 1t1 in the DJ Close Connections podcast tracklists. 1T1 is a central producer of today’s Guadeloupe bouyon, and his meeting with Ridge and Money Shaun on one record shows the constant traffic between Dominica and Guadeloupe: riddims, voices and features cross the sea in both directions.

What is More Sewo, credited on She Want That Ah?

More Sewo is the label credited with the production of She Want That Ah alongside Cross D Bridge Studio, according to the official video description. It is also the label BONNA, the bouyon artist from La Plaine in Dominica, is signed to. For Money Shaun, this credit is the only documented industry tie: no personal label and no published management were found.
