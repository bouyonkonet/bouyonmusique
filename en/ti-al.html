Ti Al: TunTun with Wizito, Baby ou Bonne and the Tolly Boys

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Ti Al is a singer whose own YouTube channel presents two of his tracks as bouyon: “Baby ou Bonne”, produced by DJ Taffy in 2023, and “TunTun”, the Wizito track released in August 2025 on the YMW Tolly Boys label, with Ti Al as the main guest. His public catalogue holds six releases since 2020, two of them with Wizito and one with Youry, in other words the core of the Tolly Boys circle. This profile follows those releases one by one and keeps what the pages call bouyon apart from what they file elsewhere, as trap or dancehall.

Who Ti Al is: what the published pages allow us to say

Ti Al publishes his music on his own YouTube channel, under the handle TiAlofficiel, and on the platforms under a label in his own name, TI AL, for most of his singles. The descriptions he writes all point to the same Instagram account, ti_al971. The 971 is the Guadeloupe code, and it is the only geographic marker he displays himself.

Two other markers point to Saint Martin, without settling anything. His first two tracks were mixed by Speyenol Di Geni Son in a studio his descriptions call Awesome Sound Record Studio SXM, SXM being the usual short form for Saint Martin, and Awesome Sound Record is the label of his first single on Deezer. His most regular partners, Wizito and Youry, belong to the YMW Tolly Boys circle, which our Tolly Boys profile ties to Sint Maarten.

No biography, no interview and no date or place of birth were found for Ti Al in the sources opened. This profile therefore gives him no origin and no town: it reports the published markers, Guadeloupe through the handle, Saint Martin through the studio and the circle, and leaves the question open.

2020 to 2022: Girl ou sé on brand new, Doudou, Left Right and Fresh

The first single listed on Deezer, “Girl ou sé on brand new”, came out on 24 October 2020 on the Awesome Sound Record label. The video, posted in January 2021 and made by G-ISLANDS FILMZ, passed 370,000 views on the day it was opened. Ti Al is credited as writer and performer, and the description files it under dancehall, not bouyon.

The next two releases open the partnership with Wizito. “DOUDOU”, released on 8 November 2021, is credited to Ti Al and Wizito, and the Ti Al channel lists it with a production by Litleboy, a name our Litleboy LSBeats767 page follows on the Dominica side, although the track page does not say whether it is him. “LEFT RIGHT”, on 24 January 2022, brings together Ti Al, Wizito and Youry, and its title on the channel carries the Tolly Boy’s mention, the DJ Chimik production and the word BOUYON in brackets. Both videos are age restricted: only their listed titles could be read.

“Fresh”, with BALY G, came out on 18 June 2022, composed by Marvoni and mixed at the same SXM studio. Its video passed 310,000 views, but its description speaks of trap, so this profile does not count it as a bouyon track. That back and forth between trap, dancehall and bouyon is common among singers of the French speaking Caribbean scene, and the page of each track is the only judge used here.

Baby ou Bonne and TunTun: the two openly bouyon tracks

“Baby ou Bonne” came out on 16 February 2023 and its audio video, posted the next day on the Ti Al channel, carries the words bouyon 2023 in its title. The composition is credited to DJ Taffy, one of the busiest producers of Dominica bouyon, the mix to YRIBBZ and the editing to DJ EYEDOL. The track passed 200,000 views on the day it was opened.

“TunTun” is the most viewed track in his catalogue. On Deezer it is signed Wizito featuring Ti Al and Dj Chimik, released on 1 August 2025 on the YMW Tolly Boys label, running one minute 42. The video posted the next day on the Ti Al channel adds the word BOUYON to the title, credits DJ CHIMIK as composer, DJ CHIMIK and TYBY on the mix, and passed 620,000 views.

Short format, shared chorus, producer named in the title: “TunTun” has the shape of current platform bouyon. It is also the track that ties Ti Al most clearly to the Tolly Boys circle label, on which our Wizito page already documents “Payé” and “Love Me”.

The limits of the record and where to listen

Six verified releases, two tracks presented as bouyon by their own video page, a third announced as such in its listed title on the channel: that is the full record. View counts are snapshots from the day the pages were opened. The Instagram and Facebook accounts named in his descriptions were not opened, and only his YouTube channel is listed among the official links of this page.

To listen, start with “TunTun” with Wizito, then “Baby ou Bonne” for the DJ Taffy touch, and “LEFT RIGHT” for the trio with Wizito and Youry. Our bouyon playlist and our pages on bouyon in Saint Martin and bouyon in Guadeloupe place these tracks in their scenes.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ti Al?

Ti Al is a singer whose YouTube channel presents “Baby ou Bonne”, produced by DJ Taffy in 2023, and “TunTun”, with Wizito and DJ Chimik in 2025, as bouyon tracks. His catalogue counts six singles since 2020, including “DOUDOU” and “LEFT RIGHT” with Wizito, the latter also with Youry.

Where is Ti Al from?

No source opened says. His published Instagram handle, ti_al971, carries the Guadeloupe code, his first two tracks were mixed in a studio identified as SXM, that is Saint Martin, and his partners come from the YMW Tolly Boys circle. This profile stays with those markers.

What is “TunTun”?

A Wizito single featuring Ti Al and Dj Chimik, released on 1 August 2025 on the YMW Tolly Boys label according to Deezer. The video posted on the Ti Al channel presents it as bouyon, credits DJ CHIMIK as composer and passed 620,000 views on the day it was opened.

Are all Ti Al tracks bouyon?

No. “Girl ou sé on brand new” is tagged dancehall and “Fresh”, with BALY G, trap in their descriptions. Only “Baby ou Bonne”, “TunTun” and “LEFT RIGHT”, going by its listed title on the channel, carry the bouyon mention.

Where can I hear Ti Al?

Start with “TunTun” and “Baby ou Bonne”, then “LEFT RIGHT”. Our bouyon playlist and the Tolly Boys page take the listening further.
