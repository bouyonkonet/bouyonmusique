C4: one verified credit, a million view record, and a name to disambiguate

Published October 20, 2026

C4 is the name credited as featured artist and co-producer on “We Not Going Home”, the record by BRG Hollywood, a bouyon artist from Sint Maarten, which in 2026 became the first record by an artist from the Dutch side of Sint Maarten to reach one million views on YouTube. That is, to date, the only verified credit for C4 in the public sources consulted for this profile, and this page says so in its introduction rather than hiding it. No published full name, no confirmed origin, no documented solo catalogue: the public record of C4 holds one record. This profile tells that record precisely, places C4 in the bouyon circuit where the track travels, sets aside one by one the namesakes who carry the same short name in other musics, and writes down plainly what the research did not find. If C4 publishes more, this profile will be completed and dated, like every page on this site.

The one central fact: We Not Going Home, with BRG Hollywood

The whole verified record of C4 turns around a single track, and that track is solid. “We Not Going Home” is a record by BRG Hollywood, a recording artist from Sint Maarten whose full name, Jaheim “BRG Hollywood” Meyers, is given by the island press. The track page on Shazam gives the full credits: BRG Hollywood on vocals, Jaheim M Meyers as songwriter, and C4 as co-producer, in the production and engineering section. The record was released on October 31, 2025 on the BankrollGang label, and Shazam lists a tempo of 160 BPM, the fast tempo that is the signature of modern bouyon. The name of C4 also stands in the release title itself, “feat. C4”, which makes him both a featured artist and a co-producer of the record, two roles the credits separate cleanly.

What happened to the record next is a documented piece of music history for its island. On June 1, 2026, the site NEWS.SX reported that the Prime Minister of Sint Maarten, Dr. Luc Mercelina, congratulated BRG Hollywood on becoming the first music artist from the Dutch side of Sint Maarten to reach one million views on YouTube, with this hit song, “We Not Going Home”, featuring C4. The official statement, quoted by the outlet, presents the milestone as a testament to the talent, hard work and determination of the artist, and as a significant moment for the cultural and creative industries of Sint Maarten. The name of C4 is therefore attached, by the press and by a government congratulation, to a documented national record. That is no small credit, even if it is, for now, the only one.

Precision matters on what this record credits and to whom. The record belongs to BRG Hollywood, the lead artist, the first artist from the Dutch side of the island at that level of views. The share of C4 is the one the published credits give: featured artist and co-producer. This profile does not turn the record of one artist into the personal record of the other. It says what the sources say, no more and no less, and that level of precision is exactly what allows a name as short as C4 to keep a clean page on a site that makes credit its rule.

What the credits say, and what they do not say

The published credits allow the role of C4 to be described with some finesse. He is not credited as a songwriter of the track: Shazam attributes the writing to Jaheim M Meyers alone. Nor is he presented as a simple guest singer dropping a verse: the production and engineering section names him co-producer, which places his work on the side of the sound, of the construction of the track, alongside his featured artist presence in the release title. In modern bouyon, a music of producers as much as of singers, that double role is a complete professional identity, even summed up in a single track.

By contrast, no source consulted gives the full name of C4. None gives his origin, his community, his island of birth or his current base. This profile does not deduce a Sint Maarten origin from the sole fact that the record belongs to a Sint Maarten artist: co-producers work from island to island across the Caribbean, and a deduction of that kind is exactly the sort of inference this site refuses to print. In the same way, no source documents a solo C4 record, an album, an EP or a second collaboration under that name in the bouyon circuit. The research was run across the streaming platforms, the Sint Maarten press and the bouyon mix lists: the result is one track, and this profile publishes that result as it stands.

No official social account of C4 could be verified during this research, so this page links to no profile. It is the same rule as for the other thin profiles on this site: an official link goes into the structured data only if it has been opened and confirmed as the artist own. For a two character name, the risk of linking a namesake account is at its highest, and caution is the only honest option.

C4 is not the other C4s: the full disambiguation

The name C4 is one of the most crowded in world music, and a careless profile would draw from it facts that do not belong to the Sint Maarten artist. The best documented namesake is a United States producer from Atlanta, raised in Augusta, Georgia, to whom HotNewHipHop devoted its Behind The Beat format: a producer of records for T.I. and Young Thug, he explains that his name comes from the piano note, and not from the explosive. That C4 works in American hip hop, with a history, placements and a geography that have no published link to bouyon, BRG Hollywood or Sint Maarten. None of his facts enters this profile.

Second major namesake, C4 Pedro is an Angolan singer of kizomba, afro house and RnB, real name Pedro Henrique Lisboa Santos, born in Luanda on July 7, 1983, founder of his own production structure and active since the late 2000s between Belgium, France and Angola. His full stage name is C4 Pedro, not C4, and his music belongs to the Lusophone world, not to the English speaking and Creole bouyon circuit of the Caribbean. Third trap, Bana C4 is a Paris based Afrobeat group, documented by music analytics platforms with its own African collaborations. Fourth, C4 Reggae Combativo is an Argentine roots reggae artist. Fifth, a Jamaican born UK rapper releases as C4_highly-Explosive. Each of these artists has his own record, his own tracks and his own audience. None of them is the co-producer of “We Not Going Home”.

The rule applied here is the rule of the other generic name profiles on this site: only facts tied to the precise credit, C4 as featured artist and co-producer of “We Not Going Home” by BRG Hollywood, are kept. A track found under the name C4 alone on a platform, with no link to BRG Hollywood, BankrollGang or Sint Maarten, is presumed to belong to a namesake and stays out. That discipline has a price, the shortness of this profile, and a benefit, its reliability: nothing written here will have to be withdrawn on the day C4 publishes his own biography.

The circuit where the track travels: the bouyon of the DJ sets

If the record of C4 holds one track, that track travels in the right circuit. The published tracklists of the Blaqrose Supreme podcast, one of the regular channels of bouyon mixes, place “We Not Going Home”, in a live version, inside two 2026 mixes: the Bouyon Mix Pt2 and the Bouyon Freestyle Mix. In those lists, the BRG Hollywood and C4 record stands in the middle of the names that make the current scene, Ridge, 1t1, Trilla-G, Signal Band, Gwada G and DJ Taffy. For a bouyon record, being carried in the mixes that serve as the unofficial radio of the genre counts as a measure of real circulation, independent of view counts.

The context of the lead artist also lights the weight of the track. The Sint Maarten press reports that BRG Hollywood was nominated at the Caribbean Music Awards 2026 in the Best Bouyon Artist and Best Bouyon Song categories, and a finalist for Bouyon Artist of the Year. Those nominations belong to BRG Hollywood, and this profile leaves them to him. But they situate the world in which the single credit of C4 exists: a lead artist in full regional recognition, a record breaking track for his island, and the bouyon mix circuit where the track keeps turning. The co-producer of such a track is no name lost in a catalogue, he is a name attached to the moment the bouyon of Sint Maarten crossed a national mark.

Sint Maarten holds a particular place in the geography of bouyon this site documents. An island shared between a Dutch side, Sint Maarten, and a French side, Saint-Martin, it stands on the road of the genre between Dominica, where bouyon was born in the late 1980s, Guadeloupe and Martinique, where it is also called jump up, and the scenes of the northern Caribbean. The record of “We Not Going Home” is presented by the island authorities as a first for the Dutch side, which makes it a dated marker of the expansion of bouyon beyond its islands of origin. C4, by his co-production credit, is one of the credited makers of that marker, and it is on that title, exactly, that he stands among the artists of this site.

Why a profile this short, and how to hear C4

This site publishes profiles of several thousand words when the verified record allows it, with discographies track by track and careers told season by season. It also publishes, when the research commands it, short profiles that state their limits. The profile of C4 is of that second family, and it owns it: after research run from the streaming platforms, the regional press and the mixes of the genre, the verified public record of C4 comes down to one track, a double role of featured artist and co-producer, a national record in which that track took part, and a presence in the bouyon mixes of 2026. Everything else would be invention, and this site does not fill blanks with inventions.

The listening therefore holds in one step, but it is a good step. Hear “We Not Going Home”, by BRG Hollywood featuring C4, released on October 31, 2025 on BankrollGang, at 160 BPM according to Shazam, then find its live version in the bouyon mixes of Blaqrose Supreme, where it stands alongside records by Ridge, 1t1 and Trilla-G. To place the sound inside the genre, our bouyon playlist gathers the bouyon of Dominica and its diaspora, and our history of bouyon page tells how this sound born in Roseau spread across the Caribbean, as far as Sint Maarten.

The open questions are written here rather than filled in. What is the full name of C4? Where does he come from, and where does he work? Has he produced or sung on other tracks in the circuit, under this name or under another? Is a solo project in preparation since the 2026 record? No public source consulted answers those questions at the time of writing, on October 20, 2026. If C4, BRG Hollywood or BankrollGang publish those elements, this profile will be completed, dated and corrected. In the meantime, the page holds its central fact, verified and dated: on the first record by an artist from the Dutch side of Sint Maarten to reach one million views on YouTube, the name credited as featured artist and co-producer is C4.

Frequently asked questions

Who is C4, the artist of this profile?

C4 is the artist credited as featured artist and co-producer on “We Not Going Home”, the bouyon record by BRG Hollywood, an artist from Sint Maarten. Shazam publishes the credits of the track, released on October 31, 2025 on BankrollGang: BRG Hollywood on vocals, Jaheim M Meyers as songwriter, and C4 as co-producer. No public source consulted gives the full name or the origin of C4.

What record did We Not Going Home set?

On June 1, 2026, the Sint Maarten press reported that Prime Minister Dr. Luc Mercelina congratulated BRG Hollywood on becoming the first music artist from the Dutch side of Sint Maarten to reach one million views on YouTube, with “We Not Going Home”, featuring C4. The record belongs to the lead artist, BRG Hollywood, and C4 is attached to it as credited featured artist and co-producer.

Is C4 the American producer C4, or C4 Pedro?

No. The American producer C4, from Augusta and based in Atlanta, is documented by HotNewHipHop for his records with T.I. and Young Thug, and his name comes from a piano note. C4 Pedro is an Angolan kizomba singer, real name Pedro Henrique Lisboa Santos, born in Luanda in 1983. The C4 of this profile is neither: he is only the name credited on the BRG Hollywood record.

Has C4 released solo records?

No solo C4 record is documented by the sources consulted for this profile, no album, no EP and no second collaboration in the bouyon circuit. The one verified recording is “We Not Going Home” with BRG Hollywood, also carried in a live version in the 2026 bouyon mixes of Blaqrose Supreme. If a solo catalogue exists or appears, this profile will be updated.

Is C4 from Sint Maarten?

That is not established. The sources consulted publish neither the origin nor the base of C4. Sint Maarten is the island of BRG Hollywood, the lead artist of the track, and the million view milestone is a record of that island. This profile does not deduce the origin of the co-producer from that of the lead artist: the question stays open, and it is written as such.

Where can I hear C4?

On “We Not Going Home”, by BRG Hollywood featuring C4, released on October 31, 2025 on BankrollGang, and in the 2026 bouyon mixes of Blaqrose Supreme, where the track appears in a live version alongside Ridge, 1t1 and Trilla-G.
