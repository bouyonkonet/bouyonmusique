Django De Cayenne

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Django De Cayenne is a rapper first: his Deezer page counts thirty five releases, nearly all outside bouyon. He enters this site through a single track, “Vice City Bouyon Bonus”, the fourth and last title of the EP “Le disque d'or”, released on May 29, 2026 on Pa ici a with Barlem B as guest. The day before, on May 28, 2026, he posted that version on his own YouTube channel with a production credit to Barlem B and a Bouyon keyword among the video tags. Two primary pages, one title: the bouyon file is thin, and this profile says so.

A bouyon version as a bonus

“Le disque d'or” gathers four titles, all with Barlem B: “Les Videurs”, “Vice City”, “Arrosage Automatique” and “Vice city Bouyon Bonus”. The last one runs 2 minutes 01 and, according to the Deezer artist page consulted in wave 31, it is written by Thibault Michel Viale. Its title reads as a reworking of “Vice City”, the second track of the EP, moved to bouyon tempo and colour and added at the end of the record. Deezer files the EP under reggaeton, which shows that the platform category is not enough to describe what it holds.

On YouTube, the artist channel posted “Django de Cayenne - Vice City Bouyon Bonus (prodby.@barlemb )” on May 28, 2026, next to the original “Vice City” and a “Vice City Akira Mashup”. The video tags name Kalash, Maureen, Shatta, Bouyon and Skyrock, a shatta and bouyon neighbourhood claimed by the artist himself. That double signal, the title on two platforms and the keyword on his own video, is what brings Django De Cayenne into this directory, where wave 31 had held him on the Deezer listing alone.

A rap catalogue

The rest of the Deezer page describes a rapper. It lists “Pussy a l'orange” and “Chuleo” by Nenasita, again with Barlem B, “Coucoune Analyste”, “Tig Mawké”, “Jersey De Cayenne”, “Sauce Chien” with Mike Shabb, and the projects “Jeune à jamais”, “Génome” and “Bouillon D'awara”. None of those titles states a bouyon tie. This profile therefore does not present Django De Cayenne as a career bouyon artist, but as a rapper who signed one verifiable bouyon version.

Barlem B is the constant of the record: guest on all four titles of “Le disque d'or” and credited for production on the bonus video. No opened page presents him as a bouyon artist in his own right, so he stays named here as the partner on this title, without a separate profile.

Origin, left open

The stage name contains Cayenne, and one album is called “Bouillon D'awara”, after a dish associated with French Guiana. Those are signals, not statements: no opened page gives a birthplace, a home base or a biography. This site therefore assigns Django De Cayenne no territory, and only points, for regional context of the genre, to our page bouyon in French Guiana without listing the artist there.

On October 23, 2026, the verifiable file comes down to one bouyon title, published on Deezer and on YouTube, with a named guest and producer. If the artist publishes further bouyon titles or a biography, this page will be completed.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Django De Cayenne?

A rapper whose Deezer page lists thirty five releases. Only one title states a bouyon tie: “Vice city Bouyon Bonus”, with Barlem B, on the EP “Le disque d'or” released on May 29, 2026 on Pa ici a.

Which is his verified bouyon title?

“Vice city Bouyon Bonus (feat. Barlem B)”, 2 minutes 01, the last track of “Le disque d'or”. The same version was posted on May 28, 2026 on his YouTube channel, with Bouyon among the video tags.

Where is Django De Cayenne from?

No opened source says. The word Cayenne in his name and the album “Bouillon D'awara” are signals, not proof, and this site assigns no origin.

Who is Barlem B?

The guest on all four titles of “Le disque d'or”, credited for production on the bouyon bonus video. No opened page presents him as a bouyon artist in his own right.

Why is this profile short?

Because the bouyon tie rests on one title inside a rap catalogue. This site prefers a short and exact profile to an inflated one.
