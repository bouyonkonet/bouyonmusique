Ransom Beatz: a steady stream of bouyon riddims in 2026

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Ransom Beatz is a producer who releases instrumentals at an unusual pace, with more than two hundred releases under his name. Long focused on afrobeat and dancehall, in 2026 he gave a full album and an EP to bouyon, and he licenses his riddims to singers.

A catalogue of instrumentals

Most of his catalogue fits in one word: instrumental. There is “Lagos Nights Instrumental”, “Afrobeat instrumental for freestyle”, some afroswing, some bashment, and dozens of singles released a few days apart. None of his pages say where he is from.

On YouTube, every track is a tagged demo, posted for listening only. The description points to his online shop and sets the rule: no release without a paid licence, downloads included.

Bouyon Instrumentals, March 2026

On March 27, 2026 he released “Bouyon Instrumentals”, twenty-one tracks. Many titles have a French flavour, “La brouette”, “La pieuvre”, “Le 69”, “Chaud”, next to “Wraith”, “Jahorina” or “Carni virus”. Alexay Beats is featured on about half of them.

Several of these riddims went up on YouTube a few days earlier. “Le 69” appeared on March 10 and “Le Brouette”, billed as dark bouyon, the next day. The RnB bouyon versions, such as “Jahorina” or “Heart on fire”, sit around 2,000 views each.

Party Node and Shotta

In July, the EP “PARTY NODE "BOUYON'” sorts its six tracks by flavour: bouyon dancehall for “SM” and “Pow Pow”, bouyon trap for “Undercover”, carnival bouyon for “ÉLAN”. The video for “ÉLAN”, made with Theodora and Rema in mind, is past 4,700 views.

His biggest number comes from a collaboration. “Shotta”, co-produced with Normae and posted on Normae's channel in March 2026, aims at the sound of 1T1 and Miimii and is close to 130,000 views. “Upside Down”, in the same trap bouyon vein, followed at the end of May.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ransom Beatz?

A producer who releases and sells instrumentals, mainly afrobeat and dancehall, and who has made bouyon riddims since 2026.

What are his bouyon projects?

The album Bouyon Instrumentals, released March 27, 2026, and the EP PARTY NODE "BOUYON', released July 5, 2026.

What is his most watched bouyon riddim?

Shotta, co-produced with Normae, which is close to 130,000 views on Normae's channel.

Can singers use his riddims?

Yes, but the YouTube videos are demos. His descriptions say a licence must be bought before any release.

Where is Ransom Beatz from?

His videos and releases do not say.
