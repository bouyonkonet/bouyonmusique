Frank White: the Zone Rouge voice and his bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

Frank White is a bouyon artist from Dominica, introduced by Cranberry TV as a member of Zone Rouge. The credits on one of his songs give his writer's name, Frank Jules. Since 2024 he has put out songs billed as bouyon on his own channel and on DJ Taffy's, alone or with Trixx, Poyzin, Beevo and Litleboy.

A Zone Rouge artist

In May 2024, Cranberry TV had him on episode 13 of the third season of Parlé. The description calls him “the Dominican bouyon artist” and promises a talk about life inside Zone Rouge, one of the island's hardest working bands, about his journey and about the band's sound.

His channel, Frank White-Official, also keeps a record of the band. It holds “Setting Trapz”, credited to Zone Rouge, and “Stop The Crime” by Kenny G with Owl, on the 2018 Bounce Riddim. The description of “Tom Dick N Harry”, posted by WizZarD HouSe RecorDs, names the writer as Frank “Frank White” Jules, next to Marcus “Trixx” Jno Baptiste.

From Duck to Tom Dick N Harry

On January 29, 2024, DJ Taffy posted “Duck”, billed “bouyon 2024”. Frank White sings it alone, and Tha WiZzard handles production, mixing and mastering. In September he uploaded “Keep Her Coming Back” to his channel, produced by Freeze and mixed by DJ Rincer.

February 2025 brought “Hurt”, billed “Bouyon 2025”, which he wrote with Poyzin. Production is credited to “litle boy” and recording to DJ Rincer. A month later, WizZarD HouSe RecorDs released “Tom Dick N Harry” with Trixx, recorded by DiYoute at YW3 Entertainment.

Hustle, Gangsta Love and the Beevo duets

2026 gave him his biggest numbers. On January 9, DJ Taffy posted “Hustle”, with Litleboy, Trixx and Kenny G, billed “bouyon 2026”: over 46,000 views. In February, Litleboy's channel added “Gangsta Love”, a bouyon freestyle by the same four voices, which has passed 65,000 views.

He also works steadily with Beevo. “Party”, by Beevo featuring Frank White, came out on DJ Taffy's channel in February 2026, followed by “Missing You”. In August, “Did You Really Care”, which the two wrote together, appeared on his channel, produced by Egg for Little House Studios and Treble Clef Music and recorded at YW3 Entertainment.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Frank White?

A bouyon artist from Dominica, a member of Zone Rouge according to Cranberry TV, credited as Frank Jules as a writer of Tom Dick N Harry.

What are his bouyon songs?

Duck and Keep Her Coming Back (2024), Hurt with Poyzin (2025), then Hustle, Gangsta Love, Party and Did You Really Care (2026).

What is his most watched song?

Gangsta Love, a bouyon freestyle with Litleboy, Kenny G and Trixx, past 65,000 views on Litleboy's channel, ahead of Hustle at over 46,000.

Who produces his songs?

Tha WiZzard on Duck, Freeze on Keep Her Coming Back, “litle boy” on Hurt and Egg for Little House Studios on Did You Really Care. DJ Rincer and DiYoute record and mix several of them.

Who does he sing with most often?

Trixx, on Tom Dick N Harry, Hustle and Gangsta Love, and Beevo, on Party, Missing You and Did You Really Care.
