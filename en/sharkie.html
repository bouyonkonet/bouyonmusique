Sharkie: Okra Slime, the title that names the riddim, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Sharkie carries the title that gives the riddim its name: “Okra Slime”, on the Okra Slime Riddim, billed as “Barbados Bouyon 2026”. Two uploads on the BimTraveler channel document it: a visualizer titled “Sharkie X 10Star”, posted on March 24, 2026, then the official audio on April 5, 2026. Writing is credited to 10Star and Jamal Beckles, and recording, production, mix and master to 10Star Records. No opened page gives a biography or a birthplace.

A title track for a riddim

The visualizer came first, on March 24, 2026, as “Sharkie X 10Star - Okra Slime ( Okra Slime Riddim ) [ Bouyon 2026 ] Official Visualizer”, running 2 minutes 41. Its short description writes “Skarky x 10Star” and then “2026 Bouyon”, with the bouyon2026 and bouyonriddim tags. Skarky is the spelling on that page; this site records it as such and keeps the name Sharkie, used in both titles.

The official audio followed on April 5, 2026, at 2 minutes 32, billed “Barbados Bouyon 2026”. Its description sets out the credits: written by 10Star and Jamal Beckles, recorded and produced by 10Star Records, mixed and mastered by 10Star Records. Jamal Beckles is a writing credit; no opened source says it is Sharkie’s legal name, and this profile does not claim it.

The 10Star Records Okra Slime batch

As with any bouyon riddim, one instrumental carries several voices. The BimTraveler channel also places Stella Billy’s “Energy” and Ian Stylez’s “Crazy Jump” on it, the latter known here only from the video listing. Sharkie’s cut is the one that shares the riddim’s name, and the visualizer, out before the batch’s other audios, works as its front door.

The “Barbados Bouyon” label and the CropOver tag describe the music and Barbados’s Crop Over season. They show how bouyon, born in Dominica, takes shape on other islands, where soca and bouyon meet. They say nothing about where Sharkie was born, and this site assigns no birthplace.

What the file holds

One title, two uploads, two dates, a pair of writing credits and one production house: that is the whole verifiable file on October 23, 2026. No official account of Sharkie’s own was opened during this research.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Sharkie?

Sharkie is the artist on “Okra Slime”, the lead cut of the Okra Slime Riddim produced by 10Star Records and billed as Barbados Bouyon 2026. No public biography was found.

When did Okra Slime come out?

The visualizer was uploaded on March 24, 2026 and the official audio on April 5, 2026, both on the BimTraveler channel.

Who wrote Okra Slime?

The official audio description credits the writing to 10Star and Jamal Beckles, and production to 10Star Records.

Why the spelling Skarky?

That is how the visualizer description spells it. Both titles write Sharkie, and this site keeps that name.

Where is Sharkie from?

No opened page names a birthplace. The titles bill the music as Barbados bouyon tied to Crop Over.
