NattyBill: bass player and bouyon riddim maker

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

NattyBill, real name Jervon Henderson according to the credits of “La Fami”, is a bass player first and a name on a cover second. You mostly find him in the credits, on live bass, for DJ Taffy, ProducerDlo or Fetty Mark. In January 2026 his name went on the front of a release next to Mr Gwada, on a riddim he built himself.

A bass player in the credits

The name turns up from one page to the next, spelled Nattybill, NattyBill or Natty Bill. In January 2024 he played bass on “Kokay ce soir” by DJ Taffy and Candy. In October 2025 he played on “Rum”, the Kassav tribute by Fetty Mark and Dondada. He is also credited on “Tie Up”, with Eks and Wotlis.

In January 2026 the credits of the Bouyon Rave Riddim gave his full name, Jervon “Nattybill” Henderson, on bass, next to Akim “Haze” Hazel on drums. ProducerDlo had sat him down for a long interview in August 2024, framed around his bass work with Ridge. In October 2026, rehearsal footage of Ridge's live band still had him on bass.

La Fami, with Mr Gwada

On January 15, 2026, Mr Gwada's channel posted “La Fami”, billed bouyon 2026. The credits are short and exact: lyrics by Kernel Stevens and Jervon Henderson, riddim and mix by NattyBill, mastering by Bouyon Nation767. It is his most played song, past 34,000 views.

It was not the first time his name led a title. Back in September 2019, DJ CashMoney 767 uploaded “Black Everyone Get Down”, presented under the Nattybill name and billed “Bouyon 2020”, a few months ahead of the year on the label. The page gives no credits, so who does what on it is not stated.

His own riddims

On his own channel he posts instrumentals. “Stomp”, presented as a bouyon instrumental, came out on October 6, 2023 and has passed 6,000 views. On December 24 that year came the “Trance Bouyon Riddim”, which he describes as a 2024 production blending trance-inspired synths with bouyon rhythms.

The Trance Bouyon Riddim description says he produced, arranged and played it alone, and that the video mixes studio and stage footage. The same channel also carries a “Spring Sewo Bouyon Riddim” and bouyon remixes, among them a “Super Gremlin”.

Frequently asked questions

Who is NattyBill?

Jervon Henderson, a bass player and bouyon riddim producer, credited on live bass for DJ Taffy, ProducerDlo and Fetty Mark.

What is his best known song?

La Fami, with Mr Gwada, posted on January 15, 2026 and billed bouyon 2026, with over 34,000 views. He made the riddim and the mix.

Which riddims has he produced?

Stomp, a bouyon instrumental posted in October 2023, the Trance Bouyon Riddim, posted in December 2023, and the Spring Sewo Bouyon Riddim on his channel.

Which songs does he play bass on?

Kokay ce soir by DJ Taffy and Candy, Rum by Fetty Mark and Dondada, Tie Up with Eks and Wotlis, and the songs of ProducerDlo's Bouyon Rave Riddim.

Does he play with Ridge?

Yes: a 2024 ProducerDlo interview is about his work with Ridge, and rehearsal footage from October 2026 has him on bass in Ridge's live band.
