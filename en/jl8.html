JL8: Baissé, Ba to Pliss and his carnival bouyon songs

By Smokiller · Published October 29, 2026

JL8 is a singer who releases under the JL8 Production name and posts his videos on his YouTube channel. Two of his songs are tagged bouyon and carnival: “Ba to Pliss” in 2022 and “Baissé”, with Parigoler and Pretty, in 2024. The latter has passed 111,000 views. His descriptions do not say where he is from.

Ba to Pliss, the first bouyon

On September 2, 2022, JL8 uploaded the video for “Ba to Pliss”. The credits are short and exact: JL8 Records on production, Kodsoulmusic as composer, JL8 Production on mix and master, basketteur973 behind the camera and Pasteur973 on the edit. The hashtags set the frame, #bouyon, #antilles and #carnaval. The video has more than 36,000 views.

The same year, his name appears in DJ Kerry's “Mix Bouyon chaud 2022”, next to Holy G, Keks Mafia, Boutcha Bwa, Natoxie and Barbar. It shows his songs were already moving through DJ bouyon selections.

Whine Up and a turn toward shatta

January 2023 brought “Whine Up”, billed this time as dancehall and shatta. The description names him as the singer and credits Kodsoulmusic for the riddim, Bilul on mix and master, gsk production on recording and NPMP Campagnie on the video. It has passed 61,000 views.

His biggest numbers actually come from songs not billed as bouyon. “CoCo”, with Champion, and “CoCo 2”, with Siyøu, each pass 119,000 views, and “WAP”, tagged shatta, is close to 100,000. “Do Brazil” and “Vanille Chocolat” round out the channel.

Baissé, the 2024 carnival song

On January 5, 2024, JL8 released “Baissé”, with Parigoler and Pretty, under the hashtags #carnaval and #bouyon 2024. The hook sits in the description: “Mo di baissé baissé, pli ba, en bas” (I said get low, get low, lower, down). With more than 111,000 views, it is his most watched bouyon song.

The team stays the same. JL8 Production produces, Kodsoulmusic composes, mixes and masters, Gsk records the vocals and npmp production directs the video. On Deezer the song is credited as “BAISSÉ (feat. PARIGOLER & Pretty b*tch)”. The hashtags aim wide: French Guiana, Martinique, Guadeloupe, Reunion and Toulouse.

Frequently asked questions

Who is JL8?

A singer who releases under the JL8 Production name and the voice behind the bouyon songs Ba to Pliss (2022) and Baissé (2024). His origin is not given in the sources we opened.

What is his most watched bouyon song?

Baissé, with Parigoler and Pretty, uploaded on January 5, 2024, with more than 111,000 views.

Who makes his riddims?

Kodsoulmusic, credited on Ba to Pliss, Whine Up and Baissé.

Does JL8 only do bouyon?

No. Whine Up is billed as dancehall and shatta, and WAP is tagged shatta. His declared bouyon songs are Ba to Pliss and Baissé.

Who directs his videos?

basketteur973 and Pasteur973 on Ba to Pliss, NPMP on Whine Up and Baissé.
