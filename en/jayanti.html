Lie To Me, with Redz

“Lie To Me” runs 2 minutes 12 and had about 1,170 views when opened. The track is presented as official audio, with Bouyon 2026 in its title. The credits are clear: Jayanti and Redz as artists, ShakoProduction on production, Ransum Records on recording, mixing and mastering.

Redz shares the billing and is known to this site only through this title. No page of Redz’s own was opened, and this profile says nothing about Redz beyond the appearance on “Lie To Me”. The name ShakoProduction recurs on several recent bouyon pages in the region; here it is simply quoted as the producer of the track.

Bouyon from Saint Lucia

The page does more than put bouyon in the title: it presents the track as bouyon music from Saint Lucia, with the island’s flag. The page itself makes that link, and this site reports it as such, without drawing a birthplace or a biography from it. The track joins a scene where bouyon, born in Dominica, is taken up by artists from neighbouring islands, which we follow on our page on bouyon in Saint Lucia.

For listening, “Lie To Me” belongs with new bouyon in 2026: a June release, right in the carnival season of the island chain.

The limits of the file

The JAYANTI MUSIC channel holds three uploads at the time of this research, and “Lie To Me” is the only one billed bouyon. The others are not presented here as bouyon titles. No interview, no legal name and no career start date were found.

If Jayanti releases further titles or an official introduction, this page will be revised and dated. In the meantime, the verified listening fits into one title: “Lie To Me”, Jayanti and Redz, Bouyon 2026.
