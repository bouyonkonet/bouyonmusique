Uglyboy: No Pwoblem and Vody 22, bouyon

By Smokiller · Published October 23, 2026

Uglyboy, also written Ugly Boy depending on the page, is a voice whose verified file comes down to three titles from 2025. The most telling is “NO PWOBLEM”, with DDK and Skin Vybz, released on November 10, 2025 and presented in its description as a new Bouyon Music track, with Voodoo Studio on production and DJ Taffy among the credited names. The second is “VODY 22”, a group video tagged #BOUYON on the Jixels channel in December 2025. The third, “KANIBAL”, is a duet with Sheyta. This profile sticks to those pages and does not fill the gaps: no biography, no legal name, no origin.

NO PWOBLEM, the title that names its genre

“NO PWOBLEM” exists in two forms. The distribution version, on the automatic Uglyboy page, came out on November 10, 2025 under the 10743430 Records DK reference and runs 2 minutes 51; it credits Uglyboy, DDK, Skin Vybz, Dj Taffy and Voodoo Studio. The official audio, posted the next day on a channel named MANAGYALIS, describes the track as a new Bouyon Music song by Uglyboy featuring DDK and Skin Vybz, gives Voodoo Studio as production and adds a set of hashtags, including bouyonmusic, DjTaffy and DominicaStyle.

That last hashtag calls for a careful reading. “DominicaStyle” points to a style reference, the Dominica way where bouyon was born, and this site does not read it as a statement of origin for Uglyboy. Likewise, a channel named MANAGYALIS posting the track shows proximity to that voice from the Jixels circle, and establishes nothing more.

KANIBAL and VODY 22, with Sheyta

On May 23, 2025, the Sheyta channel released “KANIBAL”, titled “UGLY BOY (KANIBAL) FT SHEYTA”. The description presents the track as the duo’s second title and gives Ugly Boy the same ugly_boyz0 handle quoted on the “NO PWOBLEM” page, which ties both spellings to the same voice. The page does not mention bouyon, so the title stays out of the structured data.

On December 17, 2025, the two voices meet again on “VODY 22”, an official video of 2 minutes 33 released on the Jixels channel with #BOUYON in the title. Uglyboy is credited alongside Lecus, Leseb and Sheyta, over a production by Qwann, with Yas Beats on mixing, mastering and studio work, Mighty Production directing and Jixels as executive producer.

A namesake and the limits

A search on the name also brings up a 2021 video titled “R.I.P UglyBoy”, posted by a channel of the International Prostyle team. It concerns a different person and has nothing to do with the titles described here; this site mentions it only to avoid confusion.

Beyond that, no opened page publishes a biography, a legal name or a place of origin for Uglyboy, and the accounts quoted in the descriptions were not opened. The file therefore comes down to two declared bouyon titles, “NO PWOBLEM” and “VODY 22”, and one unlabelled duet, “KANIBAL”. It will be completed if new releases appear, in the tracking of new bouyon in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Uglyboy?

Uglyboy, also written Ugly Boy, is a voice credited on “NO PWOBLEM” with DDK and Skin Vybz, on “VODY 22” with Lecus, Leseb and Sheyta, and on “KANIBAL” with Sheyta, all released in 2025. No public biography was found.

Which Uglyboy bouyon titles are verified?

“NO PWOBLEM”, presented as Bouyon Music and released on November 10, 2025, and “VODY 22”, a video tagged #BOUYON released on December 17, 2025 on the Jixels channel.

Who produced NO PWOBLEM?

The description of the official audio credits Voodoo Studio with the production, and the distribution version also lists Dj Taffy among the credited names, without stating his role.

Is Uglyboy from Dominica?

No opened source says so. The DominicaStyle hashtag on the “NO PWOBLEM” page signals a style, not an origin, and this site assigns Uglyboy no island.

Is the “R.I.P UglyBoy” video about the same artist?

No. That 2021 video concerns a different person and is not used in this profile.
