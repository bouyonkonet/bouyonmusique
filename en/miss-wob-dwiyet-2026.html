Bouyon music: Samantha Bazil, Miss Wob Dwiyet 2026

By Smokiller · Published November 5, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026

The Miss Wob Dwiyet 2026 title goes to Samantha Bazil of Wesley, crowned on Wednesday 21 October 2026 at the Massacre Indoor Facility, as part of the celebrations for Dominica 48th Independence. Two concordant reports, Writeups 24 and the DBC Radio report carried by Dominica Gazette, give the placings and the special awards.

The placings of the four contestants

Four contestants represented Dominican culture in Wob Dwiyet dress, assessed on their performance and their portrayal of the culture of Dominica, per Writeups 24. Samantha Bazil of Wesley took the title.

First runner-up was Deniscia Laurant of Petite Soufriere, followed by Ta-Donna Cornelius of Portsmouth and Addisa Jno Baptiste of Roseau. One caution this page states openly: Writeups 24 spells the runner-up surname Laurent, while DBC Radio spells it Laurant. Both spellings are flagged here instead of being merged in silence.

The special awards of the evening

Per the DBC Radio report, Samantha Bazil also won Best in Wob Dwiyet, plus the People s Choice and Creole Poise awards, which were not judged. She is an accountant at the National Bank of Dominica, whose Sales Manager Michelle Joseph said the team was elated by her success.

Deniscia Laurant, an employee of J. Astaphan and Company Ltd per DBC Radio, took three awards: Best Performing Talent, Best in Creole Spectacular Wear and Miss Photogenic. Writeups 24 confirms the Best Performing Talent and Miss Photogenic awards.

A pageant of the Ministry of Culture

The Miss Wob Dwiyet pageant is organized by the Ministry of Culture, Youth, Sports and Community Development, with the National Cultural Council and Cultural Division, per Writeups 24. The Cultural Division congratulated the winner in a public post quoted by the same report.

Contestants must be between 18 and 30 years old and single, and they are assessed on their knowledge of Dominican culture, their talent and their ability to perform traditional movements in the Wob Dwiyet, per Writeups 24.

And this Wednesday 28 October, the Madam Wob Dwiyet

Traditional dress season continues this week: the official programme places the National Madam Wob Dwiyet Pageant this Wednesday 28 October 2026, also at the Massacre Indoor Facility. Guide.dm lists the start at 8:00 PM.

The Madam Wob Dwiyet is the counterpart of the Miss pageant for the older keepers of the national dress. Its result will be added when the Dominican press publishes it, the same way the title of Samantha Bazil is recorded here.
