Nothing Into Something: the Coi Leray bouyon flip by Smokiller

By Smokiller · Published October 22, 2026

This is the story of a 48 hour turnaround. On September 18, 2026, Coi Leray released Nothing Into Something, a single produced with Diplo and published by Epic Records. On September 20, Sean "Smokiller" Francis, the Dominican-born producer who founded Bouyon Shattaz, published his version: the same track, rebuilt on bouyon percussion, presented as a bouyon warm up. This page tells that flip story, with no lyrics, from the verified facts of the file.

The original track: Coi Leray and Diplo, September 18, 2026

Nothing Into Something is an empowerment record: Coi Leray sings about coming up from nothing and building the life she has now. The official video, directed by Zev York, places her in a studio with a live band, and it is around that performance footage that the bouyon flip was built. Coi Leray is a two-time Grammy nominee: for Players, in the Best Rap Performance category, and for Baby Don't Hurt Me, with David Guetta and Anne-Marie, in the Best Pop Dance Recording category, at the 66th Grammy Awards.

The original production, made with Grammy-winning producer Diplo, leans on polished dancefloor electronics. The contrast is exactly what the flip looks for: where the original smooths, bouyon pushes.

The flip: a bouyon warm up in two days

Smokiller's version rebuilds the track on the driving percussion and call and response energy of Dominica's national sound. The format is short, as released: 1 minute 41, at about 136 BPM measured, with a DJ intro version carried to 1 minute 56 in the Bouyon Shattaz radio servicing pack. The file is among the public free downloads on smokiller.com.

The speed of the turnaround is part of the story: the flip was live two days after the single's release, while the song was still climbing. Smokiller calls this format a warm up: the song the audience already loves, at carnival tempo, with the artist's voice riding drums Dominican audiences have known since childhood.

850,000 views and the reported repost

Posted to TikTok on September 20, the flip clip passed 850,000 views: the counter read on Sean's screenshot dated October 19, 2026 shows 854,700 views, 29,900 likes, 310 comments and 2,216 saves. Those are the figures of that screenshot, presented here with their date, not a live count.

Sean states in his pitch to Dominica News Online that Coi Leray herself reposted the remix, a co-sign that pushed the Dominican sound onto timelines far beyond the Caribbean. The Dominican platform answered present: Sean's contact at Dominica News Online, Malaika, agreed to receive the article on the flip, prepared on October 19. The flip belongs to a series: Smokiller had already flipped Players, Coi Leray's other big record, into a bouyon remix.

What this flip says about the Bouyon Shattaz method

Smokiller's flip catalogue follows a constant formula: take an international track the audience knows, rebuild it on bouyon drums, and publish it as a free download from smokiller.com, as with Shenseea's Vulnerability or Ayra Starr's Heaven Baby. Every flip introduces two names to a new audience at once, Smokiller the producer and Bouyon Shattaz the platform, and every caption points back to Dominica as the source of the sound.

The lineage is claimed openly: Dominica also gave the region Wrecketeng, the style built on sampled melodies and rap vocals, which many producers credit as a root of today's bouyon. The Nothing Into Something flip works squarely in that lineage, between the sampling instinct and the drum programming of modern bouyon.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Nothing Into Something bouyon remix?

The bouyon version of the Coi Leray single with Diplo, rebuilt by Smokiller and posted to TikTok on September 20, 2026 as a bouyon warm up, two days after the original's release on Epic Records. The file runs 1 minute 41 and is a free download on smokiller.com.

How many views did the Nothing Into Something flip get?

854,700 views on TikTok, with 29,900 likes and 2,216 saves, according to the screenshot of Sean's account dated October 19, 2026. Sean also states that Coi Leray reposted the remix.

Has Smokiller remixed other Coi Leray tracks?

Yes. His catalogue also holds a bouyon remix of Players, Coi Leray's Grammy-nominated record, alongside flips of Shenseea, Ayra Starr and Bryson Tiller, all offered from smokiller.com.
